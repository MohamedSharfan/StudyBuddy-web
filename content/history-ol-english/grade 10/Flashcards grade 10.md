# StudyBuddy – History Grade 10 – Flashcards (English Medium)

> Each card has a **Front** (question) and a **Back** (answer). Cover the back, answer aloud, then check.


## Chapter 01 – Sources of Studying History

**Card 01**
**Front:** What are historical "sources"?
**Back:** Writings and creations of people of the past from which information about past human activities can be extracted.

**Card 02**
**Front:** Into which two groups are historical sources divided?
**Back:** Literary sources and archaeological sources.

**Card 03**
**Front:** What is the oldest text of Sri Lanka, and when was it written?
**Back:** Deepawamsa, about the 4th century A.D.

**Card 04**
**Front:** Who wrote the first part of Mahawamsa, and where did he live?
**Back:** Mahanama Thero, in the Diksanda Seneviya Pirivena (Anuradhapura Maha Vihara).

**Card 05**
**Front:** Up to which reign does the first part of Mahawamsa extend?
**Back:** From the Buddha's arrival to the end of King Mahasen's reign.

**Card 06**
**Front:** What is the Mahawamsa Teekawa called?
**Back:** Wansattappakasini – a Pali annotation written about four centuries after the first part.

**Card 07**
**Front:** Why was Mahawamsa composed after Deepawamsa?
**Back:** Deepawamsa recorded events very briefly with repetitions; Mahawamsa had fewer mistakes.

**Card 08**
**Front:** What makes Mahawamsa special as a record?
**Back:** The history of the country is written continuously in one text by successive writers.

**Card 09**
**Front:** Which earlier texts influenced both Deepawamsa and Mahawamsa?
**Back:** Seehala Attakatha Mahawamsa, Uttara Vihara Attakatha and Vinaya Attakatha.

**Card 10**
**Front:** Give three examples of Sandesha Kawya.
**Back:** Mayura, Thisara and Selalihini Sandesha.

**Card 11**
**Front:** What kind of poem is Perakumba Siritha? Seethawaka Hatana?
**Back:** Perakumba Siritha – Prashasthi (praise) Kawya; Seethawaka Hatana – Hatan (war) Kawya.

**Card 12**
**Front:** Which two chronicles serve the Gampola–Kotte period?
**Back:** Nikaya Sangraha (Buddhist order) and Rajawaliya (political information).

**Card 13**
**Front:** What four points should be checked when using a literary source?
**Back:** Time of writing, the author, the author's objectives and how the facts were obtained.

**Card 14**
**Front:** Which Chinese monk stayed two years in the Anuradhapura Maha Vihara?
**Back:** Fa-Hsien (5th century A.D.).

**Card 15**
**Front:** Did Hsuan Tsang visit Sri Lanka?
**Back:** No. He recorded what he learnt about the island while in India (602–664 A.D.).

**Card 16**
**Front:** Who was Joao Ribeiro?
**Back:** A Portuguese who came in March 1640, served 18 years and became a captain; wrote about 17th-century Sri Lanka.

**Card 17**
**Front:** In which languages were Philippus Baldaeus's records published?
**Back:** Dutch and German.

**Card 18**
**Front:** How did Robert Knox come to Sri Lanka, and how long was he held?
**Back:** His ship's mast broke in a storm; King Rajasinghe II held him for nineteen years.

**Card 19**
**Front:** Where did Knox escape from, and what was his book called?
**Back:** Via Arippu (Mannar); *An Historical Relation of the Island Ceylon* (1681).

**Card 20**
**Front:** Which foreign writer wrote about Sri Lanka without visiting and from India?
**Back:** Fernão de Queiroz (*Temporal and Spiritual Conquest of Ceylon*, 1688).

**Card 21**
**Front:** Who was Ptolemy, and what is his link with Sri Lanka?
**Back:** Claudius Ptolemy (90–168 A.D.), a Greek-Roman geographer of Alexandria who drew a map including Sri Lanka.

**Card 22**
**Front:** Name Tamil books of India that mention Sri Lanka.
**Back:** Seelappadikaram, Manimekalayi and Padirruppattu.

**Card 23**
**Front:** List four uses of literary sources.
**Back:** Preparing chronology; political, economic and social information; substantiating one source with another; studying international relations.

**Card 24**
**Front:** What is epigraphy?
**Back:** Writings on stone, clay slabs, walls, copper or golden plates, wood and urns.

**Card 25**
**Front:** In which script are the oldest Sri Lankan inscriptions written?
**Back:** Brahmi.

**Card 26**
**Front:** What was recorded in the earliest Brahmi inscriptions?
**Back:** Events such as the offering of caves to Bhikkus.

**Card 27**
**Front:** Between which times did inscription writing continue?
**Back:** From the 2nd century B.C. to the Kandyan kingdom.

**Card 28**
**Front:** Name the five kinds of inscription by location.
**Back:** Cave, rock, pillar, slab and seat inscriptions.

**Card 29**
**Front:** Give examples of inscriptions on copper plate, golden plate and wall.
**Back:** Panakaduwa copper plate (Vijayabahu I); Vallipuram golden plate; Sigiriya graffiti.

**Card 30**
**Front:** What is the oldest coin found in Sri Lanka?
**Back:** The Kahapana (also Purana or Dharana).

**Card 31**
**Front:** Which copper coin was used in the Polonnaruwa kingdom?
**Back:** Thamba Massa.

**Card 32**
**Front:** What is numismatics?
**Back:** The study of ancient coins.

**Card 33**
**Front:** What do foreign coins found in Sri Lanka show?
**Back:** Trade links with Rome, China and India.

**Card 34**
**Front:** What do the Isurumuni "Man and Horse's Head" figures represent, and when were they made?
**Back:** The gods Parjanya (man) and Agni (horse's head); 7th–8th century A.D.

**Card 35**
**Front:** What do the Sigiriya frescoes reveal?
**Back:** Clothes, jewellery, hair styles and beauty culture of ancient women.

**Card 36**
**Front:** Why are archaeological sources often more reliable than literary ones?
**Back:** They are mostly contemporary with the events and less affected by authors' likes and dislikes.

**Card 37**
**Front:** Name four benefits of learning history.
**Back:** Understanding society, shaping the future, knowing national identity, building reconciliation, respecting other cultures, tolerating different views.

**Card 38**
**Front:** Give three causes of destruction of archaeological sources.
**Back:** Urbanization and construction, treasure hunting, scribbling on monuments.

**Card 39**
**Front:** What may rock marks such as the Sun, Moon, conch and bow represent?
**Back:** Ancient boundaries or an ancient form of communication like road signs.


## Chapter 02 – Ancient Settlements

**Card 01**
**Front:** How long ago did Sri Lanka become a human habitation?
**Back:** About 125 000 years ago, when Homo Sapiens migrated across the Indian Ocean.

**Card 02**
**Front:** Name the three stages of human history in Sri Lanka.
**Back:** Pre-historic, proto-historic and early historic eras.

**Card 03**
**Front:** What period does the early historic era cover?
**Back:** 450 B.C. – 300 A.D.

**Card 04**
**Front:** What is the Iranamadu formation?
**Back:** A gravel and sandy-clay layer, about 32 km wide inland, laid down in short warm periods of the Pleistocene; first identified near the Iranamadu tanks; contains Stone Age tools.

**Card 05**
**Front:** How old is the gravel layer at Pathirajawela, and how deep?
**Back:** About 125 000 years old, at 45 feet.

**Card 06**
**Front:** How old is the Bundala–Wellegangoda layer?
**Back:** About 80 000 years (24 feet deep).

**Card 07**
**Front:** What decides the six environmental zones of the Stone Age?
**Back:** Differences in annual rainfall.

**Card 08**
**Front:** Name the six environmental zones.
**Back:** Semi-arid, lowland dry, lowland intermediate dry, wet, mountainous dry intermediate and arid zones.

**Card 09**
**Front:** What did wet-zone people eat that dry-zone people did not?
**Back:** Land snails (and oysters); dry-zone people hunted deer and iguana.

**Card 10**
**Front:** Which cave is 38 000 years old?
**Back:** Pahiyangala (Bulathsinhala, Kaluthara District).

**Card 11**
**Front:** What was found at Kithulgala Belilena regarding food 12 500 years ago?
**Back:** Baked seeds of wild breadfruit.

**Card 12**
**Front:** Normal size of a Stone Age group?
**Back:** 15–25 persons; maximum about 50.

**Card 13**
**Front:** How far did Stone Age people walk daily for food?
**Back:** About 7 kilometres.

**Card 14**
**Front:** Where did Stone Age people live in dry and rainy seasons?
**Back:** Open camps in the dry season; natural caves in rainy seasons.

**Card 15**
**Front:** What are geometric micro stone tools?
**Back:** Small tools (not more than about 4.5 cm) of quartz or Kahanda stone, hafted to wood or bone for cutting, scratching and digging.

**Card 16**
**Front:** Average height of Stone Age adults?
**Back:** Male 174 cm, female 166 cm.

**Card 17**
**Front:** Estimated maximum lifespan of pre-historic people?
**Back:** About 35–40 years.

**Card 18**
**Front:** What ritual evidence comes from Rawana Ella cave?
**Back:** A perforated skull, smoothed and painted with red ochre.

**Card 19**
**Front:** Why do researchers link Stone Age people with Veddas?
**Back:** Cultural and biological similarities.

**Card 20**
**Front:** What defines the proto-historic era?
**Back:** The transition between pre-history and history: burnt pots, methodical burials, iron use, permanent settlements, agriculture.

**Card 21**
**Front:** Which climate change helped create the proto-historic era?
**Back:** A Holocene change (7000–4500 B.C.) – the Ice Age ended, temperatures rose, farming became possible.

**Card 22**
**Front:** What is the oldest proof of the proto-historic era?
**Back:** About 2400 B.C. (Uva and Sabaragamuwa regions).

**Card 23**
**Front:** What is a cist burial?
**Back:** A stone-slab chamber of megalithic tradition holding pots with human remains.

**Card 24**
**Front:** Name three excavated megalithic burial sites and their dates.
**Back:** Ibbankatuwa, Galsohonkanaththa and Yatigalpoththa; 750–450 B.C.

**Card 25**
**Front:** How were burials at Beragala and Haldummulla different?
**Back:** Pots of remains were placed in boat-shaped raw-clay tubs where skeletons had been cremated.

**Card 26**
**Front:** What is "sedentism"?
**Back:** Living permanently in one place instead of roaming.

**Card 27**
**Front:** Describe the Udaranchamadama house.
**Back:** Rectangular (10.20 m × 6.80 m), wattle-and-daub walls, Iluk thatch; c. 1129 B.C.; found in 2009.

**Card 28**
**Front:** What did the Udaranchamadama painted pot show?
**Back:** Use of a potter's wheel; white base with symmetrical red lines.

**Card 29**
**Front:** What evidence shows hunting continued at Udaranchamadama?
**Back:** Bones of deer, cattle, wild boar and giant squirrel.

**Card 30**
**Front:** Why were early historic settlements in the dry zone?
**Back:** Fertile red-brown soil and rainfall of 1000–1250 mm.

**Card 31**
**Front:** Why did settlements avoid river valleys at first?
**Back:** No technology to control annual floods.

**Card 32**
**Front:** Who built an anicut across the Kirindi river?
**Back:** King Ilanaga (33–43 A.D.).

**Card 33**
**Front:** What does "Avarana" mean?
**Back:** Anicut (Amuna).

**Card 34**
**Front:** What is a Vapigama?
**Back:** A settlement developed around a tank.

**Card 35**
**Front:** Match village types: Kewattagama, Kumbhakaragama, Gopalagama, Manikaragama.
**Back:** Fishermen; potters; cowherds; gem-miners.

**Card 36**
**Front:** What are Pattanagama and Niyamgam?
**Back:** Harbour village; trading village.

**Card 37**
**Front:** Meaning of Parikkita gam?
**Back:** A village protected by a fence.

**Card 38**
**Front:** Who was a Gahapathi, a Gamika and the Dasa Gam Eththan?
**Back:** Chief householder; village chief; a panel of ten chief householders for ten villages.

**Card 39**
**Front:** What were the standard sizes of ancient settlements?
**Back:** Small village ≤ 0.15 ha; medium ≈ 2 ha; large urban > 2 ha.

**Card 40**
**Front:** What chena crops were grown?
**Back:** Kurakkan, Meneri, sesame and Thanahal.

**Card 41**
**Front:** Population of Magama and nearby settlements by the 6th century A.D.?
**Back:** About 35 000.

**Card 42**
**Front:** What does Map 2.4 show?
**Back:** Settlements around Kirindi Oya: earliest (900–500 B.C.) far from the river; later ones close after flood control.


## Chapter 03 – Evolution of Political Power in Sri Lanka

**Card 01**
**Front:** What was the pre-state era?
**Back:** The time before a single ruler/state existed, when wealthy chieftains (Parumakas) dominated small areas.

**Card 02**
**Front:** What does "Parumaka" mean and from which word does it come?
**Back:** "Main/chief"; from Sanskrit *Pramukha*.

**Card 03**
**Front:** What does the Kothgalakanda (Kandalama) inscription show?
**Back:** A Parumaka called "Thota Bojaka" (collector of port taxes) offered a cave to monks, c. 250 B.C. – provincial power.

**Card 04**
**Front:** Who was a Gruhapathi / Gahapathi?
**Back:** The chief householder of a family.

**Card 05**
**Front:** Who was a Gamika?
**Back:** The chief of a village chosen from the householders; represented the village and managed the tank and water.

**Card 06**
**Front:** What did the Periyapuliyankulama inscription record?
**Back:** A householder in the metal trade cleaned a cave and offered it to monks.

**Card 07**
**Front:** Which three Gamikas offered a cave at Korawakgala, Sithulpawwa?
**Back:** Siva, Sumana and Thidana.

**Card 08**
**Front:** What is an Ellangawa (prolapsed tank system)?
**Back:** A group of small tanks connected by canals to save water and keep land damp.

**Card 09**
**Front:** Why were tanks linked together?
**Back:** To carry overflow from upper to lower tanks and to moisten land beside canals for cultivation.

**Card 10**
**Front:** How did tank systems create Parumakas?
**Back:** Connecting tanks needed joint decisions; the most powerful Gamika represented all villages of the system.

**Card 11**
**Front:** What is a female Parumaka called?
**Back:** Parumakalu.

**Card 12**
**Front:** What kind of ruling system existed first in Sri Lanka?
**Back:** Decentralized ruling.

**Card 13**
**Front:** Which burial ground yielded a necklace with imported beads?
**Back:** Ibbankatuwa (700–450 B.C.), showing the wealth of Parumakas.

**Card 14**
**Front:** Name two provincial "kings" before centralization.
**Back:** Pochani Raja (Embul Ambe, Matale) and Duhathara Raja (Yatahalena, Kegalle).

**Card 15**
**Front:** Who was the first king according to the Mahawamsa and what did he do?
**Back:** Pandukabhaya – defeated provincial rulers (uncles), made Anuradhapura his capital, marked village boundaries.

**Card 16**
**Front:** What outside factor encouraged centralization?
**Back:** Growth of Indian Ocean trade; Sri Lanka's central position and its gems, elephants and spices.

**Card 17**
**Front:** What are the Pancha Kakudha Bhanda?
**Back:** Five royal insignia: fan (Walvidunava), slippers, sword, parasol, forehead band.

**Card 18**
**Front:** What did Devanampiyatissa receive from Ashoka?
**Back:** The five royal insignia, for a second coronation, and the title Devanampiya.

**Card 19**
**Front:** Which generals of Dutugemunu were earlier Parumakas?
**Back:** Velusumana, Pussadeva, Theraputtabhaya and Nandimitra.

**Card 20**
**Front:** What do clay stamps from Tissamaharama show?
**Back:** Official administration and seals used by Ruhuna royals/officers.

**Card 21**
**Front:** Which king's reign saw a Sri Lankan ambassador to Rome?
**Back:** Bhatikabhaya – he returned with glass beads.

**Card 22**
**Front:** Who completed the centralization of power?
**Back:** Vasabha of the Lambakarna dynasty.

**Card 23**
**Front:** What does the Vallipuram golden plate record?
**Back:** Jaffna (Nagadeepa) ruled by minister Srishigiri appointed by Vasabha.

**Card 24**
**Front:** What is the "state"?
**Back:** The king, officials, laws and institutions ruling a defined territory with the people's consent.

**Card 25**
**Front:** Name five concepts of kingship.
**Back:** Devathwa, Bodhisattva, Parvatharaja, Veerathwa and Chakravarthi.

**Card 26**
**Front:** Which king called himself Nareshvara?
**Back:** Kutakanna Abhaya (Dambulla inscription).

**Card 27**
**Front:** Which king was worshipped as God of Minneriya?
**Back:** Mahasen.

**Card 28**
**Front:** Which king appeared as Kuwera and used the title Alakapaya Maha Raja?
**Back:** Kashyapa I (Sigiriya).

**Card 29**
**Front:** Which king used "Kalinga Chakravarthi Swaminwahanse"?
**Back:** Nissankamalla.

**Card 30**
**Front:** How did kingship usually pass?
**Back:** Brother to brother (eldest younger brother) or father to son; disputes if the rule was broken.

**Card 31**
**Front:** Why did Kashyapa seize the throne?
**Back:** He was son of a second queen; Mugalan, son of the chief consort, was the rightful heir.

**Card 32**
**Front:** What was the king called after Parumakas united?
**Back:** Mahaparumaka / Mapurumuka, then Maha Raja.

**Card 33**
**Front:** Name four ancient officials and their titles.
**Back:** Maha Emathi (chief minister), Senapathi (commander), Bhandagarika (treasurer), Nagaragutika (city administrator).

**Card 34**
**Front:** Who was the Rupa Adeka?
**Back:** Officer in charge of coin minting.

**Card 35**
**Front:** Why did Dutugemunu fight Elara?
**Back:** To save the Mathota harbour and unite the country; he said it was for Buddhism.

**Card 36**
**Front:** How did Dutugemunu show statesmanship?
**Back:** Ordered respect for Elara's tomb.

**Card 37**
**Front:** What welfare did Dutugemunu provide during Ruwanweli construction?
**Back:** Alms halls at the four gates and wages for all workers.

**Card 38**
**Front:** How many large tanks did Vasabha build and why?
**Back:** Sixteen; grain was needed in South India (famine in Krishna valley).

**Card 39**
**Front:** Who protected young Vijayabahu I in Ruhuna?
**Back:** Security officer Sithnarubima Budhal Na (Panakaduwa copper plate).

**Card 40**
**Front:** Why did Vijayabahu I move the capital to Polonnaruwa?
**Back:** Trade shifted to East India; Polonnaruwa could control Trincomalee (Gokanna) harbour.

**Card 41**
**Front:** What is territorial integrity?
**Back:** Continuity and freedom of a country's territory, protected by the ruler.

**Card 42**
**Front:** Who was Mahasammatha?
**Back:** The "general will" king – one chosen/accepted by the people.


## Chapter 04 – The Ancient Society of Sri Lanka

**Card 01**
**Front:** Name the three institutions of government.
**Back:** Legislature (makes laws), executive (implements them) and judiciary (gives justice).

**Card 02**
**Front:** What was "Ekthensamiya"?
**Back:** "Agreement of the king's court" – the combined executive and legislature of the 9th century A.D.

**Card 03**
**Front:** What does the Buddhannehela inscription record?
**Back:** A land grant made with the agreement of the king's court and by the king's decree.

**Card 04**
**Front:** What was the "Sabhawa"?
**Back:** The council that functioned as the judiciary and attended land grants.

**Card 05**
**Front:** Who was the "Mahale / Maha Lekhaka"?
**Back:** The general secretary who recorded and preserved government documents.

**Card 06**
**Front:** What does the Medirigiriya inscription of Sena II mention?
**Back:** General secretary Kashyapa giving land to the Padhanaghara Eth Wehera.

**Card 07**
**Front:** Who were the "Dasagam Ettan"?
**Back:** Councils (9th c.) that represented ten villages in minor administration.

**Card 08**
**Front:** Which inscription tells of Dasagam Ettan solving alms problems?
**Back:** Kaludiya Pokuna, Matale.

**Card 09**
**Front:** Who built maternity hospitals?
**Back:** King Upatissa I (365–406 A.D.).

**Card 10**
**Front:** Which king built a special hospital for an epidemic fever?
**Back:** Kashyapa IV (898–914 A.D.).

**Card 11**
**Front:** Which king ruled 340–368 A.D. and built many hospitals?
**Back:** Buddhadasa.

**Card 12**
**Front:** Why did Gajabahu I visit the Chera state?
**Back:** For the opening of a Pattini Devala – a diplomatic visit.

**Card 13**
**Front:** Which Sri Lankan king sent envoys to Rome, and why?
**Back:** Bhatikabhaya – to fetch glass pebbles for a pooja at Ruwanweliseya.

**Card 14**
**Front:** With which country did Aggabodhi VI have strong ties?
**Back:** China – twenty missions.

**Card 15**
**Front:** Whom did Vijayabahu I marry?
**Back:** Thilokasundari, a Kalinga princess; his sister Mitta married a Pandya prince.

**Card 16**
**Front:** What were the Veleikkara?
**Back:** A mercenary force from South India that guarded the Temple of the Tooth.

**Card 17**
**Front:** What is chena cultivation?
**Back:** Burning a cleared forest plot and growing grains and vegetables; the plot was abandoned after 1–2 harvests.

**Card 18**
**Front:** What is a "Nawa Deli Hena"?
**Back:** A new, fertile chena cleared by burning.

**Card 19**
**Front:** What are Dandu Weta and Pela?
**Back:** The fence of sticks around a chena; the watch hut at the top of a tall tree.

**Card 20**
**Front:** Which king abolished the chena taxes Kethi Ada and Ketu Kanaba Aya?
**Back:** Nissankamalla (1187–1196).

**Card 21**
**Front:** Define Kumbura and Ketha.
**Back:** Kumbura – small paddy plot; Ketha (from *Kshetra*) – large field.

**Card 22**
**Front:** Name the paddy seasons.
**Back:** Maha, Yala and Meda Kanna.

**Card 23**
**Front:** What did Parakramabahu I say about water?
**Back:** No drop of rain should flow to the sea without being used.

**Card 24**
**Front:** What was "Kirigeri"?
**Back:** Cattle reared for milk (Kashyapa IV inscription).

**Card 25**
**Front:** What were the ancient words for blacksmith, goldsmith, gem-worker, ivory carver, potter?
**Back:** Kabara, Thuladhara/Swarnakara, Manikara, Dantika, Kumbhakara.

**Card 26**
**Front:** Which inscription proves weaving existed?
**Back:** Veherakema (Hambantota) – "Thathvaya", spinner of thread.

**Card 27**
**Front:** Which were the major ancient cities?
**Back:** Anuradhapura and Magama (Pura).

**Card 28**
**Front:** What was the Kalasumana?
**Back:** The bazaar at the southern gate of Anuradhapura.

**Card 29**
**Front:** What does "Poogaya" mean?
**Back:** A council or guild of merchants; also called Niyamasthana.

**Card 30**
**Front:** Which king's inscription lays down market rules at Hopitigama?
**Back:** Udaya IV (946–954) – Soraborawewa inscription.

**Card 31**
**Front:** Name three South Indian trade guilds in Sri Lanka.
**Back:** Nanadesi, Valanjiyar and Ayinurruvar.

**Card 32**
**Front:** Which harbours handled western/eastern/northern/eastern small port trade?
**Back:** Mathota (west), Gokanna/Trincomalee (east), Jambukolapattana (north), Lankapattana (east).

**Card 33**
**Front:** Who were Acharya, Veja, Vohara?
**Back:** Teachers, doctors, legal experts.

**Card 34**
**Front:** What is a drip ledge?
**Back:** A groove cut above a cave entrance to keep rain out; inscriptions were carved beneath it.

**Card 35**
**Front:** How many caves were offered to monks?
**Back:** More than 1600.

**Card 36**
**Front:** What language/script were cave inscriptions in?
**Back:** Sinhala Prakrit in Brahmi script.

**Card 37**
**Front:** What was a Pannasala?
**Back:** A clay-and-wattle thatched monk's dwelling; origin of "Pansala".

**Card 38**
**Front:** Who were the Bhanakas?
**Back:** Monks who memorized parts of the Tripitaka before it was written in Valagamba's time.

**Card 39**
**Front:** What were Vas Visima and Katina Uthsava?
**Back:** The rainy-season retreat and robe-offering ceremony.

**Card 40**
**Front:** What was a Padhanaghara?
**Back:** A meditation building of two platforms joined by a bridge, surrounded by a moat.

**Card 41**
**Front:** Which Hindu temples stood near ancient harbours?
**Back:** Thirukketheeswaram (Mathota) and Koneswaram (Trincomalee).

**Card 42**

**Card 43**

**Card 44**

**Card 45**
**Front:** How many poems are on Sigiriya's mirror wall?

**Card 46**
**Front:** Give an example each of Pali, Sanskrit and Sinhala works of the Anuradhapura era.
**Back:** Dipavamsa; Janakiharanaya; Siyabaslakara.

**Card 47**
**Front:** What was the Demala Adhikari?
**Back:** A court officer responsible for Tamil welfare (9th c.).

**Card 48**
**Front:** Who first used South Indian mercenaries?
**Back:** Kings Ilanaga (33–43 A.D.) and Abhayanaga (231–240 A.D.).

**Card 49**
**Front:** Name four foreign trader groups in ancient cities.
**Back:** South Indians, Ionians (Greeks), Cambodians (Kamboja) and Jawakans.

**Card 50**
**Front:** Which sacred place is venerated by four religions?
**Back:** Sri Pada (Adam's Peak).


## Chapter 05 – The Ancient Science and Technology in Sri Lanka

**Card 01**
**Front:** Define science and technology.
**Back:** Science is understanding the fundamentals of nature; technology is applying them practically.

**Card 02**
**Front:** What technique made 28 000-year-old symmetrical alabaster tools?
**Back:** Pressure flaking – removing splinters with force.

**Card 03**
**Front:** Why were clay-tub burial walls built perpendicular?
**Back:** To reduce pressure on the walls and keep them stable, as at Udaranchamadama.

**Card 04**
**Front:** What did the clay tube in the 1350 B.C. tomb show?
**Back:** Knowledge that air (oxygen) is needed for burning – 3250 years ago.

**Card 05**
**Front:** What was found at Kithulgala Belilena regarding housing?
**Back:** Parts of a stone parapet that probably held a leaf cover.

**Card 06**
**Front:** Describe the Udaranchamadama house.
**Back:** 1129 B.C.; stone-and-clay floor, wattle-and-daub walls, partition wall, wooden door-frame holes.

**Card 07**
**Front:** What is a Sakaporuwa?
**Back:** A potter's wheel.

**Card 08**
**Front:** How was black-and-red ware made?
**Back:** The pot was filled with chaff and fired upside down – red outside, black inside; ended after 300 B.C.

**Card 09**
**Front:** How is the Udaranchamadama painted pot like porcelain?
**Back:** Clay was settled in water to get very fine particles.
