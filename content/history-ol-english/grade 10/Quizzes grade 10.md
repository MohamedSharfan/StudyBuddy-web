## Chapter 01 – Sources of Studying History

**Question 01:**
Which of the following is an archaeological source?
A. Deepawamsa
B. Mahawamsa
C. Panakaduwa Copper Plate
D. Rajawaliya
**Correct Answer:** C
**Explanation:** The copper plate is an inscription, an archaeological source; the others are written texts.
**Difficulty:** Easy

**Question 02:**
Who wrote the first part of Mahawamsa?
A. Robert Knox
B. Mahanama Thero
C. Fa-Hsien Thero
D. Hsuan Tsang
**Correct Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Mahanama Thero of the Diksanda Seneviya Pirivena wrote it.
**Difficulty:** Easy

**Question 03:**
Which is considered the oldest text of Sri Lanka?
A. Deepawamsa
B. Nikaya Sangraha
C. Mahawamsa
D. Thupawamsa
**Correct Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Deepawamsa dates to about the 4th century A.D.
**Difficulty:** Easy

**Question 04:**

A. Joao Ribeiro
B. Fa-Hsien

**Correct Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Queiroz wrote his book in India.
**Difficulty:** Medium
