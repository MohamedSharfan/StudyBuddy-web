2. A word for *very big*
3. Meaning of *protect*

## Special section — Vocabulary for weak students

Learn these everyday words first. Each one can make many sentences.

<!-- meta {"id":"ENG-VOC-0001","word":"go","pos":"verb","theme":"Core words","difficulty":"Beginner"} -->
**Go**

- I go to school by bus.
- My father goes to work at seven.
- We went to Kandy last week.
- Let's go to the park.

<!-- meta {"id":"ENG-VOC-0002","word":"come","pos":"verb","theme":"Core words","difficulty":"Beginner"} -->
**Come**

- Come here, please.
- My aunt comes to our house every Sunday.
- The bus came late.
- Can you come to my party?

<!-- meta {"id":"ENG-VOC-0003","word":"eat","pos":"verb","theme":"Core words","difficulty":"Beginner"} -->

- Don't eat too much sugar.

## Special section — Writing vocabulary

### Essays

- In today's world, …
- There are many advantages of …
- One of the main reasons is …
- As a result, …
- On the other hand, …
- To sum up, …

### Letters

- I am writing to …
- I would be grateful if you could …
- Thank you for your letter.
- I look forward to hearing from you.
- Yours faithfully / Yours sincerely / Yours lovingly

### Notices

- NOTICE
- will be held on … at …
- All … are invited to …
- Participants should bring …
- For further details, contact …

### Picture descriptions

- In this picture, I can see …
- There is / There are …
- On the left / right …
- In the background …
- A boy is …ing
- It seems to be a … day.

### Bar charts

- The bar chart shows …
- The horizontal axis shows … / the vertical axis shows …
- the highest / the lowest
- more … than
- an equal number of …

### Pie charts

- The pie chart shows …
- accounts for …%
- the largest proportion
- the smallest share
- a quarter / half of …

### Speeches

- Good morning, respected principal, teachers and my dear friends.
- Today I am going to speak about …
- Let me tell you …
- So, friends, let us …
- Thank you for listening.

### Articles

- (Title)
- by (name)
- Have you ever thought about …?
- Experts say …
- It is time for us to …

### Stories

- One day, …
- Suddenly, …
- After a few minutes, …
- To my surprise, …
- Finally, …
- From that day onwards, …

## Limitations

- Tamil meanings are short equivalents for weak students; some English words have several Tamil equivalents — teachers may prefer a different term.
- The NIE textbook word lists were not available for checking.
