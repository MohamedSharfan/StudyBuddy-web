### Changes across years

- Paper I = 40 marks and Paper II = 60 marks in all three sessions; Test 14 = 10 marks (C3 L3 O2 M2) and Test 16 = 15 marks (C5 L5 O2 M3) in all three.
- The task types of Tests 1, 2, 4, 9, 10, 12 and 13 change from year to year (e.g. Test 1: vocabulary in 2016/2017 but picture matching in 2020; Test 9: conjunctions in 2016, word formation in 2017, vocabulary in 2020). **Practise every skill, not one fixed pattern.**
- Test 16 options in 2016 and 2020 included a **dialogue**; 2017 included a **story**. Test 14(b) was a bar graph in 2016 and 2020 and a table in 2017.
- Word counts are stated only in the 2020 scheme (Test 6 ≈ 40–50, Test 8 ≈ 50–60, Test 14 ≈ 100, Test 16 ≈ 200).

## Official marking rules for writing (2020 scheme)

- If **content = 0**, no marks are given for language, format, organisation or mechanics.
- If **language = 0**, full content marks are not given.
- The 2016/2017 reports add: spelling mistakes lose marks even when copying from a text; copying chunks earns no marks when one specific answer is expected; short, grammatically correct answers can get full marks.
