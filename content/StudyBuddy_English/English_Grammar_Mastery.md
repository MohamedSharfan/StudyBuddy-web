## Sentence construction

Topic ID: `ENG-TOP-ZERO`

### Stage 1 — See the rule

A sentence = **Subject + Verb (+ Object) (+ Place) (+ Time)**. Start with a capital letter; end with a full stop.

### Stage 2 — See examples

- Kamal reads.
- Kamal reads the newspaper.
- Kamal reads the newspaper in the garden.
- Kamal reads the newspaper in the garden every morning.

## Tenses

Topic ID: `ENG-TOP-TENSE`

### Stage 1 — See the rule

The tense shows **when**. Look for time words: *every day* → present simple; *now* → present continuous; *yesterday* → past simple; *already/just/since/for* → present perfect; *tomorrow* → future (*will*).

### Stage 2 — See examples

- I walk to school every day. (habit)
- I am walking to school now. (happening now)
- I walked to school yesterday. (finished past)
- I was walking to school when it started to rain. (in progress in the past)
- I have already walked 2 km. (past connected to now)
- I will walk to school tomorrow. (future)

A **singular** subject takes a singular verb (*he plays, she is, it has*); a **plural** subject takes a plural verb (*they play, we are, the boys have*). Find the real subject: *The box of pencils **is** ...*

## Pronouns

Topic ID: `ENG-TOP-PRON`

## Conjunctions and linking words

Topic ID: `ENG-TOP-CONJ`

### Stage 1 — See the rule

**and** (add), **but** (contrast), **so** (result), **because** (reason), **or** (choice), **although** (contrast + surprise), **when/while/before/after/until** (time), **if/unless** (condition). In writing: *However, Therefore, Firstly, Finally.*

### Stage 2 — See examples

- I was tired, but I finished my work.
- It rained, so the match was cancelled.
- She stayed home because she was ill.
- Although he is rich, he lives simply.
- Wait here until I come back.
- You will fail unless you study.

## Adjectives

Topic ID: `ENG-TOP-ADJ`

### Stage 1 — See the rule

Adjectives describe nouns. They come **before** the noun (*a tall tree*) or after *be/look/seem/feel* (*The tree is tall*). They never take -s (*two red cars*). *-ed* = how people feel (*bored*); *-ing* = what causes it (*boring*).

### Stage 2 — See examples

- She has a lovely voice.
- The soup tastes delicious.
- I was bored because the film was boring.
- They live in a big old house.
- Two new students joined our class.

## Adverbs

Topic ID: `ENG-TOP-ADV`

### Stage 1 — See the rule

Adverbs describe **how, when, where, how often**: *quickly, yesterday, here, always*. Many adverbs = adjective + **-ly** (*slow → slowly*). Irregular: *good → well, fast → fast, hard → hard*. Frequency adverbs go before the main verb (*She always walks*) but after *be* (*He is always late*).

### Stage 2 — See examples

- She sings beautifully.
- He drives carefully.
- My father always reads the newspaper.
- The students are usually quiet.
- You did well.
- He works hard.

## Modal verbs

Topic ID: `ENG-TOP-MODAL`

## Active and passive voice

Topic ID: `ENG-TOP-PASS`

### Stage 1 — See the rule

Passive = **be + past participle**. Use it when the action is more important than the doer: *Rice **is grown** in Ampara.* Active → passive: object → subject; keep the tense in *be*; add *by* + doer if needed.

- Tea is grown in the hill country.
- The window was broken yesterday.
- The new library will be opened next week.
- English is spoken all over the world.

## Direct and indirect speech

Topic ID: `ENG-TOP-REP`

### Stage 1 — See the rule

In reported speech after *said/told*, move the tense back: *am/is → was*, *are → were*, *will → would*, *can → could*, past simple → past perfect. Change pronouns and time words (*tomorrow → the next day*, *yesterday → the day before*). Questions: *asked if/whether ...* or *asked + wh-word + statement order*.

### Stage 2 — See examples

- He said, "I am hungry." → He said that he was hungry.
- She said, "I will come." → She said that she would come.
- Ravi said, "I can swim." → Ravi said that he could swim.
- "Are you ready?" she asked. → She asked if I was ready.
- "Where do you live?" he asked. → He asked where I lived.

## Question tags

Topic ID: `ENG-TOP-QTAG`

### Stage 1 — See the rule

Positive sentence → negative tag; negative sentence → positive tag. Use the **same auxiliary** and a **pronoun**: *She is ready, isn't she? They didn't come, did they?* Special: *I am → aren't I?*; *Let's → shall we?*

### Stage 2 — See examples

## Conditional sentences

Topic ID: `ENG-TOP-COND`

### Stage 1 — See the rule

**First conditional (real future):** *If + present, will + base* — *If it rains, we will stay home.* **Second (imaginary):** *If + past, would + base* — *If I had wings, I would fly.* **Zero (always true):** *If + present, present* — *If you heat ice, it melts.*

### Stage 2 — See examples

- If you study, you will pass.
- If it rains, the match will be cancelled.
- If I were rich, I would build a hospital.
- If you heat water to 100 °C, it boils.
- Unless you hurry, you will miss the bus.

## Comparisons

Topic ID: `ENG-TOP-COMP`

### Stage 1 — See the rule

Short adjectives: *-er / the -est* (*tall, taller, the tallest*). Long adjectives: *more / the most* (*more beautiful, the most beautiful*). Irregular: *good–better–best, bad–worse–worst, far–further–furthest*. Equal: *as ... as*. Never use *more* + *-er* together.

### Stage 2 — See examples

- An elephant is bigger than a horse.
- This book is more interesting than that one.
- Pidurutalagala is the highest mountain in Sri Lanka.
- My handwriting is worse than yours.
- Kamal is as tall as Ravi.
- Today is the best day of my life.

## Common mistakes of Sri Lankan O/L students

<!-- meta {"id":"ENG-GRM-CM-0001","topic_id":"ENG-TOP-ERR","type":"common_mistake","difficulty":"Beginner"} -->
**ENG-GRM-CM-0001** ❌ *He go to school.* → ✅ **He goes to school.**

- **Reason:** He/she/it + verb-s in the present simple.
- **Another correct example:** My aunt teaches Tamil.
- **Practice:** My cousin ___ (live) in Kurunegala.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

lives

</details>

<!-- meta {"id":"ENG-GRM-CM-0002","topic_id":"ENG-TOP-ERR","type":"common_mistake","difficulty":"Beginner"} -->
**ENG-GRM-CM-0002** ❌ *She have a bicycle.* → ✅ **She has a bicycle.**

- **Reason:** He/she/it → *has*.
- **Another correct example:** Our school has a big library.
- **Practice:** My father ___ a lorry. (have)

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

has

</details>

<!-- meta {"id":"ENG-GRM-CM-0003","topic_id":"ENG-TOP-ERR","type":"common_mistake","difficulty":"Beginner"} -->
**ENG-GRM-CM-0003** ❌ *I am agree.* → ✅ **I agree.**

- **Reason:** *agree* is a verb, not an adjective.
- **Another correct example:** We all agree with the principal.
- **Practice:** Correct: Are you agree?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Do you agree?

</details>

<!-- meta {"id":"ENG-GRM-CM-0004","topic_id":"ENG-TOP-ERR","type":"common_mistake","difficulty":"Beginner"} -->
**ENG-GRM-CM-0004** ❌ *Yesterday I go to town.* → ✅ **Yesterday I went to town.**

- **Reason:** *Yesterday* → past simple.
- **Another correct example:** Last week we visited Jaffna.
- **Practice:** Last night I ___ (watch) a film.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

watched

</details>

<!-- meta {"id":"ENG-GRM-CM-0005","topic_id":"ENG-TOP-ERR","type":"common_mistake","difficulty":"Beginner"} -->
**ENG-GRM-CM-0005** ❌ *There is five students.* → ✅ **There are five students.**

- **Reason:** Plural noun after *there* → *are*.
- **Another correct example:** There are many shops in the town.
- **Practice:** There ___ two dogs in the garden. (is/are)

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

are

</details>

<!-- meta {"id":"ENG-GRM-CM-0006","topic_id":"ENG-TOP-ERR","type":"common_mistake","difficulty":"Beginner"} -->
**ENG-GRM-CM-0006** ❌ *I like play cricket.* → ✅ **I like playing cricket.**

- **Reason:** *like* + -ing (or *to* + verb).
- **Another correct example:** She likes reading novels.
- **Practice:** He enjoys ___ (swim).

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

swimming

</details>

<!-- meta {"id":"ENG-GRM-CM-0007","topic_id":"ENG-TOP-ERR","type":"common_mistake","difficulty":"Beginner"} -->
**ENG-GRM-CM-0007** ❌ *She is more taller than me.* → ✅ **She is taller than me.**

- **Reason:** Do not use *more* with *-er*.
- **Another correct example:** This road is longer than that one.
- **Practice:** A car is ___ (fast) than a bicycle.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

faster

</details>

<!-- meta {"id":"ENG-GRM-CM-0008","topic_id":"ENG-TOP-ERR","type":"common_mistake","difficulty":"Beginner"} -->
**ENG-GRM-CM-0008** ❌ *I didn't went.* → ✅ **I didn't go.**

- **Reason:** *did/didn't* + base verb.
- **Another correct example:** Did you see the match?
- **Practice:** She didn't ___ (come) yesterday.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

come

</details>

<!-- meta {"id":"ENG-GRM-CM-0009","topic_id":"ENG-TOP-ERR","type":"common_mistake","difficulty":"Beginner"} -->
**ENG-GRM-CM-0009** ❌ *Can you to help me?* → ✅ **Can you help me?**

- **Reason:** No *to* after modals.
- **Another correct example:** You must wear a helmet.
- **Practice:** You should ___ (eat) more vegetables.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

eat

</details>

<!-- meta {"id":"ENG-GRM-CM-0010","topic_id":"ENG-TOP-ERR","type":"common_mistake","difficulty":"Beginner"} -->
**ENG-GRM-CM-0010** ❌ *I have many homeworks.* → ✅ **I have a lot of homework.**

- **Reason:** *homework* is uncountable.
- **Another correct example:** She gave me some good advice.
- **Practice:** Correct: I need some informations.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

I need some information.

</details>

<!-- meta {"id":"ENG-GRM-CM-0011","topic_id":"ENG-TOP-ERR","type":"common_mistake","difficulty":"Beginner"} -->
**ENG-GRM-CM-0011** ❌ *He is a honest man.* → ✅ **He is an honest man.**

- **Reason:** Vowel sound → *an*.
- **Another correct example:** It took an hour.
- **Practice:** ___ umbrella (a/an)

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

an

</details>

<!-- meta {"id":"ENG-GRM-CM-0012","topic_id":"ENG-TOP-ERR","type":"common_mistake","difficulty":"Beginner"} -->
**ENG-GRM-CM-0012** ❌ *I am going to the home.* → ✅ **I am going home.**

- **Reason:** *go home* — no *to the*.
- **Another correct example:** I came home late.
- **Practice:** Correct: She went to the home at five.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

She went home at five.

</details>

<!-- meta {"id":"ENG-GRM-CM-0013","topic_id":"ENG-TOP-ERR","type":"common_mistake","difficulty":"Beginner"} -->
**ENG-GRM-CM-0013** ❌ *My mother is cooking every day.* → ✅ **My mother cooks every day.**

- **Reason:** Habit → present simple, not continuous.
- **Another correct example:** My father reads the newspaper every morning.
- **Practice:** He ___ (walk) to school every day.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

walks

</details>

<!-- meta {"id":"ENG-GRM-CM-0014","topic_id":"ENG-TOP-ERR","type":"common_mistake","difficulty":"Beginner"} -->
**ENG-GRM-CM-0014** ❌ *I am knowing him.* → ✅ **I know him.**

- **Reason:** *know, like, want, need* are not usually used in the continuous.
- **Another correct example:** I want a glass of water.
- **Practice:** Correct: She is having two brothers.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

She has two brothers.

</details>

<!-- meta {"id":"ENG-GRM-CM-0015","topic_id":"ENG-TOP-ERR","type":"common_mistake","difficulty":"Beginner"} -->
**ENG-GRM-CM-0015** ❌ *Discuss about the problem.* → ✅ **Discuss the problem.**

- **Reason:** *discuss* takes a direct object.
- **Another correct example:** We discussed our plans.
- **Practice:** Correct: Let's discuss about the trip.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Let's discuss the trip.

</details>

<!-- meta {"id":"ENG-GRM-CM-0016","topic_id":"ENG-TOP-ERR","type":"common_mistake","difficulty":"Beginner"} -->
**ENG-GRM-CM-0016** ❌ *I was born in 15th May.* → ✅ **I was born on 15 May.**

- **Reason:** *on* + date.
- **Another correct example:** The exam is on Monday.
- **Practice:** We met ___ 3 June. (in/on/at)

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

on

</details>

<!-- meta {"id":"ENG-GRM-CM-0017","topic_id":"ENG-TOP-ERR","type":"common_mistake","difficulty":"Beginner"} -->
**ENG-GRM-CM-0017** ❌ *He married with my cousin.* → ✅ **He married my cousin. / He got married to my cousin.**

- **Reason:** *marry* someone; *get married to* someone.
- **Another correct example:** She married a teacher.
- **Practice:** Correct: My sister is married with a soldier.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

My sister is married to a soldier.

</details>

<!-- meta {"id":"ENG-GRM-CM-0018","topic_id":"ENG-TOP-ERR","type":"common_mistake","difficulty":"Beginner"} -->
**ENG-GRM-CM-0018** ❌ *Me and Ravi went to the shop.* → ✅ **Ravi and I went to the shop.**

- **Reason:** Subject → *I*; put yourself last.
- **Another correct example:** My friend and I study together.
- **Practice:** ___ and my brother play chess. (Me/I)

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

I

</details>

<!-- meta {"id":"ENG-GRM-CM-0019","topic_id":"ENG-TOP-ERR","type":"common_mistake","difficulty":"Beginner"} -->
**ENG-GRM-CM-0019** ❌ *I have seen him yesterday.* → ✅ **I saw him yesterday.**

- **Reason:** Finished past time (*yesterday*) → past simple.
- **Another correct example:** I saw a snake last night.
- **Practice:** I ___ (meet) her last week.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

met

</details>

<!-- meta {"id":"ENG-GRM-CM-0020","topic_id":"ENG-TOP-ERR","type":"common_mistake","difficulty":"Beginner"} -->
**ENG-GRM-CM-0020** ❌ *Since two years I study English.* → ✅ **I have studied English for two years.**

- **Reason:** *for* + period; present perfect for past-to-now.
- **Another correct example:** We have lived here since 2015.
- **Practice:** I have known him ___ ten years. (for/since)

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

for

</details>

<!-- meta {"id":"ENG-GRM-CM-0021","topic_id":"ENG-TOP-ERR","type":"common_mistake","difficulty":"Beginner"} -->
**ENG-GRM-CM-0021** ❌ *He told that he was tired.* → ✅ **He said that he was tired. / He told me that he was tired.**

- **Reason:** *tell* needs a person.
- **Another correct example:** She told us a story.
- **Practice:** He ___ me that he was busy. (said/told)

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

told

</details>

<!-- meta {"id":"ENG-GRM-CM-0022","topic_id":"ENG-TOP-ERR","type":"common_mistake","difficulty":"Beginner"} -->

student
