*Example:* Kavya said, "I am tired." → Kavya said that she was tired.

</details>

## Sentence correction

<!-- meta {"id":"ENG-FC-COR-0001","topic_id":"ENG-TOP-ZERO","category":"Sentence correction","difficulty":"Intermediate"} -->
**ENG-FC-COR-0001** · Intermediate

**Front:** Correct this: *Is raining.*

<details><summary>Back</summary>

**It is raining.**  
English needs a subject. Use *it* for weather.

</details>

<!-- meta {"id":"ENG-FC-COR-0002","topic_id":"ENG-TOP-POS","category":"Sentence correction","difficulty":"Intermediate"} -->
**ENG-FC-COR-0002** · Intermediate

**Front:** Correct this: *She sings beautiful.*

<details><summary>Back</summary>

**She sings beautifully.**  
To describe a verb (*sings*), use an adverb.

</details>

## Basic sentence construction

<!-- meta {"id":"ENG-FC-SB-0001","topic_id":"ENG-TOP-ZERO","category":"Basic sentence construction","difficulty":"Beginner"} -->
**ENG-FC-SB-0001** · Beginner

**Front:** Level 1: what is the pattern for *subject and verb*?

<details><summary>Back</summary>

**Subject + Verb**  
The subject is who or what does the action.  
*Example:* I study.  
*தமிழ்:* எழுவாய் (யார்?) + வினை (என்ன செய்கிறார்?)

</details>

<!-- meta {"id":"ENG-FC-SB-0002","topic_id":"ENG-TOP-ZERO","category":"Basic sentence construction","difficulty":"Beginner"} -->
**ENG-FC-SB-0002** · Beginner

**Front:** Level 2: what is the pattern for *subject, verb and object*?

<details><summary>Back</summary>

**Subject + Verb + Object**  
The object answers *what?* or *whom?* after the verb: *He plays* what? → *cricket*.  
*Example:* I study English.  
*தமிழ்:* செயப்படுபொருள் — 'எதை?' / 'யாரை?'

</details>

<!-- meta {"id":"ENG-FC-SB-0003","topic_id":"ENG-TOP-ZERO","category":"Basic sentence construction","difficulty":"Beginner"} -->
**ENG-FC-SB-0003** · Beginner

**Front:** Level 3: what is the pattern for *adding time and place*?

<details><summary>Back</summary>

**Subject + Verb + Object + Place + Time**  
Put the place (*at home, in the library*) before the time (*every day, after school*).  
*Example:* I study English every morning.  
*தமிழ்:* இடம் (எங்கே?) பின்னர் நேரம் (எப்போது?)

</details>

<!-- meta {"id":"ENG-FC-SB-0004","topic_id":"ENG-TOP-ZERO","category":"Basic sentence construction","difficulty":"Beginner"} -->
**ENG-FC-SB-0004** · Beginner

**Front:** Level 4: what is the pattern for *adding descriptions*?

<details><summary>Back</summary>

**... a/an + adjective + noun ...**  
Adjectives describe nouns.  
*Example:* I have a bicycle.  
*தமிழ்:* பெயரடை — பெயர்ச்சொல்லுக்கு முன்

</details>

<!-- meta {"id":"ENG-FC-SB-0005","topic_id":"ENG-TOP-ZERO","category":"Basic sentence construction","difficulty":"Beginner"} -->
**ENG-FC-SB-0005** · Beginner

**Front:** Level 5: what is the pattern for *adding reasons*?

<details><summary>Back</summary>

**... because + subject + verb ...**  
Use because to give a reason.  
*Example:* I like cricket.  
*தமிழ்:* because = ஏனெனில்

</details>

<!-- meta {"id":"ENG-FC-SB-0006","topic_id":"ENG-TOP-ZERO","category":"Basic sentence construction","difficulty":"Beginner"} -->
**ENG-FC-SB-0006** · Beginner

**Front:** Level 6: what is the pattern for *connecting sentences*?

<details><summary>Back</summary>

**Sentence 1 + linking word + Sentence 2**  
and adds, but shows a difference, so shows a result, because gives a reason, although shows a surprise.  
*Example:* I like cricket, but my brother likes football.  
*தமிழ்:* and = மற்றும், but = ஆனால், so = எனவே, although = இருந்தபோதிலும்

</details>

<!-- meta {"id":"ENG-FC-SB-0007","topic_id":"ENG-TOP-ZERO","category":"Basic sentence construction","difficulty":"Beginner"} -->
**ENG-FC-SB-0007** · Beginner

**Front:** Level 7: what is the pattern for *constructing paragraphs*?

<details><summary>Back</summary>

**Topic sentence + 3–4 supporting sentences + concluding sentence**  
A paragraph is a group of sentences about one idea.  
*Example:* My favourite sport is cricket. I play cricket with my friends after school. Cricket helps me stay healthy. It also teaches me teamwork. I enjoy playing cricket very much.  
*தமிழ்:* பந்தி — ஒரு கருத்தைப் பற்றிய வாக்கியங்களின் தொகுப்பு

</details>
