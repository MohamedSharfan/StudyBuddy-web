# Grade 10 – Chapter 23: Work – I

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_23/Notes.md` (Tamil). English rendering.

## 🎯 What you will learn
Two basic occupations for earning a living – from the Islamic point of view:
1. **Agriculture** (growing crops, raising livestock)
2. **Industry (manufacturing)**

---

## 1. Introduction
- Allah has placed **countless ways** on this earth for people to work and live, and has given them **the knowledge** to use them.
- Using this to try to meet one's needs is **a person's responsibility.**

---

## 2. Agriculture 🌾
- The **oldest and most basic** way of earning a living.

### The two kinds of agriculture ⭐
| Kind | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **1. Growing crops** | To obtain **plant food** |
| **2. Raising livestock** | To obtain **meat** |

👉 The Quran says both are **great blessings of Allah**.

### Evidence from the Quran
| Verse | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **6:141** | It is Allah who created gardens with trellised and untrellised plants, date palms, crops of many kinds of food, **olives and pomegranates** |
| **6:142** | He created among the livestock **some for carrying loads and some small**; eat of what is permitted |
| **55:10–12** | He laid out the earth for His creatures; in it are fruits, date palms, grain in husks and fragrant plants |
| **87:4** | He brought out **pasture** for the livestock |

### Agriculture in history
- The first human, **Adam (AS)**, began both kinds of farming (on the basis of hadith reports).

| Son of Adam (AS) | Occupation |
|---|---|
| **Habil** | **Growing crops** |
| **Qabil** | **Raising livestock** |

> ⚠️ Given exactly as in the textbook (other sources sometimes give these the other way round – confirm with the teacher).

- On this basis, early societies were seen as two groups: **farmers and herders**.

### Hadiths about agriculture ⭐
| Meaning of the hadith | Source |
|---|---|
| Whoever uses and develops **land that no one owns** **has the best right to it** | Bukhari |
| Whoever has land should **cultivate it**, or **give it to his brother** as charity | — |
| For one who plants a tree and **patiently looks after it until it bears fruit**, whoever benefits from that tree – it is accepted by Allah as **charity** for him | Ahmad |
| **Every prophet Allah sent herded sheep**; "You too?" – "Yes, **I herded sheep on the pastures of the people of Makkah**" | Bukhari |

### The practice of the Prophet ﷺ
- He **distributed** the farmland gained **at the victory of Khaybar** to those who would farm it.
- Hadiths have been compiled under headings such as **cultivating barren land, farming, partnership, and leasing land**; **books of fiqh** also contain the laws of farming.
- The Prophet ﷺ **allowed** the people of Madinah to **pollinate date palms** to increase their yield → this is regarded as **permission for farming techniques**.

### Today
- Farming and herding have been **modernised together with science** – more produce on less land in a shorter time.
- **Studying these well and taking the advice of experts** to build up the economy is **praiseworthy** in Islam.

## 3. Industry 🔨
> 📖 **Industry (handicraft / manufacturing):** a person using his **knowledge and skill** to make a needed **finished product** from a **raw material**.

### The oldest industries
| Industry | Introduced by | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Weaving** | **Idris (AS)** | **Imam Ibn Khaldun** – "al-Muqaddimah" (a widely held belief) |
| **Carpentry** | **Nuh (AS)** (may have introduced it) | His ark was made of **planks and nails** – **Surah al-Qamar 54:13** |
| Worked as a carpenter | **Zakariyya (AS)** | **Sahih Muslim** |

### The iron industry ⭐
- **Very important** in industry; it is the **raw material** for very many products.

| Verse | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **57:25** | We sent down iron, in which there is **great strength** and **many benefits** for people |
| **34:10–11** | We **made iron soft** for **Dawud (AS)**, and commanded him to make full **coats of armour** and to join the rings into **chain mail** |
| **18:94–96** | King **Dhul-Qarnayn** had the technology to build **great walls and barriers** using iron (**Surah al-Kahf**) |

### The Industrial Revolution
- After the Industrial Revolution of **the 18th century CE**, many new inventions followed.
- These are the result of human beings, as Allah's **representatives (khulafa')**, using their knowledge → **Allah**, who gave the resources and the knowledge, **is the One most deserving of thanks.**

### Two kinds of industry ⭐
| Kind | Needs | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Large-scale industry** | **Large capital and a large workforce** | (factories) |
| **Small-scale (cottage) industry** | **Ordinary investment** | Tailoring, making food products, repairing machines, bookbinding, printing, handicrafts |

### Hadiths about industry ⭐
| Meaning of the hadith | Source |
|---|---|
| "What is the best work?" – "**Work with one's own hands, and trade without lies**" | Ahmad |
| "No one eats better food than what he earns **with his own two hands**. **Dawud (AS)** used to eat from the work of his own hands" | Bukhari |

### Women and industry
- Women among the Companions also earned their living through crafts.
- Example: **Zaynab bint Jahsh (RA)**, one of the Mothers of the Believers, made her living by **weaving nets (crafting)**.

### Key guidance
- Every occupation has halal and haram aspects → **the Islamic Shari'ah must be followed.**
- The driving force of industrial growth is **new inventions** → we must develop **creativity** from now on.
- Whoever works within Islamic principles will see the fruit of his effort **in the Hereafter too**.
