# Grade 10 – Chapter 23: Work – I – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_23/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q17 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. What are the two kinds of agriculture?

A. Fishing and hunting
B. Growing crops and raising livestock
C. Weaving and carpentry
D. Trade and industry

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Growing crops to obtain plant food and raising livestock to obtain meat are the two kinds of agriculture.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. According to the textbook, which son of Adam (AS) grew crops?

A. Qabil
B. Idris
C. Shith
D. Habil

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: According to the textbook, Habil grew crops and Qabil raised livestock.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q3. Which verse says Allah created olives, pomegranates, date palms and the like?

A. 6:141
B. 6:142
C. 87:4
D. 57:25

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: 6:141 is the verse connected with growing crops. 6:142 is about livestock.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q4. What is the meaning of the hadith in Bukhari about land that no one owns?

A. Whoever uses and develops it has the best right to it
B. It belongs to the government
C. Crops must not be grown on it
D. It must be sold

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: This encourages developing barren land and farming it.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q5. According to the hadith in Ahmad, what does a person get who plants a tree and patiently looks after it until it bears fruit?

A. The reward of hajj
B. Profit only in this world
C. Whoever benefits from the tree, it is accepted by Allah as charity for him
D. Nothing

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Planting a tree becomes continuing charity – whether people or animals benefit, he gets the reward.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q6. When the Prophet ﷺ said "Every prophet Allah sent herded sheep", what did he answer to the Companions' question?

A. I did not herd
B. Yes, I too herded sheep on the pastures of the people of Makkah
C. I herded in Madinah
D. I herded in Sham

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: This hadith in Bukhari shows the virtue of herding.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q7. The Prophet's ﷺ permission to the people of Madinah to pollinate date palms is regarded as permission for what?

A. Trade
B. Industry
C. Farming techniques
D. Medicine

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: It shows that Islam allows the use of new techniques to increase production.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q8. What did the Prophet ﷺ do with the farmland gained at the victory of Khaybar?

A. He sold it
B. He distributed it to those who would farm it
C. He abandoned it
D. He built a mosque on it

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: This shows the value and necessity of farming.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q9. Which is the correct explanation of "industry"?

A. Growing crops on land
B. Buying and selling goods
C. Using knowledge and skill to make a needed finished product from a raw material
D. Raising livestock

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Industry is turning raw material → finished product. Buying and selling is trade.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q10. According to Imam Ibn Khaldun's "al-Muqaddimah", who is believed to have introduced weaving?

A. Nuh (AS)
B. Zakariyya (AS)
C. Dawud (AS)
D. Idris (AS)

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: It is widely believed that Idris (AS) introduced weaving. Nuh (AS) may have introduced carpentry.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q11. Which verse says that the ark of Nuh (AS) was made of planks and nails?

A. 34:10
B. Surah Hud 11:37
C. 57:25
D. Surah al-Qamar 54:13

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: According to the textbook it is verse 13 of Surah al-Qamar (chapter 54). This is the evidence that Nuh (AS) may have introduced carpentry.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q12. Which prophet is recorded in Sahih Muslim as having worked as a carpenter?

A. 'Isa (AS)
B. Zakariyya (AS)
C. Musa (AS)
D. Yusuf (AS)

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: A hadith in Sahih Muslim mentions that the Prophet Zakariyya (AS) worked as a carpenter.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q13. For whom did Allah make iron soft and command him to make armour and chain mail?

A. Sulayman (AS)
B. Dhul-Qarnayn
C. Dawud (AS)
D. Nuh (AS)

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Verses 34:10–11 say iron was made soft for Dawud (AS). Dhul-Qarnayn built great walls and barriers with iron.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q14. Which surah tells of the king who had the technology to build great walls and barriers using iron?

A. Surah al-Kahf
B. Surah Saba'
C. Surah al-Hadid
D. Surah al-Qamar

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Surah al-Kahf (18:94–96) tells about King Dhul-Qarnayn.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q15. When asked "What is the best work?", what did the Prophet ﷺ answer (Ahmad)?

A. Work with one's own hands and trade without lies
B. Farming and herding
C. Government service
D. Fighting in war

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: The Prophet ﷺ said that working with one's own hands and honest trade are the best kinds of work.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q16. Which kind of industry can a person take up with only ordinary investment?

A. Small-scale (cottage) industry
B. Large-scale industry
C. Iron production
D. Shipbuilding

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Large-scale industry needs large capital and a large workforce. Small-scale industries such as tailoring, making food products and printing need only ordinary investment.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q17. Which of the Mothers of the Believers made her living by weaving nets?

A. 'A'ishah (RA)
B. Hafsah (RA)
C. Khadijah (RA)
D. Zaynab bint Jahsh (RA)

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: In the Prophet's ﷺ time women, like men, worked at crafts; Zaynab bint Jahsh (RA) is an example.

Difficulty: Medium
