## 🎯 What you will learn
- The geographical position of Arabia

---

## 1. The position of Arabia 🗺️
- Islam was introduced in **the land of the Arabs**, which lies in **West Asia**.

| Direction | Border |
|---|---|
| **North** | The Syrian Desert |
| **South** | The Arabian Sea |
| **West** | The Red Sea |
| **East** | The Persian Gulf |

---

## 2. The Jahiliyyah period
- The period before Islam was introduced is called **Jahiliyyah (الجاهلية)**.
- **Meaning 1:** a time when **knowledge and good character did not prevail**.
- **Meaning 2:** a time when a way of life **contrary to Allah's guidance** prevailed.
- This state existed not only in Arabia but **in other countries too**.

---

## 3. Society 👥

### The tribal system
```
Families → Shu'ub (clans) → Qaba'il (tribes) → Shaykh (leader)
```
- Families joined together to form **shu'ub (clans)**.
- Clans joined together to form **qaba'il (tribes; singular qabilah)**.
- Tribes were run under a leader called the **shaykh**.
- Apart from this, **there was no system of government.**

### The reason
- The **physical conditions** of Arabia (scarce resources, great need).
- Those who herded animals had to compete **for grass and water** → they formed the tribal system to make themselves strong.
- The **Quraysh**, who looked after the Ka'bah, were regarded as the highest tribe; they were engaged in **trade**.

### 'Asabiyyah (عصبية) – tribal loyalty
- Over time the tribal system turned into **fanaticism**.
- Everyone devoted himself to the interests of the tribe.
- Being loyal to the tribe **in every situation, without conditions** was the rule; those who broke it were **cast out.**

### Long wars
- Long struggles over even very small wrongs.
  - A long war broke out because a tribe broke the leg of another tribe's **camel** that had grazed in its garden.
  - A long war between the tribes of **Aws and Khazraj**.
- War needed **men**, and it needed **poets** to stir up and encourage the fighting.
- 👉 So the tribe held a **celebration** whenever **a baby boy was born or a poet appeared**.
- It was the custom for boys to be **trained for war from childhood**.

---

## 4. Religion 🕋
- There was **no religion** that could guide the people; only some **rituals** existed in the name of religion.
- **The purity of the Ka'bah**, built by **Ibrahim (AS)** for the worship of the One God, **had been defiled:**
  - **Idols** were worshipped there.
  - Men and women performed **tawaf naked**.
- They lived as **mushrikun** (those who associate partners with Allah).

### A false belief in God ⭐
- They believed Allah was **the Lord (Rabb)**, but not that He was **the God (Ilah – the One worthy of worship)**.
- **(10:31) – meaning:** If asked who provides for them, who owns hearing and sight, who brings out the living from the dead, and who directs all affairs, they would say "**Allah**".
- The Quraysh's confidence when **Abrahah** came to destroy the Ka'bah – "**The Lord of the Ka'bah will look after it**" – also shows this.
- They regarded their gods as **intermediaries** for reaching Allah:
  > 📖 **(39:3) – meaning:** "We worship them only so that they may **bring us closer** to Allah."

### The Ka'bah – a festival scene
- During the **sacred months**, pilgrims came to the Ka'bah → **markets** were held.
- **Wrestling contests and poetry contests** took place.
- Winning poems were **hung on the Ka'bah** and the poets were honoured → **Arabic literature** grew rich.
- The most important collection later written down: **"as-Sab' al-Mu'allaqat" (السبع المعلقات)** – the seven hanging poems.

---

## 5. Family 👨‍👩‍👧
- There was **no balance** between men and women → no orderly family system; marriage customs were confused.
- In the **upper-class tribes** there was **"nikah sahih"** (proper marriage); there women were treated **with honour**.
- In other tribes there were many kinds of unions:
  - Just as men had many wives, women also had many men.
  - **A son married his father's wife after his father died.**
  - **Mut'ah (متعة)** – **temporary marriage.**
  - **Mothers** decided who the fathers of their children were.
- Because they lived as **nomads**, there was no settled, civilised way of life.

### Inequalities
- **Prisoners of war and people bought with money** were treated as slaves; they had to work but had **no rights at all.**
- Women were mostly **without rights**: gathering firewood, cooking, carrying water, milking, spinning thread, caring for children.
- In some tribes there was the custom of **burying baby girls alive**.

---

## 6. Conduct ⚖️

### Bad conduct
- **Murder, wine, interest (riba), adultery, revenge, highway robbery.**
- These were regarded not as crimes but as **matters of honour**.
- It is reported that a man told his wife: "After I die, do not marry a man **who does not know how to gamble or drink wine**."
- **Very few** people kept away from these sins.

### Good qualities ✅
- **Mastery of language (eloquence)**
- **Love of freedom**
- **Keeping one's word**
- **Hospitality**
- **Bravery**
- **Simplicity**

👉 These good qualities later **helped the growth of Islam**.
👉 The Prophet ﷺ transformed this very society, according to Allah's guidance, into **a model society**.
