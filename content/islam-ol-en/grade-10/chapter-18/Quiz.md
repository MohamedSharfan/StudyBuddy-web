# Grade 10 – Chapter 18: Arabia before Islam – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_18/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q15 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. What forms the western border of Arabia?

A. The Persian Gulf
B. The Red Sea
C. The Arabian Sea
D. The Syrian Desert

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: North – the Syrian Desert; south – the Arabian Sea; west – the Red Sea; east – the Persian Gulf.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. Which is one of the meanings of "Jahiliyyah"?

A. A time full of wealth
B. A time when knowledge and good character did not prevail
C. A time without war
D. The time of the prophets

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Jahiliyyah = a time when knowledge and good character did not prevail; a time when a way of life contrary to Allah's guidance prevailed.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q3. What is the correct order of the social structure of pre-Islamic Arabia?

A. Qabilah → shu'ub → family
B. Shu'ub → family → qabilah
C. Family → shu'ub → qabilah
D. Family → qabilah → shu'ub

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Families joined to form shu'ub (clans), and clans joined to form qaba'il (tribes). The tribes were run under a leader called the shaykh.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q4. What was the main reason the Arabs formed the tribal system?

A. An order from foreign rulers
B. To strengthen themselves because they had to compete for scarce resources (grass, water)
C. For the growth of education
D. Because of a religious command

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: With scarce resources and great need, herders had to compete for grass and water. So they united into tribes to make themselves strong.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q5. What does "'asabiyyah" refer to?

A. Tribal loyalty that stays faithful to the tribe in every situation, without conditions
B. The tribal leader
C. Temporary marriage
D. A collection of poems

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Over time the tribal system turned into fanaticism; that is 'asabiyyah. Those who broke it were cast out. The tribal leader is the "shaykh".

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q6. In Jahiliyyah Arabia, why did the tribe celebrate when a baby boy was born or a poet appeared?

A. They respected education
B. It was a religious ritual
C. War needed men, and poets were needed to stir up and encourage the fighting
D. The government encouraged it

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Since long wars were fought over small wrongs, men were needed to fight and poets were needed to stir up and continue the war.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q7. Which two tribes does the textbook mention as fighting a long war?

A. Quraysh and Hawazin
B. Thaqif and Kinanah
C. Banu Hashim and Banu Umayyah
D. Aws and Khazraj

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: A long war took place between the tribes of Aws and Khazraj. The war over a grazing camel is also given as an example.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q8. What did the Arabs of the Jahiliyyah believe about Allah?

A. They denied Allah completely
B. They worshipped only Allah
C. They believed Allah was the Lord (Rabb) but not the God worthy of worship (Ilah)
D. They knew nothing about Allah

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: They accepted that Allah provides and directs all affairs (10:31). But since they also worshipped idols, their belief in God was false.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q9. "We worship them only so that they may bring us closer to Allah" – which verse mentions this?

A. 39:3
B. 10:31
C. 2:255
D. 112:1

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Verse 39:3 points out that the Arabs regarded idols as intermediaries for reaching Allah.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q10. What does the Quraysh's confidence when Abrahah came to destroy the Ka'bah show?

A. They denied Allah completely
B. "The Lord of the Ka'bah will look after it" – they believed Allah was Rabb
C. They prepared for war
D. They abandoned the Ka'bah and fled

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: This confidence shows that they believed Allah was their Lord; but they did not worship Him alone.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q11. Which is the most important later collection of the poems hung on the Ka'bah?

A. The Mushaf
B. The Muwatta'
C. Riyad as-Salihin
D. As-Sab' al-Mu'allaqat

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Poems that won the poetry contests at the sacred-month markets were hung on the Ka'bah. Their most important collection is "as-Sab' al-Mu'allaqat".

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q12. What was the proper form of marriage found among the upper-class tribes called?

A. Mut'ah
B. 'Asabiyyah
C. Nikah sahih
D. Qabilah

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: "Nikah sahih" existed among the upper-class tribes, where women were treated with honour. Mut'ah is temporary marriage.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q13. Which statement about women in the Jahiliyyah period is correct?

A. Women lived with full rights in all tribes
B. They were mostly without rights; in some tribes baby girls were buried alive
C. Women were tribal leaders
D. Women went to war

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Women were made to gather firewood, cook and carry water; they had no rights. In some tribes there was also the custom of burying baby girls alive.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q14. Which of the following was NOT one of the good qualities of the Arabs of the Jahiliyyah?

A. Giving women equal rights
B. Keeping one's word
C. Mastery of language
D. Hospitality

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Eloquence, love of freedom, keeping one's word, hospitality, bravery and simplicity were their good qualities. Women did not have equal rights.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q15. What role did the good qualities of the Arabs of the Jahiliyyah play later?

A. They helped the growth of Islam
B. They opposed Islam
C. They disappeared completely
D. They played no role

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Qualities such as bravery, keeping one's word and eloquence helped the growth of Islam. The Prophet ﷺ transformed that very society into a model society.

Difficulty: Medium
