### A1. 2016 – Islam I, Q34

Question ID: P2016-I-Q34  
Year: 2016  
Paper and question: Islam I, Q34  
Marks: not stated on the paper (Islam I multiple-choice question)  
Source: OL_2016_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

The eastern border of Arabia in the time of the Prophet ﷺ

- **A.** the Syrian desert
- **B.** the Arabian Sea
- **C.** the Persian Gulf
- **D.** the Red Sea

**Correct answer:** C – the Persian Gulf *(option (3) on the original paper)*

**Explanation:** The borders of Arabia: north – the Syrian desert; south – the Arabian Sea; west – the Red Sea; east – the Persian Gulf.

### A3. 2021 (2022) – Islam I, Q39

Question ID: P2021_2022-I-Q39  
Year: 2021 (2022)  
Paper and question: Islam I, Q39  
Marks: not stated on the paper (Islam I multiple-choice question)  
Source: OL_2021_2022_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

Consider the following terms.

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| A - Jahiliyyah | D - Mushaf 'Uthman | G - Bay'at al-'Aqabah |
| B - kuttab al-wahy | E - 'asabiyyah | H - tabi'i |
| C - qabilah | F - sirah | I - poetry (verse) |

Which group in the table above contains only terms connected with pre-Islamic Arab society?

- **A.** A B C D
- **B.** A C E I
- **C.** B G I H
- **D.** B D F G

**Correct answer:** B – A C E I *(option (2) on the original paper)*

**Explanation:** Jahiliyyah, qabilah (tribe), 'asabiyyah (tribal fanaticism), poetry – pre-Islamic Arabia. Kuttab al-wahy, Mushaf 'Uthman, Bay'at al-'Aqabah, sirah, tabi'i – the Islamic period.

**Answer status:** Suggested – not an official answer (no official marking scheme was supplied); based on the Grade 10/11 textbook content
