### A1. 2019 – Islam I, Q15

Question ID: P2019-I-Q15  
Year: 2019  
Paper and question: Islam I, Q15  
Marks: not stated on the paper (Islam I multiple-choice question)  
Source: OL_2019_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

In the table below, Column I gives the actions of some people mentioned in the Quran and Column II the names of the surahs of the Quran that refer to those actions.

| | Column I | Column II |
|---|---|---|
| 1. | The story of the prophet who kept his chastity in his youth | A - Surah al-Kahf |
| 2. | The story of Prophet Musa, who freed his people from slavery | B - Surah al-Baqarah |
| 3. | The story of Prophet Ibrahim, who tried to save his people who were engaged in shirk | C - Surah al-A'raf |
| 4. | The story of the young men who protected their iman from the tyranny of the state | D - Surah Yusuf |

Which option is correct when Column II is arranged to match Column I?

- **A.** A C B D
- **B.** B C A D
- **C.** D C A B
- **D.** D C B A

**Correct answer:** D – D C B A *(option (4) on the original paper)*

**Explanation:** Yusuf (AS) – Surah Yusuf (D); Musa (AS) – al-A'raf (C); Ibrahim (AS) – al-Baqarah (B); the people of the cave – al-Kahf (A). So D C B A.

## Part B: Structured and Essay Questions (Islam II)

### B1. 2018 – Islam II, Q4 (iii)

Question ID: P2018-II-Q4.3  
Year: 2018  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q4 (iii)  
Marks: 10 marks for the whole of Q4  
Source: OL_2018_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(iii) Briefly explain five things Islam mentions as ways a young person should follow to protect himself from evils.

**Suggested answer:**

1. Giving first place to taqwa – setting aside time every day for dhikr, salawat, tawbah, istighfar, reciting the Quran and sunnah prayers.
2. Choosing good friends and good role models; receiving advice (nasihah) from good people.
3. Spending more time reading and learning, and not wasting time on trivial things.
4. Using modern technology with self-control; not being dazzled by what is false.
5. Working to a plan with a clear goal; exercise, nourishing food; not cooperating with destructive activities.

**Answer explanation:** The idea that "youth is like a sword" – it can be used to build or to destroy – may be given as an introduction.

**Marking guidance (suggested only – not an official marking scheme):** No official marking scheme.

### B2. 2020 – Islam II, Q6 (ii)

Question ID: P2020-II-Q6.2  
Year: 2020  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q6 (ii)  
Marks: 10 marks for the whole of Q6  
Source: OL_2020_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(ii) Give three of the guidelines Islam mentions for making youth meaningful.

**Suggested answer:**

Any three of the following:
1. Making one's youth pleasing to Allah; giving first place to taqwa (dhikr, tawbah, reciting the Quran, sunnah prayers).
2. Living with a clear goal, planned action, hard work and far-sightedness.
3. Developing body, mind and soul in balance – reading, education, exercise.
4. Choosing good friends and good role models.
5. Using modern technology with self-control; not wasting time on trivial things.
6. Vocational education useful to the country and the community.

**Answer explanation:** The hadith "Make use of your youth before your old age" (Hakim) may be given as an introduction.

**Marking guidance (suggested only – not an official marking scheme):** Three guidelines.
