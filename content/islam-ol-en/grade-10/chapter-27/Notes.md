---

## 1. Why is youth important?
- Youth is the stage that begins **after childhood**.
- The **physical and mental growth** in this stage has a great effect on life.
- Only by shaping this stage **carefully, in an orderly way and wisely** will the future be bright.
- It is in this stage that **physical strength, time, the support of society and opportunities to develop one's abilities** are available without hindrance.

### Sayings of the Prophet ﷺ ⭐
| Saying (meaning) | Source |
|---|---|
| "**Make use of your youth before your old age**" | Hakim |
| One of **the four questions** asked on the Day of Judgement: "**How did you use your youth?**" | — |
| One of **the seven groups** who will be in **the shade of the Throne ('Arsh)** on the plain of gathering, when there is no other shade – **a young person who grew up in the worship of Allah** | Muslim |

## 3. The youth of the Prophet ﷺ
- **Even before prophethood** he was **a model young man**.
- He did not stain his youth with the **faults typical of that age**, and lived it in a way people praised.
- When he was chosen as a prophet **at the age of forty**, those qualities **supported** him.

### His young Companions
- **Most** of those who accepted, supported and gathered around the Prophet **were young.**
- Examples: **al-Arqam, 'Ali, Mus'ab, Ja'far, Ibn 'Abbas, Ibn 'Umar, Mu'adh, Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas (RA).**
- Because the Prophet ﷺ trained and guided them, they became **the pillars that held up the fortress of Islam**.

> 📖 The Prophet ﷺ (meaning): "**When the old were calling me a liar, it was the young who believed me and confirmed my truth.**"

### Their qualities (a lesson for the next generation)
Strength of iman • right thinking • high morals • passion for a goal • facing hardship • thirst for knowledge • a disciplined life • earning one's living • a spirit of dedication

## 5. "Youth is like a sword" ⚔️
- It can be used for **constructive** work, or turned to **destructive** efforts.
- Youth is **drawn** towards whatever attracts it.

### Today's challenges
- In the name of **modernity**, gateways to the path of ruin: **cosmetics, fashion styles, stimulants of physical desire, entertainment devices, (wrong) ways of making money.**
- These lead young men and women **to ruin**.

### Whose responsibility?
- Not only young people's; **society** also has a big part.
- **Peers** play a big part in wasting youth.
- Young people have the responsibility **to recognise good friends and good role models (heroes)**, and society has the responsibility **to point them out**.

---

## 6. What young people should do ⭐
- Shape their youth to win **Allah's pleasure and people's goodwill**.
- Live **with a sense of purpose and planned action**, giving no room to **cheap pleasures and passing comforts**.
- A human being is driven by **body, mind and soul** → all three must be developed **in balance**.
- **A clear goal, a calm approach, hard work, foresight.**
- **Wide knowledge, sound morals, social concern, a spirit of sacrifice, good communication.**
- **Do not be dazzled by the fake**; do not waste time on trivial things.
- Use **modern technology with self-control**.
- **Completely avoid** planning, supporting or taking part in **destructive activities**.

### Taqwa (God-consciousness) – first place
- **Taqwa comes first** in keeping youth away from evil.
- Set aside time every day for: **dhikr, salawat, tawbah, istighfar, recitation of the Quran, sunnah prayers.**
- Spend much time **reading and studying**; **mix with good people and receive their nasihah (sincere advice)**.
- **Exercise** daily; give priority to **nutritious food**.
- **Control** energy drinks, luxury food, fancy dress and entertainment.
- **Vocational education** that benefits the country and society.
- We cannot live alone → we must work **together**.

### The poem of 'Allama Iqbal (summary)
He prays to God for the youth: give the young **his love and deep, penetrating vision**; **give the young eaglets their wings again**.
