# Grade 10 – Chapter 27: Youth and Its Mission – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_27/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q16 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. From which collection is the saying "Make use of your youth before your old age" quoted?

A. Bukhari
B. Ahmad
C. Muslim
D. Hakim

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: The textbook quotes this saying from Hakim; it is the foremost of the Prophet's ﷺ teachings about youth.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q2. Of the four questions asked on the Day of Judgement, which concerns youth?

A. Where did you store your wealth?
B. How did you build your house?
C. How did you use your youth?
D. Who were your friends?

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: How one used one's youth will certainly be asked about in the Hereafter; so it must be used well.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q3. According to the hadith in Muslim, which of the seven groups in the shade of the Throne concerns young people?

A. Young people who gathered a lot of wealth
B. Young people who excelled at sport
C. Young people who grew up in the worship of Allah
D. Young people who travelled a lot

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Young people who grew up in the worship of Allah will be in the shade of the Throne on the Day when there is no other shade.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q4. Which prophet, while still young, led a society sunk in shirk towards tawhid?

A. Ibrahim (AS)
B. Musa (AS)
C. Yusuf (AS)
D. 'Isa (AS)

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: The story of Ibrahim (AS) advising his people in his youth is pointed to in Surah al-Baqarah.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q5. In which surah is the story of Yusuf (AS), who guarded his chastity as a young man?

A. Surah Maryam
B. Surah al-Kahf
C. Surah Yusuf
D. Surah al-A'raf

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Yusuf (AS) – Surah Yusuf; Maryam (AS) – Surah Maryam; both guarded their chastity.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q6. In which surah is the story of the young people who protected their iman from the ruler's tyranny?

A. Surah al-A'raf
B. Surah al-Baqarah
C. Surah Yusuf
D. Surah al-Kahf

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: The story of the People of the Cave (Ashab al-Kahf) is in Surah al-Kahf.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q7. According to the textbook, in which surah is the story of Musa (AS), who freed his people from slavery, mentioned?

A. Surah al-Kahf
B. Surah Yusuf
C. Surah Maryam
D. Surah al-A'raf

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: The textbook says this story of Musa (AS) is mentioned in Surah al-A'raf.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q8. "When the old were calling me a liar, it was the young who believed me and confirmed my truth" – who said this?

A. Abu Bakr (RA)
B. The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ
C. 'Ali (RA)
D. 'Allama Iqbal

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: The Prophet ﷺ said this in praise of the young people who supported him; they became the pillars that held up the fortress of Islam.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q9. Which of the following is NOT mentioned in the textbook as one of the Prophet's ﷺ young Companions?

A. Mus'ab (RA)
B. Ibn 'Abbas (RA)
C. Abu Sufyan
D. Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas (RA)

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: The list is al-Arqam, 'Ali, Mus'ab, Ja'far, Ibn 'Abbas, Ibn 'Umar, Mu'adh and Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas (RA).

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q10. Who conquered Spain while young?

A. Tariq ibn Ziyad
B. Muhammad ibn al-Qasim
C. Muhammad al-Fatih
D. 'Umar ibn 'Abd al-'Aziz

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Tariq ibn Ziyad – Spain; Muhammad ibn al-Qasim – Sindh; Muhammad al-Fatih – Constantinople.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q11. Who conquered Constantinople?

A. Tariq ibn Ziyad
B. Muhammad al-Fatih
C. Muhammad ibn al-Qasim
D. Imam Sibawayh

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Muhammad al-Fatih conquered Constantinople while still young.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q12. Which imam set out usul al-fiqh?

A. Imam al-Bukhari
B. Imam ash-Shafi'i
C. Imam Sibawayh
D. Imam Malik

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Imam ash-Shafi'i – usul al-fiqh; Imam al-Bukhari – Sahih al-Bukhari; Imam Sibawayh – Arabic grammar.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q13. What does "Youth is like a sword" mean?

A. Young people must go to war
B. Youth ends quickly
C. Young people are hot-tempered
D. Youth can be used for constructive work or turned to destructive efforts

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: A sword can protect or destroy; in the same way the energy of youth can be used for good or for evil.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q14. What comes first in keeping youth away from evil deeds?

A. Exercise
B. Taqwa (God-consciousness)
C. Wealth
D. Entertainment

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: To build taqwa, time must be set aside every day for dhikr, salawat, tawbah, istighfar, Quran recitation and sunnah prayers.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q15. Which statement about the responsibility for identifying good friends and good role models (heroes) is correct?

A. Young people have the responsibility to recognise them, and society to point them out
B. It belongs only to society
C. It belongs only to young people
D. No one is responsible

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Protecting youth is not only young people's responsibility; society also has a big part.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q16. By what is a human being driven, and so what must be developed in balance?

A. Body, mind, soul
B. Wealth, position, fame
C. Food, clothing, shelter
D. Family, friends, society

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Developing body, mind and soul in balance is the basis of the mission of youth.

Difficulty: Easy
