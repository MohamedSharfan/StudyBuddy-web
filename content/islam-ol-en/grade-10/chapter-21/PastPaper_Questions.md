### B1. 2018 – Islam II, Q6 (iii)

Question ID: P2018-II-Q6.3  
Year: 2018  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q6 (iii)  
Marks: 10 marks for the whole of Q6  
Source: OL_2018_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(iii) Clarify five special features of the Islamic economic system.

**Answer:**

1. Its divine nature – it is based on Allah's guidance; it does not have the weaknesses of man-made theories.
2. Correcting inequality – wealth is shared so that it does not circulate only among the rich (59:7).
3. Harmony between social welfare and individual welfare – a balanced way, different from both capitalism and socialism.
4. Attending to spiritual and material needs at the same time – values and culture matter as well as profit.
5. No one is wronged – "you will neither wrong nor be wronged" (2:279).
6. Flexibility – apart from what is forbidden, other dealings (mu'amalat) are permitted; ijtihad for new problems.

**Answer explanation:** As the question says "clarify", explain each with evidence.

### B4. 2025 (2026) – Islam II, Q3 (iii)

Question ID: P2025_2026-II-Q3.3  
Year: 2025 (2026)  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q3 (iii)  
Marks: 10 marks for the whole of Q3  
Source: OL_2025_2026_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(iii) Briefly explain five special features of the Islamic economic system.

**Answer:**

1. Its divine nature – based on Allah's guidance; all resources belong to Allah (53:31).
2. Correcting inequality – wealth is shared so that it does not circulate only among a few (zakat, sadaqah).
3. Harmony between social welfare and individual welfare – society has a share in the individual's wealth (70:24–25).
4. Attending to spiritual and material needs at the same time; peace and contentment.
5. No injustice to anyone – interest, bribery, cheating and exploitation are forbidden (2:275, 2:188).
6. Flexibility – solutions to new problems through ijtihad; suited to all times.
7. Agreements are written down and witnessed (2:282).

**Answer explanation:** The same topic appeared in 2020 Islam II Q5 (ii).
