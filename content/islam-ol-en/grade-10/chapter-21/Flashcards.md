# Grade 10 – Chapter 21: The Islamic View of Economics – Flashcards

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_21/Flashcards.md` (Tamil), cards 1–15 in the same order.

---

Flashcard 1

Front: What is the economy (wealth)?

Back: The wealth needed to meet human needs that cannot be obtained without human effort.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 21 | Topic: Basic terms

---

Flashcard 2

Front: Capitalism

Back: An idea that gives most importance to the individual's economic interest.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 21 | Topic: Economic ideas

---

Flashcard 3

Front: Socialism

Back: An idea that rejects individual interest and makes all property public property.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 21 | Topic: Economic ideas

---

Flashcard 4

Front: What are the two kinds of foundations of the Islamic economy?

Back: Doctrinal (based on belief) and practical.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 21 | Topic: Foundations

---

Flashcard 5

Front: What are the five doctrinal principles of the Islamic economy?

Back: Resources belong to Allah; questioning about wealth; society's share in property; results depend on Allah's will; wealth is a test.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 21 | Topic: Doctrinal foundations
