# Grade 10 – Chapter 21: The Islamic View of Economics

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_21/Notes.md` (Tamil). English rendering.

## 🎯 What you will learn
- Economy and economics – basic terms
- Capitalism, socialism and the Islamic economy – a comparison
- The two kinds of foundations of the Islamic economy
- The special features of the Islamic economic system

---

## 1. Basic terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Economy (wealth)** | The **wealth** needed to meet human needs that cannot be obtained without human effort |
| **Economic effort** | The effort made to obtain these needs |
| **Economics** | The **science** that studies this effort |

### Human needs
- **Basic needs:** food, clothing, shelter.
- **Needs that arose as society developed:** education, security, telecommunications, recreation.

👉 Because people's aims and efforts differ, their economic positions also differ (high, low, middle). **The best economic plan is one that meets the needs of all people.**

---

## 2. Three economic ideas ⭐

| Idea | Emphasis | Nature |
|---|---|---|
| **Capitalism** | Gives most importance to the **individual's** economic interest | Not balanced |
| **Socialism** | **Rejects** individual interest and makes all property **public property** | Not balanced |
| **The Islamic economy** | **Harmony between individual interest and the interest of society** | **Balanced (moderate)**; can give a lasting solution |

- Capitalism and socialism are ideas made by human beings, and **they contradict each other.**
- Opposing ideas naturally oppose each other; since the Islamic idea differs from both, **both of them oppose it.**

---

## 3. The foundations of the Islamic economy – two kinds ⭐
1. **Doctrinal** (based on belief)
2. **Practical**

### 3.1 Doctrinal foundations

| Principle | Evidence (meaning) |
|---|---|
| **All resources belong to Allah**; He created them for human beings | **53:31** – whatever is in the heavens and the earth belongs to Allah; **2:29** – He created everything on earth for you |
| A person **will be questioned in the Hereafter** about wealth | **102:8** – on that Day you will surely be asked about the blessings |
| **Society also has a share** in an individual's property | **70:24–25** – in their wealth there is a known share for the one who asks and the one who is deprived |
| The result of effort depends on **Allah's will** | **17:30** – He gives generously or in measure to whom He wills |
| Wealth is given **to test** a person | **64:15** – your wealth and your children are only a test |

👉 These are **the life and spirit** of the Islamic economy. Through them come the **peace, contentment and tranquillity** that capitalism and socialism failed to give.

### 3.2 Practical foundations

| Foundation | Evidence (meaning) |
|---|---|
| 1. **Being grateful to Allah** | **27:40** – this is from my Lord's favour, to test whether I am grateful or not |
| 2. Paying society's share through **zakat and sadaqah** | **9:60** – charity is for the poor, the needy, those who collect it, those whose hearts are to be won (new Muslims), freeing slaves, debtors, the cause of Allah, and travellers |
| 3. **Moderation** in spending and saving | **17:29** – do not tie your hand to your neck (miserliness), nor stretch it out completely (waste) |
| 4. Making sure **there is no interest** in any economic activity | **2:275** – those who consume interest will not stand except as one stands who has been driven mad by Satan's touch |
| 5. Completely avoiding **bribery, cheating, gambling and exploitation** | **2:188** – do not consume one another's wealth wrongfully, nor take it to the authorities (as a bribe) to seize it |
| 6. Scholars solving new problems that arise with changing times through **ijtihad** | → So the Islamic economy is suitable for **every time, place and situation** |
| 7. Contracts being **witnessed and written down** | **2:282** – when you give a loan for a fixed term, write it down |

- There are also many **hadiths** giving practical explanation of these.
- The Prophet ﷺ was **the first to put these foundations into practice**; the Muslim community saw their excellent results **in the time of the caliphs**.

---

## 4. The special features of the Islamic economic system ⭐

| Feature | Explanation |
|---|---|
| **1. Divine nature** | Based on Allah's guidance; it has none of the **weaknesses** found in human theories |
| **2. Correcting inequalities** | An excellent **system of distribution** – **59:7:** wealth must be distributed so that it does **not circulate only among the rich** |
| **3. Harmony between social and individual interest** | This is what makes it different from capitalism and socialism |
| **4. Attending to spiritual and worldly needs at the same time** | Other systems give importance **only to profit**; in the Islamic economy, **values, morals, culture and civilisation** are as important as profit |
| **5. No injustice to anyone** | **2:279** – "You will not wrong, nor be wronged" |
| **6. Flexibility** | Economic activities belong to **mu'amalat**; apart from the few things Islam forbids, **everything else is permitted.** New matters can be resolved through **ijtihad** within the Shari'ah |

> 📌 **Mu'amalat (معاملات)** = dealings – transactions between people.
> 📌 **Barakah (بركة)** = Allah's blessing; increase. When the Islamic economy is practised in life, barakah can be seen.

### Our duty
To put the principles of the Islamic economy **into practice in our personal and social life** and so make its excellence known to the world → success in both worlds.

## ✅ Quick revision
- Capitalism = individual interest; socialism = public ownership; Islam = the balance between the two.
- Two kinds of foundations: doctrinal (belief), practical.
- Doctrines: resources belong to Allah (53:31, 2:29); questioning (102:8); society's share (70:24–25); Allah's will (17:30); a test (64:15).
- Practice: gratitude (27:40), zakat (9:60), moderation (17:29), no interest (2:275), no bribery (2:188), ijtihad, writing (2:282).
- Features: divine, fair distribution (59:7), harmony, spiritual + worldly, no injustice (2:279), flexibility.
