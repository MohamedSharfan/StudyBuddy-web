### A1. 2017 – Islam I, Q23

Question ID: P2017-I-Q23  
Year: 2017  
Paper and question: Islam I, Q23  
Marks: not stated on the paper (Islam I multiple-choice question)  
Source: OL_2017_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

A group of Grade 10 students has gone on a trip. The whole day was planned for travelling and visiting places. When the time for the Zuhr prayer came on this trip, the problem arose of how to perform the prayers. Which of the following is the correct decision to take in such a situation?

- **A.** performing the Zuhr and 'Asr prayers each at its own time
- **B.** performing jam' at-taqdim or jam' at-ta'khir
- **C.** as the necessary facilities are not available now, making them up (qada') at the night lodging
- **D.** continuing the journey and praying if a convenient place is found somewhere on the way

**Correct answer:** B – performing jam' at-taqdim or jam' at-ta'khir *(option (2) on the original paper)*

**Explanation:** A traveller has the concession of al-qasr wal-jam'; Zuhr and 'Asr may be combined, either brought forward (jam' at-taqdim) or delayed (jam' at-ta'khir). Letting a prayer become qada' is not allowed.

**Answer status:** Suggested – not an official answer (no official marking scheme was supplied); based on the Grade 10/11 textbook content

### B2. 2018 – Islam II, Q7 (ii)

Question ID: P2018-II-Q7.2  
Year: 2018  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q7 (ii)  
Marks: 10 marks (short notes on two topics)  
Source: OL_2018_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(ii) Qasr and jam' prayer

**Suggested answer:**

- A concession Allah has given so that prayer can be established in every situation of life – "al-qasr wal-jam'".
- Qasr = shortening the prayer: on a journey, praying the four-rak'ah prayers (Zuhr, 'Asr, 'Isha') as two rak'ahs; Subh and Maghrib are not shortened.
- Jam' = combining prayers: praying Zuhr–'Asr and Maghrib–'Isha at one time.
  - Jam' at-taqdim – praying the later prayer in the time of the earlier one.
  - Jam' at-ta'khir – praying the earlier prayer in the time of the later one.
- These are concessions for someone on a long permitted journey; in Hajj, prayers are also combined at 'Arafah and Muzdalifah.

**Answer explanation:** One may conclude that this concession shows Islam is an easy religion.

**Marking guidance (suggested only – not an official marking scheme):** No official marking scheme.

**Answer status:** Suggested – not an official answer (no official marking scheme was supplied); based on the Grade 10/11 textbook content

**Verification note:** Detailed conditions such as the travel distance are not in the current textbook; they are given briefly from general fiqh knowledge.

**Question:**

(iii) State the importance of prayer and clarify three social benefits gained through it.

**Suggested answer:**

Importance:
- Prayer is the pillar of Islam (Ahmad); a duty prescribed at fixed times for the believers (4:103).

Social benefits:
1. Meeting people five times a day through congregational prayer – social unity and brotherhood grow.
2. Time management – the discipline of acting at fixed times.
3. Discipline of leadership – the manners of following the imam; equality (everyone in the same row).
