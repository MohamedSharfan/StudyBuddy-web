# Grade 10 – Chapter 7: The Importance of Salah (Prayer) – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_07/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q14 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. Which verse says, "Prayer is a duty prescribed at fixed times for the believers"?

A. 4:103
B. 2:2–3
C. 29:45
D. 20:14

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Verse 4:103 confirms that prayer is an obligatory duty with fixed times (fard 'ayn) that every Muslim must perform.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q2. According to the hadith in Ahmad, what is the "pillar" of Islam?

A. Zakat
B. Striving in the way of Allah
C. Fasting
D. Prayer

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: (meaning) "The head of the matter is Islam; its pillar is prayer; and its peak is striving in the way of Allah." Striving is the "peak", not the "pillar".

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q3. To whom did the Prophet ﷺ compare a person who misses a single prayer (Ibn Hibban)?

A. A traveller who has lost his way
B. A man who has lost his family and his wealth and is left alone
C. A sick person
D. A person in debt

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: The Prophet ﷺ said that missing one prayer is as great a loss as losing one's family and wealth. This shows how precious prayer is.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q4. Which of the following are examples of the concessions given in prayer?

A. Al-qasr wal-jam' and salat al-khawf
B. Giving up prayer completely
C. Praying without wudu
D. Always praying without facing the qiblah

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Because prayer must be established in every situation, Allah has given concessions such as shortening and combining prayers (qasr and jam') and the prayer in times of fear (salat al-khawf). Giving up prayer is not a concession.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q5. What does "al-muhafazah 'ala al-salah" mean?

A. Establishing prayer
B. Shortening prayer
C. Guarding prayer
D. Calling people to prayer

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Al-muhafazah 'ala al-salah = guarding the prayer. Iqamat al-salah = establishing the prayer. Both terms are used in the Quran and hadith.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q6. In the Hadith Qudsi that shows prayer is "a chance to converse with Allah", what does Allah say when the servant recites "Iyyaka na'budu wa iyyaka nasta'in"?

A. My servant has glorified Me
B. This is between Me and My servant, and My servant shall have what he asks for
C. My servant has extolled Me
D. My servant has been forgiven

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: The verse "You alone we worship, and You alone we ask for help" is like a covenant between the servant and Allah. So Allah says, "This is between Me and My servant" (Muslim).

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q7. Which verse says, "Know that it is in the remembrance of Allah that hearts find peace"?

A. 20:14
B. 70:19
C. 11:114
D. 13:28

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Verse 13:28 says that remembering Allah brings peace of heart. Verse 20:14 says, "Establish prayer to remember Me." Both are evidence for the benefit "remembrance of Allah and peace of heart".

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q8. According to the hadith in Tirmidhi, what is "the key to Paradise"?

A. Wudu
B. Prayer
C. Zakat
D. Fasting

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: "The key to prayer is wudu; the key to Paradise is prayer." Wudu is the key to prayer – do not confuse it with the key to Paradise.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q9. "Prayer certainly prevents shameful and evil deeds" (29:45) – which benefit of prayer does this show?

A. It stops a person from sinful acts
B. It will be a witness in the Hereafter
C. It brings Allah's mercy
D. Social unity

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: A person who prays properly remembers Allah and so keeps away from evil deeds. In this way, prayer is a barrier against sin.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q10. According to the hadith in Muslim, an obligatory prayer performed with humility and submission becomes an expiation (kaffarah) for what?

A. All major sins
B. Wrongs done to others
C. Minor sins
D. Missed fasts

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: The textbook says that a prayer performed after a good wudu, with khushu' and khudu', becomes an expiation for minor sins.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q11. According to the hadith in Ahmad, with whom will a person who does not guard the prayer be in the Hereafter?

A. Qarun, Fir'awn, Haman and Ubayy ibn Khalaf
B. Abu Jahl, Abu Lahab, Utbah and Shaybah
C. Namrud, Qarun, Abu Lahab and Haman
D. Fir'awn, Namrud, Abu Jahl and Ubayy

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: For the one who guards it, prayer will be a light, a proof and salvation in the Hereafter. The one who does not guard it will be with Qarun, Fir'awn, Haman and Ubayy ibn Khalaf.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q12. What will those who enter Saqar first give as the reason (74:40–43)?

A. We did not give Zakat
B. We were not among those who prayed
C. We did not fast
D. We did not perform Hajj

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: When asked, "What brought you into Saqar?", they will say, "We were not among those who prayed." This shows that prayer protects from Hellfire.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q13. What does the textbook give as a social benefit of prayer?

A. Meeting people five times a day through congregational prayer; time management; discipline under leadership
B. Gaining more wealth
C. Gaining political power
D. Developing sporting skills

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: When the men of a village pray in congregation, the individual is bound to society, and qualities such as time management and obeying leadership develop.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q14. According to the textbook, which of the following statements about prayer is WRONG?

A. It was the first duty made obligatory on Muslims
B. It was also made obligatory on earlier prophets
C. It is the last deed to be questioned about in the Hereafter
D. It is the duty the Prophet ﷺ urged on his community in his final moments

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Prayer is the first deed to be questioned about in the Hereafter; "last" is wrong. The other three are facts stated in the textbook.

Difficulty: Hard
