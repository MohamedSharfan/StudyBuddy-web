## 1. Prayer – the foremost act of worship
- Of the basic acts of worship that Islam has made obligatory, **prayer (salah) comes first.**
- Prayer is **the sign of a believer**; it is **the dividing line between a believer and a disbeliever.**

> 📖 **(24:56):** وَأَقِيمُوا الصَّلَاةَ وَآتُوا الزَّكَاةَ وَأَطِيعُوا الرَّسُولَ لَعَلَّكُمْ تُرْحَمُونَ
> (meaning) "Establish prayer, give Zakat and obey the Messenger, so that you may receive mercy."

## 3. Concessions in prayer
Prayer must be established **in every situation of life**. For this, Allah has given concessions:
- **Al-qasr wal-jam'** (shortening and combining prayers)
- **Salat al-khawf** (the prayer in times of fear)

### Key terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Iqamat al-salah** (إقامة الصلاة) | **Establishing** prayer |
| **Al-muhafazah 'ala al-salah** (المحافظة على الصلاة) | **Guarding** (being regular and careful in) prayer |

## 4. Ten benefits of guarding the prayer ⭐

### 1) A chance to converse with Allah
Hadith Qudsi (Muslim) – (meaning) Allah says: "I have divided the prayer into two halves between Me and My servant, and My servant shall have what he asks for."

| What the servant recites | Allah's reply (meaning) |
|---|---|
| Al-hamdu lillahi rabbil-'alamin | My servant has **praised Me** |
| Ar-Rahmanir-Rahim | My servant has **extolled Me** |
| Maliki yawmid-din | My servant has **glorified Me** |
| Iyyaka na'budu wa iyyaka nasta'in | This is **between Me and My servant**, and My servant shall have what he asks for |
| Ihdinas-siratal-mustaqim … walad-dallin | This is **between Me and My servant**, and My servant shall have what he asks for |

> ⚠️ Verification: the order of the words in this Hadith Qudsi, as given in the Tamil textbook, is listed in `VERIFY.md` (Grade 10, Chapter 7). The table follows the textbook.

💡 When Surah al-Fatihah is recited, Allah answers every verse – this is why prayer is a "conversation with Allah".

### 2) Remembrance of Allah and peace of heart
- **(20:14) – meaning:** Worship Me, and establish prayer to remember Me.
- **(13:28) – meaning:** It is in the remembrance of Allah that hearts find peace.
- **Hadith (Bukhari) – meaning:** "The **comfort of my eyes** has been placed in prayer."

### 3) Receiving Allah's mercy
- **(24:56) – meaning:** Establish prayer … so that you may receive mercy.

### 4) The key to Paradise
- **Hadith (Ahmad):** **The first deed** a servant will be asked about in the Hereafter is **prayer**. If it is sound, he succeeds; if it is not, he loses.
- **Hadith (Tirmidhi):** The key to prayer is **wudu**; the key to Paradise is **prayer**.

### 5) A firm mind and a generous attitude
- **(70:19–23) – meaning:** Man was created restless; when harm touches him he panics, and when good comes to him he withholds it – **except those who are constant in their prayer.**

### 6) It stops a person from sinful acts
- **(29:45) – meaning:** Prayer certainly prevents **shameful and evil deeds**.

### 7) It brings forgiveness of sins
- **(11:114) – meaning:** Establish prayer at the two ends of the day and in part of the night. **Good deeds wipe out bad deeds.**
- **Hadith (Muslim):** If a person, when the time comes, performs wudu well and prays **with humility and submission (*khushu'* and *khudu'*)**, that prayer becomes an **expiation (*kaffarah*) for minor sins**.

### 8) It will be a witness in the Hereafter
- **Hadith (Ahmad):** For the one who guards the prayer, it will be **a light, a proof and salvation** in the Hereafter. The one who does not guard it will be with **Qarun, Fir'awn, Haman and Ubayy ibn Khalaf**.

### 9) It brings inner and outer purity
- **(5:6) – meaning:** When you get ready for prayer, wash your faces and your hands up to the elbows, wipe your heads, and wash your feet up to the ankles (wudu). Allah does not want to make things hard for you; He wants **to purify you**.

### 10) It protects from Hellfire
- **(74:40–43) – meaning:** The people of Paradise will ask the guilty, "What brought you into **Saqar** (Hellfire)?" They will say, "We were **not among those who prayed**."

💡 **Memory tip (10 benefits):** conversation – peace – mercy – Paradise – firmness – a barrier to sin – forgiveness – witness – purity – protection from Hell.

---

## 5. The social benefits of prayer
- It is an act of worship that **binds the individual to society**.
- Because the men of a village pray **in congregation (jama'ah)**, they have a chance to meet people **five times** a day.
- It brings social benefits such as **time management and discipline under leadership**.

### More important facts
- Prayer was the **first** duty made obligatory on Muslims.
- It is **a gift from the heavens** – a duty given in the heavens (during the Mi'raj).
- It was also made obligatory on **earlier prophets**.
- It is the **first** deed to be **questioned** about in the Hereafter.
- It is the duty the Prophet ﷺ urged on his community **in his final moments**.
