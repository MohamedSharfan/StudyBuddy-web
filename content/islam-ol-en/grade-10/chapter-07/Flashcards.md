Flashcard 11

Front: Which verses describe the prayer of the successful believers?

Flashcard 12

Front: سقر (Saqar)

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 7 | Topic: Benefits of prayer

Flashcard 13

Front: What are the ten benefits of guarding the prayer?

Back: Conversing with Allah, peace of heart, Allah's mercy, the key to Paradise, a firm mind, a barrier to sin, forgiveness of sins, a witness in the Hereafter, inner and outer purity, protection from Hell.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 7 | Topic: Benefits of prayer
