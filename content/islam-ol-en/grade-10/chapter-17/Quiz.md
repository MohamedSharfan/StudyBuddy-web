# Grade 10 – Chapter 17: Secondary Sources – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_17/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q15 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. Which are the most important of the secondary sources?

A. The Quran and the Sunnah
B. 'Urf and istihsan
C. Ijma' and qiyas
D. Tafsir and sirah

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: The Quran and the Sunnah are the primary sources. Of the secondary sources developed with the help of their principles, ijma' and qiyas are the most important.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. How can "ijma'" be described in short?

A. Comparing
B. Protecting the public interest
C. Custom
D. A unanimous decision

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Ijma' = joining together, resolving, reaching a unanimous decision. Comparing is qiyas.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q3. Which element is NOT part of the definition of ijma'?

A. A problem arising after the Prophet's ﷺ death
B. Having no solution in the Quran or the Sunnah
C. A decision made by the Companions during the Prophet's ﷺ lifetime
D. The unanimous view of the mujtahids of that time

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: During the Prophet's ﷺ lifetime, problems were solved through revelation. Ijma' can take place only after the Prophet's ﷺ death.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q4. In which collection is the hadith "Allah will not unite my ummah upon misguidance", a proof for ijma', found?

A. Bukhari
B. Tirmidhi
C. Muslim
D. Abu Dawud

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: The textbook quotes this hadith from Tirmidhi. The Quranic evidence is 4:115.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q5. Which of the following is NOT a benefit of ijma'?

A. Unity is kept in society
B. Social discipline is not broken
C. A life based on mutual help
D. Everyone following his own separate opinion

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Ijma' creates a united view and keeps unity. Everyone going his own way leads to division.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q6. Which ijma' is it when all the mujtahids clearly accept a decision in their own words?

A. Ijma' sarih
B. Ijma' al-akthariyyah
C. Ijma' sukuti
D. Qiyas

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Ijma' sarih (explicit consensus) is also called ijma' qawli (verbal consensus).

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q7. If one person gives a decision and the other mujtahids stay silent, expressing neither agreement nor disagreement, which ijma' is it?

A. Ijma' sarih
B. Ijma' sukuti
C. Ijma' qawli
D. Ijma' al-akthariyyah

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Sukut = silence. Imam ash-Shafi'i (RH) says ijma' sukuti cannot be taken as evidence; most scholars of the Maliki madhhab regard it as ijma'.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q8. Who said that ijma' sukuti cannot be taken as evidence?

A. Imam Malik (RH)
B. Imam ash-Shafi'i (RH)
C. Imam Ahmad (RH)
D. Imam al-Bukhari (RH)

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Imam ash-Shafi'i (RH) did not accept it. Most scholars of the Maliki madhhab regard it as ijma' – so "Malik" is the wrong answer.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q9. What is the linguistic meaning of the Arabic word "qiyas"?

A. Comparing, inferring by analogy
B. Joining together
C. Silence
D. Custom

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Qiyas = comparing, inferring by analogy. A new problem is compared with one that has a solution, and the same solution is given.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q10. In qiyas, what is "al-asl"?

A. The new problem needing a solution
B. The common cause between the two problems
C. The ruling given at the end
D. The problem whose solution is already stated

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Asl = the root – the problem whose solution is already stated. Far' = the new problem; 'illah = the common cause; hukm = the ruling.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q11. "If a person named in a bequest kills the one who made the bequest, may he be given the property?" – which pillar of qiyas is this?

A. Al-far'
B. Al-asl
C. 'Illah
D. Hukm

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: This is a problem needing a solution, with no direct solution in the Quran, Sunnah or ijma' – so it is al-far' (the branch).

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q12. An heir who killed, and a beneficiary of a bequest who killed – what is the "'illah" common to these two problems?

A. Both are Muslims
B. Both are relatives
C. Being in a hurry to get the property
D. Both are poor

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: In both cases the killing was done to get the property quickly. On the basis of this common cause, the ruling "the property is not given" also applies to the new problem.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q13. What method did the Companions use in choosing Abu Bakr (RA) as caliph?

A. Ijma' sukuti
B. Qiyas – comparing leadership in prayer with leadership of the community
C. 'Urf
D. Istishab

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: When the Prophet ﷺ was ill, Abu Bakr (RA) led the prayer as imam. Comparing this with leadership of the community, they chose him as caliph – this is evidence for qiyas.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q14. What example does the textbook give of a ruling against reason that would result if qiyas were not accepted?

A. Only wine would be haram; other intoxicants would not be haram
B. The prayer times would change
C. Fasting would no longer be obligatory
D. Zakat would not need to be paid

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: The Quran mentions only wine by name as haram. Only through qiyas, based on the common cause of intoxication, can other intoxicants also be ruled haram.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q15. Which of the following is NOT one of the seven secondary sources besides ijma' and qiyas?

A. Istihsan
B. 'Urf
C. Hadith qudsi
D. Qawl as-sahabi

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: The seven: istihsan, istishab, istislah (al-maslahah al-mursalah), 'urf, ash-shara'i' as-sabiqah, qawl as-sahabi, sadd adh-dhara'i'. Hadith qudsi is part of the Sunnah, not a secondary source.

Difficulty: Medium
