Question ID: P2017-I-Q20  
Year: 2017  
Paper and question: Islam I, Q20  
Marks: not stated on the paper (Islam I multiple-choice question)  
Source: OL_2017_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

The following are some of the secondary sources of Islam.

| Column I | Column II |
|---|---|
| (i) unanimous decision | A - al-'urf |
| (ii) drawing an analogy | B - al-ijma' |
| (iii) custom | C - maslahah mursalah |
| (iv) protecting the public good | D - al-qiyas |

Which option arranges Column II according to Column I?

- **A.** A B C D
- **B.** A C B D

**Correct answer:** C – B D A C *(option (3) on the original paper)*

**Explanation:** Unanimous decision – ijma' (B); analogy – qiyas (D); custom – 'urf (A); public good – maslahah mursalah (C). So B D A C.

### B5. 2021 (2022) – Islam II, Q3 (i), (ii), (iii)

Question ID: P2021_2022-II-Q3.1  
Year: 2021 (2022)  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q3 (i), (ii), (iii)  
Marks: 10 marks for the whole of Q3 (division among sub-questions not given)  
Source: OL_2021_2022_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(i) What is 'qiyas'?
(ii) State three of the arkan (pillars) of qiyas and explain them.
(iii) Clarify, with evidence from the Quran and Sunnah, that 'ijma' may be taken as a source of law'.

**Suggested answer:**

(i) Qiyas (linguistically: comparing, estimating) = comparing a new problem on which the Quran or Sunnah gives no ruling with a problem on which a ruling is given, on the basis of a common cause, and applying the same ruling.

(ii) Any three of the following:
1. Al-asl – the problem on which a ruling has already been given (e.g. an heir who kills has no share in the estate – Tirmidhi).
2. Al-far' – the new problem to be solved (e.g. one who kills the person who made a bequest to him).
3. Al-'illah – the cause common to both (hastening to obtain the property).
4. Al-hukm – the ruling given for the asl (the property will not be given).

(iii)
- The Quran (4:115): whoever opposes the Messenger after guidance has become clear and follows a way other than that of the believers, We will put him into the Fire → following the unanimous way of the believers is a duty.
- Hadith (Tirmidhi): "Allah will not unite my ummah upon error" → the unanimous decision of the mujtahids cannot be error.
- So not following ijma' is like separating from the Messenger; it is a source of law.

**Answer explanation:** In (iii) write the evidence together with the conclusion drawn from it.

**Marking guidance (suggested only – not an official marking scheme):** No official marking scheme.

**Answer status:** Suggested – not an official answer (no official marking scheme was supplied); based on the Grade 10/11 textbook content
