# Grade 10 – Chapter 17: Secondary Sources – Flashcards

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_17/Flashcards.md` (Tamil), cards 1–18 in the same order.

---

Flashcard 1

Front: What are secondary sources?

Back: Sources of law developed with the help of the principles laid down in the Quran and the Sunnah.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 17 | Topic: Overview

---

Flashcard 2

Front: إجماع (Ijma')

Back: Consensus – after the Prophet's death, the unanimous view of the mujtahids on a problem not solved in the Quran or Sunnah.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 17 | Topic: Ijma'

---

Flashcard 3

Front: مجتهد (Mujtahid)

Back: A scholar qualified to derive rulings from the Quran and the Sunnah.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 17 | Topic: Ijma'

---

Flashcard 4

Front: What is the Quranic evidence for ijma'?

Back: 4:115 – whoever follows a way other than the way of the believers will enter Hell.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 17 | Topic: Ijma'

---

Flashcard 5

Front: What are the three benefits of ijma'?

Back: Unity, discipline, and a life of mutual help.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 17 | Topic: Ijma'

---

Flashcard 6

Front: إجماع صريح (Ijma' sarih)

Back: Explicit consensus – the mujtahids clearly accept in words; also called ijma' qawli.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 17 | Topic: Types of ijma'

---

Flashcard 7

Front: إجماع الأكثرية (Ijma' al-akthariyyah)

Back: The majority of mujtahids agreeing on a solution.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 17 | Topic: Types of ijma'

---

Flashcard 8

Front: إجماع سكوتي (Ijma' sukuti)

Back: Silent consensus – not accepted by ash-Shafi'i; accepted by many Maliki scholars.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 17 | Topic: Types of ijma'

---

Flashcard 9

Front: قياس (Qiyas)

Back: Analogy – comparing a new problem with one that has a solution and giving it the same solution.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 17 | Topic: Qiyas

---

Flashcard 10

Front: أركان القياس (Arkan al-qiyas)

Back: The four pillars of qiyas: asl, far', 'illah, hukm.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 17 | Topic: Qiyas

---

Flashcard 11

Front: الأصل (Al-asl)

Back: The problem whose solution is already stated.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 17 | Topic: Qiyas

---

Flashcard 12

Front: الفرع (Al-far')

Back: The new problem that needs a solution.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 17 | Topic: Qiyas

---

Flashcard 13

Front: العلة ('Illah)

Back: The common feature / common cause between the two problems.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 17 | Topic: Qiyas

---

Flashcard 14

Front: الحكم (Hukm)

Back: The ruling given for the asl in the Quran / Sunnah.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 17 | Topic: Qiyas

---

Flashcard 15

Front: Which hadith is the example of the asl in qiyas?

Back: "The killer does not inherit" (Tirmidhi).

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 17 | Topic: Qiyas

---

Flashcard 16

Front: What are the seven other secondary sources?

Back: Istihsan, istishab, istislah (al-maslahah al-mursalah), 'urf, ash-shara'i' as-sabiqah, qawl as-sahabi, sadd adh-dhara'i'.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 17 | Topic: Other sources
