## 1. What are secondary sources?
- The primary sources of law are **the Quran and the Sunnah.** They **do not change**, and they contain the principles for solving **every problem** that will arise until the Last Day.
- Sources developed with the help of these principles are called **secondary sources.**
- The most important of them are **ijma' and qiyas.**

---

## 2. Ijma' (إجماع) ⭐

### Meaning
- **Linguistically:** joining together, resolving, reaching a unanimous decision.
- **In short:** **a unanimous decision (consensus).**

> 📖 **Definition:** When a problem arises **after the Prophet's ﷺ death** and its solution is **not found in the Quran or the Sunnah**, the **mujtahids** living at that time **agree unanimously** on its solution.

> 📌 **Mujtahid (مجتهد)** = a scholar qualified to derive rulings from the Quran and the Sunnah.

### Evidence
1. **(4:115) – meaning:** Whoever opposes the Messenger after guidance has become clear to him, and follows **a way other than the way of the believers**, We will leave him on the path he has chosen and admit him to Hell.
2. **Hadith (Tirmidhi) – meaning:** "Allah will **not unite my ummah upon misguidance.**"

### According to this evidence
- The Muslim community **will not unite upon misguidance.**
- The unanimous decision is **the way of the believers.**
- Not following the unanimous decision is like **separating from the Messenger.**
- Whoever leaves the Messenger and the believers and goes his own way will end in **Hell**.

### Benefits of ijma'
1. **Unity** is kept in society
2. **Discipline (order)** in society is not broken
3. It leads to a life based on **mutual help**

### The main types of ijma'

| Type | Explanation | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Ijma' sarih** (إجماع صريح) – explicit consensus | It is clear **from their own words** that all the mujtahids agree on the decision | Also called **ijma' qawli** (verbal consensus) |
| **Ijma' al-akthariyyah** (إجماع الأكثرية) | **The majority** of mujtahids agree on a solution | — |
| **Ijma' sukuti** (إجماع سكوتي) – silent consensus | One or a few give a decision, and the others **stay silent**, expressing neither agreement nor disagreement | **Imam ash-Shafi'i (RH):** it cannot be taken as evidence. Most scholars of the **Maliki madhhab**: it is ijma' |

## 3. Qiyas (قياس) ⭐
- The **most widely used** of the secondary sources.
- **Linguistically:** comparing, inferring by analogy.

> 📖 **Definition:** When a **new problem** arises whose solution is not stated in the Quran or the Sunnah, **comparing** it with a problem whose solution is stated, and applying that solution to the new problem.

- For this, there must be **something in common between the two problems.**

### Arkan al-qiyas (أركان القياس) – the four pillars of qiyas ⭐

| Pillar | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Al-asl** (الأصل) | The problem whose solution **is already stated** | "**The killer does not inherit**" (Tirmidhi) – an heir who kills the owner of the property gets no share of it |
| **2. Al-far'** (الفرع) | The problem **needing a solution**, with no solution in the Quran, Sunnah or ijma' | If **a person named in a bequest (wasiyyah)** kills the one who made the bequest, may he be given the property? |
| **3. 'Illah** (العلة) | The **common feature / common cause** between the two problems | In both, **being in a hurry to get the property** |
| **4. Hukm** (الحكم) | The **ruling given** in the Quran / Sunnah for the asl | An heir who killed for the property **is not given the property** |

👉 **Result of the qiyas:** one who killed the person who made the bequest in order to get the property quickly is also **not given the property.**

💡 **Memory tip:** **Asl** (old case) + **far'** (new case) + **'illah** (common cause) → **hukm** (ruling).

### Evidence for qiyas
1. **The hajj-as-a-debt incident:** a woman asked whether she could perform hajj for her father, who had died while hajj was obligatory on him. The Prophet ﷺ: "If your father had a **debt** and you paid it, would it benefit him?" – "Yes." – "Then **a debt owed to Allah** is even more deserving to be paid."
2. **The qiyas of the Companions:** when the Prophet ﷺ was ill, **Abu Bakr (RA) led the prayer as imam**. **Comparing that leadership in prayer with leadership of the community**, the Companions chose him as **caliph**.
3. **Reason:** the Quran has made **wine (intoxicating drink)** haram, but it does not mention **other intoxicants** that are like it or worse. If qiyas were not accepted, we would reach a ruling **against reason**: "Only wine is haram; other intoxicants are not."

### Why qiyas is necessary
- Social progress creates **new problems**; qiyas helps to solve them in the light of the Quran and the Sunnah.
- Qiyas greatly helps to show that Islam is **suitable for every age**.

## 4. Seven other secondary sources
1. **Istihsan** (استحسان)
2. **Istishab** (استصحاب)
3. **Istislah** (استصلاح) – **al-maslahah al-mursalah** (المصلحة المرسلة)
4. **'Urf** (عرف) – custom
5. **Ash-shara'i' as-sabiqah** (الشرائع السابقة) – the laws of earlier prophets
6. **Qawl as-sahabi** (قول الصحابي) – the saying of a Companion
7. **Sadd adh-dhara'i'** (سد الذرائع) – blocking the means (to harm)

👉 All the secondary sources help to solve legal problems, and they also confirm that Islam is **flexible enough for every age and situation**.

## ✅ Quick revision
- Primary sources: Quran, Sunnah; main secondary sources: ijma', qiyas.
- Ijma' = after the Prophet's death, the unanimous view of the mujtahids on a problem not solved in the Quran or Sunnah. Evidence: 4:115; "My ummah will not unite upon misguidance" (Tirmidhi).
- Benefits of ijma': unity, discipline, mutual help.
- Types: sarih (= qawli), akthariyyah, sukuti (not accepted by ash-Shafi'i; accepted by the Malikis).
- Qiyas = analogy; arkan: asl, far', 'illah, hukm.
- Evidence for qiyas: the hajj-debt hadith, choosing Abu Bakr as caliph, comparing intoxicants with wine.
- 7 more: istihsan, istishab, istislah, 'urf, ash-shara'i' as-sabiqah, qawl as-sahabi, sadd adh-dhara'i'.
