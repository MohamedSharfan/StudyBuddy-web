# Grade 10 – Chapter 26: Human Rights – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_26/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q15 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. What is the reason that Allah alone is able to give all people common, balanced and unbiased rights?

A. Allah created everyone; as His creation all people are equal
B. Human beings cannot make laws
C. Governments deny rights
D. Rights are unnecessary

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Whatever their time, race or country, all people are equal as Allah's most honoured creation; He alone can give them unbiased rights.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q2. Which statement about the rights given by Allah is correct?

A. They change with the times
B. They are birthrights; no one can deny, take away or misuse them
C. They exist only on paper
D. Kings can take them away

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: The rights given by Islam are permanent; those who take them away are not only great traitors but also deserve Allah's punishment.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q3. Which of the following is NOT a reason why rights made by human beings are called weak?

A. They differ with time and place
B. They suit human nature and work continuously
C. They exist on paper and do not work in practice
D. They are taken away just as they are given

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Suiting human nature and working continuously is a feature of the rights given by Islam, not of those made by human beings.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q4. What is the name of the agreement the Prophet ﷺ made with the people living in Madinah at the start of his hijrah there?

A. The Treaty of Hudaybiyyah
B. The Magna Carta
C. The Pledge of 'Aqabah
D. The Charter of Madinah

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Through the Charter of Madinah, Islam proclaimed human rights six centuries before the West thought of them.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q5. Which information about the Magna Carta (the Great Charter) is correct?

A. An agreement in England in 1215 between King John and the people
B. It was made in Madinah in 622
C. It was made by the United Nations in 1948
D. It was made in France

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Six centuries after the Charter of Madinah, the Magna Carta was made in England in 1215; many changes were made to it over time.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q6. Who wrote the book "Islamic Jurisprudence: An International Perspective"?

A. T. B. Jayah
B. Dr C. G. Weeramantry
C. M. C. Siddi Lebbe
D. Imam al-Mawardi

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Former judge Dr C. G. Weeramantry says that a declaration of human rights drawn only from Islamic legal literature would be comparable to today's international charter.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q7. "Whoever kills a soul unjustly, it is as if he has killed all mankind" – which verse is this?

A. 6:151
B. 2:256
C. 5:32
D. 4:32

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Verse 5:32 shows the sacredness of the right to life. 6:151 says one must not kill without due process of law.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q8. According to the hadith in Bayhaqi, through what are people ranked higher?

A. Through God-consciousness (taqwa)
B. Through colour
C. Through race
D. Through wealth

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: An Arab is not better than a non-Arab, nor a white person better than a black person; taqwa is the measure of rank – this is evidence for the right to equality.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q9. Who is one of the three people the Prophet ﷺ said he would oppose on the Day of Judgement (Bukhari)?

A. A trader
B. A poor person
C. One who enslaves people, sells them and eats from the price
D. A traveller

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: This hadith shows the right to individual freedom; so Islam encourages freeing slaves and forbids the slave trade.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q10. Which right does the verse "There is no compulsion in religion" (2:256) show?

A. The right to property
B. The right to dignity
C. The right to justice
D. Freedom of religion (individual freedom)

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: This verse shows freedom of religion – that a person may follow the religion he chooses.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q11. "Do not let hatred of a people lead you to be unjust to them; be just" – which verse is this?

A. 6:108
B. 49:12
C. 5:8
D. 4:32

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Verse 5:8 says justice must be given even to enemies – this is evidence for the right to justice.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q12. Verse 49:12, which forbids suspicion, spying and backbiting, protects which right?

A. The right to property
B. The right to life
C. The right to protection of dignity
D. The right to religious feelings

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: This verse confirms the right to protection from actions that damage one's reputation and dignity and from groundless accusations.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q13. "Men have a share of what they earn, and women have a share of what they earn" (4:32) – which right is this?

A. The right to life
B. Only the right to equality
C. The right to justice
D. The right to protection of property

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Verse 4:32 confirms that each person is the owner of the property he or she earns.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q14. What reason does verse 6:108 give for not insulting what followers of other religions worship?

A. They will start a war
B. They will then insult Allah in hostility, without knowledge
C. It wastes time
D. It is illegal

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Respecting others' religious feelings also protects the honour of Allah – this is the right to protection of religious feelings.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q15. In this chapter, the saying "Even if my daughter Fatimah stole, I would not hesitate to cut off her hand" is evidence for which right?

A. The right to justice
B. The right to property
C. Religious feelings
D. Individual freedom

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Everyone is equal before the law; there is no exception even for the Prophet's daughter – this is equal justice.

Difficulty: Easy
