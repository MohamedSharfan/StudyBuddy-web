# Grade 10 – Chapter 26: Human Rights – Flashcards

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_26/Flashcards.md` (Tamil), cards 1–13 in the same order.

---

Flashcard 1

Front: From where does Islam take human rights?

Back: From the Quran and the Sunnah.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 26 | Topic: Introduction

---

Flashcard 2

Front: Birthright

Back: A right given by Allah that no one can deny or take away.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 26 | Topic: Introduction

---

Flashcard 3

Front: What are three weaknesses of rights made by human beings?

Back: They change with time and place; they can be taken away; they exist only on paper.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 26 | Topic: Comparison

---

Flashcard 4

Front: The Charter of Madinah

Back: The agreement the Prophet ﷺ made with the people of Madinah at the start of the hijrah – the first declaration of human rights.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 26 | Topic: Historical charters

---

Flashcard 5

Front: The Magna Carta

Back: The Great Charter between King John and the people, in England in 1215.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 26 | Topic: Historical charters

---

Flashcard 6

Front: Who was Dr C. G. Weeramantry?

Back: A former judge; author of "Islamic Jurisprudence: An International Perspective".

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 26 | Topic: Historical charters

---

Flashcard 7

Front: What are the seven human rights given by Islam?

Back: Life, equality, individual freedom, justice, dignity, property, protection of religious feelings.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 26 | Topic: Seven rights

---

Flashcard 8

Front: Which two verses support the right to life?

Back: 6:151 (do not kill without due process), 5:32 (killing one is like killing all mankind).

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 26 | Topic: Right to life

---

Flashcard 9

Front: لا إكراه في الدين (La ikraha fid-din)

Back: There is no compulsion in religion (2:256) – freedom of religion.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 26 | Topic: Individual freedom

---

Flashcard 10

Front: غيبة (Ghibah)

Back: Backbiting – verse 49:12 forbids it.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 26 | Topic: Dignity

---

Flashcard 11

Front: تجسس (Tajassus)

Back: Spying, prying into others' affairs – verse 49:12 forbids it.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 26 | Topic: Dignity

---

Flashcard 12

Front: What is the essence of the hadith in Bayhaqi on equality?

Back: An Arab is not better than a non-Arab, nor a white person better than a black person; taqwa alone gives rank.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 26 | Topic: Equality

---

Flashcard 13

Front: 6:108 – the command about religious feelings?

Back: Do not insult what others worship, lest they insult Allah out of ignorance.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 26 | Topic: Religious feelings
