# Grade 10 – Chapter 25: Let Us Keep the Limits of Ajnabi and Mahram – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_25/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q13 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. Which communal duty does the textbook say would become impossible to carry out if a person lived alone?

A. Fard 'ayn
B. Mubah
C. Sunnah
D. Fard kifayah

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Fard kifayah is a duty to be carried out by the community; it is not possible for someone living alone. So the limits to keep while living in society become necessary.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q2. What is the linguistic meaning of the word "mahram"?

A. A stranger
B. A relative
C. Haram (forbidden)
D. A friend

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Linguistically, mahram carries the meaning of "haram"; technically it refers to one whom it is permanently forbidden to marry.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q3. Into how many kinds are mahram relationships divided in the textbook?

A. Two
B. Four
C. Three – family relationship, nursing relationship, marriage relationship
D. Five

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Permanent prohibition of marriage arises for three reasons: blood (family) relationship, nursing relationship and marriage relationship.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q4. Which of the following is a man's mahram because of "marriage relationship"?

A. Father's sister
B. Wife's mother
C. Nursing mother
D. Sister's daughter

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Mahrams through marriage: wife's mother, wife's daughter, son's wife, father's wife. The father's sister and sister's daughter are family relationships; the nursing mother is a nursing relationship.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q5. To which kind of mahram does "the nursing mother's sister" belong?

A. Family relationship
B. Ajnabi
C. Marriage relationship
D. Nursing relationship

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Relationships that arise through breastfeeding are nursing relationships; the nursing mother's sister is one of them.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q6. Which of the following is NOT a man's mahram (i.e. is ajnabi)?

A. Brother's daughter
B. Father's brother's daughter
C. Son's wife
D. Mother's sister

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: The father's brother's daughter (a cousin) is not in the textbook's list of mahrams, so she is ajnabi. The other three are in the mahram list.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q7. Which of the following is NOT a meaning of the word "ajnabi"?

A. One whom it is permanently forbidden to marry
B. A newcomer
C. A non-relative
D. A stranger

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: One whom it is permanently forbidden to marry is a mahram. Everyone outside the category of mahram is ajnabi – a stranger, a newcomer, a non-relative, an unconnected person.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q8. Under the limit of "lowering the gaze", which of the following is NOT something to avoid?

A. Looking at parts of the body that are 'awrah
B. Looking respectfully while speaking when there is a need
C. Staring continuously without reason
D. Looking with enjoyment

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: A respectful look when needed is not forbidden. Looking at the 'awrah, looking with enjoyment and staring without reason are what must be avoided.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q9. Which of the following is NOT one of the three eyes that will not see Hell?

A. An eye that stayed awake in Allah's path
B. An eye that wept out of fear of Allah
C. An eye that read books for a long time
D. An eye that kept itself from looking at what Allah has forbidden

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: The three eyes in the textbook: one that stayed awake in Allah's path, one that wept from fear of Allah, one that avoided looking at what is forbidden.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q10. What is the correct ruling on wearing perfume and heavily decorated clothes?

A. Always forbidden
B. Forbidden only on Eid days
C. Always permitted
D. Not forbidden at home; forbidden when going out because they can lead to social corruption

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: These are forbidden when going out, so as to keep away from what arouses men's desires; they are permitted at home.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q11. According to the hadith in Bazzar, what happens when a man and a woman who are strangers to each other are alone together?

A. Satan is present as the third with them
B. Angels protect them
C. There is no problem
D. They must get married

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Being alone together leads to evil thoughts, so situations where a man and a woman are alone together must be avoided.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q12. What is the essence of the sixth limit, about meetings between men and women?

A. Meetings should be arranged only with the good and the needs of society in mind
B. All meetings are completely forbidden
C. There are no restrictions
D. They may meet only at night

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Meetings that take a woman away from her nature, give her a bad name or remove her from her basic role bring ruin to the whole society; only meetings that serve a need and the common good are acceptable.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q13. For what reason is "the father's wife" (who is not one's own mother) a man's mahram?

A. Marriage relationship
B. Nursing relationship
C. Blood relationship
D. She is not a mahram

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: The father's wife is a permanent mahram because of the marriage relationship. Islam forbade the Jahiliyyah custom of a son marrying his father's wife.

Difficulty: Hard
