### A1. 2016 – Islam I, Q6

Question ID: P2016-I-Q06  
Year: 2016  
Paper and question: Islam I, Q6  
Marks: not stated on the paper (Islam I multiple-choice question)  
Source: OL_2016_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

Which of the following is an 'ajnabi' (non-mahram)?

- **A.** the wife's mother
- **B.** the foster mother (wet-nurse)
- **C.** the son's wife
- **D.** the paternal aunt's daughter

**Correct answer:** D – the paternal aunt's daughter *(option (4) on the original paper)*

**Explanation:** The wife's mother and the son's wife (relationship by marriage) and the foster mother (relationship by breastfeeding) are mahrams. The paternal aunt's daughter is not in the mahram list – she is ajnabi (marriage is permitted).

### B1. 2016 – Islam II, Q6 (ii), (iii)

Question ID: P2016-II-Q6.2  
Year: 2016  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q6 (ii), (iii)  
Marks: 10 marks for the whole of Q6  
Source: OL_2016_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(ii) Explain the mahrams who arise through family relationship.
(iii) Describe the rules to be observed when talking with ajnabis (non-mahrams).

**Suggested answer:**

(ii) Those a man may never marry because of family (blood) relationship:
- mother, mother's mother (upwards)
- daughter, son's / daughter's daughter (downwards)
- sister (full, paternal or maternal)
- father's sister (paternal aunt), mother's sister (maternal aunt)
- brother's daughter, sister's daughter
These are mahrams; marriage with them is haram.

(iii) 1. Lowering the gaze – not looking at the 'awrah, not looking with desire, not looking continuously without need.
2. Dignified clothing that fully covers the 'awrah.
3. Keeping Islamic manners in walking, dress and speech.
4. Avoiding perfume and adornment that arouse feelings.
5. Avoiding being alone together – "the devil is the third" (Bazzar).
6. Meetings only for social benefit and need.

**Answer explanation:** Question (ii) is about "family relationship"; mahrams by breastfeeding and by marriage need not be included here.

**Marking guidance (suggested only – not an official marking scheme):** No official marking scheme.

**Answer status:** Suggested – not an official answer (no official marking scheme was supplied); based on the Grade 10/11 textbook content

### B2. 2019 – Islam II, Q7 (iii)

Question ID: P2019-II-Q7.3  
Year: 2019  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q7 (iii)  
Marks: 10 marks (short notes on two topics)  
Source: OL_2019_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(iii) Keeping limits with ajnabis

**Suggested answer:**

Ajnabi = a non-mahram (someone one is permitted to marry). Limits to be kept while living together in society:
1. Lowering the gaze – avoiding looking at the 'awrah, looking with desire, and looking continuously without need; "the eye that did not look at what Allah forbade will not see the Fire".
2. Dignified clothing that fully covers the 'awrah.
3. Islamic manners in walking, dress, behaviour and speech.
4. Avoiding, in public, perfume and adornment that arouse feelings.
5. Avoiding being alone together – "the devil is the third" (Bazzar).
6. Meetings only for social benefit and need.

**Answer explanation:** One may conclude that these limits keep relationships healthy and prevent social decay.
