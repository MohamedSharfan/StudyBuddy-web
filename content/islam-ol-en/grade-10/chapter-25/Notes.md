# Grade 10 – Chapter 25: Let Us Keep the Limits of Ajnabi and Mahram

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_25/Notes.md` (Tamil). English rendering.

## 🎯 What you will learn
- Why the rules of ajnabi and mahram are needed
- What a mahram is, and its three kinds
- What an ajnabi is
- Six limits to keep when dealing with ajnabi (non-mahram) men

---

## 1. Why these limits?
- A human being **cannot live alone**; by nature he lives **in community**.
- If he lived alone he could not carry out the duties Islam has placed on him; carrying out **fard kifayah** (communal obligations) would become impossible.
- So we must live together with society and strengthen our relationships.
- The rules of ajnabi and mahram are needed **to explain the limits** to keep while living in this way.

---

## 2. What is a mahram (محرم)? ⭐
- **Linguistically:** carries the meaning of "**haram**" (forbidden).
- **Technically:** a man or woman whom a woman or man is **permanently forbidden to marry**, **because of family relationship or some other reason**.

### Women a man is forbidden to marry (his mahrams) – three kinds

#### (1) Family (blood) relationship
- **Mother**, mother's mother
- **Daughter**, son's daughter, daughter's daughter
- **Sister** (full sister, paternal half-sister, maternal half-sister)
- **Father's sister** (paternal aunt)
- **Mother's sister** (maternal aunt)
- **Brother's daughter** (including the daughter of a paternal or maternal half-brother)
- **Sister's daughter** (including the daughter of a paternal or maternal half-sister)

#### (2) Relationship through nursing (breastfeeding)
- **The nursing mother**, her mothers, and the mother of the (nursing) father
- A **daughter** through nursing, her daughter, a son's daughter
- A **sister** through nursing (maternal or paternal)
- **The nursing mother's sister**
- **The sister of the father** through nursing
- **The daughter of a brother** through nursing
- **The daughter of a sister** through nursing

#### (3) Relationship through marriage
- **The wife's mother** (mother-in-law)
- **The wife's daughter**
- **The son's wife** (daughter-in-law)
- **The father's wife**

💡 **Memory tip:** mahram = those one is permanently forbidden to marry for three reasons: **blood + nursing + marriage**.

---

## 3. What is an ajnabi (أجنبي)?
- **Everyone who does not fall under the category of mahram** is ajnabi.
- **Meanings:** a stranger, a newcomer, a non-relative, an unconnected person.
- That is, **those whom one is permitted to marry.**

---

## 4. Six limits to keep when dealing with ajnabi men ⭐

### (1) Lowering the gaze
Things to avoid:
- Looking at parts of the body that are **'awrah**
- Looking **with enjoyment (desire)**
- **Staring continuously** without reason or need

> 📖 **Three eyes will not see Hell (meaning):**
> 1. An eye that **stayed awake in Allah's path**
> 2. An eye that **wept out of fear of Allah**
> 3. An eye that **kept itself from looking at what Allah has forbidden**

> 📌 **'Awrah (عورة)** = the parts of the body that must be covered.

### (2) Modest clothing
- A woman should wear clothing that **fully covers the 'awrah** and is **respectable** in style.

### (3) Islamic manners
- Keeping Islamic manners in **walk, dress, conduct and speech**.

### (4) Keeping away from what arouses desire
- **Wearing perfume and heavily decorated clothes** – **not forbidden** while **at home**.
- But they are **forbidden when going out**, because they can lead to social corruption.

### (5) Avoiding being alone together
> 📖 **Hadith (Bazzar) – meaning:** "When one of you is alone with a woman who is a stranger to him, **Satan is present as the third** with them."

### (6) Meetings that consider the good and the needs of society
- Events where the two groups meet should be arranged only with **the good and the needs of society** in mind.
- Meetings between men and women that **take a woman away from her nature**, **bring her a bad name**, or **remove her from her basic role** bring **ruin not only to the two of them but to the whole of society**.

---

## 5. Conclusion
By keeping these limits that Islam has laid down for our own good, we should **bring our relationships to life**; this is what keeps **hearts alive**.

---

## ✅ Quick revision
- Mahram = (linguistic) haram; one whom marriage is permanently forbidden.
- Mahram, 3 kinds: family relationship, nursing relationship, marriage relationship.
- Mahrams through marriage: wife's mother, wife's daughter, son's wife, father's wife.
- Ajnabi = one who is not a mahram (a stranger, a non-relative).
- 6 limits: lowering the gaze, modest clothing, Islamic manners, avoiding what arouses desire, avoiding being alone, meetings for the good of society.
- 3 eyes that will not see Hell: one that kept awake, one that wept, one that did not look at what is forbidden.
- When alone together, the third is Satan (Bazzar).
