# Grade 10 – Chapter 25: Let Us Keep the Limits of Ajnabi and Mahram – Flashcards

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_25/Flashcards.md` (Tamil), cards 1–12 in the same order.

---

Flashcard 1

Front: محرم (Mahram)

Back: One whom it is permanently forbidden to marry because of family relationship or another reason. Linguistic meaning: haram (forbidden).

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 25 | Topic: Mahram

---

Flashcard 2

Front: أجنبي (Ajnabi)

Back: One who is not a mahram – a stranger, a newcomer, a non-relative, an unconnected person.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 25 | Topic: Ajnabi

---

Flashcard 3

Front: What are the three kinds of mahram relationship?

Back: Family (blood) relationship, nursing relationship, marriage relationship.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 25 | Topic: Mahram

---

Flashcard 4

Front: Which four become a man's mahram through marriage?

Back: Wife's mother, wife's daughter, son's wife, father's wife.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 25 | Topic: Mahram

---

Flashcard 5

Front: Five mahrams of a man through family relationship (in short)?

Back: Mother, daughter, sister, father's / mother's sister, brother's / sister's daughter.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 25 | Topic: Mahram

---

Flashcard 6

Front: Nursing relationship (رضاعة – rada'ah)

Back: A relationship formed by breastfeeding – the nursing mother, her sister, a sister through nursing and the like are mahram.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 25 | Topic: Mahram

---

Flashcard 7

Front: عورة ('Awrah)

Back: The parts of the body that must be covered.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 25 | Topic: Key terms

---

Flashcard 8

Front: What are the six limits to keep when dealing with an ajnabi?

Back: Lowering the gaze, clothing that covers the 'awrah, Islamic manners, avoiding what arouses desire, avoiding being alone, meetings for the good of society.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 25 | Topic: Six limits

---

Flashcard 9

Front: Which three eyes will not see Hell?

Back: An eye that stayed awake in Allah's path, an eye that wept from fear of Him, an eye that avoided looking at what is forbidden.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 25 | Topic: Lowering the gaze

---

Flashcard 10

Front: Complete: "When one is alone with a woman who is a stranger, the third…"

Back: …is Satan (Bazzar).

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 25 | Topic: Avoiding being alone

---

Flashcard 11

Front: Perfume and decorated clothes – when are they forbidden?

Back: When going out (there is no prohibition at home).

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 25 | Topic: Six limits

---

Flashcard 12

Front: What is lost if a person lives alone?

Back: He cannot carry out the duties Islam has placed on him, including fard kifayah.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 25 | Topic: Why the limits
