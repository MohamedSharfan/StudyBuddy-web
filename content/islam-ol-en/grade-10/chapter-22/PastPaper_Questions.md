### B2. 2017 – Islam II, Q7 (ii)

Question ID: P2017-II-Q7.2  
Year: 2017  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q7 (ii)  
Marks: 10 marks (short notes on two topics)  
Source: OL_2017_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(ii) The importance of work

**Suggested answer:**

- Islam values work highly; it condemns living on the work of others and begging ("whoever keeps begging will come on the Day of Judgement with no flesh on his face" – Bukhari, Muslim).
- "Allah loves the believer who works at a trade" (Tabarani).
Three reasons:
1. Work is an effort to seek Allah's bounty (62:10; 67:15).
2. Work is a duty – "whatever is necessary to fulfil a duty is itself a duty".
3. Work is worship – "whoever reaches the evening tired from halal work is forgiven" (Ibn Majah).

**Answer explanation:** Explain the reasons with evidence.

### B3. 2019 – Islam II, Q2 (iii)

Question ID: P2019-II-Q2.2  
Year: 2019  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q2 (iii)  
Marks: 10 marks for the whole of Q2  
Source: OL_2019_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(iii) Explain five limits of the Shari'ah to be observed when earning a living.

**Suggested answer:**

1. It must not involve haram goods – e.g. alcohol, pork, stolen goods.
2. It must not be through forbidden methods – e.g. robbery, interest, bribery.
3. It must not cause harm or deception to people – e.g. hoarding, adulteration, breaking promises.
4. It must not be a method that breaks public laws – e.g. the black market, smuggling.
5. It must not be contrary to good moral values – e.g. lying, greed, fraud, selfishness.

**Answer explanation:** As the question says "explain", give an example for each limit.

### B4. 2021 (2022) – Islam II, Q4 (ii), (iii)

Question ID: P2021_2022-II-Q4.2  
Year: 2021 (2022)  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q4 (ii), (iii)  
Marks: 10 marks for the whole of Q4  
Source: OL_2021_2022_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(ii) Briefly explain three things a Muslim must avoid when engaged in earning a living.
(iii) Clarify the importance of work in Islam.

**Suggested answer:**

(ii) Any three of the following:
1. Work involving haram goods (alcohol, pork, stolen goods).
2. Haram methods (robbery, interest, bribery).
3. Causing harm and deception to people (hoarding, adulteration, breaking promises).
4. Breaking public laws (the black market, smuggling).
5. Things contrary to moral values (lying, greed, fraud, selfishness).

(iii)
1. Work is an effort to seek Allah's bounty – when the prayer is over, spread out in the land and seek Allah's bounty (62:10); He made the earth easy for you (67:15).
2. Work is a duty – "whatever is necessary to fulfil a duty is itself a duty"; wealth is needed for zakat, Hajj and supporting the family.
3. Work is worship – whoever reaches the evening tired from halal work is forgiven (Ibn Majah); a concession in prayer for those who work (73:20).
4. Begging is condemned – whoever keeps begging will come on the Day of Judgement with no flesh on his face (Bukhari, Muslim).
5. "Allah loves the believer who works at a trade" (Tabarani); the one who works is better than a worshipper who lives on others' work.

**Answer explanation:** In (ii) give an example for each; in (iii) clarify with evidence.

**Marking guidance (suggested only – not an official marking scheme):** No official marking scheme.
