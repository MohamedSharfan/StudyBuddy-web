# Grade 10 – Chapter 22: The Importance of Work

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_22/Notes.md` (Tamil). English rendering.

## 🎯 What you will learn
- Why Islam values work so highly
- The Islamic position on begging
- Three reasons work is important
- What must be observed for earning to become worship
- The limits of the Shari'ah to keep in earning
- Imam al-Mawardi's three reasons why people neglect work

---

## 1. The high status of work in Islam
- **Working** to gather the wealth needed for one's life is **a noble thing.**
- A person who does not meet his own needs but **lives off other people's work** is **low** in the eyes of Islam.

### An incident (Adab ad-Dunya wad-Din – Imam al-Mawardi)
- The Companions praised a man who was always **praying and making dhikr** on a journey.
- The Prophet ﷺ: "Who **grazed his camel and cooked his food**?" – "All of us."
- The Prophet ﷺ: "Then **all of you are better than him.**"

💡 **Lesson:** those who work are better even than **worshippers** who live off other people's work.

### Begging is condemned
- When a human being, Allah's honoured creation, depends on others' work, **his dignity is lessened.**
- **Hadith (Bukhari, Muslim) – meaning:** "A person who keeps on begging will come on the Day of Judgement **with no flesh on his face**."

### The worker's hand
- The Prophet ﷺ **held for a long time** the hand of a labourer that had become hard from work.
- **Hadith (Tabarani) – meaning:** "Indeed, Allah **loves the believer who works at a trade.**"

---

## 2. Three reasons work is important ⭐

### Reason 1: Work is seeking Allah's bounty
- Everything needed for human livelihood has been placed **on this earth**; but it does not reach a person **unless he makes an effort**.

| Verse | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **7:10** | We established you on earth and placed in it the means of living; little are you grateful |
| **15:19–20** | We spread out the earth, set mountains firm in it, made plants grow, and provided in it the means of living for you and for other creatures |
| **67:15** | He made the earth easy for you; so walk in its paths and eat of His provision |
| **62:10** | When the (Jumu'ah) prayer is over, **spread through the land and seek Allah's bounty** |

### Reason 2: Work is a duty on a Muslim
This can be seen **in two ways**:
1. **A rule of the Shari'ah:** "**That without which a duty cannot be fulfilled is itself a duty.**" Duties such as zakat, hajj and supporting the family need wealth → so seeking wealth is also a duty.
2. **The view of the scholars of fiqh:** Allah's **commands point to duties.** The command "seek Allah's bounty" **(62:10)** makes earning a duty.

### Reason 3: Work is worship
- Work done according to Islamic guidance **becomes worship.**
- **Hadith (Ibn Majah) – meaning:** "Whoever reaches the evening **tired from lawful (halal) work** reaches that evening **with his sins forgiven**."
- Because work is regarded as worship, those earning a living are **allowed to recite short surahs** in prayer:
  > 📖 **(73:20) – meaning:** Recite as much of the Quran as is easy; He knows that some of you are sick, some travel through the land seeking Allah's bounty, and some fight in Allah's path.

---

## 3. Five guidelines for earning to become worship ⭐
1. **A pure intention**
2. **Not getting in the way of the basic acts of worship**
3. **Being permitted by the Shari'ah**
4. **Not crossing the limits of the Shari'ah**
5. **Doing it well and in an orderly way**

> 💡 These match the "conditions for everyday actions to become 'ibadah" learned in Chapter 6 (Ibadah).

---

## 4. The limits of the Shari'ah in earning ⭐

| Limit | Examples |
|---|---|
| 1. It must not involve **a haram item** | Wine, pork, stolen goods |
| 2. It must not be by **forbidden methods** | Robbery, interest, bribery |
| 3. It must not **harm or deceive people** | Hoarding, adulteration, breaking promises |
| 4. It must not **break the general laws** | The black market, smuggling |
| 5. It must not **go against moral values** | Lying, greed, fraud, selfishness |

---

## 5. Neglecting work – Imam al-Mawardi's three reasons ⭐

### (1) Laziness
- Islam **hates laziness**; a lazy person will always be **poor**.
- The Prophet's ﷺ supplication (meaning): "O Allah! Protect me from **laziness and helplessness**."
- **(Ahmad) – meaning:** "O Allah! Bless my ummah **in its early mornings**." (So that his ummah would rise early and be active.)

### (2) A wrong idea of tawakkul
- **True tawakkul:** engaging in a task **with full effort**, and then entrusting **the final result** to Allah.
- **Wrong understanding:** taking no interest in effort and only entrusting the result to Allah – this is **not welcome in Islam.**

### (3) Detachment and contentment
- Some people, **fearing love of the world**, avoid working too much and are content with a small income – **Islam welcomes this**; many Companions lived like that.
- **Hadith (narrated by Abu ad-Darda' (RA)) – meaning:** every day when the sun rises, two angels call out: "O people! Hurry to your Lord. **A little that is sufficient is better than much that distracts.**" (Everything except humans and jinn hears this.)

### ⚖️ Balance
- At the same time, some Companions gained **much good through their wealth**: **Abu Bakr (RA), Khadijah (RA), 'Uthman (RA), 'Abd ar-Rahman ibn 'Awf (RA).**
- Seeing this, other Companions complained: "O Messenger of Allah! **The wealthy have gone ahead of us!**"
- 👉 So let us learn Islamic guidance, **work enthusiastically and spend in good ways**.

## ✅ Quick revision
- One who works is better than a worshipper who lives off others' work (Imam al-Mawardi – Adab ad-Dunya wad-Din).
- One who keeps begging will come on the Day of Judgement with no flesh on his face (Bukhari, Muslim).
- Allah loves the believer who works at a trade (Tabarani).
- 3 reasons: seeking Allah's bounty (62:10), a duty, worship (Ibn Majah; 73:20).
- 5 limits: no haram item, no haram method, no harm to people, no breaking general law, no breaking moral values.
- Imam al-Mawardi: laziness, a wrong idea of tawakkul, detachment and contentment.
