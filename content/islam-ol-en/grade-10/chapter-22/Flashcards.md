# Grade 10 – Chapter 22: The Importance of Work – Flashcards

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_22/Flashcards.md` (Tamil), cards 1–13 in the same order.

---

Flashcard 1

Front: Who is low in the eyes of Islam?

Back: One who does not meet his own needs but lives off other people's work.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 22 | Topic: The status of work

---

Flashcard 2

Front: أدب الدنيا والدين (Adab ad-Dunya wad-Din)

Back: A book by Imam al-Mawardi – it contains the incident showing that workers are better than the worshipper.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 22 | Topic: The status of work

---

Flashcard 3

Front: What are the three reasons work is important?

Back: 1) It is seeking Allah's bounty 2) It is a duty on a Muslim 3) It is worship.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 22 | Topic: Reasons

---

Flashcard 4

Front: What is the meaning of verse 67:15?

Back: He made the earth easy for you; walk in its paths and eat of His provision.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 22 | Topic: Reasons

---

Flashcard 5

Front: Complete: "That without which a duty cannot be fulfilled…"

Back: …is itself a duty (a rule of the Shari'ah).

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 22 | Topic: Reasons

---

Flashcard 6

Front: What are the five guidelines for earning to become worship?

Back: Pure intention, no obstacle to worship, Shari'ah permission, not crossing the limits, doing it well and in order.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 22 | Topic: Earning as worship

---

Flashcard 7

Front: What are the five Shari'ah limits to keep in earning?

Back: No haram item; no haram method; no harm to people; no breaking the general laws; no breaking moral values.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 22 | Topic: Limits of the Shari'ah

---

Flashcard 8

Front: Give three examples of haram methods.

Back: Robbery, interest, bribery.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 22 | Topic: Limits of the Shari'ah

---

Flashcard 9

Front: What goes against moral values – examples?

Back: Lying, greed, fraud, selfishness.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 22 | Topic: Limits of the Shari'ah

---

Flashcard 10

Front: What three reasons does Imam al-Mawardi give for neglecting work?

Back: Laziness, a wrong idea of tawakkul, detachment and contentment.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 22 | Topic: Neglecting work

---

Flashcard 11

Front: توكل (Tawakkul)

Back: Working with full effort and entrusting the final result to Allah.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 22 | Topic: Neglecting work

---

Flashcard 12

Back: (meaning) O Allah! Protect me from laziness and helplessness.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 22 | Topic: Neglecting work
