# Grade 10 – Chapter 22: The Importance of Work – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_22/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q16 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. What did the Prophet ﷺ say about those who grazed the camel and cooked the food of the man who was always praying on the journey?

A. They were lower than that man
B. They should leave their work and pray
C. All of them were better than that man
D. They would get no reward

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: This incident shows that those who work are better than someone who worships while depending on others' work (Imam al-Mawardi – Adab ad-Dunya wad-Din).

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. How did the Prophet ﷺ say a person who keeps on begging will come in the Hereafter (Bukhari, Muslim)?

A. Without hands
B. With no flesh on his face
C. Without eyes
D. Without legs

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Continual begging lessens a person's dignity; in the Hereafter he will come with no flesh on his face.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q3. In which collection is the hadith "Indeed, Allah loves the believer who works at a trade" found?

A. Tabarani
B. Bukhari
C. Ibn Majah
D. Ahmad

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: This hadith, said while the Prophet ﷺ held for a long time the hand of a labourer made hard by work, is found in Tabarani.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q4. "When the (Jumu'ah) prayer is over, spread through the land and seek Allah's bounty" – which verse is this?

A. 67:15
B. 62:10
C. 7:10
D. 73:20

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Verse 62:10 shows that work is seeking Allah's bounty; according to the scholars of fiqh, this command makes earning a duty.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q5. According to the rule "That without which a duty cannot be fulfilled is itself a duty", why does seeking wealth become a duty?

A. Because wealth is needed to fulfil duties such as zakat, hajj and supporting the family
B. Because having a lot of wealth is a matter of pride
C. Because trading is sunnah
D. Because government tax must be paid

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Duties such as zakat, hajj and supporting the family are linked to wealth. Without wealth they cannot be fulfilled; so seeking wealth is also a duty.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q6. According to the hadith in Ibn Majah, what does a person gain who reaches the evening tired from lawful work?

A. Double wealth
B. People's praise
C. A long life
D. He reaches that evening with his sins forgiven

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: This hadith is evidence that lawful work becomes worship.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q7. Which verse allows those earning a living to recite short surahs in prayer?

A. 7:10
B. 62:10
C. 15:19–20
D. 73:20

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: 73:20 – Allah knows that some travel through the land seeking His bounty; so recite as much as is easy. This allowance is given because work is regarded as worship.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q8. Which of the following is NOT one of the guidelines for earning to become worship?

A. A pure intention
B. Making a large profit
C. Being permitted by the Shari'ah
D. Doing it well and in an orderly way

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: The five guidelines: pure intention, not getting in the way of basic worship, Shari'ah permission, not crossing the limits, doing it well and in order. A large profit is not a condition.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q9. "Hoarding, adulteration, breaking promises" break which limit of the Shari'ah?

A. Involving a haram item
B. A haram method
C. Breaking the general laws
D. Causing harm and deception to people

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Hoarding, adulteration and breaking promises cause harm and deception to people.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q10. "The black market and smuggling" are examples of which limit?

A. Going against moral values
B. A haram item
C. A method that breaks the general laws
D. A haram method

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: The black market and smuggling are methods that break the country's general laws.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q11. Which of the following pairs is correct?

A. Haram item – stolen goods
B. Haram method – pork
C. Haram item – interest
D. Breaking moral values – smuggling

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Haram items: wine, pork, stolen goods. Haram methods: robbery, interest, bribery. Breaking moral values: lying, greed, fraud, selfishness.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q12. Which scholar gave the reasons why people neglect work?

A. Imam ash-Shafi'i
B. Imam al-Mawardi
C. Imam al-Ghazali
D. Imam al-Bukhari

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Imam al-Mawardi gives three reasons: laziness, a wrong idea of tawakkul, and detachment and contentment.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q13. What is true tawakkul?

A. Engaging in a task with full effort and then entrusting the final result to Allah
B. Leaving everything to Allah without making any effort
C. Asking others for help
D. Blaming fate

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Leaving only the result to Allah without effort is a wrong understanding of tawakkul; it is not welcome in Islam.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q14. The Prophet ﷺ prayed for his ummah: "O Allah! Bless my ummah …" (Ahmad). Fill in the blank.

A. at night
B. in the evenings
C. in its early mornings
D. on Fridays

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: He asked for blessing in the early morning so that his ummah would rise early and be active; this is the opposite of laziness.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q15. "A little that is sufficient is better than much that distracts" – who narrated this hadith?

A. Abu Hurayrah (RA)
B. 'A'ishah (RA)
C. Anas (RA)
D. Abu ad-Darda' (RA)

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: This hadith, narrated by Abu ad-Darda' (RA), tells of the call made by two angels when the sun rises; it encourages detachment and contentment.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q16. Which of the following is NOT among the Companions the textbook mentions as gaining much good through their wealth?

A. Abu Bakr (RA)
B. 'Uthman (RA)
C. Abu ad-Darda' (RA)
D. 'Abd ar-Rahman ibn 'Awf (RA)

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Abu Bakr, Khadijah, 'Uthman and 'Abd ar-Rahman ibn 'Awf (RA) are the examples. Abu ad-Darda' (RA) narrated the hadith about detachment.

Difficulty: Medium
