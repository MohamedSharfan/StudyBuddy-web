---

## 1. What is the Sunnah?
- In Islam, after the Quran, **the second source is the Sunnah.**

> 📖 **Definition:** The Sunnah is **the life and the words** of the Prophet ﷺ. The Sunnah is also called **hadith**.

---

## 2. Evidence from the Quran ⭐

| Verse | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **3:31** – قُلْ إِنْ كُنْتُمْ تُحِبُّوْنَ اللهَ فَاتَّبِعُوْنِي | If you love Allah, **follow me**; Allah will love you and forgive your sins |
| **4:80** – مَنْ يُطِعِ الرَّسُوْلَ فَقَدْ أَطَاعَ اللهَ | Whoever obeys the Messenger **has obeyed Allah** |
| **59:7** – وَمَا آتَاكُمُ الرَّسُوْلُ فَخُذُوْهُ وَمَا نَهَاكُمْ عَنْهُ فَانْتَهُوْا | **Take** whatever the Messenger gives you, and **keep away** from whatever he forbids you |
| **62:2** | The Messenger recites the verses, purifies the people, and teaches **the Book and wisdom (hikmah)** |

### "Hikmah" = the Sunnah
- In many places the Quran joins the word **hikmah (wisdom)** with the word **kitab (the Book)**.
- **Imam ash-Shafi'i (RH):** here "wisdom" refers to **the Sunnah**.
- His evidence – the hadith (meaning): "**I have been given the Quran, and something like it along with it.**"

---

## 3. Evidence from the Sunnah ⭐

| Meaning of the hadith | Source |
|---|---|
| My Lord has commanded me to teach you what you do not know | Muslim |
| **"I am leaving among you two things; you will not go astray as long as you hold to them: the Quran and my Sunnah"** | Muwatta' |
| Whoever obeys me will enter Paradise; whoever disobeys me has rejected (the truth) | Bayhaqi |

👉 This is why we call the Quran **wahy matluww** and the Sunnah **wahy ghayr matluww**.

---

## 4. The relationship between the Quran and the Sunnah ⭐
- The two are **interdependent** parts of one whole.
- 💡 **Comparison:** the relationship between the Quran and the Sunnah is like the relationship between **a book and the light that helps you read it in the dark**.
- So it is impossible to **understand, practise or derive rulings from** the Quran while ignoring the Sunnah.

---

## 5. The Quran cannot be understood without the Sunnah – examples

### (1) Prayer
- The Quran **commands** us to pray.
- How should we pray? The Sunnah explains **the times, the method and the rules**.

### (2) The incident of 'Imran ibn Husayn (RA)
- A man said: "Tell us **nothing except the Quran**."
- 'Imran (RA) asked him: does the Quran say
  - that **Zuhr has four rak'ahs**?
  - that **tawaf around the Ka'bah is seven times**?
  - that **sa'y between Safa and Marwah is seven times**?
- 👉 All of these are known **only through the Sunnah**.

### (3) The incident of 'Abdullah ibn 'Umar (RA) (Muwatta')
- A man asked: "Is the **shortening and combining (qasr and jam')** of prayers mentioned in the Quran?"
- Answer: "Brother! Allah sent the Prophet ﷺ as a messenger; **we do as he did.**"

### (4) The punishment for theft
- The Quran: if theft is proven, **the hand is to be cut off**.
- Explanations given by the Sunnah:
  - **Children and the mentally ill** are exempt
  - The stolen item must be worth **more than the value of a shield**
  - The hand is cut **only up to the wrist**
  - This punishment cannot be given for stealing **food**

### (5) Others
- **The funeral prayer (salat al-janazah), zakat al-fitr, and the rules of ihram** are among the things explained only through the Sunnah.

---

## 6. Efforts to preserve the Sunnah
**The Companions, the Tabi'un** and those who came after them:
1. **Memorised** the hadiths **without mistakes.**
2. Those who could write **wrote them down and preserved them.**
3. **Handed over and reported** the hadiths they knew **with witnesses.**
4. **Put** the hadiths **into practice.**
5. Later scholars **travelled in person to find** those who knew hadiths.
6. Carried out **strict research** to separate **authentic hadiths from fabricated ones.**
7. **Compiled books of hadith.**
8. When problems arose, found **solutions** with the help of hadiths.

> 📌 **Tabi'un (تابعين)** = the next generation, who met the Companions and learned from them.

## ✅ Quick revision
- Sunnah = the life and words of the Prophet ﷺ (= hadith); the 2nd source.
- Quranic evidence: 3:31, 4:80, 59:7, 62:2.
- Hikmah = the Sunnah – Imam ash-Shafi'i (RH).
- "The Quran and the Sunnah – hold to these two and you will not go astray" (Muwatta').
- Quran = the book; Sunnah = the light that helps you read it.
- Examples: the method of prayer, Zuhr 4 rak'ahs, tawaf 7, sa'y 7, qasr and jam', the rules of the punishment for theft.
