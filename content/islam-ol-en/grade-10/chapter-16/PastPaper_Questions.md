## Part B: Structured and Essay Questions (Islam II)

### B1. 2019 – Islam II, Q6 (iii)

Question ID: P2019-II-Q6.3  
Year: 2019  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q6 (iii)  
Marks: 10 marks for the whole of Q6  
Source: OL_2019_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(iii) Explain the importance of the Sunnah, with evidence from the Quran and the hadith.

**Suggested answer:**

Sunnah = the life and words of the Prophet ﷺ; the second source after the Quran.
Evidence from the Quran:
- 3:31 – If you love Allah, follow me.
- 4:80 – Whoever obeys the Messenger has obeyed Allah.
- 59:7 – Take what the Messenger gives you, and keep away from what he forbids you.
- 62:2 – He teaches them the Book and wisdom (hikmah = the Sunnah – Imam ash-Shafi'i).
Evidence from the hadith:
- "I leave two things; you will not go astray as long as you follow them: the Quran and my Sunnah" (Muwatta').
- "Whoever obeys me will enter Paradise; whoever disobeys me has refused" (Bayhaqi).
Importance: without the Sunnah the Quran cannot be understood or put into practice – the method of prayer, 4 rak'ahs for Zuhr, 7 circuits of tawaf, qasr and jam', the conditions of the punishment for theft are all known through the Sunnah.

**Answer explanation:** The comparison may be used: the Quran = the book; the Sunnah = the light that helps us read it.

### B2. 2022 (2023) – Islam II, Q7 (ii)

Question ID: P2022_2023-II-Q7.2  
Year: 2022 (2023)  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q7 (ii)  
Marks: 10 marks (short notes on two topics)  
Source: OL_2022_2023_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(ii) The importance of the Sunnah

**Suggested answer:**

- Sunnah = the sayings, actions and approvals of the Prophet ﷺ; the second main source of Islam; "wahy ghayr matluww".
- The Quran commands obedience to the Messenger as to Allah; take what the Messenger gives you and avoid what he forbids (59:7).
- "Kitab and hikmah" – hikmah = the Sunnah (Imam ash-Shafi'i).
- It explains the Quran: the method of prayer, Zuhr as 4 rak'ahs, 7 circuits of tawaf, 7 of sa'y, qasr and jam', the rules of the punishment for theft.
- It gives laws not mentioned in the Quran.
- The Prophet's ﷺ life is the example (33:21); following the Sunnah is the way to gain Allah's love (3:31).

**Answer explanation:** Write with evidence from the Quran together with examples.
