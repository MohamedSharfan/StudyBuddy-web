Flashcard 1

Front: سنة (Sunnah)

Back: The life and words of the Prophet ﷺ; the second source of Islam.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 16 | Topic: Definition

---

Flashcard 2

Front: حديث (Hadith)

Back: Another name for the Sunnah – a report of the Prophet's ﷺ words, actions or approval.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 16 | Topic: Definition

Flashcard 3

Front: كتاب / حكمة (Kitab / Hikmah)

Back: Kitab = the Book; hikmah = wisdom – according to Imam ash-Shafi'i, hikmah is the Sunnah.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 16 | Topic: Quranic evidence

Flashcard 5

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 16 | Topic: Quranic evidence

Flashcard 6

Front: "I am leaving two things" – what are they? Source?

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 16 | Topic: Evidence from the Sunnah

Flashcard 7

Front: What comparison describes the Quran–Sunnah relationship?

Flashcard 8

Front: Which three details did 'Imran ibn Husayn (RA) point out are not in the Quran?

Back: Zuhr has 4 rak'ahs; tawaf around the Ka'bah 7 times; sa'y between Safa and Marwah 7 times.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 16 | Topic: Examples

Flashcard 9

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 16 | Topic: Key terms

Flashcard 10

Front: سعي (Sa'y)

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 16 | Topic: Key terms

Flashcard 11

Front: What four explanations does the Sunnah give about the punishment for theft?

Back: Children and the mentally ill are exempt; the item must be worth more than a shield; the hand is cut only to the wrist; not for stealing food.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 16 | Topic: Examples

Flashcard 12

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 16 | Topic: Preserving the Sunnah

Flashcard 13

Back: The funeral prayer, zakat al-fitr, and the rules of ihram.
