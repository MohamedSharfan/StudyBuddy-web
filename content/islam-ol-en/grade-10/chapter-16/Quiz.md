# Grade 10 – Chapter 16: The Importance of the Sunnah – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_16/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q14 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. What is the second source of Islam?

A. Ijma'
B. Qiyas
C. The Sunnah
D. 'Urf

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: After the Quran, the second source is the Sunnah. Ijma', qiyas and the like are secondary sources.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. Which is the correct definition of the Sunnah?

A. The opinions of the Companions
B. The life and words of the Prophet ﷺ
C. The rulings of scholars
D. Books of Quranic commentary

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: The Sunnah is the life and the words of the Prophet ﷺ. It is also called "hadith".

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q3. "Whoever obeys the Messenger has certainly obeyed Allah" – which verse is this?

A. 3:31
B. 59:7
C. 4:80
D. 62:2

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: 4:80 – "Man yuti'ir-rasula faqad ata'allah." Since obeying the Messenger is obeying Allah, following the Sunnah is obligatory.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q4. "Take whatever the Messenger gives you, and keep away from whatever he forbids you" – which verse is this?

A. 59:7
B. 4:80
C. 3:31
D. 15:9

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Verse 59:7 says that the commands and prohibitions of the Prophet ﷺ must be accepted.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q5. Who said that "hikmah" (wisdom), mentioned with "kitab" in the Quran, refers to the Sunnah?

A. Imam Abu Hanifah (RH)
B. Imam Malik (RH)
C. Imam ash-Shafi'i (RH)
D. Imam al-Bukhari (RH)

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Taking as evidence the hadith "I have been given the Quran and something like it along with it", Imam ash-Shafi'i (RH) said that hikmah = the Sunnah.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q6. "I am leaving among you two things; you will not go astray as long as you hold to them" – what are the two things?

A. Prayer and zakat
B. The Quran and the Sunnah
C. Makkah and Madinah
D. The Companions and the Tabi'un

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: This hadith, found in the Muwatta', shows that the Quran and the Sunnah together give right guidance.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q7. To what does the textbook compare the relationship between the Quran and the Sunnah?

A. A tree and its branch
B. A book and the light that helps you read it in the dark
C. A mother and her child
D. Water and the sea

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Even if you have a book in the dark, you cannot read it without light. In the same way, the Quran cannot be understood or practised without the Sunnah.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q8. Who asked a man who said "Tell us nothing except the Quran" whether the Quran says that Zuhr has four rak'ahs, tawaf is seven times and sa'y is seven times?

A. 'Abdullah ibn 'Umar (RA)
B. 'Imran ibn Husayn (RA)
C. Abu Hurayrah (RA)
D. Ibn 'Abbas (RA)

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Through these questions 'Imran ibn Husayn (RA) pointed out that the details of worship are known only through the Sunnah.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q9. Who answered a man asking whether qasr and jam' prayers are in the Quran with "We do as the Prophet ﷺ did"?

A. 'Abdullah ibn 'Umar (RA)
B. 'Imran ibn Husayn (RA)
C. 'Ali (RA)
D. 'Umar (RA)

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: This answer of 'Abdullah ibn 'Umar (RA) (Muwatta') shows that the practice of the Prophet ﷺ is our guide.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q10. Which of the following is NOT one of the explanations the Sunnah gives about the punishment for theft?

A. Children and the mentally ill are exempt
B. The hand is cut only up to the wrist
C. There is no such punishment for stealing food
D. The hand must be cut for stealing anything, however small

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: The Sunnah sets the condition that the stolen item must be worth more than the value of a shield. So this punishment does not apply to any small item.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q11. Which of the following is NOT among the things the textbook says are explained only through the Sunnah?

A. The funeral prayer
B. Zakat al-fitr
C. The rules of ihram
D. The command that prayer is obligatory

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: The Quran itself commands prayer. But details such as the times, method and rules of prayer, the funeral prayer, zakat al-fitr and the rules of ihram are explained by the Sunnah.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q12. The Quran is called "wahy matluww" and the Sunnah "…". Fill in the blank.

A. Wahy ghayr matluww
B. Ilham
C. Ijtihad
D. Qiyas

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: The Sunnah is also guidance from Allah, but it is not recited. So it is "wahy ghayr matluww".

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q13. Which of the following is NOT one of the efforts scholars made to preserve the Sunnah?

A. Memorising hadiths without mistakes
B. Strict research to separate authentic hadiths from fabricated ones
C. Compiling books of hadith
D. Changing hadiths and writing them as they wished

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Scholars worked hard to preserve hadiths without change – by memorising, writing, handing them over with witnesses, travelling in person, research and compiling books.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q14. "Whoever obeys me will enter Paradise; whoever disobeys me has rejected (the truth)" – from which collection is this hadith quoted?

A. Bukhari
B. Muwatta'
C. Bayhaqi
D. Tirmidhi

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: The textbook quotes this hadith from Bayhaqi. The hadith "I am leaving two things" is quoted from the Muwatta'.

Difficulty: Hard
