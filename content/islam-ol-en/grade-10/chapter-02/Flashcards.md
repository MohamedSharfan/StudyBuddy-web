# Grade 10 – Chapter 2: Iman and Amal (Faith and Deeds) – Flashcards

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_02/Flashcards.md` (Tamil), cards 1–13 in the same order.

---

Flashcard 1

Front: إيمان (Iman)

Back: Believing in what Islam teaches – accepting with the heart, declaring with the tongue and proving with action.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 2 | Topic: Key terms

---

Flashcard 2

Front: عمل (Amal)

Back: Putting the teachings of Islam into practice (deeds).

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 2 | Topic: Key terms

---

Flashcard 3

Front: Who is a mu'min (مؤمن)?

Back: A believer – someone who accepts the beliefs of Islam and carries out deeds according to them.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 2 | Topic: Key terms

---

Flashcard 4

Front: What is the highest branch of Iman?

Back: The declaration "La ilaha illallah".

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 2 | Topic: Branches of Iman

---

Flashcard 5

Front: What does the hadith say about haya' (حياء – modesty)?

Back: Modesty is a part of Iman (Bukhari).

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 2 | Topic: Branches of Iman

---

Flashcard 6

Front: What four areas do deeds include?

Back: Acts of worship, public service, dealings and transactions, good character.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 2 | Topic: Scope of deeds

---

Flashcard 7

Front: Righteous deeds (عمل صالح – 'amal salih)

Back: The right action done at the right time – good deeds that suit life in this world and the Hereafter.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 2 | Topic: Righteous deeds

---

Flashcard 8

Front: Ikhlas (إخلاص)

Back: Sincerity – doing a deed only for Allah's pleasure. It is the main condition for a deed to be accepted.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 2 | Topic: Ikhlas

---

Flashcard 9

Front: What does this chapter say about showing off (riya')?

Back: A deed done so that people will see it is like associating partners with Allah (18:110).

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 2 | Topic: Ikhlas

---

Flashcard 10

Front: What are the four features of a believer's actions?

Back: 1) Obeying Allah and His Messenger 2) Following the way of the Prophet ﷺ 3) Being done for Allah's pleasure 4) Expecting reward from Allah alone.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 2 | Topic: A believer's actions

---

Flashcard 11

Front: What do verses 2:25 and 16:97 emphasise?

Back: Those who do good deeds with Iman will have Paradise and a good life – Iman and deeds must go together.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 2 | Topic: Iman and deeds together

---

Flashcard 12

Front: What is the main point of the hadith about neighbours, guests and speech?

Back: Whoever believes in Allah and the Last Day should be good to his neighbour, honour his guest, and speak good or remain silent (Bukhari).

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 2 | Topic: Deeds taught by the Sunnah

---

Flashcard 13

Front: What lesson does the incident of Ali (RA) letting an enemy go teach?

Back: Every action must be for Allah alone; if it is done for oneself, there is no reward from Allah.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 2 | Topic: Ikhlas
