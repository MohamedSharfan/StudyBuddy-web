# Grade 10 – Chapter 2: Iman and Amal (Faith and Deeds) – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_02/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q13 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. Which is the correct definition of Iman?

A. Accepting with the heart, declaring with the tongue and proving with action
B. Saying the kalimah with the tongue only
C. Believing with the heart only
D. Performing acts of worship only

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Iman includes the heart, the tongue and actions. Believing only with the heart, or only saying it with the tongue, is not complete Iman.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. Which comparison does the textbook use to explain the link between Iman and amal?

A. The sea and its waves
B. A tree and its shade
C. A flower and its fragrance
D. The sun and its light

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Just as a flower cannot be separated from its fragrance, Iman and amal must always go together. Deeds without Iman, or Iman without deeds, are not accepted.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q3. According to the hadith, which is the lowest branch of Iman?

A. Removing something harmful from the road
B. Saying salam
C. Smiling
D. Being modest

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: The highest branch of Iman is the declaration "La ilaha illallah"; the lowest is removing something harmful from the road. Modesty is mentioned separately as a part of Iman; it is not "the lowest".

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q4. According to the hadith, Iman has more than how many branches?

A. Seventy
B. Forty
C. A hundred
D. Fifty

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: The Prophet ﷺ said that Iman has more than seventy branches (Bukhari). The Quran also mentions Iman and deeds together in about seventy or more places.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q5. Which verse says that "righteousness is not only turning your faces towards the east or the west" and then explains true righteousness?

A. 18:110
B. 24:39
C. 2:177
D. 16:97

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Verse 2:177 explains that true righteousness is belief, charity, prayer, Zakat, keeping promises and patience together. This shows that deeds are not limited to acts of worship.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q6. In the Quran (24:39), the deeds of those without Iman are compared to what?

A. Ashes
B. Foam
C. A spider's web
D. A mirage

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Verse 24:39 compares the deeds of disbelievers to a mirage in the desert. A thirsty person goes to it thinking it is water, but finds nothing. In the same way, a deed without Iman brings no benefit in the Hereafter.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q7. According to the textbook, in which hadith collection is the hadith "The best of the believers are those with the best character" found?

A. Bukhari
B. Ahmad
C. Tirmidhi
D. Muslim

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Although most hadiths in this chapter are taken from Bukhari, the hadith "The best of the believers are those with the best character" is cited from Ahmad.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q8. According to the hadith, one who works to help a widow or a poor person is like whom?

A. One who has performed Hajj
B. One who has died as a martyr
C. One who has recited the whole Quran
D. One who stands in prayer at night and fasts during the day

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: This hadith shows that helping others is a great deed. One who helps a widow or a poor person is like one who constantly prays at night and fasts during the day (Bukhari).

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q9. Which is the best explanation of "righteous deeds" (al-'amal al-salih)?

A. Acts of worship done only in the mosque
B. Actions that win people's praise
C. The right actions done at the right time; all actions that suit life in this world and the Hereafter
D. Only actions that involve spending money

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Righteous deeds are not limited to acts of worship. They include all good actions that suit every part of life – this world and the Hereafter, the individual and society, the spiritual and the material.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q10. What is the main condition for a deed to be accepted by Allah?

A. Sincerity (ikhlas)
B. Doing it in front of many people
C. Spending a lot of money
D. Doing it for a long time

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: A deed must be done only for Allah's pleasure. Doing it so that others will see it is showing off (riya'), which is like associating partners with Allah.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q11. What was the occasion for the revelation of verse 18:110?

A. Ali (RA) letting an enemy go in battle
B. A Companion missing a prayer
C. A Companion saying that, although he did deeds for Allah, it also pleased him when people saw them
D. Someone refusing to pay Zakat

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Verse 18:110 was revealed when a Companion asked about feeling pleased when people saw his deeds. It says that one should do good deeds without associating anyone with one's Lord. This makes it clear that a deed mixed with showing off is like shirk.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q12. Why did Ali (RA) let go, without killing him, the enemy who spat in his face?

A. Because the enemy asked for forgiveness
B. Because the battle had ended
C. Because the enemy accepted Islam
D. Because killing him then would be for his own sake, and he would get no reward from Allah

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Ali (RA) fought only for Allah. Once the enemy spat at him, he became a personal enemy, and killing him would be a selfish act. This is an excellent example of ikhlas (sincerity).

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q13. Which of the following is NOT a feature of a believer's actions?

A. Being in obedience to Allah and His Messenger
B. Expecting praise and reward from people
C. Being done in the way the Prophet ﷺ did them
D. Being done to win Allah's pleasure

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: A believer expects reward from Allah alone. Seeking praise from people is showing off, and it destroys the reward of the deed.

Difficulty: Medium
