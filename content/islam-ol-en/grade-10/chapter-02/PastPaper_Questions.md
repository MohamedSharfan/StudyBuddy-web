### A1. 2016 – Islam I, Q12

Question ID: P2016-I-Q12  
Year: 2016  
Paper and question: Islam I, Q12  
Marks: not stated on the paper (Islam I multiple-choice question)  
Source: OL_2016_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

What is conveyed by the word 'ikhlas'?

- **A.** scrupulous caution
- **B.** a life of detachment
- **C.** sincerity (purity of intention)
- **D.** God-consciousness

**Correct answer:** C – sincerity (purity of intention) *(option (3) on the original paper)*

**Explanation:** Ikhlas = the sincerity of doing deeds for Allah alone. Scrupulous caution = wara'; detachment = zuhd; God-consciousness = taqwa.

## Part B: Structured and Essay Questions (Islam II)

### B1. 2018 – Islam II, Q2 (iii)

Question ID: P2018-II-Q2.3  
Year: 2018  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q2 (iii)  
Marks: 10 marks for the whole of Q2  
Source: OL_2018_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(iii) Briefly explain five effects iman has on a Muslim's life.

**Suggested answer:**

1. A clear goal in life – the questions "Who am I? What is my task? What is my end?" are answered.
2. A driving force for good deeds – iman and deeds are like a flower and its fragrance; iman inspires good deeds (16:97).
3. A sense of responsibility – the belief that there will be judgement in the Hereafter keeps one from sins.
4. Peace of mind and contentment – belief in divine decree gives patience in hardship and gratitude in ease.
5. Good qualities grow – being good to neighbours, truthfulness, honesty and the like are branches of iman (Bukhari).
6. Courage and sacrifice – the readiness to give anything for Allah.

**Answer explanation:** As the question says "briefly explain", one sentence of explanation is needed for each effect.
