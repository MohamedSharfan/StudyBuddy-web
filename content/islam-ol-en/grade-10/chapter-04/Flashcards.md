# Grade 10 – Chapter 4: At-Taharah (Purification) – Flashcards

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_04/Flashcards.md` (Tamil), cards 1–17 in the same order.

---

Flashcard 1

Front: الطهارة (At-Taharah)

Back: Purification – outward cleanliness (body, clothes, place) and inward cleanliness (the heart).

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 4 | Topic: Importance of cleanliness

---

Flashcard 2

Front: الماء الطهور (Al-ma' al-tahur)

Back: Purifying water – clean in itself and purifies other things. E.g. rain, sea, river and spring water.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 4 | Topic: Types of water

---

Flashcard 3

Front: الماء الطاهر (Al-ma' al-tahir)

Back: Water mixed with clean things – clean in itself but does not purify other things. E.g. tea, fruit juice, king coconut water.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 4 | Topic: Types of water

---

Flashcard 4

Front: الماء المتنجس (Al-ma' al-mutanajjis)

Back: Impure water – not clean in itself and cannot purify. E.g. urine, sewage water.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 4 | Topic: Types of water

---

Flashcard 5

Front: Al-ma' al-kathir (a large amount of water)

Back: Water more than qullatayn. Its other name is al-ma' al-mutlaq.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 4 | Topic: Kinds of tahur water

---

Flashcard 6

Front: Al-ma' al-qalil (a small amount of water)

Back: Purifying water less than qullatayn.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 4 | Topic: Kinds of tahur water

---

Flashcard 7

Front: Al-ma' al-musta'mal

Back: Water less than qullatayn that has been used for an obligatory wudu or ghusl.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 4 | Topic: Kinds of tahur water

---

Flashcard 8

Front: According to the textbook, how much is qullatayn (قلتين)?

Back: About 210 litres. (⚠️ The textbook's wording here is unclear – see `VERIFY.md`.)

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 4 | Topic: Kinds of tahur water

---

Flashcard 9

Front: What three features show whether water has become najis?

Back: Colour, smell and taste.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 4 | Topic: Types of water

---

Flashcard 10

Front: حدث (Hadath)

Back: Ritual impurity – being in a state of minor or major ritual impurity.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 4 | Topic: Hadath

---

Flashcard 11

Front: How does one become pure from minor and major hadath?

Back: Minor hadath → wudu; major hadath → ghusl.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 4 | Topic: Hadath

---

Flashcard 12

Front: Hayd (حيض)

Back: A woman's menstruation. When it ends, ghusl becomes obligatory.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 4 | Topic: When ghusl is obligatory

---

Flashcard 13

Front: Nifas (نفاس)

Back: Bleeding after childbirth. When it ends, ghusl becomes obligatory.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 4 | Topic: When ghusl is obligatory

---

Flashcard 14

Front: تيمم (Tayammum)

Back: Wiping the face and both hands with clean soil when water cannot be used.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 4 | Topic: Concessions

---

Flashcard 15

Front: جبيرة (Jabirah)

Back: A bandage or plaster put on a wound. Tayammum is done in place of that part, and the other parts are washed.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 4 | Topic: Concessions

---

Flashcard 16

Front: مسح (Mash)

Back: Wiping a part of the body with a wet hand or with soil.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 4 | Topic: Key terms

---

Flashcard 17

Front: What is the meaning of verse 9:108?

Back: Allah certainly loves those who keep themselves pure.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 4 | Topic: Importance of cleanliness
