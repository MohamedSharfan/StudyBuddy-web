# Grade 10 – Chapter 4: At-Taharah (Purification) – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_04/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q14 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. In which hadith collection is the hadith "Cleanliness is half of Iman" found?

A. Bukhari
B. Abu Dawud
C. Muslim
D. Tirmidhi

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: The textbook cites this hadith from Muslim. The hadith "Allah does not accept any prayer without purification" is also in Muslim.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. In which surah, revealed to the Prophet ﷺ in the early period, is the verse "Keep your clothes clean" found?

A. Surah al-Muddaththir (74:4)
B. Surah at-Tawbah (9:108)
C. Surah al-Baqarah
D. Surah al-'Alaq

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: 74:4 is in Surah al-Muddaththir. Verse 9:108 says "Allah loves those who keep themselves pure"; it is not about clothes.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q3. What does "inward cleanliness" in Islam refer to?

A. Cleaning the inside of the house
B. Keeping the heart free from jealousy, pride, suspicion, hatred and similar qualities
C. Keeping the stomach clean
D. Cleaning the mosque

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Islam teaches that, just as the body must be clean from impurities, the heart must also be clean from bad qualities.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q4. Which water is clean in itself and can also purify other things?

A. Al-ma' al-tahir
B. Al-ma' al-mutanajjis
C. Al-ma' al-tahur
D. Al-ma' al-musta'mal

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Only tahur water is both "clean + purifying". Tahir water is clean in itself but cannot purify other things.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q5. Which of the following belongs to "al-ma' al-tahir"?

A. Rain water
B. Sea water
C. King coconut water
D. Spring water

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: King coconut water has changed by nature from the natural state of water, so it is tahir (clean, but it does not purify). Rain, sea and spring water are tahur.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q6. A large amount of water in a tank, which has stood for a long time and changed because of leaves, moss and so on, belongs to which type?

A. Al-ma' al-tahur
B. Al-ma' al-tahir
C. Al-ma' al-mutanajjis
D. Al-ma' al-musta'mal

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: The textbook gives a large amount of water that has changed through leaves, moss and so on over a long time as an example of tahur water. A change in colour does not make it tahir.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q7. If najis falls into water that is more than qullatayn, when does the water become najis?

A. As soon as the najis falls in
B. Only if one of its colour, smell or taste changes
C. It never becomes najis
D. Only if all three change

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: A large amount of water (al-ma' al-kathir) does not become najis just because najis falls into it. A change in any one of colour, smell or taste is enough; all three do not need to change.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q8. Which of the following does NOT make water less than qullatayn (al-ma' al-qalil) najis when it falls in?

A. A drop of urine
B. A drop of blood
C. A dead cat
D. A fly, ant or bee (creatures without flowing blood)

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: When najis gets into a small amount of water, the water becomes najis even if no change can be seen. But the water does not become najis if a fly, ant or bee, which have no flowing blood, falls into it.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q9. What is water less than qullatayn called once it has been used for an obligatory wudu?

A. Al-ma' al-musta'mal
B. Al-ma' al-mutlaq
C. Al-ma' al-kathir
D. Al-ma' al-tahir

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: A small amount of water used for an obligatory ghusl, wudu and the like is "musta'mal" (used water). "Mutlaq" is another name for a large amount of water (kathir).

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q10. Water less than qullatayn was used for ghusl. What must be done to use it again for wudu?

A. It must be boiled
B. It must be kept in the sun for a day
C. More clean water must be added to bring it up to qullatayn, making it tahur water
D. It can never be used again

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: If clean water is added to musta'mal water until it reaches qullatayn, it becomes purifying (tahur) water and can be used again for wudu and ghusl.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q11. What is the correct meaning of "hadath"?

A. A sunnah act of prayer
B. Being in a state of minor or major ritual impurity
C. Water mixed with najis
D. The obligatory acts of wudu

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Hadath is a state of ritual impurity. One becomes pure from minor hadath through wudu and from major hadath through ghusl.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q12. Which of the following is NOT an occasion when ghusl becomes obligatory?

A. Entering Islam
B. The end of hayd (menstruation)
C. Death
D. Waking up from sleep

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Ghusl is obligatory only after intercourse, discharge of semen, the end of hayd or nifas, entering Islam, and death. Sleep causes minor hadath, for which wudu is enough.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q13. In tayammum, which parts of the body are wiped (mash) with soil?

A. The face and both hands
B. The head and feet
C. The whole body
D. The hands and feet

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Tayammum is wiping (mash) the face and both hands using clean soil. Unlike wudu, all the parts do not need to be wiped.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q14. A person has a bandage (jabirah) on his hand because of a wound. What is the correct way for him to perform wudu?

A. He may pray without wudu
B. He must remove the bandage and wash the place
C. He may do only tayammum instead of the whole wudu
D. He must wash the other parts with water and do tayammum in place of the bandaged part

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Under the concession of jabirah, tayammum is done only in place of washing the bandaged part; the other parts of wudu must be washed with water.

Difficulty: Hard
