# Grade 10 – Chapter 10: At-Tazkiyah (Purification of the Soul)

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_10/Notes.md` (Tamil). English rendering.

## 🎯 What you will learn
- What tazkiyah is
- The three states of the soul (nafs) described in the Quran
- The importance of tazkiyah – evidence from the Quran and hadith
- Ways to purify the soul

---

## 1. What is tazkiyah?
- **Tazkiyah (تزكية)** – an Arabic word meaning **purifying, putting right.**
- In Islamic usage: **tazkiyat al-nafs (تزكية النفس)** = **purifying the soul.** In short we call it "tazkiyah".

### What a Muslim should do through tazkiyah
1. Protect the heart and one's actions from every bad quality, including **associating partners with Allah (shirk)**.
2. **Restrain** the soul from actions, desires and thoughts that Allah does not like.
3. **Develop good qualities** in the heart and keep it pure.

---

## 2. The three states of the soul (nafs) ⭐

| State | Arabic name | Meaning | Its nature |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **An-nafs al-ammarah bis-su'** (النفس الأمارة بالسوء) | **The soul that commands evil** | No urge towards good; **enslaved to desires and to Satan** |
| 2 | **An-nafs al-lawwamah** (النفس اللوامة) | **The self-blaming soul** | There is a **struggle** against desires; when it sins it **regrets** at once, blames itself and **feels remorse** |
| 3 | **An-nafs al-mutma'innah** (النفس المطمئنة) | **The soul at peace** | No anxiety or wavering; the urge towards evil is **very weak**; the urge towards good is **strong** |

⭐ The aim of tazkiyah: to reach the **third state, an-nafs al-mutma'innah**.

💡 **Memory tip:** ammarah = **commands** (evil) → lawwamah = **blames** (itself) → mutma'innah = **peace.**

---

## 3. The importance of tazkiyah

### (1) Purity of the soul is essential for success in both worlds
> 📖 **(91:9–10) – meaning:** "Indeed, whoever purifies it (the soul) has **succeeded**, and whoever corrupts it has certainly **failed**."

- Before this, Allah **swears by the sun, the moon, the night and the day** (Surah ash-Shams). This further confirms the importance of purifying the soul.

### (2) One of the main purposes of the messengers
> 📖 **(62:2) – meaning:** He sent among the unlettered people a messenger from among themselves, who **recites** His verses to them, **purifies** them, and **teaches** them the Book and wisdom (the Sunnah).

### (3) The basic qualification for Paradise
> 📖 **(79:40–41) – meaning:** Whoever feared standing before his Lord and **held back his soul from its desires**, Paradise will surely be his refuge.

### (4) The heart leads the body
> 📖 **Hadith (Bukhari, Muslim) – meaning:** "Indeed, in the body there is a **piece of flesh**. If it is corrupt, the whole body is corrupt; if it is sound, the whole body is sound. **That is the heart.**"

### (5) Mujahadat al-nafs – an excellent jihad
- **Mujahadat al-nafs (مجاهدة النفس)** = **striving against one's own self** – one of the best forms of jihad in Islam.
> 📖 **Hadith (Bukhari, Muslim) – meaning:** "The strong man is not the one who throws others down (in wrestling). The truly strong man is the one who **controls himself when he is angry**."

### (6) The supplication of the Prophet ﷺ (Muslim)
> اَللّٰهُمَّ آتِ أَنْفُسَنَا تَقْوَاهَا، وَزَكِّهَا أَنْتَ خَيْرُ مَنْ زَكَّاهَا، أَنْتَ وَلِيُّهَا وَمَوْلَاهَا

**Meaning:** O Allah! Give our souls their God-consciousness (taqwa) and purify them, for You are the best of those who purify; You are their Protector and Master.

💡 If the Prophet ﷺ, whose past and future sins were forgiven, prayed for this so earnestly, we should think how much more we need it.

### (7) Allah looks at the heart
> 📖 **Hadith (Muslim) – Abu Hurayrah (RA) – meaning:** "Allah does not look at your **bodies or your faces**; rather, He looks at your **hearts**."

👉 However great our deeds may be, **without purity of heart** they have no benefit or value with Allah.

---

## 4. Ways to purify the soul
1. Keeping away from base qualities such as **showing off (riya'), pride and envy**
2. Performing the **obligatory (fard) duties** properly
3. Performing **voluntary (nafl)** acts of worship
4. Engaging in **dhikr**
5. Reciting the **Quran** often
6. Making **tawbah** (seeking forgiveness for sins)

---

## ✅ Quick revision
- Tazkiyah = purifying; tazkiyat al-nafs = purifying the soul.
- 3 states of the nafs: **ammarah** (commands evil) → **lawwamah** (blames itself) → **mutma'innah** (at peace).
- Evidence: 91:9–10 (success / failure), 62:2 (the Prophet's mission), 79:40–41 (Paradise).
- Hadith: a piece of flesh in the body – the heart; the strong one controls his anger; Allah looks at hearts.
- Mujahadat al-nafs = striving against oneself – an excellent jihad.
