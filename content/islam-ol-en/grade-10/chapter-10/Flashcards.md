Flashcard 4

Front: النفس الأمارة بالسوء (An-nafs al-ammarah bis-su')

Flashcard 5

Front: النفس اللوامة (An-nafs al-lawwamah)

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 10 | Topic: States of the soul

---

Flashcard 6

Front: النفس المطمئنة (An-nafs al-mutma'innah)

Back: The soul at peace – with a strong urge towards good; the goal of tazkiyah.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 10 | Topic: States of the soul

---

Flashcard 7

Front: مجاهدة النفس (Mujahadat al-nafs)

Back: Striving against one's own self – one of the best forms of jihad in Islam.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 10 | Topic: Importance of tazkiyah

---

Flashcard 8

Front: What is the meaning of verses 91:9–10?

Back: Whoever purifies the soul has succeeded; whoever corrupts it has failed.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 10 | Topic: Quranic evidence

---

Flashcard 9

Front: What three tasks of the Prophet ﷺ does 62:2 mention?

Back: Reciting the verses, purifying the people, and teaching the Book and wisdom.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 10 | Topic: Quranic evidence

---

Flashcard 10

Front: What quality of the people of Paradise does 79:40–41 mention?

Back: Fearing to stand before their Lord and holding back the soul from its desires.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 10 | Topic: Quranic evidence

---

Flashcard 11

Front: اَللّٰهُمَّ آتِ أَنْفُسَنَا تَقْوَاهَا وَزَكِّهَا

Back: (meaning) O Allah! Give our souls their God-consciousness and purify them (Muslim).

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 10 | Topic: The Prophet's supplication

Flashcard 12

Front: What are the six ways of purifying the soul?

Back: Avoiding base qualities, performing the obligatory duties, voluntary worship, dhikr, reciting the Quran, and tawbah.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 10 | Topic: Ways of purification
