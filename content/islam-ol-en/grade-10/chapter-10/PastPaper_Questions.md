### A1. 2016 – Islam I, Q5

Question ID: P2016-I-Q05  
Year: 2016  
Paper and question: Islam I, Q5  
Marks: not stated on the paper (Islam I multiple-choice question)  
Source: OL_2016_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

Which of the following pairs has the closest similar meaning?

- **A.** qalbun salim - al-qalb al-mutma'inn
- **B.** sahifah - mushaf
- **C.** mujtahid - muqallid
- **D.** dunya - akhirah

**Correct answer:** A – qalbun salim - al-qalb al-mutma'inn *(option (1) on the original paper)*

**Explanation:** Qalbun salim (a sound, pure heart) and mutma'inn (a heart at peace) both describe a healthy, upright heart – close in meaning. Mujtahid–muqallid and dunya–akhirah are opposite pairs; sahifah (a sheet/scroll) and mushaf (a compiled book) are different.

## Part B: Structured and Essay Questions (Islam II)

### B1. 2017 – Islam II, Q7 (i)

Question ID: P2017-II-Q7.1  
Year: 2017  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q7 (i)  
Marks: 10 marks (short notes on two topics)  
Source: OL_2017_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

Write short notes on two of the following.
(i) Tazkiyat an-nafs

**Suggested answer:**

Tazkiyah = purifying; tazkiyat an-nafs = purifying the heart (soul).
- Protecting the heart from bad qualities, including associating partners with Allah, and developing good qualities.
- The three states of the nafs: ammarah (commanding evil), lawwamah (self-reproaching), mutma'innah (at peace) – the goal of tazkiyah is mutma'innah.
- Importance: "He who purifies it has succeeded" (91:9–10); the task of the prophets (62:2); a qualification for Paradise (79:40–41); "There is a piece of flesh in the body… it is the heart" (Bukhari, Muslim).
- Ways: avoiding showing off, pride and envy; fard and nafl worship; dhikr; reciting the Quran; tawbah.

**Answer explanation:** Include the definition, the states, the importance and the ways.

**Marking guidance (suggested only – not an official marking scheme):** No official marking scheme; write equally on the two topics chosen.

**Question:**

(i) What is the linguistic meaning of 'tazkiyat an-nafs'?
(ii) State the three states of the heart explained by the Quran.

**Suggested answer:**

(i) Tazkiyah = purifying, putting right; nafs = the heart (soul). Tazkiyat an-nafs = purifying the heart.

(ii)
1. An-nafs al-ammarah bis-su' – the heart that commands evil
2. An-nafs al-lawwamah – the self-reproaching heart
3. An-nafs al-mutma'innah – the heart at peace

**Answer explanation:** What is hoped for through tazkiyah is to reach the third state, an-nafs al-mutma'innah.
