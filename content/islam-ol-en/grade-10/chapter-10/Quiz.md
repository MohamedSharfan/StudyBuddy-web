# Grade 10 – Chapter 10: At-Tazkiyah – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_10/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q13 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. What does the Arabic word "tazkiyah" mean?

A. Purifying, putting right
B. Struggling
C. Remembering
D. Seeking forgiveness

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Tazkiyah = purifying, putting right. In Islamic usage it is short for "tazkiyat al-nafs" (purifying the soul). Seeking forgiveness is "tawbah".

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. Which state of the soul has no urge towards good and is enslaved to desires and to Satan?

A. An-nafs al-lawwamah
B. An-nafs al-mutma'innah
C. An-nafs al-ammarah bis-su'
D. An-nafs az-zakiyyah

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: "Ammarah bis-su'" = the soul that commands evil. This is the lowest state of the soul.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q3. A student tells a lie, then immediately regrets it, blames himself and feels remorse. Which state of the soul does this show?

A. An-nafs al-ammarah
B. An-nafs al-lawwamah
C. An-nafs al-mutma'innah
D. None of these states

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Lawwamah = the self-blaming soul. It struggles against desires, and when it sins it regrets at once.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q4. Which state of the soul is tazkiyah expected to achieve?

A. An-nafs al-ammarah
B. An-nafs al-lawwamah
C. An-nafs al-mutma'innah
D. All three equally

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Mutma'innah (the soul at peace) – a state without anxiety or wavering, with a strong urge towards good. This state, protected from evils, is the goal of tazkiyah.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q5. Which verse says, "Whoever purifies it (the soul) has succeeded"?

A. 62:2
B. 79:40–41
C. 13:28
D. 91:9–10

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: These are verses 9 and 10 of Surah ash-Shams. Before them Allah swears by the sun, the moon, the night and the day, which shows the importance of purifying the soul.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q6. According to the textbook, by what does Allah swear before verses 91:9–10?

A. The sun, the moon, the night and the day
B. The fig, the olive and Mount Sinai
C. Time ('asr)
D. The dawn and the ten nights

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Allah swears by creations such as the sun, the moon, the night and the day and then speaks about purifying the soul. This further confirms its importance.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q7. Which of the following is NOT one of the tasks of the Prophet ﷺ mentioned in verse 62:2?

A. Reciting the verses of the Book
B. Purifying the people
C. Teaching the Book and wisdom
D. Gathering wealth and giving it out

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Verse 62:2 mentions three tasks: reciting, purifying, and teaching the Book and wisdom (the Sunnah). This makes clear that purifying hearts is one of the main purposes of the messengers.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q8. "Whoever feared standing before his Lord and held back his soul from its desires, Paradise will be his refuge" – which verse is this?

A. 91:9–10
B. 79:40–41
C. 62:2
D. 2:255

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Verses 79:40–41 show that purifying the soul is the basic qualification for entering Paradise.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q9. In the hadith "There is a piece of flesh in the body; if it is sound, the whole body is sound", what is that piece of flesh?

A. The brain
B. The tongue
C. The heart
D. The eye

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: The Prophet ﷺ said, "That is the heart" (Bukhari, Muslim). When the heart is sound, all actions become sound.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q10. According to the Prophet ﷺ, who is truly strong?

A. The one who throws others down in wrestling
B. The one who controls himself when angry
C. The one with the most wealth
D. The one with the most friends

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: The strength to control oneself is greater than physical strength (Bukhari, Muslim). This is a form of mujahadat al-nafs (striving against oneself), an excellent kind of jihad.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q11. What does "mujahadat al-nafs" refer to?

A. Fighting on the battlefield
B. Striving against one's own self (its desires)
C. Arguing with others
D. Only fasting

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Mujahadat al-nafs = striving against oneself; resisting evil desires and controlling the soul. In Islam it is considered one of the best forms of jihad.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q12. "Allah does not look at your bodies or your faces; He looks at your hearts" – what lesson does this hadith teach?

A. Importance should be given to good looks
B. Deeds are not needed
C. Even great deeds without purity of heart have no value with Allah
D. Physical health is not important

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: In every deed Allah looks at our hearts. Even if a deed is great, it is of no benefit if the heart is not pure. But the hadith does NOT say that deeds are unnecessary.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q13. Which of the following is NOT one of the ways of purifying the soul given in the textbook?

A. Making tawbah
B. Reciting the Quran often
C. Avoiding showing off, pride and envy
D. Searching for and talking about other people's faults

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Searching for others' faults corrupts the heart. Obligatory and voluntary worship, dhikr, reciting the Quran, tawbah and avoiding base qualities are the ways to purify the soul.

Difficulty: Easy
