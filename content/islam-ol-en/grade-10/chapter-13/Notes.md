# Grade 10 – Chapter 13: Ways of Spiritual Development

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_13/Notes.md` (Tamil). English rendering.

## 🎯 What you will learn
- The place of spirituality in Islam
- What "spiritual poverty" is
- Four ways of spiritual development: **ikhlas, tawbah, wara', zuhd**

---

## 1. Spirituality in Islam
- A human being has been given **a beautiful body and a beautiful soul**. Just as we take care to make the body beautiful, we must also give importance to **keeping the soul on the right path**.
- Islam **does not separate spiritual life from worldly life.**
- A human being is made of **three parts**: **body, mind (intellect) and soul.**
- Islam does not give importance to the soul while ignoring the body, nor to the body while ignoring the soul.

> 📖 **Hadith (Muslim) – meaning:** "Allah is beautiful and He loves beauty." → We must keep the body and the heart **in balance**.

### Spiritual poverty
- Today only bodily needs are made the centre, and spiritual needs are pushed aside → a human being becomes **merely an economic animal**.
- Human values such as social concern, justice, honesty, fairness, trust and truth have become rare.
- Islamic scholars call this state **"spiritual poverty"**.
- The solution: following **the methods of the Quran and the Sunnah**, living in a way that joins this world and the Hereafter, body and soul. Methods that go against them only **harm** a person spiritually.

---

## 2. Way 1: Ikhlas (إخلاص) ⭐
> 📖 **Meaning:** purity of heart, an unmixed intention. Whatever one does, aiming **only at Allah's pleasure**.

- It is the **basic condition** for deeds to be accepted by Allah.
- **(18:110)* – meaning:** Whoever hopes to meet his Lord, let him do good deeds and not associate anyone in the worship of his Lord.
- When a deed is done with **someone else in view** alongside Allah, it becomes associating partners (shirk).
- **Hadith – meaning:** "Even a very small amount of **showing off (riya')** is **shirk**."
- Deeds done for money, influence, position, fame or worldly gain have **no value at all** with Allah.

### Hadiths
| Hadith (meaning) | Source |
|---|---|
| "**Actions are judged only by intentions**" | Bukhari, Muslim |
| Allah does not look at your **appearance or your wealth**; He looks at **your hearts and your deeds** | Muslim |
| A man who **fought so that he would be praised as "brave"** and was killed – "You have lied… it has already been said" – will be **dragged on his face into Hell** | Muslim |

💡 **Lesson:** even after giving his life, he ended up in Hell **because his intention was wrong**. So a pure intention is extremely important.

- **(11:15–16) – meaning:** Those who want only the life of this world and its glitter are paid in full **in this world**; in the Hereafter they will have **nothing but the Fire**.

## 3. Way 2: Tawbah (توبة) ⭐
> 📖 **Literal meaning:** returning, turning back.
> 📖 **Technical meaning:** turning, privately and publicly, from what Allah **dislikes** to what He **loves**.

- It is **the best way to remove the stains of sin** from the heart.
- It is turning from an old way of life **to a new one**. It is **not an empty word said with the tongue** – there must be a change in knowledge, feeling and action.
- Why people do wrong: **ignorance, uncontrolled desires, and the influence of their surroundings.**

### Hadith (Bukhari, Muslim) – meaning
Allah laughs at two men – one of whom killed the other – **both entering Paradise**: one was killed in Allah's path; the killer later **made tawbah**, accepted Islam, and then he too **died a martyr (shahid).**

### Benefits of tawbah
- One comes close to Allah
- One is saved from Hell and admitted to Paradise
- **Bad deeds are changed into good deeds**
- A prosperous life in this world

### The four conditions of tawbah ⭐
1. Making tawbah with a pure intention, **for Allah**.
2. **Giving up the sin completely.**
3. **Regretting and feeling remorse** for the sins committed.
4. If the sin concerns **another person**, approaching Allah after **seeking that person's forgiveness**.

### Key points
- Tawbah has **no time limit**; it can be made **at any time**, not only in Ramadan.
- **Delaying tawbah** is a whisper of Satan.
- Allah waits **at night** to forgive those who sinned by day, and **by day** to forgive those who sinned at night.

## 4. Way 3: Wara' (ورع) – scrupulous caution ⭐
> 📖 **Meaning:** scrupulousness – being careful to avoid what is doubtful.

### Hadiths
| Hadith (meaning) | Source |
|---|---|
| "**Leave what makes you doubt for what does not make you doubt**" | Ahmad, Tirmidhi, Nasa'i |
| "Put your hand on your chest! The heart is **uneasy before what is haram and at ease before what is halal**. A cautious Muslim, fearing the major sins, leaves **even minor sins**." – This is regarded as **the definition of wara'** | Tabarani |
| A person cannot be **muttaqi** until, for fear of falling into wrong, he **leaves even something that is not wrong** | Tirmidhi |
| "O Abu Hurayrah! Be scrupulous (cautious); then you will be **the greatest worshipper**" | Tirmidhi |

### Three levels of wara'
| Level | Explanation |
|---|---|
| **In thought** | Leaving worthless thoughts and filling the heart with thoughts of Allah |
| **In speech** | Keeping the tongue from every kind of idle talk |
| **In action** | Making one's walk, dress and conduct all halal |

- The Companions would avoid **seventy halal things for fear of falling into one haram thing**.

## 5. Way 4: Zuhd (زهد) – detachment from the world ⭐
> 📖 **Zuhd:** regarding **the life of this world as small** compared with the life of the Hereafter.

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Dar al-fana'** (دار الفناء) | This perishable worldly life |
| **Dar al-baqa'** (دار البقاء) | The everlasting life of the Hereafter |

👉 **Detachment from the world** is necessary so that we are not deceived by the illusion of dar al-fana' and forget dar al-baqa'.

### Evidence
- **(67:2) – meaning:** He created death and life **to test which of you is best in deeds.**
- **(57:20) – meaning:** The life of this world is **play, amusement and adornment**; it causes boasting and rivalry in wealth and children.
- **(5:87) – meaning:** Do not **make haram what Allah has made halal; do not transgress.**
- **(31:33) – meaning:** Do not let the life of this world **deceive you.**
- **Hadith (Ibn Majah) – meaning:** "Be **detached** from the world and Allah will love you. Be **independent** of what people have and people will love you."

### Zuhd ≠ rahbaniyyah ⚠️
| Zuhd ✅ | Rahbaniyyah ❌ |
|---|---|
| Using the world but **not attaching the heart to it** | **Renouncing the world completely** and rejecting its pleasures |
| Giving priority to the Hereafter; enjoying what is halal | Deliberately making life **hard and harsh** |
| Islam encourages it | **Monasticism** – Islam **warns against it and condemns it** |

### What zuhd means in practice
- Enjoying the world must not be **one's whole aim**.
- Love of worldly wealth must not **turn one away** from seeking the Hereafter.
- Love of fame, titles and position must not turn one away from the ranks of Paradise.
- Sorrow over a loss in this world must not **outweigh sorrow over a loss in the Hereafter**.
- One must not **hesitate** to spend in Allah's path from wealth one has earned.

## ✅ Quick revision
- Human = body + mind + soul; "Allah is beautiful and loves beauty" (Muslim).
- Spiritual poverty = ignoring spiritual needs and focusing only on bodily needs.
- **Ikhlas** = unmixed intention; "actions are by intentions"; even small showing off is shirk.
- **Tawbah** = returning; 4 conditions: pure intention, leaving the sin, regret, seeking the wronged person's forgiveness.
- **Wara'** = scrupulous caution; leave what is doubtful (Ahmad, Tirmidhi, Nasa'i); thought, speech, action.
- **Zuhd** = detachment from the world; dar al-fana' vs dar al-baqa'; rahbaniyyah (monasticism) is condemned.
