# Grade 10 – Chapter 13: Ways of Spiritual Development – Flashcards

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_13/Flashcards.md` (Tamil), cards 1–17 in the same order.

---

Flashcard 1

Front: What are the four ways of spiritual development?

Back: Ikhlas, tawbah, wara', zuhd.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 13 | Topic: Overview

---

Flashcard 2

Front: إخلاص (Ikhlas)

Back: Purity of heart, an unmixed intention – aiming only at Allah's pleasure.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 13 | Topic: Ikhlas

---

Flashcard 3

Front: رياء (Riya') – showing off

Back: Doing deeds for people's praise; even a small amount is shirk.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 13 | Topic: Ikhlas

---

Flashcard 4

Front: شرك (Shirk)

Back: Associating partners with Allah.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 13 | Topic: Ikhlas

---

Flashcard 5

Front: توبة (Tawbah)

Back: Returning, turning back – turning from what Allah dislikes to what He loves.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 13 | Topic: Tawbah

---

Flashcard 6

Front: What are the four conditions of tawbah?

Back: 1) A pure intention for Allah 2) Giving up the sin completely 3) Regret and remorse 4) If the sin concerns a person, seeking his forgiveness.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 13 | Topic: Tawbah

---

Flashcard 7

Front: What are the three reasons people do wrong?

Back: Ignorance, uncontrolled desires, and the influence of their surroundings.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 13 | Topic: Tawbah

---

Flashcard 8

Front: ورع (Wara')

Back: Scrupulous caution – being careful to avoid what is doubtful.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 13 | Topic: Wara'

---

Flashcard 9

Front: متقي (Muttaqi)

Back: A God-fearing person – one who, fearing to fall into wrong, leaves even what is not wrong.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 13 | Topic: Wara'

---

Flashcard 10

Front: What are the three levels of wara'?

Back: Thought (thinking of Allah), speech (no idle talk), action (walk, dress and conduct kept halal).

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 13 | Topic: Wara'

---

Flashcard 11

Front: Give an example of the Companions' wara'.

Back: They avoided seventy halal things for fear of falling into one haram thing.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 13 | Topic: Wara'

---

Flashcard 12

Front: زهد (Zuhd)

Back: Detachment from the world – regarding this life as small compared with the Hereafter.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 13 | Topic: Zuhd

---

Flashcard 13

Front: دار الفناء (Dar al-fana')

Back: This perishable worldly life.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 13 | Topic: Zuhd

---

Flashcard 14

Front: دار البقاء (Dar al-baqa')

Back: The everlasting life of the Hereafter.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 13 | Topic: Zuhd

---

Flashcard 15

Front: رهبانية (Rahbaniyyah)

Back: Monasticism – renouncing the world completely; Islam condemns it.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 13 | Topic: Zuhd

---

Flashcard 16

Front: "Be detached from the world…" – what is the full meaning of the hadith?

Back: …and Allah will love you; be independent of what people have, and people will love you (Ibn Majah).

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 13 | Topic: Zuhd

---

Flashcard 17

Front: What is spiritual poverty?

Back: The state in which spiritual needs are pushed aside, only bodily needs are made the focus, and human values become rare.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 13 | Topic: Spirituality in Islam
