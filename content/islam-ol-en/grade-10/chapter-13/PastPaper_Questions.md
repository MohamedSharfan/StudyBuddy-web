Just as 'wara'' refers to scrupulous caution, what does 'taqwa' refer to?

- **A.** repentance
- **B.** God-consciousness (fear of Allah)
- **C.** a life of detachment
- **D.** entrusting one's affairs (to Allah)

**Correct answer:** B – God-consciousness (fear of Allah) *(option (2) on the original paper)*

### A5. 2020 – Islam I, Q7

Question ID: P2020-I-Q07  
Year: 2020  
Paper and question: Islam I, Q7  
Marks: not stated on the paper (Islam I multiple-choice question)  
Source: OL_2020_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

A person does a good deed with the sincere intention "for Allah", but also hopes that people will praise him. That deed is

- **A.** associating partners with Allah.
- **B.** a good deed.
- **C.** something to be welcomed.
- **D.** something permitted.

**Correct answer:** A – associating partners with Allah. *(option (1) on the original paper)*

**Explanation:** When the praise of someone else is aimed at in a deed alongside Allah, it is showing off (riya'); "even a little showing off is shirk" – the incident in which 18:110 was revealed.

### A6. 2021 (2022) – Islam I, Q33

Question ID: P2021_2022-I-Q33  
Year: 2021 (2022)  
Paper and question: Islam I, Q33  
Marks: not stated on the paper (Islam I multiple-choice question)  
Source: OL_2021_2022_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

In the table below, Column I gives some technical terms and Column II their explanations.

| Column I | Column II |
|---|---|
| (1) tawbah | A - a heart enslaved to desires and to the devil |
| (2) zuhd | B - excellent good character |
| (3) an-nafs al-ammarah bis-su' | C - the best way to remove the stains of sin |
| (4) khuluq hasan | D - a way of life free of attachment |

Which option is correct when Column II is arranged, in order, to match Column I?

- **A.** A B C D
- **B.** B C D A
- **C.** C D A B
- **D.** D C B A

**Correct answer:** C – C D A B *(option (3) on the original paper)*

**Explanation:** Tawbah – removing the stain of sin (C); zuhd – a life of detachment (D); an-nafs al-ammarah – a heart enslaved to desire (A); khuluq hasan – good character (B). So C D A B.

### B4. 2022 (2023) – Islam II, Q5 (iii)

Question ID: P2022_2023-II-Q5.2  
Year: 2022 (2023)  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q5 (iii)  
Marks: 10 marks for the whole of Q5  
Source: OL_2022_2023_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(iii) State what 'tawbah' is and give the conditions for it to be accepted.

**Suggested answer:**

Tawbah (linguistically: returning) = turning, in secret and in public, from what Allah dislikes to what He loves (seeking forgiveness for sin).
Conditions:
1. A sincere intention, for Allah
2. Completely giving up the sin
3. Regretting the sin committed
4. Resolving never to commit that sin again
5. If the sin concerns another person, obtaining that person's forgiveness / returning his right
