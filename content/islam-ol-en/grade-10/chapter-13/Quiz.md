# Grade 10 – Chapter 13: Ways of Spiritual Development – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_13/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q16 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. According to the textbook, what three basic parts is a human being made of?

A. Body, wealth, family
B. Body, mind, soul
C. Mind, position, fame
D. Soul, society, country

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: A human being is made of three parts: body, mind and soul. Islam does not give importance to only one of them while ignoring another.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. Which state do Islamic scholars call "spiritual poverty"?

A. Having no money
B. Pushing aside spiritual needs, focusing only on bodily needs, so that human values become rare
C. Having no education
D. Being physically ill

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Spiritual poverty is the state in which a person becomes merely an economic animal and values such as justice, honesty, trust and truth become rare. It does not mean having no money.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q3. In this chapter, what is the hadith "Allah is beautiful and He loves beauty" (Muslim) used to show?

A. One should live in luxury
B. Only physical beauty matters
C. The body and the heart must be kept in balance
D. Only the soul matters

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Islam does not support a beautiful body with an ugly soul, or a beautiful soul with a neglected body. The two must be in balance.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q4. Complete this saying of the Prophet ﷺ: "Even a very small amount of showing off is …"

A. a minor sin
B. makruh
C. permitted
D. associating partners with Allah (shirk)

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: When someone else's praise becomes an aim of a deed alongside Allah, it becomes associating partners. So the Prophet ﷺ warned that even a small amount of showing off is shirk.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q5. The hadith "Actions are judged only by intentions" is directly connected with which spiritual way?

A. Zuhd
B. Wara'
C. Tawbah
D. Ikhlas

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: This hadith (Bukhari, Muslim) shows that every deed needs a pure intention; that is ikhlas.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q6. According to the hadith in Muslim, what will happen in the Hereafter to a man who fought to be praised as "brave" and was killed?

A. He will be dragged on his face into Hell
B. He will enter Paradise as a martyr
C. He will receive half the reward
D. He will be forgiven

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Even though he gave his life, his aim was people's praise, so Allah will say "You have lied" and order him to be thrown into Hell. A great deed without a pure intention is worthless.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q7. What is the literal meaning of "tawbah"?

A. Scrupulous caution
B. Returning, turning back
C. Detachment from the world
D. Purity of heart

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Tawbah = returning, turning back; turning from what Allah dislikes to what He loves. Scrupulous caution is "wara'", detachment is "zuhd", and purity of heart is "ikhlas".

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q8. Which of the following is NOT a condition for tawbah to be accepted?

A. A pure intention for Allah
B. Giving up the sin completely
C. Doing it only in the month of Ramadan
D. Regretting the sin committed

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Tawbah has no time limit; it can be made at any time. The four conditions: pure intention, leaving the sin, regret, and if the sin concerns another person, seeking that person's forgiveness.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q9. A person wrongfully took his friend's money. What else must he do for his tawbah to be complete?

A. Praying two rak'ahs is enough
B. He must wait until next Ramadan
C. It is enough if nobody finds out
D. He must ask that friend for forgiveness

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: If the sin concerns another person, one must approach Allah after seeking that person's forgiveness – this is the fourth condition of tawbah.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q10. What does the textbook say about "delaying tawbah"?

A. It is a whisper of Satan
B. It is permitted
C. It is better to make tawbah in old age
D. It is sunnah

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Only Allah knows when death will come. So delaying tawbah, or not caring about it, is a whisper of Satan.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q11. The hadith "Leave what makes you doubt for what does not make you doubt" is the basis of which quality?

A. Generosity
B. Zuhd
C. Tawbah
D. Wara' (scrupulous caution)

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Wara' is avoiding what is doubtful in religion. This hadith is found in Ahmad, Tirmidhi and Nasa'i.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q12. According to the hadith in Tabarani, what way did the Prophet ﷺ give to know whether something is halal or haram?

A. Asking only a scholar
B. Doing what the majority does
C. Putting one's hand on one's chest – the heart is uneasy before haram and at ease before halal
D. Tossing a coin

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: The heart is uneasy before what is haram and at ease before what is halal. This hadith is also regarded as the definition of wara'.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q13. "Keeping the tongue from every kind of idle talk" is which level of wara'?

A. Wara' in speech
B. Wara' in thought
C. Wara' in action
D. Wara' in food

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Wara' should be present in thought, speech and action. Avoiding idle talk is wara' in speech; making one's walk, dress and conduct halal is wara' in action.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q14. What does "dar al-baqa'" refer to?

A. This perishable worldly life
B. Life in the grave
C. The everlasting life of the Hereafter
D. The mosque

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Dar al-fana' = this perishable world; dar al-baqa' = the everlasting Hereafter. Zuhd is needed so that we are not deceived by dar al-fana' and forget dar al-baqa'.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q15. Which is the correct difference between zuhd and rahbaniyyah?

A. They are the same; Islam encourages both
B. Zuhd is using the world without attachment to it; rahbaniyyah is monasticism that renounces the world completely – Islam condemns it
C. Rahbaniyyah is better; zuhd is condemned
D. Zuhd means deliberately living a hard life

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Zuhd is NOT renouncing the world completely, nor deliberately making life hard. Doing that is rahbaniyyah (monasticism), which Islam warns against and condemns (5:87 – do not make the halal haram).

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q16. "He created death and life to test which of you is best in deeds" – which verse is this?

A. 67:2
B. 57:20
C. 31:33
D. 5:87

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Verse 67:2 shows that worldly life is a test, and explains the need for detachment. Verse 57:20 describes worldly life as play and adornment.

Difficulty: Medium
