# Grade 10 – Chapter 15: The Revelation and Compilation of the Quran

## 1. The first revelation ⭐

| Point | Fact |
|---|---|
| The final scripture | The Quran – the **last** of the four scriptures |
| The Prophet's ﷺ age | **Forty** |
| Year | About **610 CE** |
| Place | **The cave of Hira'** in Makkah (while he was in seclusion) |
| Night | **Laylat al-Qadr** |
| The angel who brought it | **Jibril (AS)** |
| First verses revealed | **The first five verses of Surah al-'Alaq** |

### The event
1. Jibril (AS): **"Iqra'" (Read / Recite)** → the Prophet ﷺ: "I cannot read."
2. Jibril (AS) squeezed him tightly, released him and again said "Iqra'" – this happened **three times.**
3. "What shall I read?" → Jibril recited the first five verses of Surah al-'Alaq, and the Prophet ﷺ recited them.
4. Because of this new experience he felt **shock and fear**, returned home and said, **"Cover me."**
5. His wife **Khadijah (RA)** covered him, comforted him and gave him courage.
6. She took him to her relative **Waraqah ibn Nawfal**, who had studied the earlier scriptures. He made clear that the Prophet ﷺ was **Allah's Messenger** and that the one who had come was **the angel Jibril (AS)**.

## 2. How the Quran was revealed
- Verses were revealed **according to circumstances and needs** – one or two verses, many verses, or a whole surah at a time.
- Jibril (AS) would tell the Prophet ﷺ **before or after** which verse / surah a new verse / surah should be placed; the Prophet ﷺ would have the scribes of revelation note it.
- The Quran continued to be revealed **until the Prophet's ﷺ death**.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Tartib al-nuzul** (ترتيب النزول) | The **order in which the Quran was revealed**, step by step |
| **Tartib al-tilawah** (ترتيب التلاوة) | The order the Prophet ﷺ showed for **reciting** the Quran |

---

## 3. Stage 1: The time of the Prophet ﷺ
- The Prophet ﷺ **memorised** the verses and told Companions who could write **to write them down**.
- Those who wrote down the revelation = **kuttab al-wahy (كتّاب الوحي)**, the scribes of revelation.
- In Makkah **few** people could read and write, and writing materials were **scarce**.
- It was written on **leather, thick bones and white stones.**
- The Arabs had **very strong memories** – a special feature of that society. The Companions memorised easily.
- ⚠️ In the Prophet's ﷺ time the Quran was **not compiled into a single book.**
- Every **Ramadan**, Jibril (AS) recited the verses revealed so far, and the Prophet ﷺ **checked** his memorisation.
- In the **Ramadan before his death** he checked it **twice**; **Zayd ibn Thabit (RA)** was present and listening.

---

## 4. Stage 2: The time of Abu Bakr (RA) ⭐

### The reason
- After the Prophet's ﷺ death, **false prophets (mutanabbi'un)** and **apostates (murtaddun – those who left Islam)** caused trouble.
- A battle was fought against them at **Yamamah**.
- In that battle **many huffaz** who had memorised the Quran **were martyred.**
- **'Umar (RA)** feared that the Quran might be lost → he advised the caliph to compile it.

### What happened
- The caliph at first **hesitated**, saying it was **"something the Prophet ﷺ did not do"**; when he understood the situation he **agreed.**
- The task was given to a committee led by **Zayd ibn Thabit (RA)** (he too hesitated at first).
- **Two men were stationed at the door of al-Masjid an-Nabawi**, and it was announced that the pages written in the Prophet's ﷺ time should be handed in **with witnesses**.
- These were checked, written out and handed to the caliph → the caliph named it **"the Mushaf"**.

### The journey of the Mushaf
**Abu Bakr (RA)** → (after his death) **'Umar (RA)** → 'Umar (RA) gave it to his daughter **Hafsah (RA)**, a wife of the Prophet ﷺ.

## 5. Stage 3: The time of 'Uthman (RA) ⭐

### The reason
- In the campaigns in **Armenia and Azerbaijan**, there were soldiers from **Iraq and from Sham (Syria)**.
- They recited the Quran **in different ways** and **argued** that their own way was correct.
- **Hudhayfah ibn al-Yaman (RA)** observed this and told the caliph that Muslims must not be divided in the name of the Quran.

### Why the recitations differed
- At that time each tribe had its **own Arabic dialect** → the **pronunciation** differed; **the meaning did not conflict.**
- The Prophet ﷺ had allowed them to recite in **their own dialects**.

### What 'Uthman (RA) did
1. He formed a committee led by **Zayd ibn Thabit (RA)** that included **members of the Quraysh**.
2. Based on the Mushaf of Abu Bakr's (RA) time and **the dialect of the Quraysh**, **eight copies** were prepared.
3. They were sent to **each province**, together with **qurra'** (reciters) who could recite in the Quraysh dialect.
4. The order: recite as these reciters do; copy only from the copy that was sent; **burn all other copies held before.**

### The result
- Everyone came to recite the Quran **in one way**.
- 'Uthman (RA) = **"Jami' al-Quran" (جامع القرآن)** – the one who gathered the Quran.
- The copy of the Quran from his time = **"al-Mushaf al-'Uthmani" (المصحف العثماني).**

## 6. Allah's protection
> 📖 **(15:9) – meaning:** "Indeed, it is We who sent down this Quran, and We will surely protect it."

## 7. The three stages – comparison table

| | Time of the Prophet ﷺ | Time of Abu Bakr (RA) | Time of 'Uthman (RA) |
|---|---|---|---|
| State | Memorised + written on separate pages; not in book form | One collection – "the Mushaf" | Eight copies – one way of reciting |
| Reason | Revelation was still coming | Huffaz martyred at the Battle of Yamamah | Differences in recitation and arguments |
| Advice from | — | 'Umar (RA) | Hudhayfah ibn al-Yaman (RA) |
| Responsible | Kuttab al-wahy | Committee of Zayd ibn Thabit (RA) | Zayd ibn Thabit (RA) + a committee of Quraysh |

💡 **Zayd ibn Thabit (RA)** is linked with all three stages: he was present when the Prophet ﷺ checked the Quran in the final Ramadan, and he led both compilation committees.

## ✅ Quick revision
- First revelation: age 40, about 610 CE, the cave of Hira', Laylat al-Qadr, Surah al-'Alaq 1–5, "Iqra'" ×3.
- Khadijah (RA) comforted him; Waraqah ibn Nawfal confirmed his prophethood.
- Kuttab al-wahy = the scribes of revelation; they wrote on leather, bones and stones.
- Tartib al-nuzul = order of revelation; tartib al-tilawah = order of recitation.
- Abu Bakr (RA): Battle of Yamamah → 'Umar's advice → Zayd's committee → "the Mushaf" → 'Umar → Hafsah.
- 'Uthman (RA): Hudhayfah ibn al-Yaman's observation → 8 copies in the Quraysh dialect → Jami' al-Quran → al-Mushaf al-'Uthmani.
- 15:9 – Allah Himself will protect the Quran.
