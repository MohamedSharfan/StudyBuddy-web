# Grade 10 – Chapter 15: The Revelation and Compilation of the Quran – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_15/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q16 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. How old was the Prophet ﷺ when he received the first revelation?

A. Forty
B. Twenty-five
C. Fifty-three
D. Sixty-three

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: The first revelation came in the Prophet's ﷺ fortieth year, about 610 CE, in the cave of Hira', on Laylat al-Qadr.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. Which verses of the Quran were revealed first?

A. The first five verses of Surah al-'Alaq
B. The first five verses of Surah al-Muddaththir
C. The whole of Surah al-Fatihah
D. Ayat al-Kursi

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: After saying "Iqra'" three times, Jibril (AS) recited the first five verses of Surah al-'Alaq.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q3. After the first revelation, who took the Prophet ﷺ to Waraqah ibn Nawfal?

A. Abu Bakr (RA)
B. 'Ali (RA)
C. Khadijah (RA)
D. Abu Talib

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: His wife Khadijah (RA) covered him, comforted him, gave him courage and took him to her relative Waraqah ibn Nawfal, who had studied the earlier scriptures.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q4. Who were called "kuttab al-wahy"?

A. Those who wrote down the revelation on the Prophet's ﷺ instruction
B. Those who recite the Quran
C. False prophets
D. Those who compiled the hadiths

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: The Prophet ﷺ told Companions who could write to write down the revelation. Those who did so were called "kuttab al-wahy".

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q5. On what were the verses of the Quran written in the Prophet's ﷺ time?

A. In paper books
B. On things such as leather, thick bones and white stones
C. Only on metal plates
D. Only on cloth

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Writing materials were very scarce at that time, so they wrote on leather, thick bones, white stones and similar things.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q6. What does "tartib al-nuzul" refer to?

A. The order the Prophet ﷺ showed for reciting the Quran
B. The order in which the Quran was revealed step by step
C. A translation of the Quran
D. A method of memorising the Quran

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Nuzul = coming down; tartib al-nuzul = the order of revelation. Tilawah = recitation; tartib al-tilawah = the order of recitation.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q7. Which statement about the Quran in the Prophet's ﷺ time is correct?

A. It was compiled into one book
B. It was not written down at all
C. It was not compiled into a book; it was preserved in memory and on separate pages
D. Eight copies were made

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Since revelation continued until the Prophet's ﷺ death, it was not made into a book. The Companions memorised it, and the scribes wrote it on separate pages.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q8. Who was present when the Prophet ﷺ checked the Quran twice with Jibril (AS) in the Ramadan before his death?

A. 'Umar (RA)
B. Hudhayfah ibn al-Yaman (RA)
C. Abu Hurayrah (RA)
D. Zayd ibn Thabit (RA)

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Zayd ibn Thabit (RA) was listening. Later he led the compilation committees in the times of Abu Bakr (RA) and 'Uthman (RA).

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q9. What was the immediate reason for compiling the Quran in the time of Abu Bakr (RA)?

A. Differences in ways of recitation
B. The invention of paper
C. Many huffaz who had memorised the Quran were martyred at the Battle of Yamamah
D. The conquest of Makkah

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Many huffaz were martyred at the Battle of Yamamah against the false prophets and apostates, which raised the fear that the Quran might be lost. Differences in recitation were the reason in 'Uthman's (RA) time.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q10. Who advised Abu Bakr (RA) to compile the Quran?

A. 'Uthman (RA)
B. 'Umar (RA)
C. 'Ali (RA)
D. Zayd ibn Thabit (RA)

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: 'Umar (RA) feared the Quran might be lost, so he gave the advice. The caliph first hesitated, saying it was "something the Prophet ﷺ did not do", but later agreed.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q11. To whom was the copy of the Quran compiled in Abu Bakr's (RA) time finally handed over?

A. 'Uthman (RA)
B. 'A'ishah (RA)
C. Hafsah (RA)
D. Zayd ibn Thabit (RA)

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: The Mushaf passed from Abu Bakr (RA) → 'Umar (RA) → Hafsah (RA), 'Umar's daughter and a wife of the Prophet ﷺ.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q12. In 'Uthman's (RA) time, who noticed Muslims reciting the Quran in different ways and told the caliph?

A. Abu Hurayrah (RA)
B. 'Umar (RA)
C. Khalid ibn al-Walid (RA)
D. Hudhayfah ibn al-Yaman (RA)

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Hudhayfah ibn al-Yaman (RA) noticed the Iraqis and the people of Sham reciting in different ways and arguing during the campaigns in Armenia and Azerbaijan.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q13. Why did the people of Sham and Iraq recite the same Quran in different ways?

A. Because of the tribes' different Arabic dialects the pronunciation differed; the meaning did not conflict
B. They had different Qurans
C. They had changed the Quran
D. They did not know Arabic

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Each tribe had its own dialect, so only the pronunciation differed. The Prophet ﷺ had allowed each to recite in their own dialect.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q14. How many copies of the Quran were prepared and sent to the provinces in 'Uthman's (RA) time?

A. Four
B. Eight
C. Six
D. Ten

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Based on the Mushaf of Abu Bakr's (RA) time and the Quraysh dialect, eight copies were prepared and sent to the provinces with reciters.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q15. Why is 'Uthman (RA) called "Jami' al-Quran"?

A. He was the first to memorise the Quran
B. He was the first of the scribes of revelation
C. He translated the Quran
D. He gathered the Quran so that everyone recited it in one way

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: He sent copies in the Quraysh dialect, ordered the others to be burnt, and united the whole ummah in one way of reciting. So he is called "Jami' al-Quran" (the one who gathered the Quran).

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q16. "Indeed, it is We who sent down this Quran, and We will surely protect it" – which verse is this?

A. 2:2
B. 62:2
C. 96:1
D. 15:9

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Verse 15:9 says that Allah Himself has taken responsibility for protecting the Quran. That is why it remains unchanged until today.

Difficulty: Easy
