- The importance of good character
- Arabic words that mean good character

- Good qualities described in Surah Luqman and Surah al-Furqan

---

## 1. Why is good character important?
- Good character **defines** what a mature, acceptable life should look like.
- It should show in **every part of life, in every situation, at all times**.
- Good character is what gives human beings a **unique, high position** that sets them apart from other living things.
- **Every society** that has appeared in the world has given good character a special place.

---

## 2. Arabic words that mean good character

| Arabic word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Khuluq hasan** (خُلُق حَسَن) | Good character |
| **Akhlaq hasanah** (أخلاق حسنة) | Good characteristics (plural) |
| **Makarim al-akhlaq** (مكارم الأخلاق) | Noble / the finest characteristics |
| **Adab** (أدب) / **Adab (plural: Aadab)** (آداب) | Good manners / manners |

- The **Quran and the Sunnah** play a major role in teaching good character.

---

## 3. The Prophet ﷺ – the peak of good character
- The Quran describes the Prophet ﷺ as **the peak of good character** and tells us to **learn good character from him**.
- "**The best of you are those who are best in character**" – this shows how necessary it is to live with good character.
- The Prophet ﷺ had good character from childhood:
  - **Al-Amin (الأمين)** – the trustworthy one
  - **As-Sadiq (الصادق)** – the truthful one
- Even after he was chosen as a prophet, he dealt with people with good character.
- His good character led even **the bitterest enemies of Islam** to accept Islam and become its **defenders**.
- One reason Islam spread **beyond Arabia** was the good character it trained people in.

---

## 4. Good qualities in Surah Luqman
The advice Luqman gave his son on how a Muslim should behave (31:13–19)*:
1. **Do not associate anything** with Allah.
2. Establish the **prayer**.
3. **Enjoin good and forbid evil.**
4. Be **patient** in hardship.
5. **Do not turn your face away** from people (do not treat them with contempt).
6. **Do not walk proudly** on the earth.
7. Be **moderate** in your walk.
8. **Lower your voice** – the most hated of voices is the **braying of the donkey**.

> ⚠️ *The textbook prints this verse reference as "(12:19)". Surah Luqman is the 31st surah – confirm with the teacher.

---

## 5. Qualities of the true servants of Allah ('Ibad ar-Rahman) in Surah al-Furqan
(25:63–73)*:
1. They walk on the earth **humbly**.
2. When ignorant people address them, they **do not argue but withdraw with dignity**.
3. They spend much of **the night** worshipping their Lord.
4. They seek protection from **the punishment of Hell**.
5. They spend **moderately, neither wastefully nor stingily**.
6. They do not call on anything **as a partner** with Allah.
7. They do not **kill** unjustly.
8. They do not commit **adultery (zina)**.
9. They do not give **false witness**.
10. When they pass by idle things, they **pass by with dignity**.
11. When they hear the verses of the Quran, they do not behave **as if blind and deaf**.
12. They pray for **a happy family**.

> ⚠️ *The textbook prints this verse reference as "(63:73)". Surah al-Furqan is the 25th surah – confirm with the teacher.
