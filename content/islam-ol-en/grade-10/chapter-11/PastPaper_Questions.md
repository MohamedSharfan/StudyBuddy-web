## Part B: Structured and Essay Questions (Islam II)

### B1. 2017 – Islam II, Q4 (i), (ii)

Question ID: P2017-II-Q4.1  
Year: 2017  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q4 (i), (ii)  
Marks: 10 marks for the whole of Q4  
Source: OL_2017_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(i) What is the importance of morality (akhlaq) in Islam?
(ii) State three moral qualities mentioned in Surah Luqman.

**Suggested answer:**

(i) - It is good character that defines a mature, accepted life.
- Morality is the reason man stands apart from other creatures and reaches a high rank.
- The Prophet ﷺ said, "I was sent only to perfect good character"; "the best of you are those best in character".
- The Prophet's good character turned even enemies into guardians of Islam, and was a reason Islam spread beyond Arabia.

(ii) Any three of the following (31:13–19):
1. Not associating partners with Allah
2. Establishing prayer
3. Enjoining good and forbidding evil
4. Patience in hardship
5. Not turning one's face away from people; not walking with arrogance
6. Moderation in walking; lowering one's voice

**Answer explanation:** As (ii) asks for moral qualities, points 5 and 6 are the most suitable.

**Marking guidance (suggested only – not an official marking scheme):** No official marking scheme.

**Answer status:** Suggested – not an official answer (no official marking scheme was supplied); based on the Grade 10/11 textbook content

**Verification note:** Part (iii) of this question is under the chapter on Abu Bakr (RA) (Grade 11, Chapter 18).

### B2. 2019 – Islam II, Q3 (iii)

Question ID: P2019-II-Q3.3  
Year: 2019  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q3 (iii)  
Marks: 10 marks for the whole of Q3  
Source: OL_2019_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(iii) List and explain five good qualities mentioned in Surah al-Furqan.

**Suggested answer:**

Any five of the following (25:63–73 – the qualities of 'Ibad ar-Rahman):
1. They walk on the earth humbly – modestly, without arrogance.
2. When the ignorant argue with them, they say "salam" and withdraw with dignity.
3. They worship their Lord at night, prostrating and standing.
4. They seek protection from the punishment of the Fire.
5. They spend moderately, without extravagance or miserliness.
6. They do not associate partners with Allah; they do not kill unjustly; they do not commit adultery.
7. They do not give false testimony; when they pass by idle talk they pass by with dignity.
8. They pray for a happy family.

**Answer explanation:** As the question says "list and explain", a brief explanation is needed for each quality.

**Marking guidance (suggested only – not an official marking scheme):** No official marking scheme.
