# Grade 10 – Chapter 11: The Islamic View of Good Character – I – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_11/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q12 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. According to the textbook, what gives human beings a unique, high position that sets them apart from other living things?

A. Physical strength
B. Wealth
C. Good character
D. Scientific discoveries

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: It is good character that raises a human being to a unique, high position. This is why every society throughout history has given good character a special place.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. Which of the following Arabic words does NOT refer to good character?

A. Khuluq hasan
B. Makarim al-akhlaq
C. Aadab
D. Najasah

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Najasah means impurity. Khuluq hasan, akhlaq hasanah, makarim al-akhlaq and adab/aadab are the words that refer to good character.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q3. By what names was the Prophet ﷺ called before he became a prophet?

A. Al-Amin, as-Sadiq
B. Al-Faruq, as-Siddiq
C. Al-Khalil, al-Kalim
D. Ar-Rahim, al-Karim

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Because he had excellent character from childhood, the Prophet ﷺ was called "al-Amin" (the trustworthy) and "as-Sadiq" (the truthful). Al-Faruq and as-Siddiq are the titles of 'Umar (RA) and Abu Bakr (RA).

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q4. What result of the Prophet's ﷺ good character does the textbook mention?

A. Even the bitterest enemies of Islam accepted Islam and became its defenders
B. Everyone immediately became rich
C. No wars took place at all
D. Islam spread only within Arabia

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: The Prophet's ﷺ good character turned even enemies into defenders of Islam. Also, the good character Islam trained people in helped it spread beyond Arabia – so D is wrong.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q5. According to Surah Luqman, which is the most hated of voices?

A. The sound of thunder
B. The braying of the donkey
C. The barking of a dog
D. The sound of the wind

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Luqman advises, "Lower your voice; the most hated of voices is the braying of the donkey." The lesson is to speak modestly.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q6. Which of the following is NOT part of Luqman's advice in Surah Luqman?

A. Enjoining good and forbidding evil
B. Being patient in hardship
C. Being moderate in one's walk
D. Fasting

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Luqman's advice in the textbook: not associating partners, prayer, enjoining good, patience, not treating people with contempt, no pride, walking moderately, lowering the voice. Fasting is not mentioned here.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q7. According to Surah al-Furqan, what do Allah's true servants do when ignorant people come to argue with them?

A. Answer them harshly
B. Fight with them
C. Do not argue but withdraw with dignity
D. Complain about them to others

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: The servants of Allah do not get involved in useless arguments with ignorant people but withdraw with dignity. This is a quality of patience and self-control.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q8. What does Surah al-Furqan say about how the servants of Allah spend?

A. They spend everything
B. They spend nothing
C. They spend moderately, neither wastefully nor stingily
D. They spend to show off

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Islam loves moderation. Both excessive spending (waste) and miserliness are wrong.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q9. Which of the following is NOT one of the qualities of the servants of Allah described in Surah al-Furqan?

A. Worshipping much at night
B. Not giving false witness
C. Praying for a happy family
D. Walking proudly on the earth

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: The servants of Allah walk humbly on the earth. Walking proudly is not their quality; Luqman also forbade it.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q10. According to Surah al-Furqan, how do the servants of Allah NOT behave when they hear the verses of the Quran?

A. As if listening attentively
B. As if blind and deaf
C. Weeping
D. Prostrating

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: When they hear the verses of the Quran, they do not behave as if blind and deaf (careless); instead they reflect on them and accept them.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q11. What does the saying "The best of you are those who are best in character" stress?

A. The necessity and importance of living with good character
B. The importance of wealth
C. Only the importance of knowledge
D. The importance of family background

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: In Islam a person's excellence is measured by his character, not by wealth or family name.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q12. Luqman's advice "Do not turn your face away from people" forbids which bad quality?

A. Miserliness
B. Pride that treats others with contempt
C. Lying
D. Laziness

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Turning one's face away from others shows pride that looks down on them. That is why "Do not walk proudly on the earth" is said together with it.

Difficulty: Hard
