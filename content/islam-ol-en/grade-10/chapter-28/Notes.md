# Grade 10 – Chapter 28: Physical and Mental Health

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_28/Notes.md` (Tamil). English rendering.

## 🎯 What you will learn
- Health – a blessing
- Four ways to keep the body healthy: eating habits, exercise, cleanliness, rest
- Health of the heart (mind) – why it comes first
- Causes of diseases of the heart and their remedies

---

## Part 1: Physical health 💪

### Health – a great blessing
> 📖 **Hadith (Tirmidhi) – meaning:** "Whoever wakes up in the morning **healthy in body, safe in his home, and with food for that day**, it is as if he has been given **the whole world with everything in it**."

- The Prophet ﷺ prayed for health **every day**:
  > (meaning) "O Allah! Grant health to my **body**, and to my **sight and hearing**." **(Abu Dawud)**
- > 📖 **(Muslim) – meaning:** "**The strong believer** is better and more beloved to Allah than the weak believer."

### Why does a Muslim need health?
- Physical health is needed to carry out **the basic acts of worship, social service and work**.
- The Companions made themselves strong, living as **"horsemen by day and devout worshippers by night"**.
- The Prophet ﷺ (meaning): "Take advantage of **five things before five things**" – one of them: take advantage of **health before sickness**.
- **A healthy heart can be found only in a healthy body.**

---

### Way 1: Eating habits 🍎 ⭐
- **Moderation** in food; avoid overeating and food **that does not suit the body**.

> 📖 **Hadith (Tirmidhi) – meaning:** "A person fills no vessel **worse than his stomach**. If he must eat, then **one third for food, one third for drink and one third for air**."

| Part of the stomach | For |
|---|---|
| ⅓ | Food |
| ⅓ | Water |
| ⅓ | Air |

#### 'Umar's (RA) explanation (Kanz al-'Ummal)
- I warn you against filling your stomachs with food and drink.
- Eating until full **ruins the body, causes disease and brings laziness in prayer.**
- Moderation **keeps the body sound and avoids waste.**
- Allah **dislikes bloated bodies.**
- A person who chooses **his desires over his religion will be ruined.**

#### Things to avoid
- **Smoking and drug use** – must be avoided completely; they are not only **haram**, they also **destroy** health and well-being.
- Becoming addicted to **energy drinks**.
- Even permitted drinks such as **coffee and tea** harm health if taken **in excess**.

### Way 2: Exercise 🏊 ⭐
- Keeping the body in good shape is also **'ibadah.**
- **Benefits:** strong and healthy organs; stronger **immunity**; protection from germs; energetic movement.
- Islam encourages it: "Teach your children **swimming, archery and horse riding**."
- Exercise should take into account **age, weight and disability**.

### Way 3: Cleanliness 🚿
- Cleanliness not only of body and clothes, but also of **food, home, workplace and things we use**.
> 📖 **Hadith (Bukhari) – meaning:** "Even if you are not in a state requiring ghusl, **take a bath on Friday, wash your head and apply perfume**."
- Making the body smell pleasant with **pure perfumes** is praiseworthy.

### Way 4: Rest 😴
- A tired body cannot work energetically; rest removes **weariness, fatigue and exhaustion** and brings **freshness**.
> 📖 **(28:73) – meaning:** Out of His mercy He made **night and day** for you – so that you may **rest** at night, **seek His bounty** by day, and be grateful.

> 📖 **Hadith (Bukhari) – meaning:** "There are two blessings that many people **neglect**: **health and free time (rest)**."

💡 **Memory tip – 4 ways to physical health:** **F**ood – **E**xercise – **C**leanliness – **R**est.

## Part 2: Health of the heart (mental health) 🧠❤️

### Priority to the heart
- Islam asks us to give **priority to the heart** just as to the body.
- Allah accepts the deeds of the body **only when the heart is free from stains**.
> 📖 **(Bukhari, Muslim) – meaning:** "Allah does not look at your **bodies or your forms**; He looks at your **hearts and your deeds**."

- In Islam, **good deeds of the heart carry more reward than good deeds of the limbs**.
- **Sins of the heart are more serious than sins of the limbs.**

### Adam (AS) vs Iblis ⭐
| | Adam (AS) | Iblis |
|---|---|---|
| Kind of sin | A sin **of the limbs** | A sin **of the heart** |
| Did he realise it? | **He realised it and asked forgiveness** | **He never realised it** |
| Result | Allah **forgave him** | He was **cursed and expelled** |

- A person **realises a sin of the limbs at once**; the chance of realising a sin of the heart is **small**.
- **(91:9–10) – meaning:** Whoever purifies the soul succeeds; whoever buries it in sins fails.

### The heart drives the body
> 📖 **(Bukhari) – meaning:** "There is a **piece of flesh** in the body. If it is sound, the whole body is sound; if it is corrupt, the whole body is corrupt. **That is the heart.**"
- **The medical world** accepts that when the mind is affected, the body becomes ill.
- People look at the body; **Allah looks at the heart.**
- The heart is something special to human beings; **only the heart has the power to know God**.

### Causes of diseases of the heart
| Causes of bodily disease | Causes of disease of the heart |
|---|---|
| Malnutrition | **Weak faith** in Allah |
| Infections | **Excessive desire** |
| Accidents | **Satan's prompting** |
| | **An undisciplined way of life** |
| | Living **to please others** |

### Diseases of the heart that result
**Pride, envy, looking down on others, being drawn to scheming and fraud, selfishness.**
→ Such people have **no brightness** in their faces, **no contentment** in life, and are always **confused**.

### Ways to nourish the heart ⭐
- Always **remembering Allah**
- **Dhikr, du'a, istighfar**
- Spending at least a little time **alone in munajat** (intimate conversation) with Allah
- **Reflecting on creation**
- **Night worship**
- Increasing **sunnah prayers**
- **Voluntary (nafl) fasts**
- **Giving charity**
