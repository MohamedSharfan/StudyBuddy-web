# Grade 10 – Chapter 28: Physical and Mental Health – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_28/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q16 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. According to the hadith in Tirmidhi, when is a person as if "given the whole world with everything in it"?

A. When he has a lot of wealth
B. When he has many friends
C. When he gets a high position
D. When he wakes up with a healthy body, a safe home and food for that day

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Health, safety and the day's food are such great blessings that having all three is equal to having the whole world.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. "The strong believer is better and more beloved to Allah than the weak believer" – in which collection is this hadith?

A. Muslim
B. Bukhari
C. Tirmidhi
D. Abu Dawud

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: This hadith in Muslim shows the importance of keeping physical strength and health. The du'a asking for health "in my body, sight and hearing" is in Abu Dawud.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q3. How does the textbook describe the way of life by which the Companions made themselves strong?

A. Traders by day and sleepers by night
B. Always soldiers
C. Always ascetics
D. Horsemen by day and devout worshippers by night

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: They lived as horsemen active with physical strength by day and as worshippers by night, keeping body and heart in balance.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q4. According to the hadith in Tirmidhi, how should the stomach be divided when eating?

A. One third food, one third water, one third air
B. Half food, half water
C. All food
D. Three quarters food

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Saying "A person fills no vessel worse than his stomach", the Prophet ﷺ told us to divide it into ⅓ food, ⅓ water, ⅓ air.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q5. According to 'Umar (RA), which of the following is NOT a result of eating until full?

A. It ruins the body
B. It increases memory
C. It brings laziness in prayer
D. It causes disease

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: 'Umar's (RA) statement recorded in Kanz al-'Ummal: eating until full ruins the body, causes disease and brings laziness in prayer.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q6. What does the textbook say about smoking and drug use?

A. They may be used in moderation
B. They are only makruh
C. They are not only haram but also destroy health and well-being; they must be avoided completely
D. Adults may use them

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Smoking and drugs must be avoided completely. Even permitted things such as coffee and tea harm health if taken in excess.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q7. Which sports does Islam say we should teach our children?

A. Football, cricket, tennis
B. Running, weightlifting, boxing
C. Swimming, archery, horse riding
D. Wrestling, chess, kabaddi

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Islam teaches: teach them swimming, archery and horse riding.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q8. "Even if you are not in a state requiring ghusl, take a bath on Friday, wash your head and apply perfume" – which way to health is this linked to?

A. Eating habits
B. Cleanliness
C. Exercise
D. Rest

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: This hadith (Bukhari) shows that Islam stresses the need to keep the body clean.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q9. Which verse says He made night and day "so that you may rest at night and seek His bounty by day"?

A. 91:9
B. 28:73
C. 2:255
D. 13:28

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Verse 28:73 shows the importance of rest; through rest, tiredness goes and freshness comes.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q10. According to the hadith in Bukhari, which two blessings do many people neglect?

A. Wealth and position
B. Education and family
C. Health and free time (rest)
D. Friends and food

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: While people have health and free time, they do not realise their value and neglect them.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q11. Which statement about sins is correct from the Islamic point of view?

A. Sins of the limbs are more serious than sins of the heart
B. Sins of the heart are more serious than sins of the limbs
C. Both are equal
D. The heart has no sins

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: A person realises a sin of the limbs at once; the chance of realising a sin of the heart is small. So sins of the heart are more serious.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q12. Why was Iblis's sin regarded as greater than the sin of Adam (AS)?

A. Because Iblis sinned first
B. Because Iblis committed many sins
C. Adam (AS) realised his sin of the limbs and asked forgiveness; Iblis's sin was a sin of the heart – he never realised it
D. Adam (AS) committed no sin at all

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Adam (AS) was forgiven; Iblis did not realise the sin of his heart (pride), so he was cursed and expelled.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q13. "There is a piece of flesh in the body; if it is sound, the whole body is sound" – what is the main point of this hadith?

A. Food is important
B. Exercise is necessary
C. It is about heart disease
D. Although the heart is hidden, it is what drives the body

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: If the heart is good, actions are sound; if it is corrupt, actions are corrupt too. The medical world also accepts that when the mind is affected, the body becomes ill.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q14. Which of the following is NOT a cause of disease of the heart mentioned in the textbook?

A. Taking part in night worship
B. Excessive desire
C. Living to please others
D. Weak faith in Allah

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Night worship nourishes the heart. Weak faith, excessive desire, Satan's prompting, an undisciplined life and living to please others are the causes.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q15. What does "munajat" refer to?

A. Praying in congregation
B. Speaking intimately (praying) to Allah while alone
C. Advising others
D. Fasting

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Spending at least a little time alone in munajat with Allah is one of the ways to nourish the heart.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q16. According to this chapter, what is special to human beings and has the power to know God?

A. The heart
B. The brain
C. The eye
D. The tongue

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: People look at the body; Allah looks at the heart. Only the heart has the power to know God.

Difficulty: Medium
