### B2. 2022 (2023) – Islam II, Q4 (iii)

Question ID: P2022_2023-II-Q4.3  
Year: 2022 (2023)  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q4 (iii)  
Marks: 10 marks for the whole of Q4  
Source: OL_2022_2023_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(iii) Explain mental (spiritual) health with evidence from the Quran and the hadith.

**Suggested answer:**

- Islam gives priority to the heart just as to the body; deeds are accepted only when the heart is free of stain – "Allah does not look at your bodies or your forms, but He looks at your hearts and your deeds" (Bukhari, Muslim).
- The heart drives the body – "In the body there is a piece of flesh… it is the heart" (Bukhari).
- He who purifies it succeeds; he who corrupts it fails (91:9–10).
- Sins of the heart are more serious than sins of the limbs – Adam (AS) realised his error and asked forgiveness; Iblis (a sin of the heart – pride) did not.
- Causes of a sick heart: weakness of belief in Allah, etc. → diseases of the heart such as envy, pride and anger.
- Ways of nourishing the heart: dhikr ("in the remembrance of Allah hearts find rest" – 13:28), reciting the Quran, prayer, tawbah, the company of good people.

**Answer explanation:** Evidence is needed from both the Quran and the hadith.

**Marking guidance (suggested only – not an official marking scheme):** No official marking scheme.

**Answer status:** Suggested – not an official answer (no official marking scheme was supplied); based on the Grade 10/11 textbook content

**Verification note:** Check verse 13:28 and the list of ways of nourishing the heart against the textbook.

### B4. 2025 (2026) – Islam II, Q7 (iii), (iv)

Question ID: P2025_2026-II-Q7.3  
Year: 2025 (2026)  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q7 (iii), (iv)  
Marks: 10 marks (short notes on two topics)  
Source: OL_2025_2026_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(iii) The evils of drug use
(iv) Eating habits in Islam

**Suggested answer:**

(iii) The evils of drug use:
- Smoking and drug use are haram; they destroy physical health and well-being.
- By analogy with alcohol (qiyas), other intoxicating drugs are also haram.
- Protecting the intellect is one of the aims of the Shari'ah; intoxication destroys the mind.
- Harm to mental health; family breakdown, economic loss, crime, social decay.
- Destroying oneself is a great offence; neglect of worship.

(iv) Eating habits in Islam:
- Moderation in food; ⅓ food, ⅓ drink, ⅓ air (Tirmidhi).
- Eat and drink, but do not waste (7:31).
- Halal and pure food; avoiding what does not suit the body.
- 'Umar (RA): the warning about filling the stomach (Kanz al-'Ummal).

**Answer explanation:** Write on the topic chosen with evidence from the Quran and hadith.

**Marking guidance (suggested only – not an official marking scheme):** No official marking scheme.
