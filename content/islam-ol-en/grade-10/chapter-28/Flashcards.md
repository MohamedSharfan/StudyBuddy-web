# Grade 10 – Chapter 28: Physical and Mental Health – Flashcards

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_28/Flashcards.md` (Tamil), cards 1–13 in the same order.

---

Flashcard 1

Front: What are the four ways to keep the body healthy?

Back: Eating habits, exercise, cleanliness, rest.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 28 | Topic: Physical health

---

Flashcard 2

Front: What is the meaning of the Prophet's ﷺ du'a for health (Abu Dawud)?

Back: O Allah! Grant health to my body, and to my sight and hearing.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 28 | Topic: Physical health

---

Flashcard 3

Front: "Take advantage of five things before five things" – which one does this chapter mention?

Back: Taking advantage of health before sickness.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 28 | Topic: Physical health

---

Flashcard 4

Front: What are the three parts of the stomach (Tirmidhi)?

Back: ⅓ food, ⅓ water, ⅓ air.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 28 | Topic: Eating habits

---

Flashcard 5

Front: كنز العمال (Kanz al-'Ummal)

Back: A hadith collection – it contains 'Umar's (RA) statement on moderation in eating.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 28 | Topic: Eating habits

---

Flashcard 6

Front: What are four benefits of exercise?

Back: Strong organs, stronger immunity, protection from germs, energetic movement.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 28 | Topic: Exercise

---

Flashcard 7

Front: Which three sports does Islam encourage?

Back: Swimming, archery, horse riding.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 28 | Topic: Exercise

---

Flashcard 8

Front: Which two blessings do many people neglect (Bukhari)?

Back: Health and free time (rest).

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 28 | Topic: Rest

---

Flashcard 9

Front: The difference between the sins of Adam (AS) and Iblis?

Back: Adam – a sin of the limbs; he realised it, asked forgiveness and was forgiven. Iblis – a sin of the heart; he did not realise it and was cursed.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 28 | Topic: Health of the heart

---

Flashcard 10

Front: What are the five causes of disease of the heart?

Back: Weak faith, excessive desire, Satan's prompting, an undisciplined life, living to please others.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 28 | Topic: Health of the heart

---

Flashcard 11

Front: Five examples of diseases of the heart?

Back: Pride, envy, looking down on others, scheming and fraud, selfishness.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 28 | Topic: Health of the heart

---

Flashcard 12

Front: مناجاة (Munajat)

Back: Speaking intimately with / praying to Allah while alone.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 28 | Topic: Nourishing the heart

---

Flashcard 13

Front: What are the ways to nourish the heart?

Back: Dhikr, du'a, istighfar, munajat, reflecting on creation, night worship, sunnah prayers, nafl fasts, charity.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 28 | Topic: Nourishing the heart
