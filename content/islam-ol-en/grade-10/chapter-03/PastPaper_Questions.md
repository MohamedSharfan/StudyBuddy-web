### A1. 2017 – Islam I, Q2

Question ID: P2017-I-Q02  
Year: 2017  
Paper and question: Islam I, Q2  
Marks: not stated on the paper (Islam I multiple-choice question)  
Source: OL_2017_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

The term showing that the prophets were protected from sins

- **A.** mu'jizat
- **B.** 'ismah
- **C.** rahmah
- **D.** hidayah

**Correct answer:** B – 'ismah *(option (2) on the original paper)*

**Explanation:** 'Ismah = the prophets being protected from sins and errors. Mu'jizat = miracles; hidayah = guidance.
