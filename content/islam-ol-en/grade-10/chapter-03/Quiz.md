# Grade 10 – Chapter 3: Risalah and Hidayah (Messengership and Guidance) – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_03/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q13 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. What does "risalah" refer to?

A. The guidance and teachings that Allah gave to His messengers
B. Laws made by people using their own reason
C. The readiness of the heart to accept right guidance
D. The reckoning on the Day of Judgement

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Risalah (the divine message) is the guidance and teachings that Allah gave to His messengers. The readiness to accept right guidance is "hidayah" – the two must not be confused.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. Which statement about "hidayah" is correct?

A. All people receive it automatically
B. It is obtained by human effort alone
C. It is the readiness to accept and follow the risalah; only those whom Allah wills receive it
D. It is the name of a Book

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Although the risalah is conveyed to everyone, hidayah – the blessing of accepting and following it – is given only to those whom Allah wills.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q3. According to the lesson, in which area can human reason NOT give firm guidance on its own?

A. Technological progress
B. Moral values and the unseen
C. Scientific discoveries
D. Mathematical calculations

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Human reason achieves amazing things in technology and science. But divine guidance is needed to know high moral values and the unseen.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q4. Which verse shows that "reward or punishment is given only after divine guidance has been given"?

A. 6:90
B. 2:285
C. 3:101
D. 20:134

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Verse 20:134 says that if people had been punished before a messenger came, they would have asked, "Why did You not send us a messenger?" Because Allah is just, He judges only after giving guidance.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q5. "These are the ones whom Allah guided; so follow their guidance" (6:90) – this verse is evidence for which need for risalah?

A. The need for a practical example
B. The need for social unity
C. The need to know the unseen
D. The need to follow one's forefathers

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: A practical example has a greater effect than preaching alone. Because the messengers were such examples, this verse tells us to follow them.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q6. According to the lesson, what would have happened if only human reason had been given the right to guide?

A. Everyone would have held the same opinion
B. Science would not have developed
C. Human society would have split into thousands of groups and unity would have been impossible
D. The Books would not have been needed

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Because each person's reason thinks differently, society would split if reason were the only guide. Risalah shows the points on which people must unite, and so protects unity.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q7. What is the total number of suhuf revealed before the Books?

A. 100
B. 110
C. 104
D. 120

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Adam 10 + Shith 50 + Idris 30 + Ibrahim 10 + Musa 10 = 110 suhuf.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q8. Which prophet was given the largest number of suhuf (50)?

A. Adam (AS)
B. Idris (AS)
C. Shith (AS)
D. Ibrahim (AS)

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Shith (AS) was given 50 suhuf. Idris (AS) was given 30, and Adam, Ibrahim and Musa (AS) were given 10 each.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q9. The Quran mentions the suhuf of which prophets only?

A. Adam and Shith
B. Idris and Adam
C. Shith and Idris
D. Ibrahim and Musa

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Although suhuf were given to five prophets, the Quran mentions only the suhuf of Ibrahim (AS) and Musa (AS).

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q10. To whom was the Zabur revealed?

A. Musa (AS)
B. Dawud (AS)
C. Isa (AS)
D. Ibrahim (AS)

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Zabur – Dawud (AS); Tawrah – Musa (AS); Injil – Isa (AS); Furqan (the Quran) – Muhammad ﷺ.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q11. Which prophet was given both suhuf and a Book?

A. Musa (AS)
B. Isa (AS)
C. Dawud (AS)
D. Adam (AS)

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Musa (AS) was given 10 suhuf and the Tawrah. According to the lesson, Adam (AS) was given only suhuf, and Dawud and Isa (AS) were given only Books.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q12. Which verse speaks about those who, when told to follow what Allah has sent down, say, "We will follow the way of our forefathers"?

A. 3:101
B. 2:285
C. 31:21
D. 6:90

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Verse 31:21 condemns those who leave divine guidance and blindly follow their forefathers. It asks: even if Satan was calling their forefathers to Hell?

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q13. What must a Muslim be certain of regarding the final Book?

A. There is no need to believe in the earlier Books
B. Following the Quran, the final Book, is the final choice for right guidance
C. Any Book may be followed
D. Only the suhuf should be followed

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: A Muslim must believe that all the Books came from Allah (2:285). At the same time, following the Quran, the final Book, is the final choice for right guidance.

Difficulty: Medium
