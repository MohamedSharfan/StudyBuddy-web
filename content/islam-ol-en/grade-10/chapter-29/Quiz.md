# Grade 10 – Chapter 29: Keeping to Moderation – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_29/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q15 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. What does "wasatiyyah" mean?

A. Extremism
B. Harshness
C. Monasticism
D. Taking the middle position between two extremes

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Wasatiyyah is the middle way between two extremes. Islam loves this in all matters.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. Which verse calls the Muslim community "ummatan wasatan" (a balanced community)?

A. 17:29
B. 2:143
C. 7:31
D. 2:201

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: 2:143 – "We have made you a balanced community, so that you may be witnesses over mankind, and the Messenger a witness over you."

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q3. Which of the following goes against the position of wasatiyyah?

A. Being extreme only in worldly matters
B. Being extreme only in spiritual matters
C. Both A and B
D. Keeping body and soul in balance

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Being extreme only in worldly matters or only in spiritual matters both go against moderation. Keeping both in balance is wasatiyyah.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q4. What was the essence of what the Prophet ﷺ told 'Abdullah ibn 'Amr (RA), who fasted all day and prayed all night?

A. Worship even more
B. Give up worship completely
C. Your body, your wife and your visitors have rights over you; fast and also break your fast; pray and also sleep
D. Perform hajj

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: The Prophet ﷺ taught moderation even in worship; on hearing this, Ibn 'Amr (RA) eased his harshness (Bukhari).

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q5. Which of the following was NOT one of the decisions of the three Companions who thought their worship was not enough?

A. I will pray all night without sleeping
B. I will fast all year
C. I will perform hajj every year
D. I will never marry

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: The three decisions: praying all night, fasting all year, and not marrying. Hajj was not mentioned.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q6. What final warning did the Prophet ﷺ give after hearing the three Companions' decisions?

A. "There is no need to marry"
B. "You are the best"
C. "Do even more"
D. "Whoever turns away from my Sunnah is not of me"

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: The Prophet ﷺ, who feared Allah most, himself fasted and broke his fast, prayed and slept, and married – this balanced Sunnah must be followed.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q7. What did the Prophet ﷺ sternly ask three times of Mu'adh ibn Jabal (RA), who led the prayer reciting a long surah?

A. "Mu'adh! Are you putting people to trial?"
B. "Why did you come late?"
C. "Why did you recite a short surah?"
D. "Why did you lead as imam?"

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: The imam must consider the condition of those praying behind him. The Prophet ﷺ said, "Make things easy, do not make them hard; encourage, do not drive away" (Muslim).

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q8. Which of the following was NOT part of the Prophet's ﷺ advice in the Mu'adh (RA) incident?

A. Make things easy for people
B. Always recite long surahs
C. Encourage people
D. Do not make things hard

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Mu'adh (RA) was criticised precisely for reciting a long surah. The advice: make things easy, do not make them hard, encourage, do not drive away.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q9. Who was sent during hajj to collect pebbles for throwing at the jamarat and brought back small pebbles?

A. Ibn 'Abbas (RA)
B. Abu Hurayrah (RA)
C. Ibn 'Umar (RA)
D. Anas (RA)

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: When people thought big stones were better, Ibn 'Abbas (RA) brought small pebbles. The Prophet ﷺ approved them and warned against extremes in religion (Ahmad).

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q10. How many times did the Prophet ﷺ say "Ruined are those who go to extremes"?

A. Once
B. Three times
C. Twice
D. Seven times

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: He said it three times as a warning (Muslim). Imam an-Nawawi (RH) explains that these are people who go beyond the limits with excessive strictness.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q11. Which scholar explained the phrase "those who go to extremes"?

A. Imam an-Nawawi
B. Imam ash-Shafi'i
C. Imam al-Mawardi
D. Imam al-Ghazali

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Imam an-Nawawi (RH) explains them as people who go beyond the limits and act with excessive strictness.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q12. According to the hadith in Musnad Ahmad, where are the remains of earlier peoples, who placed heavy burdens on themselves and were destroyed, found?

A. In deserts
B. In monasteries and hermitages
C. In the seas
D. In the mountains

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Those who placed heavy burdens on themselves in religion were destroyed; their remains are in monasteries and hermitages – the result of monasticism.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q13. "Eat and drink, but do not waste by excess" – which verse is this?

A. 31:19
B. 17:29
C. 7:31
D. 2:143

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: 7:31 stresses moderation in food and drink. 17:29 is about spending, and 31:19 about walking and speech.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q14. According to verse 17:29, what two extremes must be avoided in spending?

A. Trade and debt
B. Saving and investment
C. Charity and zakat
D. Miserliness and extravagance

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Tying the hand to the neck (miserliness) and stretching it out completely (extravagance) are both wrong; the middle way between them is right.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q15. According to verses 2:200–201, who is praised?

A. Those who ask for good in this world and the Hereafter and for protection from the Fire
B. Those who ask for good only in this world
C. Those who think only of the Hereafter and renounce the world
D. Those who ask for nothing

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Those who ask only for this world have no share in the Hereafter. Asking for the good of both this world and the Hereafter is moderation – and it is praised.

Difficulty: Medium
