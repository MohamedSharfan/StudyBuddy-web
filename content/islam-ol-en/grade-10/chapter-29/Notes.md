# Grade 10 – Chapter 29: Keeping to Moderation

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_29/Notes.md` (Tamil). English rendering.

## 🎯 What you will learn
- What wasatiyyah (moderation) is
- Ummatan wasatan – the balanced community
- Moderation in worship – incidents from the hadith
- Moderation in spending, food, walking and speech
- The warning against going to extremes in religion
- Balance between this world and the Hereafter

---

## 1. What is wasatiyyah (وسطية)? ⭐
> 📖 **Taking the middle position between two extremes** = wasatiyyah.

- Islam loves this middle way **in all matters** and commands us to follow it.

> 📖 **(2:143) – meaning:** "And thus We have made you (O believers) **a balanced, middle community**, so that you may be **witnesses** over mankind, and the Messenger a witness over you."

- The Muslim community = **"ummatan wasatan" (أمة وسطا) – a balanced (middle) community.**
- So **all matters** of social and individual life should follow a balanced way.

### What goes against wasatiyyah ❌
- Being extreme **only in worldly matters**.
- Being extreme **only in spiritual matters**.

---

## 2. Moderation in worship

### (1) The incident of 'Abdullah ibn 'Amr (RA) (Bukhari) ⭐
- The Prophet ﷺ: "I have heard that you **fast all day and stand in prayer all night**." – "Yes."
- The Prophet ﷺ (meaning): "**Do not do that.** Fast, and also leave fasting. Pray (at night), and also sleep. **Your body has a right over you, your wife has a right over you, and your visitors have a right over you.**"
- 👉 Hearing this, Ibn 'Amr (RA) **eased the harshness** with which he had approached his spiritual practice.

### (2) The incident of the three Companions (Bukhari) ⭐
Some Companions, after learning about the Prophet's ﷺ way of life, decided that their own worship was not enough:

| Companion | Decision |
|---|---|
| The first | "I will **pray all night (without sleeping)**" |
| The second | "I will **fast all year (without a break)**" |
| The third | "I will **never marry**" |

**The Prophet's ﷺ reply (meaning):**
> "By Allah, I **fear Allah and am more devoted to Him** than you. Yet I **fast and I also break my fast; I pray (at night) and I also sleep; and I marry women.** **Whoever turns away from my Sunnah is not of me.**"

### (3) The incident of Mu'adh ibn Jabal (RA) (Muslim) ⭐
- When Mu'adh (RA) led the prayer as imam, out of devotion he recited **a long surah**.
- A man praying behind him lost patience and complained to the Prophet ﷺ.
- The Prophet ﷺ said sternly, **three times**: "**Mu'adh! Are you putting people to trial?**"
- His advice (meaning): "**Make things easy** for people; **do not make them hard. Encourage them; do not drive them away.**"

## 3. A warning against going to extremes in religion ⚠️

### Ibn 'Abbas (RA) – the stones for the jamarat
- During hajj, while staying at **Muzdalifah**, the Prophet ﷺ sent **Ibn 'Abbas (RA)** to collect pebbles for throwing **at the jamarat**.
- While people were keen to use **big stones**, thinking that was better, Ibn 'Abbas (RA) brought back **small pebbles**.
- The Prophet ﷺ **approved** them and said (meaning): "**Beware of going to extremes in religion**" **(Ahmad)**.

### "Ruined are those who go to extremes"
- The Prophet ﷺ said this **three times** as a warning **(Muslim).**
- **Imam an-Nawawi (RH):** these are **people who go beyond the limits and act with excessive strictness.**
- 👉 Those who abandon moderation **will lose in this world and in the Hereafter.**

### Heavy burdens (Musnad Ahmad)
> (meaning) "**Do not place heavy burdens on yourselves, or you will be destroyed.** People before you placed heavy burdens on themselves and were destroyed. Their remains can be seen **in monasteries and hermitages**."

---

## 4. Body and soul – balance
- Allah, who gave us a body, also gave us **a soul**.
- **Building up the body while neglecting the soul, or building up the soul while neglecting the body, is not Islam.**
- Just as we fulfil our duties to Allah, we must fulfil our duties **to His servants – to fellow Muslims and to non-Muslims**.

---

## 5. Moderation in daily life

| Area | Evidence | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Spending** | **17:29** | Do not tie your hand to your neck (do not be **a miser**), nor stretch it out completely (do not be **a spendthrift**) – or you will end up blamed and full of regret |
| **Food and drink** | **7:31** | Eat and drink, but **do not waste by excess** |
| **Walking and speech** | **31:19** | Be **moderate** in your walk; lower your voice; **the loud braying of the donkey** is the most hated of voices |

👉 31:19 – condemns walking **too slowly or too fast**.

---

## 6. Balance between this world and the Hereafter
> 📖 **(2:200–201) – meaning:**
> - Among people are some who say, "Our Lord! Give us (everything) **in this world**" – they will have **no share in the Hereafter.**
> - And among them are some who say, "Our Lord! Give us **good in this world and good in the Hereafter**, and protect us from the punishment of the Fire."

👉 Asking **only for the good of this world** is condemned; asking for the good of **both this world and the Hereafter** is praised.
