Flashcard 6

Front: ركوع (Ruku')

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 8 | Topic: Steps of prayer

Flashcard 7

Front: سُبْحَانَ رَبِّيَ الْعَظِيْمِ

Back: (meaning) Glory be to my Lord, the Most Great – recited three times in ruku'.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 8 | Topic: Recitations

Flashcard 8

Front: اعتدال (I'tidal)

Back: Rising from ruku' and standing upright.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 8 | Topic: Steps of prayer

---

Flashcard 9

Front: سَمِعَ اللهُ لِمَنْ حَمِدَهُ

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 8 | Topic: Recitations

Flashcard 10

Front: سُبْحَانَ رَبِّيَ الْأَعْلَى

Back: (meaning) Glory be to my Lord, the Most High – recited three times in sujud.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 8 | Topic: Recitations

---

Flashcard 11

Front: What are the seven parts of the body that must touch the ground in sujud?

Back: Both feet, both knees, both palms and the forehead.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 8 | Topic: Steps of prayer

---

Flashcard 12

Front: افتراش (Iftirash)

Back: Sitting on the left foot with the right toes planted – used between the prostrations and in the first tahiyyat.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 8 | Topic: Sitting positions

---

Flashcard 13

Front: تورك (Tawarruk)

Back: In the final tahiyyat, sitting on the ground with the left foot passed under the right shin.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 8 | Topic: Sitting positions

---

Flashcard 14

Front: What du'a is recited while sitting between the two prostrations?

Back: Rabbighfir li warhamni wajburni warfa'ni warzuqni wahdini wa'afini.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 8 | Topic: Recitations

---

Flashcard 15

Front: قنوت (Qunut)

Back: The du'a recited in i'tidal in the 2nd rak'ah of Subh and in the witr from the 16th night of Ramadan (sunnah in the Shafi'i madhhab).

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 8 | Topic: Steps of prayer

---

Flashcard 16

Front: الصلاة الإبراهيمية (The Ibrahimi salawat)

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 8 | Topic: Recitations
