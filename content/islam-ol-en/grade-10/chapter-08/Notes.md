# Grade 10 – Chapter 8: Sifat al-Salah (How to Pray)

## 🎯 What you will learn
- Basic facts about prayer
- Why prayer must be performed correctly (hadith)
- The steps of prayer – from standing to the salam
- What is recited in each position
- Differences between men and women

---

## 1. Basic facts
- **Sifat al-salah (صفة الصلاة)** = the method / form of prayer.
- Prayer is the **second** of the five pillars of Islam.
- It was made obligatory **during the Mi'raj**.
- After the Mi'raj, for **the first two days**, Jibril (AS) stood as **imam** and showed the Prophet ﷺ how to perform the prayer.
- **(Tirmidhi):** The first thing a person will be asked about in the Hereafter is prayer; if it is sound, there is success; if not, there is grief and sorrow.

### The incident "You have not prayed" (Bukhari)
- A man prayed in a hurry and then greeted the Prophet ﷺ with salam.
- The Prophet ﷺ said: **"Go back and pray, for you have not prayed"** – this happened **three times**.
- Then the Prophet ﷺ taught him: say the takbir → recite what you know of the Quran → bow **calmly** → rise and stand properly → prostrate **calmly** → sit **calmly**.

💡 **Lesson:** **calmness (pausing)** in every position of prayer is essential.

---

## 2. The steps of prayer ⭐

### Step 1: Standing facing the qiblah
- Anyone who is able must **stand upright facing the qiblah**.
- **Men:** a small gap between the two feet. **Women:** the feet close together.

### Step 2: Making the intention (niyyah)
- **Niyyah (نية)** = the intention made in the heart.
- Saying the first takbir, **"Allahu akbar"**, with the intention fixed in the mind = **takbirat al-ihram (تكبيرة الإحرام)** (the opening takbir).
- **Minimum intention:** "I am praying the fard of Subh."
- **Full intention:** "I am praying the two rak'ahs of the fard of Subh, facing the Ka'bah, for Allah."
- In congregation: the imam adds **"imaman"** (as imam) and the follower adds **"ma'muman"** (as a follower) to the intention.

### Step 3: Raising and folding the hands
- When saying the takbir, the Prophet ﷺ raised his hands **up to his ears** (Muslim) / **up to his shoulders** (Bukhari).
- The fingers straight and slightly apart, **the palms facing the qiblah**, the tips of the thumbs level with the lower part of the ears or the shoulders.
- The hands are folded **between the chest and the navel**: **the right hand over the left**, with the right palm holding the left wrist.
- **Men:** keep the elbows a little away from the sides. **Women:** keep them close.

### Step 4: Du'a al-istiftah (the opening supplication)
- The opening du'a recited after takbirat al-ihram. The Prophet ﷺ recited several du'as; any of them may be recited. Example – **Wajjahtu:**

> وَجَّهْتُ وَجْهِيَ لِلَّذِي فَطَرَ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضَ حَنِيفًا مُسْلِمًا وَمَا أَنَا مِنَ الْمُشْرِكِيْنَ، إِنَّ صَلَاتِي وَنُسُكِي وَمَحْيَايَ وَمَمَاتِي لِلَّهِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِيْنَ، لَا شَرِيْكَ لَهُ وَبِذَلِكَ أُمِرْتُ وَأَنَا مِنَ الْمُسْلِمِيْنَ

### Step 5: Reciting Surah al-Fatihah
- Say **"A'udhu billahi minash-shaytanir-rajim"** and **"Bismillahir-Rahmanir-Rahim"**, then recite al-Fatihah **calmly and correctly**.
- Al-Fatihah with the Bismillah must be recited **in every rak'ah**.
- In **the first two rak'ahs**, reciting another surah or passage after al-Fatihah is **sunnah**.
- It is **mustahabb** (recommended) for the surah in the second rak'ah to be a little shorter than the one in the first.
- In **the third and fourth rak'ahs**, only al-Fatihah is recited.

### Step 6: Ruku' (ركوع – bowing)
- Raise the hands, say the takbir and bow.
- Bow until **the head, neck and back** are level, and hold **the knees** with the palms spread.
- **Men:** keep the elbows away from the sides. **Women:** keep them close to the sides.
- **Recite three times:**

> **سُبْحَانَ رَبِّيَ الْعَظِيْمِ (وَبِحَمْدِهِ)**
> (meaning) Glory be to my Lord, the Most Great, and praise be to Him.

### Step 7: I'tidal (rising from ruku')
- Rise while raising the hands and saying:

> **سَمِعَ اللهُ لِمَنْ حَمِدَهُ** – (meaning) Allah hears the one who praises Him.

- When standing upright, with the hands at the sides, say:

> **رَبَّنَا لَكَ الْحَمْدُ مِلْءَ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَمِلْءَ الْأَرْضِ وَمِلْءَ مَا شِئْتَ مِنْ شَيْءٍ بَعْدُ**
> (meaning) Our Lord, to You belongs all praise – praise filling the heavens, filling the earth and filling whatever else You will.

### Step 8: Sujud (سجود – prostration)
- Go into sujud saying "Allahu akbar" without raising the hands.
- **Seven parts of the body** must touch the ground: **both feet, both knees, both palms and the forehead.**
- The fingers point towards the qiblah, level with the ears or the shoulders.
- **Men:** keep the elbows away from the sides and the stomach away from the thighs. **Women:** keep them close.
- **Recite three times:**

> **سُبْحَانَ رَبِّيَ الْأَعْلَى (وَبِحَمْدِهِ)**
> (meaning) Glory be to my Lord, the Most High, and praise be to Him.

### Step 9: Sitting between the two prostrations (iftirash)
- Rise saying the takbir and **sit on the left foot, with the tips of the right toes planted on the ground** = the **iftirash (افتراش)** sitting.
- The hands rest on the thighs, the fingers towards the qiblah, level with the knees.

> **رَبِّ اغْفِرْ لِي وَارْحَمْنِي وَاجْبُرْنِي وَارْفَعْنِي وَارْزُقْنِي وَاهْدِنِي وَعَافِنِي**
> (meaning) My Lord, forgive me and have mercy on me; make up for my shortcomings; raise me; provide for me; guide me; and grant me well-being.

### Step 10: The second sujud
- The same as the first sujud; recite the tasbih calmly.
- Then rise for the second rak'ah while saying the takbir.

### Step 11: Qunut (قنوت)
- In **the second rak'ah of the Subh prayer**, and in **the witr prayer from the 16th night of Ramadan to its end**, after the du'a of i'tidal.
- Recited with both palms raised to shoulder level in front of the face.
- It is **sunnah according to the Shafi'i madhhab.**
- It begins: **اَللّٰهُمَّ اهْدِنِي فِيمَنْ هَدَيْتَ ...** (the full du'a is in the textbook).

### Step 12: At-tahiyyat (التحيات)

| | First tahiyyat | Final tahiyyat |
|---|---|---|
| When? | In 3- or 4-rak'ah prayers, **after the second sujud of the 2nd rak'ah** | In every prayer, **after the second sujud of the last rak'ah** |
| Sitting | **Iftirash** | **Tawarruk** (تورك) – the right foot planted, the left foot passed under the right shin, sitting on the ground |
| What is recited | The tahiyyat **up to (not including) the Ibrahimi salawat** | The tahiyyat **together with the Ibrahimi salawat** |

> **التَّحِيَّاتُ الْمُبَارَكَاتُ الصَّلَوَاتُ الطَّيِّبَاتُ لِلّٰهِ، السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكَ أَيُّهَا النَّبِيُّ وَرَحْمَةُ اللهِ وَبَرَكَاتُهُ، السَّلَامُ عَلَيْنَا وَعَلَى عِبَادِ اللهِ الصَّالِحِيْنَ، أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لَا إِلٰهَ إِلَّا اللهُ وَأَشْهَدُ أَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا عَبْدُهُ وَرَسُوْلُهُ**

> **The Ibrahimi salawat (as-salat al-Ibrahimiyyah):**
> اَللّٰهُمَّ صَلِّ عَلَى مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى آلِ مُحَمَّدٍ، كَمَا صَلَّيْتَ عَلَى إِبْرَاهِيْمَ وَعَلَى آلِ إِبْرَاهِيْمَ، وَبَارِكْ عَلَى مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى آلِ مُحَمَّدٍ، كَمَا بَارَكْتَ عَلَى إِبْرَاهِيْمَ وَعَلَى آلِ إِبْرَاهِيْمَ، إِنَّكَ حَمِيْدٌ مَجِيْدٌ

#### How the hands are placed in tahiyyat
- The hands rest on the thighs, level with the knees.
- The **index finger** of the right hand is bent slightly inwards and pressed by the tip of the thumb; the other three fingers are folded.
- When saying **"illallah"**, the index finger is **raised fully**.
- It is sunnah to look at that finger until the salam, and at other times to look at **the place of prostration**.

### Step 13: Giving the salam
> **السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكُمْ وَرَحْمَةُ اللهِ**
- Turn the face **to the right** with the first salam and **to the left** with the second. With this, the prayer is complete.

### After the prayer
- Du'as made after the obligatory prayer are **accepted** (*maqbul*).
- Du'as should **begin and end with praise of Allah (hamd) and salawat on the Prophet ﷺ**.

## 3. Differences between men and women (summary)

| Position | Men | Women |
|---|---|---|
| Standing | A small gap between the feet | Feet close together |
| Folding the hands | Elbows a little away from the sides | Close to the sides |
| Ruku' | Elbows away from the sides | Close to the sides |
| Sujud | Elbows away from the sides and stomach away from the thighs | Close together |

## ✅ Quick revision
- Sifat al-salah = the method of prayer; prayer = the 2nd pillar; made obligatory during the Mi'raj.
- Takbirat al-ihram = the first takbir, said with the intention.
- The opening du'a = du'a al-istiftah (Wajjahtu).
- Ruku': Subhana rabbiyal-'azim ×3; sujud: Subhana rabbiyal-a'la ×3.
- I'tidal: Sami'allahu liman hamidah; Rabbana lakal-hamd…
- Seven parts in sujud: 2 feet, 2 knees, 2 palms, the forehead.
- Iftirash (sitting between prostrations, first tahiyyat); tawarruk (final tahiyyat).
- Qunut: 2nd rak'ah of Subh + witr from the 16th night of Ramadan – sunnah in the Shafi'i madhhab.
