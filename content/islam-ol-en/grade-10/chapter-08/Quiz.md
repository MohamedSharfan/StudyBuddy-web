# Grade 10 – Chapter 8: Sifat al-Salah (How to Pray) – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_08/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q15 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. When was prayer made obligatory?

A. During the Hijrah
B. During the Battle of Badr
C. During the Mi'raj
D. During the conquest of Makkah

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Prayer, the second pillar of Islam, was made obligatory during the Mi'raj. After that, Jibril (AS) stood as imam for the first two days and showed how to pray.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. How many times did the Prophet ﷺ tell a man who had prayed in a hurry, "Go back and pray, for you have not prayed"?

A. Once
B. Three times
C. Twice
D. Seven times

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: It happened three times. Then, when the man asked to be taught, the Prophet ﷺ taught him to pray calmly and without hurry in every position (Bukhari).

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q3. What is the main lesson we learn from the incident "You have not prayed"?

A. Prayer must be performed only at home
B. Only long surahs should be recited
C. Calmness and composure are essential in every position of prayer
D. Prayer must be performed twice

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: The man hurried and did not pause in the positions, so his prayer was not correct. Calmness is needed in ruku', i'tidal, sujud and sitting.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q4. What is "takbirat al-ihram"?

A. The takbir said when going into ruku'
B. The first takbir of the prayer, said with the intention
C. The takbir said before the salam
D. The extra takbirs of the Eid prayer

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Takbirat al-ihram is the first "Allahu akbar", said with the intention fixed in the mind. The prayer begins with it.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q5. When praying in congregation, which word must the follower add to the intention?

A. Imaman
B. Munfaridan
C. Ma'muman
D. Musafiran

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: The imam who leads the prayer adds "imaman", and the follower adds "ma'muman", to the intention.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q6. What is the correct way to fold the hands in prayer?

A. The left hand over the right, below the navel
B. The right hand over the left, between the chest and the navel
C. Leaving both hands hanging
D. Placing both hands on the shoulders

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: The textbook says that the right hand is placed over the left, the right palm holding the left wrist, folded between the chest and the navel.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q7. What is "du'a al-istiftah"?

A. The opening du'a recited after takbirat al-ihram
B. The du'a recited after the salam
C. The qunut du'a
D. The du'a recited while sitting between the prostrations

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Istiftah = opening. It is the opening du'a recited after saying takbirat al-ihram and folding the hands; "Wajjahtu…" is an example.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q8. In which rak'ahs is it sunnah to recite another surah after al-Fatihah?

A. In all rak'ahs
B. Only in the last two rak'ahs
C. Only in the first two rak'ahs
D. Only in the third rak'ah

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Reciting another surah after al-Fatihah is sunnah in the first two rak'ahs. In the third and fourth rak'ahs, only al-Fatihah is recited.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q9. What must be recited three times in ruku'?

A. Subhana rabbiyal-a'la (wa bihamdih)
B. Sami'allahu liman hamidah
C. Rabbighfir li warhamni
D. Subhana rabbiyal-'azim (wa bihamdih)

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: In ruku', "Subhana rabbiyal-'azim" (my Lord, the Most Great); in sujud, "Subhana rabbiyal-a'la" (my Lord, the Most High). Remember: "greatness" when bowing, "highness" when lowest in sujud.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q10. When is "Sami'allahu liman hamidah" said?

A. When going into sujud
B. When rising from ruku' (coming to i'tidal)
C. When giving the salam
D. When sitting between the prostrations

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: When rising from ruku' with the hands raised, one says, "Allah hears the one who praises Him." On standing upright, "Rabbana lakal-hamd…" is recited.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q11. Which of the following is NOT one of the seven parts of the body that must touch the ground in sujud?

A. The forehead
B. Both knees
C. Both elbows
D. Both feet

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: The seven parts are both feet, both knees, both palms and the forehead. The elbows must not touch the ground; men should keep them away from their sides.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q12. What is the name of the sitting in which one sits on the left foot with the tips of the right toes planted on the ground?

A. Iftirash
B. Tawarruk
C. I'tidal
D. Iq'a'

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Iftirash is used when sitting between the prostrations and in the first tahiyyat. In the final tahiyyat, one sits on the ground with the left foot passed under the right shin – this is tawarruk.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q13. According to the Shafi'i madhhab, on which occasions is it sunnah to recite the qunut?

A. In the last rak'ah of every prayer
B. In the second rak'ah of the Subh prayer, and in the witr from the 16th night of Ramadan to its end
C. Only in the Jumu'ah prayer
D. In the third rak'ah of the Maghrib prayer

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: The qunut is recited in the second rak'ah of Subh and in the witr of the second half of Ramadan, after the du'a of i'tidal.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q14. In tahiyyat, when should the index finger of the right hand be raised fully?

A. At the beginning of the tahiyyat
B. When reciting the salawat
C. When saying "illallah"
D. When giving the salam

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: While reciting "Ashhadu an la ilaha illallah", the index finger is raised fully at "illallah", and it is sunnah to look at it until the salam.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q15. Which is one difference between the first tahiyyat and the final tahiyyat?

A. The first tahiyyat is recited in the tawarruk sitting
B. In the final tahiyyat, the Ibrahimi salawat is also added
C. The final tahiyyat is recited in the iftirash sitting
D. The first tahiyyat is in every prayer

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: The first tahiyyat (only in 3- or 4-rak'ah prayers) is recited in iftirash, up to the Ibrahimi salawat. The final tahiyyat is recited in tawarruk, together with the Ibrahimi salawat.

Difficulty: Hard
