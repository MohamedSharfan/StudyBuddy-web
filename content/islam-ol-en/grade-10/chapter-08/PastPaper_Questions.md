Marks: not stated on the paper (Islam I multiple-choice question)

Choose the correct answer concerning the following statements.
- A - Takbirat at-tahrimah and du'a al-iftitah are two different things.
- B - Imam Malik (RH) stated that the term hikmah mentioned in the Quran refers to wisdom.
- C - Reaching the nisab and the completion of a year are among the conditions for zakat to become obligatory.
- D - During Hajj it is not wrong for men to cover the head and for women to cover the face.

On his way back from the playground, Anwar goes to the mosque, joins a congregational prayer in its second rak'ah, and sits for at-tahiyyat in every rak'ah. Which prayer did he join?

- **A.** Zuhr
- **B.** 'Asr
- **C.** Maghrib
- **D.** 'Isha

**Correct answer:** C – Maghrib *(option (3) on the original paper)*

**Explanation:** Joining Maghrib in the second rak'ah: he sits in the imam's 2nd rak'ah (first tahiyyat), in the imam's 3rd rak'ah (final tahiyyat), and then in the rak'ah he completes on his own – he sits in all three rak'ahs. This does not happen in the four-rak'ah prayers.

Consider the following main actions of prayer.
- A - reciting Surah al-Fatihah
- B - the second sajdah
- C - takbirat at-tahrimah with the intention
- D - sitting in tawarruk and saying salam
- E - making sajdah and sitting in iftirash
- F - making ruku' and coming to i'tidal

Choose the answer set that puts the above actions in order.

- **A.** A B C D E F
