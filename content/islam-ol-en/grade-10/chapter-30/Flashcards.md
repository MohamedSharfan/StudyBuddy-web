Flashcard 1

Front: فقه الأولويات (Fiqh al-awlawiyyat)

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 30 | Topic: Key terms

---

Flashcard 2

Front: أولويات (Awlawiyyat)

Back: Priorities.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 30 | Topic: Key terms

---

Flashcard 3

Front: What is the essence of fiqh al-awlawiyyat?

Back: Put first what deserves priority; put later what should be later; do not neglect what is serious.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 30 | Topic: Definition

---

Flashcard 4

Front: Over how long was the Quran revealed?

Back: 23 years – in stages, gradually.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 30 | Topic: Gradual order

---

Flashcard 5

Front: المن والسلوى (Al-mann was-salwa)

Back: Manna and quails – the valuable food Allah gave the Children of Israel (2:61).

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 30 | Topic: Putting the better first

---

Flashcard 6

Front: خير أمة (Khayra ummah)

Back: The best community – the name with which the Quran praised the Muslim community that carried the da'wah forward.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 30 | Topic: Da'wah

---

Flashcard 7

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 30 | Topic: Levels of rulings

Flashcard 9

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 30 | Topic: Levels of rulings

Flashcard 10

Front: مكروه (Makruh)

Back: Disliked.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 30 | Topic: Levels of rulings

---

Flashcard 11

Front: What are the four basic rules of priority?

Back: A sunnah does not come before a fard; the haram does not come before the halal; falsehood must not overcome truth; the unqualified must not take precedence over the qualified.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 30 | Topic: Basic rules

---

Flashcard 12

Front: What priorities do verses 2:221 and 16:30 show?

Back: 2:221 – in marriage, a believer rather than a mushrik; 16:30 – effort for the Hereafter rather than for this world.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 30 | Topic: Quranic priorities

---

Flashcard 13

Front: What things that are not equal do verses 35:19–22 mention?

Back: The blind and the seeing, darkness and light, shade and heat, the living and the dead.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 30 | Topic: Quranic comparisons

---

Flashcard 14

Front: Who was given priority in choosing the caliph, and from among whom?

Back: Abu Bakr (RA) – from among Abu 'Ubaydah, 'Umar and Abu Bakr (RA).

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 30 | Topic: The Companions' example
