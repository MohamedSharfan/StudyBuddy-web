# Grade 10 – Chapter 30: Fiqh al-Awlawiyyat – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_30/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q16 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. What does the Arabic term "fiqh al-awlawiyyat" mean?

A. The laws of worship
B. The legal understanding of what must be given priority
C. The laws of trade
D. Family law

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Awlawiyyat = priorities. Fiqh al-awlawiyyat is the legal understanding of what must be put first and what must be put later.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. While the people of an area are struggling because of drought, a group is getting ready for its third 'umrah. According to fiqh al-awlawiyyat, what takes priority at that time?

A. The third 'umrah
B. Relief aid for the people affected by drought
C. Both are equal
D. Nothing needs to be done

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: A third 'umrah is a good, sunnah act; but at that time helping people suffering from hunger is more important.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q3. Over how many years was the Quran revealed in stages?

A. Ten years
B. Forty years
C. Twenty-three years
D. In a single day

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: It was revealed gradually over 23 years according to social, political and economic needs – so that its teachings would be put into practice step by step.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q4. What does Allah's condemnation of the Children of Israel for asking for ordinary food instead of manna and quails (2:61) teach?

A. Food is not important
B. One must fast
C. Ordinary food is best
D. What is of higher quality must be put before what is of lower quality

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Condemning them for leaving the valuable food Allah gave and asking for ordinary food shows that the better must be put first.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q5. When the qiblah dispute arose, what did verse 2:177 make clear?

A. The qiblah of the Jews is right
B. A qiblah is not needed
C. There is no virtue in merely facing directions; the good lies in the substance – iman and good deeds
D. One must face east

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: The Quran tells us to give priority to substance over outward form.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q6. What is the correct Islamic position on war and peace?

A. War is the rule; peace is the exception
B. Peace is the rule; war is the exception
C. Both are equal
D. War is compulsory

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: The Quran puts peace before war; war is only for when it cannot be avoided.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q7. Which verse does the textbook give as evidence that "effort for the Hereafter must be put before effort for this world"?

A. 2:221
B. 2:61
C. 39:9
D. 16:30

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: 16:30 – effort for the Hereafter. 2:221 – putting a believer before a mushrik in marriage.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q8. In the Makkan period, to what did the Prophet ﷺ give priority in da'wah?

A. Forming the community
B. Trade
C. War
D. Forming individuals

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: The Prophet ﷺ saw that by forming individuals a community could be formed. Later, priority was given to the community, and the community itself carried the da'wah forward.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q9. How did the Quran praise the Muslim community that carried the da'wah forward?

A. Ummatan wasatan
B. The Ansar
C. Ahl al-Kitab
D. Khayra ummah (the best community)

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: The Quran praised that community, which carried the da'wah forward, as "khayra ummah" (the best community).

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q10. Which of the following is NOT one of the five levels into which legal scholars ranked matters?

A. Fard
B. Qiyas
C. Makruh
D. Wajib

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: The five levels: fard, wajib, ja'iz, makruh, haram. Qiyas is a secondary source of law, not a level of ruling.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q11. Which of the basic rules of fiqh al-awlawiyyat is correct?

A. A sunnah may come before a fard
B. A sunnah cannot come before a fard; the haram cannot come before the halal
C. The haram may come before the halal
D. The unqualified may take precedence over the qualified

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: A sunnah cannot come before a fard; the haram cannot come before the halal; falsehood must not overcome truth; the unqualified must not take precedence over the qualified.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q12. "Those who know and those who do not know are not equal" – which verse is this?

A. 39:9
B. 35:19–22
C. 2:177
D. 16:30

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: 39:9 shows the importance of knowledge. 35:19–22 says the blind and the seeing, darkness and light, shade and heat, the living and the dead are not equal.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q13. Why did the Prophet ﷺ give different answers to different Companions who asked the same question, "What is the best deed?"

A. He gave advice centred on each one's needs, state of mind and weaknesses
B. The Prophet ﷺ forgot
C. The Companions asked wrongly
D. Both answers were wrong

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Priorities differ according to the situation and each person's character – this is fiqh al-awlawiyyat in practice.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q14. What does the textbook say has pushed the Muslim community towards division today?

A. Too much worship
B. Too much education
C. Slackness in the wajib matter of staying united
D. Too much wealth

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: By putting later the unity that should come first, social problems have grown and the community has become divided.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q15. Which is an example of the Companions following fiqh al-awlawiyyat when choosing the caliph after the Prophet's ﷺ death?

A. Choosing 'Uthman (RA) first
B. Putting Abu Bakr (RA) forward from among Abu 'Ubaydah, 'Umar and Abu Bakr (RA)
C. Not choosing anyone
D. Choosing 'Ali (RA)

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Putting Abu Bakr (RA) forward from among the three was an act of foresight; it protected the caliphate.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q16. Which of the following is NOT among the things to put first in youth?

A. Idle entertainment
B. Skills
C. Spiritual training
D. Knowledge that sets life in order

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Youth is a training period for a prosperous future; knowledge, skills, emotional maturity and spiritual training are what come first in it.

Difficulty: Easy
