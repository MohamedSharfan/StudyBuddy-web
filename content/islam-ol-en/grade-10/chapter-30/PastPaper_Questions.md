## Part A: Multiple-Choice Questions (Islam I)

### A1. 2016 – Islam I, Q9

Question ID: P2016-I-Q09  
Year: 2016  
Paper and question: Islam I, Q9  
Marks: not stated on the paper (Islam I multiple-choice question)  
Source: OL_2016_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

When Salim was trying to carry out a sunnah 'amal (deed) done with money, the people of his neighbouring village were caught in a flood and were in need of help from others. Which of the following statements is suitable for guiding Salim in this situation?

- **A.** Don't worry, disasters are natural; carry out your wish.
- **B.** You don't need to give your money; non-governmental organisations will come running; go in the path of Allah and come back.
- **C.** What you are going to do is a sunnah act. Our brothers and sisters are in difficulty; help those people with your money.
- **D.** True, this is a complicated matter. Your brother-in-law is suffering without a permanent job. Give that money to him; it will be like looking after that family.

**Correct answer:** C – What you are going to do is a sunnah act. Our brothers and sisters are in difficulty; help those people with your money. *(option (3) on the original paper)*

**Explanation:** According to fiqh al-awlawiyyat (the fiqh of priorities), giving relief to disaster victims takes priority over a sunnah deed (textbook example: drought relief > a third 'umrah).

### A2. 2017 – Islam I, Q37

Question ID: P2017-I-Q37  
Year: 2017  
Paper and question: Islam I, Q37  
Marks: not stated on the paper (Islam I multiple-choice question)  
Source: OL_2017_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

Nusri is a young man closely connected with his village mosque. While coming to the mosque for the Zuhr prayer, he sees a man lying in a pool of blood on the road after being hit by a motorcycle. What should he give priority to on this occasion?

- **A.** going quickly to the mosque, praying, and then taking the patient to hospital
- **B.** going to the mosque, thinking someone will take the patient to hospital
- **C.** taking the patient to hospital immediately
- **D.** handing over the responsibility of taking the patient to someone else

**Correct answer:** C – taking the patient to hospital immediately *(option (3) on the original paper)*

**Explanation:** Saving a life is urgent; since there is time for Zuhr, taking the injured man to hospital first is the priority (fiqh al-awlawiyyat).

## Part B: Structured and Essay Questions (Islam II)

### B1. 2016 – Islam II, Q4 (i), (iii)

Question ID: P2016-II-Q4.2  
Year: 2016  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q4 (i), (iii)  
Marks: 10 marks for the whole of Q4  
Source: OL_2016_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(i) What is meant by 'fiqh al-awlawiyyat'?
(iii) State a difficult situation that can arise in daily life and explain what should be given priority in it.

**Suggested answer:**

(i) Fiqh al-awlawiyyat = the legal approach concerning what must be given priority. Not giving priority to the less important over the more important; putting first what should come first and later what should come later, and giving everything its proper place.

(iii) Example: as a person prepares to perform 'umrah for the third time, the people of a neighbouring area are suffering without food because of a flood / drought. 'Umrah is a sunnah deed; helping the victims is an urgent social duty. So on that occasion priority must be given to relief. It is a rule of the Shari'ah that a sunnah cannot come before a fard.

**Answer explanation:** Any example may be used in (iii), but the reason for the priority must be explained.
