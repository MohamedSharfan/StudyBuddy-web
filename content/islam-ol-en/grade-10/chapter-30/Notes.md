## 1. What is fiqh al-awlawiyyat (فقه الأولويات)? ⭐
> 📖 **Meaning:** **"the legal understanding of what must be given priority."**

### Explanation
A Muslim:
- must not give priority to **what is unimportant over what is important**.
- must not give priority to **what is important over what is most important**.
- must give priority to **what deserves priority**.
- must put later **what should be put later**.
- must not **neglect what is serious**.

---

## 2. Everyday examples

### Example 1: Drought vs a third 'umrah 🌵
- The people of an area are suffering from **drought** and struggling to meet their daily needs.
- At the same time, a group is getting ready to perform its **third 'umrah**.
- **Priority:** from both the religious and the social point of view, **giving relief aid** takes priority at that time over a third 'umrah.

### Example 2: Youth ⏳
- Youth = **a training period** for a prosperous future.
- "**Make use of your youth before old age comes**" – the Prophet ﷺ.
- **What must come first** at this stage: **knowledge, skills, emotional maturity and spiritual training** that set life in order.
- **Not suitable:** spending time on play, revelry and idle entertainment.

## 3. Priorities shown by the Quran and the Sunnah ⭐

### (1) Gradual order
- The Quran **was not revealed all at once.**
- It was revealed **in stages over twenty-three (23) years**, according to social, political and economic needs.
- The reason: its teachings were to be put into practice **step by step**.
- The Prophet ﷺ too, in his da'wah, **put first what should come first.**
- When introducing laws, the Quran and the Sunnah followed **a gradual order**.

### (2) Putting the better first
- Allah gave **the Children of Israel** a valuable food called **manna and quails (al-mann was-salwa)**.
- But they asked Musa (AS) for **ordinary food**.
- Allah **condemned** this and told them to prefer the manna and quails **(2:61).**

### (3) Substance over outward form
- When a dispute arose over whether **the qiblah of the Jews or the qiblah of the Muslims** was right, the Quran said **there is no virtue in merely facing directions**; the good lies in the substance of deeds – **iman and good deeds** **(2:177).**

### (4) Peace rather than war
- In Islam, **peace is the rule; war is the exception.**

### (5) More evidence
| Matter | Priority | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Marriage | **A believer rather than a mushrik** | **2:221** |
| Effort | **Effort for the Hereafter rather than effort for this world** | **16:30** |

### (6) Da'wah – the individual or the community?
- **The Makkan period:** priority to forming individuals (by forming individuals, a community can be formed).
- The reason: the **physical and social conditions** found in the societies of Makkah and Madinah.
- **Later:** priority to **the community** rather than individuals; the community itself carried the da'wah forward.
- The Quran praised that community as **"khayra ummah" (خير أمة) – the best community**.

---

## 4. Why is fiqh al-awlawiyyat important?
- Every matter has **its own value and worth**.
- When things are not given their due at the right time, **social injustice** results.
- When attention is too much or too little, **confusion** results.
- When **less valuable things come forward** and more valuable things **are pushed back**, there is turmoil.

### The five levels of rulings in the Shari'ah ⭐
The Shari'ah does not see all matters at the same level. The legal scholars ranked them:

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Fard** (فرض) | Obligatory duty |
| **Wajib** (واجب) | Compulsory |
| **Ja'iz** (جائز) | Permitted |
| **Makruh** (مكروه) | Disliked |
| **Haram** (حرام) | Forbidden |

### Basic rules ⭐
- **A sunnah cannot come before a fard.**
- **The haram cannot come before the halal.**
- **Falsehood must not overcome truth.**
- **The unqualified must not take precedence over the qualified.**

### Comparisons in the Quran
- **(35:19–22) – meaning:** **the blind and the seeing, darkness and light, shade and heat, the living and the dead** are not equal.
- **(39:9) – meaning:** **those who know and those who do not know** are not equal.

---

## 5. Priorities in the hadith – suited to each person
- The Prophet ﷺ gave priority to different things according to the situation and **each person's character**.
- When asked "What is the best deed?":
  - to one person he answered → "**Praying on time.**"
  - to another → "**Believing in Allah.**"
- 👉 Although all of them were Companions, he gave advice centred on **each one's needs, state of mind and weaknesses**.

---

## 6. Today's urgent need
- Because of **individual character, circumstances, changing times and growth of knowledge**, knowing what to put first is an **urgent need** today.
- Not putting first what should come first is **making social problems grow**.
- **Slackness** in the **wajib** matter of **staying united** has pushed the Muslim community towards **division**.

### The example of the Companions – choosing the caliph ⭐
- After the Prophet's ﷺ death, the Companions followed this approach in **choosing the caliph**.
- Of the three – **Abu 'Ubaydah (RA), 'Umar (RA) and Abu Bakr (RA)** – putting **Abu Bakr (RA)** forward was an act of **foresight**.
- Their choice **protected the caliphate (khilafah).**
