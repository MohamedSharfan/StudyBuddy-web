# Grade 10 – Chapter 6: Ibadah (Worship) – Flashcards

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_06/Flashcards.md` (Tamil), cards 1–15 in the same order.

---

Flashcard 1

Front: عبادة (Ibadah)

Back: Worship – obeying Allah by doing what He commanded and avoiding what He forbade.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 6 | Topic: Meaning of ibadah

---

Flashcard 2

Front: What does the verb عَبَدَ ('abada) mean?

Back: He worshipped, he obeyed, he submitted.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 6 | Topic: Meaning of ibadah

---

Flashcard 3

Front: عمارة (Imarah)

Back: Developing the earth.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 6 | Topic: Three responsibilities

---

Flashcard 4

Front: خلافة (Khilafah)

Back: Managing the earth as Allah's representative (steward) on it.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 6 | Topic: Three responsibilities

---

Flashcard 5

Front: Which verse states the purpose of creation?

Back: Quran 51:56 – (meaning) I did not create the jinn and human beings except to worship Me.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 6 | Topic: Purpose of creation

---

Flashcard 6

Front: What are the five aims of ibadah?

Back: God-consciousness (taqwa), closeness to Allah, keeping away from sin, developing good qualities, entering Paradise.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 6 | Topic: Aims of ibadah

---

Flashcard 7

Front: What are the two conditions for basic acts of worship to be accepted?

Back: 1) Being based on the Quran and the Sunnah 2) Being done with a sincere intention.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 6 | Topic: Types of ibadah

---

Flashcard 8

Front: What are the four things acts of worship are connected with?

Back: The body/action (prayer), the heart (ikhlas), wealth (Zakat), speech (dhikr).

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 6 | Topic: Types of ibadah

---

Flashcard 9

Front: Udhiyah (أضحية)

Back: Qurban – slaughtering an animal as a sacrifice for Allah; an act of worship connected with wealth.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 6 | Topic: Types of ibadah

---

Flashcard 10

Front: Tilawah (تلاوة)

Back: Reciting the Quran – an act of worship connected with speech.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 6 | Topic: Types of ibadah

---

Flashcard 11

Front: What are the five conditions for a daily action to become ibadah?

Back: Permission of the Shari'ah, sincere intention, not crossing the limits, not hindering basic worship, doing it properly and in order.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 6 | Topic: Daily actions as ibadah

---

Flashcard 12

Front: فرض عين (Fard 'ayn)

Back: An individual obligation – every Muslim must do it; whoever does not is sinful.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 6 | Topic: Fard and sunnah

---

Flashcard 13

Front: فرض كفاية (Fard kifayah)

Back: A communal obligation – if enough people in the community do it, the duty is lifted from the others; if no one does it, everyone is sinful.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 6 | Topic: Fard and sunnah

---

Flashcard 14

Front: سنة (Sunnah) – as a type of worship

Back: Acts of worship done in addition to the fard; e.g. sunnah prayers, dhikr, saying salam, visiting the sick.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 6 | Topic: Fard and sunnah

---

Flashcard 15

Front: When does eating become ibadah?

Back: When one eats halal food with the intention of strengthening the body for Allah's sake.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 6 | Topic: Daily actions as ibadah
