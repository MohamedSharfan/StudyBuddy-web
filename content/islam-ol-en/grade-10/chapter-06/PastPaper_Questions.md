### B2. 2019 – Islam II, Q7 (iv)

Question ID: P2019-II-Q7.4  
Year: 2019  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q7 (iv)  
Marks: 10 marks (short notes on two topics)  
Source: OL_2019_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(iv) Life is an act of worship

**Suggested answer:**

- "I did not create jinn and mankind except to worship Me" (51:56, meaning) – so the whole of life includes 'ibadah.
- 'Ibadah = complete obedience joined with the highest love for Allah; not only the defined acts of worship such as prayer and fasting, but daily actions such as eating, sleeping and work can also become 'ibadah.
- The five conditions for a daily action to become 'ibadah: permitted by the Shari'ah; a pure intention; not going beyond the limits of the Shari'ah; not getting in the way of the basic acts of worship; doing it well and properly.
- Example: eating halal food to strengthen the body for Allah is 'ibadah; earning in a halal way is 'ibadah.

**Answer explanation:** Include the definition, evidence, conditions and an example.

### B5. 2025 (2026) – Islam II, Q4 (iii)

Question ID: P2025_2026-II-Q4.3  
Year: 2025 (2026)  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q4 (iii)  
Marks: 10 marks for the whole of Q4  
Source: OL_2025_2026_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(iii) Clarify the conditions for a Muslim's daily activities to be accepted as 'ibadah'.

**Suggested answer:**

1. Permission of the Shari'ah – the act must be permitted (work is 'ibadah; selling alcohol, interest and gambling can never be 'ibadah).
2. A sincere intention (ikhlas) – doing it for Allah's pleasure.
3. Not going beyond the limits of the Shari'ah – e.g. avoiding interest, adulteration and hoarding in trade.
4. Not getting in the way of the basic acts of worship – e.g. a game that gets in the way of prayer is not 'ibadah.
5. Doing it well and properly.

**Answer explanation:** Give an example for each condition.
