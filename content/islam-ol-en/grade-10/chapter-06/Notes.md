# Grade 10 – Chapter 6: Ibadah (Worship)

## 🎯 What you will learn
- A Muslim's three responsibilities
- What *ibadah* is

- The types of ibadah
- The conditions for daily actions to become ibadah
- *Fard* (*'ayn* and *kifayah*) and *sunnah*

---

## 1. A Muslim's three responsibilities

| Responsibility | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1. **Ibadah** | Worshipping and obeying Allah |
| 2. **Imarah** | Developing the earth |
| 3. **Khilafah** | Managing the earth as Allah's representative (steward) on it |

⭐ Of these, **ibadah comes first and is the most important.**

---

## 2. What is ibadah?
- The word **ibadah (عبادة)** comes from the verb **'abada (عَبَدَ)**.
- It means: **he worshipped, he obeyed, he submitted.**
- So ibadah = **worshipping, serving and obeying.**

> 📖 **Islamic definition:**
> Ibadah is **obeying Allah** by doing what He has commanded and keeping away from what He has forbidden.

> 📖 **Another explanation:**
> Ibadah = **complete obedience together with the greatest love for Allah.**

### The purpose of creation
> 📖 **(51:56) – meaning:** "I did not create the jinn and human beings except to worship Me."

💡 So ibadah covers **the whole of life**. It is not only fixed acts of worship such as prayer; **every good deed** done in daily life is also ibadah. For this, every action must be done **according to the Shari'ah**.

---

## 3. The aims of ibadah
Acts of worship have the high aim of gaining **salvation in both worlds**:
1. To train the heart and develop **God-consciousness (taqwa)**
2. To increase **closeness to Allah**
3. To train us to **keep away from sins**
4. To develop good qualities such as **sacrifice, patience and caring for others**
5. To gain Allah's pleasure and so **enter Paradise**

---

## 4. Types of ibadah

### Type 1: Basic, fixed acts of worship
- Prayer, Zakat, fasting, Hajj and so on.
- **Two conditions for them to be accepted:**
  1. They must be **based on the Quran and the Sunnah**
  2. They must be done **with a sincere intention**

These can be divided further into four:

| Connected with | Examples |
|---|---|
| 1. **The body and action** | Prayer, fasting, Hajj |
| 2. **The heart** | Ikhlas (sincerity), patience |
| 3. **Wealth** | Zakat, sadaqah (charity), *udhiyah* (sacrifice of an animal) |
| 4. **Speech** | Dhikr, *tilawah* (recitation) of the Quran |

💡 Some acts of worship are connected with more than one of these. Example: **Hajj** – body + wealth + heart.

### Type 2: Acts of worship connected with daily life
- Daily actions such as eating, sleeping and working **become ibadah** if the conditions laid down by Islam are kept.
- **Example:** if a person eats halal food in order to strengthen his body for Allah's sake, even eating becomes ibadah.

---

## 5. Five conditions for a daily action to become ibadah ⭐

| Condition | Explanation | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **a) Permitted by the Shari'ah** | The action must be allowed by the Shari'ah | Work is ibadah; but selling alcohol, interest (riba) and gambling can never be ibadah |
| **b) Sincere intention (ikhlas)** | It must be done for Allah and for His pleasure | Work done with the intention of earning a living, seeking halal income and spending in Allah's way is ibadah |
| **c) Not crossing the limits of the Shari'ah** | Even in a permitted action, the limits must be kept | Trade is ibadah; but interest, adulteration and hoarding must be avoided |
| **d) Not getting in the way of basic acts of worship** | It must not disturb acts of worship that have fixed times | A game that prevents the five daily prayers is not ibadah |
| **e) Done properly and in an orderly way** | Islam is a balanced religion that values order and good work | Not keeping the rules of a game, or not obeying its leaders – this is not ibadah |

---

## 6. Acts of worship according to their importance

### 6.1 Fard (فرض) – obligatory acts of worship

| Type | Explanation | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Fard 'ayn** (فرض عين) – individual obligation | **Every Muslim** must do it; whoever does not is **sinful** | The five daily prayers, fasting in Ramadan, supporting one's family |
| **Fard kifayah** (فرض كفاية) – communal obligation | **If some people in the community do it, the duty is lifted from the others**; if not enough people do it, **everyone is sinful** | Funeral (janazah) duties; producing experts such as religious scholars, scientists, female obstetricians and engineers |

### 6.2 Sunnah (سنة) – recommended acts
- Acts of worship that are **not part of the fard** and are done **in addition to** the fard.
- **Benefits:**
  - They bring more reward and closeness to Allah.
  - They can **make up for shortcomings** in the obligatory acts of worship.
- **Examples:** sunnah prayers, dhikr, saying salam, visiting the sick.

## ✅ Quick revision
- A Muslim's responsibilities: **ibadah, imarah, khilafah** – ibadah is the most important.
- Ibadah ← 'abada = he worshipped, obeyed, submitted.
- Ibadah = complete obedience with the greatest love for Allah; the purpose of creation (51:56).
- For basic acts of worship to be accepted: based on the Quran and Sunnah + a sincere intention.
- Four connections: body, heart, wealth, speech.
- Five conditions for a daily action to become ibadah: permission, ikhlas, not crossing limits, not hindering worship, order.
- Fard 'ayn (individual duty), fard kifayah (communal duty), sunnah (additional).
