# Grade 10 – Chapter 6: Ibadah (Worship) – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_06/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q14 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. Of the three responsibilities given to a Muslim, which comes first and is the most important?

A. Imarah
B. Khilafah
C. Ibadah
D. Da'wah

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Of ibadah (worship), imarah (developing the earth) and khilafah (stewardship), ibadah comes first and is the most important.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. What is the name of the responsibility "developing the earth"?

A. Imarah
B. Khilafah
C. Ibadah
D. Ikhlas

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Imarah = developing the earth. Khilafah = managing the earth as Allah's representative.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q3. From which Arabic verb does the word "ibadah" come?

A. 'Amila (عمل)
B. 'Abada (عبد)
C. Amana (آمن)
D. Aslama (أسلم)

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Ibadah comes from the verb 'abada, which means he worshipped, he obeyed, he submitted.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q4. Which verse says that the purpose for which jinn and human beings were created is ibadah?

A. 2:177
B. 18:110
C. 74:4
D. 51:56

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: 51:56 – (meaning) "I did not create the jinn and human beings except to worship Me." This shows that ibadah covers the whole of life.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q5. What is the best explanation of ibadah?

A. Complete obedience together with the greatest love for Allah
B. Only the prayer performed in the mosque
C. Only the good deeds done in the month of Ramadan
D. Obedience out of fear only

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: The obedience in ibadah must come from love for Allah. It is not limited to fixed acts of worship.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q6. Zakat, sadaqah and udhiyah are acts of worship connected with what?

A. Speech
B. The heart
C. Wealth
D. The body only

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: These acts of worship are done by spending money or property, so they are connected with wealth. Dhikr and recitation of the Quran are connected with speech.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q7. Which example does the textbook give of an act of worship connected with the body, wealth and the heart together?

A. Dhikr
B. Hajj
C. Patience
D. Saying salam

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: The Hajj journey needs physical effort, wealth and a sincere heart. This shows that some acts of worship are connected with more than one area.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q8. A person works hard selling alcohol to support his family. Why is this not ibadah?

A. Because he misses his prayers
B. Because the action is not permitted by the Shari'ah
C. Because he does not work properly
D. Because work can never be ibadah

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Work can be ibadah, but the first condition is that the Shari'ah permits it. Selling alcohol, interest and gambling are never ibadah, whatever the intention.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q9. A person's cricket playing prevents him from performing the five daily prayers. Even if the other conditions are met, it is not ibadah. Which condition has been broken?

A. Not getting in the way of basic acts of worship
B. Sincere intention
C. Permission of the Shari'ah
D. Doing it properly

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: A daily action that disturbs basic acts of worship with fixed times goes outside the bounds of ibadah.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q10. Trade involving adulteration or hoarding is not ibadah. Which condition does this relate to?

A. Doing it properly
B. Not getting in the way of basic acts of worship
C. Not crossing the limits of the Shari'ah
D. Permission of the Shari'ah

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Trade is permitted (so the "permission" condition is met). But if it involves crossing the limits – interest, adulteration, hoarding and the like – it is not ibadah.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q11. Which statement about "fard 'ayn" is correct?

A. It is enough if some people in the community do it
B. Every Muslim must do it; whoever does not is sinful
C. One may do it if one wishes
D. Only scholars need to do it

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Fard 'ayn is an obligation on each individual – for example the five daily prayers, fasting in Ramadan and supporting one's family.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q12. Which of the following is an example of "fard kifayah"?

A. Fasting in Ramadan
B. The five daily prayers
C. Supporting one's family
D. Producing the female obstetricians that society needs

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: If enough people in the community carry out a fard kifayah, the duty is lifted from the others; if no one does it, everyone is sinful. Producing experts and carrying out funeral duties are examples.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q13. Which is one of the benefits of performing sunnah acts of worship?

A. One no longer needs to do the fard
B. They can make up for shortcomings in the obligatory acts of worship
C. They are more important than the fard
D. Whoever leaves them is sinful

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Sunnah acts are in addition to the fard. They bring more reward and closeness to Allah and make up for shortcomings in the fard. But they do not replace the fard.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q14. Which of the following is an act of worship "connected with the heart"?

A. Ikhlas
B. Dhikr
C. Sadaqah
D. Fasting

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Ikhlas (sincerity) and patience are acts of worship connected with the heart. Dhikr is connected with speech, sadaqah with wealth and fasting with the body.

Difficulty: Easy
