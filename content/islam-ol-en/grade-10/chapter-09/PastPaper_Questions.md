## Part B: Structured and Essay Questions (Islam II)

### B1. 2016 – Islam II, Q5

Question ID: P2016-II-Q5.1  
Year: 2016  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q5  
Marks: 10 marks  
Source: OL_2016_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(i) What is meant by "du'a is worship"?
(ii) State the three du'as the Prophet taught should be recited after the Subh (Fajr) prayer.
(iii) Clarify the terms tasbih, tahmid and takbir.

**Suggested answer:**

(i) Man is always in need of Allah. By asking Allah for his needs, he accepts Allah's power and his own helplessness – this is the expression of servitude ('ubudiyyah). So the Prophet ﷺ said, "Du'a is worship." Allah is angry with the one who does not ask Him for his needs. The basic meaning of the word "salah" is also supplication.

(ii) 1. لَا إِلٰهَ إِلَّا اللهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيْكَ لَهُ، لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ يُحْيِي وَيُمِيْتُ وَهُوَ عَلَى كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيْرٌ – 10 times (Tirmidhi)
2. اَللّٰهُمَّ أَجِرْنِي مِنَ النَّارِ – 7 times (Abu Dawud)
3. اَللّٰهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ عِلْمًا نَافِعًا وَرِزْقًا طَيِّبًا وَعَمَلًا مُتَقَبَّلًا (Ibn Majah)

(iii) - Tasbih: declaring Allah free of all faults by saying "Subhan Allah" (Allah is free of all imperfection).
- Tahmid: praising Allah by saying "al-hamdu lillah" (all praise is for Allah).
- Takbir: glorifying Allah by saying "Allahu akbar" (Allah is the greatest).
Each of these is recited 33 times after prayer.

**Answer explanation:** In (ii) the Arabic text or its correct meaning may be written.

### B2. 2024 (2025) – Islam II, Q1 (i)

Question ID: P2024_2025-II-Q1.1  
Year: 2024 (2025)  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q1 (i)  
Marks: 20 marks for the whole of Q1 (no separate marks given for sub-questions)  
Source: OL_2024_2025_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

Write short answers.
(i) State two of the rewards for the person who recites the 'kalimat at-tawhid' after the Subh prayer.

**Suggested answer:**

Any two of the following (if recited 10 times after Subh, before speaking to anyone – Tirmidhi):
1. 10 good deeds are written.
2. 10 bad deeds are wiped out.
3. One's rank is raised by 10 degrees.
4. Protection throughout that day from evils and from the harm of the devil.

**Answer explanation:** The kalimat at-tawhid: "La ilaha illallahu wahdahu la sharika lahu, lahu al-mulku wa lahu al-hamdu yuhyi wa yumitu wa huwa 'ala kulli shay'in qadir".

**Marking guidance (suggested only – not an official marking scheme):** Two rewards.
