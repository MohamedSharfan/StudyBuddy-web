# Grade 10 – Chapter 9: What to Recite after the Prayer – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_09/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q14 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. What is the basic meaning of the Arabic word "salah"?

A. Fasting
B. Supplication
C. Purity
D. Charity

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: The basic meaning of salah is supplication (du'a). This is why du'as are given importance in the prayer and after it.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. According to the hadith of the Prophet ﷺ, with whom does Allah become angry?

A. With the one who makes a lot of du'a
B. With the one who prays for a long time
C. With the one who does not ask Allah for his needs
D. With the one who prays at night

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: A human being is always in need of Allah. Not asking Him for one's needs is a sign of arrogance, so Allah becomes angry. The Prophet ﷺ also said, "Du'a is worship."

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q3. What did the Prophet ﷺ say first when the congregational prayer ended?

A. Astaghfirullah (three times) – Allahumma antas-salam…
B. Ayat al-Kursi
C. Subhanallah 33 times
D. Surah al-Ikhlas

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: When the prayer ended, the Prophet ﷺ first sought forgiveness three times and then said, "Allahumma antas-salam wa minkas-salam…"

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q4. In "Tabarakta ya dhal-jalali wal-ikram", what does "dhal-jalali wal-ikram" mean?

A. Possessor of mercy and kindness
B. Possessor of dominion and praise
C. The One who gives life and causes death
D. Possessor of Majesty and Honour

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: "Ya dhal-jalali wal-ikram" = O Possessor of Majesty and Honour! This is the meaning given in the textbook.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q5. What is the meaning of "Allahumma la mani'a lima a'tayta…"?

A. O Allah! No one can withhold what You give, and no one can give what You withhold
B. O Allah! Give me more
C. O Allah! Save me from the Fire
D. O Allah! Give me beneficial knowledge

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: These words accept Allah's complete authority. They also say that a wealthy person's wealth cannot protect him from Allah's punishment (Bukhari, Muslim).

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q6. What is the virtue of reciting Subhanallah, Alhamdulillah and Allahu akbar 33 times each after every prayer and completing 100 with the kalimah?

A. Food will be provided for the whole day
B. Sins are forgiven even if they are like the foam of the sea
C. Protection until the next prayer
D. Freedom from Hellfire

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: 33+33+33 = 99; with the kalimah it is 100. Its virtue: sins are forgiven even if they are as much as the foam of the sea. "Protection until the next prayer" is the virtue of Ayat al-Kursi.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q7. What does "Allahu akbar" mean?

A. Allah is perfect and free from all faults
B. All praise belongs to Allah
C. Allah is the Greatest
D. Allah is One

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Subhanallah = Allah is perfect; Alhamdulillah = praise belongs to Allah; Allahu akbar = Allah is the Greatest.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q8. About which recitation was it said that, for the one who recites it after every prayer, "nothing stands between him and Paradise except death"?

A. Surah al-Ikhlas
B. Ayat al-Kursi
C. Surah al-Falaq
D. The kalimah of tawhid

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: This is about Ayat al-Kursi (2:255) (Nasa'i). Also, the one who recites it after an obligatory prayer remains under Allah's protection until the next prayer (Tabarani).

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q9. Which verse of the Quran is Ayat al-Kursi?

A. 2:255
B. 2:285
C. 3:18
D. 112:1

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Ayat al-Kursi is verse 255 of Surah al-Baqarah. Verse 2:285 is about belief in the Books and the messengers.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q10. According to Surah an-Nas, where are those who whisper evil suggestions (waswas) found?

A. Only among the angels
B. Only among the jinn
C. Only among human beings
D. Among both jinn and human beings

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Surah an-Nas says that such whisperers are among both jinn and human beings. So we must also seek protection from people who stir up evil thoughts.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q11. What three kinds of help are asked for in the du'a "Allahumma a'inni 'ala dhikrika wa shukrika wa husni 'ibadatik"?

A. Wealth, health and knowledge
B. Remembering Allah, thanking Him and worshipping Him well
C. Forgiveness, Paradise and freedom from Hell
D. Patience, courage and a long life

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: This du'a (Abu Dawud, Nasa'i) asks Allah for help to remember Him, to thank Him and to worship Him well.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q12. Which of the following is NOT one of the virtues for the person who recites the kalimah of tawhid 10 times after Subh before speaking to anyone?

A. 10 good deeds are written
B. 10 bad deeds are erased
C. He is raised 10 ranks
D. His wealth increases tenfold

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: 10 good deeds, 10 bad deeds erased, 10 ranks raised, and protection from evils and from Satan for that day – these are the virtues in the hadith of Tirmidhi. An increase in wealth is not mentioned.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q13. How many times, and after which prayers, should "Allahumma ajirni minan-nar" be recited?

A. 10 times – Zuhr and Asr
B. 3 times – after every prayer
C. 7 times – Subh and Maghrib
D. 33 times – Isha

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: If one recites it 7 times after Subh or Maghrib before speaking to anyone and dies that day or that night, he will be freed from Hellfire (Abu Dawud).

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q14. In the du'a "'ilman nafi'an wa rizqan tayyiban wa 'amalan mutaqabbalan", what does "rizqan tayyiban" mean?

A. Beneficial knowledge
B. Pure (halal) provision
C. Accepted deeds
D. A long life

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: 'Ilman nafi'an = beneficial knowledge; rizqan tayyiban = pure (halal) provision; 'amalan mutaqabbalan = accepted deeds. This du'a is recited after Subh (Ibn Majah).

Difficulty: Hard
