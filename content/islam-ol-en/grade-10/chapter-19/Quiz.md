# Grade 10 – Chapter 19: The Da'wah of the Prophet ﷺ and Its Challenges – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_19/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q17 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. In which surah does the first command to carry out da'wah appear?

A. Surah al-Muddaththir
B. Surah al-'Alaq
C. Surah al-Fatihah
D. Surah al-Ikhlas

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: "O you who are wrapped in a cloak! Arise and warn" (74:1–2) – Surah al-Muddaththir. Surah al-'Alaq contains the first revelation, not the command for da'wah.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q2. Where did the Prophet ﷺ call out to the public and deliver his message?

A. The cave of Hira'
B. The hill of Safa
C. The plain of 'Arafah
D. Ta'if

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: After telling his family and friends first, and then his relatives at a meal, he climbed the hill of Safa and called the public.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q3. Which was one of the reasons the Quraysh opposed the Prophet's ﷺ da'wah?

A. Because the Prophet ﷺ was a foreigner
B. It was directly against their way of worship and would make them lose their influence
C. Because the Prophet ﷺ was poor
D. Because the Prophet ﷺ declared war

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: The message seemed new and bitter to them; they saw it as against their way of worship and as making them lose their influence.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q4. For how many years did the Prophet ﷺ carry out da'wah in secret?

A. Three years
B. One year
C. Five years
D. Ten years

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: For the first three years he called people in secret. After that he began preaching publicly.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q5. During the secret da'wah, where were those who accepted Islam given education and spiritual training?

A. Al-Masjid an-Nabawi
B. Shi'b Abi Talib
C. The Ka'bah
D. Dar al-Arqam

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Education and tarbiyah were given in the house of al-Arqam (RA). This first school is remembered to this day as "Dar al-Arqam".

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q6. Who were called "hanifs"?

A. Those who did not take part in shirk during the Jahiliyyah
B. Those who went to Abyssinia
C. The people of Madinah
D. False prophets

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Those who did not take part in idol worship during the Jahiliyyah were called hanifs; they too accepted Islam early.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q7. When did the first hijrah to Abyssinia take place, and with how many people?

A. Year 5 of prophethood – about fifteen
B. Year 7 of prophethood – a hundred people
C. Year 12 of prophethood – 72 people
D. 1 AH – 12 people

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: First, about fifteen went to Abyssinia in year 5 of prophethood, and then a hundred in year 7. Year 12 – 72 people is the second pledge of 'Aqabah.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q8. Which just ruler gave protection to the Muslims who went to Abyssinia?

A. Heraclius
B. Muqawqis
C. Kisra
D. The Najashi

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: The Quraysh tried to bring them back, but the attempt failed because the just ruler, the Najashi, gave them protection.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q9. Where, and for how long, was the Prophet's ﷺ family kept apart under the social boycott?

A. Ta'if – one year
B. Shi'b Abi Talib – three years
C. Abyssinia – five years
D. 'Aqabah – two years

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: The Banu Hashim and the Banu al-Muttalib were kept apart in the valley of Shi'b Abi Talib for three years. The boycott document was hung in the Ka'bah.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q10. How did the social boycott come to an end?

A. The Prophet ﷺ fought the Quraysh
B. Abu Talib apologised
C. Some young men, seeing their pitiful state, tore up the document hanging in the Ka'bah
D. The Najashi sent a letter

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Some young men who saw their pitiful condition tore up the boycott document, and the Prophet ﷺ and the others were freed.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q11. What did the Prophet ﷺ do when the people of Ta'if threw stones at him and drove him out?

A. He cursed them
B. He declared war on them
C. He prayed for the good of those people
D. He never returned to Makkah

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Even while being so badly treated, the Prophet ﷺ prayed only for their good – a proof of his patience and mercy.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q12. On returning to Makkah from Ta'if, under whose protection did the Prophet ﷺ have to live?

A. Mut'im
B. Abu Talib
C. Abu Lahab
D. Abu Sufyan

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Since Abu Talib had died, he had no choice but to live under the protection of a man named Mut'im.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q13. Who was sent to teach Islam in Yathrib?

A. Abu Bakr (RA)
B. Bilal (RA)
C. 'Ali (RA)
D. Mus'ab ibn 'Umayr (RA)

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: With the twelve who took part in the first pledge of 'Aqabah, Mus'ab ibn 'Umayr (RA) was sent to teach Islam in Yathrib.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q14. Which statement about "Bay'at al-'Aqabah 2" (the second pledge of 'Aqabah) is correct?

A. 15 people took part in year 5 of prophethood
B. In year 12 of prophethood, 72 people from Yathrib renewed the pledge and invited the Prophet to Yathrib
C. It was made with the Quraysh in 6 AH
D. It was made with the Jews

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: At the second 'Aqabah, 72 people renewed the pledge and invited the Prophet to Yathrib. The 6 AH treaty with the Quraysh is Hudaybiyyah.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q15. Why did the people of Yathrib accept the Prophet's ﷺ message easily?

A. They were friends of the Quraysh
B. The Prophet ﷺ gave them money
C. They had already heard about the last prophet from the Jews
D. They had learned Islam in Abyssinia

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: The Jews living in Madinah used to say that a last prophet would come, so the pilgrims accepted what the Prophet ﷺ said.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q16. What was Yathrib called after the hijrah?

A. Makkah al-Mukarramah
B. Dar al-Arqam
C. Bayt al-Maqdis
D. Madinat an-Nabi

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: With the Prophet's ﷺ arrival, Yathrib came to be called "Madinat an-Nabi" (the City of the Prophet).

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q17. In which year was the peace treaty of Hudaybiyyah made?

A. 1 AH
B. 2 AH
C. 6 AH
D. 10 AH

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: After the three battles of Badr, Uhud and al-Khandaq, the treaty was made at Hudaybiyyah in 6 AH. The Quraysh broke it within a short time. 1 AH – the treaty with the Jews.

Difficulty: Medium
