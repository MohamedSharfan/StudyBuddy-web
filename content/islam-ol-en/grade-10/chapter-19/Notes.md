# Grade 10 – Chapter 19: The Da'wah of the Prophet ﷺ and Its Challenges

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_19/Notes.md` (Tamil). English rendering.

## 🎯 What you will learn
- What da'wah is and when it began
- Secret and public da'wah in Makkah
- The opposition and persecution of the Quraysh
- The hijrah to Abyssinia, the social boycott, the journey to Ta'if
- The pledges of 'Aqabah and the hijrah to Madinah
- The challenges faced in Madinah

---

## 1. The beginning of da'wah
> 📖 **Da'wah (دعوة):** **inviting** people to Allah – the **chief mission** of the Prophet ﷺ.

- After reaching **the age of 40**, the Prophet ﷺ began this mission **by Allah's command**.
- The command to call people came while the shock of the first revelation had not fully passed:
  > 📖 **(74:1–2) – meaning:** "O you who are wrapped in a cloak! Arise and warn (the people)." (**Surah al-Muddaththir**)

---

## 2. The Makkan period – stages of da'wah ⭐

| Stage | Explanation |
|---|---|
| 1 | First he told **his family and friends** |
| 2 | He invited his relatives to **a meal**, explained the revelation and asked for their support |
| 3 | He climbed **the hill of Safa**, called the general public and delivered the message |

### Why the Quraysh opposed him
- The message seemed **new and bitter** to them.
- It was **directly against their way of worship.**
- It would make them **lose their influence.**

### Secret da'wah – the first three years
- Those who accepted Islam: some of his family, some of his friends, good people in society, **hanifs** (those who did not take part in shirk in the Jahiliyyah), and **slaves** persecuted by their masters.
- They were given **education and spiritual training (tarbiyah)** in the house of **al-Arqam (RA)**.
- This first school is remembered to this day as **"Dar al-Arqam"**.

---

## 3. Public da'wah and opposition

### Leaders of the opposition
**Abu Lahab, Abu Jahl** and others.

### Harsh words
They tormented the Prophet ﷺ by calling him **a liar, a sorcerer, a madman, a poet, and one without descendants (cut off)**. They warned the public not to pay attention to him.

### Physical abuse
- They spread **thorns** on the path he walked.
- They **twisted his neck.**
- While he was in **sujud**, they put **the rotten entrails of a camel** around his neck.
- They threw small stones at him at first, and later beat him **until he was wounded**.
- They persecuted the Muslims too, **men and women alike**; **slaves** were treated cruelly.
- 👉 The Prophet ﷺ and the Muslims stayed **patient and did not strike back**.

---

## 4. The hijrah to Abyssinia ⭐
- When the persecution could neither be borne nor resisted, he sent Companions to **Abyssinia (Habashah, Ethiopia)**.

| Stage | Year | Number |
|---|---|---|
| First | **Year 5 of prophethood** | About **fifteen (15)** |
| Second | **Year 7 of prophethood** | **A hundred people** |

- The Quraysh went to Abyssinia to try to bring them back – **they failed.**
- **The just ruler, the Najashi (Negus)**, gave the Muslims **protection**.

> 📌 **Nubuwwah** = prophethood. "Year 5 of prophethood" = the 5th year after prophethood began.

### Hamzah and 'Umar (RA) accept Islam
- When **Hamzah (RA) and 'Umar (RA)** accepted Islam, the Muslims gained **some courage**; but persecution and death threats continued.

---

## 5. Abu Talib's protection and the schemes of the Quraysh
- The Prophet's ﷺ **uncle, Abu Talib**, played a great part in protecting him.
- The Quraysh asked Abu Talib to **stop** the preaching, or else **hand the Prophet over to them** – he **refused.**
- They tried to turn him away by offering **land, women and gold**.
- They proposed **a compromise in worship between the two sides**.
- 👉 The Prophet ﷺ **agreed to none of this.**

---

## 6. The social boycott – Shi'b Abi Talib ⭐
- They placed the Prophet's ﷺ family, **the Banu Hashim and the Banu al-Muttalib**, under a social boycott.
- They **hung the boycott document in the Ka'bah**.
- They were kept apart in a valley called **Shi'b Abi Talib** for **three years**.
- They suffered without water or food; **the cries of small children** could be heard far away.
- Anyone found helping them secretly was **punished.**
- Seeing their pitiful state, **some young men** **tore up the document** hanging in the Ka'bah → they were freed.

---

## 7. The Year of Sorrow and the journey to Ta'if
- Shortly after their release, **Abu Talib and Khadijah (RA)** died – the two who had supported him until then.
- This gave the Quraysh **an opportunity**; the trials became harsher than before.
- The Prophet ﷺ went to **Ta'if** and introduced himself to the tribal leaders as Allah's Messenger.
- They **mocked him, set ruffians on him who threw stones**, and drove him out.
- 👉 Even then the Prophet ﷺ **prayed for the good of those people.**
- On returning to Makkah he had to live under the **protection of Mut'im ibn 'Adi**.

---

## 8. The pledges of 'Aqabah ⭐
- At a place called **'Aqabah** he met some pilgrims from **Yathrib (Madinah)** and introduced himself as Allah's Messenger.
- They had already heard about the last prophet from **the Jews**, so they **accepted.**

| Event | Details |
|---|---|
| **First 'Aqabah** (the next pilgrimage season) | **Twelve (12)** came to Makkah, accepted Islam and took the pledge |
| A teacher sent | **Mus'ab ibn 'Umayr (RA)** was sent to teach Islam in Yathrib |
| **Second 'Aqabah – "Bay'at al-'Aqabah 2"** | In **year 12 of prophethood**, **72 people** from Yathrib came, renewed the pledge and invited the Prophet ﷺ **to come to Yathrib** |

> 📌 **Bay'ah (بيعة)** = a pledge / an oath of allegiance.

---

## 9. The hijrah to Madinah
- In response to the invitation, **the Muslims went first** to Madinah.
- Fearing danger if the Prophet ﷺ also left, the Quraysh **decided to kill him.**
- **While they were surrounding his house**, the Prophet ﷺ left safely.
- The Prophet ﷺ and **Abu Bakr (RA)** passed through many trials and reached Madinah.
- With the Prophet's arrival, Yathrib came to be called **"Madinat an-Nabi" (the City of the Prophet)**.

---

## 10. Challenges in Madinah
### Continued harassment by the Quraysh
- They stirred up **the Jews** of Madinah against the Muslims and warned the pilgrims of Madinah.
- **Raids** on the southern border of Madinah, **seizing** livestock, **setting fire** to orchards.
- Finally they **marched with armies**.

### Battles
- The Muslims fought without traditional military training; the Quraysh, with their great forces, **were defeated.**
- The Muslims received **the help of angels** → their faith in Allah grew.
- Three battles: **Badr, Uhud, al-Khandaq (the Trench).**
- **6 AH:** the peace treaty of **Hudaybiyyah** – the Quraysh **broke it** within a short time.
- After further events, **Makkah came under Muslim control** → Islam spread rapidly.

### The Jews
- **1 AH:** a treaty with the Jews.
- They **conspired** with the Quraysh and acted against the treaty → they were **expelled** from Madinah → Madinah became peaceful.

---

## 11. The international spread of Islam
- The Prophet ﷺ conveyed the message of Islam **by letters to small kingdoms and great empires**.
- Islam began to spread beyond Arabia; in **the time of the caliphs** the Islamic state expanded.
- 👉 He won the hearts of the very people who had persecuted him, until they held him **dearer than their own lives** – through **patience and the right approach**.
