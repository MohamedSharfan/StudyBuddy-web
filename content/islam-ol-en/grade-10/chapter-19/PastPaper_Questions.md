Question ID: P2016-I-Q19  
Year: 2016

Marks: not stated on the paper (Islam I multiple-choice question)  
Source: OL_2016_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

Those called 'Hanifs' were

- **A.** people in the time of Jahiliyyah who did not take part in associating partners with Allah
- **B.** those given the good news of Paradise
- **C.** those who took part in the hijrah
- **D.** members of the majlis ash-shura

**Correct answer:** A – people in the time of Jahiliyyah who did not take part in associating partners with Allah *(option (1) on the original paper)*

**Explanation:** The Hanifs – people who, even in the time of Jahiliyyah, did not worship idols and followed the monotheism of Ibrahim (AS).

## Part B: Structured and Essay Questions (Islam II)

### B1. 2016 – Islam II, Q3

Question ID: P2016-II-Q3.1  
Year: 2016  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q3  
Marks: 10 marks  
Source: OL_2016_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(i) Who supported the Prophet in his da'wah during the Makkan period?
(ii) Explain clearly the methods the Quraysh used to stop the Prophet's da'wah.
(iii) Describe the second pledge of 'Aqabah.

**Suggested answer:**

(i) Khadijah (RA), Abu Bakr (RA), 'Ali (RA), Zayd ibn Harithah (RA), his uncle Abu Talib, Hamzah (RA), 'Umar (RA), al-Arqam (RA) (who gave his house as "Dar al-Arqam").

(ii) 1. Harsh words – they mocked him as a liar, a sorcerer, a madman and a poet; they warned people not to go to him.
2. Physical harm – thorns on his path, camel entrails placed on him in sujud, stoning; they tortured slaves and Muslims.
3. Pressure on Abu Talib – they asked him to stop the preaching or hand the Prophet over.
4. Temptation – they offered land, women and gold; they asked for compromise in worship.
5. Social boycott – they shut the Banu Hashim and Banu al-Muttalib in Shi'b Abi Talib for three years.
6. They tried to bring back those who had gone to Abyssinia; in the end they planned to kill the Prophet.

(iii) In year 12 of prophethood (in the Hajj season), 72 people from Yathrib met the Prophet ﷺ at a place called 'Aqabah, confirmed that they had accepted Islam and renewed their pledge of allegiance (bay'ah). They invited the Prophet to come to Yathrib and promised to protect him. This is "the Second Pledge of 'Aqabah". As a result, the Muslims began to make hijrah to Madinah; this led to the formation of the Islamic state.

**Answer explanation:** In (iii) the year, the number of people, the content of the pledge and its result should be mentioned.

**Marking guidance (suggested only – not an official marking scheme):** No official marking scheme; all three sub-questions must be answered.

**Answer status:** Suggested – not an official answer (no official marking scheme was supplied); based on the Grade 10/11 textbook content

**Verification note:** The list of supporters in (i) is based on general knowledge of the sirah; the textbook mentions Abu Talib, Khadijah (RA), Hamzah, 'Umar (RA) and al-Arqam (RA).

### B3. 2018 – Islam II, Q5

Question ID: P2018-II-Q5.1  
Year: 2018  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q5  
Marks: 10 marks  
Source: OL_2018_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(i) What is meant by 'da'wah'?
(ii) State three challenges the Prophet ﷺ faced during his da'wah in the Makkan period.
(iii) Briefly explain the importance of the hijrah journey.

**Suggested answer:**

(i) Da'wah (دعوة) = calling people to Allah – the foremost task of the Prophet ﷺ; it began by Allah's command after prophethood was given (74:1–2).

(ii) Any three of the following:
1. Harsh words – mocked as a liar, a sorcerer, a madman and a poet.
2. Physical harm – thorns on his path, camel entrails placed on him in sujud, stoning; cruelty to the Muslims and slaves.
3. Temptation; demands for compromise in worship.
4. The three-year social boycott in Shi'b Abi Talib.
5. Being stoned by the people of Ta'if; finally, the plot to kill him.

(iii) - The Prophet and the Muslims were saved from the Quraysh's plot to kill him.
- Yathrib became "Madinat an-Nabi", and the first Islamic society and state were formed.
- The brotherhood of the Muhajirun and the Ansar arose; da'wah spread freely.
- It became the starting point of the Islamic (Hijri) calendar.

**Answer explanation:** In (iii) mention the personal, social and political importance of the hijrah.

**Marking guidance (suggested only – not an official marking scheme):** No official marking scheme.

**Answer status:** Suggested – not an official answer (no official marking scheme was supplied); based on the Grade 10/11 textbook content

### B5. 2020 – Islam II, Q7 (iv)

Question ID: P2020-II-Q7.4  
Year: 2020  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q7 (iv)  
Marks: 10 marks (short notes on two topics)  
Source: OL_2020_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(iv) The Prophet's ﷺ work of calling to Islam in the Makkan period

**Suggested answer:**

- At the age of 40, da'wah began by Allah's command (74:1–2).
- The first three years were secret da'wah – family members, friends, Hanifs and slaves accepted Islam; education and tarbiyah in Dar al-Arqam.
- Then he invited his relatives to a meal and called them; the public call from the hill of Safa.
- Opposition: led by Abu Lahab and Abu Jahl – harsh words, physical harm, temptation, the three-year boycott in Shi'b Abi Talib.
- The hijrahs to Abyssinia in years 5 and 7 of prophethood; Hamzah and 'Umar (RA) accepted Islam.
- The Year of Sorrow (the deaths of Abu Talib and Khadijah); the journey to Ta'if.
- The pledges of 'Aqabah (12 people, then 72) → the hijrah to Madinah.
- Patience and a suitable approach were the reasons for the success of the da'wah.

**Answer explanation:** Write in stages, in chronological order.
