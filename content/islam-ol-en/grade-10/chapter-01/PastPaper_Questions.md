### A1. 2016 – Islam I, Q24

Question ID: P2016-I-Q24  
Year: 2016  
Paper and question: Islam I, Q24  
Marks: not stated on the paper (Islam I multiple-choice question)  
Source: OL_2016_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

Which of the following does NOT refer to the 'Day of Judgement'?

- **A.** Yawm al-Akhirah
- **B.** Yawm an-Nahr
- **C.** Yawm al-Qiyamah
- **D.** Yawm ad-Din

**Correct answer:** B – Yawm an-Nahr *(option (2) on the original paper)*

**Explanation:** Yawm an-Nahr = 10 Dhul-Hijjah (the day of sacrifice). The others are names of the Day of Judgement.

### B1. 2018 – Islam II, Q2 (i)

Question ID: P2018-II-Q2.1  
Year: 2018  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q2 (i)  
Marks: 10 marks for the whole of Q2 (division among sub-questions not given)  
Source: OL_2018_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(i) What is 'iman mufassal'?

**Suggested answer:**

Iman mufassal = detailed faith – the declaration of belief that sets out each of the six pillars of iman separately:
آمَنْتُ بِاللهِ وَمَلَائِكَتِهِ وَكُتُبِهِ وَرُسُلِهِ وَالْيَوْمِ الْآخِرِ وَالْقَدْرِ خَيْرِهِ وَشَرِّهِ مِنَ اللهِ تَعَالَى
"I believe in Allah, His angels, His Books, His messengers, the Last Day, and in divine decree – that all good and evil happen by the will of Allah." (meaning)

**Answer explanation:** (The brief form of faith is called "iman mujmal".)

**Marking guidance (suggested only – not an official marking scheme):** Definition + the six elements.

**Answer status:** Suggested – not an official answer (no official marking scheme was supplied); based on the Grade 10/11 textbook content

**Verification note:** The term "iman mufassal" is not in the current textbook; its content is the six pillars in the Hadith of Jibril. Part (ii) of this question is under Grade 11, Chapter 4.
