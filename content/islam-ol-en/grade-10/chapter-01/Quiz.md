# Grade 10 – Chapter 1: Aqeedah (Islamic Creed) – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_01/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q13 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. In Islamic usage, which is the correct explanation of "aqeedah"?

A. Customs handed down from our ancestors
B. Beliefs built on final and certain evidence
C. Ideas formed by reason alone
D. The rulings of acts of worship such as prayer and fasting

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Aqeedah is belief built on certain evidence (the Quran and the Sunnah). The rulings of worship belong to Fiqh; neither reason alone nor custom is the basis of aqeedah.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. What are the basic sources of Islamic aqeedah?

A. The Quran and the Sunnah
B. The Quran and the opinions of scholars
C. The Sunnah and social custom
D. Reason and philosophy

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: The Quran and the Sunnah are the basis of 'ilm al-aqeedah. They are what tell us about the basic beliefs of Islam.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q3. How much of the Quran speaks about belief?

A. A quarter
B. More than half
C. More than a third
D. A tenth

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: More than a third of the Quran speaks about belief. This shows how important belief is in Islam.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q4. In the hadith that explains the six pillars of Iman, who asked the Prophet ﷺ the question?

A. Abu Bakr (RA)
B. Mika'il (AS)
C. Umar (RA)
D. Jibril (AS)

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: When Jibril (AS) asked, "Tell me about Iman," the Prophet ﷺ stated the six beliefs. It is recorded in Bukhari, which is why it is called "the Hadith of Jibril".

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q5. Which verse of the Quran is given in this chapter as evidence that "all the prophets taught the same aqeedah"?

A. 2:25
B. 42:13
C. 16:97
D. 2:255

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Verse 42:13 says that the same religion was enjoined on Nuh, Ibrahim, Musa and Isa (AS) and on the Prophet ﷺ. This shows that aqeedah does not change with the times.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q6. Belief in qada' and qadar comes under which division of aqeedah?

A. An-Nubuwwat
B. Ar-Ruhaniyyat
C. Al-Ilahiyyat
D. As-Sam'iyyat

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Belief in Allah and belief in qada' and qadar both come under "Al-Ilahiyyat". Because divine decree is linked to Allah's will, it belongs to the division about Allah.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q7. Belief in the Books comes under which division?

A. An-Nubuwwat
B. Al-Ilahiyyat
C. As-Sam'iyyat
D. Ar-Ruhaniyyat

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: The Books came to people through the prophets. So belief in the prophets, the messengers and the Books all comes under "An-Nubuwwat".

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q8. Which of the following is NOT included in "Ar-Ruhaniyyat"?

A. Angels
B. Jinn
C. Devils (shayatin)
D. Mizan

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Ruhaniyyat is about creations beyond the senses: angels, jinn, devils and the ruh. The mizan (the scales) belongs to the Hereafter, so it comes under "As-Sam'iyyat".

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q9. The grave, ba'th, hisab, sirat, jannah and nar belong to which division?

A. Al-Ilahiyyat
B. As-Sam'iyyat
C. An-Nubuwwat
D. Ar-Ruhaniyyat

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Everything connected with life in the Hereafter comes under "As-Sam'iyyat".

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q10. Which of the following is NOT given as a reason why aqeedah later lost its purpose?

A. Political divisions
B. The influence of foreign ideas
C. Excessive use of reason, beyond its limits
D. Firmly following the Quran and the Sunnah

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Following the Quran and the Sunnah protects aqeedah; it is not a cause of the problem. Political divisions, foreign ideas and excessive use of reason turned aqeedah into a mere topic of debate.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q11. Which of the following is NOT one of the mistaken groups that appeared in matters of aqeedah?

A. Mu'tazilah
B. Jabriyyah
C. Ahl al-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah
D. Murji'ah

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Ahl al-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah are those who follow the way of the Prophet ﷺ and his Companions. Jabriyyah, Qadariyyah, Murji'ah and Mu'tazilah are examples of groups with mistaken views.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q12. Who is NOT one of the imams who worked to protect the purity of Islamic aqeedah?

A. Imam Ahmad ibn Hanbal
B. Imam Abu al-Hasan al-Ash'ari
C. Imam Abu Mansur al-Maturidi
D. Imam Bukhari

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Imam Bukhari is famous as a compiler of hadith. The imams named in this chapter as protectors of aqeedah are Ahmad ibn Hanbal, Abu al-Hasan al-Ash'ari and Abu Mansur al-Maturidi.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q13. A student says, "Times have changed, so we can change our beliefs too." According to this chapter, which is the correct response?

A. Correct – every age needs a new aqeedah
B. Wrong – aqeedah does not change, because all the prophets taught the same aqeedah
C. Correct – aqeedah can be changed to suit reason
D. It can be changed if scholars wish

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: From Nuh (AS) to the Prophet ﷺ, all taught the same aqeedah (42:13). So aqeedah is not something that changes with the times. It was the excessive use of reason that caused the mistaken groups to appear.

Difficulty: Hard
