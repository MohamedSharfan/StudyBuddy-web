# Grade 10 – Chapter 1: Aqeedah (Islamic Creed) – Flashcards

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_01/Flashcards.md` (Tamil), cards 1–15 in the same order.

---

Flashcard 1

Front: عقيدة (Aqeedah)

Back: Belief. Its plural is aqa'id (عقائد).

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 1 | Topic: Meaning of aqeedah

---

Flashcard 2

Front: What is 'ilm al-aqeedah / 'ilm al-aqa'id?

Back: The branch of knowledge that developed to set out the beliefs of Islam.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 1 | Topic: Meaning of aqeedah

---

Flashcard 3

Front: What are the six pillars of Iman?

Back: Belief in Allah, the angels, the Books, the messengers, the Last Day, and qada' and qadar (that good and bad happen according to Allah's will).

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 1 | Topic: Six pillars of Iman

---

Flashcard 4

Front: الإلهيات (Al-Ilahiyyat)

Back: The division that includes belief in Allah and belief in qada' and qadar.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 1 | Topic: Four divisions of aqeedah

---

Flashcard 5

Front: النبوات (An-Nubuwwat)

Back: The division that includes belief in the prophets, the messengers and the Books.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 1 | Topic: Four divisions of aqeedah

---

Flashcard 6

Front: الروحانيات (Ar-Ruhaniyyat)

Back: The division about things beyond the senses: angels, jinn, devils (shayatin) and the ruh.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 1 | Topic: Four divisions of aqeedah

---

Flashcard 7

Front: السمعيات (As-Sam'iyyat)

Back: Matters of the Hereafter: the grave, ba'th, mahshar, hisab, mizan, hawd, sirat, jannah and nar.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 1 | Topic: Four divisions of aqeedah

---

Flashcard 8

Front: Mizan (ميزان)

Back: The scales on which deeds will be weighed in the Hereafter.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 1 | Topic: The Hereafter

---

Flashcard 9

Front: Sirat (صراط)

Back: The bridge (path) that must be crossed in the Hereafter.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 1 | Topic: The Hereafter

---

Flashcard 10

Front: Ba'th (بعث)

Back: The raising of the dead to life again in the Hereafter (resurrection).

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 1 | Topic: The Hereafter

---

Flashcard 11

Front: Hisab (حساب)

Back: Being called to account for one's deeds in the Hereafter (the reckoning).

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 1 | Topic: The Hereafter

---

Flashcard 12

Front: Which verse of the Quran shows that aqeedah is the same for all prophets?

Back: Quran 42:13 – the same religion was enjoined on all the prophets.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 1 | Topic: One aqeedah for all prophets

---

Flashcard 13

Front: Name the four mistaken groups that appeared in matters of aqeedah.

Back: Jabriyyah, Qadariyyah, Murji'ah, Mu'tazilah.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 1 | Topic: Mistaken groups

---

Flashcard 14

Front: Who are the three imams who protected aqeedah?

Back: Ahmad ibn Hanbal, Abu al-Hasan al-Ash'ari, Abu Mansur al-Maturidi.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 1 | Topic: Imams who protected aqeedah

---

Flashcard 15

Front: Who are Ahl al-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah?

Back: Those who follow the way of the Prophet ﷺ and his Companions.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 1 | Topic: Ahl al-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah
