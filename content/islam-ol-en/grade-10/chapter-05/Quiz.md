# Grade 10 – Chapter 5: An-Najasah (Impurity) – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_05/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q13 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. On what basis is najis divided into three types?

A. Its colour
B. The way it is cleaned
C. Its quantity
D. The part of the body it comes from

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Najis is divided into ordinary, light and severe najis according to the way it has to be cleaned.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. Which of the following is NOT ordinary najis?

A. Vomit
B. Pus
C. Breast milk
D. An alcoholic drink

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Breast milk is not najis; it is one of the exceptions. Vomit, pus and alcoholic drinks are all ordinary najis.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q3. Which of the following animals, if it dies by itself, is NOT considered najis?

A. A chicken
B. A goat
C. A cat
D. A fish

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: The dead body of an animal is generally najis. However, fish and locusts that die by themselves are not najis, because they are permitted to be eaten.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q4. When washing ordinary najis with water, what must be removed before the washing is complete?

A. Its colour only
B. All three: its taste, colour and smell
C. Its smell only
D. Its colour and smell only

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: After removing the najis, the place must be washed until its taste, colour and smell are all gone. Soap or washing powder may be used if needed.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q5. After cleaning, which of the following, if it remains, means the place is NOT considered clean?

A. Both colour and smell together, or taste alone
B. Only dampness
C. The smell of soap
D. The coolness of the water

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: If colour and smell remain together, or if taste alone remains, the najis is considered to be still present, so the place is not clean.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q6. Which of the following is "light najis"?

A. The urine of any child under two years old
B. The urine of a baby boy under two years old who is fed only on breast milk
C. The urine of a baby girl who is fed only on breast milk
D. The urine of a boy under five years old

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: All three conditions are needed: a baby boy, under two years old, fed only on breast milk. A baby girl's urine does not belong to this type.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q7. How is light najis cleaned?

A. By washing seven times
B. By washing with water mixed with soil
C. By squeezing out the najis and then sprinkling enough clean water over it
D. By leaving it to dry in the sun

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: For light najis, sprinkling water is enough. Washing seven times and using water mixed with soil are for severe najis.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q8. Which of the following is severe najis?

A. A dog's saliva
B. A cat's urine
C. Human blood
D. Cow dung

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Everything from dogs and pigs – their faeces, urine, saliva, sweat and blood – is severe najis. The other options are ordinary najis.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q9. Severe najis touched a place, but no colour, smell or taste can be noticed. What must be done?

A. Nothing needs to be done
B. Washing once is enough
C. Sprinkling water is enough
D. It must be washed seven times, including once with water mixed with soil

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Washing seven times is compulsory for severe najis even when none of its features can be noticed. The water mixed with soil may be used in the first washing or in one of the middle washings.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q10. When cleaning severe najis in running water or in a large body of water such as the sea or a tank, what is sufficient?

A. Dipping it once
B. Stirring the water a little, dipping the item and then rinsing it six times
C. Rinsing it ten times
D. Only rubbing it with soil

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: According to the textbook, in a large body of water or running water it is sufficient to stir the water, dip the item and then rinse it six times.

Difficulty: Hard

Verification Note: The "six times" figure follows the Tamil textbook as recorded in `VERIFY.md` (Grade 10, Chapter 5). Confirm with a teacher before relying on it.

---

Q11. Can a drum be made from the skin of a dead cow?

A. No; the skin of a dead animal is always najis
B. Yes, and it may also be used as food
C. Yes; once the skin has been properly tanned, it may be used for purposes other than food
D. Only if it is washed seven times

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: The skin of any animal except dogs and pigs may be used for purposes other than food once it has been tanned. A cow is neither a dog nor a pig, so its tanned skin can be used.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q12. What is the ruling on toddy, with nothing else added, that turns into vinegar by itself?

A. It may be used as food
B. It remains najis
C. It may be used only as medicine
D. It may be used after washing seven times

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: The textbook states that if natural toddy with nothing else mixed into it turns into vinegar by itself, it may be used as food.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q13. If najis falls on butter that is in a solid state, what should be done?

A. All the butter must be thrown away
B. Remove the part touched by the najis and use the rest
C. Boil it
D. Wash it seven times

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: In solid foods such as ghee, butter and cheese, najis does not spread. So only the part touched by the najis needs to be removed, and the rest can be used.

Difficulty: Medium
