### A1. 2018 – Islam I, Q39

Question ID: P2018-I-Q39  
Year: 2018  
Paper and question: Islam I, Q39  
Marks: not stated on the paper (Islam I multiple-choice question)  
Source: OL_2018_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

The important point to note when washing clothes stained with najis in a washing machine

- **A.** washing well
- **B.** using scented washing powder
- **C.** washing three times
- **D.** taking each garment out of the washing machine and washing it at least once by letting water run over it

**Correct answer:** D – taking each garment out of the washing machine and washing it at least once by letting water run over it *(option (4) on the original paper)*

**Explanation:** If najis mixes with a small amount of water, that water becomes najis; so after the najis has been removed, each garment must be washed by letting clean water run over it.

### A2. 2021 (2022) – Islam I, Q10

Question ID: P2021_2022-I-Q10  
Year: 2021 (2022)  
Paper and question: Islam I, Q10  
Marks: not stated on the paper (Islam I multiple-choice question)  
Source: OL_2021_2022_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

The urine of a boy under two years old who takes only breast milk as food

- **A.** is severe najis.
- **B.** is light najis.
- **C.** is najis that cannot be cleaned.
- **D.** is ordinary najis.

**Correct answer:** B – is light najis. *(option (2) on the original paper)*

**Explanation:** This is najasah mukhaffafah (light impurity) – sprinkling with water is enough.

### B1. 2017 – Islam II, Q1 (i)

Question ID: P2017-II-Q1.1  
Year: 2017  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q1 (i)  
Marks: 20 marks for the whole of Q1 (no separate marks given for sub-questions)  
Source: OL_2017_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

Give short answers.
(i) State two divisions of najis.

**Suggested answer:**

Any two of the following:
1. Ordinary najis (e.g. faeces, urine, blood)
2. Light najis (the urine of a boy under two who drinks only breast milk)
3. Severe najis (things relating to dogs and pigs)

**Answer explanation:** The textbook divides najis into three according to the method of cleaning.

**Marking guidance (suggested only – not an official marking scheme):** Any two divisions.

### B2. 2022 (2023) – Islam II, Q3 (ii)

Question ID: P2022_2023-II-Q3.2  
Year: 2022 (2023)  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q3 (ii)  
Marks: 10 marks for the whole of Q3  
Source: OL_2022_2023_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(ii) State the three kinds of najis.

**Suggested answer:**

1. Najasah mukhaffafah – light impurity (e.g. the urine of a boy under two who drinks only breast milk)
2. Najasah mutawassitah – medium impurity (e.g. faeces, urine, blood)
3. Najasah mughallazah – severe impurity (things relating to dogs and pigs)

**Answer explanation:** An example may be added for each.

**Marking guidance (suggested only – not an official marking scheme):** All three kinds.
