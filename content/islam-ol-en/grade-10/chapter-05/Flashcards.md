# Grade 10 – Chapter 5: An-Najasah (Impurity) – Flashcards

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_05/Flashcards.md` (Tamil), cards 1–13 in the same order.

---

Flashcard 1

Front: النجاسة (An-Najasah)

Back: Impurity.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 5 | Topic: Meaning of najasah

---

Flashcard 2

Front: What are the three types of najis?

Back: Ordinary najis, light najis and severe najis.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 5 | Topic: Types of najis

---

Flashcard 3

Front: Give five examples of ordinary najis.

Back: Faeces, urine, blood, pus, vomit (also: madhy, wady, alcoholic drinks, dead bodies of animals).

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 5 | Topic: Ordinary najis

---

Flashcard 4

Front: Madhy and wady belong to which type of najis?

Back: Ordinary najis.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 5 | Topic: Ordinary najis

---

Flashcard 5

Front: Which dead creatures are NOT najis?

Back: Fish and locusts that die by themselves; creatures with no flowing blood, such as ants and bees.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 5 | Topic: Exceptions

---

Flashcard 6

Front: Which parts of a dead animal that is permitted to be eaten are NOT najis?

Back: Bones, horns, nails, hair and feathers.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 5 | Topic: Exceptions

---

Flashcard 7

Front: What are the three conditions for light najis?

Back: The urine of a baby boy + under two years old + fed only on breast milk.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 5 | Topic: Light najis

---

Flashcard 8

Front: Severe najis is connected with which animals?

Back: Dogs and pigs – their faeces, urine, saliva, sweat and blood.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 5 | Topic: Severe najis

---

Flashcard 9

Front: How is severe najis cleaned?

Back: By washing seven times, one of which (the first or one of the middle washings) is with water mixed with soil.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 5 | Topic: Severe najis

---

Flashcard 10

Front: How many times must an item with severe najis be rinsed in a large body of water or running water?

Back: Six times, after stirring the water and dipping the item. (⚠️ Figure as stated in the textbook – confirm with a teacher.)

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 5 | Topic: Severe najis

---

Flashcard 11

Front: Which tanned skins may be used?

Back: The skin of any animal except dogs and pigs – for purposes other than food.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 5 | Topic: Cleaning and using impure things

---

Flashcard 12

Front: Najis falls on ghee, butter or cheese. What can be done?

Back: Remove the part touched by the najis and use the rest.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 5 | Topic: Cleaning and using impure things

---

Flashcard 13

Front: What is the ruling on toddy that turns into vinegar by itself?

Back: If nothing else was added to it, it may be used as food.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 5 | Topic: Cleaning and using impure things
