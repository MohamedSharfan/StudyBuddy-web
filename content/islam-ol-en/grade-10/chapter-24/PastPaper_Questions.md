### A1. 2022 (2023) – Islam I, Q32

Question ID: P2022_2023-I-Q32  
Year: 2022 (2023)  
Paper and question: Islam I, Q32  
Marks: not stated on the paper (Islam I multiple-choice question)  
Source: OL_2022_2023_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

Which is NOT a requirement for goods being sold in trade?

- **A.** belonging to the seller
- **B.** being present at the place of sale
- **C.** being able to be handed over to the buyer
- **D.** being clean and useful according to Islam

**Correct answer:** B – being present at the place of sale *(option (2) on the original paper)*

**Explanation:** Goods for sale must belong to the seller, be able to be handed over, and be clean and useful; being at the place of sale is not a condition.

### B1. 2018 – Islam II, Q1 (vii)

Question ID: P2018-II-Q1.7  
Year: 2018  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q1 (vii)  
Marks: 20 marks for the whole of Q1  
Source: OL_2018_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(vii) Write two qualities that goods for sale must have.

**Suggested answer:**

Any two of the following:
1. Belonging to the seller
2. Being able to be handed over to the buyer
3. Being in stock (in hand)
4. Being clean and useful according to the Shari'ah

**Answer explanation:** These are the conditions concerning "the goods", the second basic element of a sale.

**Marking guidance (suggested only – not an official marking scheme):** Two qualities.
