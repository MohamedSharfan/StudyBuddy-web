# Grade 10 – Chapter 24: Work – II – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_24/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q16 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. Which is the correct definition of "trade"?

A. Giving an item free of charge
B. Lending money
C. Producing an item
D. Receiving something in exchange for a possession and handing over its ownership to another person

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Trade is handing over ownership after receiving something in exchange (the price). Producing is industry or agriculture.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. What does the textbook say the trader's role is?

A. Collecting taxes for the government
B. The link between the producer and the consumer
C. Lending money at interest
D. Only a producer

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Agricultural and industrial products reach people through trade; the trader is the link between the two.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q3. Why did 'Umar (RA) warn that those without knowledge of the laws of trade should not trade in the market?

A. Willingly or not, they may end up consuming interest
B. They will make a large profit
C. They will not pay tax
D. They will waste goods

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: It is difficult to tell trade and interest apart; a person without legal knowledge can fall into interest without realising it.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q4. What are the three pillars of trade?

A. Buyer and seller, the item traded, ijab and qabul
B. Capital, profit, loss
C. Shop, goods, money
D. Witness, writing, oath

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: From the Islamic point of view, these three pillars and their conditions must all be fulfilled in a sale.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q5. Which of the following is NOT a condition for the buyer and seller?

A. Being mature
B. Being of sound mind
C. Being wealthy
D. Knowing the details of the transaction

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Maturity, sound mind and knowledge of the transaction are the conditions. There is no need to be wealthy.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q6. A person tries to sell an item that is not yet his and that he does not have in hand. Which conditions for the traded item does this break?

A. Belonging to the seller, and being in stock
B. Cleanliness and usefulness
C. Ijab and qabul
D. Being mature

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: The traded item must belong to the seller, be deliverable, be in stock, and be clean and useful.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q7. What does "ijab" refer to?

A. The seller stating that he has sold the item
B. The buyer stating that he has accepted the item
C. The price of the item
D. Profit

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Ijab = the seller's statement; qabul = the buyer's acceptance. These may take place verbally, in writing, or by other methods in common use.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q8. Which of the following is NOT forbidden in trade?

A. Hoarding
B. Adulteration
C. Unnecessary oaths
D. Making a fair profit

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Making a fair profit is permitted. Unfair pricing, hoarding, adulteration, unnecessary oaths and the like are what is forbidden.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q9. Which of the following is NOT a benefit of trading according to Islamic principles?

A. Buying and selling becomes worship
B. Good relations between buyers and sellers are kept
C. Protection from false accusations
D. No need to pay tax

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: The five benefits: becoming worship, good relations, stability, protection from accusations, freedom from haram. Breaking the general laws is forbidden.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q10. What is the capital of a service worker?

A. Land
B. A shop
C. Machines
D. Knowledge, skill, time and effort

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: In a service occupation no goods are sold; the worker's knowledge, skill, time and effort are the capital.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q11. Which of the following is a "professional (knowledge-based)" service occupation?

A. Security work
B. Qadi (judge)
C. Minor staff work
D. Office support work

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Professional: teaching, nursing, clerical work, qadi. Manual: minor staff work, office support work, security work.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q12. What does the hadith "Taking a rope, gathering firewood and selling it is better than begging" teach?

A. Cutting firewood is the only good work
B. Begging is permitted
C. Islam does not measure a person's status by his work; any halal work is better than begging
D. Hard work is not needed

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Whatever the work, Islam respects it and the person who does it; even simple work is more dignified than begging.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q13. When Abu Dharr al-Ghifari (RA) asked for a position, what was the essence of the Prophet's ﷺ reply?

A. "You are strong; I will give you a position at once"
B. "Asking for a position is sunnah"
C. "You are weak; a position is a trust; if its duties are not done properly it will bring regret and sorrow in the Hereafter"
D. "No qualification is needed for a position"

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: One must choose work that suits one's ability, and must not seek an unsuitable position for the sake of status (Muslim).

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q14. According to the hadith in Tirmidhi, which judge will be in Paradise?

A. One who understood the truth and judged against it
B. One who understood the truth and judged by it
C. One who judged without knowledge
D. One who never judged

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Of the three kinds of judges, only one will be in Paradise – the one who understood the truth and judged by it. The other two will be in Hell.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q15. Which saying of the Prophet ﷺ concerns the rights of a service worker?

A. "Pay the wage after a year"
B. "A worker needs no wage"
C. "Give the worker his wage before his sweat dries"
D. "Half the wage is enough"

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: This saying stresses that service workers must be paid the right wage at the right time.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q16. What does the textbook say about a worker who expects more pay but gives less service?

A. He will get extra pay
B. He earns only Allah's curse
C. It is permitted
D. It is sunnah

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Work becomes worship only when it is treated as a trust and done with trustworthiness and honesty; reducing one's service earns Allah's curse.

Difficulty: Medium
