# Grade 10 – Chapter 24: Work – II – Flashcards

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_24/Flashcards.md` (Tamil), cards 1–15 in the same order.

---

Flashcard 1

Front: What is trade?

Back: Receiving something in exchange for a possession / item and handing over its ownership to another person.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 24 | Topic: Trade

---

Flashcard 2

Front: With whom will the trustworthy, honest traders be in the Hereafter (Tirmidhi)?

Back: With the prophets and the martyrs (shuhada').

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 24 | Topic: Trade

---

Flashcard 3

Front: What are the three pillars of trade?

Back: Buyer and seller; the item traded; ijab and qabul.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 24 | Topic: Pillars of trade

---

Flashcard 4

Front: What are the three conditions for the buyer and seller?

Back: Being mature, being of sound mind, knowing the details of the transaction.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 24 | Topic: Pillars of trade

---

Flashcard 5

Front: What are the four conditions for the traded item?

Back: Owned by the seller, able to be handed over, in stock, clean and useful according to the Shari'ah.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 24 | Topic: Pillars of trade

---

Flashcard 6

Front: إيجاب (Ijab)

Back: The seller stating that he has sold the item (the offer).

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 24 | Topic: Pillars of trade

---

Flashcard 7

Front: قبول (Qabul)

Back: The buyer stating that he has accepted the item (the acceptance).

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 24 | Topic: Pillars of trade

---

Flashcard 8

Front: What are the eight things forbidden in trade?

Back: Unnecessary oaths, false measures and weights, hoarding, unfair prices, adulteration, lying, deception, breaking the general laws.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 24 | Topic: Forbidden in trade

---

Flashcard 9

Front: What are the five benefits of trading the Islamic way?

Back: It becomes worship, good relations, stability, protection from accusations, freedom from haram.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 24 | Topic: Benefits

---

Flashcard 10

Front: What is a service occupation?

Back: Providing a service to someone and receiving the proper wage for it.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 24 | Topic: Service occupations

---

Flashcard 11

Front: What are the two kinds of service occupation?

Back: Professional (teaching, nursing, clerical work, qadi); manual (minor staff work, security work).

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 24 | Topic: Service occupations

---

Flashcard 12

Front: قاضي (Qadi)

Back: A judge.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 24 | Topic: Service occupations

---

Flashcard 13

Front: "A position is a trust" – to whom was this said?

Back: To Abu Dharr al-Ghifari (RA) (Muslim).

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 24 | Topic: Suitable work

---

Flashcard 14

Front: What is the meaning of verse 4:58 about giving justice?

Back: When you judge between people, judge with justice, without bias.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 24 | Topic: Duties of service workers

---

Flashcard 15

Front: What is the chief duty of a service worker?

Back: To provide the service in line with the wage received and the agreements made.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 24 | Topic: Duties of service workers
