## 1. Trade 🛒

> 📖 **Definition:** the act of receiving **something in exchange for a possession or an item** and **handing over its ownership to another person**.

- Agricultural and industrial products reach people **through trade**.
- The trader is **the link between the producer and the consumer.**
- So Islam has **permitted** trade and has **praised** the trader's service.

> 📖 **Hadith (Tirmidhi) – meaning:** "The trustworthy, honest traders will be (in the Hereafter) **with the prophets and the martyrs (shuhada')**."

### Why is trade complicated?
- Some people are confused and **cannot tell trade apart from interest**.
- Others do not know the conditions for it to be halal and think **only of making a profit**.
- So Islamic legal scholars have explained the laws of trade on the basis of the Quran and the Sunnah; it is **a duty** for a Muslim trader to know them and act on them.

### 'Umar's (RA) warning
> **No one should trade in our market except those who understand the laws of trade**; otherwise, willingly or not, they may end up **consuming interest**. *(meaning)*

---

## 2. The three pillars of trade and their conditions ⭐

### Pillar 1: The buyer and the seller
1. Must be **mature (past puberty)**
2. Must be **of sound mind**
3. Must **know the details of the transaction**

### Pillar 2: The item being traded
1. Must **belong to the seller**
2. Must be **able to be handed over to the buyer**
3. Must be **in stock (in hand)**
4. Must be **clean (pure) and useful** according to the Islamic Shari'ah

### Pillar 3: Ijab and qabul (إيجاب – قبول) – offer and acceptance
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Ijab** | **The seller** stating that he **has sold** the item |
| **Qabul** | **The buyer** stating that he **has accepted** it |

- This may take place **verbally, in writing, or by other methods in common use**.

💡 **Memory tip:** **Persons – item – agreement** (buyer/seller – item – ijab/qabul).

2. **Cheating in measure and weight**
3. **Hoarding**
4. **Unfair pricing**
5. **Adulteration**

## 4. Benefits of keeping to Islamic principles (5)
1. Buying and selling **becomes worship.**
2. **Good relations** are kept between buyers and sellers.
3. The business becomes **stable.**
4. Protection **from false accusations.**
5. Freedom **from haram dealings.**

## 5. Service occupations 👨‍🏫👮
> 📖 **Service occupation:** providing **a service** to someone and receiving the proper **wage** for it.

- A service worker's **capital** is his **knowledge, skill, time and effort.**
- Providing services according to one's knowledge and skill and earning an income from it is **a practice Islam permits.**

### The two kinds of service occupation ⭐
| Kind | Examples |
|---|---|
| **a) Professional (knowledge-based)** | Teaching, **nursing**, **clerical work**, **qadi** (judge) |
| **b) Manual (physical labour)** | Minor staff work, office support work, security (guard) work |

### Islam does not measure status by occupation
- Some jobs may bring more social status and pay; but Islam **does not measure a person's status by the work he does.**
- **Hadith – meaning:** "For one of you to **take his rope, gather firewood**, carry it on his back and sell it is **better than begging from people**."

### Work that suits one's ability
- Every person has **special abilities** from Allah; it is best to recognise them and do **work that suits them**.
- Islam does not allow taking up **unsuitable work for the sake of status or pay**.

#### The incident of Abu Dharr al-Ghifari (RA) (Muslim)
- "O Messenger of Allah! Will you not appoint me to **a position**?"
- The Prophet ﷺ patted his shoulder: "Abu Dharr! **You are weak, and a position is a trust (amanah).** On the Day of Judgement it will be **regret and sorrow**, except for one who takes it rightfully and fulfils its duties properly." *(meaning)*

#### Three kinds of judges (Tirmidhi)
| Kind | Place |
|---|---|
| One who understood the truth and judged **by it** | **Paradise** |
| One who understood the truth and judged **against it** | **Hell** |
| One who judged **without knowledge** | **Hell** |

👉 Those who do not seek work suited to the different abilities Allah gave them **misuse Allah's blessing**.

## 6. The rights and duties of a service worker ⭐

### Rights
- A service occupation is **in exchange for a wage**.
- Service workers must be **treated with respect**; their services must not be wasted, **their rights must be protected, and they must be paid the right wage at the right time**.
- **Hadith – meaning:** "Give the worker his wage **before his sweat dries**."
- Their contribution to a country's economic development is **huge**.

### Duties
- The chief duty is to provide the service **in line with the wage received and the agreements made**.
- **(4:58) – meaning:** Allah commands that when you judge between people you judge **with justice** (without bias). → Those who provide the service of justice must do it **properly**.
- One who expects more pay but **gives less service** earns only **Allah's curse**.
- If every service worker regards his work as **a trust (amanah)** and acts **with trustworthiness and honesty**, it **becomes worship**.
