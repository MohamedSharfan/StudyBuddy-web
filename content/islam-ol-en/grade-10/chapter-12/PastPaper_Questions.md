Which of the following phrases refers to Prophet Muhammad ﷺ?

- **A.** Rabb an-nas
- **B.** Hudan lin-nas
- **C.** Rabb al-'alamin
- **D.** Rahmatan lil-'alamin

**Correct answer:** D – Rahmatan lil-'alamin *(option (4) on the original paper)*

### B1. 2016 – Islam II, Q7 (iii)

Question ID: P2016-II-Q7.3  
Year: 2016  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q7 (iii)  
Marks: 10 marks (short notes on two topics)  
Source: OL_2016_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(iii) The benefits of giving (charity)

**Suggested answer:**

Giving = giving others part of the wealth Allah has granted.
Benefits:
1. In this world it earns the love of Allah and the goodwill of people.
2. A great reward in the Hereafter (2:274).
3. The heart is purified from miserliness and success is gained (64:16).
4. The gap between rich and poor narrows, and social unity and brotherhood grow.
5. Barakah comes into the wealth; giving does not reduce it, and what is with Allah remains (16:96).

**Answer explanation:** The textbook mentions five benefits of giving and the verses 2:274, 64:16 and 16:96.

**Marking guidance (suggested only – not an official marking scheme):** No official marking scheme.

**Answer status:** Suggested – not an official answer (no official marking scheme was supplied); based on the Grade 10/11 textbook content

### B3. 2021 (2022) – Islam II, Q2 (iii)

Question ID: P2021_2022-II-Q2.2  
Year: 2021 (2022)  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q2 (iii)  
Marks: 10 marks for the whole of Q2  
Source: OL_2021_2022_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(iii) State five of the good qualities Islam teaches, and explain them.

**Suggested answer:**

1. Love – love for Allah, the Prophet ﷺ, oneself, others, other creatures and the environment; love is connected with iman.
2. Sacrifice – giving up one's own wishes for the good of others (e.g. the Ansar sharing their property with the Muhajirun).
3. Giving (charity) – spending wealth generously for Allah's pleasure.
4. Honesty (amanah) – being trustworthy in promises, responsibilities and trade (the Prophet ﷺ – al-Amin).
5. Self-control of the senses – guarding the eyes, ears, tongue and other senses from what is haram.
6. Truthfulness, patience, humility, forgiveness (Grade 10 Ch. 11; Grade 11 Ch. 11).

**Answer explanation:** As the question says "explain", give the meaning and evidence / an example for each quality.

**Marking guidance (suggested only – not an official marking scheme):** No official marking scheme.

**Answer status:** Suggested – not an official answer (no official marking scheme was supplied); based on the Grade 10/11 textbook content

**Verification note:** The example of the Ansar's sacrifice is based on general knowledge of the sirah.
