# Grade 10 – Chapter 12: The Islamic View of Good Character – II – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_12/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q18 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. Among the names of Allah, what does "al-Wadud" mean?

A. The Most Gracious
B. The Most Loving (the One who loves)
C. The One who makes rich
D. The Giver

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Names of Allah connected with love: ar-Rahman (the Most Gracious), ar-Rahim (the Most Merciful), al-Wadud (the Most Loving). Al-Mughni and al-Mu'ti are names connected with generosity.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q2. What lesson did the Prophet ﷺ teach through the event of the captives of Hawazin?

A. Captives must be freed
B. Allah has more love for His servants than a mother has for her child
C. Feeding children is a duty
D. Women must not be taken captive in war

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Pointing to a mother who hugged and fed her baby as soon as she found it, the Prophet ﷺ asked, "Would she throw her child into the fire?" and explained that Allah's love is greater than this (Bukhari).

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q3. What does the hadith "You will not believe until you love one another" teach?

A. A person without love does not have complete iman
B. Love has nothing to do with iman
C. One should love only relatives
D. Love benefits only in the Hereafter

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: This hadith (Muslim) shows that love is part of iman. A person without love does not have complete iman, and a person without iman cannot enter Paradise.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q4. "If you truly love Allah, follow me; Allah will love you" – which verse is this?

A. 9:24
B. 76:8–9
C. 3:31
D. 6:38

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Verse 3:31 shows that the true sign of love for Allah is following the Prophet ﷺ.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q5. Which of the following is NOT one of the three things that give the sweetness of iman (Bukhari, Muslim)?

A. Allah and His Messenger being dearer than everything else
B. Loving a person only for the sake of Allah
C. Hating to return to disbelief as one would hate to be thrown into fire
D. Asking Allah for a lot of wealth

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: The three things that give the sweetness of iman: the greatest love for Allah and His Messenger, loving for Allah's sake, and hating disbelief. Asking for wealth is not among them.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q6. What do Islamic scholars explain "mahabbah" to be?

A. Love mixed with awe – because of it acts of worship are done willingly
B. Desire for wealth
C. Only love between friends
D. Only fear

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Love for Allah brings worship to life. This state of love mixed with awe is mahabbah.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q7. When asked who most deserves good treatment, how many times did the Prophet ﷺ answer "your mother"?

A. Once
B. Twice
C. Three times
D. Four times

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: He said "your mother" three times, and the fourth time "your father" (Bukhari). The reason is the hardship the mother bore in carrying and feeding the child (31:14).

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q8. According to verse 31:14, after how many years did the mother wean the child?

A. One year
B. Two years
C. Three years
D. Six months

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Verse 31:14 says that she weaned him only after two years.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q9. What happened to the sinful woman who gave water to a thirsty dog, and to the woman who tied up a cat without food (Bukhari)?

A. Both went to Paradise
B. Both went to Hell
C. The first went to Paradise, the second to Hell
D. The first went to Hell, the second to Paradise

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: These hadiths teach that there is a great reward with Allah for showing mercy to living creatures, and a great punishment for tormenting them.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q10. What is the Islamic position on dogs and pigs?

A. They are najis, so they may be tormented
B. Although they are najis, it is not permitted to torment them
C. They are not najis
D. Killing them is a duty

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Although dogs and pigs are najis animals, Islam does not permit tormenting them. Mercy must be shown to every living thing.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q11. Who brought all his wealth for the expedition of Tabuk and said, "I have left Allah and His Messenger for my family"?

A. 'Umar (RA)
B. 'Uthman (RA)
C. 'Ali (RA)
D. Abu Bakr (RA)

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: This sacrifice of Abu Bakr (RA) is one of the finest examples of sacrifice in Islamic history.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q12. During a severe famine, who refused to sell trade goods worth five thousand dirhams and gave them up to face the army of Heraclius?

A. 'Abd ar-Rahman ibn 'Awf (RA)
B. 'Uthman (RA)
C. Abu Bakr (RA)
D. Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas (RA)

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Even when traders came offering a large profit, 'Uthman (RA) refused to sell and gave everything up for the Muslim army.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q13. Which verse was revealed about the Ansari couple who put their children to sleep, put out the lamp and let their guest eat?

A. 59:9
B. 59:8
C. 9:111
D. 2:274

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: 59:9 – "They prefer others over themselves, even though they are in great need." Verse 59:8 praises the Muhajirun who made hijrah as "as-Sadiqun".

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q14. What does verse 9:111 say about sacrifice?

A. Sacrifice is only a loss in this world
B. Allah has bought the believers' lives and wealth in exchange for Paradise
C. Sacrifice is not required
D. Sacrifice is rewarded only in this world

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Sacrifice may look like a loss on the outside, but 9:111 confirms that its reward is Paradise.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q15. Which of the following is NOT a reward of generosity?

A. Growth in wealth
B. Dangers being removed
C. Happiness and peace of mind
D. Wealth decreasing and poverty following

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Charity may look like a loss on the outside, but in reality it brings growth in wealth. Allah returns the "goodly loan" given to Him many times over.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q16. What did the Prophet ﷺ say to the trader who hid wet grain under the heap (Muslim)?

A. Do not trade when it rains
B. Whoever cheats is not one of us
C. Sell it at a lower price
D. Throw the grain away

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: The wet grain should have been kept on top where people could see it. Hiding a defect while selling is cheating, and a cheat is not one of the Prophet's followers.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q17. Which quality does the Prophet's ﷺ saying "Even if it were my daughter Fatimah, I would cut off her hand" show?

A. Generosity
B. Sacrifice
C. Honesty and justice (uprightness)
D. Self-control

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: When someone interceded for a woman of a noble family who had stolen, the Prophet ﷺ showed the uprightness of applying the law equally to everyone.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q18. According to the hadith in Ibn Majah, with whom will the honest, truthful trader be in the Hereafter?

A. With the angels
B. With the prophets, the truthful and the martyrs
C. With kings
D. With the wealthy

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Honest trade earns a very high rank – being with the prophets, the truthful (siddiqun) and those who died in Allah's path (shuhada').

Difficulty: Medium
