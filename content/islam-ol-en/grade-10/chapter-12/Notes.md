# Grade 10 – Chapter 12: The Islamic View of Good Character – II

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_12/Notes.md` (Tamil). English rendering.

## 🎯 What you will learn
Five good qualities taught by Islam:
1. **Love**
2. **Sacrifice**
3. **Generosity**
4. **Honesty (integrity)**
5. **Guarding the senses**

---

## 1. Love ❤️

### Allah's love
- Showing love is **one of Allah's beautiful attributes.**
- Names of Allah connected with love:

| Name | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Ar-Rahman** | The Most Gracious (whose mercy is boundless) |
| **Ar-Rahim** | The Most Merciful (whose love is unequalled) |
| **Al-Wadud** | The Most Loving (the One who loves) |

- Allah greatly loves those who show love to His servants.
- **The captives of Hawazin (Bukhari):** a mother searching for her baby found it, hugged it and fed it. The Prophet ﷺ asked, "Would she throw her child into the fire?" – "No." – "**Allah has more love for His servants than this mother has for her child.**"
- The Prophet ﷺ = **a mercy to all the worlds** (21:107)*.
- "**What cannot be won with arrows can be won with love.**"

### Love and iman
> 📖 **Hadith (Muslim) – meaning:** "You will not enter Paradise until you believe, and **you will not believe until you love one another.**"

- Showing love takes nothing away from us; instead it brings Allah's love and other people's love and respect → **happiness, unity, harmony and prosperity** in society.

### The two kinds of love
1. **Love for Allah**
2. **Love for His creation**

#### (a) Love for Allah
- Our love for Allah must be **deeper** than our love for anything created.
- **(9:24) – meaning:** If parents, children, brothers, wives, family, wealth, trade and homes are dearer to you **than Allah, His Messenger and striving in His path**, then wait for your Lord's punishment.
- **Three things that give the sweetness of iman (Bukhari, Muslim):**
  1. Allah and His Messenger are dearer to him **than everything else**
  2. He loves a person **only for the sake of Allah**
  3. He hates to return to disbelief (kufr) after being saved from it, **as he would hate to be thrown into fire**
- **(3:31) – meaning:** If you love Allah, **follow me**; Allah will love you.
- **Mahabbah (محبة):** love mixed with awe – because of it, acts of worship are done **willingly and gladly**.
- **(76:8–9) – meaning:** Out of love for Allah they feed **the poor, the orphan and the captive**: "We feed you only for the sake of Allah; we want no reward or thanks from you."
- **Hadith (Bukhari, Muslim) – meaning:** when Allah loves someone, He tells **Jibril** → Jibril loves him → **those in the heavens** love him → he is given acceptance **on earth**.

#### (b) Love for the Prophet ﷺ
- Taking the Prophet's ﷺ life as **our example**; giving priority to what he liked and disliked.
- Showing love also to the Prophet's ﷺ **family**.
- Love for the **Companions (Sahabah)**: accepting their virtues, and keeping **silent** rather than arguing about their faults or disputes.
- Accepting that all the **Ummahat al-Mu'minin** (the wives of the Prophet ﷺ) are **honoured and pure**.

#### (c) Love for oneself and for others
- **Love for oneself** = looking after one's own rights and duties. **Destroying oneself is a grave sin.**
- **Parents** deserve love, gratitude and obedience.
  - **(31:14) – meaning:** His mother carried him with hardship upon hardship, and weaned him after **two years**; be grateful to Me and to your parents.
  - **Hadith (Bukhari):** Who most deserves good treatment? → **your mother (3 times)**, then **your father.**
  - **(17:24) – meaning:** Lower to them the wing of humility out of mercy, and pray: "My Lord! Have mercy on them as they brought me up when I was small."
- **Parents towards children:** avoid harshness, scolding and constant criticism – "**A child who grows up with criticism learns only to criticise.**"
- Love for **all people** – relatives, friends, neighbours, orphans, workers, and **non-Muslims** too.

#### (d) Love for other living things and for the environment
- **(6:38) – meaning:** The creatures that walk on the earth and the birds that fly are **communities like you.**
- **Hadith (Ahmad, Hakim) – meaning:** Do not make the backs of animals into **chairs** (do not sit on them idly).
- **Hadith (Abu Dawud) – meaning:** Show mercy to those on the earth, and **the One above the heavens** will show mercy to you.
- Although **dogs and pigs** are najis, **it is not permitted to torment them.**
- **(Bukhari):** a sinful woman who gave water to a thirsty dog went to **Paradise**; a woman who tied up a cat without food went to **Hell**.
- **Caring for the environment** – keeping it clean and beautiful and developing it – also comes within love.

## 2. Sacrifice 🤲
> 📖 **Sacrifice:** giving up one thing to gain another. Helping others with one's body or wealth with the **sincere (ikhlas)** intention of doing it for Allah, and bearing the hardship that comes with it.

- Meeting **another person's need** without regard to one's own; helping without being forced and without selfishness.
- Islam is called **"a religion that blossomed through sacrifice"**.

### Examples from history
| Event | Explanation |
|---|---|
| **The Makkan Companions** | Amid the persecution of the Quraysh they sacrificed their own interests, comfort and even their lives. Allah praised them as **"as-Sadiqun"** (the truthful – 59:8) and **"al-Fa'izun"** (the successful – 9:20) |
| **Abu Bakr (RA) – the expedition of Tabuk** | He brought **all his wealth**. "What have you left for your family?" – "**Allah and His Messenger.**" |
| **'Uthman (RA)** | During a severe famine, to face the army of Heraclius, he sacrificed trade goods worth **five thousand dirhams** instead of selling them |
| **An Ansari Companion's hospitality (Bukhari, Muslim)** | There was only enough food for the children. They put the children to sleep, put out the lamp, pretended to eat and let the guest eat → **59:9** was revealed: "They **prefer others over themselves**, even though they are in great need." |

### The reward of sacrifice
- Sacrifice may look like a loss on the outside, but it is **worship ('ibadah)**; its reward is **Paradise.**
- **(9:111) – meaning:** Allah has **bought from the believers their lives and their wealth** in exchange for Paradise.

## 3. Generosity 🎁
> 📖 **Generosity:** giving others **a part of the wealth Allah has given you.**

- Allah is **al-Mughni** (the One who makes rich) and **al-Mu'ti** (the Giver).
- There is nothing wrong with enjoying wealth oneself with one's family; but when Allah tells us to give to others, it is a duty to do so.
- **Miserliness (stinginess)** → hatred, enmity, rivalry and envy grow → it is **strongly condemned.**

### Evidence
- **(2:274) – meaning:** Those who spend by night and by day, secretly and openly, have their reward with their Lord; **no fear will be on them, nor will they grieve.**
- **(64:16) – meaning:** Those who are saved from their own miserliness are **the successful.**
- Charity = **"a goodly loan"** given to Allah – He will return it **multiplied many times**.
- **(16:96) – meaning:** What you have will come to an end; **what is with Allah will remain.**
- **Hadith (Bukhari):** the Prophet ﷺ was **more generous than the blowing wind**.

### The rewards of generosity ⭐
1. In this world, **Allah's love and people's goodwill**
2. **A great reward** in the Hereafter
3. **Growth (barakah)** in wealth
4. **Dangers are removed**
5. **Happiness and peace** of mind

## 4. Honesty (integrity) 🤝
> 📖 **Honesty:** a broad word that covers truthfulness, nobility and uprightness. **Honesty and trustworthiness are like the two sides of one coin.**

- Because the Prophet ﷺ was so honest, he was praised as **as-Sadiq and al-Amin**; the people of Makkah left their valuables with him **in trust (amanah)**.

### Hadiths
| Meaning of the hadith | Source |
|---|---|
| The Prophet ﷺ waited **three days** at the place where he had promised to meet someone – "Young man, you have put me in difficulty" | Abu Dawud |
| To a man who hid wet grain underneath: "**Whoever cheats is not one of us**" | Muslim |
| A believer may have many faults, but he will not have **lying and dishonesty** | Ahmad |
| When the days come in which **trusts are lost**, the Last Day has come | Bukhari |
| A **trustworthy agent** who gladly carries out his master's order is one of those who give charity | Bukhari – Kitab al-Ijarah |
| **The honest, truthful trader** will be with the prophets, the truthful (siddiqun) and the martyrs (shuhada') in the Hereafter | Ibn Majah |

### Honesty in trade
- **(17:35) – meaning:** Give full measure when you measure, and weigh with an accurate balance.
- **(83:1–6) – meaning:** **Woe** to those who cheat in measure and weight – who take in full when they buy, and give less when they sell…

### Examples from history
- **The Treaty of Hudaybiyyah:** a Companion escaped and came to the Muslims before the treaty was finished being written; to keep his word, the Prophet ﷺ **sent him back, with great sorrow.**
- When someone interceded for a **woman of a noble family** who had stolen: "**Even if it were my daughter Fatimah, I would cut off her hand.**"
- **'Amr ibn al-'As (RA):** his honesty in **offering his own nose to be cut off** in place of the culprit who had cut the nose of a statue led many people to enter Islam.
- **Muhyiddin 'Abd al-Qadir al-Jilani (RH):** as a student, obeying his mother, he **told the robber the truth** and so changed the robber into a good man.

👉 We must avoid deception, trickery, lies, adulteration and false oaths. The Quran warns that a person who cheats will have to **bring that very thing with him on the Day of Judgement**.

## 5. Guarding the senses (self-control) 👁️
- Good character shows itself when the senses are guarded; if they are not guarded, **bad qualities** develop.
- A human being is full of desires, weak, and can become a slave to his emotions.
- So **controlling the senses and keeping life on the right path** is his chief responsibility.
