> 📝 In the textbook this chapter is written as **a conversation between an elder and some students** (Haris, Hafiz, Hisham, Hamid, Hudhayfah).

---

## 1. The three kinds of knowledge

| Knowledge | Explanation |
|---|---|
| **Sense knowledge** | Knowledge based on the senses. Given to **every living thing**, limited to what it needs |
| **Reason (intellect)** | Based on **thinking** together with sense knowledge – given to **human beings** |
| **Revealed knowledge (wahy)** | The **highest** knowledge, **beyond** both sense knowledge and reason |

### The differences ⭐
- Sense knowledge and reason are **built into the human being**; they **become sharper** as a person grows.
- Wahy is not like this – it is **not given to each person individually.** It is given to **prophets chosen from among people**, and reaches everyone else when they **convey** it.
- Wahy **guides the first two kinds of knowledge** and leads a person **to the truth**.
- Revealed knowledge is given to help the human being, who has reason, **to choose the right path**.

---

## 2. The meaning of the word wahy
### Linguistically
**Wahy (وحي)** = a sign, an inspiration, something arising in the mind, sending a message, speaking secretly.

### The sense in which the Quran uses it
- Passing a message **very quickly and very secretly**, in a way others do not know and only the one addressed understands.
- **The speech of Allah sent down from Allah to His messengers, very secretly and very quickly, in a way others do not perceive.**

---

## 3. The two kinds of wahy ⭐

| | **Wahy matluww** (وحي متلو) | **Wahy ghayr matluww** (وحي غير متلو) |
|---|---|---|
| Meaning | **Recited** revelation | **Unrecited** revelation |
| How it came | Allah recited it to the messengers through **Jibril (AS)**; they recited it to the people exactly as it was | Came to the messengers in various other ways |
| Preservation | People preserved it **orally or in writing** | — |
| Best example | **The Quran** | The **hadiths** of the Prophet ﷺ |

### The special feature of the Quran
- The Quran recited to the Prophet ﷺ has been preserved **exactly as it was, in memory and in writing, until today**.
- This is a feature that **other scriptures do not have**.

### Examples of wahy ghayr matluww
| Example | Explanation | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **True dreams** | Ibrahim (AS) **saw in a dream that he was sacrificing** his son Isma'il (AS) | 37:102 |
| **A voice from behind a veil** | When Musa (AS) went to **Mount Sinai (Tur Sina)**, Allah spoke to him as an unseen voice | 7:143, 20:11–13 |
| **Signs (gestures)** | Zakariyya (AS) taught his people **by signs** to glorify and praise Allah | 19:11 |
| **The hadiths of the Prophet ﷺ** | These are also regarded as wahy ghayr matluww | — |

## 4. Why is wahy necessary? ⭐

### (1) To overcome one's desires
- Allah has placed the urge of **desire (hawa)** in human beings to test them.
- Desire overpowers reason and pulls a person towards worldly pleasures.
- Wahy gives clarity about **good and evil, right and wrong** → the one who trusts and follows it **gradually overcomes his desires.**

### (2) To know what the senses cannot perceive
- Many things in the universe **cannot be perceived by human senses**; human sense knowledge is **limited.**
- Example: scientists say that some animals have **sharper senses** than humans.
- People who trusted only their senses believed **the earth was flat**, and even tried **to kill** the scientists who said it was **round**.

### (3) To know the relationship between Allah and His creation
- As Allah's **representative (khalifah)** on earth, a human being needs **wahy as the guide** to know the relationship between Allah and His creation.

### (4) To answer life's basic questions
Basic questions that arise in the human heart:
1. **Who am I?**
2. **Where did I come from?**
3. **Where am I going?**
4. **What is the purpose of my birth?**

- **Without the help of wahy**, sense knowledge or reason alone **cannot give a definite answer** to these.
- People who tried ended up saying: **idols are gods**; **there is no God**; **humans came from monkeys**.
- Examples: **atheists**, who deny God, and **materialists**, who deny the Hereafter.

---

## ✅ Quick revision
- Knowledge is of 3 kinds: sense knowledge (all living things), reason (humans), wahy (through prophets).
- Wahy (linguistic) = a sign, inspiration, secret speech, a message.
- **Wahy matluww** = recited through Jibril → the Quran.
- **Wahy ghayr matluww** = not recited → true dreams (37:102), a voice from behind a veil (7:143, 20:11–13), signs (19:11), hadith.
- Why wahy is needed: to overcome desires, to know what is beyond the senses, to know the Allah–creation relationship, to answer questions like "Who am I?".
