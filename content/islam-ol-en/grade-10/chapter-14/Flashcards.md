# Grade 10 – Chapter 14: The Importance of Wahy – Flashcards

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_14/Flashcards.md` (Tamil), cards 1–12 in the same order.

---

Flashcard 1

Front: وحي (Wahy)

Back: Linguistically: a sign, inspiration, a message, secret speech. The speech of Allah sent secretly and quickly from Allah to His messengers.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 14 | Topic: Meaning of wahy

---

Flashcard 2

Front: What are the three kinds of knowledge?

Back: Sense knowledge, reason, and revealed knowledge (wahy).

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 14 | Topic: Kinds of knowledge

---

Flashcard 3

Front: What is reason based on?

Back: On thinking together with sense knowledge – it belongs only to human beings.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 14 | Topic: Kinds of knowledge

---

Flashcard 4

Front: وحي متلو (Wahy matluww)

Back: Revelation recited through Jibril (AS) – e.g. the Quran.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 14 | Topic: Kinds of wahy

---

Flashcard 5

Front: وحي غير متلو (Wahy ghayr matluww)

Back: Unrecited revelation – e.g. true dreams, a voice from behind a veil, signs, hadith.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 14 | Topic: Kinds of wahy

---

Flashcard 6

Front: Wahy ghayr matluww – give the example and verse for a true dream.

Back: Ibrahim's (AS) dream of sacrificing his son – 37:102.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 14 | Topic: Kinds of wahy

---

Flashcard 7

Front: A voice from behind a veil

Back: A voice heard without the speaker being seen – Allah speaking to Musa (AS) at Mount Sinai (7:143, 20:11–13).

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 14 | Topic: Kinds of wahy

---

Flashcard 8

Front: Which form of wahy does verse 19:11 mention?

Back: Signs – Zakariyya (AS) taught his people by signs.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 14 | Topic: Kinds of wahy

---

Flashcard 9

Front: What are the four basic questions of the human heart?

Back: Who am I? Where did I come from? Where am I going? What is the purpose of my birth?

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 14 | Topic: Why wahy is necessary

---

Flashcard 10

Front: Who are atheists and materialists?

Back: Atheist = one who denies God; materialist = one who denies the Hereafter.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 14 | Topic: Why wahy is necessary

---

Flashcard 11

Front: What are the four reasons wahy is necessary?

Back: To overcome desires, to know what is beyond the senses, to know the Allah–creation relationship, and to answer life's basic questions.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 14 | Topic: Why wahy is necessary

---

Flashcard 12

Front: Desire (هوى – hawa)

Back: An urge Allah has placed in human beings to test them; it overpowers reason and pulls towards worldly pleasures.

Grade: 10 | Chapter: 14 | Topic: Why wahy is necessary
