# Grade 10 – Chapter 14: The Importance of Wahy – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_14/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q14 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. Which knowledge has been given to every living thing, limited to what it needs?

A. Revealed knowledge (wahy)
B. Reason
C. Divine knowledge
D. Sense knowledge

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Sense knowledge is given to every living thing. Human beings have, in addition, reason, which is based on thinking.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. What is the main difference between revealed knowledge and the other two kinds of knowledge?

A. Wahy becomes sharper as a person grows
B. Wahy is also given to animals
C. Wahy is not given to each person individually; it is given to chosen prophets and reaches others through them
D. Wahy is lower than reason

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Sense knowledge and reason are built into a person and grow sharper. But wahy is given only to prophets and is conveyed by them. It is the highest knowledge, guiding the other two.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q3. Linguistically, which of the following is NOT a meaning of the word "wahy"?

A. A sign
B. Preaching publicly
C. Sending a message
D. Speaking secretly

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Wahy = a sign, inspiration, something arising in the mind, sending a message, speaking secretly. Its basic nature is secrecy and speed, not public preaching.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q4. What does "wahy matluww" refer to?

A. Revelation recited to the messengers through Jibril (AS)
B. Unrecited revelation
C. Revelation received through dreams
D. The opinions of scholars

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Matluww = recited. This is the revelation recited through Jibril (AS), which the messengers recited to the people exactly as it was.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q5. What is the best example of wahy matluww?

A. The hadiths of the Prophet ﷺ
B. The dream of Ibrahim (AS)
C. The signs of Zakariyya (AS)
D. The Quran

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: The Quran is the best example of wahy matluww. Hadiths, true dreams and signs belong to wahy ghayr matluww.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q6. What special feature of the Quran, not found in other scriptures, does the textbook mention?

A. It has been preserved exactly, in memory and in writing, until today
B. It is in Arabic
C. It is long
D. It was revealed in a single day

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: The Quran recited to the Prophet ﷺ has been preserved without change, in memory and in writing, until today.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q7. The dream of Ibrahim (AS) in which he was sacrificing his son (37:102) is an example of which kind of wahy?

A. Wahy matluww
B. Only ilham (inspiration)
C. Sense knowledge
D. Wahy ghayr matluww

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: True dreams belong to unrecited revelation (wahy ghayr matluww).

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q8. With whom, and where, did Allah speak as an unseen voice?

A. Isa (AS) – Bayt al-Maqdis
B. Nuh (AS) – on the ark
C. Musa (AS) – Mount Sinai
D. Ibrahim (AS) – Makkah

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: When Musa (AS) went to Mount Sinai, Allah spoke to him as an unseen voice (7:143, 20:11–13). This is an example of wahy ghayr matluww.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q9. Which prophet taught his people by signs to glorify and praise Allah (19:11)?

A. Zakariyya (AS)
B. Yahya (AS)
C. Yunus (AS)
D. Harun (AS)

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Verse 19:11 says that Zakariyya (AS) taught his people by signs.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q10. What does the textbook say about desire as a reason why human beings need revealed knowledge?

A. Desire always urges towards good
B. Desire overpowers reason and pulls towards worldly pleasures; wahy gives clarity about good and evil and helps overcome it
C. Reason alone can overcome desire
D. There is no such thing as desire

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Allah has placed desire in human beings to test them. The one who trusts and follows wahy gradually overcomes his desires through the clarity it gives about right and wrong.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q11. What historical example does the textbook give of the result of trusting only sense knowledge?

A. The invention of the steam engine
B. Believing the earth was flat and trying to kill the scientists who said it was round
C. The growth of medicine
D. The building of ships

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Human sense knowledge is limited. Because their senses could not see it, people believed the earth was flat and even tried to kill the scientists who told the truth.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q12. Which of the following is NOT one of the basic questions that arise in the human heart?

A. Who am I?
B. Where did I come from?
C. Will it rain tomorrow?
D. What is the purpose of my birth?

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: "Who am I? Where did I come from? Where am I going? What is the purpose of my birth?" – these are the basic questions that cannot be answered definitely without the help of wahy.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q13. Which of the following is NOT one of the wrong conclusions, mentioned in the textbook, that people reached when they sought answers to the basic questions without wahy?

A. They said idols are gods
B. They believed in the Hereafter
C. They said humans came from monkeys
D. They said there is no God

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Belief in the Hereafter is a truth taught by wahy. Those who reasoned without wahy reached wrong conclusions such as idol worship, atheism and materialism, which denies the Hereafter.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q14. Under which kind of wahy are the hadiths of the Prophet ﷺ counted?

A. Wahy ghayr matluww
B. Wahy matluww
C. They are not wahy
D. Sense knowledge

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Hadiths are also guidance from Allah, but they were not recited like the Quran. So they are counted as wahy ghayr matluww.

Difficulty: Medium
