### A1. 2016 – Islam I, Q15

Question ID: P2016-I-Q15  
Year: 2016  
Paper and question: Islam I, Q15  
Marks: not stated on the paper (Islam I multiple-choice question)  
Source: OL_2016_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

What gives the correct answer to questions such as "Who am I?" and "Where did I come from?"

- **A.** sense perception
- **B.** reason
- **C.** knowledge from revelation (wahy)
- **D.** scientific knowledge

**Correct answer:** C – knowledge from revelation (wahy) *(option (3) on the original paper)*

**Explanation:** Basic questions such as "Who am I? Where did I come from? Where am I going?" cannot be answered with certainty without the help of revelation.

### A3. 2020 – Islam I, Q15

Question ID: P2020-I-Q15  
Year: 2020  
Paper and question: Islam I, Q15  
Marks: not stated on the paper (Islam I multiple-choice question)  
Source: OL_2020_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

The table below gives the names of some prophets and the ways in which revelation came to them.

| Names of the prophets | Ways in which revelation came |
|---|---|
| (1) Ibrahim (AS) | A - a voice |
| (2) Musa (AS) | B - a sign |
| (3) Zakariyya (AS) | C - a dream |

Which option arranges, in order, the ways revelation came to these prophets?

- **A.** A B C
- **B.** A C B
- **C.** C A B
- **D.** C B A

**Correct answer:** C – C A B *(option (3) on the original paper)*

**Explanation:** Ibrahim (AS) – a true dream (C); Musa (AS) – a voice (A); Zakariyya (AS) – a sign (B). So C A B.

### B1. 2016 – Islam II, Q1 (i)

Question ID: P2016-II-Q1.1  
Year: 2016  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q1 (i)  
Marks: 20 marks for the whole of Q1 (no separate marks given for sub-questions)  
Source: OL_2016_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

Give short answers.
(i) Name the two divisions of wahy.

**Suggested answer:**

1. Wahy matluww (revelation that was recited) – e.g. the Quran
2. Wahy ghayr matluww (revelation that was not recited) – e.g. the hadith

**Answer explanation:** What was recited through Jibril (AS) is matluww; true dreams, a voice, signs and the hadith are ghayr matluww.

**Marking guidance (suggested only – not an official marking scheme):** Correctly naming both divisions.
