# Grade 10 – Chapter 20: The Importance of the Sirah – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_10/Chapter_20/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q15 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. What does the word "sirah" refer to?

A. Commentary on the Quran
B. The science of Islamic law
C. The life history of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ
D. The history of the Companions

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Sirah is the special word for the life history of the Prophet ﷺ; it is studied as a separate field of knowledge.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. Which verse says the Prophet ﷺ was sent to "all of mankind" as a bearer of good news and a warner?

A. 21:107
B. 33:21
C. 34:28
D. 1:1

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Verse 34:28 shows that the Prophet ﷺ is a messenger not for one particular people only, but for the whole of mankind.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q3. Just as Allah is "Rabb al-'Alamin", how is the Prophet ﷺ described (21:107)?

A. Kaffatan lin-Nas
B. Rahmatan lil-'Alamin
C. Rabb an-Nas
D. Khatam an-Nabiyyin

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Allah is the Lord of all the worlds (Rabb al-'Alamin – 1:1); the Prophet ﷺ is a mercy to all the worlds (Rahmatan lil-'Alamin – 21:107).

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q4. Parallel to "Rabb an-Nas" (114:1), how is the Prophet ﷺ described?

A. Rahmatan lil-'Alamin
B. Kaffatan lin-Nas – the Prophet for the whole of mankind
C. Al-Amin
D. As-Sadiq

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Allah is the Lord of mankind (Rabb an-Nas); the Prophet ﷺ is the Prophet for all mankind (Kaffatan lin-Nas – 34:28).

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q5. Which of the following is NOT one of the reasons the Prophet's ﷺ sirah has been preserved?

A. Another messenger came after him and compiled it
B. His history was written and preserved very accurately
C. Books were written in every language
D. He is the final messenger to the world

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: The Prophet ﷺ is the final messenger; no other messenger will come after him. That is exactly why his message had to be preserved.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q6. Who was the first to write the sirah?

A. Muhammad 'Abd al-Malik ibn Hisham
B. Shaykh Muhammad al-Ghazali
C. Imam al-Bukhari
D. Muhammad ibn Ishaq

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Muhammad ibn Ishaq (151 AH) was the first to write it, under the name "Sirat Rasul Allah". That book is not available in later times.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q7. Who wrote the early sirah book that is still available today?

A. Muhammad ibn Ishaq
B. Muhammad 'Abd al-Malik ibn Hisham
C. Sayyid Sulaiman Nadvi
D. Imam Malik

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: The book written by Ibn Hisham (218 AH), quoting Ibn Ishaq's book, is still available. These two books are regarded as the first books of sirah.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q8. In which year did Muhammad ibn Ishaq die?

A. 110 AH
B. 218 AH
C. 151 AH
D. 256 AH

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Ibn Ishaq – 151 AH; Ibn Hisham – 218 AH.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q9. What does the hadith "Do not praise me excessively as 'Isa, son of Maryam, was praised" teach?

A. The Prophet must not be praised at all
B. The sirah must not be written
C. One must not speak about 'Isa (AS)
D. The Prophet must be presented correctly as the servant of Allah and His Messenger, without exaggeration

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: The Prophet ﷺ said, "I am only a servant; say: the servant of Allah and His Messenger." This is why the sirah is written accurately, without exaggeration.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q10. Which verse says the Prophet is closer to the believers than their own selves?

A. 33:6
B. 9:128
C. 7:157
D. 33:21

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Verse 33:6 shows that loving the Prophet ﷺ is connected with iman.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q11. "Indeed, in the Messenger of Allah you have an excellent example" – which verse is this?

A. 33:21
B. 34:28
C. 21:107
D. 3:31

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: 33:21 – the Prophet's ﷺ example is not limited to a particular time, place or race; everyone can follow it.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q12. Who said, "We should study the sirah as a soldier looks to his commander and a student to his teacher"?

A. Sayyid Sulaiman Nadvi
B. Imam ash-Shafi'i
C. Ibn Hisham
D. Shaykh Muhammad al-Ghazali

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Shaykh Muhammad al-Ghazali (1917–1996) said the sirah should be studied with knowledge and feeling together, to build a pure and balanced life.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q13. Who said, "Whether one is poor, a ruler or a victorious soldier – whoever one is, there is an example for everyone in the sirah"?

A. Shaykh Muhammad al-Ghazali
B. Sayyid Sulaiman Nadvi
C. Muhammad ibn Ishaq
D. 'Abd al-Malik ibn Hisham

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: This statement of Sayyid Sulaiman Nadvi shows that the sirah guides every kind of person.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q14. Why is the sirah called "the channel of the basic sources of Islam"?

A. The Quran and the Sunnah reached us through the Prophet ﷺ; the Quran itself became his life and his words
B. The sirah is more important than the Quran
C. The sirah replaces the Sunnah
D. The sirah was created by scholars

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: It was the Prophet ﷺ who gave us the Quran and the Sunnah. The sirah is the practical example of living by them.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q15. What is the next generation, who met the Tabi'un and learned from them, called?

A. The Companions (Sahabah)
B. The Muhajirun
C. The Tabi' al-Tabi'in
D. The Ansar

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Companions → Tabi'un → Tabi' al-Tabi'in. These three generations made extraordinary efforts to preserve the sirah.

Difficulty: Medium
