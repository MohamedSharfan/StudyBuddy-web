## 1. What is the sirah?
> 📖 **Sirah (سيرة):** the special word for **the life history** of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ. On this basis the sirah has been studied and written as **a separate field of knowledge**.

---

## 2. The Prophet ﷺ – a guide for everyone ⭐
> 📖 **(34:28) – meaning:** (O Prophet!) We have sent you to **all of mankind** as a bearer of good news and a warner; but most people do not know.

### Allah and the Prophet – a beautiful comparison

| Allah | The Prophet ﷺ |
|---|---|
| **Rabb al-'Alamin** (ربّ العالمين) – Lord of all the worlds **(1:1)** | **Rahmatan lil-'Alamin** (رحمة للعالمين) – a mercy to all the worlds **(21:107)** |
| **Rabb an-Nas** (ربّ الناس) – Lord of mankind **(114:1)** | **Kaffatan lin-Nas** (كافة للناس) – the Prophet for the whole of mankind **(34:28)** |

👉 This is why his sirah has been studied and preserved for **more than fourteen centuries**.

---

## 3. Why the sirah has been preserved ⭐
1. He is **the final messenger** to the world; no other messenger will come after him → his message **must be preserved.**
2. People who accepted him and shaped their lives by him have existed **from his time until today**.
3. His history has been **written and preserved very accurately**; in every age efforts have been made to protect his biography from distortion.
4. Books about his life have been written **in every language**, from many angles, not only by Muslims but **also by non-Muslims**.

### The role of the Companions
- While the Prophet ﷺ was alive, they observed **very closely** his appearance, movements, teaching, actions, likes and dislikes.
- They passed these on to later generations **with witnesses**.
- They **went from house to house** to get explanations from eyewitnesses, and **carefully examined** those who reported.
- The Companions, the **Tabi'un**, and the **Tabi' al-Tabi'in** after them made extraordinary efforts.
- 👉 So the sirah has been compiled in such a way that **we too can see** the Prophet ﷺ as if we stood near him like the people of his time.

> 📌 **Tabi'i (تابعي)** = one who met the Companions. **Tabi' al-Tabi'in (تبع التابعين)** = the next generation, who met the Tabi'un.

---

## 4. The first books of sirah ⭐

| Author | Died | Book | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Muhammad ibn Ishaq** | **151 AH** | **"Sirat Rasul Allah"** – **the first to write** the sirah | **Not available** in later times |
| **Muhammad 'Abd al-Malik ibn Hisham** | **218 AH** | Written quoting Ibn Ishaq's book | **Still available today** |

👉 These two books are regarded as **the first books of sirah**.

> 📌 The textbook includes a picture of the sirah book "ar-Raheeq al-Makhtum".

## 5. The sirah must not be presented wrongly
The Prophet ﷺ warned his Companions:
- **(Bukhari) – meaning:** "Whoever **deliberately lies** about me, let him take his place **in the Fire**."
- **(Bukhari) – meaning:** "Do not **praise me excessively** as the Christians praised 'Isa, son of Maryam. I am only a servant. So say: **the servant of Allah and His Messenger**."

👉 That is why the sirah has been written **correctly, accurately and without distortion**.

### The Prophet ﷺ – not an ordinary man
- Though a human being, he is **the purest** of human beings; **his past and future sins are forgiven**; he has many special qualities.
- **Loving him is connected with iman.**

| Verse | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **33:6** | The Prophet is **closer to the believers than their own selves** |
| **9:128** | A messenger has come to you from among yourselves; your suffering **grieves him**; he is deeply concerned for your good; he is **kind and merciful** to the believers |
| **7:157** | Those who believe in him, honour him, help him and follow the light (the Book) sent down with him are **the successful** |

## 6. Why we need to study the sirah ⭐

### Shaykh Muhammad al-Ghazali (1917–1996)
> We should study the sirah as a soldier looks to **his commander**, a student to **his teacher**, and followers to **their leader**. **Knowledge and feeling** should come together. We should understand its wisdom and build a life that is **pure, thoughtful and balanced**. *(paraphrase)*

### A model for everyone
> 📖 **(33:21) – meaning:** "Indeed, in the Messenger of Allah you have **an excellent example**."
- This example is not limited to a particular **time, place or race**.

### Sayyid Sulaiman Nadvi
> Whether one is poor or well-off, a ruler or ruled, a learner, a victorious soldier, someone surrounded by suffering or pursued by troubles – **whoever one is**, there is an example for everyone in the sirah. *(paraphrase)*

### Further
- Many people have read the sirah with an open mind, **been drawn to it**, and accepted Islam.
- The sirah is **the channel of the basic sources of Islam**: the Quran and the Sunnah reached us through the Prophet ﷺ; the Quran itself became **his life and his words**.
- In his **Farewell Sermon** he said he was leaving the Quran and the Sunnah → the sirah = **the practical example of the Islamic way of life.**

> 💡 "Others **were shaped by history**; the Prophet ﷺ **shaped history itself**."
