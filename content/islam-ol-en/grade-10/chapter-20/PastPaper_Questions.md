### A1. 2016 – Islam I, Q20

Question ID: P2016-I-Q20  
Year: 2016  
Paper and question: Islam I, Q20  
Marks: not stated on the paper (Islam I multiple-choice question)  
Source: OL_2016_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

The first person to write the biography of the Prophet ﷺ

- **A.** Ibn Ishaq
- **B.** Ibn Hisham
- **C.** Ibn Khaldun
- **D.** Ibn Khallikan

**Correct answer:** A – Ibn Ishaq *(option (1) on the original paper)*

**Explanation:** The first sirah book was written by Ibn Ishaq (151 AH); the book available today is the one by Ibn Hisham (218 AH), which quotes it.

## Part B: Structured and Essay Questions (Islam II)

### B1. 2019 – Islam II, Q6 (ii)

Question ID: P2019-II-Q6.2  
Year: 2019  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q6 (ii)  
Marks: 10 marks for the whole of Q6  
Source: OL_2019_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(ii) Present the reasons why the sirah should be studied.

**Suggested answer:**

1. The Prophet ﷺ is a beautiful example for everyone (33:21) – not limited by time, place or race.
2. The sirah is the channel of the basic sources of Islam (Quran, Sunnah); a practical example of the Islamic way of life.
3. Loving the Prophet is connected with iman (33:6) – the sirah is needed to know him.
4. The sirah has an example for everyone – poor, rich, ruler and ruled (Sayyid Sulayman Nadwi).
5. To build a pure, balanced life where knowledge and feeling meet (Shaykh Muhammad al-Ghazali).
6. Many people have embraced Islam after being drawn by reading the sirah.

**Answer explanation:** It is best to present the reasons with evidence.

### B3. 2024 (2025) – Islam II, Q6 (iii)

Question ID: P2024_2025-II-Q6.2  
Year: 2024 (2025)  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q6 (iii)  
Marks: 10 marks for the whole of Q6  
Source: OL_2024_2025_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(iii) Explain three research efforts made in the early period to preserve 'Sirat Rasul Allah'.

**Suggested answer:**

Any three of the following:
1. The Companions observed very closely, while the Prophet ﷺ was alive, his appearance, movements, teachings, actions, likes and dislikes.
2. They passed them on to later generations with witnesses.
3. Going from house to house to get explanations directly from those who had seen; examining carefully the person who reported.
4. The extraordinary efforts of the Tabi'un and the Tabi' at-Tabi'in.
5. Muhammad ibn Ishaq (151 AH) wrote "Sirat Rasul Allah"; Ibn Hisham (218 AH) wrote his work quoting it.

**Answer explanation:** As the question says "explain", one sentence of explanation is needed for each effort.
