### V1. 2018 – Islam I, Q23

Question ID: P2018-I-Q23  
Year: 2018  
Paper and question: Islam I, Q23  
Marks: not stated on the paper (Islam I multiple-choice question)  
Source: OL_2018_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

What must be recited after the third takbir in the janazah prayer

- **A.** Surah al-Fatihah
- **B.** salawat on the Prophet ﷺ
- **C.** supplication for the deceased
- **D.** saying salam

<details><summary>Original Tamil-medium wording (for traceability)</summary>

ஜனாஸா தொழுகையில் மூன்றாம் தக்பீருக்குப் பின்னர் ஓதவேண்டியது
(1) ஸூரதுல் பாதிஹா

(4) ஸலாம் கூறல்

**Correct answer:** C – supplication for the deceased *(option (3) on the original paper)*

**Explanation:** In the janazah prayer: after the 1st takbir al-Fatihah, after the 2nd takbir salawat, after the 3rd takbir supplication for the deceased, after the 4th takbir du'a and salam.

### V3. 2018 – Islam I, Q30

Question ID: P2018-I-Q30  
Year: 2018  
Paper and question: Islam I, Q30  
Marks: not stated on the paper (Islam I multiple-choice question)

The meaning of 'hayya 'ala as-salah'

- **A.** The prayer has begun.
- **B.** Come quickly to prayer.
- **C.** Come quickly to success.
- **D.** Prayer is better than sleep.

<details><summary>Original Tamil-medium wording (for traceability)</summary>

'ஹய்ய அலஸ் ஸலாஹ்' என்பதன் பொருள்

### V5. 2020 – Islam II, Q1 (vii)

Question ID: P2020-II-Q1.7  
Year: 2020  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q1 (vii)  
Marks: 20 marks for the whole of Q1  
Source: OL_2020_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(vii) Define 'aqiqah' and 'udhiyah'.

<details><summary>Original Tamil-medium wording (for traceability)</summary>

(vii) 'அகீகா', 'உழ்ஹியா' என்பவற்றை வரைவிலக்கணப்படுத்துக.

</details>

**Suggested answer:**

- Aqiqah: an animal slaughtered for Allah in thanks for the birth of a child (usually on the seventh day).
- Udhiyah (qurban): an animal slaughtered to seek nearness to Allah on the day of 'Eid al-Adha and the days of tashriq.

**Answer explanation:** Most Shafi'i scholars consider both to be sunnah mu'akkadah.

**Marking guidance (suggested only – not an official marking scheme):** The definition of both.

**Answer status:** Suggested – not an official answer (no official marking scheme was supplied); based on the Grade 10/11 textbook content

**Verification note:** 'Aqiqah and udhiyah are not explained in the chapters of the current Grade 10/11 textbooks (udhiyah appears only by name as "an act of worship connected with wealth"); the answer is based on general fiqh knowledge.
