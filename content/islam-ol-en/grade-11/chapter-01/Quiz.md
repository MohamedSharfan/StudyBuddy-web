# Grade 11 – Chapter 1: Aqeedah – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_01/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q13 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. What does the term "aqeedah" mean?

A. Believing and accepting firmly without room for any doubt
B. The law
C. Acts of worship
D. Good character

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Aqeedah = firm belief without doubt. The law is "Shari'ah".

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. What is the plural of "aqeedah"?

A. Shari'ah
B. Arkan al-iman
C. Al-'aqa'id
D. Akhlaq

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Aqeedah – singular; al-'aqa'id – plural. In Islamic usage, the beliefs connected with arkan al-iman are called aqeedah.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q3. Which is the correct difference between aqeedah and Shari'ah?

A. Both changed in every age
B. Aqeedah remained the same in every age; the Shari'ah changed with time, place and circumstances
C. The Shari'ah remained the same; aqeedah changed
D. They are the same

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: All the prophets taught the same aqeedah; but the laws (Shari'ah) differed according to the time and the society.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q4. In which surah is the verse that says the Prophet ﷺ was given the same religion enjoined on Nuh, Ibrahim, Musa and 'Isa (AS)?

A. Surah al-Mu'minun (23)
B. Surah al-Ikhlas (112)
C. Surah al-Baqarah (2)
D. Surah ash-Shura (42)

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: 42:13 (ash-Shura) – "Establish the religion and do not be divided in it." The chapter's opening verse, 23:44 (al-Mu'minun), is about messengers being called liars.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q5. Which statement about aqeedah in the time of the Prophet ﷺ and the Companions is correct?

A. It was in a sound state; they believed and lived by it exactly in its original form
B. The Companions gave rational explanations
C. There were many disputes about it
D. Many groups appeared

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: They did not argue, had no doubts and gave no rational explanations; they accepted what the Messenger taught exactly as it was.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q6. Which of the following is NOT a factor that made aqeedah lose its simplicity?

A. Political internal conflicts
B. Following the Quran and the Sunnah exactly
C. Using reason beyond its limits
D. The ideas of outsiders

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Following the Quran and the Sunnah exactly protects aqeedah. Political conflict, outside ideas, excessive reasoning, extremism, desires, plots and superstitions are the factors.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q7. As a result of these factors, how did matters of aqeedah change?

A. They became even simpler
B. They became laws
C. They disappeared completely
D. They became subject to mere philosophical disputes and argumentative debates

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Aqeedah, which should purify the heart, was in danger of coming under philosophical inquiry and becoming a mere subject of research.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q8. Which of the following does the textbook NOT mention as a school of thought contrary to pure Islamic aqeedah?

A. The Khawarij
B. Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah
C. The Murji'ah
D. The Mu'tazilah

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: The Khawarij, Qadariyyah, Jabriyyah, Mu'tazilah, Murji'ah and Shi'ah are the groups the textbook mentions. Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah are those who follow the straight path (next chapter).

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q9. On what basis did the Shi'ah appear?

A. A debate about destiny
B. A political dispute over who was entitled to the caliphate after the Prophet ﷺ
C. A difference of opinion about the method of prayer
D. The laws of trade

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: The Shi'ah, who arose from a political dispute, later grew into a group with a differing aqeedah.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q10. Who said, "It is better that Islam lives and I die, than that I live and Islam dies"?

A. Imam Abu al-Hasan al-Ash'ari
B. Abu Ja'far at-Tahawi
C. Imam Ahmad ibn Hanbal
D. Abu Mansur al-Maturidi

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: This statement of Imam Ahmad (RH), who struggled against the Mu'tazilah, is evidence of the sacrifice he made to protect aqeedah.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q11. Against which ideas did Imam Ahmad ibn Hanbal (RH) struggle?

A. The Mu'tazilah
B. The Jabriyyah
C. The Khawarij
D. The Shi'ah

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: According to the textbook, Imam Ahmad (RH) struggled against the ideas of the Mu'tazilah.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q12. Of the four imams the Grade 11 textbook names as protectors of the purity of aqeedah, which one does NOT appear in the Grade 10 textbook?

A. Imam Ahmad ibn Hanbal
B. Imam Abu al-Hasan al-Ash'ari
C. Abu Ja'far at-Tahawi
D. Abu Mansur al-Maturidi

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: The Grade 10 textbook names three; Grade 11 adds Abu Ja'far at-Tahawi to them.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q13. What does history confirm happens whenever ideas against pure aqeedah appear?

A. Islam will be destroyed
B. Nothing happens
C. Everyone accepts those ideas
D. People arise who protect the ummah and guide it in the shade of Islamic aqeedah

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: History shows that in every age Allah has raised scholars who protect the purity of aqeedah.

Difficulty: Easy
