## Part B: Structured and Essay Questions (Islam II)

### B1. 2017 – Islam II, Q5 (iii)

Question ID: P2017-II-Q5.3  
Year: 2017  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q5 (iii)  
Marks: 10 marks for the whole of Q5  
Source: OL_2017_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(iii) Explain five qualities the communication media should have.

**Suggested answer:**

1. Based on evidence – publishing information only after verifying it (49:6; "it is a lie to pass on everything one hears" – Muslim).
2. Trustworthiness – truth and honesty (amanah); avoiding guesswork, fantasy, lies and mixing truth with falsehood.
3. Impartiality – giving news justly, without bias (4:135).
4. Protecting the public good – freedom of expression without harming the public good; not spreading indecency (24:19).
5. Good manners – civil language; avoiding evil words (4:148; 16:125).

**Answer explanation:** As the question says "explain", one sentence of explanation is needed for each quality.

### B2. 2021 (2022) – Islam II, Q5 (i), (ii), (iii)

Question ID: P2021_2022-II-Q5.1  
Year: 2021 (2022)  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q5 (i), (ii), (iii)  
Marks: 10 marks for the whole of Q5 (division among sub-questions not given)  
Source: OL_2021_2022_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(i) Give the names of two Companions whom the Prophet ﷺ encouraged to compose poetry against the Qurayshi disbelievers.
(ii) State three of the media methods used by the Prophet ﷺ in the early period.
(iii) Briefly describe five qualities of communication media according to Islam.

**Suggested answer:**

(i) Any two of: Hassan ibn Thabit (RA), Ka'b ibn Malik (RA), 'Abdullah ibn Rawahah (RA).

(ii) Any three of the following:
1. Calling in a loud voice from the hill of Safa (26:214)
2. The markets of 'Ukaz, Majannah and Dhul-Majaz
3. The back of a camel, the mosque minbar, the mi'dhanah
4. Khutbahs (Jumu'ah, 'Eid, the sermon at 'Arafah)
5. The platform of the Ashab as-Suffah, the halqah (study circles)
6. Letters and envoys (Diwan al-Insha')

(iii)
1. Based on evidence – investigating news thoroughly (49:6); passing on everything one hears is lying (Muslim).
2. Trustworthiness – truth, honesty (amanah); avoiding guesswork, fantasy and suppression of facts (6:143).
3. Impartiality – being just, without bias (4:135).
4. Protecting the public good – not spreading indecency (24:19).
5. Good manners – civil language (4:148; 16:125).

**Answer explanation:** In (iii) the Banu al-Mustaliq incident (49:6) may be given as an example.

### B5. 2025 (2026) – Islam II, Q7 (ii)

Question ID: P2025_2026-II-Q7.2  
Year: 2025 (2026)  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q7 (ii)  
Marks: 10 marks (short notes on two topics)  
Source: OL_2025_2026_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(ii) Media ethics from the Islamic point of view

**Suggested answer:**

- By the rule that all things are permissible in principle, modern media may be used to spread good ideas and protect moral values.
- The qualities the media should follow:
  1. Based on evidence – investigating thoroughly (49:6 – the Banu al-Mustaliq incident); spreading everything one hears is lying (Muslim).
  2. Trustworthiness – truth, honesty (amanah); avoiding guesswork, fantasy and suppression of facts (6:143).
  3. Impartiality – justice without bias (4:135).
  4. The public good – not spreading indecency (24:19).
  5. Good manners – civil language (4:148; 16:125).
- What Islam expects: a free media that protects justice, freedom and human rights; unity.

**Answer explanation:** The qualities of the media were also asked in 2021 (2022) and 2022 (2023).
