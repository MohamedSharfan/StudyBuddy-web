# Grade 11 – Chapter 31: Islam and the Media – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_31/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q20 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. What did the Prophet ﷺ do when the verse "And warn your nearest relatives" was revealed?

A. He went inside the Ka'bah and gave a speech
B. He climbed the hill of Safa, called the Quraysh in a loud voice and introduced Islam
C. He wrote letters
D. He made the hijrah

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: When 26 Ash-Shu'ara': 214 was revealed, he used a custom of the Jahiliyyah – but with dignity – and called out from Safa.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. Into which three groups are the media classified today?

A. Print, electronic and social media
B. Radio, television and film
C. Letter, telegram and telephone
D. Speech, writing and gesture

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Today's three kinds of media developed from early forms such as gesture, facial expression, speech and writing.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q3. Which verse says, "(O Prophet!) Strive against them with it (the Quran) a great striving"?

A. 26 Ash-Shu'ara': 214
B. 31 Luqman: 6
C. 6 Al-An'am: 153
D. 25 Al-Furqan: 52

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: The Prophet ﷺ used the Quran itself as his main medium – his weapon.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q4. Who bought a singing-girl to turn away those who wished to embrace Islam?

A. Abu Jahl
B. Abu Lahab
C. An-Nadr ibn al-Harith
D. Umayyah ibn Khalaf

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: In this situation 31 Luqman: 6 was revealed – about those who buy idle talk to lead people astray.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q5. Which of these Companions was NOT among those the Prophet ﷺ told to answer the disbelievers' insulting poems in verse?

A. Hassan ibn Thabit (RA)
B. Ka'b ibn Malik (RA)
C. 'Abdullah ibn Rawahah (RA)
D. Mus'ab ibn 'Umayr (RA)

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Mus'ab (RA) was the representative sent to Madinah.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q6. Which of these is one of the markets the Prophet ﷺ used as a medium?

A. 'Ukaz
B. Kufa
C. Basra
D. Ta'if

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: 'Ukaz, Majannah and Dhul-Majaz – the markets of that time.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q7. After drawing a straight line on the ground with lines on both sides of it, which verse did the Prophet ﷺ recite?

A. 2 Al-Baqarah: 201
B. 6 Al-An'am: 153
C. 49 Al-Hujurat: 6
D. 16 An-Nahl: 125

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: "This is My straight path, so follow it, and do not follow other paths" – on the side lines the devil calls.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q8. What did the Prophet ﷺ teach by showing the body of a dead lamb with cut ears (Muslim)?

A. Sheep must be treated kindly
B. One must not cheat in trade
C. This world is even more worthless to Allah than this thing is to you
D. One must eat halal food

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: An example from the Sunnah of using an object as a teaching aid.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q9. About whom did the Prophet ﷺ speak while joining his index and middle fingers (Bukhari)?

A. The one who cares for an orphan
B. The one who looks after his parents
C. The one who looks after his neighbours
D. The one who has memorised the Quran

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: "I and the one who cares for an orphan will be like this in Paradise."

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q10. Which platform in Masjid an-Nabawi was used as a communication centre?

A. The minbar
B. The mi'dhanah
C. The halqah
D. The platform of Ashab as-Suffah

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: The platform of Ashab as-Suffah – a communication centre; halqah – study circles.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q11. What was the name of the records department created by the Prophet ﷺ?

A. Diwan al-Barid
B. Diwan al-Insha'
C. Diwan al-Khatam
D. Diwan al-Barid wal-Akhbar

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Diwan al-Insha' – envoys and scribes were appointed to carry the message and for written communication.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q12. Who carried the Prophet's ﷺ letter to the Muqawqis, ruler of Egypt?

A. 'Amr ibn Umayyah (RA)
B. Dihyah al-Kalbi (RA)
C. Hatib ibn Abi Balta'ah (RA)
D. 'Abdullah ibn Hudhafah (RA)

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: The Najashi – 'Amr ibn Umayyah; Heraclius – Dihyah; Kisra – 'Abdullah ibn Hudhafah.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q13. Which postal department did 'Umar (RA) create, following the example of the Prophet's ﷺ letters?

A. Diwan al-Barid
B. Diwan al-Insha'
C. Diwan ar-Rasa'il
D. Diwan al-Katib

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Under the Umayyads it developed into "Diwan al-Barid wal-Akhbar"; pigeons and postal carriages were used.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q14. Who was appointed to gather news of the Quraysh's movements secretly while the Prophet ﷺ stayed in the cave of Thawr?

A. al-'Abbas (RA)
B. 'Ali (RA)
C. Khalid ibn al-Walid (RA)
D. 'Abdullah ibn Abi Bakr (RA)

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: During the three days in the cave of Thawr, 'Abdullah ibn Abi Bakr (RA) gathered the news.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q15. Which bird does the Quran say Sulayman (AS) used to learn news of distant lands?

A. Pigeon
B. Crow
C. Hoopoe (hudhud)
D. Eagle

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: The hoopoe brought the news about Bilqis, the queen of Saba' – evidence of awareness of what is happening around.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q16. "It is enough of a lie for a person to pass on everything he hears (without checking)" – this relates to which quality of the media?

A. Based on evidence (verified)
B. Good manners
C. Protecting the public good
D. Impartiality

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Information must not be published without first being verified (Muslim).

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q17. Which verse was revealed in connection with the report that the Banu al-Mustaliq had left Islam?

A. 24 An-Nur: 19
B. 4 An-Nisa': 135
C. 27 An-Naml: 64
D. 49 Al-Hujurat: 6

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: If a wicked person brings you news, investigate it; the Prophet ﷺ checked through Khalid ibn al-Walid (RA) and learned they had not left Islam.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q18. "O believers! Stand firm for justice; bear true witness even if it is against yourselves, your parents or your close relatives" – this verse is evidence for which quality of the media?

A. Trustworthiness
B. Impartiality
C. Protecting the public good
D. Good manners

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: 4 An-Nisa': 135 – journalism must not fall victim to prejudice or bias.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q19. Which verse says that those who love to spread indecency among the believers will have punishment in both worlds?

A. 24 An-Nur: 19
B. 4 An-Nisa': 148
C. 16 An-Nahl: 125
D. 6 Al-An'am: 143

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Protecting the public good – exposing sexual misconduct freely may tempt the weak.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q20. Which legal maxim is the basis for Islam permitting the use of modern media?

A. Necessity makes the forbidden permissible
B. In principle, all things are permissible
C. Avoid what is doubtful
D. Harm must be removed

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Modern media may be used to spread good ideas, to develop social culture and to protect moral values.

Difficulty: Hard
