# Grade 11 – Chapter 31: Islam and the Media

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_31/Notes.md` (Tamil). English rendering.

## 🎯 What you will learn

- The power and kinds of today's media
- The Quran and Sunnah – the oldest means of communication
- The public and indirect methods of communication the Prophet ﷺ used
- Letters, envoys and diwans (departments)

- The 5 qualities the media should follow

## 📖 Opening verse

> وَأَنذِرْ عَشِيرَتَكَ الْأَقْرَبِينَ

**Meaning:** And warn your nearest relatives. **(26 Ash-Shu'ara': 214)**

---

## 1️⃣ Today's media

- A powerful and influential force; it enters homes without permission.
- It plays a huge part in informing people and stirring their feelings; it has changed news, business, entertainment and consumption.
- Early communication: gestures, facial expressions, sound signals, signs, speech, writing.
- ⭐ Today's kinds: **print media, electronic media, social media**.
- Many benefits and many harms: it drives people against tradition, religion and culture; it leads to crime; it creates **addiction (domination)** in everyone from children to the elderly.
- More than a superpower's navy, air force or army, it is media power that decides a country's success or failure.

---

## 2️⃣ The Quran – the finest medium ⭐

- ⭐ **The Quran and Sunnah**, the basic sources of Islam, are **the oldest means of communication**, guiding the whole of human society.
- The Prophet ﷺ used the Quran itself as his **main medium – his weapon**.
- **25 Al-Furqan: 52** – (O Prophet!) Strive against them with it (the Quran) **a great striving**.
- Even great poets fell silent before its style.

### The enemies' counter-media

- The disbelievers campaigned: "Do not listen to this Quran; busy yourselves with other things."
- ⭐ **An-Nadr ibn al-Harith** bought a singing-girl and turned away those who wished to embrace Islam by giving them food, wine and songs.
- The verse revealed about this: **31 Luqman: 6** – those who buy idle talk to lead people astray from the way of Allah without knowledge.

### Counter-propaganda in poetry

- When the disbelievers composed insulting poems, the Prophet ﷺ told **Hassan ibn Thabit, Ka'b ibn Malik and 'Abdullah ibn Rawahah** (RA) to answer them in verse.

## 3️⃣ Public methods of communication used by the Prophet ﷺ

| Method | Explanation |
|---|---|
| ⭐ **The hill of Safa** | When 26:214 was revealed, he climbed Safa, called the Quraysh in a loud voice and introduced Islam (a custom of the Jahiliyyah; but done with dignity, without removing his clothes) |
| Markets | **'Ukaz, Majannah, Dhul-Majaz** |
| Others | The back of a camel, the mosque minbar, the **mi'dhanah** (the place where the adhan is called) |
| Khutbahs | Jumu'ah, 'Eid, the eclipse prayer, the prayer for rain, the nikah khutbah; **the sermon at 'Arafah** during Hajj |
| The platform of **Ashab as-Suffah** | One side of Masjid an-Nabawi – a communication centre |
| **Halqah** | Study circles |
| Relayed takbir | When large crowds gathered for congregational prayer, the takbir was relayed from person to person |

💡 Lesson: a Muslim may use methods of communication developed by others, **within the limits of the Shari'ah**.

### Unusual teaching aids

| Incident | What it taught |
|---|---|

| ⭐ The body of a dead lamb with cut ears (in the market) | However worthless this is to you, **this world is even more worthless to Allah** (Muslim) |
| Joining the index and middle fingers | I and **the one who cares for an orphan** will be like this in Paradise (Bukhari) |

## 4️⃣ Indirect communication – letters and envoys

- ⭐ **Diwan al-Insha'** (ديوان الإنشاء) – the **records department** created by the Prophet ﷺ; envoys and scribes were appointed.

### Representatives

| Place | Who was sent |
|---|---|
| Abyssinia (year 5 of prophethood) | A delegation led by **Ja'far ibn Abi Talib** (RA) |
| Madinah (year 11 of prophethood) | **Mus'ab ibn 'Umayr** (RA) |
| Yemen (Madinan period) | **Mu'adh ibn Jabal** (RA) |

- ⚠️ The textbook says Mus'ab (RA) was sent to Madinah in "year 11 of prophethood"; this is usually said to be after the First Pledge of 'Aqabah (year 12 of prophethood) – confirm with your teacher.

### Letters to rulers ⭐

| Ruler / country | Carried by |
|---|---|

| The Muqawqis – Egypt | Hatib ibn Abi Balta'ah (RA) |

## 5️⃣ Awareness of information

- During the hijrah, **three days in the cave of Thawr**; ⭐ **'Abdullah ibn Abi Bakr** (RA) was appointed to gather news of the Quraysh's movements.
- A letter to **al-'Abbas (RA)** asking him to delay coming to Madinah, so he could report on the polytheists' activities.
- **Examining camel dung** to learn about the approach of the enemy army.
- He sent the first hijrah group only after learning about the king of Abyssinia, his religion, the people's character and his foreign policy.
- ⭐ The Quran: **Sulayman (AS)** used **the hoopoe (hudhud)** to learn news of distant lands; the news about **Bilqis, the queen of Saba' (Sheba)**.

---

## 6️⃣ The Islamic view of the media

- ⭐ Legal maxim: "**In principle, all things are permissible**" → it is permitted to use modern media to spread good ideas, to develop social culture and to protect moral values.

### The 5 qualities the media should follow ⭐

| # | Quality | Evidence / explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Based on evidence (verified)** | It is enough of a lie for a person to pass on everything he hears (without checking) (Muslim); **49 Al-Hujurat: 6** – if a wicked person brings you news, investigate it; **27 An-Naml: 64** – bring your proof if you are truthful |
| 2 | **Trustworthiness** | Truth and amanah (honesty); avoiding guesswork, fantasy, illusion, suppression of facts, lies and mixing truth with falsehood; **6 Al-An'am: 143** – inform me with knowledge |
| 3 | **Impartiality** | Not falling victim to social prejudice or bias; **4 An-Nisa': 135** – bear witness justly even if it is against your parents or relatives |
| 4 | **Protecting the public good** | Freedom of expression must not harm the public good; exposing sexual misconduct can itself become an incitement; **24 An-Nur: 19** – those who love to spread indecency will have punishment in both worlds |
| 5 | **Good manners (decency)** | Civil language; **4 An-Nisa': 148** – Allah does not like evil words spoken aloud; **16 An-Nahl: 125** – argue in the best way |

### The Banu al-Mustaliq incident ⭐

- A man reported that the Banu al-Mustaliq tribe had left Islam → 49:6 was revealed.
- The Prophet ﷺ sent **Khalid ibn al-Walid** (RA) to check; when he observed them at night through scouts, it was confirmed that they had not left Islam.

### What Islam expects

A free media – protecting justice, freedom and human rights; upholding moral values at personal, family, national and international level; unity; a prosperous future.
