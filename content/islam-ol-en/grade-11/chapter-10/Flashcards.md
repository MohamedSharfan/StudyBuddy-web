# Grade 11 – Chapter 10: The Holy Places of Islam – Flashcards

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_10/Flashcards.md` (Tamil), cards 1–17 in the same order.

---

Flashcard 1

Front: What are the three holy places of Islam?

Back: The Ka'bah (al-Masjid al-Haram), al-Masjid an-Nabawi, al-Masjid al-Aqsa.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 10 | Topic: The three holy places

---

Flashcard 2

Front: The virtue of prayer in the three mosques (as given in the textbook)?

Back: Al-Masjid al-Haram – 100,000; al-Masjid an-Nabawi – 1,000; al-Masjid al-Aqsa – 500.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 10 | Topic: The three holy places

---

Flashcard 3

Front: بكة (Bakkah)

Back: Another name for Makkah – it appears in verse 3:96.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 10 | Topic: The Ka'bah

---

Flashcard 4

Front: Who took Makkah in 440 CE?

Back: Qusayy, of the line of 'Adnan – from then the leadership of Makkah passed to the Quraysh.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 10 | Topic: The Ka'bah

---

Flashcard 5

Front: الجرهم (Jurhum)

Back: The tribe that completely filled in the well of Zamzam before leaving; 'Abd al-Muttalib rediscovered it.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 10 | Topic: The Ka'bah

---

Flashcard 6

Front: By whose hand was the Black Stone set in place?

Back: By the Prophet's ﷺ hand – in the rebuilding 30 years after the Year of the Elephant.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 10 | Topic: The Ka'bah

---

Flashcard 7

Front: When was the conquest of Makkah?

Back: 8 AH.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 10 | Topic: The Ka'bah

---

Flashcard 8

Front: الإسراء (Al-Isra')

Back: The Prophet's night journey from Makkah to al-Masjid al-Aqsa.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 10 | Topic: Al-Aqsa

---

Flashcard 9

Front: المعراج (Al-Mi'raj)

Back: The journey from al-Aqsa to the heavens (621 CE) – the prayer was given as a gift.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 10 | Topic: Al-Aqsa

---

Flashcard 10

Front: سدرة المنتهى (Sidrat al-Muntaha)

Back: The Lote Tree of the Boundary in the heavens – where the meeting with Allah took place during the Mi'raj.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 10 | Topic: Al-Aqsa

---

Flashcard 11

Front: When was Palestine conquered? Who took charge?

Back: 636 CE (15 AH); 'Umar (RA) – "the Covenant of 'Umar".

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 10 | Topic: Al-Aqsa

---

Flashcard 12

Front: When were the Crusades?

Back: 1088 – 1290 CE (as given in the textbook).

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 10 | Topic: Al-Aqsa

---

Flashcard 13

Front: 1924, 1948, 1967 in the history of al-Aqsa?

Back: 1924 – the fall of the caliphate; 1948 – Israel created; 1967 – al-Aqsa set on fire and Nur ad-Din Zangi's minbar burnt (textbook date; usually given as 1969).

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 10 | Topic: Al-Aqsa

---

Flashcard 14

Front: In whose house did the Prophet ﷺ stay when building al-Masjid an-Nabawi began?

Back: The house of Abu Ayyub al-Ansari (RA) (he arrived in Madinah on 27 September 622 CE).

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 10 | Topic: Al-Masjid an-Nabawi

---

Flashcard 15

Front: أصحاب الصفة (Ashab as-Suffah)

Back: Poor Companions without homes who stayed on the bench (suffah) of al-Masjid an-Nabawi.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 10 | Topic: Al-Masjid an-Nabawi

---

Flashcard 16

Front: Six roles of al-Masjid an-Nabawi in the Prophet's ﷺ time?

Back: School, court, parliament, treasury, military camp, reception hall.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 10 | Topic: Al-Masjid an-Nabawi

---

Flashcard 17

Front: The original size of al-Masjid an-Nabawi, and its size after Khaybar?

Back: Originally 35 m × 30 m with 3 doors; after Khaybar – 2,500 m².

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 10 | Topic: Al-Masjid an-Nabawi
