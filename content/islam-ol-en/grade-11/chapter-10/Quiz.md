# Grade 11 – Chapter 10: The Holy Places of Islam – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_10/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q20 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. According to the hadith in Bukhari, which are the three mosques to which journeys (for worship) may be made?

A. Al-Masjid al-Haram, al-Masjid an-Nabawi, al-Masjid al-Aqsa
B. Al-Masjid al-Haram, Masjid Quba', al-Masjid an-Nabawi
C. Al-Masjid al-Aqsa, Masjid al-Qiblatayn, al-Masjid al-Haram
D. Al-Masjid an-Nabawi, Masjid Quba', al-Masjid al-Aqsa

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: "La tushaddu ar-rihalu illa ila thalathati masajid" – journeys may be made only to these three.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. One prayer in al-Masjid an-Nabawi is equal to how many prayers?

A. One hundred thousand
B. Five hundred
C. One thousand
D. One hundred

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Al-Masjid al-Haram – 100,000; al-Masjid an-Nabawi – 1,000; al-Masjid al-Aqsa – 500 (as given in the textbook).

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q3. Which verse says, "The first house set up for mankind is the one at Bakkah"?

A. 2:144
B. 2:125
C. 3:96
D. 9:108

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Verse 3:96 shows that the Ka'bah is the first house of God built in the world; "Bakkah" = Makkah.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q4. What happened to the Ka'bah in the time of Nuh (AS)?

A. It was enlarged
B. It was burnt
C. It was destroyed by the flood, and the site later rose like a sand mound
D. It was made the qiblah

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Ibrahim (AS), together with Isma'il, rebuilt the Ka'bah that had been destroyed by the flood in Nuh's (AS) time.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q5. On which visit to Makkah did Ibrahim (AS) build the Ka'bah with his son?

A. The first
B. The second
C. The third
D. The fourth

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Ibrahim (AS) came to Makkah four times; the Ka'bah was built on the fourth visit.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q6. Which verse tells us to take as a place of prayer the place where Ibrahim's (AS) footprint can still be seen on the stone he stood on while raising the Ka'bah's walls?

A. 2:126
B. 2:125
C. 3:96
D. 2:144

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: 2:125 – take Maqam Ibrahim as a place of prayer. 2:126 – Ibrahim's (AS) prayer for Makkah.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q7. Who rediscovered the well of Zamzam, which the Jurhum tribe had filled in before leaving?

A. Qusayy
B. Abu Talib
C. Hashim
D. 'Abd al-Muttalib

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: 'Abd al-Muttalib, of the line of Hashim, took charge of the Ka'bah and rediscovered Zamzam; the elephant army event happened in his time.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q8. Who set the Black Stone in its place when the Ka'bah was rebuilt?

A. The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ (after being appointed arbiter)
B. 'Abd al-Muttalib
C. Abu Bakr (RA)
D. Qusayy

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: In the rebuilding 30 years after the Year of the Elephant, when a dispute arose among the Quraysh, it was placed by the hand of the Prophet ﷺ, who had been appointed arbiter.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q9. Which verse tells of the qiblah being changed towards al-Masjid al-Haram?

A. 17:1
B. 2:177
C. 3:96
D. 2:144

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: 2:144 – "We see you turning your face towards the sky… so turn your face towards al-Masjid al-Haram."

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q10. According to the hadith in Bukhari narrated by Abu Dharr (RA), how much time was there between al-Masjid al-Haram and al-Masjid al-Aqsa?

A. Forty years
B. One hundred years
C. One thousand years
D. Ten years

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: The first mosque built on earth was al-Masjid al-Haram; the next was al-Masjid al-Aqsa; the gap was forty years.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q11. Which prophet recovered the lost al-Masjid al-Aqsa, and which prophet rebuilt and enlarged it splendidly?

A. Dawud (AS), Sulayman (AS)
B. Musa (AS), Harun (AS)
C. Ishaq (AS), Ya'qub (AS)
D. Zakariyya (AS), Yahya (AS)

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Many from Musa (AS) onwards tried but could not; Dawud (AS) recovered it; Sulayman (AS) rebuilt and enlarged it.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q12. What is the Prophet's ﷺ night journey from al-Masjid al-Haram to al-Masjid al-Aqsa called?

A. Mi'raj
B. Hijrah
C. Isra'
D. 'Umrah

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Makkah → al-Aqsa = Isra'; from al-Aqsa to the heavens (Sidrat al-Muntaha) = Mi'raj.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q13. After the conquest of Palestine, under what name is the agreement made with the Christians on taking charge of Jerusalem still preserved?

A. The Charter of Madinah
B. The Covenant of 'Umar
C. The Treaty of Hudaybiyyah
D. The Magna Carta

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: The agreement 'Umar (RA) made with the Christians in 636 CE (15 AH) is "the Covenant of 'Umar".

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q14. After the Prophet ﷺ, who was the first to give the adhan at al-Masjid al-Aqsa?

A. 'Umar (RA)
B. 'Ali (RA)
C. Abu 'Ubaydah (RA)
D. Bilal (RA)

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: The first person to pray there after the Prophet was 'Umar (RA); the first to give the adhan was Bilal (RA).

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q15. Which leaders are named as recovering al-Aqsa and Palestine from the Crusaders?

A. Tariq ibn Ziyad, Muhammad ibn al-Qasim
B. Salah ad-Din al-Ayyubi, Mahmud al-Qutuz
C. Muhammad al-Fatih, 'Umar ibn 'Abd al-'Aziz
D. Khalid ibn al-Walid, 'Amr ibn al-'As

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: In the Crusades of 1088–1290 CE, Salah ad-Din al-Ayyubi and Mahmud al-Qutuz, rulers of Egypt, recovered al-Aqsa (as given in the textbook).

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q16. In 1967, when Israelis set fire to al-Masjid al-Aqsa, whose historic minbar was burnt?

A. Salah ad-Din al-Ayyubi's
B. 'Umar's (RA)
C. Sultan Nur ad-Din Zangi's
D. King Fahd's

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: According to the textbook, Sultan Nur ad-Din Zangi's minbar was burnt. (Historical sources usually date this arson to 1969 – see the Notes.)

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q17. How was the site of al-Masjid an-Nabawi chosen?

A. The place where the Prophet's ﷺ camel first knelt; bought from two orphans
B. By a vote of the people
C. Where Abu Ayyub's (RA) house stood
D. State land given free

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: The land belonged to two orphans; the Prophet ﷺ bought it and built the mosque, working alongside his Companions.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q18. What were the poor Companions without homes, who stayed in the part of al-Masjid an-Nabawi set aside for them, called?

A. The Ansar
B. The Ahl al-Bayt
C. The Muhajirun
D. Ashab as-Suffah (the people of the bench)

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: The poor Companions who stayed in one part of al-Masjid an-Nabawi were called Ashab as-Suffah.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q19. Which was NOT a role of al-Masjid an-Nabawi in the Prophet's ﷺ time?

A. A school
B. A trading market
C. A military camp
D. A court

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Al-Masjid an-Nabawi served as a place of prayer, school, court, parliament, treasury, military camp and reception hall – but not as a market.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q20. What was the area of al-Masjid an-Nabawi after it was enlarged following the Battle of Khaybar?

A. 1,050 square metres
B. 2,500 square metres
C. 5,000 square metres
D. 35 × 30 metres

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: At first 35 m long and 30 m wide; after Khaybar, 15 m was added to the length and 20 m to the width, making 2,500 m².

Difficulty: Hard
