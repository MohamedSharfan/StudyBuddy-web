# Grade 11 – Chapter 10: The Holy Places of Islam

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_10/Notes.md` (Tamil). English rendering.

## 🎯 What you will learn
- The three holy places and their spiritual status
- The Ka'bah – its history
- Al-Masjid al-Aqsa – its history
- Al-Masjid an-Nabawi – its history and role

> 📖 **Opening hadith of the chapter (Bukhari):** لَا تُشَدُّ الرِّحَالُ إِلَّا إِلَى ثَلَاثَةِ مَسَاجِدَ: مَسْجِدِيْ هٰذَا، وَالْمَسْجِدُ الْحَرَامُ، وَالْمَسْجِدُ الْأَقْصَى
> **Meaning:** "Journeys (for worship) may be made **only to three mosques**: this mosque of mine (al-Masjid an-Nabawi), al-Masjid al-Haram and al-Masjid al-Aqsa."

---

## 1. The three holy places ⭐
- Mosques are the holy places of Islam; **three of them are the most important:** **the Ka'bah (al-Masjid al-Haram), al-Masjid al-Aqsa and al-Masjid an-Nabawi.**
- They are bound up with **the life of the Muslim ummah**.
- They take precedence over other mosques because the Prophet ﷺ named **only these three** as places of pilgrimage.

### Spiritual status (Bukhari*) ⭐
| Mosque | One prayer = |
|---|---|
| **Al-Masjid al-Haram** | **100,000** prayers |
| **Al-Masjid an-Nabawi** | **1,000** prayers |
| **Al-Masjid al-Aqsa** | **500** prayers |

> ⚠️ *The textbook gives Bukhari as the source for all three figures. The narration in Bukhari mentions the 1,000 for the Prophet's Mosque; the 100,000 and 500 figures are reported in other collections – confirm with the teacher.

---

## 2. The Ka'bah (كعبة الله) ⭐
- The house of God with **the first rank** in Islam; it is also **the first** house of God **built** in the world.
> 📖 **(3:96) – meaning:** The first house set up for mankind is the one at **Bakkah (Makkah)** – full of blessing and guidance for the worlds.

### Historical timeline
| Time | Event |
|---|---|
| The beginning | **Adam (AS)** built it first (historical reports); there is also a common belief that **the angels** built it before him |
| The time of Nuh (AS) | It was **destroyed by the flood** and the site rose like a sand mound |
| Ibrahim (AS) | By Allah's command he brought his wife **Hajar** and his son **Isma'il** from Palestine and left them alone → Allah gave the never-failing spring of **Zamzam** → people settled there |
| Ibrahim's (AS) 4th visit | Ibrahim (AS) came to Makkah **four times**; on the **fourth**, **father and son together** rebuilt the Ka'bah |
| | Ibrahim (AS) prayed for Makkah and its people **(2:126)** |
| | He raised the walls **standing on a stone**; **the footprint** pressed into it can still be seen today; Allah tells us to take that place as a place of prayer **(2:125)** – **Maqam Ibrahim** |
| **440 CE** | **Qusayy**, of the line of **'Adnan**, a descendant of Isma'il (AS), took Makkah → leadership passed to **the Quraysh** |
| Later | **'Abd al-Muttalib**, of the line of Hashim – administration of the Ka'bah; he rediscovered the **well of Zamzam**, which the **Jurhum** tribe **had completely filled in** before leaving; in his time came **the event of Abrahah's elephant army** |
| **30 years after** the Year of the Elephant | The Ka'bah was **rebuilt**; in the Quraysh dispute over who would set **the Black Stone** in place, it was placed **by the hand of the Prophet ﷺ**, who was appointed as arbiter |
| After the hijrah | The Prophet ﷺ and the Companions prayed facing **al-Masjid al-Aqsa**; the Prophet longed to face the Ka'bah → **the change of qiblah (2:144)** |
| **8 AH** | **The conquest of Makkah** – the Prophet ﷺ established **tawhid** there |

👉 Since the time of Ibrahim (AS) it has been **the symbol of tawhid**; today it is **the site of the duty of hajj** and **the heart of the Muslim ummah.**

> 📖 **(2:144) – meaning:** (O Prophet!) We see you turning your face towards the sky; We will turn you to a qiblah that pleases you; so turn your face **towards al-Masjid al-Haram**.

## 3. Al-Masjid al-Aqsa (المسجد الأقصى) ⭐
- The Muslims' holy place **in Palestine**; its history is as old as the Ka'bah's.
- Most hold that **Adam (AS)** built it first and it fell into ruin; later **Ibrahim (AS)** rebuilt it.

> 📖 **Hadith (Bukhari) – Abu Dharr al-Ghifari (RA) – meaning:** "Which mosque was built first on earth?" – "**Al-Masjid al-Haram**." "Then which?" – "**Al-Masjid al-Aqsa**." "How long between them?" – "**Forty years**."

- Allah made al-Aqsa and the lands around it **holy land**; in history these lands are part of **"Sham"**.
- The reason for its virtue: **many prophets lived and died there.**

### Historical timeline
| Time | Event |
|---|---|
| After Ibrahim (AS) | **Ishaq (AS) and Ya'qub (AS)** looked after it |
| The time of Yusuf (AS) | As a result of his brothers' treachery, Ya'qub's (AS) family settled **in Egypt** → they **lost** al-Aqsa |
| | Many prophets from **Musa (AS)** onwards tried to recover it but could not |
| | **Dawud (AS)** recovered it; **Sulayman (AS)** **rebuilt and enlarged** it splendidly |
| | Then many prophets taught there until **'Isa (AS)** came |
| **621 CE** | **The Mi'raj** – the first stage was from Makkah to al-Aqsa; there he **led the earlier prophets in prayer**; then the meeting with Allah at **Sidrat al-Muntaha**; he received **the prayer** as a gift; al-Aqsa was **the (first) qiblah** |
| | The journey from al-Masjid al-Haram to al-Aqsa = **the Isra'**; the first verse of Surah al-Isra' (Bani Isra'il) mentions it. Some scholars say the Isra' took place to confirm that Palestine, the land of the prophets, now belonged to Muhammad ﷺ |
| **632 CE** | The Prophet ﷺ died; threats from the Romans and Persians; the armies sent by Abu Bakr (RA) won victories in 'Umar's (RA) time |
| **636 CE (15 AH)** | **The conquest of Palestine**; **'Umar (RA)** was invited to take charge of Jerusalem (al-Quds); an agreement with the Christians – known to this day as **"the Covenant of 'Umar"** |
| | 'Umar (RA) cleaned a filthy area **with his own hands** and prayed **two rak'ahs** – the **first person** to pray there after the Prophet; the first to give the adhan was **Bilal (RA)**; it was built to hold **three thousand** worshippers |
| **1088–1290 CE** | **The Crusades**; leaders who recovered it – **Salah ad-Din al-Ayyubi and Mahmud al-Qutuz*** (rulers of Egypt) |
| **1924** | The fall of the Islamic **caliphate**; the Arab lands were divided |
| **1948** | **Israel** was created in Palestine |
| **1967*** | Israelis **set fire** to al-Aqsa; the historic **minbar of Sultan Nur ad-Din Zangi** was burnt |

> ⚠️ *Dates and names as in the textbook. Historical sources usually date the arson that destroyed Nur ad-Din's minbar to **1969**, and know the Mamluk sultan as **Sayf ad-Din Qutuz** (famous for defeating the Mongols at 'Ayn Jalut) – confirm with the teacher.

> 📌 According to the textbook: the battle between the Muslims and **the Dajjal** – one of the major signs of the Last Day, when **'Isa (AS)** will descend and kill the Dajjal – will take place in **the holy land** where al-Aqsa stands.

## 4. Al-Masjid an-Nabawi (المسجد النبوي) ⭐
- It holds **the second** spiritual rank in Islam.
- Its history begins **with the Prophet's ﷺ hijrah**.
- Having reached Madinah on **27 September 622 CE**, the Prophet ﷺ began the building work while staying at the house of **Abu Ayyub (al-Ansari) (RA)**.

### How it was built
- **The place where the Prophet's ﷺ camel first knelt** was chosen; it belonged to **two orphans** and was **bought** from them.
- The Prophet ﷺ **worked hard himself alongside his Companions.**
- Both sides of the entrance were of **stone**; the walls of **stone and mud**; **pillars of palm trunks**; a **roof of palm fronds.**
- At first it was **35 m long and 30 m wide, with three doors.**
- On one side were **rooms for the Prophet's wives**; the Prophet ﷺ moved into them from Abu Ayyub's house.
- One part was for **poor Companions who had no homes** → **Ashab as-Suffah (the people of the bench).**

> 📖 **(9:108) – meaning:** A mosque founded on God-consciousness from the first day is more worthy for you to stand in (to pray); in it are men who love to purify themselves; and Allah loves those who purify themselves.

### The role of al-Masjid an-Nabawi in the Prophet's ﷺ time ⭐
Not only a place of prayer, but also:
- **A school** (education)
- **A court** (judging disputes)
- **A parliament** (consultation on state affairs)
- **A treasury** (storing and distributing zakat and taxes)
- **A military camp** (planning, training)
- **A reception hall** (guests, envoys)

### Expansion
- After **the Battle of Khaybar**: **15 m** was added to its length and **20 m** to its width, making **2,500 square metres.**
- Later **'Umar and 'Uthman (RA)**, the **Umayyad, Abbasid, Mamluk and Ottoman** rulers, and finally **King Fahd ibn 'Abd al-'Aziz of Saudi Arabia** enlarged it → today it is one of **the largest mosques in the world**.
