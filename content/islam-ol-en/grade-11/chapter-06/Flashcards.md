# Grade 11 – Chapter 6: Al-Qada' wal-Qadar – Flashcards

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_06/Flashcards.md` (Tamil), cards 1–13 in the same order.

---

Flashcard 1

Front: القضاء (Al-qada')

Back: Literally – creating, decreeing, carrying out.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 6 | Topic: Key terms

---

Flashcard 2

Front: القدر (Al-qadar)

Back: Literally – determining, pre-arranging, measuring out.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 6 | Topic: Key terms

---

Flashcard 3

Front: العلم ('Ilm) / القدرة (Qudrah) / الإرادة (Iradah)

Back: 'Ilm = knowledge; qudrah = power; iradah = will.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 6 | Topic: Key terms

---

Flashcard 4

Front: Four groups that arose by relying on reason alone in the matter of qadar?

Back: The Jabriyyah, Qadariyyah, Mu'tazilah, Murji'ah.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 6 | Topic: Revelation and reason

---

Flashcard 5

Front: What are the two wrong attitudes towards qadar?

Back: 1) Giving first place to human will (denying Allah's will) 2) Denying human will (blaming Allah for sins).

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 6 | Topic: Wrong attitudes

---

Flashcard 6

Front: What two points do the Quran and the hadith stress?

Back: 1) Everything happens by Allah's will 2) A human being has freedom of action and is responsible for his deeds.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 6 | Topic: Two points

---

Flashcard 7

Front: Four things the angel writes in the womb?

Back: Provision, deeds, lifespan, whether wretched or happy (Bukhari, Muslim).

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 6 | Topic: Allah's will

---

Flashcard 8

Front: What is the meaning of verses 99:7–8?

Back: Whoever does an atom's weight of good will see it; whoever does an atom's weight of evil will see it.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 6 | Topic: Human responsibility

---

Flashcard 9

Front: "We are going from one qadar to another" – who said it, and when?

Back: 'Umar (RA) – when an epidemic spread in Sham, answering Abu 'Ubaydah (RA).

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 6 | Topic: Examples

---

Flashcard 10

Front: What did 'Umar (RA) give the thief who used qadar as his excuse?

Back: The punishment for theft, plus 30 extra lashes for mocking qadar.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 6 | Topic: Examples

---

Flashcard 11

Front: Is qadar behind us or in front of us?

Back: Qadar – a hidden secret behind us; freedom of choice – in front of us.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 6 | Topic: No contradiction

---

Flashcard 12

Front: 57:22–23 – the purpose of qadar?

Back: So that we do not despair over what we miss, nor exult (become arrogant) over what we are given.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 6 | Topic: Purpose of qadar

---

Flashcard 13

Front: Qualities of a believer who believes in qadar?

Back: Contentment, patience, gratitude, never despairing, learning lessons and continuing to try.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 6 | Topic: Purpose of qadar
