### A2. 2023 (2024) – Islam I, Q21

Question ID: P2023_2024-I-Q21  
Year: 2023 (2024)  
Paper and question: Islam I, Q21  
Marks: not stated on the paper (Islam I multiple-choice question)  
Source: OL_2023_2024_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

In the table below, Column I gives some Arabic words and Column II their meanings.

| Column I | Column II |
|---|---|
| (1) 'ilm | A - decreeing (determining) |
| (2) qudrah | B - knowledge |
| (3) iradah | C - power |
| (4) qadar | D - will |

Which option is correct when Column II is arranged, in order, to match Column I?

- **A.** A B C D
- **B.** B C D A
- **C.** B D C A
- **D.** D B C A

**Correct answer:** B – B C D A *(option (2) on the original paper)*

**Explanation:** 'Ilm – knowledge (B); qudrah – power (C); iradah – will (D); qadar – decreeing (A). So B C D A.

### A4. 2025 (2026) – Islam I, Q37

Question ID: P2025_2026-I-Q37  
Year: 2025 (2026)  
Paper and question: Islam I, Q37  
Marks: not stated on the paper (Islam I multiple-choice question)  
Source: OL_2025_2026_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

What should a Muslim's belief about qada' and qadar be?

- **A.** acting in obedience to the Shari'ah, in keeping with human freedom of action, with the belief that only what Allah wills takes place
- **B.** believing that, because qada' and qadar is a completely hidden secret, man has no freedom of choice in it
- **C.** acting on conclusions about qada' and qadar reached by using reason alone
- **D.** believing that people's own actions are the cause of the successes and failures in human life

**Correct answer:** A – acting in obedience to the Shari'ah, in keeping with human freedom of action, with the belief that only what Allah wills takes place *(option (1) on the original paper)*

**Explanation:** The two aspects stressed by the Quran and hadith: Allah's will + man's freedom of action and responsibility. (B) is the Jabriyyah view, (D) the Qadariyyah view.

**Answer status:** Suggested – not an official answer (no official marking scheme was supplied); based on the Grade 10/11 textbook content

### B1. 2017 – Islam II, Q1 (vii)

Paper and question: Islam II, Q1 (vii)

Source: OL_2017_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(vii) Give two benefits gained, according to Islam, from believing in divine decree.

**Suggested answer:**

Any two of the following:
1. Not being crushed in spirit over what one has missed (57:22–23)
2. Not being carried away with joy over what one has received
3. Patience in hardship and gratitude in ease (Bukhari)
4. Contentment; continuing to strive without despair

**Answer explanation:** Belief in divine decree keeps a person from losing balance.

**Marking guidance (suggested only – not an official marking scheme):** Any two benefits.

### B3. 2025 (2026) – Islam II, Q7 (i)

Question ID: P2025_2026-II-Q7.1  
Year: 2025 (2026)  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q7 (i)  
Marks: 10 marks (short notes on two topics)  
Source: OL_2025_2026_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

Write short notes on any two of the following.
(i) The duties a Muslim must fulfil during natural disasters

**Suggested answer:**

- Believing in qada' and qadar, accepting Allah's will and being patient; "Seek help through patience and prayer" (2:45).
- Turning back to Allah through tawbah, du'a and istighfar.
- Rescuing the victims and protecting lives – whoever saves one life is as if he saved all mankind (5:32).
- Providing food, clothing, shelter and medical help; helping through sadaqah and zakat – such social duties are fard kifayah.
- Helping everyone without distinction of race or religion; cooperating with the authorities.
- Avoiding exploitation such as hoarding and raising prices.
- Keeping health and cleanliness; preventing the spread of infectious disease ('Umar (RA) – the Sham epidemic incident; the textbook says cholera).
- Priority (fiqh al-awlawiyyat) – urgent help takes priority over voluntary acts of worship.

**Answer explanation:** Write from the three angles of belief, worship and social duty.

**Marking guidance (suggested only – not an official marking scheme):** No official marking scheme; write equally on the two topics chosen.

**Answer status:** Needs verification – a suggested answer that could not be fully confirmed from the textbook; check with a teacher
