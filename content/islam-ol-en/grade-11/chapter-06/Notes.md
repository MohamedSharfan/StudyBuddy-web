# Grade 11 – Chapter 6: Al-Qada' wal-Qadar (Divine Decree)

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_06/Notes.md` (Tamil). English rendering.

## 🎯 What you will learn
- Qada' and qadar – the meaning of the words
- Why we must believe in qadar on the basis of revelation
- Two wrong attitudes towards qadar
- Two points stressed by the Quran and the hadith
- Why Allah established qadar

> 📖 **Opening verse of the chapter (6:38 – al-An'am) – meaning:** "We have left nothing out of the Book (Our record); then to their Lord they will all be gathered."

---

## 1. Meaning of the words ⭐

| Word | Literal meaning |
|---|---|
| **Qada' (القضاء)** | Creating, decreeing (judging), carrying out |
| **Qadar (القدر)** | Determining, pre-arranging, measuring out |

- These two terms are connected with Allah's **knowledge ('ilm), power (qudrah) and will (iradah)**.
- Scholars of aqeedah place qada' and qadar in the **"ilahiyyat"** section of aqeedah (the section about God).
- Belief in qada' and qadar is **one of the main parts of iman.**

---

## 2. It must be believed on the basis of revelation
- Qada' and qadar are a matter of belief → they must be believed **according to the guidance of the Quran and hadith, which is based on revelation**.
- 💡 **Comparison:** the knowledge of revelation is like **a lamp in the hand of a person walking in the dark**. On a dark path full of thorns, bushes and obstacles, a sensible person will not walk without a lamp.
- Thinking **only with reason**, leaving revelation aside, leads to **wrong conclusions**.
- Groups such as **the Jabriyyah, Qadariyyah, Mu'tazilah and Murji'ah** appeared because they thought about matters of iman on the basis of reason alone; they are **deviant groups.**

---

## 3. Two wrong attitudes towards qadar ⭐

| Attitude | Belief | Weakness |
|---|---|---|
| **1) Those who give first place to human will** | **Human actions** alone are the cause of success and failure; **they deny that everything happens by Allah's will** | They cannot explain the good and bad things that happen **beyond** their own plans, wishes and efforts |
| **2) Those who deny human will** | They say **Allah** is the cause of **all the good and evil** they do | They try to **justify the sins** they commit by following their desires – the Quran **condemns them strongly** |

> 📖 **(6:148) – meaning:** Those who associate partners will say: "If Allah had willed, neither we nor our forefathers would have associated anything (with Him), nor would we have forbidden anything."

---

## 4. Two points stressed by the Quran and the hadith ⭐
1. **Everything happens by Allah's will.**
2. A human being has been given **freedom of action**; so **he himself is responsible** for all his deeds.

### Point 1: Allah's will – evidence
- (meaning) "No hardship befalls except by Allah's will."
- **(2:284) – meaning:** He forgives whom He wills and punishes whom He wills; Allah has power over all things.
- **(6:59) – meaning:** **Not a leaf falls** without His knowledge.
- **Hadith (Bukhari, Muslim) – meaning:** each person in the mother's womb is **a drop for 40 days**, then a clot for a similar period, then a lump of flesh for a similar period; then an angel is sent and **four things** are written: **provision, deeds, lifespan, and whether he will be wretched or happy**; then the **ruh** is breathed in. A person may be only **an arm's length** from Paradise when what is written overtakes him, and he does the deeds of the people of Hell and enters Hell; and the reverse also happens.

👉 **Qada' and qadar means:** Allah is all-powerful and mighty; He **has already written** everything that is going to happen – the lifespan of the universe and of every living thing, success and failure, profit and loss, health and sorrow, wealth and poverty, disasters, whether a person will be good or bad; everything happens as He has written; **no one can change His will, and no fault can be found in His knowledge.**

### Point 2: Human freedom of action and responsibility – evidence
- **(41:46) – meaning:** Whoever does good, it is for himself; whoever does evil, it is against himself; your Lord is never unjust to His servants.
- **(42:30) – meaning:** Whatever misfortune befalls you is **because of what your own hands have earned.**
- **(99:7–8) – meaning:** Whoever does **an atom's weight** of good will see it; whoever does an atom's weight of evil will see it.
- **Hadith (Muslim) – meaning:** The strong believer is more beloved to Allah than the weak believer… **Be keen** on what benefits you; seek Allah's help; **do not give up (do not be helpless).** If something bad happens, do not say, "If only I had done this, it would not have happened"; say instead, "**Allah decreed it, and what He willed He did.**"

### 'Umar (RA) – the incident of the thief ⭐
- A thief was asked, "Why did you steal?" – "**It happened by Allah's will.**"
- 'Umar (RA) gave him **thirty extra lashes**.
- The reason: "**After the punishment for theft, lashes are given for mocking Allah's qadar.**"

👉 Allah has given a human being **full freedom of action**; he may do good if he wishes and evil if he wishes; **he himself is responsible** for his deeds.

---

## 5. Do the two contradict each other? ⭐
- On the surface they may seem to contradict; this causes confusion among ordinary people.
- **Clarification:**
  - Qada' and qadar = a completely hidden secret **behind us** – **only Allah knows it.**
  - A person's freedom of choice = what is **in front of us**; it is within **our sight and knowledge**.
- So Allah **will certainly question** us about whether we **chose** good or evil, what leads to success or to failure.
- 👉 **The correct belief:** believing that only what Allah wills happens, while acting by our freedom of action **in line with the Shari'ah and human reason**.

### 'Umar (RA) – the epidemic in Sham ⭐
- The Companions, led by 'Umar (RA), were travelling towards **Sham (Syria)**; news came that **cholera*** had spread there.
- 'Umar (RA) tried to avoid going there.
- **Abu 'Ubaydah (RA):** "**Are you trying to flee from Allah's qadar?**"
- **'Umar (RA):** "**No – we are going from one qadar to another qadar.**" *(meaning, as given in the textbook)*
- 👉 This answer clears up many confusions about qadar (taking precautions is also qadar).

> ⚠️ *The textbook calls the disease "cholera". The well-known report of this incident refers to a plague (ta'un) – confirm with the teacher.

## 6. Why did Allah establish qadar? ⭐
> 📖 **(57:22–23 – al-Hadid) – meaning:** No misfortune happens on earth or in yourselves except that it is written in a Book before We bring it into being; that is easy for Allah. (This is) **so that you do not despair over what has escaped you, nor exult (become arrogant) over what He has given you.**

- **Despair and exultation** – both are weaknesses that make a person **lose balance**; belief in qadar protects from them.

> 📖 **Hadith – meaning:** "How wonderful is the affair of the believer! **Everything** is good for him. If harm befalls him, he is **patient** – and that is good for him; if good befalls him, he is **grateful** – and that too is good for him."

> ⚠️ The Tamil source gives this hadith's reference as Bukhari; it is well known from Sahih Muslim – confirm with the teacher.

### Qualities of a believer who believes in qadar
- A heart that is always **content**
- **Patience**
- **Gratitude** to Allah
- He never **despairs** or **loses heart**
- He takes **lessons** from the results and keeps **trying**
👉 This is **the way of the prophets and the Companions.**

## ✅ Quick revision
- Qada' = creating, decreeing, carrying out; qadar = determining, pre-arranging, measuring out.
- Connected with 'ilm, qudrah, iradah; the ilahiyyat section.
- Revelation = a lamp in the dark; those who relied on reason alone – Jabriyyah, Qadariyyah, Mu'tazilah, Murji'ah.
- Two points: Allah's will (2:284, 6:59; the hadith of the womb) + human responsibility (41:46, 42:30, 99:7–8).
- 'Umar (RA): 30 extra lashes for the thief; "from one qadar to another".
- The purpose of qadar 57:22–23 – no despair, no exultation; "how wonderful is the affair of the believer".
