# Grade 11 – Chapter 6: Al-Qada' wal-Qadar – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_06/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q15 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. What is the literal meaning of the word "qadar"?

A. Creating, decreeing
B. Returning, turning back
C. Covering, concealing
D. Determining, pre-arranging, measuring out

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Qadar = determining, pre-arranging, measuring out. Creating, decreeing and carrying out are the meanings of "qada'".

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. With which attributes of Allah are the terms qada' and qadar connected?

A. Hearing, seeing, speaking
B. Mercy, forgiveness, love
C. Knowledge ('ilm), power (qudrah), will (iradah)
D. Justice, patience, gratitude

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Allah knows everything ('ilm) and determines it by His power (qudrah) and according to His will (iradah) – this is qada' and qadar.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q3. In which section of aqeedah have scholars of aqeedah placed qada' and qadar?

A. An-nubuwwat
B. Al-ilahiyyat
C. Ar-ruhaniyyat
D. As-sam'iyyat

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Since qada' and qadar are connected with Allah's will, they belong to the ilahiyyat section (about God).

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q4. To what does the textbook compare the knowledge of revelation?

A. The sea
B. A lamp in the hand of a person walking in the dark
C. The root of a tree
D. Rain

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Just as one cannot walk a dark path full of thorns and bushes without a lamp, qadar, a hidden matter, cannot be understood without revelation.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q5. Those who say, "If Allah had willed, neither we nor our forefathers would have associated partners" (6:148), show which wrong attitude?

A. Denying human will and making Allah the cause of their sins, to justify them
B. Giving first place to human will
C. The correct belief
D. Atheism

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: They make Allah the cause of the sins they commit by following their desires; the Quran condemns them strongly.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q6. What is the weakness of those who think human actions alone cause success and failure, and deny Allah's will?

A. They worship too much
B. They will not work
C. They cannot explain the good and bad things that happen beyond their own plans, wishes and efforts
D. They have no weakness

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: However much a person plans, unexpected things happen; those who deny Allah's will cannot explain them.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q7. "Not a leaf falls without Allah's knowledge" – which verse is this?

A. 6:59
B. 2:284
C. 42:30
D. 41:46

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: 6:59 shows that Allah's knowledge and will encompass everything.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q8. Which of the following is NOT one of the four things written by the angel in the mother's womb?

A. Provision
B. Lifespan
C. The name of the country of birth
D. Whether he will be wretched or happy

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: According to the hadith in Bukhari and Muslim, what is written is: provision, deeds, lifespan, and whether wretched or happy.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q9. "Whatever misfortune befalls you is because of what your own hands have earned" (42:30) – what does this stress?

A. Everything is by Allah's will only
B. A human being has freedom of action; he himself is responsible for his deeds
C. There is no misfortune
D. A human being can do nothing

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: 41:46, 42:30 and 99:7–8 stress human freedom of action and responsibility.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q10. Why did 'Umar (RA) give thirty extra lashes to the thief who said "It happened by Allah's will"?

A. Because he stole twice
B. Only because he lied
C. For mocking Allah's qadar
D. Because he tried to run away

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: In addition to the punishment for theft, lashes were given for using qadar to justify his sin – that is, for mocking qadar.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q11. What explanation does the textbook give for why qadar and human freedom of choice do not contradict each other?

A. Qadar is in front of us; choice is behind us
B. Qadar is a hidden secret behind us (only Allah knows it); freedom of choice is in front of us, within our knowledge
C. They are the same thing
D. They really do contradict each other

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: We cannot use a qadar we do not know as an excuse; we will be questioned about whether we chose the good among the choices in front of us.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q12. When an epidemic spread in Sham, who asked "Are you fleeing from Allah's qadar?" and who answered "We are going from one qadar to another"?

A. 'Umar (RA) asked; Abu 'Ubaydah (RA) answered
B. Khalid (RA) asked; 'Uthman (RA) answered
C. 'Ali (RA) asked; Abu Bakr (RA) answered
D. Abu 'Ubaydah (RA) asked; 'Umar (RA) answered

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Abu 'Ubaydah (RA) asked; 'Umar (RA) answered. The lesson: taking precautions also falls under Allah's qadar.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q13. According to verses 57:22–23, what is the purpose of Allah establishing qadar?

A. So that one does not despair over what is missed nor exult over what is given
B. So that people make no effort
C. To frighten people
D. To gather wealth

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Despair and exultation are both weaknesses that make a person lose balance; belief in qadar protects from them.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q14. According to the hadith "How wonderful is the affair of the believer", what does a believer do when harm befalls him?

A. He despairs
B. He gives up trying
C. He blames Allah
D. He is patient; and that too becomes good for him

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Patience in harm and gratitude in good – both turn out to be good for the believer.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q15. According to the hadith in Muslim, what should a believer say when something bad happens?

A. "If only I had done this, it would not have happened"
B. "Allah decreed it; it happened by His will"
C. "This is not my fate"
D. "Someone plotted it"

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: The Prophet ﷺ said: do not say "If only I had done…"; say "It happened by Allah's will." At the same time, one should be keen on what benefits and seek Allah's help.

Difficulty: Medium
