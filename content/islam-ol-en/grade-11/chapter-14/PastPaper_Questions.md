|---|---|
| (1) Musa (AS) | A - healing a sick person by touching him |
| (2) Ibrahim (AS) | B - a she-camel coming out of a rock |

### B1. 2017 – Islam II, Q2

Question ID: P2017-II-Q2.1  
Year: 2017  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q2  
Marks: 10 marks (division among sub-questions not given)  
Source: OL_2017_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(i) What are mu'jizat?
(ii) State three miracles given to the messengers.
(iii) List five miracles mentioned in the Quran.

**Suggested answer:**

(i) Mu'jizat (miracles) = events that happen contrary to the normal course of nature. Allah gave them to the prophets to confirm the message He gave them; human beings cannot challenge them or produce anything like them.

(ii) 1. Ibrahim (AS) – the burning fire became cool
2. Musa (AS) – his staff became a snake
3. Salih (AS) – a she-camel came out of a rock
(Also: 'Isa (AS) – healing the sick by touching them; Muhammad ﷺ – the Quran)

(iii) Miracles mentioned in the Quran:
1. The fire becoming cool for Ibrahim (AS)
2. The staff of Musa (AS) becoming a snake
3. A she-camel coming out of a rock for Salih (AS)
4. 'Isa (AS) healing the sick
5. The Quran given to the Prophet ﷺ (its challenge – 2:23, 17:88)

**Answer explanation:** In (iii) the miracles of the Quran's language, science and prophecy may also be mentioned (e.g. Pharaoh's body – 10:92; the Roman victory – 30:2–4).

**Marking guidance (suggested only – not an official marking scheme):** No official marking scheme.

**Answer status:** Needs verification – a suggested answer that could not be fully confirmed from the textbook; check with a teacher

**Verification note:** It is not clear whether (iii) "five miracles mentioned in the Quran" means the miracles of the prophets or the miraculous features of the Quran itself; both interpretations are given – confirm with the teacher.

### B2. 2020 – Islam II, Q2 (i), (ii)

Question ID: P2020-II-Q2.1  
Year: 2020  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q2 (i), (ii)  
Marks: 10 marks for the whole of Q2 (division among sub-questions not given)  
Source: OL_2020_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(i) What is 'mu'jizat al-Quran'?
(ii) State three of the reasons why the Quran is the main source of Islamic law.

**Suggested answer:**

(i) Mu'jizat al-Quran = the miraculous nature of the Quran – its uniqueness, such that even if mankind and jinn joined together they could not produce anything like it (17:88); its miracles of language, science and prophecy. The Quran is the lasting miracle given to the Prophet ﷺ.

(ii) Any three of the following:
1. It was revealed by Allah, who created man, who knows past, present and future, and who is free of all defect – the authority to make law is His alone.
2. It contains laws suited to all people and all times.
3. It is preserved, with Allah's guarantee (15:9), without any change or distortion.
4. It gives laws directly and also gives the basis for ijtihad (e.g. al-khamr, intoxicants).
5. It does not have the weaknesses of human law (limited knowledge, imbalance, changing from time to time).

**Answer explanation:** In (ii) it is suitable to compare it with human laws.

### B3. 2021 (2022) – Islam II, Q7 (i)

Question ID: P2021_2022-II-Q7.1  
Year: 2021 (2022)  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q7 (i)  
Marks: 10 marks (short notes on two topics)  
Source: OL_2021_2022_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

Write short notes on any two of the following.
(i) The Quran is a miracle

**Suggested answer:**

- A miracle (mu'jizah) = something that happens contrary to the normal course; given to the prophets to confirm their message. In each age the miracles related to the field that was famous then (Musa – defeating sorcery; 'Isa – medicine); Muhammad ﷺ – the Quran.
- The miracle of language: it surpassed the skill of the poets (al-Walid ibn al-Mughirah admitted it); its challenges – 52:34, 11:13, 2:23, 17:88 – have not been met to this day; it is the reference book for Arabic grammar; the incident of Surah an-Najm; memorised by hundreds of thousands.
- The scientific miracle: the barrier between two seas (55:19–20); healing in honey (16:69); oxygen decreasing as one rises higher (6:125); the formation of the embryo (Professor Keith Moore).
- The miracle of prophecy: the preservation of Pharaoh's body (10:92); the victory of the Romans (30:2–4).

**Answer explanation:** Write equally on the three kinds of miracle, with evidence.

**Marking guidance (suggested only – not an official marking scheme):** No official marking scheme; write equally on the two topics chosen.
