## Part 1: The Quran – a source of law ⚖️

### The weaknesses of man-made laws
- Society needs laws to run properly; in some societies they are made **by individuals**, in others **by social groups**.
- Because of the limits of human thinking, they turn out **unsuitable, impossible to follow, unbalanced, and in need of changing from time to time**.
- The reason: human knowledge is **limited**; feelings and wishes are **varied**; a person sees **only the present**; he cannot decide correctly about **the future**.

### The uniqueness of the Islamic Shari'ah ⭐
- Beyond human thinking, it takes as its sources of law **the Quran and the Sunnah, given through revelation**.
- It also recognises **ijtihad** as a method of making rules suited to the conditions of time and place.
- 👉 A Shari'ah with **both flexibility and firmness**.
- The One who sent down the Quran created human beings, knows human nature, **has no shortcomings and knows past, present and future** → **the authority to make law belongs to Him alone.**
- His laws can be followed **by all people in every age** – from the Prophet's ﷺ time **until the end of the world**.
- As Allah has guaranteed, it is **subject to no change or distortion** – it is preserved in hearts, on pages and in many forms.

### Risalah and wahy
- Allah has given suitable laws through **the messengers** = **risalah**; the channel = **wahy (revelation).**
- **The Quran is the collection of the "wahy matluww"** given to the Prophet ﷺ → **the first basic source of law** in Islam.
- The step-by-step methods of building the Muslim community, from Makkah to Madinah, were shown through revelation.
- When revelation **was delayed**, the Prophet ﷺ decided **by ijtihad** – if it was right, Allah **approved** it; if wrong, Allah **guided** him.
> 📖 **(Abu Dawud) – meaning:** "When I have not received revealed instruction, I judge between you on the basis of **my own ijtihad**."

### Two ways the Quran acts as a source of law ⭐
1. It gives **the law directly**.
2. It gives **the basis for a law** and leaves room to derive laws from it → **a way for ijtihad.**

- Commands and prohibitions are sometimes **detailed** and sometimes **brief**; the brief ones are explained **by the Sunnah**, and when needed **by ijtihad**.

### Example: al-khamr (intoxicating drink) ⭐
| Source | Contribution |
|---|---|
| **The Quran** | **Al-khamr**, the intoxicant made from **dates**, was made haram |
| **The Sunnah** | Showed **the punishment** for it |
| **Ijtihad** | Established that **all intoxicants made from other things too**, not only dates, are haram |

👉 Human laws are **human-made**; over time they stop working. The Quran is **divinely made**; it can work in every age; it alone has **divine value, binding power and human acceptance**.

## Part 2: The Quran – a miracle ✨

### What is a miracle (mu'jizah)?
- When something happens **contrary to the normal course of nature**, we call it a miracle.
- Allah gave miracles to the prophets **to confirm the message** He gave them.

| Prophet | Miracle |
|---|---|
| **Ibrahim (AS)** | The burning **fire became cool** |
| **Musa (AS)** | The **staff** he used to knock down leaves **turned into a snake** |
| **Salih (AS)** | **A camel came out of a rock** |

### A miracle suited to its time ⭐
- In each age, miracles appeared in **the field people considered most important**:

| Time | Popular field | Prophet | Miracle |
|---|---|---|---|
| | **Sorcery (magic)** | **Musa (AS)** | Miracles that defeated sorcery |
| | **Medicine** | **'Isa (AS)** | **Healing the sick by touch** |
| | **Arabic eloquence and literature** | **Muhammad ﷺ** | **The Quran** |

- The Prophet ﷺ himself pointed out (meaning): "The miracle I was given is **the Quran**."

### The miracle of language ⭐
- When the Quran was revealed, **mastery of Arabic** was highly valued; **even nomads** could compose poetry.
- Poetry contests were held every year **at the market of 'Ukaz**; winning poems were hung on **the Ka'bah**.
- In that situation, the Quran showed its miracle by **surpassing the skill of the poets** of the time.

#### Al-Walid ibn al-Mughirah ⭐
- An enemy of Islam; after speaking with the Prophet ﷺ he told **Abu Jahl** (meaning):
  > "There is no one in the world who can compose poetry better than I. By God, his words are not like any of it. What he says is **sweet and pleasing; its top is fruitful and its bottom is lush.** It overcomes everything; nothing overcomes it."
- The famous poet **Labid** admitted his inability before the Quran and **stopped composing poetry altogether.**

#### The Quran's challenge ⭐
- The Quran is **not the Prophet's ﷺ own composition**, nor did he **hear it from another skilled person**; it is the words of Allah revealed through wahy.

| Challenge | Verse |
|---|---|
| Bring **a Quran** like it | **52:34** |
| Bring **ten ayat (chapters)** | **11:13** |
| Bring **one chapter (surah)** | **2:23** |
| Even if **humans and jinn together** tried, they could not bring anything like it | **17:88** |

> ⚠️ The textbook says "ten ayat (11:13)"; 11:13 is usually understood as "ten surahs" – confirm with the teacher.

- This challenge has **never been met** by anyone, in the Prophet's ﷺ time or after.

#### Arabic grammar and tafsir
- Following the general rule that "grammar is drawn from literature", the Quran is **the reference book for Arabic grammar**.
- **Imam az-Zamakhshari** wrote a tafsir from the literary point of view – **"al-Kashshaf" (الكشاف).**

#### Its power of attraction – the incident of Surah an-Najm (Bukhari) ⭐
- One night the Prophet ﷺ was praying **near the Ka'bah, reciting Surah an-Najm**; the Qurayshi disbelievers were listening in hiding and enjoying it.
- When he recited the last ayah, "**So prostrate to Allah and worship Him**", the Prophet ﷺ went down in sujud; **the disbelievers listening on the other side also fell into sujud without realising it.**

#### Memorisation – a miracle
- No scripture is memorised completely by people; but **hundreds of thousands, from children to the elderly**, in every age have **memorised the whole Quran**.

### The scientific miracle ⭐
- The Quran is **not a science book**; but scientific facts discovered later are mentioned in it **in advance**, or its ideas have been **confirmed** by science.
- It asks us to **observe and study** Allah's creation.

| Scientific fact | Verse |
|---|---|
| He let the two seas meet; between them is **a barrier** they do not cross | **55:19–20** |
| In the creation of the heavens and the earth and the alternation of night and day are **signs for people of understanding** | **3:190** |
| **Healing in honey** | **16:69** |
| The higher one goes from the earth, **the less dense the oxygen** | **6:125** |

- **Professor James Jeans** (as quoted in the textbook) asks in amazement **who told Muhammad** what had been discovered over fifty years through the senses and research.
- **Professor Keith Moore** confirms through research the Quran's account of **the development of the embryo**.

### The miracle of prophecy ⭐
#### (1) Pharaoh's body – 10:92
- The Quran said that **Pharaoh**, the bitter enemy of Musa (AS), **would be drowned and his body preserved**.
- This was not proven in the Prophet's ﷺ time; in **the 19th century CE** his body was found during excavations; it is kept today **in the Egyptian Museum**.

#### (2) The war between the Romans and the Persians – 30:2–4 (ar-Rum)
- When Islam had been introduced **only in Makkah**, **the Persians won** a war between the Romans and the Persians **in southern Arabia***.
- The Quraysh warned: "Just as the Persians defeated the People of the Book, we will defeat you."
- At that time the Quran foretold that **in another war the Romans would defeat the Persians**.
- In a war fought while the Prophet ﷺ was living **in Madinah (623 CE)**, **the Romans won**, just as the Quran had said.

> ⚠️ *The textbook says "southern Arabia". Verse 30:3 says "in the nearest land", usually understood as the region of Syria and Palestine – confirm with the teacher.

## ✅ Quick revision
- 2:2 – a Book without doubt; guidance for the God-conscious.
- Human laws: unsuitable, unbalanced, changed from time to time.
- The Shari'ah = flexible + firm; the Quran = the first source of law (wahy matluww).
- 2 ways: direct law; the basis for law (a way for ijtihad). Example: al-khamr.
- Miracles: Ibrahim – the fire became cool; Musa – the staff became a snake; Salih – a camel from a rock; 'Isa – medicine; Muhammad ﷺ – the Quran.
- Language: 'Ukaz; al-Walid ibn al-Mughirah; Labid; the challenge 52:34, 11:13, 2:23, 17:88; az-Zamakhshari – al-Kashshaf; sujud at Surah an-Najm.
- Science: 55:19–20, 3:190, 16:69, 6:125; James Jeans, Keith Moore.
- Prophecy: Pharaoh's body 10:92 (19th century); the Roman victory 30:2–4 (623 CE).
