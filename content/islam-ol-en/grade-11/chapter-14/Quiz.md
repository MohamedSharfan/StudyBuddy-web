# Grade 11 – Chapter 14: Al-Quran – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_14/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q20 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. Which of the following does the textbook NOT give as a reason for the weaknesses of laws made by human thinking?

A. Human beings know past, present and future
B. Feelings and wishes are varied
C. A person sees only the present
D. Human knowledge is limited

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Only Allah knows past, present and future; that is why the authority to make law belongs to Him alone.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. Why does the Islamic Shari'ah have "both flexibility and firmness"?

A. No law ever changes
B. All laws can change
C. The Quran and the Sunnah are fixed, and there is also recognition for making rules through ijtihad suited to time and place
D. The government changes the laws

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: The basic sources do not change (firmness); ijtihad gives solutions for new situations (flexibility).

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q3. The Quran is the collection of which kind of revelation?

A. Wahy ghayr matluww
B. Dreams
C. Ilham
D. Wahy matluww

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: The Quran is the collection of recited revelation (wahy matluww) – the first basic source of law in Islam.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q4. What did the Prophet ﷺ do when revelation was delayed?

A. He gave no judgement
B. He decided by his own ijtihad; if right he received approval, if wrong he received guidance
C. He held a vote among the Companions
D. He asked Jewish scholars

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: "When I have not received revealed instruction, I judge on the basis of my own ijtihad" (Abu Dawud).

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q5. What are the two ways the Quran acts as a source of law?

A. Giving the law directly; giving the basis for a law and leaving room for ijtihad
B. Giving only laws; telling only stories
C. Laws of worship; laws of trade
D. Makkan laws; Madinan laws

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: It states some laws directly; for others it gives only the basis and shows the way for ijtihad.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q6. In the example of "al-khamr", what was the contribution of ijtihad?

A. It made the intoxicant from dates haram
B. It showed the punishment for it
C. It permitted wine
D. It established that all intoxicants made from other things are also haram

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Quran – the intoxicant from dates (al-khamr) is haram; Sunnah – the punishment; ijtihad – all intoxicants are haram.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q7. What does "miracle" refer to?

A. An event people like
B. A scientific discovery
C. Something happening contrary to the normal course of nature
D. A poetry contest

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Allah gave the prophets miracles, contrary to nature, to confirm the message He gave them.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q8. To which prophet does the miracle of a camel coming out of a rock belong?

A. Musa (AS)
B. 'Isa (AS)
C. Ibrahim (AS)
D. Salih (AS)

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Ibrahim – the fire became cool; Musa – the staff became a snake; Salih – a camel from a rock.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q9. In the age when medicine was famous, who showed the miracle of healing the sick by touch?

A. Musa (AS)
B. 'Isa (AS)
C. Sulayman (AS)
D. Yunus (AS)

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: In each age miracles were in the popular field: sorcery – Musa; medicine – 'Isa; Arabic literature – Muhammad ﷺ (the Quran).

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q10. At the time the Quran was revealed, at which market were poetry contests held every year?

A. 'Ukaz
B. Mina
C. Yamamah
D. Ta'if

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Poems that won at the market of 'Ukaz were hung on the Ka'bah.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q11. Who, after speaking with the Prophet ﷺ, told Abu Jahl, "What he says is sweet; it overcomes everything"?

A. Al-Walid ibn al-Mughirah
B. Labid
C. Abu Lahab
D. 'Utbah

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Even al-Walid ibn al-Mughirah, an enemy of Islam, admitted the linguistic miracle of the Quran.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q12. Which famous poet admitted his inability before the Quran and stopped composing poetry altogether?

A. Labid
B. Al-Walid
C. Az-Zamakhshari
D. Abu Jahl

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Labid admitted his inability before the Quran.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q13. Which verse says, "Even if humans and jinn together tried, they could not bring anything like it"?

A. 2:23
B. 11:13
C. 52:34
D. 17:88

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: 52:34 – a Quran; 11:13 – ten; 2:23 – one chapter; 17:88 – not even humans and jinn together could.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q14. What is the name of the tafsir by Imam az-Zamakhshari, written from a literary point of view?

A. Muwatta'
B. Al-Kashshaf
C. Riyad as-Salihin
D. Muqaddimah

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Imam az-Zamakhshari's tafsir is called "al-Kashshaf".

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q15. What happened when the Prophet ﷺ recited the last ayah of Surah an-Najm and went down in sujud (Bukhari)?

A. The Quraysh laughed
B. Nothing happened
C. The Quraysh attacked
D. The disbelievers listening in hiding also fell into sujud without realising it

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: This is evidence of the Quran's power of attraction.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q16. "He let the two seas meet; between them is a barrier they do not cross" – which verse is this?

A. 16:69
B. 55:19–20
C. 6:125
D. 3:190

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: 55:19–20 is an example of the Quran's scientific miracle.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q17. In which verse did the Quran point out that honey has healing properties?

A. 10:92
B. 6:125
C. 16:69
D. 30:2

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: 16:69 (an-Nahl) – the healing in honey. 6:125 – oxygen decreasing as one goes higher.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q18. Who confirmed through research the Quran's account of the development of the embryo?

A. Professor James Jeans
B. Imam az-Zamakhshari
C. Professor Keith Moore
D. Ibn Khaldun

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Professor Keith Moore – embryo development; Professor James Jeans – the one who asked in amazement "Who told Muhammad?"

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q19. When was the Quran's prophecy that Pharaoh's body would be preserved proven?

A. In the Prophet's ﷺ time
B. In 'Umar's (RA) time
C. During excavations in the 19th century CE
D. It has not been proven yet

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: 10:92 – Pharaoh's body was found in the 19th century CE and is still in the Egyptian Museum today.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q20. According to the prophecy of verses 30:2–4, when did the war take place in which the Romans defeated the Persians?

A. 610 CE
B. 623 CE – while the Prophet ﷺ was living in Madinah
C. 632 CE
D. 636 CE

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: When the Persians won during the Makkan period, the Quran foretold that the Romans would win; this came true in 623 CE.

Difficulty: Hard
