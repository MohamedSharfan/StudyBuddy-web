# Grade 11 – Chapter 28: The Model Islamic Family

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_28/Notes.md` (Tamil). English rendering.

## 🎯 What you will learn

- The union of a righteous couple – an act of 'ibadah
- The goal of the model family – Paradise, and protection from the Fire
- The duties of husband, wife and parents
- The family life of the Prophet ﷺ – the model
- Caring for neighbours and blood relatives

---

## 📖 Opening verse

> وَمِنْ آيَاتِهِ أَنْ خَلَقَ لَكُم مِّنْ أَنفُسِكُمْ أَزْوَاجًا لِّتَسْكُنُوا إِلَيْهَا وَجَعَلَ بَيْنَكُم مَّوَدَّةً وَرَحْمَةً ۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَآيَاتٍ لِّقَوْمٍ يَتَفَكَّرُونَ

**Meaning:** Among His signs is that He created for you wives from among yourselves so that you may live with them in peace, and He placed love and affection between you; in this are signs for people who reflect. **(30 Ar-Rum: 21)**

---

## 1️⃣ Introduction

- Marriage = the **seed** of family life; just as a good seed takes deep root, grows into a tree and gives good fruit, a good family and society grow from the union of a righteous man and woman.
- ⭐ Islam has made the lawful union of a righteous couple an act of **'ibadah**.
- That union, made in the name of Allah, is **strong and firm**; it is filled with **love, affection and mercy**.
- What a model family should give: **contentment, happiness, peace and comfort of the eyes**.
- 25 Al-Furqan: 74 – "Our Lord! Grant us comfort of the eyes through our wives and offspring."

## 3️⃣ The model husband / head of the family

- **A good husband** to his wife, and **a model father** to his children.
- When he enters the house he greets them **with a cheerful face** and **says salam**.
  - The Prophet ﷺ to Anas (RA): "My dear son! When you go in to your family, say salam; it will be a blessing for you and for your family." (The textbook gives no source.)
- When his wife is tired he lends a helping hand, and comforts her with gentle words.
- With religious commitment he guides his wife and children towards **spiritual growth**.
  - ⭐ **20 Ta-Ha: 132** – (O Prophet!) **Command your family to pray**, and be patient in it.
- He respects his wife's feelings and corrects faults with dignity and kindness.
- ⭐ He keeps a **balance** between his parents, wife and children; he wrongs none of them; he is good to his parents.
- **4 An-Nisa': 34** – Men are **in charge of (responsible for)** women, because of the distinction Allah has given them and because they spend (on them).

### The Prophet's ﷺ family life – the model ⭐

- Good relations with his wives, humour, love and a gentle nature.
- He **ran races** with 'A'ishah (RA).
- He ate whatever food was put before him; he never **found fault with food**.
- He **took part in the household work**.

---

## 4️⃣ The hadith on responsibility (Bukhari) ⭐

"Each of you is a shepherd (responsible), and each of you will be questioned about his responsibility."

| Person | Responsible for |
|---|---|
| The ruler | The citizens |
| The head of the family | His wife and children |
| The wife | Her husband's home |

---

## 5️⃣ The model wife

- With **Allah's pleasure** as her goal, she obeys her husband and keeps him content.
- ⭐ Hadith (Ibn Majah): "A woman who dies while her husband is **pleased with her** will enter Paradise."

> ⚠️ **Note:** This hadith is also reported by Tirmidhi; some hadith scholars have criticised its chain. The textbook cites Ibn Majah – confirm the grading with your teacher.

---

## 6️⃣ Parents and children

- Children who feel their parents' love grow up healthy and strong.
- ⭐ When **Fatimah (RA)** came to the Prophet ﷺ, he would stand up, welcome her, kiss her and seat her in his own place; Fatimah (RA) did the same for him; even when he was ill before his death he welcomed and kissed her. (Bukhari)
- A good father **spends generously** on his family; he is not miserly.
  - ⭐ Hadith (Bukhari): The best dinar a person spends is **the dinar he spends on his family**.
- Giving children opportunities for **good education**; the parents too keep learning.
- **Being aware** of what the children search for, read and write; guiding them to use the media properly for good things.

---

## 7️⃣ Neighbours and blood relatives

- They keep good relations with neighbours; they wish for their neighbours what they wish for themselves; they share joys, comfort them in hardship, cover their faults and live without causing them trouble.
- They maintain blood ties and strengthen relationships.
- ⭐ Hadith (Bukhari): The deeds that lead into Paradise – not associating partners with Allah, **establishing prayer, giving zakat, and keeping the ties of kinship**.
- Keeping ties with blood relatives brings **increase in provision and in lifespan**.
