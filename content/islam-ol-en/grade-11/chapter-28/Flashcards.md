# Grade 11 – Chapter 28: The Model Islamic Family – Flashcards

---

Flashcard 1

Front: The four things a model Islamic family should give?

Back: Contentment, happiness, peace, comfort of the eyes.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 28 | Topic: Introduction

---

Flashcard 2

Front: مَوَدَّة وَرَحْمَة (Mawaddah wa rahmah) – 30:21

Back: Love and mercy – what Allah placed between husband and wife; one of His signs.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 28 | Topic: Introduction

---

Flashcard 3

Front: The goal of the ideal Islamic family?

Back: Paradise; protecting oneself and one's family from the Fire.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 28 | Topic: Goal of the family

---

Flashcard 4

Front: What does a good husband do when his wife is tired?

Back: He lends a helping hand and gives comfort and joy with gentle words.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 28 | Topic: The model husband

---

Flashcard 5

Front: When the husband sees faults in his wife or children?

Back: He corrects them with dignity and kindness.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 28 | Topic: The model husband

---

Flashcard 6

Front: The husband's conduct between parents, wife and children?

Back: He keeps a balance; he wrongs none of them; he is good to his parents.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 28 | Topic: The model husband

---

Flashcard 7

Front: The wife's goal and conduct?

Back: With Allah's pleasure as her goal, she obeys her husband and keeps him content.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 28 | Topic: The model wife

---

Flashcard 8

Front: The responsibility of the head of the family (Bukhari)?

Back: He is responsible for his wife and children, and will be questioned about them.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 28 | Topic: Responsibility

---

Flashcard 9

Front: What do children need to grow up healthy?

Back: To feel their parents' love and affection.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 28 | Topic: Parents and children

---

Flashcard 10

Front: The parents' duty regarding the media?

Back: To guide children to use it properly, to learn and explore what is good and useful.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 28 | Topic: Parents and children

---

Flashcard 11

Front: How does a model family maintain relationships?

Back: Without limiting itself to parents and children, it keeps blood ties and gives mutual help.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 28 | Topic: Relatives and neighbours

---

Flashcard 12

Front: The final outcome of the model family in the Hereafter?

Back: Having lived in the world with happiness and dignity, it enters Paradise as a family in the Hereafter.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 28 | Topic: Goal of the family
