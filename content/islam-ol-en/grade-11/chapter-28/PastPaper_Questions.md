### B1. 2018 – Islam II, Q1 (x)

Question ID: P2018-II-Q1.10  
Year: 2018  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q1 (x)  
Marks: 20 marks for the whole of Q1  
Source: OL_2018_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(x) State two duties children must fulfil towards their parents.

**Suggested answer:**

Any two of the following:
1. Obeying parents and treating them with respect; not even saying "uff" to them (17:23)
2. Being grateful to them (31:14)
3. Caring for them and being good to them in old age
4. Praying for them (17:24)

**Answer explanation:** The Quran mentions being good to parents next to tawhid.

**Marking guidance (suggested only – not an official marking scheme):** Two duties.

### B2. 2020 – Islam II, Q7 (i)

Question ID: P2020-II-Q7.1  
Year: 2020  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q7 (i)  
Marks: 10 marks (short notes on two topics)  
Source: OL_2020_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

Write short notes on any two of the following.
(i) The qualities of a model Islamic family

**Suggested answer:**

- A union filled with love and mercy (30:21); contentment, happiness and comfort of the eyes (25:74).
- Its goal is Paradise – protecting oneself and one's family from the Fire (66:6).
- A good husband: entering the house with a cheerful face and saying salam; helping his wife; commanding the family to pray (20:132); keeping a balance between parents, wife and children.
- A good wife: obeying her husband with Allah's pleasure as her aim.
- The awareness that each will be questioned about his responsibility (Bukhari).
- Love for the children and good education; spending generously on the family (the best dinar).
- Caring for neighbours and blood relatives – increase in provision and lifespan.

**Answer explanation:** The Prophet's ﷺ family life (running races with 'A'ishah (RA), taking part in housework) may be given as an example.

**Marking guidance (suggested only – not an official marking scheme):** No official marking scheme; write equally on the two topics chosen.

### B3. 2022 (2023) – Islam II, Q6 (i), (ii)

Question ID: P2022_2023-II-Q6.1  
Year: 2022 (2023)  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q6 (i), (ii)  
Marks: 10 marks for the whole of Q6 (division among sub-questions not given)  
Source: OL_2022_2023_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(i) What reward in the Hereafter did the Prophet ﷺ mention for a woman who dies while her husband is pleased with her?
(ii) State three duties of the parents in a model Muslim family.

**Suggested answer:**

(i) She will enter Paradise (Ibn Majah).

(ii) Any three of the following:
1. Showing love to the children (the Prophet ﷺ stood up to welcome Fatimah (RA) and kissed her – Bukhari).
2. Spending generously on the family – the best dinar is the one spent on the family (Bukhari).
3. Giving the children opportunities for good education.
4. Guiding them with awareness about what they learn and the media they use.
5. Commanding the family to pray and bringing them up with religious commitment (20:132; 66:6).

**Answer explanation:** In (ii) one piece of evidence may be added for each duty.

**Marking guidance (suggested only – not an official marking scheme):** No official marking scheme.
