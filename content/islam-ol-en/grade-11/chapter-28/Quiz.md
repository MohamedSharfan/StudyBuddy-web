# Grade 11 – Chapter 28: The Model Islamic Family – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_28/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q16 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. What has Islam made the lawful union of a righteous couple?

A. A business contract
B. An act of 'ibadah
C. Only a social custom
D. A temporary arrangement

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: It is a strong union made in the name of Allah; it should be filled with love, affection and mercy.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. Which verse says, "O believers! Protect yourselves and your families from the Fire"?

A. 39 Az-Zumar: 15
B. 52 At-Tur: 26
C. 20 Ta-Ha: 132
D. 66 At-Tahrim: 6

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: The ideal Islamic family has Paradise as its goal, and protection from the Fire as its foundation.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q3. Which verse says that the real losers are those who bring loss on themselves and their families on the Day of Judgement?

A. 39 Az-Zumar: 15
B. 30 Ar-Rum: 21
C. 4 An-Nisa': 34
D. 25 Al-Furqan: 74

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: 39:15 warns, "that is the clear loss".

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q4. Which verse mentions the people of Paradise saying, "When we were among our families in the world, we were fearful about the Hereafter"?

A. 66 At-Tahrim: 6
B. 20 Ta-Ha: 132
C. 52 At-Tur: 26–27
D. 4 An-Nisa': 34

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: 52:26–27 – they will say that Allah was gracious and saved them and their families from the scorching punishment.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q5. To whom did the Prophet ﷺ give the advice to say salam when going in to one's family?

A. Abu Hurayrah (RA)
B. Anas (RA)
C. Ibn 'Abbas (RA)
D. 'Ali (RA)

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: "My dear son! When you go in to your family, say salam; it will be a blessing for you and for your family."

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q6. Which verse says, "(O Prophet!) Command your family to pray, and be patient in it"?

A. 20 Ta-Ha: 132
B. 66 At-Tahrim: 6
C. 31 Luqman: 17
D. 2 Al-Baqarah: 43

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: The model head of the family follows this command well and guides the family to spiritual growth.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q7. Which of the following does the textbook NOT mention about the Prophet's ﷺ family life?

A. Running races with 'A'ishah (RA)
B. Never finding fault with food
C. Taking part in the household work
D. Being harsh at home

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: The Prophet ﷺ lived as a model of love, gentleness and good humour.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q8. What reasons does 4 An-Nisa': 34 give for "men are in charge of women"?

A. Men are more educated
B. Men are older
C. The distinction Allah has given men, and their spending of their wealth
D. Men pray more

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: The verse gives the distinction and the responsibility for spending as the reasons.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q9. According to the hadith "Each of you is responsible", what is the wife responsible for?

A. The country
B. Her husband's home
C. The neighbours
D. The mosque

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: The ruler – the citizens; the head of the family – his wife and children; the wife – her husband's home (Bukhari).

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q10. "A woman who dies while her husband is pleased with her will enter Paradise" – what is the source of this hadith?

A. Bukhari
B. Muslim
C. Tirmidhi
D. Ibn Majah

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: The textbook gives it as Ibn Majah.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q11. What did the Prophet ﷺ do when his daughter Fatimah (RA) came to him (Bukhari)?

A. He said salam while remaining seated
B. He stood up, welcomed her, kissed her and seated her in his own place
C. He told her to go to another room
D. He did nothing

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Even when he was ill before his death he welcomed and kissed Fatimah (RA) – a model of love for one's children.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q12. What is the best dinar a person spends (Bukhari)?

A. The dinar he spends on his family
B. The dinar spent on building a mosque
C. The dinar spent on travel
D. The dinar invested in trade

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: A good father is not miserly in spending on his wife and children.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q13. Which of the following is NOT something model parents should do regarding their children's education?

A. Create opportunities for good education
B. Be aware of what the children read and write
C. Guide them to use the media for good things
D. Stop learning themselves

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Parents too keep learning and develop their religious knowledge and abilities.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q14. Which of the following is NOT mentioned in the hadith (Bukhari) about the deeds that lead into Paradise?

A. Not associating partners with Allah
B. Establishing prayer
C. Performing Hajj
D. Keeping the ties of kinship

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Not associating partners, prayer, zakat and kinship ties – these four are in the hadith.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q15. What does the textbook give as the benefit of keeping ties with blood relatives?

A. Fame
B. Increase in provision and lifespan
C. Wealth decreases
D. Gaining a position

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Keeping ties with blood relatives brings increase in provision and lifespan.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q16. Which is the correct way for a model Muslim family to treat neighbours?

A. They cover their faults, and wish for them what they wish for themselves
B. They spread their faults
C. They have no contact with them
D. They help only relatives

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: They share joys, comfort them in hardship, live without causing trouble and are generous to them.

Difficulty: Easy
