# Grade 11 – Chapter 5: The Finality of Prophethood – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_05/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q18 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. Which verse calls Muhammad ﷺ "the last of the prophets" (khatam an-nabiyyin)?

A. 33:40 (al-Ahzab)
B. 61:6 (as-Saff)
C. 5:3 (al-Ma'idah)
D. 21:107 (al-Anbiya')

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: 33:40 – "He is the Messenger of Allah and the last of the prophets."

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. What is the position of Islam on rejecting the finality of Muhammad's ﷺ prophethood?

A. It is kufr
B. It is a minor sin
C. It is permitted
D. It is makruh

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: The position of Islam is that Muhammad ﷺ is the last; rejecting this is kufr.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q3. Which statement about the relationship between nabi and rasul is correct?

A. Every prophet is a messenger
B. Every messenger is a prophet; but not every prophet is a messenger
C. Prophet and messenger are unrelated
D. The rank of prophet is higher than that of messenger

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: The rank of messenger is more special than that of prophet. So if there is "no other prophet", it is even more certain that there is "no other messenger".

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q4. Which verse mentions 'Isa (AS) giving good news of a messenger called "Ahmad" who would come after him?

A. 33:40
B. 61:6
C. 5:3
D. 3:31

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: 61:6 (as-Saff) – this prophecy of 'Isa (AS) is one of the Quranic proofs of the finality of prophethood.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q5. Who explained the verse "Today I have perfected your religion for you" (5:3) by saying "they will need no other religion and no other messenger"?

A. Imam Ibn Kathir
B. Imam Abu Hanifah
C. Imam al-Bukhari
D. Imam an-Nawawi

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Imam Ibn Kathir (RH) – this is the greatest favour Allah has done for this community; the Prophet ﷺ was sent as the last prophet and as the prophet for both humans and jinn.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q6. When was verse 5:3 revealed?

A. During the hijrah
B. With the first revelation
C. A few days before the Prophet's ﷺ death
D. During the Battle of Badr

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: This verse, announcing that the religion was complete, was revealed a few days before the Prophet's ﷺ death.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q7. In the hadith of the parable of the building (Bukhari, Muslim), to what does the Prophet ﷺ compare himself?

A. The foundation of the building
B. The roof of the building
C. The one who came to fill the small gap left unfinished in the building
D. The door of the building

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: The Prophet ﷺ is the one who filled the gap left in a beautiful house and brought the line of prophets to an end.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q8. Which of the following is NOT one of the six things by which the Prophet ﷺ was favoured over the other prophets?

A. The eloquence of jawami' al-kalim
B. Living to a hundred years of age
C. The whole earth being made a mosque
D. War booty being made lawful

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: The six favours: jawami' al-kalim, help through awe, booty made lawful, the whole earth a mosque and pure, a prophet to the whole world, the end of the line of prophets (Tirmidhi, Ibn Majah).

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q9. Which of the Prophet's ﷺ names means "the last"?

A. Al-Mahi
B. Al-Hashir
C. Al-'Aqib
D. Ahmad

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Al-'Aqib = the last; al-Mahi = the eraser (of kufr); al-Hashir = the gatherer (of people in the Hereafter).

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q10. What is the explanation of the name "al-Mahi"?

A. People will be gathered around me
B. Through me Allah will erase kufr
C. There is no prophet after me
D. I am the one who praises most

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Al-Mahi = the eraser – through the Prophet ﷺ Allah will erase kufr.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q11. According to the hadith in Abu Dawud, how many liars claiming to be prophets will appear in the Prophet's ﷺ ummah?

A. Ten
B. Thirty
C. Twenty
D. Forty

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Thirty liars will appear; but "I am the last of the prophets; there is no prophet after me."

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q12. Which of the following is NOT one of the rational reasons for sending prophets?

A. A community to which no prophet has been sent
B. The previous prophet's teaching being lost or distorted
C. The previous prophet's teaching being incomplete
D. People having a lot of wealth

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: The four reasons: a community without a prophet or the message, the teaching lost or distorted, the teaching incomplete, the need for another prophet's help. None of these exists after the Prophet ﷺ.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q13. Who said, about a false prophet who asked for a chance to show the signs of his prophethood, "Even the one who asks him for signs becomes a kafir"?

A. Imam Malik
B. Imam ash-Shafi'i
C. Imam Abu Hanifah
D. Imam Ahmad

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Since there is no prophet at all after Muhammad ﷺ, asking a false prophet for signs amounts to accepting that he might be one, said Imam Abu Hanifah (RH).

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q14. Which Companion killed al-Aswad al-'Ansi, the false prophet from Yemen?

A. Wahshi ibn Harb
B. 'Ali (RA)
C. Khalid ibn al-Walid
D. Fayruz ad-Daylami

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Al-Aswad al-'Ansi was killed by Fayruz ad-Daylami; Musaylimah was killed by Wahshi ibn Harb at the Battle of Yamamah.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q15. In which battle was Musaylimah, the false prophet from Najd, killed?

A. Badr
B. Nahawand
C. Butaha
D. Yamamah

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Musaylimah was killed in Abu Bakr's (RA) time at the Battle of Yamamah by a Companion named Wahshi ibn Harb.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q16. Who was the woman false prophet who married Musaylimah and accepted Islam after he was killed?

A. Sajah
B. Hind
C. Umm Jamil
D. Tulayhah

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Sajah tried to march against Madinah, failed, and married Musaylimah; after he was killed she accepted Islam.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q17. Who claimed prophethood, later re-entered Islam, and died a martyr at the Battle of Nahawand?

A. Tulayhah
B. Al-Aswad al-'Ansi
C. Musaylimah
D. Sajah

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Tulayhah accepted Islam in 630 CE, claimed prophethood in 631, was defeated by Khalid ibn al-Walid's army in 632 (the Battle of "Butaha" in the textbook), accepted Islam again, and died a martyr at Nahawand.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q18. Which of the following is NOT one of today's methods of campaigning against the finality of prophethood?

A. Wrong interpretations of the Quran and hadith
B. Explaining authentic hadiths correctly
C. Creating doubts about the foundations of Islam
D. Making fabricated hadiths

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Giving correct explanations is the work of scholars; wrong interpretations, fabricated hadiths and creating doubts are the methods of the opposing campaign.

Difficulty: Easy
