### A1. 2018 – Islam I, Q7

Question ID: P2018-I-Q07  
Year: 2018  
Paper and question: Islam I, Q7  
Marks: not stated on the paper (Islam I multiple-choice question)  
Source: OL_2018_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

The person who claimed to be a prophet in the time of the Prophet ﷺ

- **A.** Tulayhah
- **B.** Sajah
- **C.** Musaylimah
- **D.** al-Aswad al-'Ansi

**Correct answer:** C – Musaylimah *(option (3) on the original paper)*

Observe the names given in the table below.

| A - Ahmad | B - Khalil Allah | C - Hashir |
| D - Siddiq | E - 'Aqib | F - Dhun-Nurayn |

Which option contains only other names of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ?

- **A.** A, B, C, D

## Part B: Structured and Essay Questions (Islam II)

### B1. 2019 – Islam II, Q3 (ii)

Question ID: P2019-II-Q3.2  
Year: 2019  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q3 (ii)  
Marks: 10 marks for the whole of Q3  
Source: OL_2019_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(ii) What is khatm an-nubuwwah? Present one piece of evidence each from the Quran and the hadith concerning it.

**Suggested answer:**

Khatm an-nubuwwah = the finality of prophethood – Muhammad ﷺ is the last prophet; no prophet or messenger will come after him. Rejecting this is kufr.
Evidence from the Quran: 33:40 – "Muhammad is not the father of any of your men, but he is the Messenger of Allah and the last of the prophets (khatam an-nabiyyin)." (meaning)
Evidence from the hadith: "My example and that of the other prophets is like a man who built a house but left the place of one brick unfinished… I am the one who came to complete it; the line of prophets ended with me" (Bukhari, Muslim). Or "There is no prophet after me" (Abu Dawud).

**Answer explanation:** Definition + one verse + one hadith.

**Question:**

(ii) State the six distinctions given only to Prophet Muhammad ﷺ and not to the other prophets.

**Suggested answer:**

(Tirmidhi, Ibn Majah)
1. "Jawami' al-kalim" – the eloquence of expressing wide meanings in few words
2. Being helped by awe (ru'b) cast into the enemy
3. The spoils of war (ghanimah) being made lawful
4. The whole earth being made a mosque and a means of purification
5. Being sent as prophet to the whole world
6. The coming of prophets being completed with him
