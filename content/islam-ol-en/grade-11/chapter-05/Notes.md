- Evidence from the Quran (3)

> 📖 **Opening verse of the chapter (33:40 – al-Ahzab):**
> مَا كَانَ مُحَمَّدٌ أَبَا أَحَدٍ مِنْ رِجَالِكُمْ وَلٰكِنْ رَسُوْلَ اللهِ وَخَاتَمَ النَّبِيِّيْنَ وَكَانَ اللهُ بِكُلِّ شَيْءٍ عَلِيْمًا
> **Meaning:** "Muhammad ﷺ is not the father of any of your men, but he is **the Messenger of Allah and the Seal (the last) of the prophets**. And Allah has full knowledge of all things."

---

## 1. The position of Islam ⭐
- Believing in the prophets and messengers is **a most important part of iman.**
- The position of Islam is that **Muhammad ﷺ is the last**, and **no prophet will come** after him.
- ⚠️ **Rejecting this is kufr.**

---

## 2. Evidence from the Quran ⭐

### (1) 33:40 – al-Ahzab
- "He is the Messenger of Allah and **the last of the prophets** (khatam an-nabiyyin)."
- 👉 If there is **no prophet** after him, then it is even more certain that there is **no messenger** after him.

#### The relationship between nabi (prophet) and rasul (messenger) ⭐
- **The rank of messenger is more special than the rank of prophet.**
- **Every messenger is a prophet;** but **not every prophet is a messenger.**

### (2) 61:6 – as-Saff
- 'Isa (AS) (meaning): "...and bringing **good news** of a messenger who will come after me, whose name is **Ahmad**."
- This is the message 'Isa (AS) gave his people about Muhammad ﷺ. Many other prophets also **foretold** this.

### (3) 5:3 – al-Ma'idah
- (meaning) "...Today I have **perfected your religion for you**, completed My favour upon you, and approved Islam as your religion."
- It was revealed **a few days before the Prophet's ﷺ death**.
- **Imam Ibn Kathir (RH)** (meaning): "This is **the greatest favour** Allah has done for this community. He has perfected the religion for them, so they **will need no other religion and no other messenger**, because Allah sent Muhammad ﷺ as **the last prophet and as the prophet for both humans and jinn**."

---

## 3. Evidence from the hadith ⭐

### (1) The parable of the building (Bukhari, Muslim)
> (meaning) "My example and that of the other prophets is like a man who **built a house** and completed it. People admired it but pointed to **one small gap** left unfinished. **I am the one who has come to fill that gap** (in the building of Islam). With me the line of prophets has come to an end."

### (2) Six special favours (Tirmidhi, Ibn Majah)
> (meaning) "I have been favoured over the other prophets with **six things**."

| No. | Favour |
|---|---|
| 1 | The eloquence of **"jawami' al-kalim"** (saying much in few words) |
| 2 | I have been helped by **awe (ru'b)** |
| 3 | **War booty (ghanimah) has been made lawful** for me |
| 4 | **The whole earth has been made a mosque and pure** for me |
| 5 | I have been sent **as a prophet to the whole world** |
| 6 | **The coming of prophets has been ended with me** |

### (3) The Prophet's names (Bukhari, Muslim)
> (meaning) "I have several names."

| Name | Arabic | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Muhammad** | محمد | (The praised one) |
| **Ahmad** | أحمد | (The one who praises most) |
| **Al-Mahi** | الماحي | **The eraser** – through me Allah **will erase kufr** |
| **Al-Hashir** | الحاشر | **The gatherer** – people will be gathered (in the Hereafter) around me |
| **Al-'Aqib** | العاقب | **The last** – so there is no prophet after me |

### (4) Thirty liars (Abu Dawud)
> (meaning) "**Thirty liars** will appear in my ummah, each claiming to be a prophet. But **I am the last of the prophets**; there is no prophet after me."

---

## 4. Rational evidence ⭐
**The reasons** for sending prophets:
1. There must be a community to which **no prophet has been sent**, or to which the message of a prophet sent to another people **has not reached**.
2. The teaching of the previous prophet must have **been lost** or **distorted**.
3. The teaching of the previous prophet must have been **incomplete**.
4. A prophet must have needed **the help of another prophet**.

👉 After Muhammad ﷺ, **none of these reasons applies.** So there is **no need at all** for another prophet.
👉 **The Companions, the Tabi'un and the Tabi' al-Tabi'in** all **agreed unanimously** on this.

### Imam Abu Hanifah (RH)
- In his time a false prophet said: "Give me a chance to **show the signs** of my prophethood."
- The Imam said (meaning): "**Even the one who asks him to show those signs becomes a kafir**, because there is no prophet at all after Muhammad ﷺ."

---

## 5. False prophets who appeared in history ⭐
- Because of weak iman and ignorance, some people accepted false prophets → the danger of people going astray and society splitting.
- **The scholars and rulers** of each time **defeated** the campaigns of false prophethood.

| False prophet | Region | Time | End |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Al-Aswad al-'Ansi** | **Yemen** | The Prophet's ﷺ time | Killed by a Companion named **Fayruz ad-Daylami** |
| **Musaylimah** | **Najd** | The Prophet's ﷺ time | Killed in Abu Bakr's (RA) time **at the Battle of Yamamah** by a Companion named **Wahshi ibn Harb** |
| **Sajah** (a woman) | — | — | Tried to march against Madinah; when that failed she **married Musaylimah**; after he was killed she **accepted Islam** |
| **Tulayhah** | — | Accepted Islam in **630 CE**; claimed prophethood in **631** | In **632**, after his forces were defeated **at the Battle of Butaha*** by **Khalid ibn al-Walid**'s army, he re-entered Islam; he admitted his wrong, fought in many battles, and finally **died a martyr at the Battle of Nahawand** |

> ⚠️ *The textbook names this battle "Butaha" (بطاحة). Historical sources usually name the battle in which Khalid ibn al-Walid defeated Tulayhah as Buzakhah (بزاخة) – confirm with the teacher.

---

## 6. Campaigns against it today
There are people **even today** who campaign against the finality of prophethood. Their methods:
- Giving **wrong interpretations of the Quran and the hadith**
- Making **fabricated hadiths**
- Creating **doubts about the foundations of Islam**

👉 Islamic scholars have answered these clearly.
👉 **Doubting the finality of prophethood can destroy iman itself**; so it is essential for all Muslims to be **clear and alert**.
