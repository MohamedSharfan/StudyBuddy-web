# Grade 11 – Chapter 13: Ways of Spiritual Development

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_13/Notes.md` (Tamil). English rendering.

## 🎯 What you will learn
- The human being – body and soul
- Islamic practices that purify the soul
- Four spiritual qualities: **sabr, tawakkul, ma'rifah, rida**
- Qalbun salim (a sound, pure heart)

> 📖 **Opening hadith of the chapter (Bukhari):** أَلَا إِنَّ فِي الْجَسَدِ مُضْغَةً إِذَا صَلَحَتْ صَلَحَ الْجَسَدُ كُلُّهُ وَإِذَا فَسَدَتْ فَسَدَ الْجَسَدُ كُلُّهُ أَلَا وَهِيَ الْقَلْبُ
> **Meaning:** "Know that in the body there is **a piece of flesh**. If it is sound, the whole body is sound; if it is corrupt, the whole body is corrupt. Know that **it is the heart.**"

---

## 1. The human being – body and soul ⭐
- A human being is **a handful of earth** taken from Allah's earth, together with **the ruh (soul)** breathed in from Allah.
> 📖 **(38:71–72 – Sad) – meaning:** I am going to create a human being **from clay**; when I have shaped him and breathed into him **of My ruh**, fall down in **sujud** to him.

| Part | Origin | Its health depends on |
|---|---|---|
| **Body** | **From the earth** | **The earth** |
| **Soul** | **From Allah** | **Allah** |

- Only when both are sound is there **complete health.**
- 👉 **Believing in Allah is the first step to a healthy soul.**

### Ways to put the heart right
- All religions and philosophies accept the importance of the heart; modern science tries to find solutions through **psychology**.
- Islam guides us to put the heart right and strengthen it **by connecting it with Allah**.
- **Practices:** **prayer, zakat, fasting, hajj, reciting the Quran, dhikr, and reflecting on Allah's creation.**
- When this training succeeds, high spiritual qualities appear: **sabr, tawakkul, ma'rifah, rida.**

---

## 2. As-sabr (الصبر) – patience ⭐
> 📖 **Meaning:** patience – **a strong, noble quality** with the power to restrain bad traits such as **haste, anger, panic, greed and revenge.**

- **(Bukhari) – meaning:** The truly strong man is not the one who wins at wrestling; the truly strong man is the one who **controls himself when he is angry**.
- **(7:126) – meaning:** Our Lord! **Pour patience upon us**, and take us (in death) as people fully submitted to You.
- **(Muslim) – meaning:** The affair of the believer is **wonderful**; in happiness he is **grateful**, in hardship he is **patient** – and both bring a good outcome.
- **(2:155–156) – meaning:** We will test you with fear, hunger and loss of wealth, lives and crops; when hardship strikes, the patient say, "**We belong to Allah, and to Him we return.**"

### The forms of patience (according to the situation) ⭐
| Situation | Form of patience |
|---|---|
| When anger comes | **Restraining it** |
| When greed comes | **Being content** |
| When trials come | **Bearing them** |
| Worry and sorrow | **Enduring them** |
| When the heart is drawn to sin | **Keeping away** |
| Weariness in good deeds | **Standing firm** |
| Losses | **Not letting them overwhelm** |
| When life's burdens increase | **Carrying them** |
| Problems | **Facing them** |
| The urge for revenge | **Forgiving** |
| On hearing decisions one dislikes | **Accepting them** |

- **(Muslim) – meaning:** "**Patience is light.**" – it lights up the path to success.

## 3. At-tawakkul (التوكل) – reliance on Allah ⭐
- **Linguistically:** **entrusting (handing over responsibility).**
- A combination of **effort + trust in Allah**.
- **In Islamic usage:** **entrusting the outcome of our efforts to Allah**; an expression of trust in Allah.
> 📖 **(8:2) – meaning:** The believers are those whose hearts **tremble** when Allah is mentioned; when His verses are recited their **iman increases**; and **in their Lord they put their trust**.

### Sabab (means) and tawakkul
- Allah has made the success of every task depend on **asbab (means and causes)**; but **only Allah decides the outcome.**
- A true believer **uses the means and entrusts the outcome to Allah.**
- Those who do not believe in Allah put **all their trust in the means**; but the outcome still happens only as Allah wills.

### Two reasons the believers rely on Allah ⭐
1. **The means are also Allah's arrangement**; without Allah's will, success cannot come from the means alone.
2. They are certain that by trusting Allah they will receive **the reward for it, in this world or the Hereafter**.

### Evidence
- **(3:173) – meaning:** When told, "The enemy has gathered against you, so fear them," it only **increased their faith**, and they said: "**Allah is sufficient for us, and He is the best Guardian.**"
- **(Abu Dawud) – meaning:** Whoever says when leaving home **بِسْمِ اللهِ تَوَكَّلْتُ عَلَى اللهِ لَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللهِ** (In the name of Allah; I rely on Allah; there is no power and no strength except with Allah), it is said to him: "You **are guided, you are taken care of, and you are protected**"; and **Satan goes away from him.**
- **(Bukhari):** The last words of Ibrahim (AS) **when he was thrown into the fire**: **حَسْبِيَ اللهُ وَنِعْمَ الْوَكِيْلُ** – (meaning) Allah is sufficient for me, and He is the best Guardian.
- **(3:159) – meaning:** When you **have decided** on something, put your trust in Allah; Allah **loves** those who rely on Him.

### Times to rely on Allah
When leaving home, in work and business, in studies, when facing an enemy, when going to sleep.

## 4. Al-ma'rifah (المعرفة) – knowing Allah ⭐
- **Linguistically:** **knowledge.**
- **In Islamic spiritual usage:** attaining **the deep joy of the heart** by **knowing Allah, the Lord, as if seeing Him**.

> 📖 **Imam al-Ghazali (RH)** (meaning): "The pleasure of each thing lies in its own nature. **The pleasure of the eye** is in seeing a beautiful sight; **the pleasure of the ear** is in hearing a beautiful sound; **the pleasure of the heart is in knowing Allah.**"

> 📖 **Muhyiddin 'Abd al-Qadir al-Jilani (RH)** (meaning): "**Empty yourself** of creation through Allah's decree, of your desires through His commands, and of your own will through His action. Then you will become fit to be **a vessel that carries the knowledge of Allah**."

### Qualities of one who has attained ma'rifah
- He sees even **the hardships** he suffers for Allah's sake **as joy**.
- He **does not hesitate or hold back** from following the Shari'ah **completely**, from setting aside **more and more time** to worship Him, or from **dedicating everything** he has.
- He **depends entirely on Allah**.

---

## 5. Ar-rida (الرضا) – contentment ⭐
- **Linguistically:** **being pleased (content).**
- Being content and satisfied with **every situation** that comes to us by Allah's arrangement.
- **The highest inner state** of a believer; in this state **Allah is pleased with the servant and the servant is pleased with Allah.**

### Bay'at ash-Shajarah / Bay'at ar-Ridwan ⭐
- On the basis of a **dream** the Prophet ﷺ saw, the Companions set out joyfully for Makkah to perform **'umrah**.
- When they **were stopped**, they were **forced** to be ready to fight a great army with only **their travelling swords**.
- The pledge the Companions made, accepting this **wholeheartedly**, = **Bay'at ash-Shajarah (the Pledge under the Tree)** = **Bay'at ar-Ridwan (the Pledge of Pleasure).**
> 📖 **(98:8 – al-Bayyinah) – meaning:** "...**Allah is pleased with them, and they are pleased with Him.**"

> ⚠️ The textbook links this verse (98:8) with Bay'at ar-Ridwan; the verse that speaks directly of Bay'at ar-Ridwan is known to be 48:18 (Surah al-Fath) – confirm with the teacher.

### Sayings of scholars
- **Imam al-Qushayri (RH)** (meaning): "Whoever wishes to reach the station of rida, let him hold to **what pleases Allah**."
- **Fudayl ibn 'Iyad (RH)** (meaning): "The contentment of **rida is higher than detachment from the world**, because the contented person **does not desire anything** beyond his station."

---

## 6. Qalbun salim (قلب سليم) – a sound, pure heart ⭐
- The four spiritual qualities above **are linked with one another**; all of them **purify the heart**.
- They bring the servant's heart to the pure state called **qalbun salim**.
- When the servant meets Allah in the Hereafter, Allah expects **the heart that carries the ruh He gave** to have reached that pure state – that is **the state of success.**
> 📖 **(26:88–89 – ash-Shu'ara') – meaning:** "The Day when **neither wealth nor children will benefit**, except whoever comes to Allah **with a sound heart**."

## ✅ Quick revision
- Human = earth (body) + ruh (soul) – 38:71–72; iman = the first step to a healthy soul.
- Practices: prayer, zakat, fasting, hajj, Quran recitation, dhikr, reflecting on creation.
- **Sabr** = the noble quality that restrains bad traits; 7:126, 2:155–156; "patience is light" (Muslim).
- **Tawakkul** = entrusting; effort + trust; 8:2, 3:173, 3:159; hasbiyallahu wa ni'mal-wakil (Ibrahim AS).
- **Ma'rifah** = knowledge; knowing Allah as if seeing Him, with deep joy – Imam al-Ghazali, 'Abd al-Qadir al-Jilani.
- **Rida** = contentment; Bay'at ar-Ridwan; 98:8; Imam al-Qushayri, Fudayl ibn 'Iyad.
- **Qalbun salim** – 26:88–89.
