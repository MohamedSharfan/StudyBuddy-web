Marks: not stated on the paper (Islam I multiple-choice question)

On which occasion is the du'a "Bismillahi tawakkaltu 'ala Allah" recited?

- **A.** on waking from sleep
- **B.** on entering the mosque
- **C.** on coming out of the toilet
- **D.** on leaving the house

**Correct answer:** D – on leaving the house *(option (4) on the original paper)*

### B1. 2017 – Islam II, Q5 (ii)

Question ID: P2017-II-Q5.2  
Year: 2017  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q5 (ii)  
Marks: 10 marks for the whole of Q5  
Source: OL_2017_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(ii) Point out three occasions that call for patience, and the three forms of patience to be shown on them.

**Suggested answer:**

| Occasion | Form of patience |
|---|---|
| When anger comes | Restraining oneself |
| When trials come | Bearing them |
| When the heart is drawn towards sin | Keeping away from it |
(Also: greed – being content; the urge for revenge – forgiving; weariness in good deeds – standing firm)

**Answer explanation:** Each occasion must be written as a pair with its form.

### B2. 2018 – Islam II, Q1 (i)

Question ID: P2018-II-Q1.1  
Year: 2018  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q1 (i)  
Marks: 20 marks for the whole of Q1 (no separate marks given for sub-questions)  
Source: OL_2018_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

Give short answers.
(i) What were the words Prophet Ibrahim (AS) said when he was thrown into the fire?

**Suggested answer:**

حَسْبِيَ اللهُ وَنِعْمَ الْوَكِيْلُ – "Hasbiya Allahu wa ni'ma al-wakil" (Allah is sufficient for me, and He is the best disposer of affairs).

**Answer explanation:** This is the highest expression of tawakkul (Bukhari).
