# Grade 11 – Chapter 13: Ways of Spiritual Development – Flashcards

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_13/Flashcards.md` (Tamil), cards 1–17 in the same order.

---

Flashcard 1

Front: روح (Ruh)

Back: The soul – breathed in from Allah; its health depends on Allah.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 13 | Topic: Body and soul

---

Flashcard 2

Front: The origins of the body and the soul?

Back: Body – from the earth; soul – from Allah (38:71–72).

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 13 | Topic: Body and soul

---

Flashcard 3

Front: What are the four spiritual qualities?

Back: Sabr (patience), tawakkul (reliance on Allah), ma'rifah (knowing Allah), rida (contentment).

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 13 | Topic: Four qualities

---

Flashcard 4

Front: Seven practices that purify the soul?

Back: Prayer, zakat, fasting, hajj, reciting the Quran, dhikr, reflecting on creation.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 13 | Topic: Practices

---

Flashcard 5

Front: الصبر (As-sabr)

Back: Patience – a strong quality that restrains haste, anger, panic, greed and revenge.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 13 | Topic: Sabr

---

Flashcard 6

Front: Anger / greed / revenge – the forms of patience?

Back: Restraining / being content / forgiving.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 13 | Topic: Sabr

---

Flashcard 7

Front: التوكل (At-tawakkul)

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 13 | Topic: Tawakkul

---

Flashcard 8

Front: سبب (Sabab)

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 13 | Topic: Tawakkul
