# Grade 11 – Chapter 13: Ways of Spiritual Development – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_13/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q20 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. According to the textbook, a human being is formed from the combination of what?

A. A handful of earth from the ground and the ruh breathed in from Allah
B. Water and fire
C. Light and air
D. Fire and earth

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: 38:71–72 – He created him from clay and breathed into him of His ruh. The body is from the earth; the soul is from Allah.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. What is the first step to making the soul healthy?

A. Exercise
B. Believing in Allah
C. Good food
D. Sleeping a lot

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Since the soul came from Allah, its health depends on Allah; so iman is the first step.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q3. Which of the following is NOT one of the bad traits that "sabr" restrains?

A. Haste
B. Gratitude
C. Revenge
D. Anger

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Patience restrains bad traits such as haste, anger, panic, greed and revenge. Gratitude is a good quality.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q4. In which verse is the supplication "Our Lord! Pour patience upon us"?

A. 2:155
B. 7:126
C. 8:2
D. 3:159

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: 7:126 (al-A'raf) – a supplication asking Allah for patience.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q5. What do the patient say when hardship strikes (2:156)?

A. This is not our fate
B. We belong to Allah, and to Him we return
C. Someone plotted this
D. Why are we being tested?

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: "Inna lillahi wa inna ilayhi raji'un" – the words of the patient.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q6. What form of patience should be practised when greed comes?

A. Being content
B. Restraining it
C. Forgiving
D. Facing it

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Anger – restraining; greed – being content; revenge – forgiving; problems – facing them.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q7. What is the form of patience when the urge for revenge arises?

A. Bearing it
B. Keeping away
C. Forgiving
D. Being content

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Controlling the urge for revenge and forgiving is patience in that situation.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q8. How did the Prophet ﷺ describe patience (Muslim)?

A. Patience is an ocean
B. Patience is a sword
C. Patience is light
D. Patience is a mountain

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Because patience lights up a person's path to success, it is described as "light".

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q9. What is the literal meaning of "tawakkul"?

A. Knowledge
B. Patience
C. Being pleased (content)
D. Entrusting (handing over responsibility)

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Tawakkul = entrusting; ma'rifah = knowledge; rida = contentment; sabr = patience.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q10. What does a believer with true tawakkul do about the asbab (means)?

A. Ignores the means completely
B. Puts all his trust in the means
C. Hands responsibility to other people
D. Uses the means and entrusts the outcome to Allah

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Allah has made success depend on the means, but He alone decides the outcome. Trusting the means alone is the way of those who do not believe in Allah.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q11. Which is one of the reasons the believers rely on Allah?

A. People cannot be trusted
B. No effort is needed
C. Everything happens by itself
D. The means are also Allah's arrangement; without His will, success cannot come from the means alone

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: The other reason: certainty that by trusting Allah they will be rewarded in this world or the Hereafter.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q12. What were the last words of Ibrahim (AS) when he was thrown into the fire (Bukhari)?

A. La ilaha illallah
B. Inna lillahi wa inna ilayhi raji'un
C. Allahu akbar
D. Hasbiyallahu wa ni'mal-wakil

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: "Allah is sufficient for me, and He is the best Guardian" – the highest expression of tawakkul.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q13. According to the hadith in Abu Dawud, which is NOT among the things gained by one who says "Bismillahi tawakkaltu 'ala Allah…" when leaving home?

A. The reward of fasting that day
B. He is taken care of
C. Satan goes away from him
D. He is guided

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Guidance, being taken care of, being protected, and Satan going away – these are what the hadith mentions.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q14. What is the meaning of "ma'rifah" in Islamic spiritual usage?

A. Gaining worldly knowledge
B. Attaining deep joy of the heart by knowing Allah as if seeing Him
C. Earning money
D. Writing poetry

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Linguistically ma'rifah = knowledge; spiritually, knowing Allah deeply and gaining deep joy of the heart.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q15. Who said, "The pleasure of the heart comes from knowing Allah"?

A. Fudayl ibn 'Iyad
B. Imam al-Qushayri
C. Imam al-Ghazali
D. Imam an-Nawawi

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Imam al-Ghazali (RH) – the pleasure of the eye is in a beautiful sight, of the ear in a beautiful sound, of the heart in knowing Allah.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q16. Who said, "You will become fit to be a vessel that carries the knowledge of Allah"?

A. Imam Malik
B. Imam al-Ghazali
C. Imam ash-Shafi'i
D. Muhyiddin 'Abd al-Qadir al-Jilani

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: 'Abd al-Qadir al-Jilani (RH) said that one who empties himself of creation, desires and his own will becomes a vessel that carries the knowledge of Allah.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q17. What does "rida" mean?

A. Being content and satisfied with every situation that comes by Allah's arrangement
B. Giving up everything
C. Pleasing other people
D. Gathering wealth

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: In the state of rida, Allah is pleased with the servant and the servant is pleased with Allah.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q18. What is the name of the pledge through which the Companions reached the station of rida?

A. The Pledge of 'Aqabah
B. The Charter of Madinah
C. Bay'at ash-Shajarah (Bay'at ar-Ridwan)
D. The Covenant of 'Umar

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: When they set out for 'umrah and were stopped, they wholeheartedly accepted being forced to be ready to fight – the Pledge under the Tree / the Pledge of Pleasure.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q19. Who said, "The contentment of rida is higher than detachment from the world"?

A. Fudayl ibn 'Iyad
B. Imam al-Qushayri
C. Imam al-Ghazali
D. 'Abd al-Qadir al-Jilani

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Fudayl ibn 'Iyad (RH) explained that the contented person desires nothing beyond his station. Imam al-Qushayri – whoever wants rida should hold to what pleases Allah.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q20. "On that Day neither wealth nor children will benefit, except whoever comes to Allah with a sound heart" – what is this sound heart called?

A. Qalbun salim
B. An-nafs al-ammarah
C. An-nafs al-lawwamah
D. Ma'rifah

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: 26:88–89 – qalbun salim (a sound, pure heart). The four spiritual qualities bring the heart to this state.

Difficulty: Medium
