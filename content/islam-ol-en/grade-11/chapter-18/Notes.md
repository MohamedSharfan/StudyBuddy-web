# Grade 11 – Chapter 18: Al-Khulafa' ar-Rashidun – Caliph Abu Bakr as-Siddiq (RA)

> 📖 **Opening hadith of the chapter (Ahmad):** فَعَلَيْكُمْ بِسُنَّتِيْ وَسُنَّةِ الْخُلَفَاءِ الرَّاشِدِيْنَ الْمَهْدِيِّيْنَ، فَتَمَسَّكُوْا بِهَا وَعَضُّوْا عَلَيْهَا بِالنَّوَاجِذِ
> **Meaning:** "Hold fast to my Sunnah and the Sunnah of the rightly guided caliphs; **bite onto it with your molar teeth.**"

---

## 1. Al-Khulafa' ar-Rashidun (الخلفاء الراشدون) ⭐
- **The four Companions** who ruled after the Prophet's ﷺ death.
- All their affairs – personal, family, religious, social, political, economic, educational and cultural – **sought Allah's pleasure, and were honest and unselfish, following the guidance of Allah and His Messenger**.
- So the Prophet ﷺ **foretold** that they would be **"the rightly guided caliphs"**.

| Order | Caliph |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Abu Bakr (RA)** |
| 2 | **'Umar (RA)** |
| 3 | **'Uthman (RA)** |
| 4 | **'Ali (RA)** |

---

## 2. Abu Bakr (RA) – a short life history ⭐

| Point | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Own name** | **'Abdullah ibn Abi Quhafah** |
| **Born** | **573 CE, Makkah** |
| In the Jahiliyyah | **An excellent trader**; **a respected leader** in society |
| Relationship with the Prophet | **Friends even before** the Prophet was chosen as a prophet |
| Accepting Islam | When the Prophet ﷺ told him, he accepted **without any hesitation**; **the first of the men** to accept Islam |
| Caliphate | Took office in **11 AH**; ruled for **two years and three months** |
| Death | **13 AH**, at the age of **63** |

### Events that show his excellence

#### (1) The disagreement with 'Umar (RA)
- A small disagreement arose between Abu Bakr and 'Umar (RA); the Prophet's ﷺ face **turned red with anger.**
- Abu Bakr (RA), afraid, said: "O Prophet, **I was the one who wronged him.**"
- The Prophet ﷺ said to those present (**twice**) (meaning): "When Allah sent me as a messenger, you all said **I was lying**; but **Abu Bakr confirmed my truth**, and gave **his life and all his wealth** for me. Will you **set apart from me** such a friend of mine?"
- **Abu ad-Darda' (RA):** after this, no one ever treated him in a way that **hurt** him.

#### (2) Da'wah on the very first day of accepting Islam
On the day he accepted Islam he brought some leading people of Makkah to the Prophet and they accepted Islam:
**'Uthman, Talhah, az-Zubayr, Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas, 'Abd ar-Rahman ibn 'Awf (RA).**

#### (3) The title "as-Siddiq" ⭐
- When he heard the news that the Prophet ﷺ had gone on the **Mi'raj**, he **confirmed it without the slightest hesitation.**
- That is why he was given the title **"as-Siddiq" (the one who confirmed the truth)**.

---

## 3. His personal qualities ⭐

| Situation | Quality shown |
|---|---|
| He stood by the Prophet **during the hijrah**; gave **brave support** in a time of crisis | **Courage, loyalty** |
| When the Companions **were shaken** by the news of the Prophet's ﷺ death, he acted **very calmly**, **comforted** the people and brought things back to normal | **Calmness, leadership** |
| In the difficult early days of the caliphate he protected Islam and the Islamic state through **firm and wise decisions** | **Firmness, wisdom** |

👉 From the time he accepted Islam until his death, he devoted **his whole life** to it.

---

## 4. How he was chosen as caliph ⭐
- **No caliph was chosen or appointed** while the Prophet ﷺ was alive.
- On the day the Prophet ﷺ died, **the Ansar** gathered at **"Saqifat Bani Sa'idah"** to discuss choosing a leader.
- Some Companions who heard of this went there and joined them.
- There **Abu Bakr (RA)** was accepted as caliph, and those gathered gave him **bay'ah (pledge of allegiance)**.

### Evidence that he was the most qualified ⭐
| Evidence (meaning) | Source |
|---|---|
| In his last days, when illness kept the Prophet ﷺ from even standing, **although many Companions including 'Umar (RA) were present, he told Abu Bakr (RA) to lead the prayer** | — |
| On the last day, when the Prophet came to the prayer supported on the shoulders of **'Abbas (RA) and 'Ali (RA)**, Abu Bakr (RA), who was leading the prayer, **tried to step back into the row**; the Prophet ﷺ **stopped him**, sat down and prayed following him | Bukhari |
| "Take **Abu Bakr and 'Umar**, who will come after me one after the other, as **your guides**" | Tirmidhi |
| A woman asked: "What if I come and do not find you (you have died)?" – the Prophet ﷺ: "**If you do not find me, go to Abu Bakr**" | Bukhari |

## 5. His services and contributions (7) ⭐
1. **Taking charge of the caliphate** after the Prophet's ﷺ death and **continuing good government.**
2. Defeating the challenge of **the false prophets** with the help of experienced **commanders**, proving **the strength of the caliphate**.
3. Defeating **the plot of those who refused to pay zakat**.
4. **Compiling the Quran into a single book.**
5. **Appointing** many governors with political experience and **stabilising** the administration.
6. Defeating the threats to the Islamic world from **rulers on the frontiers**.
7. **Appointing the most suitable person as caliph** to rule after him.

👉 He stabilised the Islamic state **within two years**.

## ✅ Quick revision
- Al-Khulafa' ar-Rashidun = Abu Bakr, 'Umar, 'Uthman, 'Ali (RA); the command to hold to their Sunnah (Ahmad).
- 'Abdullah ibn Abi Quhafah; 573 CE Makkah; trader; the first man to accept Islam.
- On the first day: 'Uthman, Talhah, az-Zubayr, Sa'd, 'Abd ar-Rahman (RA) entered Islam.
- "As-Siddiq" for confirming the Mi'raj.
- Bay'ah at Saqifat Bani Sa'idah; 11 AH; 2 years 3 months; died 13 AH aged 63.
- Evidence of qualification: leading the prayer in the Prophet's place (Bukhari); follow Abu Bakr and 'Umar (Tirmidhi); "If you do not find me, go to Abu Bakr" (Bukhari).
- 7 contributions: good government, false prophets, zakat-refusers, compiling the Quran, governors, frontier security, appointing the next caliph.
