## Part A: Multiple-Choice Questions (Islam I)

### A1. 2017 – Islam I, Q10

Question ID: P2017-I-Q10  
Year: 2017  
Paper and question: Islam I, Q10  
Marks: not stated on the paper (Islam I multiple-choice question)  
Source: OL_2017_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

Which of the following statements about al-Khulafa' ar-Rashidun is WRONG?

- **A.** They were given the good news of Paradise.
- **B.** They were related to the Prophet by marriage.
- **C.** Each was known by a special title.
- **D.** They all had Madinah as their capital.

**Correct answer:** D – They all had Madinah as their capital. *(option (4) on the original paper)*

**Explanation:** 'Ali (RA) had Kufa as his seat of government, so the statement that all four had Madinah as their capital is wrong. All four were given the good news of Paradise; all were related to the Prophet by marriage; they have titles such as as-Siddiq, al-Faruq, Dhun-Nurayn and Asadullah.

Marks: not stated on the paper (Islam I multiple-choice question)

What task was carried out by Caliph Abu Bakr (RA)?

- **A.** introducing the Hijri year
- **B.** compiling the Quran into a book
- **C.** setting up government departments
- **D.** conquering Egypt

**Correct answer:** B – compiling the Quran into a book *(option (2) on the original paper)*

**Explanation:** Compiling the Quran into a book was the work of Abu Bakr (RA). The Hijri year, government departments and the conquest of Egypt – the time of 'Umar (RA).

### B1. 2017 – Islam II, Q4 (iii)

Question ID: P2017-II-Q4.2  
Year: 2017  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q4 (iii)  
Marks: 10 marks for the whole of Q4  
Source: OL_2017_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(iii) Briefly explain five tasks Abu Bakr (RA) carried out during his caliphate.

**Suggested answer:**

1. Taking up the caliphate after the Prophet's ﷺ death and continuing good government.
2. Defeating the challenges of the false prophets (such as Musaylimah) through experienced commanders.
3. Defeating the plot of those who refused to pay zakat.
4. After huffaz were martyred in the Battle of Yamamah, compiling the Quran into a book on 'Umar's (RA) advice.
5. Appointing experienced governors and stabilising the administration.
6. Defeating threats on the frontiers; appointing 'Umar (RA) as a suitable caliph to follow him.

**Answer explanation:** As five tasks are asked for, explain each in one line.

### B4. 2025 (2026) – Islam II, Q4 (i)

Question ID: P2025_2026-II-Q4.1  
Year: 2025 (2026)  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q4 (i)  
Marks: 10 marks for the whole of Q4 (division among sub-questions not given)  
Source: OL_2025_2026_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(i) State two incidents that justify Abu Bakr (RA) being qualified to be appointed the first caliph.

**Suggested answer:**

Any two of the following:
1. When the Prophet ﷺ was ill in his last days, he told Abu Bakr (RA) to lead the prayer, although many others including 'Umar (RA) were present.
2. When the Prophet ﷺ came to the prayer on his last day, Abu Bakr (RA) tried to step back, but the Prophet ﷺ stopped him and prayed following him (Bukhari).
3. "After me, take Abu Bakr and 'Umar as your guides" (Tirmidhi).
4. Telling a woman, "If you do not find me, go to Abu Bakr" (Bukhari).

**Answer explanation:** The last incident was also asked in 2021 (2022) Q25 (MCQ).

**Marking guidance (suggested only – not an official marking scheme):** Two incidents.
