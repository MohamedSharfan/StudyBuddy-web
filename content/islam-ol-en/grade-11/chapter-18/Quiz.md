# Grade 11 – Chapter 18: Caliph Abu Bakr as-Siddiq (RA) – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_18/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q15 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. Who are called "al-Khulafa' ar-Rashidun"?

A. The Umayyad caliphs
B. The Abbasid caliphs
C. The four Companions who ruled after the Prophet's ﷺ death
D. The four madhhab imams

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Abu Bakr, 'Umar, 'Uthman and 'Ali (RA) – the Prophet ﷺ foretold that they would be the rightly guided caliphs.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. According to the hadith in Ahmad, what must be "bitten onto with the molar teeth"?

A. Only the Quran
B. The madhhabs
C. Wealth
D. The Prophet's Sunnah and the Sunnah of the rightly guided caliphs

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: This hadith shows that the way of the rightly guided caliphs is also an example to be followed.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q3. What was Abu Bakr's (RA) own name?

A. 'Abd ar-Rahman ibn 'Awf
B. 'Abdullah ibn Abi Quhafah
C. 'Umar ibn al-Khattab
D. 'Abdullah ibn 'Umar

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: 'Abdullah ibn Abi Quhafah – born in Makkah in 573 CE.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q4. In which year was Abu Bakr (RA) born?

A. 573 CE
B. 570 CE
C. 583 CE
D. 610 CE

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: 573 CE, Makkah.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q5. Why was Abu Bakr (RA) given the title "as-Siddiq"?

A. Because he gave a lot of wealth
B. Because he compiled the Quran
C. Because he was the first caliph
D. Because he confirmed without hesitation the news that the Prophet ﷺ had gone on the Mi'raj

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: As-Siddiq = the one who confirmed the truth. On hearing the news of the Mi'raj he believed it without the slightest hesitation.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q6. Which of the following was NOT among the leading Makkans whom Abu Bakr (RA) brought into Islam on the day he accepted it?

A. 'Uthman (RA)
B. Talhah (RA)
C. 'Abd ar-Rahman ibn 'Awf (RA)
D. 'Umar (RA)

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: The list is 'Uthman, Talhah, az-Zubayr, Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas and 'Abd ar-Rahman ibn 'Awf (RA).

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q7. During the disagreement with 'Umar (RA), what was the essence of what the Prophet ﷺ said about Abu Bakr (RA)?

A. He did wrong
B. When everyone called me a liar he confirmed my truth and gave his life and wealth; will you set apart such a friend?
C. Keep him away
D. He must not become caliph

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: He said it twice; after this, Abu ad-Darda' (RA) said, no one ever treated Abu Bakr in a way that hurt him.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q8. What personal quality did Abu Bakr (RA) show when the Companions were shaken by the Prophet's ﷺ death?

A. Acting calmly, comforting the people and bringing things back to normal
B. Anger
C. Silence
D. Fear

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Calmness and leadership in a time of crisis showed his character.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q9. Where did the Ansar gather on the day the Prophet ﷺ died to discuss choosing a leader?

A. Dar al-Arqam
B. Masjid Quba'
C. Saqifat Bani Sa'idah
D. Shi'b Abi Talib

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Some Companions also joined them there, accepted Abu Bakr (RA) as caliph and gave him bay'ah.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q10. Which of the following is NOT evidence that Abu Bakr (RA) was the most qualified to be caliph?

A. The Prophet ﷺ told him to lead the prayer when he was ill
B. That he was the oldest person
C. "If you do not find me, go to Abu Bakr" (Bukhari)
D. "Take Abu Bakr and 'Umar as your guides" (Tirmidhi)

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Age is not given as evidence; the Prophet's instructions and words are the evidence.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q11. On whose shoulders was the Prophet ﷺ supported when he came to the prayer on his last day (Bukhari)?

A. Abu Bakr and 'Umar (RA)
B. 'Uthman and 'Ali (RA)
C. 'Abbas and 'Ali (RA)
D. Bilal and Anas (RA)

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: When Abu Bakr (RA), who was leading the prayer, tried to step back, the Prophet ﷺ stopped him, sat down and prayed following him.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q12. In which year did Abu Bakr (RA) take up the caliphate, and for how long did he rule?

A. 23 AH; twelve years
B. 13 AH; ten years
C. 11 AH; two years and three months
D. 35 AH; five years

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: He took office in 11 AH, ruled for about two and a half years, and died in 13 AH at the age of 63.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q13. Which of the following was NOT one of Abu Bakr's (RA) contributions?

A. Defeating the challenge of the false prophets
B. Making eight copies and sending them to the provinces
C. Compiling the Quran into a single book
D. Defeating the plot of those who refused to pay zakat

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Sending eight copies was in 'Uthman's (RA) time; Abu Bakr (RA) first compiled the Quran into one collection.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q14. At what age did Abu Bakr (RA) die?

A. 63
B. 60
C. 65
D. 70

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: He died in 13 AH at the age of 63 (the same age as the Prophet ﷺ).

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q15. Who was the first of the men to accept Islam?

A. Abu Bakr (RA)
B. 'Umar (RA)
C. 'Uthman (RA)
D. 'Ali (RA)

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Abu Bakr (RA), who had been the Prophet's friend even before prophethood, accepted Islam without any hesitation.

Difficulty: Easy
