Grade: 11 | Chapter: 18 | Topic: Key terms

Flashcard 3

Front: Abu Bakr (RA) – own name and birth?

Back: 'Abdullah ibn Abi Quhafah; 573 CE, Makkah.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 18 | Topic: Life history

---

Flashcard 4

Front: الصديق (As-Siddiq)

Back: The one who confirmed the truth – Abu Bakr's (RA) title, because he confirmed the news of the Mi'raj without hesitation.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 18 | Topic: Life history

---

Flashcard 5

Front: The five who accepted Islam through Abu Bakr (RA)?

Back: 'Uthman, Talhah, az-Zubayr, Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas, 'Abd ar-Rahman ibn 'Awf (RA).

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 18 | Topic: Life history

---

Flashcard 6

Front: سقيفة بني ساعدة (Saqifat Bani Sa'idah)

Back: The place where the Ansar gathered on the day of the Prophet's death and gave bay'ah to Abu Bakr (RA).

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 18 | Topic: Choosing the caliph

---

Flashcard 7

Front: Abu Bakr (RA) – length of caliphate and death?

Back: 11 AH – 2 years 3 months; died in 13 AH aged 63.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 18 | Topic: Life history

---

Flashcard 8

Front: Three hadith proofs of Abu Bakr's (RA) qualification?

Back: Made imam when the Prophet was ill (Bukhari); take Abu Bakr and 'Umar as guides (Tirmidhi); "If you do not find me, go to Abu Bakr" (Bukhari).

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 18 | Topic: Choosing the caliph

---

Flashcard 9

Front: Abu Bakr's (RA) seven contributions?

Back: Continuing good government; defeating false prophets; defeating zakat-refusers; compiling the Quran; appointing governors; frontier security; appointing the next caliph.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 18 | Topic: Contributions

---

Flashcard 10

Front: Abu Bakr's (RA) three personal qualities?

Back: Courage in the hijrah; calmness at the Prophet's death; firmness and wisdom in the caliphate.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 18 | Topic: Personal qualities

---

Flashcard 11

Front: What did Abu ad-Darda' (RA) say about Abu Bakr (RA)?

Back: After the Prophet's rebuke, no one ever treated Abu Bakr in a way that hurt him.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 18 | Topic: Life history
