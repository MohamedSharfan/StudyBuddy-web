**Question:**

(ii) The national contribution of Muslims in Sri Lanka

**Suggested answer:**

1. Trade – pioneers in exporting gems, ivory and spices; trade advisers to the kings (4 Muslim advisers at the court of Parakramabahu the Great).
2. Transport – introduced the "tavalam" transport system.
3. Defence of the country – against the Portuguese, they obtained military help for King Mayadunne from the Zamorin of Calicut and also fought themselves.
4. The independence struggle – leaders such as T. B. Jayah joined the majority community and contributed to independence in 1948.
5. Education and social revival – the work of Siddi Lebbe, Jayah and others.

**Answer explanation:** It is best to mention contributions in several fields.

### B4. 2020 – Islam II, Q7 (iii)

Question ID: P2020-II-Q7.3  
Year: 2020  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q7 (iii)  
Marks: 10 marks (short notes on two topics)  
Source: OL_2020_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(iii) The history of the introduction of Islam to Sri Lanka

**Suggested answer:**

- Even before Islam (from BCE times) the Arabs had trade links with Sri Lanka; the reasons were Sri Lanka's location on the East–West trade route and the Arabs' experience of sea travel.
- According to "Muslim Nesan" (Siddi Lebbe), the Prophet ﷺ sent Wahb ibn Abi Habsa (RA) to the king of Sri Lanka in 628 CE.
- "'Aja'ib al-Hind" (Ibn Shahriyar): the account of the envoy sent from Sri Lanka in the time of 'Umar (RA).
- "Futuh al-Buldan" (al-Baladhuri): the account of Dathopatissa II sending the widows and orphans of Arab traders.
- Fearing al-Hajjaj's cruelty, the Banu Hashim settled on the coasts of Sri Lanka (Tennent – "Ceylon").
- The preacher Khalid ibn Bakaya, sent by the caliph in Baghdad (gravestone of 337 AH); Arab coins and gravestones are evidence.

**Answer explanation:** Write in chronological order, with evidence.

1. Exporting gems, ivory and spices abroad.
2. Earning foreign exchange through exports and increasing the country's income; encouraging people to take up production.

4. Working for agricultural improvement; taking part in farming themselves.
5. Bringing weavers from abroad and developing weaving.
6. Introducing and managing the "tavalam" transport system.
7. Advising the government on import and export trade (4 Muslim trade advisers at Parakramabahu's court).

**Answer explanation:** In (iii) explain each contribution in one sentence.
