# Grade 11 – Chapter 22: History and Contribution of Sri Lankan Muslims – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_22/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q18 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. What led the Arabs to make contact with Sri Lanka?

A. Sri Lanka's deserts
B. Sri Lanka's military strength
C. Sri Lanka's location, and the Arabs' knowledge and experience of sea travel
D. A famine in Arabia

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Sri Lanka lay in the middle of the East–West trade route; the Arabs were experts in sea travel.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. Who stated that the Arabs on the Malabar coast and in Sri Lanka were experts in maritime activities?

A. Ptolemy
B. al-Idrisi
C. Pliny
D. Tennent

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Pliny (23–79 CE). Ptolemy (150 CE) – the knowledge of Sri Lanka in his map was obtained from the Arabs.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q3. According to M. C. Siddi Lebbe's "Muslim Nesan", which Companion did the Prophet ﷺ send to the king of Sri Lanka?

A. Mu'adh ibn Jabal
B. Wahb ibn Abi Habsa
C. Mus'ab ibn 'Umayr
D. Khalid ibn Bakaya

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Wahb ibn Abi Habsa was sent in 628 CE and returned home in 632 CE.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q4. In which year was Wahb ibn Abi Habsa sent to Sri Lanka?

A. 610 CE
B. 632 CE
C. 628 CE
D. 715 CE

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: 628 CE – sent to the king of Sri Lanka; 632 CE – returned home.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q5. Who wrote "'Aja'ib al-Hind", which helps us infer that Islam may have been introduced to Sri Lanka in the time of 'Umar (RA)?

A. Ibn Shahriyar
B. al-Baladhuri
C. Thomas Arnold
D. James Emerson Tennent

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: The envoy sent by the Arabs of Sri Lanka learned about Islam from 'Umar (RA) and died on the Makran coast on his way back; his servant reached Sri Lanka and passed on the information.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q6. Where did the envoy sent from Sri Lanka die on his way back (according to Ibn Shahriyar)?

A. Madinah
B. The Makran coast, Pakistan
C. Yemen
D. The Maldives

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: The servant who went with him reached Sri Lanka and passed on the information about Islam.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q7. Who wrote the book "Ceylon", which mentions that the Banu Hashim, fearing al-Hajjaj's cruelty, settled on the coasts of Sri Lanka?

A. al-Idrisi
B. al-Baladhuri
C. S. Paranavitana
D. Sir James Emerson Tennent

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: This happened in the 8th century CE, in the time of the Umayyad caliphs 'Abd al-Malik and al-Walid.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q8. Which Sri Lankan king sent the widows and orphans of Arab traders who had died in Sri Lanka back to Arabia?

A. Parakramabahu the Great
B. Dathopatissa II
C. Mayadunne
D. The Kandyan king

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: This event is in al-Baladhuri's book "Futuh al-Buldan".

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q9. Where were the women and the ship from Sri Lanka captured by pirates?

A. Aden
B. Makran
C. Calicut
D. Debal, near Karachi

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: They were captured at Debal; Raja Dahir, king of Sindh, ignored al-Hajjaj's request.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q10. In which year, and under whose leadership, was Sindh conquered by the Muslims?

A. 715 CE – Muhammad ibn Qasim
B. 628 CE – Wahb ibn Abi Habsa
C. 636 CE – 'Umar (RA)
D. 1186 CE – Parakramabahu

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Because al-Hajjaj's request was ignored, an army was sent under Muhammad ibn Qasim, and Sindh was conquered in 715 CE.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q11. Whose is the gravestone set up in 337 AH and found in 1787?

A. Khalid ibn Bakaya
B. Wahb ibn Abi Habsa
C. Ibn Shahriyar
D. Abu al-Aswad

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Khalid ibn Bakaya was the preacher sent to Colombo by the caliph in Baghdad (Abbasid) at the request of the Sri Lankan Muslims.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q12. What confirms that Muslims lived in Sri Lanka in the 2nd and 3rd centuries AH?

A. Chinese records
B. Only Sinhala literature
C. European maps
D. Arab coins found in Sri Lanka

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Arab coins, gravestones and inscriptions are the evidence.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q13. Why did the early Muslims gain a monopoly in Sri Lanka's import and export trade?

A. They seized it by military force
B. They did not pay taxes
C. Their good character – they had no wish to occupy the land, behaved courteously with the local kings and people, and provided services
D. They were greater in number

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Through their good character they gained the monopoly and also became trade advisers to the government.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q14. Which of the following was NOT one of the coastal towns where Sri Lankan Muslims first settled?

A. Colombo
B. Beruwala
C. Matara
D. Gampola

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: At first: Colombo, Beruwala, Galle, Matara; later: Kurunegala, Gampola, the central hill country.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q15. Which transport system did Sri Lankan Muslims introduce and manage?

A. Tavalam
B. Railway
C. Palanquin
D. Ships only

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: They introduced the system called "tavalam" into the transport methods existing in the country until then.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q16. How many Muslims served as trade advisers at the court of Parakramabahu the Great (1156–1186)?

A. 2
B. 4
C. 7
D. 10

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Professor S. Paranavitana confirms what the geographer al-Idrisi reports.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q17. Which Sri Lankan king sent Muslims as envoys to obtain the help of the Zamorin, king of Calicut, to drive out the Portuguese?

A. Dathopatissa II
B. Mayadunne
C. Parakramabahu the Great
D. Vimaladharmasuriya

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: The Muslims obtained military help and also joined the army and fought themselves.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q18. In which year was the self-rule ended in 1815 regained?

A. 1948
B. 1915
C. 1956
D. 1972

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: The voice raised by Muslim leaders including T. B. Jayah, together with the majority community, was among the factors behind independence in 1948.

Difficulty: Easy
