### B2. 2019 – Islam II, Q5 (iii)

Question ID: P2019-II-Q5.3  
Year: 2019  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q5 (iii)  
Marks: 10 marks for the whole of Q5  
Source: OL_2019_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(iii) State what fiqh is, and briefly introduce the four imams of the madhhabs.

**Suggested answer:**

Fiqh (الفقه) = understanding; the discipline covering the Islamic laws derived from the Quran, the Sunnah and ijtihad; the first of the Islamic sciences to arise.
1. Imam Abu Hanifah (80–150 AH): Nu'man ibn Thabit; born in Kufa; "al-Imam al-A'zam"; his teacher Hammad; "al-Fiqh al-Akbar"; madrasat ar-ra'y.
2. Imam Malik (93–179 AH): Malik ibn Anas; Madinah; "Imam al-Madinah"; his book "al-Muwatta'"; madrasat al-athar.
3. Imam ash-Shafi'i (150–204 AH): Muhammad ibn Idris; Gaza (Palestine); "'Alim Quraysh"; his books "al-Umm" and "ar-Risalah"; he combined athar and ra'y; most Sri Lankan Muslims follow this madhhab.
4. Imam Ahmad ibn Hanbal (164–241 AH): Baghdad; his teacher Imam ash-Shafi'i; his book "Musnad Ahmad"; madrasat al-athar; he struggled against the Mu'tazilah.

**Answer explanation:** For each imam mention some of: name, period, birthplace, book, distinction.

**Marking guidance (suggested only – not an official marking scheme):** No official marking scheme.

**Answer status:** Suggested – not an official answer (no official marking scheme was supplied); based on the Grade 10/11 textbook content

### B5. 2023 (2024) – Islam II, Q7 (i)

Question ID: P2023_2024-II-Q7.1  
Year: 2023 (2024)  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q7 (i)  
Marks: 10 marks (short notes on two topics)  
Source: OL_2023_2024_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

Write short notes on any two of the following.
(i) Imam ash-Shafi'i (RH)

**Suggested answer:**

- 150–204 AH; his name was Muhammad ibn Idris; born in Gaza, Palestine; of Qurayshi descent; title "'Alim Quraysh".
- Educated in Makkah and Madinah; his teachers – Imam Malik and Imam Abu Yusuf (as in the textbook); government service in Yemen; mufti and teacher in Baghdad; later Egypt.
- Besides the Sunnah and fiqh, Arabic literature and medicine; a poet.
- Books: Musnad al-Imam ash-Shafi'i, Kitab al-Umm, ar-Risalah.
- A progressive approach that took in both madrasat al-athar and madrasat ar-ra'y; his rulings in Baghdad = qadim (old), his rulings in Egypt = jadid (new).
- The Shafi'i madhhab – the official madhhab of Egypt in the time of Salah ad-Din; followed by most people in Sri Lanka, Indonesia, Malaysia and Egypt.

**Answer explanation:** Include his life history, education, books, approach and the spread of his madhhab.
