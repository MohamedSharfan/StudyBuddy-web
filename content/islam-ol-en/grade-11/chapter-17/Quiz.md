# Grade 11 – Chapter 17: The Science of Fiqh – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_17/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q20 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. What does the word "fiqh" mean?

A. Understanding
B. Belief
C. History
D. Recitation

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Fiqh = understanding; it contains the laws derived on the basis of the Quran, the Sunnah and ijtihad.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. Which Islamic science, based on ijtihad, was the first to appear?

A. Hadith
B. Tajwid
C. Tafsir
D. Fiqh

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: The science of fiqh is regarded as the foremost and the first of the Islamic sciences to appear.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q3. Which period is called the "golden age" of fiqh?

A. The time of the Companions
B. The time of the Tabi' al-Tabi'in
C. The time of the Tabi'un
D. The Ottoman period

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Fiqh began to grow as a separate science in the time of the Tabi'un; the time of the Tabi' al-Tabi'in was its golden age and led to the madhhabs.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q4. Who is a "muqallid"?

A. A scholar who makes ijtihad
B. A compiler of hadith
C. An ordinary person who follows the rulings of the mujtahids
D. A caliph

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: The efforts of the madhhab imams made it easy for ordinary people, the muqallids, to live according to Islam.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q5. What was Imam Abu Hanifah's (RH) own name?

A. Nu'man ibn Thabit
B. Malik ibn Anas
C. Muhammad ibn Idris
D. Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Hanbal

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Nu'man ibn Thabit – Abu Hanifah; Malik ibn Anas – Malik; Muhammad ibn Idris – ash-Shafi'i; Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Hanbal – Ahmad.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q6. In which year was Imam Abu Hanifah (RH) born?

A. 150 AH
B. 93 AH
C. 80 AH
D. 204 AH

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Abu Hanifah – 80–150 AH; Malik – 93–179; ash-Shafi'i – 150–204; Ahmad – 164–241.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q7. Who was praised as "al-Imam al-A'zam"?

A. Imam ash-Shafi'i
B. Imam Malik
C. Imam Ahmad
D. Imam Abu Hanifah

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Abu Hanifah – al-Imam al-A'zam; Malik – Imam al-Madinah; ash-Shafi'i – 'Alim Quraysh.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q8. Who was Imam Abu Hanifah's (RH) teacher?

A. Imam Malik
B. Hammad ibn Abi Sulayman
C. Imam ash-Shafi'i
D. Imam Abu Yusuf

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: He excelled in the class of Hammad, the famous scholar of Kufah, and after Hammad's death took over the class.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q9. Why is Imam Abu Hanifah's way of thinking called "madrasat ar-ra'y"?

A. Because the reliability of hadiths was unclear, he judged by ijtihad, using as evidence the hadiths he found to be sound
B. He did not use hadiths at all
C. He lived in Madinah
D. He was a poet

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Because the reliability of hadiths was unclear in his time, he gave more room to ijtihad.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q10. In which caliph's time was the Hanafi madhhab declared the state madhhab?

A. Caliph al-Hakam II
B. Caliph al-Mutawakkil
C. Caliph Harun
D. Sultan Salah ad-Din

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Because Imam Abu Yusuf served as chief judge in Caliph Harun's time, the Hanafi madhhab became the state madhhab.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q11. Why is Imam Malik's (RH) way of thinking called "madrasat al-athar"?

A. He used only reasoned opinion
B. Living in Madinah, hadiths were easy to obtain, so he gave solutions based on the Quran and the Sunnah
C. He lived in Egypt
D. He was a trader

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Athar = hadith-based thinking; hadiths were easily available in Madinah.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q12. Which student of Imam Malik introduced the Maliki madhhab into Spain?

A. Imam Abu Yusuf
B. Yahya al-Andalusi
C. Al-Hasan ash-Shaybani
D. Zufar

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: It was introduced through Yahya al-Andalusi and became the state madhhab there in the time of Caliph al-Hakam II.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q13. Where was Imam ash-Shafi'i (RH) born?

A. Baghdad
B. Kufah
C. Madinah
D. Gaza in Palestine

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Imam ash-Shafi'i, of the Quraysh, was born in Gaza, Palestine, and died in Egypt.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q14. Which book of fiqh was compiled by Imam ash-Shafi'i (RH)?

A. Al-Muwatta'
B. Al-Fiqh al-Akbar
C. Kitab al-Umm
D. Musnad Ahmad

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Ash-Shafi'i – Musnad al-Imam ash-Shafi'i, Kitab al-Umm, ar-Risalah; Malik – al-Muwatta'; Abu Hanifah – al-Fiqh al-Akbar; Ahmad – al-Musnad.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q15. Why was Imam ash-Shafi'i's way of thinking "progressive (balanced)"?

A. He worked in Yemen
B. He rejected hadiths
C. He was a doctor
D. He learned from Imam Malik and from Imam Abu Yusuf of the Hanafi madhhab, and took in both madrasat al-athar and madrasat ar-ra'y

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: By combining the two schools of thought, the Shafi'i madhhab has a balanced, progressive character (as given in the textbook).

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q16. What are the rulings Imam ash-Shafi'i gave in Egypt called?

A. Qadim (old)
B. Jadid (new)
C. Ra'y
D. Athar

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Those given in Baghdad – qadim; those given in Egypt – jadid.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q17. Who declared the Shafi'i madhhab the state madhhab in Egypt?

A. Sultan Salah ad-Din
B. Caliph al-Hakam II
C. Caliph al-Mutawakkil
D. Caliph Harun

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: This created favourable conditions for the Shafi'i madhhab to grow; most Muslims in Sri Lanka, Indonesia, Malaysia and Egypt follow it.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q18. Who was the main teacher of Imam Ahmad ibn Hanbal (RH)?

A. Hammad
B. Malik
C. Ash-Shafi'i
D. Abu Hanifah

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Imam Ahmad was a student of Imam ash-Shafi'i and Imam Abu Yusuf.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q19. In which caliph's time was the Hanbali madhhab declared the state madhhab?

A. The Abbasid Caliph al-Mutawakkil
B. Caliph Harun
C. Sultan Salah ad-Din
D. Caliph al-Hakam II

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Today it is the officially recognised madhhab in Saudi Arabia.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q20. Which of the following pairs is WRONG?

A. Imam Malik – Imam al-Madinah
B. Imam ash-Shafi'i – 'Alim Quraysh
C. Imam Ahmad – al-Musnad
D. Imam Abu Hanifah – madrasat al-athar

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Imam Abu Hanifah's way of thinking was madrasat ar-ra'y; madrasat al-athar – Malik and Ahmad.

Difficulty: Hard
