# Grade 11 – Chapter 25: Islamic Teachings on Earning a Living – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_25/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q19 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. Which verse says, "When the Jumu'ah prayer is over, spread out in the land and seek the bounty of Allah"?

A. 2 Al-Baqarah: 188
B. 7 Al-A'raf: 85
C. 62 Al-Jumu'ah: 10
D. 83 Al-Mutaffifin: 1

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: 62:10 – a verse that joins prayer and work; it also tells us to remember Allah much.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. According to Islam, what is the way to true prosperity?

A. Meeting only economic needs
B. Meeting only spiritual needs
C. Renouncing the world
D. Keeping a balance between soul and body

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Spiritual needs must be met just like economic needs; earning a living is only a means of reaching the goal.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q3. In earning a living, which situation does Islam strictly tell us to avoid?

A. Wealth harming one's iman
B. Making a profit
C. Engaging in trade
D. Saving

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Islam, the religion of tawhid, does not allow money and goods to be worshipped as gods.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q4. "Traders will be raised on the Day of Judgement as wrongdoers, except those who fear Allah, speak the truth and do good" – what is the source of this hadith?

A. Bukhari, Muslim
B. Tirmidhi, Ibn Hibban
C. Abu Dawud
D. Bayhaqi

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: The textbook gives it as Tirmidhi and Ibn Hibban.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q5. Which surah says, "Woe to those who cheat in measure and weight"?

A. Al-Mutaffifin (83)
B. Al-Jumu'ah (62)
C. Sad (38)
D. An-Nisa' (4)

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: 83:1–6 – those who take full measure from people but give less will stand for judgement on a mighty Day.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q6. For whom did the Prophet ﷺ pray, regarding selling, buying and claiming one's due?

A. The one who makes the most profit
B. The one who sells quickly
C. The one who is generous
D. The one who sells with oaths

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: "May Allah have mercy on a man who is generous…" (Bukhari, Tirmidhi).

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q7. According to the hadith, what is the result of swearing (falsely) in trade?

A. Profit increases
B. It may create demand for the goods, but it destroys the barakah
C. Customers increase
D. No effect at all

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Bukhari – swearing may make the goods sell, but it destroys the barakah and the profit.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q8. When the Prophet ﷺ said, "The traders are the wrongdoers", what explanation did he give?

A. Trade is haram
B. Traders do not pray
C. They do not pay zakat
D. They swear and so sin, and they tell lies when they speak

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Allah has made trade lawful; but false oaths and lies are the cause of sin (Ahmad).

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q9. Which verse says, "Do not consume one another's wealth unjustly"?

A. 2 Al-Baqarah: 279
B. 4 An-Nisa': 161
C. 2 Al-Baqarah: 188
D. 38 Sad: 24

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: 2:188 – a verse condemning taking others' property, for example by false oaths.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q10. Why is hoarding haram in Islam?

A. Because goods are hoarded in expectation of a price rise, causing hardship to people
B. Because storing goods is always haram
C. Because the goods will spoil
D. Because the government has banned it

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Greed and lack of concern for others are the causes of hoarding; people suffer many hardships.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q11. According to the hadith, whoever hoards food for how many days has cut himself off from Allah?

A. Three
B. Seven
C. Thirty
D. Forty

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Whoever hoards for forty days has cut himself off from Allah, and Allah has cut Himself off from him (Ahmad, al-Hakim).

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q12. According to the textbook, what is interest (riba)?

A. Trading profit
B. Any amount taken over and above the principal sum of money
C. The zakat amount
D. Rent

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Interest is any amount taken over the principal; trading profit is permissible.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q13. What warning does the Quran give to those who do not give up interest?

A. War has been declared by Allah and His Messenger
B. Their prayer will not be accepted
C. They cannot perform Hajj
D. They will be fined

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Al-Baqarah 278–279 – it warns that interest is like waging war with Allah, a warning given for no other offence.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q14. Which of the following is NOT one of the seven destructive major sins (Bukhari)?

A. Sorcery
B. Consuming interest
C. Consuming the wealth of orphans
D. Leaving a voluntary fast

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: The seven: shirk, sorcery, unlawful killing, interest, orphans' wealth, fleeing from battle, slandering chaste women.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q15. Who was explained to be the man in the river of blood that the Prophet ﷺ saw in his dream?

A. One who swore false oaths
B. A hoarder
C. One who consumes interest
D. A thief

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: The man on the bank threw stones into his mouth and drove him back; "The one you saw in the river is the one who consumes interest" (Bukhari).

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q16. What does the hadith (Bayhaqi) say about someone who buys goods knowing they were stolen?

A. He is innocent
B. He shares in its sin and crime
C. It is fine if he buys at a lower price
D. It is enough if he gives sadaqah

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Buying or selling property obtained unjustly is haram.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q17. As long as neither of two partners cheats the other, how is Allah with them (Abu Dawud)?

A. As judge
B. As witness
C. As accountant
D. As the third partner

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: The meaning is that if one of them cheats, Allah withdraws.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q18. "When Allah makes something haram for a people to eat, He also makes its price haram" – what example does the hadith give?

A. The Jews selling fat and consuming its price
B. Selling alcohol
C. Taking interest
D. Selling pork

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Although fat had been made haram for the Jews, they sold it and consumed its price (Abu Dawud).

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q19. In which situation is selling even a halal item forbidden?

A. Selling to a non-Muslim
B. Selling grapes to those who buy them to make alcohol
C. Selling on credit
D. Selling at a low price

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Selling to those who will use it for a haram act is forbidden – selling weapons to trouble-makers is the same.

Difficulty: Medium
