The thing the Prophet warned "destroys the barakah in trade"

### B3. 2021 (2022) – Islam II, Q7 (iv)

Question ID: P2021_2022-II-Q7.4  
Year: 2021 (2022)  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q7 (iv)  
Marks: 10 marks (short notes on two topics)  
Source: OL_2021_2022_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(iv) Interest and its evils

**Suggested answer:**

- Definition: interest (riba) is any amount taken over and above the principal sum of money; both taking and giving it are condemned.
- The Quran: give up what remains of interest; otherwise there is war from Allah and His Messenger (2:278–279); if you repent, your principal is yours (2:279); those who consume interest will rise like one struck by the devil (2:275); a painful punishment for those who took interest (4:161).
- Hadith: one of the seven destructive major sins (Bukhari); the man seen in a dream in a river of blood is the one who consumes interest (Bukhari).
- Its evils: consuming others' property wrongfully; exploiting the poor; wealth gathering in a few hands, inequality; the spirit of mutual help and giving is destroyed; Allah's anger, punishment in the Hereafter.
- The solution: interest-free Islamic financial methods (mudarabah, musharakah, murabahah, qard hasan).

**Answer explanation:** Write the social and economic evils together with evidence from the Quran and hadith.

**Marking guidance (suggested only – not an official marking scheme):** No official marking scheme.

**Answer status:** Suggested – not an official answer (no official marking scheme was supplied); based on the Grade 10/11 textbook content

### B4. 2024 (2025) – Islam II, Q7 (ii)

Question ID: P2024_2025-II-Q7.2  
Year: 2024 (2025)  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q7 (ii)  
Marks: 10 marks (short notes on two topics)  
Source: OL_2024_2025_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(ii) Hoarding in trade

**Suggested answer:**

- Hoarding (ihtikar) = hoarding goods in expectation of a price rise in the market – haram.
- Reasons: greed, no concern for others → great hardship for people.
- Hadiths:
  - "The one who hoards is a sinner" (Muslim, Abu Dawud).
  - "The one who brings needed goods to market at the right time is blessed; the one who hoards is cursed" (Ibn Majah).
  - "Whoever hoards food for forty days has cut himself off from Allah, and Allah has cut Himself off from him" (Ahmad, Hakim).
  - "How evil is the hoarder! If prices fall he is sad; if they rise he is glad" (Bayhaqi).
- Results: artificial shortage, rising prices, harm to the poor, social injustice.

**Answer explanation:** Include the definition, the ruling, the evidence and the results.
