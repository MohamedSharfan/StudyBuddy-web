A muhaddith is

## Part B: Structured and Essay Questions (Islam II)

### B1. 2017 – Islam II, Q3

Question ID: P2017-II-Q3.1  
Year: 2017  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q3  
Marks: 10 marks  
Source: OL_2017_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(i) State the meaning of 'hadith' as an Islamic technical term.
(ii) Point out three methods used to preserve the hadiths.
(iii) Briefly describe the contribution of Imam Muslim (RH) to the field of hadith.

**Suggested answer:**

(i) What includes the three – the sayings (qawl), actions (fi'l) and approvals (taqrir) of the Prophet ﷺ – is called hadith (Sunnah).

(ii) 1. The Prophet ﷺ commanded that even one verse known from him be passed on.
2. He warned that those who falsely attribute to him what he did not say will take their place in the Fire.
3. Care was taken to memorise hadiths accurately and to write them down.
4. Companions were appointed to teach hadith; the Ashab as-Suffah were used.
5. Later, by order of 'Umar ibn 'Abd al-'Aziz (RH), Imam az-Zuhri collected the hadiths; fabricated hadiths were examined critically and the hadiths were graded.

(iii) Imam Muslim ibn al-Hajjaj (RH) – born in Naysabur in 204 AH, died in 261 AH. He compiled "Sahih Muslim", the second book of the Sihah as-Sittah (about 4000 hadiths). He selected and arranged only authentic hadiths, using strict conditions. His book is respected next after Bukhari; there are many commentaries and translations of it.

**Answer explanation:** In (iii) the place of birth, period, book and its distinction should be mentioned.

**Marking guidance (suggested only – not an official marking scheme):** No official marking scheme.

**Answer status:** Suggested – not an official answer (no official marking scheme was supplied); based on the Grade 10/11 textbook content
