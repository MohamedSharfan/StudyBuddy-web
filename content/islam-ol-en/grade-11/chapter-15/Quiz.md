# Grade 11 – Chapter 15: As-Sunnah – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_15/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q18 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. Which three things does the Sunnah include?

A. Words (qawl), actions (fi'l), approval (taqrir)
B. Belief, worship, character
C. The Quran, ijma', qiyas
D. Prayer, fasting, zakat

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: The Prophet's ﷺ words, actions and approval are called the Sunnah or hadith.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. What does the Prophet's ﷺ "taqrir" refer to?

A. The Prophet's words
B. The Prophet's actions
C. The Prophet's approval
D. The Prophet's dream

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Taqrir = approval – the Prophet ﷺ accepting, without objection, what others did.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q3. Which is the correct difference between the Quran and the Sunnah?

A. The Quran's wording and meaning are Allah's; the Sunnah's wording is the Prophet's and its meaning is Allah's
B. The wording and meaning of both are the Prophet's
C. The Sunnah's wording is Allah's
D. They are the same

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: The Quran is wahy matluww; the Sunnah is wahy ghayr matluww – unrecited revelation.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q4. "He does not speak from his own desire; it is nothing but revelation revealed to him" – which verse is this?

A. 3:31
B. 4:80
C. 59:7
D. 53:3–4

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: 53:3–4 (an-Najm) – evidence that the Sunnah is also based on revelation.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q5. In verse 53:5, who is the "one mighty in power" who teaches the Prophet ﷺ?

A. Mika'il (AS)
B. Israfil (AS)
C. Jibril (AS)
D. Abu Bakr (RA)

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: "One mighty in power (Jibril) teaches him."

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q6. Which of the following is NOT one of the roles of the Sunnah?

A. Explaining what the Quran says briefly
B. Putting the Quran's principles into practice
C. Guiding the way for ijtihad
D. Cancelling the laws of the Quran and creating a new religion

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: The Sunnah only explains, stresses and puts the Quran into practice.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q7. According to the hadith in the Muwatta', what are the two things the Prophet ﷺ left, following which "you will not go astray"?

A. Makkah and Madinah
B. The Book of Allah and the Sunnah of His Messenger
C. Prayer and fasting
D. The Companions and the Tabi'un

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: The Quran and the Sunnah together give right guidance.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q8. Which of the following was NOT one of the steps the Prophet ﷺ took to preserve the Sunnah?

A. Telling people to pass on even a single sentence about him
B. Compiling all the hadiths into one book himself
C. Appointing some Companions to learn and teach hadith
D. Warning that those who falsely attribute words to him will go to Hell

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Hadiths were not compiled into a book in the Prophet's ﷺ time; that effort became important later, in the time of 'Umar ibn 'Abd al-'Aziz.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q9. In which caliph's time did the effort to compile hadith into books become important?

A. Abu Bakr (RA)
B. 'Umar ibn al-Khattab (RA)
C. 'Umar ibn 'Abd al-'Aziz (RH)
D. 'Uthman (RA)

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Abu Bakr's (RA) time – compiling the Quran; 'Umar ibn 'Abd al-'Aziz's (RH) time – compiling hadith into books.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q10. Who collected hadiths on the order of Caliph 'Umar ibn 'Abd al-'Aziz?

A. Imam (Ibn) Shihab az-Zuhri
B. Imam Malik
C. Imam al-Bukhari
D. Imam Ahmad

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Imam (Ibn) Shihab az-Zuhri collected hadiths; but the work was not completed.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q11. What examples does the textbook give of hadith collections that appeared in the time of the Tabi'un?

A. Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim
B. Al-Kashshaf, al-Muqaddimah
C. Sunan Abi Dawud, Sunan an-Nasa'i
D. Imam Malik's al-Muwatta', Imam Ahmad's al-Musnad

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Al-Muwatta' (Imam Malik), al-Musnad (Imam Ahmad).

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q12. What was the result of fabricated hadiths starting to spread?

A. Meeting those who knew hadith in person, finding out the truth and writing it down; critical examination and grading – the development of the science of hadith
B. Hadiths were completely abandoned
C. The Quran was changed
D. The Companions stopped narrating hadith

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: The science of examining hadith developed to tell the fabricated from the authentic, and authentic collections became available.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q13. What is "as-Sihah as-Sittah"?

A. Six authentic collections of hadith
B. Six books of tafsir
C. Six madhhabs of fiqh
D. Six books of sirah

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: The six leading collections of hadith, ranked next after the Quran.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q14. How many hadiths does the textbook say are in Sahih al-Bukhari?

A. 4000
B. 4800
C. 4341
D. 7275

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Al-Bukhari – 7275; Muslim – 4000; at-Tirmidhi – 4800; Abu Dawud – 4800; an-Nasa'i – 4341; Ibn Majah – 4000.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q15. Where was Imam Muslim born?

A. Bukhara
B. Sijistan
C. Nishapur
D. Qazwin

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Al-Bukhari – Bukhara; Muslim – Nishapur; Abu Dawud – Sijistan; Ibn Majah – Qazwin; an-Nasa'i – Nasa; at-Tirmidhi – Tirmidh.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q16. Who are the authors of the books of as-Sihah as-Sittah named "Sunan"?

A. Al-Bukhari, Muslim, at-Tirmidhi
B. Abu Dawud, an-Nasa'i, Ibn Majah
C. Malik, Ahmad, ash-Shafi'i
D. Al-Bukhari, Abu Dawud, an-Nasa'i

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Sahih – al-Bukhari, Muslim; Jami' – at-Tirmidhi; Sunan – Abu Dawud, an-Nasa'i, Ibn Majah.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q17. Which of the six hadith imams died last (303 AH)?

A. Imam al-Bukhari
B. Imam an-Nasa'i
C. Imam at-Tirmidhi
D. Imam Ibn Majah

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Deaths: al-Bukhari – 256; Muslim – 261; Ibn Majah – 273; Abu Dawud – 275; at-Tirmidhi – 279; an-Nasa'i – 303.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q18. What was Imam Abu Dawud's own name?

A. Sulayman
B. Muhammad
C. Ahmad
D. Muslim

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Abu Dawud – Sulayman ibn al-Ash'ath; an-Nasa'i – Ahmad ibn Shu'ayb; al-Bukhari, at-Tirmidhi and Ibn Majah – Muhammad.

Difficulty: Hard
