# Grade 11 – Chapter 15: As-Sunnah

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_15/Notes.md` (Tamil). English rendering.

## 🎯 What you will learn
- The Sunnah – its definition and three parts
- The difference between the Quran and the Sunnah
- Evidence that the Sunnah is a source of law
- Efforts made to preserve the Sunnah
- The history of hadith compilation
- As-Sihah as-Sittah – the six hadith imams

> 📖 **Opening verse of the chapter (53:3–4 – an-Najm):** وَمَا يَنْطِقُ عَنِ الْهَوَى إِنْ هُوَ إِلَّا وَحْيٌ يُوْحَى
> **Meaning:** "He does not speak **from his own desire**; it is **nothing but revelation** revealed to him."

---

## 1. What is the Sunnah? ⭐
> 📖 What includes the Prophet's ﷺ **words, actions and approval** is called **the Sunnah or hadith**.

| Part | Arabic | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Qawl** | قول | The Prophet's **words / teaching** |
| **Fi'l** | فعل | The Prophet's **actions** |
| **Taqrir** | تقرير | The Prophet's **approval** (accepting what others did without objecting) |

### The Quran vs the Sunnah ⭐
| | **The Quran** | **The Sunnah** |
|---|---|---|
| Kind of revelation | **Wahy matluww** (recited revelation) | **Wahy ghayr matluww** (unrecited revelation) |
| **Wording** | **Allah's** | **The Prophet's ﷺ** |
| **Meaning** | **Allah's** | **Allah's** |

---

## 2. The Sunnah as a source of law – evidence ⭐

| Verse | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **53:3–5** | He does not speak from desire; it is revelation; **one mighty in power (Jibril)** teaches him |
| **4:80** | Whoever obeys the Messenger **has obeyed Allah** |
| **59:7** | **Take (willingly)** whatever the Messenger gives you |
| **3:31** | If you love Allah, **follow me**; Allah will love you |

### The roles of the Sunnah
- The Prophet ﷺ **put into practice** what the Quran set out **as principles**.
- He also **solved the difficulties** that arise in practising them as a human being.
- It **explains** what the Quran says **briefly**.
- It **stresses and explains** the teachings **further**.
- It points out some things the Quran did not mention and **guides the way for ijtihad.**

### Evidence from the hadith
| Hadith (meaning) | Source |
|---|---|
| My Lord has commanded me to **teach you what you do not know** of what He has taught me | Muslim |
| **I have been given the Quran and something like it along with it** | — |
| I am leaving **two things**; you will not go astray as long as you follow them: **the Book of Allah and the Sunnah of His Messenger** | Muwatta' |

👉 Just as with the Quran, it is **the duty of the Muslim community** to learn, preserve, practise and teach **the Sunnah**.

---

## 3. What the Prophet ﷺ did to preserve the Sunnah ⭐
1. He told people to **pass on** what they knew about him, **even a single sentence**.
2. He **warned** that whoever **falsely** attributes to him what he did not say will **take his place in Hell**.
3. He took care that hadiths were **memorised accurately and written down**.
4. He **appointed** some Companions to **learn and teach** hadith; he also used **Ashab as-Suffah** for this.

👉 So the Prophet's **qawl, fi'l and taqrir** were all **closely observed and preserved** by the Companions.

---

## 4. The history of hadith compilation ⭐

| Stage | Event |
|---|---|
| After the Prophet's ﷺ death | Legal problems showed the need for hadith; the Companions presented hadiths **with witnesses**; younger Companions and the Tabi'un learned them **orally**; as an exception, also **in writing** |
| Compiling the Quran | In the time of **Caliph Abu Bakr (RA)** (for fear it would be lost) |
| **The effort to compile hadith into books** | Became important in the time of **Caliph 'Umar ibn 'Abd al-'Aziz (RH)**; on the caliph's order **Imam (Ibn) Shihab az-Zuhri** collected hadiths; but the work **was not completed** |
| The time of the Tabi'un | Hadith teaching in the **capitals** of the Muslim state; books of hadith appeared – e.g. **Imam Malik – al-Muwatta'; Imam Ahmad – al-Musnad** |
| Later | **Fabricated hadiths** spread, aimed at taking Muslims away from Islam → the effort to **meet those who knew hadith in person, find out the truth and write it down** → hadiths were **critically examined** and **graded**; the **science of hadith** developed |

## 5. As-Sihah as-Sittah (الصحاح الستة) – the six collections ⭐
- Six authentic books of hadith; the leading collections, **ranked next after the Quran**; there are many commentaries and translations of them.

| Name | Known as | Father | Birthplace | Born (AH) | Collection | Hadiths | Died (AH) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Muhammad | **al-Bukhari** | Isma'il | **Bukhara** | 194 | **Sahih al-Bukhari** | **7275** | 256 |
| Muslim | — | al-Hajjaj | **Nishapur** | 204 | **Sahih Muslim** | **4000** | 261 |
| Muhammad | **at-Tirmidhi** | 'Isa | **Tirmidh** | 209 | **Jami' at-Tirmidhi** | **4800** | 279 |
| Sulayman | **Abu Dawud** | al-Ash'ath | **Sijistan** | 201 | **Sunan Abi Dawud** | **4800** | 275 |
| Ahmad | **an-Nasa'i** | Shu'ayb | **Nasa** | 215 | **Sunan an-Nasa'i** | **4341** | 303 |
| Muhammad | **Ibn Majah** | Yazid | **Qazwin** | 209 | **Sunan Ibn Majah** | **4000** | 273 |

> 📌 The numbers of hadiths and the dates are as given in the textbook; other sources sometimes give slightly different figures.

💡 **Memory tip:**
- **Sahih** (2): al-Bukhari, Muslim
- **Jami'** (1): at-Tirmidhi
- **Sunan** (3): Abu Dawud, an-Nasa'i, Ibn Majah
- **Most hadiths:** al-Bukhari (7275); **born first:** al-Bukhari (194 AH); **died last:** an-Nasa'i (303 AH).

## ✅ Quick revision
- Sunnah = qawl + fi'l + taqrir; wahy ghayr matluww; the wording is the Prophet's, the meaning is Allah's.
- Evidence: 53:3–5, 4:80, 59:7, 3:31; "to teach you what you do not know" (Muslim); "I am leaving two things" (Muwatta').
- The Prophet's steps to preserve it: telling people to pass it on, warning liars of Hell, memorisation and writing, appointing teachers, Ashab as-Suffah.
- Compilation: 'Umar ibn 'Abd al-'Aziz – Imam Ibn Shihab az-Zuhri; al-Muwatta' (Malik), al-Musnad (Ahmad).
- As-Sihah as-Sittah: al-Bukhari, Muslim, at-Tirmidhi, Abu Dawud, an-Nasa'i, Ibn Majah.
