### B2. 2019 – Islam II, Q1 (vi)

Question ID: P2019-II-Q1.6  
Year: 2019  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q1 (vi)  
Marks: 20 marks for the whole of Q1  
Source: OL_2019_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(vi) Present two of the conditions for proper talaq.

**Suggested answer:**

Any two of the following:
1. Pronouncing it while the woman is in a period of purity (tuhr)
2. Pronouncing it without having had intercourse in that period
3. The second talaq in the next tuhr and the third in the tuhr after that (not pronouncing all three at once)

**Answer explanation:** Talaq pronounced in this way is "talaq sunni"; pronouncing it during menstruation or in a tuhr with intercourse is "talaq bid'i".

**Marking guidance (suggested only – not an official marking scheme):** Two conditions.

### B3. 2022 (2023) – Islam II, Q7 (iii)

Question ID: P2022_2023-II-Q7.3  
Year: 2022 (2023)  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q7 (iii)  
Marks: 10 marks (short notes on two topics)  
Source: OL_2022_2023_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(iii) The kinds of 'iddah

**Suggested answer:**

- 'Iddah = the period a woman whose marriage has ended must wait without remarrying; Allah's command; observing it is 'ibadah.
- Kinds:
  1. The 'iddah for the husband's death – 4 months 10 days (2:234)
  2. The 'iddah of a pregnant woman – until she gives birth (65:4)
  3. The 'iddah for talaq raj'i – three menstrual periods / quru' (2:228)
  4. Ila' – 4 months (according to the textbook notes)
- If talaq is given before the marriage is consummated, there is no 'iddah.
- Rules: it is best to observe the 'iddah in the husband's house; maintenance (nafaqah) during the 'iddah is the husband's duty.
- Benefits: confirming pregnancy, a chance to reunite, mourning for the husband.

**Answer explanation:** Give the period and the Quranic evidence for each kind.
