# Grade 11 – Chapter 29: Talaq and 'Iddah (الطلاق والعدة)

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_29/Notes.md` (Tamil). English rendering.

## 🎯 What you will learn

- The situation in which Islam permits divorce
- The steps Islam shows for avoiding talaq
- The three kinds of dissolution of marriage: talaq, faskh, khul'
- Improper (bid'i) and proper (sunni) talaq
- Raj'i and ba'in (sughra, kubra) talaq
- Zihar, li'an, ila'
- 'Iddah – meaning, benefits, kinds and rules

---

## 📖 Opening verse

> يَا أَيُّهَا النَّبِيُّ إِذَا طَلَّقْتُمُ النِّسَاءَ فَطَلِّقُوهُنَّ لِعِدَّتِهِنَّ وَأَحْصُوا الْعِدَّةَ ۖ وَاتَّقُوا اللَّهَ رَبَّكُمْ

**Meaning:** O Prophet! When you divorce women, divorce them in a way suited to counting their 'iddah (that is, outside their menstrual periods), keep count of the 'iddah, and fear Allah, your Lord. **(65 At-Talaq: 1)**

---

## 1️⃣ Divorce – the position of Islam

- Marriage is **a sacred contract that is meant to last**; that is why Islam has made clear the duties and rights of husband and wife.
- It has made it essential, before marriage, to **check suitability and for both to express their consent**.
- It guides the couple to talk openly and keep their life on the right path.
- ⭐ Divorce is permitted – **as an exception** – only when **all efforts at reconciliation have failed** and there is no way left but to separate.

| Hadith (meaning) | Source |
|---|---|
| ⭐ Of the things Allah has permitted, **the most hated to Him is talaq** | Abu Dawud |
| When one of the devils says, "Today I separated this family", Iblis is delighted | Muslim |
| Three things count as real even if done in jest; one of them is **talaq** | Tirmidhi |

- Islam condemns treating divorce **as a game or a joke**.

> ⚠️ **Note:** Hadith scholars have discussed the chain of the "most hated permissible thing" hadith (some grade it weak). The textbook cites Abu Dawud.

---

## 2️⃣ Steps for avoiding talaq (in order)

1. Husband and wife try to solve the problems **by themselves**.
2. If they cannot, **one person** from the husband's side and **one** from the wife's side try to **reconcile** them.
3. If everything fails, arrangements are made for talaq **with kindness**.

- ⭐ **2 Al-Baqarah: 229** – Either keep them (as wives) in a proper way, or release them (without accusations) in a good way (with gratitude and kindness).

---

## 3️⃣ The three kinds of dissolution of marriage ⭐

| Kind | Arabic | Who / how |
|---|---|---|
| **Talaq** | الطلاق | **The husband** releases his wife from the marriage bond |
| **Faskh** | الفسخ | The marriage is declared **invalid (annulled)** |
| **Khul'** | الخلع | **The wife** asks for and obtains release from her husband |

### (1) Talaq

- The husband has the right to pronounce talaq three times; but ⭐ **pronouncing it only once is safest and wisest**.
- "I have divorced you" = one talaq. It may also be done **in writing**.
- Talaq takes effect **even if said in jest or sent in writing**.

### (2) Faskh

- When it becomes known that the conditions of marriage were not met, the nikah becomes invalid.
  - Example: it becomes known that husband and wife are **mahram** to each other.
- When something happens that prevents the marriage from continuing.
  - Example: one of the couple **leaves the religion (apostasy)**.
- If the cause is clear, they must separate at once; if it has to be established through argument, they must go to **the qadi** for a decision.

### (3) Khul'

- The wife obtains release by **giving the husband something as a gift** or **returning the mahr he gave her**.
- ⭐ It must be carried out **before the qadi**.
- 💡 As **talaq** is for the husband, so **khul'** is for the wife.

---

## 4️⃣ Talaq – two kinds by method and timing

| Improper – **talaq bid'i** (الطلاق البدعي) | Proper – **talaq sunni** (الطلاق السني) |
|---|---|
| Pronouncing all three talaqs **at once** | Pronouncing it while the woman is **in purity (tuhr)** |
| Pronouncing it while the wife is **menstruating** | In that period, **without having had intercourse** |
| Pronouncing it in a **tuhr in which intercourse took place** | The 2nd talaq in the next tuhr |
| | The 3rd talaq in the tuhr after that |

- Stopping after one or two is still proper talaq.
- ⭐ **Tuhr (الطهر)** = the **period of purity**, when the woman is free of menstruation.

---

## 5️⃣ Talaq by whether it can be revoked

```
                  Talaq
                /       \
        Revocable        Irrevocable
         (raj'i)           (ba'in)
                         /        \
                     Minor        Major
                   (sughra)      (kubra)
```

| Kind | Explanation |
|---|---|
| **Talaq raj'i** (الرجعي) | After the first / second talaq the husband may take her back **before the 'iddah ends** |
| **Baynunah sughra** (minor separation) | If the 'iddah ends without a third talaq – they may come together again **with a new nikah** |
| ⭐ **Baynunah kubra** (major separation) | Once **all three talaqs** are complete – only after she **marries another man**, he divorces her, and that 'iddah ends, may she be joined to her former husband by a new nikah |

---

## 6️⃣ Talaq under other names

| Name | Arabic | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| **Zihar** | الظهار | Comparing one's wife physically **to one's mother**; the couple may come together again after carrying out **kaffarah** (expiation); otherwise talaq takes place |
| **Li'an** | اللعان | **Accusing the wife of adultery and swearing oaths**, ending in separation |
| **Ila'** | الإيلاء | Swearing, "By Allah, I will not have relations with you"; ⭐ **a period of four months** – they may reunite within it; otherwise talaq takes place |

---

## 7️⃣ 'Iddah (العدة) ⭐

- Word meaning: **"counting", "reckoning"** – because the period is counted in numbers (3 months / 4 months 10 days).
- A woman who has lost her husband or been divorced remaining bound by certain rules for a fixed period.
- 'Iddah is **Allah's command**; observing it is **'ibadah**.

### Benefits of 'iddah

1. Reward is gained, as it is Allah's command.
2. If the woman is **pregnant**, it is known, and the father is established.
3. If it is **talaq raj'i**, there is time to reunite.

- ⭐ If the talaq is given **before the marriage is consummated**, there is no 'iddah.

### Kinds of 'iddah

| Kind | Period | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 'Iddah for the husband's death | ⭐ **4 months 10 days** – for every wife, regardless of age or stage | 2 Al-Baqarah: 234 |
| 'Iddah of a pregnant woman | ⭐ **Until she gives birth** | 65 At-Talaq: 4 |
| 'Iddah for talaq raj'i | ⭐ **Until three menstrual periods** have passed | 2 Al-Baqarah: 228 |

### Rules during 'iddah

- Avoiding adornment and make-up → ⭐ **"ihdad" (الإحداد – mourning)**.
- She must not marry or take part in marriage talks.
- Avoiding jewellery.
- It is best to observe 'iddah **in the husband's house**.
- Providing maintenance **(nafaqah – النفقة)** for the wife in 'iddah is **the duty of the husband** who gave the talaq.

⚠️ The textbook's word list includes الواجب، السنة، الحرام، المباح (wajib, sunnah, haram, mubah); but these legal rulings on talaq are not explained in the lesson – confirm with your teacher.

## 💡 Memory tip

- **"Talaq – Faskh – Khul'"** → husband releases / declared invalid / wife's request.
- 'Iddah numbers: **death 4 months 10 days | pregnancy → birth | raj'i → 3 periods | ila' → 4 months**.

## ✅ Quick revision

- Of permitted things, the most hated to Allah is talaq (Abu Dawud).
- Talaq takes effect even if said in jest (Tirmidhi).
- 2:229 – keep in a proper way or release in a good way.
- Talaq / faskh / khul' (before the qadi).
- Bid'i: all three at once, during menstruation, in a tuhr with intercourse.
- Raj'i – taking back within the 'iddah; sughra – new nikah; kubra – marriage to another man needed.
- Zihar – kaffarah; li'an – accusation; ila' – 4 months.
