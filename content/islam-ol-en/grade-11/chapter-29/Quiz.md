# Grade 11 – Chapter 29: Talaq and 'Iddah – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_29/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q20 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. "Of the things Allah has permitted, the most hated to Him is talaq" – what is the source of this hadith?

A. Muslim
B. Abu Dawud
C. Tirmidhi
D. Bukhari

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Abu Dawud. Iblis being delighted when a devil separates a family – Muslim; talaq taking effect even in jest – Tirmidhi.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q2. Which of the following does the hadith mention as one of three things that count as real even if done in jest?

A. Talaq
B. Trade
C. Salam
D. Fasting

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Tirmidhi – Islam condemns treating divorce as a joke.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q3. When a couple cannot solve their problem between themselves, what is the next step Islam shows?

A. Pronouncing three talaqs at once
B. Going to court
C. One person from the husband's side and one from the wife's side trying to reconcile them
D. Living apart

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Solve it themselves → reconciliation by both sides → if that fails, talaq with kindness.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q4. Which verse says, "Either keep them as wives in a proper way, or release them in a good way"?

A. 2 Al-Baqarah: 228
B. 2 Al-Baqarah: 234
C. 65 At-Talaq: 4
D. 2 Al-Baqarah: 229

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: 2:229 – they should separate without accusations, with gratitude and kindness.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q5. What are the three main kinds of dissolution of marriage set out by the jurists?

A. Talaq, faskh, khul'
B. Zihar, li'an, ila'
C. Raj'i, ba'in, sunni
D. 'Iddah, ihdad, nafaqah

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Talaq – the husband releases; faskh – annulment; khul' – the wife asks for release.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q6. Although the husband has the right to pronounce three talaqs, what is safest and wisest?

A. Pronouncing all three at once
B. Pronouncing it only once
C. Pronouncing it only in writing
D. Pronouncing it during menstruation

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Pronouncing only one talaq protects the chance of coming together again.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q7. If it becomes known that husband and wife are mahram to each other, which kind of dissolution takes place?

A. Khul'
B. Ila'
C. Faskh
D. Zihar

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: When it becomes known that the conditions of marriage were not met, the nikah becomes invalid – faskh. One of the couple leaving the religion is also an example of faskh.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q8. Which statement about khul' is correct?

A. The husband releases the wife
B. The wife obtains release by giving a gift or returning the mahr; it must be carried out before the qadi
C. It can take place without a qadi
D. It happens because they are found to be mahram

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: As talaq is for the husband, khul' is the wife's right to obtain release from a husband she does not want.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q9. Which of the following is NOT improper talaq (talaq bid'i)?

A. Pronouncing all three talaqs at once
B. Pronouncing it while the wife is menstruating
C. Pronouncing it in a tuhr in which intercourse took place
D. Pronouncing one talaq in a tuhr without intercourse

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Pronouncing it in a tuhr without intercourse is proper talaq (sunni).

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q10. What does "tuhr" refer to?

A. The period of purity, when the woman is free of menstruation
B. The menstrual period
C. The 'iddah period
D. The period after childbirth

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Proper talaq must be pronounced only in the period of tuhr.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q11. Which talaq allows the husband to take his wife back before the 'iddah ends, after the first or second talaq?

A. Talaq ba'in
B. Baynunah kubra
C. Talaq raj'i
D. Talaq bid'i

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Raj'i – revocable; it does not cause a permanent separation.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q12. After the major separation (baynunah kubra), when all three talaqs are complete, how can she be joined to her former husband?

A. By being taken back within the 'iddah
B. A new nikah alone is enough
C. By carrying out kaffarah
D. She marries another man, he divorces her, and after the 'iddah ends there is a new nikah with the former husband

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: If the 'iddah ends without a third talaq, a new nikah alone is enough – that is baynunah sughra.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q13. What is it called when a man compares his wife physically to his mother?

A. Zihar
B. Li'an
C. Ila'
D. Khul'

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Zihar – they may come together again after carrying out kaffarah; otherwise talaq takes place.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q14. How long a period is given to a man who swears, "By Allah, I will not have relations with you"?

A. Three months
B. Four months
C. Four months and ten days
D. One year

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Ila' – they may reunite within the four-month period; otherwise talaq takes place.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q15. What does the word "'iddah" mean?

A. Waiting
B. Separating
C. Counting, reckoning
D. Grief

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: It is so called because the period is reckoned by counting.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q16. Which of the following is NOT a benefit of 'iddah?

A. Gaining reward, as it is Allah's command
B. Knowing about a pregnancy and establishing the father
C. Time to reunite if it is talaq raj'i
D. Increasing the amount of the mahr

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: The textbook mentions three benefits: reward for obeying Allah's command, knowing about pregnancy, and time to reunite.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q17. If the husband dies, how long is the 'iddah the wife must observe?

A. Three menstrual periods
B. Three months
C. Until childbirth
D. Four months and ten days

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: 2 Al-Baqarah: 234 – for all women regardless of age or stage (except a pregnant woman).

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q18. Until when is the 'iddah of a wife who was pregnant when her husband died?

A. Until she gives birth
B. Four months and ten days
C. Three menstrual periods
D. One year

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: 65 At-Talaq: 4 – the 'iddah of pregnant women lasts until they give birth.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q19. What is a woman's refraining from adornment and make-up during the 'iddah called?

A. Nafaqah
B. Ihdad
C. Tuhr
D. Kaffarah

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Al-ihdad. Nafaqah – maintenance during the 'iddah (the husband's duty).

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q20. In which situation does a divorced woman NOT have to observe 'iddah?

A. If she is pregnant
B. If the husband dies
C. If the talaq is given before the marriage is consummated
D. If the first talaq is pronounced

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: If a man divorces his wife before the marriage is consummated, 'iddah is not required.

Difficulty: Hard
