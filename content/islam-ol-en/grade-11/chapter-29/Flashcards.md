# Grade 11 – Chapter 29: Talaq and 'Iddah – Flashcards

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_29/Flashcards.md` (Tamil), cards 1–14 in the same order.

---

Flashcard 1

Front: When does Islam permit divorce?

Back: As an exception – when all efforts at reconciliation have failed and there is no way left but to separate.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 29 | Topic: Islam's position

Flashcard 2

Front: What has Islam made essential so that marriage lasts?

Back: Checking suitability before marriage; both expressing their consent.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 29 | Topic: Islam's position

Flashcard 3

Front: The devil's deed that delights Iblis most (Muslim)?

Back: Saying, "Today I separated this family."

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 29 | Topic: Islam's position

---

Flashcard 4

Front: الفسخ (Al-faskh) – two examples?

Back: The couple are found to be mahram; one of them leaves the religion.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 29 | Topic: Kinds of dissolution

---

Flashcard 5

Front: If the cause of faskh has to be established through argument?

Back: They must go to the qadi for a decision.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 29 | Topic: Kinds of dissolution

---

Flashcard 6

Front: الطلاق السني (At-talaq as-sunni) – the steps?

Back: 1st talaq in a tuhr without intercourse → 2nd in the next tuhr → 3rd in the tuhr after that.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 29 | Topic: Kinds of talaq

---

Flashcard 7

Front: الطلاق البائن (At-talaq al-ba'in) – two kinds?

Back: Baynunah sughra (minor separation – a new nikah is enough); baynunah kubra (major separation).

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 29 | Topic: Kinds of talaq

---

Flashcard 8

Front: اللعان (Al-li'an)

Back: Accusing the wife of adultery and swearing oaths, ending in separation.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 29 | Topic: Other names

---

Flashcard 9

Front: What must be done to reunite after zihar?

Back: Expiation – kaffarah must be carried out.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 29 | Topic: Other names

---

Flashcard 10

Front: 'Iddah – Islamic definition?

Back: A woman who has lost her husband or been divorced remaining bound by certain rules for a fixed period; Allah's command, an act of 'ibadah.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 29 | Topic: 'Iddah

---

Flashcard 11

Front: The 'iddah for talaq raj'i?

Back: Until three menstrual periods have passed (2 Al-Baqarah: 228).

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 29 | Topic: 'Iddah

---

Flashcard 12

Front: What must be avoided during 'iddah?

Back: Adornment, make-up, jewellery, marriage, marriage talks.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 29 | Topic: 'Iddah

---

Flashcard 13

Front: The best place to observe 'iddah?

Back: The husband's house.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 29 | Topic: 'Iddah

---

Flashcard 14

Front: النفقة (An-nafaqah)

Back: Maintenance for the wife observing 'iddah – the duty of the husband who gave the talaq.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 29 | Topic: 'Iddah
