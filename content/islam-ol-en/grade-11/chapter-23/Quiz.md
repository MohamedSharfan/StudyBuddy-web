# Grade 11 – Chapter 23: A Sri Lankan Muslim Pioneer – 1 (M. C. Siddi Lebbe) – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_23/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q19 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. When and where was the scholar M. C. Siddi Lebbe born?

A. 1822, Jaffna
B. 1864, Colombo
C. 11 June 1838, Kandy
D. 1838, Galle

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: He was born in Kandy on 11 June 1838. 1822 – Arumuga Navalar; 1864 – Anagarika Dharmapala.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. Who was the first Muslim lawyer appointed in Sri Lanka?

A. Muhammad Lebbe (Siddi Lebbe's father)
B. M. C. Abdul Rahman
C. Wapchi Marikar
D. T. B. Jayah

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: In 1833 the British government appointed Muhammad Lebbe as a lawyer; the "Ceylon Almanac" praised him as "the leader of the Moors of Ceylon".

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q3. What did the people of his area call Siddi Lebbe's elder brother?

A. Appa Pulavar
B. Leader of the Moors
C. Father of the revival
D. Marakkayar

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: An 'alim and a lawyer, he was also a qadi judge, and a poet who composed more than 300 verses.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q4. Which of Siddi Lebbe's sisters served as head teacher of the Muslim girls' college he started in Kandy?

A. Sayyida Umma
B. Muthu Nachchiya
C. Amina Umma
D. Fathima Nachchiya

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Muthu Nachchiya, an 'alimah, was head teacher of the college on Trincomalee Street. Sayyida Umma – his wife.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q5. What did Siddi Lebbe learn from the man known as "Kalawatte Alim Appa Pulavar"?

A. English
B. Sinhala
C. Arabic (further study)
D. Law

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: He studied Arabic further, and mastered it, with Shaykh Muhammad Alim of Akurana.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q6. In which year was Siddi Lebbe promoted to advocate of the Supreme Court?

A. 1864
B. 1862
C. 1871
D. 1882

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: 1862 – Kandy District lawyer; 1864 – Supreme Court advocate; 1871 – marriage; 1882 – Muslim Nesan.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q7. On what date was the newspaper "Muslim Nesan" started?

A. 1 January 1892
B. 11 June 1885
C. 5 March 1880
D. 12 December 1882

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: 12 December 1882 – through it he gained the honour of being "the first journalist of the Sri Lankan Muslims".

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q8. Over what period did Muslim Nesan appear continuously?

A. 1882 – 1898
B. 1882 – 1944
C. 1892 – 1935
D. 1882 – 1977

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: It appeared from 1882 to 1944 and served as a weapon in the struggle to meet the community's needs.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q9. Which revolutionary did Siddi Lebbe go to the harbour to welcome, despite the government's objection?

A. Jamal ad-Din al-Afghani
B. Sir Sayyid Ahmad Khan
C. 'Urabi (Arabi) Pasha
D. Abdullah Badib

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: He gathered the Muslims, went to welcome 'Urabi Pasha, exiled from Egypt, and treated him hospitably.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q10. Which of these strengthened the spiritual side of Siddi Lebbe?

A. Abdullah Badib of Yemen
B. Jamal ad-Din al-Afghani
C. Sir Sayyid Ahmad Khan
D. Governor Havelock

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: His elder brother, Kalawatte Alim Appa Pulavar and Abdullah Badib – spiritual influence. Al-Afghani, Ahmad Khan and 'Urabi Pasha – influence on his reform ideas.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q11. Which college was founded in Colombo as a result of Siddi Lebbe's campaign for education?

A. Zahira College
B. Royal College
C. Ananda College
D. Nalanda College

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Zahira College, Colombo, was founded with the help of wealthy men such as Wapchi Marikar.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q12. Which Governor came in 1892 to visit Siddi Lebbe's Muslim girls' schools?

A. Governor Brownrigg
B. Governor Havelock
C. Governor Ward
D. Governor Manning

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Satisfied with the work for girls' education, Governor Havelock visited those schools in 1892.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q13. Which languages did Siddi Lebbe insist Sri Lankan Muslims should master?

A. Arabic, Urdu, Tamil, English
B. Arabic, Persian, Tamil, Sinhala
C. Tamil and English only
D. Arabic, Tamil, English, Sinhala

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Mastery of all four languages; Arabic should be the home language; he compared someone who does not know Tamil to a blind man.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q14. What is regarded as the first Tamil novel published in Sri Lanka?

A. Gnanadeepam
B. Muslim Nesan
C. Hassan Bey Charithram
D. Ceylon Almanac

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: "Hassan Bey Charithram", written by Siddi Lebbe, came out as a separate book in 1885; it is built as a story within a story.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q15. What did Siddi Lebbe publish in 1892 to revive Muslims who had grown weak in spiritual matters?

A. Gnanadeepam
B. Muslim Nesan
C. Hassan Bey Charithram
D. Islam Mithran

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Gnanadeepam was published in 1892; Muslim Nesan in 1882.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q16. As a result of the Muslims' request of 1880, who was appointed as a nominated member of the Legislative Council in 1889?

A. Wapchi Marikar
B. M. C. Abdul Rahman
C. T. B. Jayah
D. Abdul Hameed

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: As a result of Siddi Lebbe's campaign for political rights, M. C. Abdul Rahman was appointed in 1889.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q17. When did the Muslim marriage registration effort begun by Siddi Lebbe come into force?

A. From 1928
B. From 1898
C. From 1948
D. From 1935, with the recommendation of the Akbar Committee

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: It came into force from 1935, with the recommendation of the Akbar Committee appointed in 1928.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q18. What caused superstitions to creep in among Sri Lankan Muslims in Siddi Lebbe's time?

A. Arab culture
B. The influence of South Indian culture
C. Chinese culture
D. African culture

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Because European imperialism ended the healthy religious environment, South Indian cultural influence and superstitious rituals crept in.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q19. On what date did the Sri Lankan government issue a postage stamp in Siddi Lebbe's memory?

A. 11-06-1898
B. 04-02-1948
C. 11-01-1977
D. 12-12-1982

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: It honoured him by issuing a postage stamp on 11-01-1977. He died of asthma in 1898.

Difficulty: Medium
