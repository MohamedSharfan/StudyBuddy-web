## Part B: Structured and Essay Questions (Islam II)

### B1. 2019 – Islam II, Q7 (i)

Question ID: P2019-II-Q7.1  
Year: 2019  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q7 (i)  
Marks: 10 marks (short notes on two topics)  
Source: OL_2019_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

Write short notes on any two of the following.
(i) Scholar M. C. Siddi Lebbe

**Suggested answer:**

- Born in Kandy on 11 June 1838; his father Muhammad Lebbe was Sri Lanka's first Muslim lawyer (1833).
- Mastery of Tamil, English and Arabic; 1862 district lawyer, 1864 Supreme Court advocate.
- On 12 December 1882 he started the newspaper "Muslim Nesan" and became "the first journalist of the Sri Lankan Muslims"; "Gnanadeepam" in 1892.
- Educational work: he was the reason Zahira College, Colombo, was founded; schools in many towns, three schools for Muslim girls.
- "Hassan Bey Charithram" – Sri Lanka's first Tamil novel (1885).
- He worked for the political representation of Muslims, marriage registration and the removal of superstitious practices.
- He died in 1898; a postage stamp was issued on 11-01-1977.

**Answer explanation:** Write centred on his being "the father of the revival of the Sri Lankan Muslims".

### B2. 2025 (2026) – Islam II, Q6 (iii)

Question ID: P2025_2026-II-Q6.2  
Year: 2025 (2026)  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q6 (iii)  
Marks: 10 marks for the whole of Q6  
Source: OL_2025_2026_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(iii) Briefly describe five social reform works carried out by the scholar Siddi Lebbe.

**Suggested answer:**

Any five of the following:
1. Journalism – "Muslim Nesan" in 1882, "Gnanadeepam" in 1892; "the first journalist of the Sri Lankan Muslims".
2. Educational work – an association for educational development with Wapchi Marikar; he paved the way for Zahira College, Colombo; schools in many places.
3. Education of Muslim girls – separate schools in places such as Kandy and Kurunegala.
4. Language education – stressing mastery of four languages: Arabic, Tamil, English and Sinhala.
5. Literary work – "Hassan Bey Charithram" (1885) – regarded as Sri Lanka's first Tamil novel.
6. Political rights – awareness for Muslim representation in the Legislative Council (1889 – M. C. Abdul Rahman appointed).
7. Reform of marriage registration – stressing its need (it later came into force in 1935).
8. Removing superstitious practices – rooting out rituals that came in through South Indian influence.

**Answer explanation:** Describe each work in one sentence.

**Marking guidance (suggested only – not an official marking scheme):** No official marking scheme.
