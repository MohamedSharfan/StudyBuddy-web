# Grade 11 – Chapter 30: The Islamic View of Worldly Life – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_30/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q15 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. Which verse says, "It is He who made the earth easy for you; so walk through its regions and eat of His provision"?

A. 6 Al-An'am: 32
B. 18 Al-Kahf: 46
C. 67 Al-Mulk: 15
D. 2 Al-Baqarah: 201

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: 67:15 – a verse showing that the world has been made subject to human beings.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. What is Islam's position on worldly life?

A. The world must be completely renounced
B. Worldly pleasures must be enjoyed to the full
C. Worldly life and the life of the Hereafter must be seen separately
D. One must neither renounce the world nor drown in it – the middle position

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Islam presents a balanced view between the two extremes; nor must the world and the Hereafter be seen separately.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q3. How does Islam regard working for progress in this world?

A. As an act of 'ibadah
B. As a sin
C. As makruh
D. As unnecessary

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Developing the world through agriculture, science, industry and invention is good work; it is 'ibadah.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q4. The work of developing the world belongs to which of the three great tasks placed on human beings?

A. 'Ibadah
B. Khilafah
C. 'Imarah
D. Da'wah

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Al-'imarah – the task of developing and building up the earth.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q5. Which of the following was NOT one of the resolutions made by the three Companions?

A. Standing in prayer all night
B. Fasting for ever
C. Keeping away from his wife
D. Always being on the Hajj journey

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Night prayer, perpetual fasting and keeping away from one's wife – these were the three resolutions.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q6. What warning did the Prophet ﷺ give the three Companions?

A. Whoever turns away from my way is not of me
B. What you are doing is excellent
C. Worship even more
D. Renounce the world

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: The Prophet ﷺ pointed out that he slept and prayed at night, fasted and also left fasts, and married (Bukhari).

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q7. What source does the textbook give for the saying "There is no monasticism in Islam"?

A. Bukhari
B. Muslim
C. Ahmad
D. Abu Dawud

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: The Prophet ﷺ condemned living in renunciation of the world (Ahmad).

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q8. Which verse says, "The life of this world is only play and amusement; surely the life of the Hereafter is what lasts"?

A. 64 At-Taghabun: 15
B. 6 Al-An'am: 32
C. 18 Al-Kahf: 46
D. 67 Al-Mulk: 15

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: 6:32 – a verse showing the triviality of worldly life.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q9. Which surah says, "Wealth and children are the adornments of the life of this world"?

A. Al-Kahf (18:46)
B. At-Taghabun (64:15)
C. Al-An'am (6:32)
D. An-Nahl (16:8)

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: 18:46 – they do not last.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q10. Which verse says, "Your wealth and your children are only a test"?

A. 6 Al-An'am: 32
B. 18 Al-Kahf: 46
C. 2 Al-Baqarah: 201
D. 64 At-Taghabun: 15

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: 64:15 – the idea that the world is a place of testing.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q11. What clear conclusion is drawn from the two kinds of verses about the world?

A. The world is completely useless
B. Compared with the Hereafter the world is trivial; but seen as the way to success in the Hereafter it is important
C. The world is more important than the Hereafter
D. The world and the Hereafter have no connection

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Salvation in the Hereafter can be gained only by using this world; one cannot succeed by renouncing it.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q12. In which verse is the prayer "Our Lord! Give us good in this world and good in the Hereafter, and protect us from the punishment of the Fire"?

A. 2 Al-Baqarah: 201
B. 25 Al-Furqan: 74
C. 67 Al-Mulk: 15
D. 66 At-Tahrim: 6

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ – 2:201.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q13. What is the state of those who pray, "Our Lord! Give us everything in this world"?

A. They will receive good in both worlds
B. They are the best believers
C. They may get a share in this world, but they will have no share at all in the Hereafter
D. They will get nothing even in this world

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: The world is their whole attention; Allah may give them their share in this world, but they have no share in the Hereafter.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q14. What does the textbook say about the group who seek monasticism, looking only to the Hereafter?

A. Allah loves them very much
B. Allah does not like them; such a life is also not practically possible
C. It is the sunnah way
D. It is an obligatory duty

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: The Quran praises those who see the world and the Hereafter together.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q15. What will wrong understandings of worldly life do to a Muslim?

A. Help success in the Hereafter
B. Become an obstacle to the ideal life
C. Have no effect at all
D. Increase wealth

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Only a clear view keeps worldly life on the right path and guides to salvation in the Hereafter.

Difficulty: Easy
