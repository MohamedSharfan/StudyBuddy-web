# Grade 11 – Chapter 30: The Islamic View of Worldly Life – Flashcards

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_30/Flashcards.md` (Tamil), cards 1–11 in the same order.

---

Flashcard 1

Front: Two extreme views people hold about worldly life?

Back: (1) Salvation comes only by renouncing the world completely; (2) the world is a single chance – it must be enjoyed to the full.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 30 | Topic: Three views

---

Flashcard 2

Front: Three approaches Islam forbids?

Back: Renouncing the world; drowning in the world; seeing worldly life and the Hereafter separately.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 30 | Topic: Three views

---

Flashcard 3

Front: العمارة (Al-'imarah)

Back: The task of developing the earth through agriculture, science, industry and invention.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 30 | Topic: Key terms

Flashcard 4

Front: The three Arabic terms in the lesson's word list?

Back: العبادة ('ibadah), العمارة ('imarah), الخلافة (khilafah).

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 30 | Topic: Key terms

Flashcard 5

Front: How did the Prophet ﷺ explain his balance between worship and life?

Back: I sleep and I pray at night; I fast and I also leave fasts; and I marry.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 30 | Topic: The three Companions

---

Flashcard 6

Front: The ideas given by the two kinds of verses about the world?

Back: (1) The world is trivial, deceptive and a test; (2) the world is valuable and has been made subject to human beings.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 30 | Topic: Verses about the world

---

Flashcard 7

Front: Three animals created for riding and as adornment (An-Nahl)?

Back: Horses, mules, donkeys.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 30 | Topic: Verses about the world

---

Flashcard 8

Front: Where is the life of those who want only the world focused?

Back: On gathering worldly comforts, pleasures and conveniences; even in prayer they ask only for the world.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 30 | Topic: Two kinds of people

---

Flashcard 9

Front: The qualities of those who ask for good in both worlds?

Back: Broad vision, an open heart, clear sight; though they hope for worldly good, they do not forget the Hereafter.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 30 | Topic: Two kinds of people

---

Flashcard 10

Front: Whom does the Quran condemn, and whom does it praise?

Back: It condemns those whose only goal is the world; it praises those who see the world and the Hereafter together.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 30 | Topic: Two kinds of people

---

Flashcard 11

Front: May a Muslim live renouncing the world?

Back: No – he cannot live that way, and must not.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 30 | Topic: Three views
