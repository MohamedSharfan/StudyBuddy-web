## Part B: Structured and Essay Questions (Islam II)

### B1. 2017 – Islam II, Q7 (iv)

Question ID: P2017-II-Q7.4  
Year: 2017  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q7 (iv)  
Marks: 10 marks (short notes on two topics)  
Source: OL_2017_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(iv) The Islamic view of worldly life

**Suggested answer:**

- Renouncing the world and drowning in the world are two extremes; Islam presents the middle position between them.
- Working for progress in this world is 'ibadah; developing the earth ('imarah) is man's task (67:15).
- The incident of the three Companions – "Whoever turns away from my way is not of me" (Bukhari); "There is no monasticism in Islam" (Ahmad).
- The world is trivial and a test (6:32; 18:46; 64:15); but seen as the way to success in the Hereafter it is important.
- The believer asks for good in both worlds (2:201).

**Answer explanation:** Explain the central idea of balance with evidence.
