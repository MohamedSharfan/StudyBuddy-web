# Grade 11 – Chapter 30: The Islamic View of Worldly Life

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_30/Notes.md` (Tamil). English rendering.

## 🎯 What you will learn

- Two extreme views people hold about worldly life
- The balanced (middle) position of Islam
- The Prophet's ﷺ warning about monasticism
- Two kinds of Quran verses about the world
- The prayer asking for good in both worlds

---

## 📖 Opening verse

> هُوَ الَّذِي جَعَلَ لَكُمُ الْأَرْضَ ذَلُولًا فَامْشُوا فِي مَنَاكِبِهَا وَكُلُوا مِن رِّزْقِهِ ۖ وَإِلَيْهِ النُّشُورُ

**Meaning:** It is He who made the earth easy (to live on) for you; so walk through its regions and eat of His provision; and to Him is the resurrection. **(67 Al-Mulk: 15)**

---

## 1️⃣ Why is this view necessary?

- A clear view of worldly life → keeps worldly life on the right path and guides to **salvation in the Hereafter**.
- A wrong understanding → **an obstacle** to the ideal life of a Muslim.

---

## 2️⃣ Three views ⭐

| View | Idea |
|---|---|
| ❌ Renunciation (monasticism) | Salvation comes only by completely renouncing the world |
| ❌ Total enjoyment | Worldly life is a single chance that will not come again; it must be enjoyed to the full; holding back is foolish |
| ✅ **Islam – the middle way** | One must **neither renounce** the world **nor drown** in it; nor must one **separate** worldly life from the life of the Hereafter |

- Salvation in the Hereafter is the basic goal; but Islam does not forbid working for progress in this world – ⭐ it regards it as **'ibadah**.
- Developing the world through agriculture, science, industry and invention is good work.
- ⭐ This belongs to the task of **"'imarah" (العمارة – developing the earth)**, one of the **three great tasks** placed on human beings.
  - Key terms of the lesson: **العبادة ('ibadah) • العمارة ('imarah) • الخلافة (khilafah)**.
- ⭐ A Muslim **cannot live, and must not live,** renouncing the world.

---

## 3️⃣ The incident of the three Companions (Bukhari) ⭐

| Companion | Resolution |
|---|---|
| The first | I will stand in prayer all night, every night |
| The second | I will fast for ever |
| The third | I will keep away from my wife |

**The Prophet's ﷺ reply:** I, who fear Allah most among you, **sleep and also pray** at night; I **fast and also leave** voluntary fasts; and I **marry**.

- ⭐ "**Whoever turns away from my way (Sunnah) is not of me.**"
- ⭐ "**There is no monasticism in Islam**" (Ahmad).

> ⚠️ **Note:** "Whoever turns away from my Sunnah is not of me" is authentic (Bukhari). The exact wording "there is no monasticism in Islam" is not found with a sound chain in the hadith collections, though its meaning is supported by other reports. The textbook cites Ahmad – confirm with your teacher.

---

## 4️⃣ Two kinds of verses about the world

### (a) Worldly life is trivial, deceptive and a test

| Verse | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 6 Al-An'am: 32 | The life of this world is **only play and amusement**; the Hereafter is what lasts |
| 18 Al-Kahf: 46 | **Wealth and children** are **the adornments** of the life of this world |
| 64 At-Taghabun: 15 | Your wealth and your children are only **a test** |

### (b) Worldly life is valuable; it has been made subject to human beings

| Verse | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 67 Al-Mulk: 15 | He made the earth easy for you; travel through its regions and eat of what He has provided |
| 16 An-Nahl | Horses, mules and donkeys – He created them for riding and as adornment |

- ⚠️ The textbook gives the verse about horses and mules as (16 An-Nahl: 80); this meaning is in 16 An-Nahl: 8 – confirm with your teacher.

### The clear conclusion ⭐

- **Compared with** the lasting joy of the Hereafter, this world is **trivial**.
- Seen **as the way** to success in the Hereafter, this world is **important** – one cannot succeed in the Hereafter by ignoring or renouncing it.

---

## 5️⃣ Two kinds of people and their prayers

| Group | Prayer | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Whose whole attention is the world | "Our Lord! Give us (what we want) **in this world**!" | They may get a share in this world; ⭐ **they have no share in the Hereafter** |
| With broad vision and clear sight | ⭐ "Our Lord! Give us **good in this world** and **good in the Hereafter**, and protect us from **the punishment of the Fire**!" **(2 Al-Baqarah: 201)** | Success in both worlds |

> رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ

- The Quran **condemns** those whose only goal is the world, and **praises** those who see the two together.
- Allah does not like the group who seek **monasticism**, looking only to the Hereafter; such a life is also not practically possible.

---

## 💡 Memory tip

**"No renouncing, no drowning – Islam is the middle path."**
The world is like **a field** for the Hereafter – only what is sown here is harvested there.
