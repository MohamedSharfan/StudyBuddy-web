# Grade 11 – Chapter 21: Caliph 'Ali (RA) – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_21/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q16 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. Who was the father of 'Ali (RA)?

A. Abu Talib
B. 'Abd al-Muttalib
C. al-'Abbas
D. Hamzah

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: 'Ali (RA) was the son of Abu Talib, one of the brothers of the Prophet's ﷺ father.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. How old was 'Ali (RA) when prophethood was given to the Prophet ﷺ?

A. Ten
B. Five
C. Fifteen
D. Twenty

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: While still a boy of ten he accepted Islam without hesitation and gained the honour of being "the first boy to enter Islam".

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q3. What does the textbook give as the best example of 'Ali's (RA) courage?

A. Praying openly at the Ka'bah
B. Compiling the Quran
C. Building the navy
D. Sleeping in the Prophet's bed in his place on the night of the hijrah, while the Quraysh surrounded the house

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: He slept in the Prophet's bed despite the danger of being cut to pieces. Praying openly at the Ka'bah – 'Umar (RA).

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q4. How did the Prophet ﷺ praise 'Ali (RA)?

A. As-Siddiq
B. Dhun-Nurayn
C. Asadullah (the Lion of Allah)
D. Al-Faruq

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Because he was unique in accepting the challenge of any enemy, however strong, and going forward to attack, he was praised as "Asadullah".

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q5. Why were the circumstances of 'Ali's (RA) selection as caliph different from those of the three before him?

A. He was chosen by inheritance
B. He wanted to become caliph
C. He lost an election
D. The rebels who had killed 'Uthman (RA) held Madinah at sword-point, so there was no suitable situation for the people of knowledge to meet and consult

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: The first three selections took place with the people's consultation and full approval; 'Ali's (RA) selection took place in a crisis.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q6. What did 'Ali (RA) say when the rebels pressed him to accept the caliphate?

A. I accept at once
B. You do not have the right to choose the caliph; only if the leading figures who have the community's recognition agree can I take it up
C. I will never accept
D. Choose Talhah

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Upholding Islamic political principles, he made the agreement of the leading figures a condition.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q7. Who advised 'Ali (RA) to be flexible in the matter of removing governors?

A. Abu Bakr and 'Umar (RA)
B. al-'Abbas and Hamzah (RA)
C. Talhah and az-Zubayr (RA)
D. al-Hasan and al-Mughirah (RA)

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Al-Hasan and al-Mughirah (RA) gave this advice, but the caliph stayed firm in his decision.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q8. Which of the following is NOT one of the battles fought among Muslims in 'Ali's (RA) time?

A. Badr
B. Siffin
C. Nahrawan
D. Jamal (the Camel)

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Badr was the battle against the Quraysh in the Prophet's ﷺ time. Jamal, Siffin and Nahrawan took place among Muslims in 'Ali's (RA) time.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q9. What responsibility did 'Ali (RA) hold under Abu Bakr (RA)?

A. The judicial service
B. Army commander
C. Distributing the funds of the Bayt al-Mal
D. Head of the navy

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Abu Bakr – distributing the Bayt al-Mal; 'Umar – the judicial service; 'Uthman – chief adviser.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q10. Why did 'Ali (RA), in his rule, delay the measures concerning the killing of 'Uthman (RA)?

A. He was not concerned about it
B. For the safety of the people and the caliphate, until political stability came
C. The culprits were his friends
D. The rebels did not allow it

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: In that chaotic situation hasty action would have caused more bloodshed, so he waited until stability came.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q11. Which of the following is NOT one of 'Ali's (RA) services?

A. Developing the city of Kufa
B. Reorganising the police department
C. Building prisons
D. Creating the first Islamic navy

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: The first navy was in 'Uthman's (RA) time (27 AH). 'Ali (RA): developing Kufa, reorganising the police, judicial halls, prisons, schools.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q12. Which famous grammarian added vowel marks to the letters of the Quran in 'Ali's (RA) time?

A. Imam Sibawayh
B. Abu al-Aswad ad-Du'ali
C. Imam az-Zamakhshari
D. Zayd ibn Thabit

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: The vowel marks were added through Abu al-Aswad ad-Du'ali – this helped non-Arabs too to recite correctly.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q13. Which group, who misunderstood Islam and engaged in extremism, did 'Ali (RA) bring under control during his rule?

A. The Mu'tazilah
B. The Murji'ah
C. The Khawarij
D. The Jabriyyah

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: He controlled the outrages of the Khawarij; in the end he was martyred by a Khariji.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q14. Who attacked and martyred 'Ali (RA)?

A. Abu Lu'lu'ah
B. 'Abd ar-Rahman ibn Muljam, a Khariji
C. 'Abdullah ibn Saba'
D. Musaylimah

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: He was martyred in Ramadan, 40 AH, after being attacked by 'Abd ar-Rahman ibn Muljam, a Khariji.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q15. When was 'Ali (RA) martyred?

A. Dhul-Hijjah, 35 AH
B. Dhul-Hijjah, 23 AH
C. Ramadan, 40 AH
D. Jumada al-Akhirah, 13 AH

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: 40 AH Ramadan – 'Ali; 35 AH – 'Uthman; 23 AH – 'Umar; 13 AH – Abu Bakr.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q16. Which city, his seat of government, did 'Ali (RA) develop?

A. Kufa
B. Madinah
C. Damascus
D. Baghdad

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: 'Ali's (RA) seat of government was the city of Kufa.

Difficulty: Easy
