## 1. A short life history ⭐

| Point | Fact |
|---|---|
| Father | **Abu Talib**, one of the brothers of the Prophet's ﷺ father |
| Upbringing | Grew up **in the care** of the Prophet ﷺ **from early childhood** |
| Accepting Islam | **A boy of ten** when prophethood was given to the Prophet; accepted Islam **at once**, without any hesitation → **the first boy to enter Islam** |
| Title | **"Asadullah" (أسد الله) – the Lion of Allah** – praise given by the Prophet ﷺ |
| Martyrdom | **Ramadan, 40 AH** – after an attack by **'Abd ar-Rahman ibn Muljam**, a **Khariji** |

---

## 2. His personal qualities ⭐
- From childhood he excelled in **bravery, wisdom and strength of personality**.
- Because he lived with the Prophet, he took part from the very beginning in **Islamic work and social work**.

### Courage – the night of the hijrah ⭐
- At the time of the hijrah to Madinah, a large number of Qurayshi disbelievers **surrounded the house**, determined to kill the Prophet.
- Even at this dangerous moment – when the Quraysh might burst in at any time, attack together and **cut him to pieces** – he **slept in the Prophet's bed in his place.**
- 👉 This is **the best example of his courage.**

### Bravery
- **An excellent horseman, and very strong.**
- He took part in **all the battles** fought to protect Islam and the Muslims, and showed astonishing ability.
- He was unique in accepting the challenge of any enemy, however strong, and going forward to attack → **"Asadullah"** (the Lion of Allah).

### Knowledge and justice
- He excelled in knowledge and spent his **wisdom and insight** for Islam.
- He also served as **a judge** who gave **excellent religious rulings**.
- He was **a leading member of the advisory councils of all the caliphs** before him.

> ⚠️ **Note:** The title "Asadullah" (Lion of Allah) is best known in the sources as a title of Hamzah (RA); 'Ali (RA) is also praised as a lion in some reports. Learn the textbook's wording for the exam and confirm with your teacher.

## 3. The background to his taking up the caliphate ⭐
- The circumstances of 'Ali's (RA) selection were **completely different from those of the three before him.**

| The first three caliphs | 'Ali (RA) |
|---|---|
| Chosen according to Islamic political principles, **with the people's consultation and full approval** | In that society **he was the most suitable person**; but there was **no suitable situation** for the people of knowledge to gather or consult |

### The situation
- A crowd from the outer provinces, made up of different elements – **new converts to Islam, uneducated people, and people who did not understand the reality** – had gathered in the capital.
- Having **killed the caliph ('Uthman RA) in a revolt**, they held Madinah under their control **at sword-point**.
- Protecting the Islamic caliphate and bringing the world back to normal was **both necessary and urgent**.
- The trouble-makers pressed one of **'Ali, Talhah, az-Zubayr, 'Abdullah ibn 'Umar, Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas (RA)** to take responsibility; **none of them came forward.**
- Finally they **forced** 'Ali (RA), warning that if he did not accept, they would **put all the people of Madinah to the sword**.

### 'Ali's (RA) position ⭐
> "**You do not have the right to choose the caliph. Only if the leading figures who have the community's recognition agree** can I take up the caliphate."

- The crowd gathered the leading figures **at sword-point** and obtained their **consent**; only then did he take office.

### Removing governors, and the wars
- To bring the administration under control in that chaotic situation, he planned to **remove some of the provincial governors**.
- **Al-Hasan and al-Mughirah (RA)** advised him to be **flexible**; but the caliph stayed **firm** in his decision.
- This grew very quickly and led to **sorrowful events** that cannot be forgotten in Muslim history:

| Three battles among Muslims |
|---|
| **The Battle of the Camel (Jamal)** |
| **The Battle of Siffin** |
| **The Battle of Nahrawan** |

## 4. His services and work ⭐

### In the time of the earlier caliphs
| Caliph | 'Ali's (RA) responsibility |
|---|---|
| **Abu Bakr (RA)** | **Distributing the funds of the Bayt al-Mal** |
| **'Umar (RA)** | **The judicial service** |
| **'Uthman (RA)** | **A chief adviser** in the administration |
| In the councils of all of them | **Senior adviser** |

### In his own caliphate
- Because his rule was **full of challenges**, he had no chance to carry out large projects; still, he took many urgent measures to organise the administration and protect **the stability** of the caliphate:

1. **Removing some governors** who had earned the people's dissatisfaction.
2. **Delaying the measures concerning the killing of 'Uthman (RA)** until political stability came, for the safety of the people and of the caliphate.
3. **Controlling the outrages of the Khawarij**, who misunderstood Islam and engaged in extremist acts.
4. **Developing the city of Kufa**, his seat of government.
5. Measures for **urban development and cultural improvement**.
6. **Reorganising the police department.**
7. **Separate halls for judicial hearings.**
8. **Shelters for neglected livestock.**
9. Building **prisons**.
10. **Schools** for teaching **Arabic grammar and fiqh**.
11. Through the famous grammarian **Abu al-Aswad ad-Du'ali**, **vowel marks (harakat)** were added to the letters of the Quran.

---

## 5. His martyrdom ⭐
- Having carried out many services in a short time, 'Ali (RA) was attacked by **'Abd ar-Rahman ibn Muljam**, a **Khariji**, in **Ramadan, 40 AH**, and was **martyred.**

---

| Caliph | Born | Caliphate | Died | How | Title |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Abu Bakr** | 573 CE | 11 AH | 13 AH (aged 63) | Natural death | **As-Siddiq** |
| **'Umar** | 580s CE | 13 AH | 23 AH | Abu Lu'lu'ah (a Magian) | — |
| **'Uthman** | 576 CE | After 23 AH | 35 AH (aged 82) | Rebels | **Dhun-Nurayn, Jami' al-Quran** |
| **'Ali** | — | After 35 AH | Ramadan, 40 AH | Ibn Muljam (a Khariji) | **Asadullah** |

## ✅ Quick revision
- Son of Abu Talib; grew up in the Prophet's care; accepted Islam at 10 – the first boy.
- Courage of sleeping in the Prophet's bed on the night of the hijrah; "Asadullah".
- Took office under pressure while rebels controlled Madinah at sword-point; "I will accept only if the leading figures agree".
- Al-Hasan and al-Mughirah – advice to be flexible; the battles of Jamal, Siffin and Nahrawan.
- Under the earlier caliphs: distributing the Bayt al-Mal (Abu Bakr), justice ('Umar), adviser ('Uthman).
- Services: developing Kufa, reorganising the police, judicial halls, prisons, schools; Abu al-Aswad ad-Du'ali – vowel marks for the Quran.
- Ramadan, 40 AH – Ibn Muljam (a Khariji).
