Flashcard 1

Front: Who was 'Ali's (RA) father?

Back: Abu Talib – the brother of the Prophet's ﷺ father.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 21 | Topic: Life history

---

Flashcard 2

Front: Who was the first boy to enter Islam?

Back: 'Ali (RA) – at the age of ten.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 21 | Topic: Life history

---

Flashcard 3

Front: أسد الله (Asadullah)

Back: The Lion of Allah – praise given by the Prophet ﷺ to 'Ali (RA) (according to the textbook).

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 21 | Topic: Personal qualities

---

Flashcard 4

Front: What did 'Ali (RA) do on the night of the hijrah?

Back: While the Quraysh surrounded the house, he slept in the Prophet's bed in his place.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 21 | Topic: Personal qualities

---

Flashcard 5

Front: 'Ali's (RA) condition for accepting the caliphate?

Back: The agreement of the leading figures who had the community's recognition.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 21 | Topic: Taking up the caliphate

---

Flashcard 6

Front: The three battles of 'Ali's (RA) time?

Back: Jamal (the Camel), Siffin, Nahrawan – fought among Muslims.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 21 | Topic: Taking up the caliphate

---

Flashcard 7

Front: 'Ali's (RA) responsibilities under the earlier caliphs?

Back: Abu Bakr – distributing the Bayt al-Mal; 'Umar – the judicial service; 'Uthman – chief adviser.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 21 | Topic: Services

---

Flashcard 8

Front: Abu al-Aswad ad-Du'ali

Back: The grammarian who added vowel marks to the letters of the Quran in 'Ali's (RA) time.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 21 | Topic: Services

---

Flashcard 9

Front: Six main services of 'Ali (RA)?

Back: Developing Kufa, reorganising the police, judicial halls, prisons, livestock shelters, schools for Arabic grammar and fiqh.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 21 | Topic: Services

---

Flashcard 10

Front: الخوارج (Al-Khawarij)

Back: A group who misunderstood Islam and engaged in extremism; 'Ali (RA) brought them under control.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 21 | Topic: Services

---

Flashcard 11

Front: 'Ali's (RA) martyrdom?

Back: Ramadan, 40 AH – an attack by 'Abd ar-Rahman ibn Muljam, a Khariji.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 21 | Topic: Martyrdom

---

Flashcard 12

Front: The years of death of the four caliphs?

Back: Abu Bakr – 13 AH; 'Umar – 23 AH; 'Uthman – 35 AH; 'Ali – 40 AH.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 21 | Topic: Comparison
