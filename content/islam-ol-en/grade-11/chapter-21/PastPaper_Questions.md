### A4. 2024 (2025) – Islam I, Q10

Question ID: P2024_2025-I-Q10  
Year: 2024 (2025)  
Paper and question: Islam I, Q10  
Marks: not stated on the paper (Islam I multiple-choice question)  
Source: OL_2024_2025_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

In the table below, Column I gives the names of the four Rightly Guided Caliphs and Column II the important tasks they carried out during their caliphates.

| Column I | Column II |
|---|---|
| (1) Abu Bakr (RA) | A - creating the navy |
| (2) 'Umar (RA) | B - adding vowel marks to the letters of the Quran through the famous grammarian Abu al-Aswad ad-Du'ali |
| (3) 'Uthman (RA) | C - defeating the plot of the group who refused to pay zakat |
| (4) 'Ali (RA) | D - introducing the Hijri calendar year |

Which option is correct when Column II is arranged, in order, to match Column I?

- **A.** A, B, C, D
- **B.** A, C, B, D
- **C.** B, A, C, D
- **D.** C, D, A, B

**Correct answer:** D – C, D, A, B *(option (4) on the original paper)*

**Explanation:** Abu Bakr – defeating those who refused zakat (C); 'Umar – the Hijri calendar (D); 'Uthman – the navy (A); 'Ali – vowel marks through Abu al-Aswad (B). So C, D, A, B.

**Answer status:** Suggested – not an official answer (no official marking scheme was supplied); based on the Grade 10/11 textbook content

### B4. 2025 (2026) – Islam II, Q5 (iii)

Question ID: P2025_2026-II-Q5.2  
Year: 2025 (2026)  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q5 (iii)  
Marks: 10 marks for the whole of Q5  
Source: OL_2025_2026_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(iii) State and briefly explain five of the urgent measures 'Ali (RA) took to protect the stability of the caliphate when he was appointed caliph.

**Suggested answer:**

- After 'Uthman (RA) was killed, while Madinah was under the armed control of the rebels, protecting the caliphate and bringing things back to normal was an urgent need.
- Taking office only with the agreement of the leading figures who had the community's recognition – protecting the legitimacy of the caliphate.
- Removing some provincial governors to bring the administration under control (staying firm even though al-Hasan and al-Mughirah (RA) advised flexibility).
- Moving the capital to Kufa and developing it.
- Reorganising the police; building halls of justice and prisons.
- Facing the battles of Jamal, Siffin and Nahrawan to put down the revolts.

**Answer explanation:** As "urgent measures" are asked for, write them in connection with the background to his taking office.

**Marking guidance (suggested only – not an official marking scheme):** No official marking scheme.

**Answer status:** Needs verification – a suggested answer that could not be fully confirmed from the textbook; check with a teacher

**Verification note:** The textbook gives the removal of governors and the three battles, and in the "services" section the development of Kufa, the reorganisation of the police, etc.; check the list of "five urgent measures" against the textbook. The moving of the capital to Kufa is based on general historical knowledge.
