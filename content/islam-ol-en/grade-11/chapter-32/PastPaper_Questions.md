### A1. 2019 – Islam I, Q27

Question ID: P2019-I-Q27  
Year: 2019  
Paper and question: Islam I, Q27  
Marks: not stated on the paper (Islam I multiple-choice question)  
Source: OL_2019_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

Shameem, riding his motorcycle to the mosque for congregational prayer, gave a lift to a friend he saw on the road, without a helmet. How can this act be regarded?

- **A.** helping to build brotherhood
- **B.** a cause of increasing rewards
- **C.** developing a helpful attitude
- **D.** an act that breaks the law

**Correct answer:** D – an act that breaks the law *(option (4) on the original paper)*

**Explanation:** Carrying a passenger without a helmet breaks the country's civil law; respecting the laws of the nation is a Muslim's duty.

While a curfew was in force to control the spread of COVID-19, some people who usually went to all five congregational prayers kept going to the mosque and praying devoutly. How can this act be regarded?

- **A.** It is an act that brings twenty-seven times the reward.
- **B.** It is fard kifayah.

### B1. 2016 – Islam II, Q6 (i)

Question ID: P2016-II-Q6.1  
Year: 2016  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q6 (i)  
Marks: 10 marks for the whole of Q6  
Source: OL_2016_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(i) Write the meaning of 'ummah'.

**Suggested answer:**

Ummah – linguistically "mother", "community". A group of people united by one belief and one goal. The Muslim ummah = those who, without distinction of race, language or colour, have accepted the kalimah and taken Islam as their way of life; its special qualities are being balanced, enjoining good and forbidding evil, and bearing witness to mankind (3:110, 2:143).

**Answer explanation:** State the linguistic meaning and the meaning in Islamic usage.

### B3. 2018 – Islam II, Q7 (iii)

Question ID: P2018-II-Q7.3  
Year: 2018  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q7 (iii)  
Marks: 10 marks (short notes on two topics)  
Source: OL_2018_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(iii) Patriotism (love of the nation)

**Suggested answer:**

- A Muslim belongs both to the worldwide Muslim ummah and to the Sri Lankan ummah; fulfilling both responsibilities is a moral duty.
- Expressions of patriotism: love for and loyalty to the nation; contributing to national unity; facing its challenges; respecting its civil laws; cooperating to end anti-social activities; learning the national languages; praying for the nation.
- In history, Sri Lankan Muslims fought against the Portuguese, and figures such as T. B. Jayah contributed to the independence struggle – evidence of patriotism.
- As a balanced ummah, it is not difficult to reconcile love of the nation and love of the community.

**Answer explanation:** Stress that patriotism is not contrary to Islam.
