## 📖 Opening verse

> وَمَا كَانَ النَّاسُ إِلَّا أُمَّةً وَاحِدَةً فَاخْتَلَفُوا

**Meaning:** Mankind was (at first) one single ummah (following one religion); then (out of envy) they split into many groups. **(10 Yunus: 19)**

---

## 1️⃣ Ummah – meaning

- ⭐ Linguistically: **"mother", "community"**.
- Definitions given by sociologists:
  - A group of people who came from one origin
  - A community united by inherited characteristics
  - A body of people united by one belief and one goal
  - People joined together by religion, region or time
- In the Islamic tradition it is a **technical term** of religious sociology.
- ⭐ The Quran uses the word "ummah" in **more than fifty** places.

### What becomes clear from the Quran

- Human beings, born of the same father and mother and living as one ummah with one belief, split into many groups because of disagreement.
- ⭐ Allah sent prophets to unite the divided groups through the kalimah "**La ilaha illallah**".

| Verse | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 10 Yunus: 19 | They were one ummah, and then they split |
| 23 Al-Mu'minun: 51–52 | O messengers! Eat of the good things and do righteous deeds; this ummah of yours is one ummah; I am your Lord, so fear Me |
| 42 Ash-Shura: 8 | Had Allah willed, He would have made them one ummah; He admits whom He wills into His mercy |
| 11 Hud: 118 | Had your Lord willed, He would have made all people one ummah; they will continue to differ, except those on whom He has mercy |

- ⭐ Forgetting differences, believing in the One Allah and uniting as one ummah is something that comes **from Allah's mercy**.

---

## 2️⃣ The Muslim ummah – definition ⭐

- Islamic social thinkers: **Imam Ibn Khaldun, al-Farabi, Rashid Rida**.
- Definition: a collection of individuals who share the common feature of **belonging to the religion of Islam**, and who have these special qualities –
  1. **Being balanced (the middle way)**
  2. **Enjoining good and forbidding evil**
  3. **Bearing witness to mankind**

| Verse | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ⭐ 3 Al 'Imran: 110 | You are **the best ummah** raised for mankind; you enjoin good and forbid evil |
| ⭐ 2 Al-Baqarah: 143 | We have made you **a balanced (middle) ummah**, so that you may be witnesses over mankind |

- ⭐ Paying no attention to differences of nation, race, language or colour, everyone who has declared the kalimah "**La ilaha illallah Muhammadur Rasulullah**" and taken Islam as his way of life is a member of the Muslim ummah.

---

## 3️⃣ The origin of the Muslim ummah ⭐

- It was **the cherished dream** of **Ibrahim (AS)**, the father of the prophets, and **Isma'il (AS)**.
- **While building the Ka'bah** they prayed: "O Allah! Make us both Muslims submitting to You, and make from our descendants a Muslim ummah submitting to You."
  - (The textbook gives no verse number; this is the meaning of 2 Al-Baqarah: 128.)
- Signs that the prayer was accepted:
  - **Muhammad ﷺ**, the founder of the ummah, came from the line of Isma'il (AS).
  - **The Ka'bah** unites all the Muslims of the world as one ummah.

---

## 4️⃣ 9 guidelines for protecting the ummah ⭐

1. Holding fast to the Quran and Sunnah
2. Obeying the leadership
3. Giving importance to collective duties
4. Freedom of opinion for individuals
5. Accepting the **consensus decision** when there is disagreement on public matters
6. Muslims acting together in unity
7. Bearing witness and acting as a model
8. Maintaining brotherhood
9. Maintaining equality

- Each individual's strength and safety depend **on the strength and safety of the ummah**.
- **Salvation in the Hereafter** too is for those who live joined to the ummah.

---

## 5️⃣ The warning about division

- ⭐ Hadith (Ibn Majah): The Children of Israel split into **seventy-one** groups; my ummah will split into **seventy-three** groups; all will enter the Fire except one; that one group is **as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah**.
- This must not be misunderstood: organisations within the ummah such as a **firqah, ta'ifah, hizb, jama'ah, madhhab, harakah, tariqah, jam'iyyah** **cannot be regarded as groups that will enter the Fire** if they accept Islamic 'aqidah and the Shari'ah.
- Individuals and organisations working for the survival and spread of the ummah belong to **ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah**.

### Broad-mindedness ⭐

- Hadith: "Whoever **prays our prayer**, **faces our qiblah** and **eats what we have slaughtered** is one of us." (The textbook gives no source; this hadith is in Bukhari.)
- 💡 Rather than splitting over **minor matters**, we should **unite on the fundamentals**.

---

## 6️⃣ Our duties as the Sri Lankan ummah

Fulfilling our responsibilities as members of the worldwide Muslim ummah and as members of **the Sri Lankan ummah** is a moral duty.

**What we should practise as people of Sri Lanka:**

- Love for the nation; loyalty to the nation
- Respecting the feelings of the nation's people
- Contributing to national unity; facing the challenges the nation meets
- Respecting the nation's **civil laws**
- **Giving up** ethnic and religious **self-interest**
- Cooperating to end anti-social activities
- Following the Prophet's ﷺ example in dealing with other communities
- Keeping our religious and cultural identity and **the limits of the Shari'ah**
- Friendly dialogue with fellow communities
- ⭐ **Learning the national languages**
- Pursuing common goals
- **Praying for the nation**

- ⭐ As a balanced ummah, it is not difficult to reconcile **love of the nation and love of the community**.
