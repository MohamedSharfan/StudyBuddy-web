# Grade 11 – Chapter 32: The Concept of the Ummah – Flashcards

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_32/Flashcards.md` (Tamil), cards 1–12 in the same order.

---

Flashcard 1

Front: Four definitions of the ummah given by sociologists?

Back: People from one origin; a community united by inherited traits; people united by one belief and goal; people joined by religion, region or time.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 32 | Topic: Meaning of ummah

---

Flashcard 2

Front: 42 Ash-Shura: 8 – meaning?

Back: Had Allah willed, He would have made them one ummah; He admits whom He wills into His mercy.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 32 | Topic: The ummah in the Quran

---

Flashcard 3

Front: 11 Hud: 118 – meaning?

Back: Had your Lord willed, He would have made all people one ummah; apart from those on whom He has mercy, they will continue to differ.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 32 | Topic: The ummah in the Quran

---

Flashcard 4

Front: What does the Quran say is a matter of Allah's mercy?

Back: Forgetting differences, believing in the One Allah and uniting as one ummah.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 32 | Topic: The ummah in the Quran

---

Flashcard 5

Front: Who is a member of the Muslim ummah?

Back: Everyone, regardless of nation, race, language or colour, who has declared the kalimah and taken Islam as his way of life.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 32 | Topic: The Muslim ummah

Flashcard 6

Front: The prayer of Ibrahim and Isma'il (AS) while building the Ka'bah?

Back: Make us both Muslims submitting to You; and make from our descendants a Muslim ummah submitting to You.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 32 | Topic: Origin of the ummah

Flashcard 7

Front: The first three guidelines for protecting the ummah?

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 32 | Topic: Protecting the ummah

Flashcard 8

Flashcard 10

Front: The Prophet's ﷺ position on division in the ummah?
