# Grade 11 – Chapter 32: The Concept of the Ummah – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_32/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q19 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. In which meanings is the word "ummah" generally used linguistically?

A. Father, country
B. Mother, community
C. Religion, law
D. Tribe, language

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Ummah – linguistically "mother" and "community".

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. In roughly how many places does the Quran use the word "ummah"?

A. Ten places
B. Twenty places
C. More than fifty places
D. More than a hundred places

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: The textbook says it is used in more than fifty places.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q3. Which verse says, "Mankind was one single ummah; then they split into many groups"?

A. 10 Yunus: 19
B. 11 Hud: 118
C. 42 Ash-Shura: 8
D. 3 Al 'Imran: 110

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: 10:19 – one religion at first; division came through envy.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q4. Through what did Allah send the prophets to unite the divided groups of mankind?

A. The sword
B. Wealth
C. Language
D. The kalimah "La ilaha illallah"

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: The prophets were sent to unite people through the kalimah.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q5. Which verse says, "O messengers! Eat of the good things … this ummah of yours is one ummah"?

A. 2 Al-Baqarah: 143
B. 23 Al-Mu'minun: 51–52
C. 10 Yunus: 19
D. 11 Hud: 118

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: 23:51–52 – "I am your Lord, so fear Me."

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q6. Which of the following is NOT one of the Islamic social thinkers who defined the Muslim ummah?

A. Imam Ibn Khaldun
B. Al-Farabi
C. Rashid Rida
D. Sir Sayyid Ahmad Khan

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: The textbook mentions three: Ibn Khaldun, al-Farabi and Rashid Rida.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q7. Which of the following is NOT one of the special qualities mentioned in the definition of the Muslim ummah?

A. Being balanced
B. Enjoining good and forbidding evil
C. Belonging to one race
D. Bearing witness to mankind

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: The Muslim ummah pays no attention to differences of nation, race, language or colour.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q8. Which verse says, "You are the best ummah raised for mankind; you enjoin good and forbid evil"?

A. 3 Al 'Imran: 110
B. 2 Al-Baqarah: 143
C. 42 Ash-Shura: 8
D. 23 Al-Mu'minun: 52

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: 3:110 – the best ummah; 2:143 – the balanced ummah.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q9. Which verse says, "We have made you a balanced ummah, so that you may be witnesses over mankind"?

A. 3 Al 'Imran: 110
B. 10 Yunus: 19
C. 11 Hud: 118
D. 2 Al-Baqarah: 143

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: 2:143 – being balanced, and bearing witness.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q10. The origin of the Muslim ummah was whose cherished dream?

A. Musa and Harun (AS)
B. Ibrahim and Isma'il (AS)
C. Nuh (AS)
D. 'Isa (AS)

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: While building the Ka'bah they prayed for a Muslim ummah to be raised from their descendants.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q11. What is a sign that the prayer of Ibrahim and Isma'il (AS) was accepted?

A. Muhammad ﷺ came from the line of Isma'il (AS), and the Ka'bah unites the Muslims as one ummah
B. Rain fell in Makkah
C. Only the appearance of the well of Zamzam
D. The Jews accepted Islam

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: The textbook gives both of these as signs.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q12. Which of the following is NOT one of the guidelines given by the Quran and Sunnah for protecting the ummah?

A. Obeying the leadership
B. Freedom of opinion for individuals
C. Maintaining equality
D. Everyone making decisions separately on their own

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: When there is disagreement on public matters, the consensus decision must be accepted.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q13. According to the hadith (Ibn Majah), into how many groups did the Children of Israel split?

A. Seventy
B. Seventy-one
C. Seventy-two
D. Seventy-three

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: The Children of Israel – 71; the Prophet's ummah – 73; all but one in the Fire; that one is as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q14. In the hadith about the 73 groups, which group enters Paradise?

A. As-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah
B. The Mu'tazilah
C. The Khawarij
D. The Jabriyyah

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Individuals and organisations working for the survival and spread of the ummah belong to ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q15. What is the textbook's position on organisations within the ummah such as madhhabs, jama'ahs, tariqahs and jam'iyyahs?

A. All of them are groups that will enter the Fire
B. They must be banned
C. If they accept Islamic 'aqidah and the Shari'ah, they cannot be regarded as groups that will enter the Fire
D. They are outside the ummah

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: The textbook says the hadith of the 73 groups must not be misunderstood.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q16. "Whoever prays our prayer, faces our qiblah and eats what we have slaughtered is one of us" – what does this hadith teach?

A. Rather than splitting over minor matters, we should unite on the fundamentals
B. Prayer alone is enough
C. Food rules are what matter most
D. The qiblah may be changed

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: The Prophet ﷺ presented the idea of the ummah with great broad-mindedness.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q17. Which of the following is NOT one of the guidelines Muslims should follow as people of Sri Lanka?

A. Respecting the nation's civil laws
B. Learning the national languages
C. Putting ethnic and religious self-interest first
D. Praying for the nation

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: The guideline is to give up ethnic and religious self-interest.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q18. As a balanced ummah, what does the textbook say it is not difficult for Muslims to reconcile?

A. Wealth and poverty
B. Love of the nation and love of the community
C. Politics and business
D. Education and sport

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: All of these are drawn from the general guidance of Islam.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q19. According to the textbook, on what do each individual's strength and safety depend?

A. His wealth
B. Only his family
C. The government
D. The strength and safety of the ummah

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Salvation in the Hereafter too is for those who live joined to the ummah.

Difficulty: Easy
