# Grade 11 – Chapter 27: Marriage (النكاح)

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_27/Notes.md` (Tamil). English rendering.

## 🎯 What you will learn

- The family, and the importance of marriage
- The legal status of marriage (sunnah, wajib)
- The three purposes of marriage
- Nikah – its meaning; mahram (permanent, temporary)
- The 4 essentials (fard) for a valid nikah: bride and groom, ijab–qabul, wali, witnesses
- Mahr and walimah

## 📖 Opening verse

> وَأَنكِحُوا الْأَيَامَىٰ مِنكُمْ وَالصَّالِحِينَ مِنْ عِبَادِكُمْ وَإِمَائِكُمْ

**Meaning:** Marry off those among you who have no spouse. **(24 An-Nur: 32)**

---

## 1️⃣ The family and marriage

- **The family is the foundation** of Islamic life; some scholars call Islam "**a family-centred religion**".
- The family = a small society; **the first seed** of the larger society. Only if the seed is healthy will the tree be healthy.
- The Quran explains family law in detail, **linking it with iman**; it also speaks about the families of the prophets.
- Marriage is **the way of the prophets**.

### Hadith (Bukhari) ⭐

O young people! Whoever among you is able should marry, for it **lowers the gaze** and **guards chastity**. Whoever is not able should **fast**, for it is a protection for him.

---

## 2️⃣ The legal status of marriage

| Situation | Ruling |
|---|---|
| A man with physical strength and financial means | ⭐ **Sunnah** |
| If such a man fears he will fall into sin | ⭐ **Wajib** |
| A woman | The same rule; but **there is no condition of financial means** |

---

## 3️⃣ The purposes of marriage (from the Shari'ah's point of view)

### 🔸 (1) Producing righteous offspring

- The long-term survival of the human race; producing good **dhurriyyah** (offspring).
- Beyond simply having children, building a long line of descendants → the mechanism by which the human race continues.

### 🔸 (2) Bringing peace of body and mind

- **30 Ar-Rum: 21** – Among Allah's signs is that He created for you spouses from among yourselves so that you may find peace in them, and He placed love and mercy between you.
- ⭐ The Quran describes the bond of husband and wife with the word **"sakan" (السكن – tranquillity)**.
- Peace of mind → greater effectiveness, many times more productivity.
- Peace of body = protection from sins, crimes, deadly diseases and social ills.
- ⭐ A married person = **"muhsan" (المحصن)**; this comes from the word **"hisn" (a fortress)** → marriage is a fortress of protection.
- "Whoever marries has established half of his religion" (the textbook gives no source for this).

### 🔸 (3) Creating a good social structure

- The family of husband and wife is the basis of the social structure.
- **25 Al-Furqan: 74** – Our Lord! Grant us comfort of the eyes through our wives and offspring, and make us leaders of the God-fearing (muttaqin).
- ⭐ Hadith (Bukhari): The leader is **responsible** for the people, the man for his family, the woman for the home and children; each will be questioned.

💡 Marriage that helps fulfil these purposes = **'ibadah** (worship).

---

## 4️⃣ Nikah (النكاح) – meaning

- From the Arabic word "**nakaha**".
- ⭐ A **contract**, made with God-consciousness, by which a man and a woman gain the status of husband and wife **religiously, legally and socially**.
- Because it takes place in the name of Allah before witnesses → **'ibadah + social contract**.
- Only a man and woman who are **ajnabi** (non-mahram, "strangers") to each other may marry; marriage between **mahrams** is haram.

---

## 5️⃣ Mahram (المحرم)

**A close relative whom one is not permitted to marry.** Two kinds: **temporary mahram** and **permanent mahram**.

⚠️ The textbook's list orders them "1. temporary, 2. permanent", and then calls the permanent mahram "the first kind" – confirm with your teacher.

### 🔸 Permanent mahram – three causes ⭐

**Blood relationship | Relationship by marriage | Relationship by breastfeeding (milk)**

(The lists below are from a man's point of view.)

#### (a) Blood relationship

1. Mother, mother's mother … (upwards)
2. Daughters, daughters' children … (downwards)
3. Sisters (children of one's own parents) and their children … (downwards)
4. Children of one's own grandparents – **this stops at the first level**: one may not marry the sisters of one's mother or father (aunts); but ⭐ **one may marry their children (cousins)**.

#### (b) Relationship by marriage

1. Wife's mother, her mother's mother … (upwards)
2. Daughters of a wife with whom the marriage was consummated, and their children … (downwards)
3. Son's wife, son's son's wife, daughter's son's wife … (downwards)
4. Father's wife

#### (c) Relationship by breastfeeding

Mahram status also arises from drinking the milk of a woman who is not one's own mother:

1. The woman who gave the milk
2. Her mother
3. Her husband's mother
4. Her sister
5. Her husband's sister
6. Her husband's children, and their children
7. A sister related through drinking milk from the same woman

**Conditions for breastfeeding to create mahram status** ⭐

- The milk was drunk when the child was **under two years old**
- It was drunk directly from the breast
- At least **five sucklings** (separate feeds) were taken

### 🔸 Those forbidden temporarily

1. Two full sisters at the same time
2. A woman and her paternal aunt at the same time
3. A woman and her maternal aunt at the same time
4. A woman who is already someone's wife
5. A woman divorced with **three talaqs** – temporarily, for her first husband
6. A **mushrik** woman
7. Adulteresses – until they repent and become completely clean of it

💡 Those one may marry = **ajnabis** (الأجنبي); those one may not = **mahrams**.

## 6️⃣ The 4 essentials (fard) for a valid nikah ⭐

| # | Element |
|---|---|
| 1 | Groom – bride |
| 2 | Ijab – qabul |
| 3 | Wali |
| 4 | Witnesses |

### Conditions for the groom

- Being a Muslim
- Not already having **four** wives at the same time
- Being **ajnabi** to the bride
- Not being in **ihram** for Hajj

### Conditions for the bride

- Being **a Muslim or one of the Ahl al-Kitab** (أهل الكتاب – People of the Book)
- Not being another man's wife
- Not being in **'iddah**
- Not being in ihram for Hajj

### Ijab (الإيجاب) – the offer

- The words said by the **wali** to declare that he is giving the bride to the groom.
- ⭐ The words "**ankahtuka**" (أنكحتك) and "**zawwajtuka**" (زوجتك) are best; it may also be done in a language that is understood.

### Qabul (القبول) – the acceptance

- The words said by **the groom** to declare that he accepts the bride given by the wali.
- ⭐ There must be **no delay** between the ijab and the qabul.

### Wali (الولي)

- Hadith (Ibn Majah): A woman **cannot marry herself off**; a woman **cannot act as wali** for another woman.
- A marriage made without the wali's permission is **invalid**.

**Those entitled to be wali (in order):**

1. Father
2. Father's father
3. Full brother
4. Paternal half-brother
5. Full brother's son
6. Paternal half-brother's son
7. Father's full brother (paternal uncle)
8. His son
9. Father's paternal half-brother
10. His son
11. Grandfather's full brother
12. Grandfather's paternal half-brother
13. Their sons
14. ⭐ **The judge (hakim)**

- If the earlier one is absent or loses his eligibility, the next one takes the right.

**The 6 qualifications of a wali:** Muslim, male, adult, of sound mind, free, not in ihram.

### Witnesses (الشاهد – shahid)

- ⭐ A nikah without a wali and **two witnesses** is **batil** (invalid).
- Conditions: the wali's conditions + knowing the language of the contract + the faculties of speech, hearing and sight.

---

## 7️⃣ Mahr (المهر) ⭐

- The **obligatory gift** given **by the groom to the bride** at marriage; giving it is **wajib**.
- Quran: Give the women you marry their mahr graciously.
  - ⚠️ The textbook gives this as (4 An-Nisa': 5); this meaning about mahr is generally found in 4 An-Nisa': 4 – confirm with your teacher.
- It may be given as money, goods, property or **a service**.
- Some scholars say that making the contract without stating the mahr is **makruh**.

---

## 8️⃣ Walimah (الوليمة)

- In general, a feast for a happy occasion; in usage, the feast given **on behalf of the groom after the marriage, once the couple have come together**.
- ⭐ Ruling: **sunnah mu'akkadah** (a strongly recommended sunnah).
- Giving it after the couple come together is best; giving it after the contract is also acceptable.
- According to the husband's means; **simple, avoiding extravagance and waste**.
- It is best to invite relatives, friends, neighbours, poor and rich **without discrimination**.

| Mahr vs walimah | Mahr | Walimah |
|---|---|---|
| For whom | The bride | The guests (a feast) |
| Who gives it | The groom | On behalf of the groom |
| Ruling | Wajib | Sunnah mu'akkadah |

## ✅ Quick revision

- 24:32 – marry off those who have no spouse.
- Sunnah (for those with means); wajib (if there is fear of sin).
- 3 purposes: righteous offspring, peace of body and mind (sakan, 30:21), social structure (25:74).
- Permanent mahram – blood, marriage, breastfeeding.
- Breastfeeding: under 2 years, from the breast, at least 5 sucklings.
- 4 essentials: bride and groom, ijab–qabul, wali, 2 witnesses.
- Order of walis: from the father … to the judge (hakim) – 14.
- Mahr – wajib; walimah – sunnah mu'akkadah.
