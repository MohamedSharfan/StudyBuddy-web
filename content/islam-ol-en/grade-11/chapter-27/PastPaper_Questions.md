### A3. 2017 – Islam I, Q12

Marks: not stated on the paper (Islam I multiple-choice question)

In which of the following situations is marriage haram?

- **A.** after marrying and divorcing three women, trying for a fourth marriage
- **B.** a man living with four wives agreeing to a fifth marriage
- **C.** marrying without the first wife's consent
- **D.** marrying the wife's full sister while the wife is alive

**Correct answer:** D – marrying the wife's full sister while the wife is alive *(option (4) on the original paper)*

### B1. 2016 – Islam II, Q1 (viii)

Question ID: P2016-II-Q1.8  
Year: 2016  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q1 (viii)  
Marks: 20 marks for the whole of Q1  
Source: OL_2016_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(viii) What are the meanings of the terms ijab and qabul?

**Suggested answer:**

- Ijab: the words said by the bride's wali to declare that he gives the bride to the groom (e.g. "ankahtuka", "zawwajtuka").
- Qabul: the words said by the groom to declare that he accepts the bride given by the wali.

**Answer explanation:** Both are among the essentials (fard) of nikah; there must be no delay between them.

**Marking guidance (suggested only – not an official marking scheme):** The meaning of both terms.

(ii) State three reasons why polygamy is permitted.

**Suggested answer:**

Any three of the following:
1. Giving protection and care to widows and orphans (4:3 – background concerning orphans).
2. Providing a life partner for women when there are more women than men in society (e.g. after wars).
