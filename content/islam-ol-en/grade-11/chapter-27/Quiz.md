# Grade 11 – Chapter 27: Marriage – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_27/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q20 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. Which verse says, "Marry off those among you who have no spouse"?

A. 30 Ar-Rum: 21
B. 25 Al-Furqan: 74
C. 4 An-Nisa': 29
D. 24 An-Nur: 32

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: 24:32 – وَأَنكِحُوا الْأَيَامَىٰ مِنكُمْ.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. What did the Prophet ﷺ tell young people who are not able to marry (Bukhari)?

A. To fast
B. To stay in seclusion
C. To go to work
D. To pray more

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Those who are able should marry – it lowers the gaze and guards chastity; for those not able, fasting is a protection.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q3. If a man with physical strength and financial means fears he will fall into sin, what is the ruling on marriage for him?

A. Sunnah
B. Mubah
C. Wajib
D. Makruh

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Normally sunnah; wajib if there is fear of sin. For a woman there is no condition of financial means.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q4. What does "sakan", the word the Quran uses to describe the bond of husband and wife, mean?

A. A fortress
B. Tranquillity
C. Offspring
D. Contract

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: As-sakan – tranquillity (30:21). Hisn – fortress; dhurriyyah – offspring.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q5. The word "muhsan", which the Quran uses for a married person, comes from which word?

A. Hisn (fortress)
B. Hasan (beauty)
C. Ihsan (excellence)
D. Sakan (tranquillity)

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Marriage is a fortress of protection; the married person is someone kept safe inside it.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q6. Which verse does the textbook give for the purpose of creating a good social structure?

A. 24 An-Nur: 32
B. 30 Ar-Rum: 21
C. 25 Al-Furqan: 74
D. 4 An-Nisa': 1

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: 25:74 – grant us comfort of the eyes through our wives and offspring, and make us leaders of the muttaqin.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q7. From which Arabic word does the term "nikah" come?

A. Zawwaja
B. Nakaha
C. 'Aqada
D. Hasuna

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: From nakaha; a contract for the religious, legal and social status of husband and wife.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q8. What are the three causes of permanent mahram status?

A. Blood, friendship, neighbourhood
B. Blood relationship, relationship by marriage, relationship by breastfeeding
C. Relationship by marriage, talaq, 'iddah
D. Breastfeeding, trade, neighbourhood

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Permanent mahram status arises from all three: blood, marriage and breastfeeding.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q9. On the basis of blood relationship, a man is permitted to marry ONE of the following. Who?

A. His mother's sister
B. His father's sister
C. His sister's daughter
D. His father's sister's daughter

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: The prohibition on the children of grandparents stops at the first level – one may not marry one's aunts, but one may marry their children.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q10. Who is among those one may not marry because of a relationship by marriage?

A. The wife's mother
B. The wife's sister (after the wife's death)
C. The wife's friend
D. The aunt's daughter

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: The wife's mother, the daughters of a wife with whom the marriage was consummated, the son's wife and the father's wife – permanent mahrams.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q11. Which of the following is NOT one of the conditions for breastfeeding to create mahram status?

A. Drinking while a child under two years old
B. Drinking directly from the breast
C. Drinking at least five sucklings
D. Even a single suckling is enough

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: The textbook says at least five sucklings must have been taken.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q12. Who is among those forbidden in marriage only temporarily?

A. Mother
B. The woman who gave one milk
C. The full sister of one's existing wife (at the same time)
D. Daughter

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Two sisters cannot be wives at the same time – this is a temporary prohibition. The others are permanent mahrams.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q13. Which of the following is NOT one of the essentials for a valid nikah?

A. Ijab – qabul
B. Walimah
C. Wali
D. Witnesses

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: The 4 essentials: bride and groom, ijab–qabul, wali, witnesses. Walimah is sunnah mu'akkadah.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q14. Which is a correct condition for the bride?

A. Being a Muslim or one of the Ahl al-Kitab
B. She may be a mushrik
C. She may marry while in 'iddah
D. She may marry while in ihram

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Muslim/Ahl al-Kitab, not another man's wife, not in 'iddah, not in ihram.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q15. Which Arabic words are best for the ijab?

A. Qabiltu, raditu
B. Bismillah, al-hamdu lillah
C. Ankahtuka, zawwajtuka
D. Tallaqtu, raja'tu

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: أنكحتك، زوجتك – the ijab words said by the wali; it may also be done in a language that is understood.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q16. What is the important rule about ijab and qabul?

A. The wali must say the qabul
B. There must be no delay between them
C. Both must be only in writing
D. There must be a day's gap

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Ijab – the wali; qabul – the groom; there must be no delay between the two.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q17. In the order of those entitled to be wali, who comes first and who comes last?

A. Full brother – father
B. Mother – judge
C. Father's father – maternal uncle
D. Father – the judge (hakim)

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: First: the father; last (14th): the judge. If the earlier one is absent, the next takes the right.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q18. What is the status of a nikah without a wali and two witnesses?

A. Makruh
B. Sahih (valid)
C. Batil (invalid)
D. Sunnah

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: A nikah without a wali and two witnesses is batil.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q19. Which statement about mahr is correct?

A. It is given by the bride to the groom
B. It is only sunnah
C. It is an obligatory gift from the groom to the bride; it may be given as money, goods, property or a service
D. It can only be given as money

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Giving mahr is wajib; some scholars say that making the contract without stating the mahr is makruh.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q20. What is the ruling on walimah?

A. Sunnah mu'akkadah
B. Fard
C. Wajib
D. Makruh

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Giving a walimah is sunnah mu'akkadah; it is best given simply, without extravagance, inviting people without discrimination.

Difficulty: Easy
