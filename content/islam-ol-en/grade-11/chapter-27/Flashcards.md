Flashcard 1

Front: How do some scholars describe Islam?

Back: "A family-centred religion" – the family is the foundation of Islamic life.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 27 | Topic: Family and marriage

Flashcard 2

Front: The three purposes of marriage?

Back: Producing righteous offspring; peace of body and mind; a good social structure.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 27 | Topic: Purposes of marriage

Flashcard 3

Front: الذرية (Adh-dhurriyyah)

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 27 | Topic: Purposes of marriage

Flashcard 4

Front: 30 Ar-Rum: 21 – meaning?

Back: It is a sign of Allah that He created spouses for you from among yourselves so that you find peace, and placed love and mercy between you.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 27 | Topic: Purposes of marriage

Flashcard 5

Front: The hadith on family responsibility (Bukhari)?

Back: The leader is responsible for the people, the man for the family, the woman for the home and children; each will be questioned.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 27 | Topic: Purposes of marriage

---

Flashcard 6

Front: المحرم (Al-mahram)

Back: A close relative whom one may not marry – two kinds, permanent and temporary.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 27 | Topic: Mahram

---

Flashcard 7

Front: الأجنبي (Al-ajnabi)

Back: A non-mahram ("stranger") whom one is permitted to marry.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 27 | Topic: Mahram

---

Flashcard 8

Front: The 7 who become mahram through breastfeeding?

Back: The woman who gave the milk, her mother, her husband's mother, her sister, her husband's sister, her husband's children and grandchildren, the milk sister.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 27 | Topic: Mahram

---

Flashcard 9

Front: A woman divorced with three talaqs – for her first husband?

Back: She is temporarily not permitted to marry him.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 27 | Topic: Mahram

---

Flashcard 10

Front: The 4 conditions for the groom?

Back: Muslim; not having 4 wives at the same time; ajnabi to the bride; not in ihram.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 27 | Topic: Essentials of nikah

---

Flashcard 11

Front: The hadith on the wali (Ibn Majah)?

Back: A woman cannot marry herself off; a woman cannot act as wali for another woman.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 27 | Topic: Wali

---

Flashcard 12

Front: The 6 qualifications of a wali?

Back: Muslim, male, adult, of sound mind, free, not in ihram.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 27 | Topic: Wali

---

Flashcard 13

Front: Extra conditions for witnesses?

Back: In addition to the wali's conditions – knowing the language of the contract; the faculties of speech, hearing and sight.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 27 | Topic: Witnesses

---

Flashcard 14

Front: الوليمة (Al-walimah) – the best way?

Back: After the couple come together, on the groom's behalf, without extravagance, inviting everyone – poor and rich – without discrimination.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 27 | Topic: Walimah
