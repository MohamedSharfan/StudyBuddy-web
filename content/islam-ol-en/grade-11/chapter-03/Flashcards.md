# Grade 11 – Chapter 3: At-Tawhid – Flashcards

---

Flashcard 1

Front: التوحيد (At-tawhid)

Back: Accepting Allah alone as the one God and obeying Him – belief in one God.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 3 | Topic: Definition

---

Flashcard 2

Front: وحد (Wahada)

Back: The root of the word tawhid.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 3 | Topic: Word root

---

Flashcard 3

Front: واحد (Wahid)

Back: One.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 3 | Topic: Word root

---

Flashcard 4

Front: وحّد (Wahhada)

Back: The verb from which tawhid is derived – he made one, he singled out.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 3 | Topic: Word root

---

Flashcard 5

Front: What are the three meanings of tawhid?

Back: 1) Allah is the Creator of everything 2) He alone is worthy of worship 3) He is perfect and without fault.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 3 | Topic: Three meanings

---

Flashcard 6

Front: لَا إِلٰهَ إِلَّا أَنَا فَاعْبُدُوْنِ

Back: (meaning) There is no god except Me, so worship Me (21:25).

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 3 | Topic: Worship

---

Flashcard 7

Front: لَيْسَ كَمِثْلِهِ شَيْءٌ

Back: (meaning) There is nothing like Him (42:11).

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 3 | Topic: Perfection

---

Flashcard 8

Front: What attributes of Allah does Surah al-Ikhlas (112:1–4) mention?

Back: One, Self-Sufficient, does not give birth, was not born, has no equal.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 3 | Topic: Perfection

---

Flashcard 9

Front: What are the four proofs of the importance of tawhid?

Back: The call of all the messengers; many verses including a whole surah; a condition for good deeds; Paradise forbidden to those who reject it.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 3 | Topic: Importance

---

Flashcard 10

Front: What is the warning in verse 39:65?

Back: If you associate partners, all your good deeds will be wasted and you will be among the losers.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 3 | Topic: Importance

---

Flashcard 11

Front: What is the warning in verse 5:72?

Back: Paradise is forbidden to whoever associates partners with Allah; his refuge is the Fire.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 3 | Topic: Importance

---

Flashcard 12

Front: Which three sets of verses show that Allah is the Creator?

Back: 39:62–63, 40:62, 23:88–89.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 3 | Topic: Creator
