# Grade 11 – Chapter 3: At-Tawhid – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_03/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q13 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. From which root does the word "tawhid" come?

A. Wahada
B. 'Abada
C. Amana
D. Aslama

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Tawhid comes from the root "wahada"; it is taken directly from the verb "wahhada". "'Abada" is the root of 'ibadah.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. What does the word "wahid" mean?

A. The Creator
B. One
C. Worship
D. A messenger

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Wahid = one. From this came the word tawhid, meaning belief in one God.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q3. Which of the following is NOT one of the meanings of the word "tawhid"?

A. Singling out
B. Making one
C. Unifying
D. Making many

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Tawhid = singling out, making one, unifying. Making many is its direct opposite.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q4. Which verse says that it was revealed to every messenger, "There is no god except Me, so worship Me"?

A. 5:72 (al-Ma'idah)
B. 112:1 (al-Ikhlas)
C. 21:25 (al-Anbiya')
D. 42:11 (ash-Shura)

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Verse 21:25 shows that tawhid was the main message of all the messengers from Adam (AS) to Muhammad ﷺ.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q5. In which surah is the verse "The keys of the heavens and the earth belong to Him"?

A. Al-Ma'idah (5)
B. Al-Ikhlas (112)
C. Al-Anbiya' (21)
D. Az-Zumar (39:62–63)

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Verses 39:62–63 say that Allah is the Creator and Guardian of all things.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q6. Which surah is evidence for the meaning of tawhid "Allah is perfect and free from every fault"?

A. Surah al-Fatihah
B. Surah al-Ikhlas
C. Surah al-Anbiya'
D. Surah al-Mu'minun

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Surah al-Ikhlas (112:1–4): Allah is One, Self-Sufficient, does not give birth nor was He born, and has no equal. 42:11 is also evidence for this.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q7. "There is nothing like Him; He is the All-Hearing, the All-Seeing" – which verse is this?

A. 23:88
B. 40:62
C. 39:65
D. 42:11

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: 42:11 (ash-Shura) shows Allah's perfection – that nothing is equal to Him.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q8. According to verses 23:88–89, when the idolaters are asked "In whose hand is the dominion of all things?", what will they answer?

A. The idols'
B. Allah's
C. The kings'
D. We do not know

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: They will say "Allah's". Allah asks, "Then how are you deluded?" – the contradiction of accepting the Creator but associating partners in worship.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q9. Which of the following does the textbook NOT give as a reason why tawhid calls us to worship Allah alone?

A. Allah is the Creator of everything
B. He is the One who governs
C. He has helpers
D. He is free from all faults and perfect

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Allah is Self-Sufficient; He needs no helper. Creation, governance and perfection are the reasons.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q10. "If you associate partners, all your deeds will be wasted and you will be among the losers" (39:65) – which aspect of the importance of tawhid does this show?

A. Tawhid is an essential condition for good deeds to be accepted
B. Paradise being forbidden
C. Having a whole surah
D. The call of all the messengers

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Shirk destroys all good deeds; so tawhid is the basic condition for good deeds to be accepted.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q11. Which verse says that Allah has forbidden Paradise to whoever associates partners with Him?

A. 21:25 (al-Anbiya')
B. 39:65 (az-Zumar)
C. 5:72 (al-Ma'idah)
D. 112:4 (al-Ikhlas)

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: 5:72 – the refuge of one who associates partners is the Fire; the wrongdoers will have no helpers.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q12. Which of the following is NOT a proof of the importance of tawhid?

A. Tawhid is only a sunnah matter
B. The Quran has many verses on tawhid, including a whole surah
C. Paradise is forbidden to those who reject tawhid
D. All the messengers called to tawhid

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Tawhid is the foundation of Islam; it is not a sunnah but the foundation of the whole religion.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q13. Verse 40:62 asks, "So how are you turned away (from the truth)?" Which meaning of tawhid is this connected with?

A. Allah is the Creator of everything
B. Allah does not give birth, nor was He born
C. Paradise is forbidden to those who associate partners
D. A condition for good deeds

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: 40:62 – "He is the Creator of all things; there is no god but Him" – evidence for the meaning that He is the Creator.

Difficulty: Hard
