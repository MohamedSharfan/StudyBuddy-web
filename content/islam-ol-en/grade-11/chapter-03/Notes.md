# Grade 11 – Chapter 3: At-Tawhid (التَّوْحِيْدُ)

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_03/Notes.md` (Tamil). English rendering.

## 🎯 What you will learn
- Tawhid – the root and meaning of the word
- The three meanings of tawhid
- The importance of tawhid – four proofs

> 📖 **Opening verse of the chapter (21:25 – al-Anbiya'):**
> وَمَا أَرْسَلْنَا مِنْ قَبْلِكَ مِنْ رَسُوْلٍ إِلَّا نُوْحِيْ إِلَيْهِ أَنَّهُ لَا إِلٰهَ إِلَّا أَنَا فَاعْبُدُوْنِ
> **Meaning:** "(O Prophet!) We never sent any messenger before you without revealing to him: 'There is no god (worthy of worship) except Me, so worship Me.'"

---

## 1. Tawhid – introduction
- Tawhid is **the main message** that all the messengers brought to the world, **from Adam (AS) to the final messenger Muhammad ﷺ**.

### The root of the word ⭐
| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Wahada** (وحد) – root | — |
| **Wahid** (واحد) | **One** |
| **Wahhada** (وحّد) – verb | "Tawhid" is derived from this |
| **Tawhid** (توحيد) | **Making one, singling out, unifying** |

- In this way the word is used to mean **the belief in one God (monotheism)**.

> 📖 **In Islamic usage:** **accepting Allah alone as the one God and obeying Him** = tawhid.

---

## 2. The three meanings of tawhid ⭐

### (1) Allah is the Creator of everything
He is **the Owner** of everything; He **provides** all that is needed; He **governs** this universe.

| Verse | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **39:62–63 (az-Zumar)** | Allah is the Creator of all things and the Guardian over all things; **the keys of the heavens and the earth** belong to Him |
| **40:62 (al-Mu'min / Ghafir)** | That is Allah, your Lord, the Creator of all things; there is no god but Him; **so how are you turned away?** |
| **23:88–89 (al-Mu'minun)** | In whose hand is the dominion of all things – who protects and against whom there is no protection? They will say "**Allah's**." Say: "Then how are you deluded?" |

### (2) Allah alone is worthy of worship
| Verse | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **21:25 (al-Anbiya')** | We revealed to every messenger: there is no god except Me, **so worship Me** |

### (3) Allah is perfect and free from every fault
| Verse | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **112:1–4 (al-Ikhlas)** | Allah is **One**; He is **Self-Sufficient** (needs no one); He **does not give birth, nor was He born**; there is **none equal to Him** |
| **42:11 (ash-Shura)** | **There is nothing like Him**; He is **the All-Hearing, the All-Seeing** |

👉 **Summary:** tawhid calls people to worship and obey Allah alone. The reasons are:
- Allah is **the Creator of everything**
- He is **the One who governs**
- He is **free from all faults, perfect**

💡 **Memory tip:** **Creation – worship – perfection** (He is the Creator; worship is for Him alone; He is without fault).

---

## 3. The importance of tawhid – four proofs ⭐

| No. | Proof |
|---|---|
| 1 | The Quran states that **all the messengers** called people to accept and live by tawhid |
| 2 | The Quran contains **many verses** stressing tawhid, including **a whole surah** (Surah al-Ikhlas) |
| 3 | Tawhid has been made **an essential condition** for **good deeds to be accepted** |
| 4 | **Paradise has been made forbidden to those who reject tawhid** |

### Evidence
> 📖 **(39:65 – az-Zumar) – a condition for good deeds – meaning:**
> It has been revealed to you and to those before you: "If you **associate partners** (with Allah), **all your deeds will be wasted**, and you will surely be among the losers."

> 📖 **(5:72 – al-Ma'idah) – Paradise forbidden – meaning:**
> Whoever associates partners with Allah, Allah **has forbidden Paradise to him**, and his refuge is **the Fire**; the wrongdoers will have no helpers.

---

## ✅ Quick revision
- Tawhid ← wahada (root); wahid = one; wahhada (verb) → tawhid = making one, singling out, unifying.
- Tawhid = accepting Allah alone as the one God and obeying Him; the main message of all the prophets (21:25).
- 3 meanings: Creator (39:62–63, 40:62, 23:88–89); alone worthy of worship (21:25); perfect (112:1–4, 42:11).
- 4 proofs: the call of all the messengers; a whole surah; a condition for good deeds (39:65); Paradise forbidden to those who associate partners (5:72).
