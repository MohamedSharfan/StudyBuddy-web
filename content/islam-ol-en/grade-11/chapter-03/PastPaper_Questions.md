### A1. 2018 – Islam I, Q1

Question ID: P2018-I-Q01  
Year: 2018  
Paper and question: Islam I, Q1  
Marks: not stated on the paper (Islam I multiple-choice question)  
Source: OL_2018_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

Among the names of Allah, the meaning of the word 'ar-Rahim' is

- **A.** the Most Pure
- **B.** the Much-Forgiving
- **C.** the One of matchless love
- **D.** the All-Knowing

**Correct answer:** C – the One of matchless love *(option (3) on the original paper)*

**Explanation:** Ar-Rahim = the One of matchless love / the incomparably Merciful. The Most Pure – al-Quddus; the Much-Forgiving – al-Ghafur; the All-Knowing – al-'Alim.

The meaning of 'al-Mughni' in 'al-Asma' al-Husna'

- **A.** the One who withholds wealth
- **B.** the One who destroys wealth

### A6. 2025 (2026) – Islam I, Q34

Question ID: P2025_2026-I-Q34  
Year: 2025 (2026)  
Paper and question: Islam I, Q34  
Marks: not stated on the paper (Islam I multiple-choice question)  
Source: OL_2025_2026_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

Which of the following statements about tawhid is WRONG?

- **A.** Paradise is forbidden for whoever associates partners with Allah.
- **B.** Every Muslim must understand tawhid and act on it.
- **C.** The messengers called people to accept tawhid and live by it.
- **D.** Good deeds done without tawhid will be rewarded in the Hereafter.

**Correct answer:** D – Good deeds done without tawhid will be rewarded in the Hereafter. *(option (4) on the original paper)*

**Explanation:** Deeds without tawhid (mixed with shirk) become worthless (39:66); so (D) is wrong.
