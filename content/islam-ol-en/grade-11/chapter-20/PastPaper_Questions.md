### A1. 2019 – Islam I, Q40

Question ID: P2019-I-Q40  
Year: 2019  
Paper and question: Islam I, Q40  
Marks: not stated on the paper (Islam I multiple-choice question)  
Source: OL_2019_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

To which countries was the Islamic empire extended in the time of Caliph 'Uthman (RA)?

- **A.** Alexandria, Morocco, Tunisia
- **B.** Argentina, Spain, Britain
- **C.** Senegal, Madagascar, India
- **D.** India, Pakistan, Nepal

**Correct answer:** A – Alexandria, Morocco, Tunisia *(option (1) on the original paper)*

**Explanation:** In 'Uthman's (RA) time, rule extended to parts of North Africa such as Alexandria, southern Egypt, Tunisia and Morocco.

## Part B: Structured and Essay Questions (Islam II)

### B1. 2023 (2024) – Islam II, Q1 (vi)

Question ID: P2023_2024-II-Q1.6  
Year: 2023 (2024)  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q1 (vi)  
Marks: 20 marks for the whole of Q1  
Source: OL_2023_2024_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(vi) State two of the main tasks of Caliph 'Uthman (RA).

**Suggested answer:**

Any two of the following:
1. Preparing copies of the Quran so that it would be recited in one way, and sending them to the provinces ("Jami' al-Quran").
2. Creating the first Islamic navy under the leadership of Mu'awiyah (RA).
3. Expanding Masjid an-Nabawi and Masjid al-Haram.
4. Extending Islamic rule.

**Answer explanation:** The navy was also asked about in 2020 Q33 (MCQ).

**Marking guidance (suggested only – not an official marking scheme):** Two tasks.

**Answer status:** Suggested – not an official answer (no official marking scheme was supplied); based on the Grade 10/11 textbook content

**Verification note:** Check the mosque expansions and the extension of rule against the textbook.
