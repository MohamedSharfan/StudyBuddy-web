# Grade 11 – Chapter 20: Caliph 'Uthman (RA) – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_20/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q17 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. About whom did the Prophet ﷺ say, "Should I not feel shy before a man before whom even the angels feel shy?"

A. Abu Bakr (RA)
B. 'Umar (RA)
C. 'Ali (RA)
D. 'Uthman (RA)

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: This hadith (Muslim) is the best evidence of 'Uthman's (RA) deeply modest nature.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. To which clan did 'Uthman (RA) belong?

A. Hashim
B. 'Adi
C. Taym
D. Umayyah

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: He was born in 576 CE in the Umayyah clan of the Quraysh tribe.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q3. Through whose invitation did 'Uthman (RA) accept Islam?

A. 'Umar (RA)
B. 'Ali (RA)
C. Abu Bakr (RA)
D. Khadijah (RA)

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: One of the first ten to enter Islam, 'Uthman (RA) accepted Islam at the age of 34 through the invitation of Abu Bakr (RA).

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q4. How old was 'Uthman (RA) when he accepted Islam?

A. 24
B. 34
C. 40
D. 44

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: According to the textbook he was thirty-four at the time.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q5. Why is 'Uthman (RA) called "Dhun-Nurayn"?

A. Because he prayed a great deal at night
B. Because he made the hijrah twice
C. Because he married two of the Prophet's ﷺ daughters, one after the other, and lived with them in an excellent way
D. Because he compiled the Quran

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Dhun-Nurayn = the possessor of two lights. The title earned by compiling the Quran is "Jami' al-Quran".

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q6. What does "al-'Asharah al-Mubashsharah" refer to?

A. Ten caliphs
B. The first ten angels
C. Ten prophets
D. The ten given the good news of Paradise

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: 'Uthman (RA) is one of the ten who were given the good news of Paradise.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q7. What task did 'Uthman (RA) carry out during the 'umrah journey of 6 AH?

A. He was sent to the Quraysh as the Prophet's envoy for peace
B. He led the prayer as imam
C. He commanded the army
D. He rebuilt the Ka'bah

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: At the time of the Hudaybiyah event, 'Uthman (RA) was sent to the Quraysh as the envoy for peace.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q8. Who was NOT one of the six people appointed by 'Umar (RA) to choose the next caliph?

A. 'Ali (RA)
B. Abu 'Ubaydah (RA)
C. Talhah (RA)
D. Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas (RA)

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: The six: 'Uthman, 'Ali, Talhah, az-Zubayr, 'Abd ar-Rahman ibn 'Awf, Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas (RA).

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q9. After the decision of the committee of six, who gave bay'ah to 'Uthman (RA) first?

A. 'Ali (RA)
B. Talhah (RA)
C. 'Abd ar-Rahman ibn 'Awf (RA)
D. az-Zubayr (RA)

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: After 'Abd ar-Rahman (RA) gave bay'ah, the other Companions gave bay'ah too.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q10. On the basis of which copy were the eight copies of the Quran made in 'Uthman's (RA) time?

A. 'Ali's (RA) copy
B. The copy compiled in Abu Bakr's (RA) time and kept by Hafsah (RA)
C. Ibn Mas'ud's (RA) copy
D. A newly written copy

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Under the leadership of Zayd ibn Thabit (RA), eight copies were made from the copy kept by Hafsah (RA).

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q11. To whom does the honour of building the first navy in the Islamic world belong?

A. 'Uthman (RA)
B. 'Umar (RA)
C. 'Ali (RA)
D. Abu Bakr (RA)

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: With 'Uthman's (RA) approval, the navy was built in 27 AH under the leadership of Mu'awiyah (RA), governor of Sham.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q12. Under whose leadership, and in which year, was the navy of 'Uthman's (RA) time built?

A. Khalid ibn al-Walid – 13 AH
B. Sa'd – 15 AH
C. 'Amr ibn al-'As – 20 AH
D. Mu'awiyah (RA), governor of Sham – 27 AH

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Under Mu'awiyah (RA), in 27 AH.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q13. Which of the following is NOT mentioned in the textbook among the areas to which Islamic rule spread under 'Uthman (RA)?

A. Spain
B. Tunisia
C. Morocco
D. Alexandria

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: The textbook mentions Alexandria, the southern parts of Egypt, Tunisia, Morocco, the Mediterranean islands and Persian regions.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q14. In which years did 'Uthman (RA) expand Masjid al-Haram and Masjid an-Nabawi?

A. 26 AH – al-Haram; 29 AH – an-Nabawi
B. 29 AH – al-Haram; 26 AH – an-Nabawi
C. 13 AH – both
D. 35 AH – both

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Masjid al-Haram in 26 AH; Masjid an-Nabawi in 29 AH.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q15. Who played the full part in creating the unrest in 'Uthman's (RA) time?

A. 'Abdullah ibn Saba', a Jew
B. Abu Lu'lu'ah
C. Musaylimah
D. Abu Jahl

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: 'Abdullah ibn Saba' played the full part in causing unrest by wrongly criticising the administrative changes.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q16. When, and at what age, was 'Uthman (RA) martyred?

A. 35 AH, aged 82
B. 23 AH, aged 63
C. 40 AH, aged 63
D. 13 AH, aged 63

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Surrounded by the rebels, he was martyred in 35 AH at the age of 82.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q17. What role did 'Uthman (RA) hold in the administration of Abu Bakr (RA)?

A. Army commander
B. A leading member of the shura council, in the position of a minister
C. Qadi
D. Governor

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: He contributed as a shura member under Abu Bakr (RA) and as a close adviser in 'Umar's (RA) time.

Difficulty: Medium
