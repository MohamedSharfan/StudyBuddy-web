### A1. 2019 – Islam I, Q22

Question ID: P2019-I-Q22  
Year: 2019  
Paper and question: Islam I, Q22  
Marks: not stated on the paper (Islam I multiple-choice question)  
Source: OL_2019_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

Which of the following tasks was carried out by Caliph 'Umar (RA)?

- **A.** introducing the Hijri year.
- **B.** compiling the Quran into a book.
- **C.** founding the navy.
- **D.** conquering Constantinople.

**Correct answer:** A – introducing the Hijri year. *(option (1) on the original paper)*

**Explanation:** 'Umar (RA) introduced the Hijri calendar. Compiling the Quran into a book – Abu Bakr (RA); the navy – 'Uthman (RA); Constantinople – Muhammad al-Fatih.

In the table below, Columns I and II refer to the units set up to make 'Umar's (RA) administration easier.

| Column I | Column II |
|---|---|
| (1) wali | A - chief secretary |
| (2) katib | B - police officer |
| (3) qadi | C - governor |
| (4) sahib al-ahdath | D - chief judge |

Which option is correct when Column II is arranged, in order, to match Column I?

- **A.** A B D C
- **B.** B D A C
- **C.** C A D B
- **D.** D A C B

**Correct answer:** C – C A D B *(option (3) on the original paper)*

**Explanation:** Wali – governor (C); katib – chief secretary (A); qadi – judge (D); sahib al-ahdath – police officer (B). So C A D B.

## Part B: Structured and Essay Questions (Islam II)

### B1. 2020 – Islam II, Q7 (ii)

Question ID: P2020-II-Q7.2  
Year: 2020  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q7 (ii)  
Marks: 10 marks (short notes on two topics)  
Source: OL_2020_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(ii) The work of 'Umar (RA) as caliph

**Suggested answer:**

- He was appointed by Abu Bakr (RA), after consultation, in 13 AH, and ruled for about ten and a half years.
- He extended the caliphate to regions that had been under Roman and Persian rule (including Palestine).
- He laid down the rights and duties of the caliph, and rules for appointing governors.
- He made the judiciary independent; in the regional units he appointed officials such as the wali, katib, qadi, sahib al-kharaj and sahib al-ahdath.
- He established the Bayt al-Mal (zakat, jizyah, ghanimah, kharaj); he set up an organised army.
- He minted a distinct coinage for the first time; he introduced the Hijri calendar.
- Building cities, health services, irrigation.

**Answer explanation:** The administrative reforms are the main point.
