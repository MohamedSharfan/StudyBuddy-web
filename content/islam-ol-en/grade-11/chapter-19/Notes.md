# Grade 11 – Chapter 19: Caliph 'Umar (RA)

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_19/Notes.md` (Tamil). English rendering.

## 🎯 What you will learn
- 'Umar (RA) – a short life history, and how he accepted Islam
- His personal qualities
- How he was appointed caliph
- Administrative reforms and new offices
- His death

> 📖 **Opening hadith of the chapter (Hakim):** إِنَّ اللهَ جَعَلَ الْحَقَّ عَلَى لِسَانِ عُمَرَ وَقَلْبِهِ
> **Meaning:** "Indeed, Allah has placed **the truth on 'Umar's tongue and in his heart**."

---

## 1. A short life history ⭐

| Point | Fact |
|---|---|
| Born | In the **580s CE**, in the **'Adi** clan |
| Social standing | **A man of honour** among the Quraysh |
| Skills | **Could read and write**; **a wrestler** |
| At first | One of the **leading enemies** of the Prophet ﷺ and of Islam |
| Accepting Islam | When the Quraysh decided that killing the Prophet was the way to bring peace to Makkah, he accepted Islam **while on his way to carry this out** – in **year 6 of prophethood** |
| Caliphate | **22 Jumada al-Akhirah, 13 AH** |
| Length of rule | About **ten and a half years** |
| Death | **26 Dhul-Hijjah, 23 AH** – after being badly wounded in an attack by **Abu Lu'lu'ah**, **a Persian Magian (Zoroastrian)** |

---

## 2. His personal qualities ⭐

### (1) Courage and bravery
- His acceptance of Islam gave the Muslims **new life and energy**; it was **a great blow** to the Qurayshi disbelievers.
- Right after accepting Islam he **knocked on the door of every Qurayshi leader** to tell them he had become a Muslim.
- He **announced it at the Ka'bah too**, and made it possible for the Muslims to **pray there openly**.
- The idolaters were furious but, before his bravery and courage, **did not dare to do anything.**

### (2) Wide knowledge and wisdom
- On many occasions **revelation came confirming his advice**.

### (3) Foresight
- In the time of Caliph Abu Bakr (RA) he **advised that the Quran be compiled into a book and preserved**.

---

## 3. His appointment as caliph ⭐
- **Before his own death**, Abu Bakr (RA) wished to appoint 'Umar (RA) as caliph.
- He called many important Companions **individually and in groups** and **consulted** them.
- All of them expressed **complete satisfaction**.
- Abu Bakr (RA) **declared** 'Umar (RA) the next caliph, and the people accepted him **unanimously**.
- He took up the caliphate on **22 Jumada al-Akhirah, 13 AH**.

---

## 4. His services and contributions ⭐

### Expanding the caliphate
- To free people oppressed by the **Roman and Persian** superpowers and give them a prosperous life, he **continued** the expansion that Abu Bakr (RA) had begun.
- He appointed **excellent administrators as governors**.
- He used **various methods** to learn of people's grievances and **settled them immediately**.
- Because of his **honest and strict** administration, the Islamic state became prosperous and strong, and **people of neighbouring lands** too were keen to live under it.

### New arrangements (as the Islamic state grew many times larger) ⭐
1. Rules and limits for **the caliph's rights, duties and relations with the people**.
2. Rules and regulations for **appointing governors**, and defining their powers.
3. Making **the judiciary independent**.
4. Appointing officials **to collect state taxes**.
5. Creating **regional units** to make administration easier, and appointing officials (table below).
6. Establishing **the Bayt al-Mal (state treasury)**, with sources of income: **zakat, jizyah, ghanimah, kharaj, and the property of those who died without heirs.**
7. **An organised army.**
8. **A distinct coinage minted for the first time** in Islamic history.
9. **Building cities, health services and irrigation**.
10. Introducing **the Hijri calendar**.

### Officials of the regional units ⭐
| Arabic title | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| **Wali (الوالي)** | **Governor** |
| **Katib (الكاتب)** | **Chief secretary** |
| **Katib ad-diwan (كاتب الديوان)** | **Military secretary** |
| **Sahib al-kharaj (صاحب الخراج)** | **Official for collecting revenue (land tax)** |
| **Sahib al-ahdath (صاحب الأحداث)** | **Police officer** |
| **Sahib bayt al-mal (صاحب بيت المال)** | **Officer in charge of the treasury** |
| **Qadi (القاضي)** | **Chief judge** |

### Sources of income of the Bayt al-Mal
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Zakat** | The obligatory charity of Muslims |
| **Jizyah (الجزية)** | The protection tax of non-Muslim citizens |
| **Ghanimah (الغنيمة)** | Spoils of war |
| **Kharaj (الخراج)** | Land tax |
| Heirless property | Property left by those who had no heirs |

👉 By providing many systems and services, 'Umar (RA) carried out a government that is **a model for the whole of mankind**.

## ✅ Quick revision
- "The truth on 'Umar's tongue and in his heart" (Hakim).
- Born in the 580s CE, 'Adi clan; a wrestler; accepted Islam in year 6 of prophethood.
- Courage: knocked on the doors of the Qurayshi leaders; open prayer at the Ka'bah.
- Knowledge: revelation confirmed his advice; foresight: advice to compile the Quran.
- Appointed by Abu Bakr (RA) after consultation; 22 Jumada al-Akhirah, 13 AH.
- Reforms: independent judiciary, Bayt al-Mal, army, first coinage, Hijri calendar, building cities.
- Officials: wali, katib, katib ad-diwan, sahib al-kharaj, sahib al-ahdath, sahib bayt al-mal, qadi.
- About 10½ years; 26 Dhul-Hijjah, 23 AH – Abu Lu'lu'ah.
