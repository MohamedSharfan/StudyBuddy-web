# Grade 11 – Chapter 19: Caliph 'Umar (RA) – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_19/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q15 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. In which collection is the hadith "Indeed, Allah has placed the truth in 'Umar's heart and on his tongue"?

A. Bukhari
B. Hakim
C. Muslim
D. Tirmidhi

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: The textbook quotes this hadith from Hakim.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q2. In which clan was 'Umar (RA) born?

A. Hashim
B. Umayyah
C. 'Adi
D. Taym

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: 'Umar (RA), a man of honour among the Quraysh, was born in the 580s CE in the clan of 'Adi.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q3. When did 'Umar (RA) accept Islam?

A. Year 6 of prophethood
B. Year 1 of prophethood
C. 1 AH
D. 8 AH

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: He accepted Islam in year 6 of prophethood, while on his way to carry out the decision to kill the Prophet.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q4. Which of the following did 'Umar (RA) NOT do as soon as he accepted Islam?

A. Knock on the door of every Qurayshi leader to tell them he had become a Muslim
B. Go secretly to Abyssinia
C. Make it possible to pray openly at the Ka'bah
D. Announce it at the Ka'bah

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: 'Umar (RA) acted openly and bravely; the idolaters did not dare to do anything before him.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q5. What shows 'Umar's (RA) wide knowledge and wisdom?

A. He was a wrestler
B. He wrote poetry
C. He traded
D. On many occasions revelation came confirming his advice

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Revelation came confirming many of his views.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q6. What best example of 'Umar's (RA) foresight does the textbook give?

A. Advising, in Abu Bakr's (RA) time, that the Quran be compiled into a book and preserved
B. Introducing the Hijri calendar
C. Minting coins
D. Organising the army

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: After the Battle of Yamamah, fearing the Quran might be lost, 'Umar (RA) gave this advice – evidence of his foresight.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q7. How was 'Umar (RA) appointed caliph?

A. An election at Saqifat Bani Sa'idah
B. Abu Bakr (RA) consulted the important Companions and declared him the next caliph; the people accepted him unanimously
C. Chosen by a committee of six
D. Inheritance

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: After everyone expressed complete satisfaction, Abu Bakr (RA) declared him caliph.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q8. On what date did 'Umar (RA) take up the caliphate?

A. 22 Jumada al-Akhirah, 13 AH
B. 12 Rabi' al-Awwal, 11 AH
C. 26 Dhul-Hijjah, 23 AH
D. 18 Dhul-Hijjah, 35 AH

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: 22 Jumada al-Akhirah, 13 AH – took office; 26 Dhul-Hijjah, 23 AH – death.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q9. Which of the following is NOT one of the reforms introduced by 'Umar (RA)?

A. Making the judiciary independent
B. Sending eight copies of the Quran to the provinces
C. A distinct coinage for the first time in Islamic history
D. Introducing the Hijri calendar

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Sending eight copies was done by 'Uthman (RA).

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q10. In the regional units, who was the "sahib al-ahdath"?

A. The governor
B. The chief judge
C. The officer in charge of the treasury
D. The police officer

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Wali – governor; sahib bayt al-mal – treasury; qadi – chief judge; sahib al-ahdath – police.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q11. What was the responsibility of the "sahib al-kharaj"?

A. Military secretary
B. Chief secretary
C. Official for collecting revenue (land tax)
D. Police officer

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Kharaj = land tax; the one who collected it was the sahib al-kharaj.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q12. What office was "katib ad-diwan"?

A. Military secretary
B. Governor
C. Officer in charge of the treasury
D. Judge

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Katib – chief secretary; katib ad-diwan – military secretary.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q13. Which of the following was NOT a source of income of the Bayt al-Mal?

A. Zakat
B. Jizyah
C. Ghanimah
D. Interest

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Zakat, jizyah, ghanimah, kharaj and heirless property – these were the sources of income. Interest is haram in Islam.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q14. Who attacked and badly wounded 'Umar (RA)?

A. Abu Jahl
B. Musaylimah
C. Abu Lu'lu'ah, a Persian Magian
D. Ibn Muljam

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: After about ten and a half years of rule he died on 26 Dhul-Hijjah, 23 AH, as a result of Abu Lu'lu'ah's attack.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q15. About how long did 'Umar (RA) rule?

A. Two and a half years
B. Twelve years
C. Ten and a half years
D. Four and a half years

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Abu Bakr – about 2¼ years; 'Umar – about 10½ years.

Difficulty: Easy
