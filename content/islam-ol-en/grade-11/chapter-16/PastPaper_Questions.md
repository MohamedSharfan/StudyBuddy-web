### A1. 2017 – Islam I, Q27

Question ID: P2017-I-Q27  
Year: 2017  
Paper and question: Islam I, Q27  
Marks: not stated on the paper (Islam I multiple-choice question)  
Source: OL_2017_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

What is 'ijtihad'?

- **A.** a gathering of Islamic legal scholars
- **B.** an advisory council
- **C.** the effort made to find the solution to a problem
- **D.** Islamic legal institutions

**Correct answer:** C – the effort made to find the solution to a problem *(option (3) on the original paper)*

**Explanation:** Ijtihad (from the root "jahada" – to strive) = the research effort made by a qualified scholar on a problem for which the Quran and Sunnah give no direct solution.

The most correct Islamic way of finding solutions to problems for which the Quran and Sunnah give no definite solution

- **A.** fiqh
- **B.** tafsir
- **C.** qiyas
- **D.** ijtihad

**Correct answer:** D – ijtihad *(option (4) on the original paper)*

**Explanation:** When the Quran and Sunnah give no direct solution, the research effort made by qualified scholars based on them is ijtihad. Qiyas is only one method of ijtihad.
