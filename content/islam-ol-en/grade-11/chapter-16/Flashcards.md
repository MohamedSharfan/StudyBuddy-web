# Grade 11 – Chapter 16: Al-Ijtihad – Flashcards

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_16/Flashcards.md` (Tamil), cards 1–10 in the same order.

---

Flashcard 1

Front: الاجتهاد (Al-ijtihad)

Back: The research effort made by those with deep knowledge, based on the Quran and Sunnah, for a problem with no direct solution in them.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 16 | Topic: Definition

---

Flashcard 2

Front: جهد (Jahada)

Back: The root word of ijtihad – to strive.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 16 | Topic: Definition

---

Flashcard 3

Front: المجتهد (Al-mujtahid)

Back: A legal scholar qualified to make ijtihad.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 16 | Topic: Key terms

---

Flashcard 4

Front: أولي الأمر (Ulul-amr)

Back: Those in authority – in 4:59; according to the mufassirun's explanation (in the textbook), the mujtahids.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 16 | Topic: Ulul-amr

---

Flashcard 5

Front: Imam ash-Shawkani's definition of ijtihad?

Back: A legal scholar using his abilities to derive, from the deep meanings of the primary sources, solutions to practical problems.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 16 | Topic: Definition

---

Flashcard 6

Front: The order of judging given by Mu'adh (RA)?

Back: The Quran → the Prophet's Sunnah → ijtihad based on both (Tirmidhi).

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 16 | Topic: Mu'adh's incident

---

Flashcard 7

Front: The seven qualities of a mujtahid?

Back: Muslim; deep knowledge of the Quran and Sunnah; expertise in the sciences; mastery of Arabic; understanding earlier ijtihad; clarity about the problem; taqwa and good conduct.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 16 | Topic: Qualities of a mujtahid

---

Flashcard 8

Front: The reward for ijtihad (Bukhari)?

Back: Right decision – two rewards; wrong decision – one reward.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 16 | Topic: Reward

---

Flashcard 9

Front: المفسرين (Al-mufassirun)

Back: Commentators on the Quran.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 16 | Topic: Key terms

---

Flashcard 10

Front: What does the last part of verse 4:59 say?

Back: If you disagree about anything, refer it to Allah and the Messenger.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 16 | Topic: Opening verse
