> 📖 **Opening verse of the chapter (4:59 – an-Nisa'):** يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِيْنَ آمَنُوْا أَطِيْعُوا اللهَ وَأَطِيْعُوا الرَّسُوْلَ وَأُولِي الْأَمْرِ مِنْكُمْ فَإِنْ تَنَازَعْتُمْ فِيْ شَيْءٍ فَرُدُّوْهُ إِلَى اللهِ وَالرَّسُوْلِ
> **Meaning:** "O believers! Obey Allah, obey the Messenger and **those in authority (ulul-amr)** among you. If you **disagree** about anything, **refer it** to Allah and the Messenger."

---

## 1. The sources of Islam – two kinds

| Kind | What they include |
|---|---|
| **Primary sources** | **The Quran, the Sunnah** |
| **Secondary sources** | Those derived from the two **through ijtihad** |

- The secondary sources act as **support to the primary sources** during the ijtihad carried out to solve the legal problems that arise from time to time.

---

## 2. What is ijtihad? ⭐

| | Explanation |
|---|---|
| **Root word** | **Jahada (جهد)** |
| **Meaning** | **To strive (make an effort)** |
| **Technical meaning** | When the Quran or the Sunnah **has not directly given** the solution to a new problem, **those with deep knowledge** of both make **a research effort** to reach a solution based on them |
| Those who do this work | **Mujtahids (المجتهد)** |

> 📖 **Imam ash-Shawkani's definition** (meaning): ijtihad is **a legal scholar using all his abilities** to derive, from the deep meanings of the primary sources of Islam, solutions to the practical problems the Shari'ah faces.

---

## 3. Ijtihad in the Prophet's ﷺ time
- New problems arose even while the Prophet ﷺ was alive; Allah solved some through **revelation**; sometimes **the Prophet ﷺ** put forward solutions.

### The incident of Mu'adh ibn Jabal (RA) (Tirmidhi*) ⭐
When **Mu'adh (RA)** was sent to **Yemen as governor**:

| The Prophet's ﷺ question | Mu'adh's (RA) answer |
|---|---|
| How will you judge when new problems are brought to you? | By what is said in **the Quran, the word of Allah** |
| And if you do not find it there? | I will take it from **the example of the Messenger of Allah** |
| And if you do not find it there either? | I will **make ijtihad based on both** and judge |

- The Prophet ﷺ praised him (meaning): "**All praise is for Allah, who has inspired** the messenger of the Messenger of Allah to such a solution."

> ⚠️ *Some hadith scholars have criticised the chain of this narration – confirm with the teacher before quoting its grading.

💡 **The order of seeking a ruling:** **Quran → Sunnah → ijtihad.**

- The Prophet ﷺ also **trained** the Companions by creating **different situations** in which they had to make ijtihad.

---

## 4. Who are the ulul-amr (أولي الأمر)? ⭐
- The **mufassirun (commentators on the Quran)** have explained that the **"ulul-amr"** in verse **4:59** are **the mujtahids** (as given in the textbook; another well-known explanation is "those in authority / rulers").

---

## 5. Seven qualities a mujtahid must have ⭐
1. Being **a Muslim**
2. Having **deep knowledge of the Quran and the Sunnah**
3. Having **expertise in the (Islamic) sciences**
4. Having **mastery of Arabic**
5. **Understanding the ijtihad of earlier mujtahids**
6. Being **clear about the problems** taken up for research
7. Having **taqwa and good conduct**

💡 **Memory tip:** **Muslim – Quran/Sunnah – Sciences – Arabic – Earlier ijtihad – Problem clarity – Taqwa.**

---

## 6. The reward for ijtihad ⭐
> 📖 **Hadith (Bukhari) – meaning:** "If a mujtahid makes ijtihad and his decision is **right**, he has **two rewards**. If he makes ijtihad and his decision is **wrong**, he has **one reward**."

| Decision | Reward |
|---|---|
| **Right** | **2** |
| **Wrong** | **1** |

👉 This **encourages** those **who are qualified** to take part in ijtihad.

---

## 7. Key Arabic terms
| Arabic | Meaning |
|---|---|
| الاجتهاد | Ijtihad – the effort to derive rulings through research |
| جهد | Jahada – to strive (root word) |
| المجتهد | Mujtahid – a scholar qualified to make ijtihad |
| أولي الأمر | Ulul-amr – those in authority (explained as the mujtahids) |

- Primary sources – Quran, Sunnah; secondary sources – those derived through ijtihad.

- Mu'adh (RA) – governor of Yemen: Quran → Sunnah → ijtihad (Tirmidhi).
- 4:59 – ulul-amr = the mujtahids (the mufassirun).
- 7 qualities of a mujtahid.
- Right – 2 rewards; wrong – 1 reward (Bukhari).
