# Grade 11 – Chapter 16: Al-Ijtihad – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_16/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q13 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. From which root word does "ijtihad" come?

A. Qadara
B. Wahada
C. 'Abada
D. Jahada

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Ijtihad comes from the root "jahada"; its meaning is to strive.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. What are the primary sources of Islam?

A. Ijma', qiyas
B. Al-Muwatta', al-Musnad
C. Ijtihad, 'urf
D. The Quran, the Sunnah

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: The Quran and the Sunnah are the primary sources; those derived from them through ijtihad are the secondary sources.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q3. When is ijtihad carried out?

A. First, for every problem
B. When the Quran or the Sunnah has not directly given the solution to a new problem
C. As a replacement for the Quran
D. When rejecting the Sunnah

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: When there is no direct solution, those with deep knowledge of the Quran and the Sunnah research a solution based on them – that is ijtihad.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q4. Who defined ijtihad as "a legal scholar using his abilities to derive, from the deep meanings of the primary sources, solutions to practical problems"?

A. Imam ash-Shawkani
B. Imam ash-Shafi'i
C. Imam an-Nawawi
D. Imam al-Ghazali

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: This definition is Imam ash-Shawkani's.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q5. To which region was Mu'adh ibn Jabal (RA) sent as governor?

A. Yemen
B. Sham
C. Egypt
D. Iraq

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: When he was sent to Yemen, the Prophet ﷺ asked him how he would judge new problems (Tirmidhi).

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q6. According to Mu'adh's (RA) answer, what is the correct order for seeking a ruling?

A. Quran → Sunnah → ijtihad
B. Ijtihad → Sunnah → Quran
C. Sunnah → Quran → ijtihad
D. Quran → ijtihad → Sunnah

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: First the word of Allah, then the Prophet's example, and if it is in neither, ijtihad based on them.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q7. What did the Prophet ﷺ do on hearing Mu'adh's (RA) answer?

A. He scolded him
B. He did not send him to Yemen
C. He praised Allah, who had inspired such a solution, and commended him
D. He told him to follow only the Quran

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: This approval shows that ijtihad is a permitted and praised method.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q8. Whom have the mufassirun explained the "ulul-amr" in verse 4:59 to be?

A. The mujtahids
B. Traders
C. Poets
D. Soldiers

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: The mufassirun have explained that the ulul-amr, to be obeyed after Allah and the Messenger, are the mujtahids (as given in the textbook).

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q9. Which of the following is NOT one of the qualities a mujtahid must have?

A. Mastery of Arabic
B. A lot of wealth
C. Taqwa and good conduct
D. Understanding the ijtihad of earlier mujtahids

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: The seven qualities: Muslim, deep knowledge of the Quran and Sunnah, expertise in the sciences, mastery of Arabic, understanding earlier ijtihad, clarity about the problem, taqwa and good conduct. Wealth is not one of them.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q10. According to the hadith in Bukhari, how many rewards does a mujtahid get whose decision is right?

A. One
B. Ten
C. Three
D. Two

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Two rewards for a right decision; one reward for a wrong decision.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q11. What is the position of a qualified mujtahid whose ijtihad leads to a wrong decision?

A. He becomes a sinner
B. He gets one reward
C. He is punished
D. He gets nothing

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: He gets one reward for the effort of seeking the truth – this encourages qualified people to take part in ijtihad.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q12. What did the Prophet ﷺ do for the Companions with regard to ijtihad?

A. He forbade ijtihad
B. He told them to wait for revelation for everything
C. He trained them by creating different situations in which they had to make ijtihad
D. He told them to ask the Jews

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: There is much evidence that the Prophet ﷺ trained the Companions in ijtihad.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q13. What is the role of the secondary sources?

A. To change the primary sources
B. To create a new religion
C. To support the primary sources during ijtihad, to solve legal problems that arise from time to time
D. To reject hadiths

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: The secondary sources work only as support to the Quran and the Sunnah.

Difficulty: Medium
