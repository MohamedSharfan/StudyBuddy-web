# Grade 11 – Chapter 2: Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah – Flashcards

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_02/Flashcards.md` (Tamil), cards 1–16 in the same order.

---

Flashcard 1

Front: أهل السنة والجماعة (Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah)

Back: Those who follow the way of life of the Prophet ﷺ and his Companions, based on the beliefs they held.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 2 | Topic: Definition

---

Flashcard 2

Front: السنة (Sunnah) / الجماعة (Jama'ah) – literal meaning?

Back: Sunnah = the way; Jama'ah = the main body (great majority).

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 2 | Topic: Definition

---

Flashcard 3

Front: The Children of Israel / this ummah – how many sects?

Back: Children of Israel – 71; this ummah – 73; only one will be saved from the Fire (Ibn Majah).

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 2 | Topic: Opening hadith

---

Flashcard 4

Front: What are the eight features of the aqeedah of Ahl as-Sunnah?

Back: Allah, the angels, the Quran, risalah, the Hereafter, the Companions, sin, the awliya'.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 2 | Topic: Eight features

---

Flashcard 5

Front: كُن (Kun)

Back: "Be" – when Allah wills something, this command is enough and it is.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 2 | Topic: Allah

---

Flashcard 6

Front: كلام الله (Kalam Allah)

Back: The speech of Allah – the Quran.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 2 | Topic: The Quran

---

Flashcard 7

Front: معجزات (Mu'jizat)

Back: Miracles given to the prophets.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 2 | Topic: Risalah

---

Flashcard 8

Front: عصمة ('Ismah)

Back: The protection the prophets have from sins and errors.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 2 | Topic: Risalah

---

Flashcard 9

Front: الشفاعة (Ash-shafa'ah)

Back: Intercession in the Hereafter – it takes place only with Allah's permission.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 2 | Topic: The Hereafter

---

Flashcard 10

Front: أهل البيت (Ahl al-Bayt)

Back: The family of the Prophet ﷺ – who must be honoured.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 2 | Topic: The Companions

---

Flashcard 11

Front: الكفر (Al-kufr) / النفاق (An-nifaq)

Back: Kufr = disbelief; nifaq = hypocrisy.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 2 | Topic: Key terms

---

Flashcard 12

Front: What three acts cause kufr?

Back: Regarding a sin as not a sin, regarding the haram as not haram, mocking the Shari'ah.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 2 | Topic: Sin

---

Flashcard 13

Front: ولي (Wali) / وليّ الله (Wali of Allah)

Back: A friend of Allah – one given a special rank because of closeness to Allah.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 2 | Topic: The awliya'

---

Flashcard 14

Front: الكرامات (Al-karamat)

Back: Marvels that Allah gives to the awliya'.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 2 | Topic: The awliya'

---

Flashcard 15

Front: السلف الصالح (As-salaf as-salih)

Back: The righteous predecessors (the early generations such as the Companions and the Tabi'un).

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 2 | Topic: Key terms

---

Flashcard 16

Front: الأشعرية (Al-Ash'ariyyah) / الماتريدية (Al-Maturidiyyah)

Back: Ash'ariyyah – the aqeedah tradition following Imam Abu al-Hasan al-Ash'ari; Maturidiyyah – the tradition following Imam Abu Mansur al-Maturidi.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 2 | Topic: Key terms
