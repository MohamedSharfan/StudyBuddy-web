# Grade 11 – Chapter 2: Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_02/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q15 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. According to the hadith in Ibn Majah, into how many sects will the Prophet's ﷺ ummah split?

A. Seventy-one
B. Seventy-two
C. One hundred
D. Seventy-three

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: The Children of Israel split into 71 sects; this ummah will split into 73; all except one will be in the Fire.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. What did the Prophet ﷺ say about "that one sect" that will be saved from the Fire?

A. Those who are greatest in number
B. Those who worship the most
C. Only the Arabs
D. Those who are on what I and my Companions are on today

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Those who follow the way of the Prophet ﷺ and the Companions are that one group – this is Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q3. What do the words "Sunnah" and "Jama'ah" mean?

A. Sunnah – duty; Jama'ah – prayer
B. Sunnah – belief; Jama'ah – family
C. Sunnah – the way; Jama'ah – the main body (great majority)
D. Sunnah – scripture; Jama'ah – the prophets

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Sunnah = the way; Jama'ah = the main body. This expression has been in use since the time of the Companions.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q4. According to the aqeedah of Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah, which statement about Allah is WRONG?

A. He has no beginning and no end
B. He does not dwell in anything and does not merge with anything
C. He unites with His creation and becomes one with it
D. The believers will be blessed with seeing Him in the Hereafter

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Allah does not dwell in anything; He does not unite or merge with anything. He is unique and without equal.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q5. When Allah wills something, which command is enough for it to be?

A. "Kun" (Be)
B. "Iqra'"
C. "Bismillah"
D. "Allahu akbar"

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: When Allah wills something, the command "Kun" (Be) is enough, and it is.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q6. Which statement about the angels is correct?

A. They were created from fire
B. Human beings know their number
C. They were created from light; only Allah knows their number
D. Satan is the foremost of them

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: The angels were created from light; Jibril (AS) is the foremost of them. Devils and jinn are other creations of Allah.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q7. What is the status of the earlier scriptures after the revelation of the Quran?

A. They also remain valid
B. They are no longer valid
C. They are higher than the Quran
D. They are still being revealed

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: After the Quran was revealed as the final scripture, the earlier scriptures are no longer valid. Allah Himself has guaranteed the protection of the Quran.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q8. What is the quality by which the prophets are protected from sins and errors called?

A. Mu'jizat
B. Karamat
C. 'Ismah
D. Shafa'ah

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: 'Ismah = protection from sins. Mu'jizat = the prophets' miracles; karamat = marvels given to the awliya'; shafa'ah = intercession.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q9. What is the position of Ahl as-Sunnah on shafa'ah (intercession) in the Hereafter?

A. There is no such thing as shafa'ah
B. The prophets, angels and the righteous can intercede with Allah's permission
C. Anyone can intercede without permission
D. Only the angels will intercede

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: The Prophet ﷺ, the other prophets, the angels and the righteous can intercede – but only with Allah's permission.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q10. What is the position of Ahl as-Sunnah on a Muslim who commits a major sin?

A. He is a sinner but not a kafir; even if he enters Hell, he will not stay there forever
B. He has become a kafir
C. He has no punishment at all
D. He will be in Hell forever

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: If Allah wills, He will forgive him and admit him to Paradise; if He wills, He will punish him and then admit him to Paradise.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q11. Which of the following does the textbook mention as something that causes kufr?

A. Committing a sin and then regretting it
B. Forgetting and eating while fasting
C. Making a mistake in prayer
D. Regarding the haram as not haram

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Regarding a sin as not a sin, regarding the haram as not haram, and mocking the Shari'ah cause kufr.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q12. What is the position on calling a Muslim "kafir" when he has not done anything that causes kufr or shirk?

A. It is a grave wrong
B. It is permitted
C. It is sunnah
D. It is only makruh

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Declaring Muslims kafir without reason is a grave wrong – this distinguishes Ahl as-Sunnah from extreme groups such as the Khawarij.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q13. What is the belief of Ahl as-Sunnah about the Companions?

A. They are higher than the prophets
B. They rank next to the prophets; they are trustworthy and honest; finding fault with them is a sin
C. They may be criticised
D. Only some of them are trustworthy

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: The Companions are the best of the believers; it is necessary to honour and love the Ahl al-Bayt and the Ummahat al-Mu'minin.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q14. Which statement about the awliya' is correct?

A. They are exempt from the basic duties
B. They are given karamat; but no one is ever exempt from the basic religious duties
C. They are higher than the prophets
D. They receive revelation

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: However high a spiritual level is reached, basic duties such as prayer and fasting are never removed.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q15. According to Ahl as-Sunnah, where does the life after death begin?

A. In the grave (qabr)
B. On the plain of gathering
C. In Paradise
D. On the Sirat bridge

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: The life after death begins in the grave; the questioning in the grave and its comfort and torment are true.

Difficulty: Easy
