### A2. 2021 (2022) – Islam I, Q36

Question ID: P2021_2022-I-Q36  
Year: 2021 (2022)  
Paper and question: Islam I, Q36  
Marks: not stated on the paper (Islam I multiple-choice question)  
Source: OL_2021_2022_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

Which of the following statements about Islamic 'aqidah is WRONG?

- **A.** The Quran and the hadith are the sources of Islamic 'aqidah.
- **B.** Many groups arose among Muslims when they began to think about 'aqidah rationally.
- **C.** Those who follow the way of the righteous predecessors (as-salaf as-salihun) and the Companions are called "Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah".

**Correct answer:** D – In the periods of the Prophet and the caliphs there were difficulties in understanding 'aqidah. *(option (4) on the original paper)*

**Explanation:** In the time of the Prophet ﷺ and the caliphs, 'aqidah was clearly understood on the basis of the Quran and Sunnah; the groups arose only later through rationalist arguments.

### A6. 2024 (2025) – Islam I, Q38

Question ID: P2024_2025-I-Q38  
Year: 2024 (2025)  
Paper and question: Islam I, Q38  
Marks: not stated on the paper (Islam I multiple-choice question)  
Source: OL_2024_2025_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

In the table below, column "I" gives the titles of those connected with the Prophet ﷺ and column "II" their meanings.

| Column I | Column II |
|---|---|
| (1) Ahl al-Bayt | A - those who write the revelation |
| (2) Ummahat al-Mu'minin | B - the Prophet's family |
| (3) Ashab as-Suffah | C - the Prophet's wives |
| (4) Kuttab al-Wahy | D - the companions of the platform |

Which option is correct when column "II" is written to match column "I"?

- **A.** A, B, C, D
- **B.** B, C, D, A
- **C.** D, C, A, B
- **D.** D, C, B, A

**Correct answer:** B – B, C, D, A *(option (2) on the original paper)*

**Explanation:** Ahl al-Bayt – the family (B); Ummahat al-Mu'minin – the wives (C); Ashab as-Suffah – the companions of the platform (D); Kuttab al-Wahy – the scribes of revelation (A). So B, C, D, A.

**Question:**

(i) What is explained by the phrase Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah?

**Suggested answer:**

"Sunnah" = way; "jama'ah" = the majority (the main body). Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah are those who follow the way of life of the Prophet ﷺ and his Companions, based on the belief they held. The hadith "those who are on the way I and my Companions are on" (Ibn Majah) refers to them.

**Question:**

(iii) Briefly explain the 'aqidah of Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah concerning the Companions.

**Suggested answer:**

- The Companions are the highest of the believers; they hold a high rank next after the prophets.
- Among them are many who were given the good news of Paradise.
- It is necessary to honour and love the Ahl al-Bayt (the Prophet's family) and the Ummahat al-Mu'minin (the Prophet's wives).
- All the Companions are trustworthy and honest; finding fault with them and criticising them is a sin.

**Answer explanation:** One of the eight features of the 'aqidah of Ahl as-Sunnah.
