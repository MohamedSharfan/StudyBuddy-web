# Grade 11 – Chapter 2: Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_02/Notes.md` (Tamil). English rendering.

## 🎯 What you will learn
- The meaning of the expression "Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah"
- Who they are – evidence from the hadith
- Eight features of the aqeedah of Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah

> 📖 **Opening hadith of the chapter (Ibn Majah):**
> إِنَّ بَنِيْ إِسْرَائِيلَ قَدِ افْتَرَقُوْا عَلَى إِحْدَى وَسَبْعِيْنَ فِرْقَةً وَتَفْتَرِقُ أُمَّتِيْ عَلَى ثَلَاثٍ وَسَبْعِيْنَ فِرْقَةً كُلُّهَا فِي النَّارِ إِلَّا وَاحِدَةً...
> **Meaning:** The Children of Israel split into **seventy-one (71)** sects. My ummah will split into **seventy-three (73)** sects. All of them will be in the Fire **except one**. When asked, "Which one is that?", he said: "**Those who are on what I and my Companions are on today.**"

---

## 1. Meaning of the words ⭐

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Sunnah** (السنة) | **The way (method)** |
| **Jama'ah** (الجماعة) | **The main body (the great majority)** |

- This expression has been used in the Muslim community **from the time of the Companions until today**.

> 📖 **In Islamic usage:** Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah = **those who follow the way of life of the Prophet ﷺ and his Companions, based on the beliefs they held.**

- The Prophet ﷺ said those who will enter Paradise are **"those who are on the way I and my Companions were on."** → this points to Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah.

---

## 2. The essence of the aqeedah of Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah – eight features ⭐

### (1) Allah
- **The Creator** of everything; He has **no beginning and no end.**
- He has **power** over all things; He is **perfect**; He **knows** everything.
- **The Ever-Living**; **the All-Hearing, the All-Seeing**; **the One worthy of worship.**
- **Free of all needs, unique, without equal.**
- He **does not dwell in anything**; He **does not unite or merge** with anything.
- What He wills happens; what He does not will does not happen.
- He alone gives **rizq (provision)**, **cures illness and removes hardship.**
- **The believers will be blessed with seeing Him in the Hereafter.**
- When He wills something, the command **"Kun" (Be)** is enough, and it is.

### (2) The angels
- Allah created the angels **from light**.
- **Only Allah** knows **their number**.
- They are engaged in various tasks; **Jibril (AS) is the foremost** of them.
- By Allah's command they look after **the welfare of His servants**.
- **Devils (shayatin) and jinn** are also among Allah's creations.

### (3) The Quran
- The Quran is **the kalam (speech) of Allah.**
- Before it He revealed many scriptures; after the Quran was revealed as **the final scripture**, they **are no longer valid.**
- It has been **preserved** from the time it was revealed until today; **Allah Himself has guaranteed** its protection.

### (4) Risalah (messengership)
- It is true that prophets and messengers were sent from time to time.
- Through them Allah gave **commands and prohibitions**.
- They were given **mu'jizat** (miracles).
- They have the quality of **'ismah** and are **protected** from sins and errors.
- We must believe in all of them **without any distinction or discrimination**.
- **Muhammad ﷺ is the final prophet**; messengership **ended** with him; no prophet or messenger will come after him.

### (5) The Hereafter
- There is a life after death; it begins **in the grave**.
- **The questioning in the grave, and its comfort and torment,** are true.
- There will also be a reckoning in the Hereafter. **The Sirat, the Mizan, Paradise and Hell** are true and lasting, and **have already been created.**
- In the Hereafter, **the Prophet ﷺ, the other prophets, the angels and the righteous** can make shafa'ah (intercession) – **only with Allah's permission.**

### (6) The Companions
- The Companions are **the best of the believers**; they hold a high rank **next to the prophets**.
- Among them are many who were **given the good news of Paradise**.
- It is necessary to **honour and love** the **Ahl al-Bayt** (the Prophet's ﷺ family) and the **Ummahat al-Mu'minin** (the Prophet's wives).
- All the Companions are **trustworthy and honest**; **finding fault with them and criticising them is a sin.**

### (7) Sin ⭐
- **A Muslim who commits a major sin is a sinner, but not a kafir.**
- Even if he enters Hell, he **will not stay there forever**.
- If Allah wills, He will **forgive** a sinning believer **and admit him to Paradise**; if He wills, He will **punish him and then** admit him to Paradise.
- Calling a Muslim **a kafir** when he has not done anything that causes **kufr or shirk** is **a grave wrong.**
- **What causes kufr:**
  - regarding a sin as **not a sin**
  - regarding **the haram as not haram**
  - **mocking the Shari'ah**
- **Shafa'ah is real**; those qualified for it can intercede according to their rank.

### (8) The awliya'
- **Friends of Allah** = the awliya'.
- According to their closeness to Allah, He gives **a special rank** to whom He wills.
- They are given **karamat** (marvels).
- ⚠️ However high a spiritual level a servant reaches, he is **never exempt from the basic religious duties.**

## 3. Key Arabic terms (the textbook list)
