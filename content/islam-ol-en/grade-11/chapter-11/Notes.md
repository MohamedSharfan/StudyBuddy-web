# Grade 11 – Chapter 11: The Islamic View of Good Character

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_11/Notes.md` (Tamil). English rendering.

## 🎯 What you will learn
- Good character – meaning and Arabic terms
- The importance of good character (Quran, hadith)
- The need for good character – who should decide?
- Four good qualities taught by Islam: **patience, courage, humility, forgiveness**

> 📖 **Opening verse of the chapter (68:4 – al-Qalam):** وَإِنَّكَ لَعَلَى خُلُقٍ عَظِيْمٍ – **Meaning:** "(O Prophet) indeed, you are of **a great (noble) character**."

---

## 1. What is good character?
- Good character = **morality** = **what one should practise and keep to.**
- An **essential** part of society.

| Arabic term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Adab (الآداب)** | Manners |
| **Akhlaq (الأخلاق)** | Good character traits |
| **Suluk (السلوك)** | Conduct (behaviour) |
| **Ahsan al-khuluq (أحسن الخلق)** | The finest character – the way Islam describes good character |

### The parts of Islam
Islam has many parts – **'aqa'id (beliefs), 'ibadat (worship), mu'amalat (dealings), munakahat (marriage law), jinayat (criminal law), akhlaq (character)** – but **akhlaq is linked with all the other parts and adorns them all.**

---

## 2. The importance of good character ⭐

### The Prophet ﷺ – the example
- **(68:4)** – you are of great character.
- **(33:21)** – in the Messenger of Allah there is an excellent example.
- **(Muwatta') – meaning:** "I was sent **to perfect good character**."
- **'A'ishah (RA) (Muslim) – meaning:** "His character **was the Quran**."

### Good character is a high form of worship
| Hadith (meaning) | Source |
|---|---|
| On the Day of Judgement **nothing will be heavier in the believer's scale than good character**; Allah hates the one who is obscene and harsh in speech; through good character one reaches **the rank** of one who **prays and fasts** | Ahmad |
| A servant reaches **the lowest** level of Hell **because of bad conduct** | Tabarani |

### Good character is part of iman
| Hadith (meaning) | Source |
|---|---|
| Who is **most complete in iman**? – **The one with the best character** | Tabarani |
| Who is **closest to Allah**? – **Those with the best character** | Ibn Hibban |
| What is **the best thing** given to people? – **Good character** | Tirmidhi |

---

## 3. The need for good character ⭐
- Morality, a moral code, principles and education are needed **only by human society**.
- A human being has **freedom of thought, reason and conscience**; so that he does not misuse these, Allah has prescribed **the good path of morality**.
- Since a human being was created as Allah's **khalifah (representative)**, morality is essential for carrying out his duties and running the world properly.

### Conscience
- Allah has placed a moral sense **in human nature**; it keeps **stirring** deep in the heart.
- Even when morals decline through bad surroundings or company, the conscience **keeps warning**.
- **If ignored** → a person without morals; **if taken as a lesson** → an excellent person of good character.

### Who should decide? ⭐
- Only **Allah and His Messenger** can decide what is good conduct and what is bad conduct.
- A human being cannot: he is **weak**; his knowledge is **limited**; his likes and dislikes **differ**; his reason and conscience are **weak.**

## 4. Four good qualities taught by Islam ⭐

### (1) Patience (الصبر – sabr)
- **The basis** of success in both worlds.
- The word "sabr" appears **in more than 70** places in the Quran.

| Evidence | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **2:45** | Seek help **through patience and prayer** |
| **2:155** | We will test you with **fear, hunger, and loss of wealth, lives and crops**; give good news to the patient |
| Bukhari | Allah gives patience to whoever strives to be patient; no one is given **a gift better than patience** |
| Bukhari | When Allah wants good for someone, He sends him **trials** (to test his patience) |
| Tirmidhi | On the Day of Judgement, those who lived in comfort in this world, seeing the reward of those who passed their trials, will **wish** "**if only our skins had been cut with scissors**" in trials |

👉 The truly patient person stays patient **without wavering** in success and in failure.

### (2) Courage (الشجاعة – shaja'ah)
- Islam does not want to produce **cowards**; it wants people with **physical strength and strength of mind**.
- **Supplication (Bukhari) – meaning:** "O Allah! I seek Your protection from **worry and grief**; **helplessness, laziness, cowardice and miserliness**; **the burden of debt and being overpowered by people**."
- **(Muslim):** the strong believer is better than the weak believer.
- **The example of the Prophet ﷺ:**
  - He wrestled several times with **Rukanah**, the wrestler, and **won*.**
  - He ran **races** with his wife **'A'ishah (RA)**.
- **The source of courage = iman.**
> 📖 **(Tirmidhi) – meaning:** Even if the whole world gathered to benefit you, they could not benefit you beyond what Allah has written for you; and if the whole world gathered to harm you, they could not harm you except with what Allah has written.
- Courage drives one to reach one's goal and trains one to face challenges **boldly**.

> ⚠️ *The textbook calls Rukanah "the wrestler of Madinah". Biographical sources usually describe Rukanah ibn 'Abd Yazid as a Qurayshi wrestler of Makkah – confirm with the teacher.

### (3) Humility (التواضع – tawadu')
- Keeping away from **pride and arrogance** and behaving humbly with others; it brings **high rank and honour** with Allah.

| Evidence | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **25:63** | The servants of the Most Merciful walk on the earth **humbly**; when the foolish address them they say "**Salam (peace)**" and withdraw |
| Muslim | Whoever is humble for Allah's sake, Allah **will surely raise his rank** |
| Muslim | Allah has revealed to me: be humble; let no one boast over another, and let no one **wrong** another |

**The humility of the Prophet ﷺ:**
- He greeted **children** playing by the roadside **with salam** and made them happy.
- When **Anas (RA)** passed children he greeted them with salam: "The Prophet ﷺ used to do this" **(Bukhari, Muslim).**
- When **Tamim ibn Usayd (RA)** came to Madinah, the Prophet ﷺ, who was preaching from the minbar, **came down from the minbar** and answered his questions with humility and love **(Muslim).**

### (4) Forgiveness (العفو – 'afw)
- **One of Allah's attributes**; several of His beautiful names show it: **al-Ghafur (الغفور), al-Ghaffar (الغفار), at-Tawwab (التواب), al-'Afuww (العفو).**
- Islam praises those who have this quality as "**excellent, God-fearing, model Muslims**" and "**those who have won Allah's love and pleasure**".

| Evidence | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **3:134** | Those who give in prosperity and in hardship, **who swallow their anger and forgive people**; Allah loves those who do good |
| **42:43** | Whoever is patient and forgives – **that is truly a matter of great resolve (courage)** |
| **24:22** | (Below – the incident of Abu Bakr) |

#### The incident of Abu Bakr (RA) ⭐
- Some people spread **slander** about **'A'ishah (RA)**.
- Abu Bakr (RA) **swore** to **stop the help** he used to give to one who had been involved in it.
- Allah revealed **(24:22) – meaning:** Let not those of wealth and means among you **swear not to give** to relatives, the poor and those who emigrated; **let them pardon and overlook.** **Do you not love that Allah should forgive you?** Allah is Forgiving, Merciful.
- 👉 **Lesson:** when a servant forgives the faults of others, **Allah forgives the servant's faults.**
