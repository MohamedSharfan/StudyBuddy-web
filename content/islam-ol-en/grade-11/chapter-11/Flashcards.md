Flashcard 1

Front: الآداب (Adab) / الأخلاق (Akhlaq) / السلوك (Suluk)

Back: Adab – manners; akhlaq – good character traits; suluk – conduct.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 11 | Topic: Key terms

---

Flashcard 2

Front: أحسن الخلق (Ahsan al-khuluq)

Back: The finest character – the name Islam uses for good character.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 11 | Topic: Key terms

Flashcard 3

Front: العقائد / العبادات / المعاملات / المناكحات / الجنايات

Back: 'Aqa'id – beliefs; 'ibadat – worship; mu'amalat – dealings; munakahat – marriage law; jinayat – criminal law.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 11 | Topic: The parts of Islam

Flashcard 4

Front: Complete: "I was sent to perfect…"

Back: …good character (Muwatta').

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 11 | Topic: Importance

---

Flashcard 5

Front: What did 'A'ishah (RA) say about the Prophet's character?

Back: His character was the Quran (Muslim).

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 11 | Topic: Importance

---

Flashcard 6

Front: Good character is part of iman – three hadiths?

Back: The most complete in iman has the best character (Tabarani); those closest to Allah have the best character (Ibn Hibban); the best thing given to people is good character (Tirmidhi).

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 11 | Topic: Importance

---

Flashcard 7

Front: Why can a human being not decide morality by himself?

Back: He is weak; his knowledge is limited; likes and dislikes differ; reason and conscience are weak.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 11 | Topic: Who decides

---

Flashcard 8

Front: What are the four good qualities in this chapter?

Back: Patience (sabr), courage (shaja'ah), humility (tawadu'), forgiveness ('afw).

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 11 | Topic: Four qualities

---

Flashcard 9

Front: الصبر (As-sabr)

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 11 | Topic: Patience

Flashcard 10

Front: الشجاعة (Ash-shaja'ah)

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 11 | Topic: Courage

Flashcard 11

Front: The eight things the Prophet ﷺ sought protection from (Bukhari)?

Back: Worry, grief, helplessness, laziness, cowardice, miserliness, the burden of debt, being overpowered by people.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 11 | Topic: Courage

---

Flashcard 12

Front: التواضع (At-tawadu')

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 11 | Topic: Humility

Flashcard 13

Front: العفو (Al-'afw)

Back: Forgiveness – one of Allah's attributes.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 11 | Topic: Forgiveness

Flashcard 14

Front: الغفور (al-Ghafur) / الغفار (al-Ghaffar) / التواب (at-Tawwab)

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 11 | Topic: Forgiveness

Flashcard 15

Front: Three qualities of the good-doers in verse 3:134?

Back: Giving in prosperity and hardship, swallowing anger, forgiving people.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 11 | Topic: Forgiveness

Flashcard 16
