# Grade 11 – Chapter 11: The Islamic View of Good Character – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_11/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q20 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. "Indeed, you are of a great character" – which verse is this?

A. 33:21
B. 68:4
C. 3:134
D. 25:63

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: 68:4 (al-Qalam) – "Wa innaka la'ala khuluqin 'azim." 33:21 is about the excellent example.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. How does Islam describe good character?

A. Al-jinayat
B. Al-'ibadat
C. Al-munakahat
D. Ahsan al-khuluq

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Ahsan al-khuluq = the finest character. 'Ibadat – worship; munakahat – marriage law; jinayat – criminal law.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q3. What special place does akhlaq (good character) have among the parts of Islam?

A. It is separate from the other parts
B. It is linked with all the other parts and adorns them all
C. It has very little importance
D. It belongs only to worship

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Good character adorns everything – 'aqa'id, 'ibadat, mu'amalat, munakahat and jinayat.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q4. In which collection is the saying "I was sent to perfect good character"?

A. Muwatta'
B. Bukhari
C. Tirmidhi
D. Tabarani

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: The textbook quotes it from the Muwatta'.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q5. Who said, when asked about the Prophet's ﷺ life, "His character was the Quran"?

A. Abu Bakr (RA)
B. 'Ali (RA)
C. Anas (RA)
D. 'A'ishah (RA)

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: This statement of 'A'ishah (RA) is in Muslim.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q6. According to the hadith in Ahmad, what will be heaviest in the believer's scale on the Day of Judgement?

A. Wealth
B. Long prayers only
C. Good character
D. Family pride

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Through good character a person reaches even the rank of one who prays and fasts.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q7. According to the hadith in Ibn Hibban, who is closest to Allah?

A. The one with the most wealth
B. Those with the best character
C. The one who travels most
D. The one who talks most

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Good character is part of iman; those with the best character are closest to Allah.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q8. According to the textbook, who can decide what is good conduct and what is bad conduct?

A. Each person by his own reason
B. Allah and His Messenger
C. The majority of society
D. The government

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: A human being is weak; his knowledge is limited; likes and dislikes differ. So only Allah and His Messenger can decide.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q9. What happens if a person ignores the warning of his conscience?

A. He rises to become an excellent person of good character
B. He becomes a person without morals
C. Nothing changes
D. He becomes very intelligent

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: If he takes the conscience's warning as a lesson, he rises to good character; if he ignores it, he loses his morals.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q10. In more than how many places does the word "sabr" (patience) appear in the Quran?

A. 10
B. 30
C. 70
D. 100

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Its mention in more than 70 places shows the importance of patience.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q11. "Seek help through patience and prayer" – which verse is this?

A. 42:43
B. 2:155
C. 3:134
D. 2:45

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: 2:45. 2:155 – We will test you with fear, hunger and losses; give good news to the patient.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q12. According to the hadith in Tirmidhi, what will those who lived in comfort in this world wish for in the Hereafter?

A. That they had been given even more wealth
B. To return to the world and trade
C. That their skins had been cut with scissors in trials – when they see the reward for patience
D. That they had had more friends

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Seeing the great rank of those who passed their trials with patience, they will wish for the same.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q13. What example does the textbook give of the Prophet's ﷺ courage and physical strength?

A. Winning a horse race
B. Only wielding a sword at Badr
C. Beating the wrestler Rukanah in wrestling; racing with 'A'ishah (RA)
D. A swimming contest

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: The Prophet ﷺ was an example by taking part in bold activities that strengthen the body.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q14. According to the textbook, what is the source of courage?

A. Wealth
B. Fame
C. Body size
D. Iman

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: The belief that even if the whole world gathered, no one could benefit or harm except as Allah has written (Tirmidhi) gives courage.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q15. What do the servants of the Most Merciful do when the foolish try to argue with them (25:63)?

A. Answer harshly
B. Fight
C. Say "Salam (peace)" and withdraw
D. Backbite

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: This is an expression of humility – walking modestly on the earth and withdrawing without arguing with the foolish.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q16. In the incident where the Prophet ﷺ came down from the minbar while preaching and humbly answered someone's questions, who was that person?

A. Anas (RA)
B. Tamim ibn Usayd (RA)
C. Abu Dharr (RA)
D. Mu'adh (RA)

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: The Prophet ﷺ came down from the minbar to answer Tamim ibn Usayd (RA), who had come to Madinah to learn the laws of Islam (Muslim).

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q17. Which of the following is NOT one of Allah's names connected with forgiveness?

A. Al-Jabbar
B. Al-Ghaffar
C. At-Tawwab
D. Al-Ghafur

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Al-Ghafur, al-Ghaffar, at-Tawwab and al-'Afuww are the names connected with forgiveness given in the textbook.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q18. "Whoever is patient and forgives – that is truly a matter of great resolve" – which verse is this?

A. 42:43
B. 3:134
C. 24:22
D. 2:155

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: 42:43 (ash-Shura) describes forgiveness as an act of great resolve (courage).

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q19. Which verse was revealed when Abu Bakr (RA) swore to stop the help he gave to one involved in the slander against 'A'ishah (RA)?

A. 3:134
B. 25:63
C. 42:43
D. 24:22

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: 24:22 – do not swear not to give; pardon and overlook; do you not love that Allah should forgive you?

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q20. What main truth does verse 24:22 teach?

A. When a servant forgives others' faults, Allah forgives the servant's faults
B. Forgiving others is weakness
C. An oath must never be broken
D. One must not help relatives

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: "Do you not love that Allah should forgive you?" – forgiving others is a way of receiving Allah's forgiveness.

Difficulty: Easy
