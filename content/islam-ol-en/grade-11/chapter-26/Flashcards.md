# Grade 11 – Chapter 26: Islamic Finance – Flashcards

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_26/Flashcards.md` (Tamil), cards 1–12 in the same order.

---

Flashcard 1

Front: What is a high form of worship relating to wealth in Islam?

Back: Earning wealth in the right way and spending it in good ways.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 26 | Topic: Introduction

---

Flashcard 2

Front: Three good intentions for earning halal (at-Tabarani)?

Back: Not having to beg from others; providing for one's family; caring for one's neighbours.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 26 | Topic: Introduction

---

Flashcard 3

Front: المرابحة (Al-Murabahah)

Back: Selling at cost price + profit; payment all at once or in instalments.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 26 | Topic: Permitted methods

---

Flashcard 4

Front: المضاربة (Al-Mudarabah) – the two parties?

Back: The investor who puts in the capital; the entrepreneur who works with knowledge and experience.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 26 | Topic: Permitted methods

---

Flashcard 5

Front: Profit and loss in mudarabah?

Back: Profit – shared by both; loss – borne by the investor alone (if there was no negligence).

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 26 | Topic: Permitted methods

---

Flashcard 6

Front: المشاركة (Al-Musharakah)

Back: Partnership trade run by several partners who put in capital and manpower; loss by share proportion.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 26 | Topic: Permitted methods

---

Flashcard 7

Front: الإجارة (Al-Ijarah)

Back: Leasing / hire purchase.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 26 | Topic: Permitted methods

---

Flashcard 8

Front: السلم (As-Salam)

Back: Advance-payment sale.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 26 | Topic: Permitted methods

---

Flashcard 9

Front: القرض الحسن (Al-Qard al-hasan)

Back: A benevolent loan – a loan given to someone in need, with nothing extra charged.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 26 | Topic: Permitted methods

---

Flashcard 10

Front: Six activities forbidden in Islamic finance?

Back: Interest, gambling, cheating, adulteration, fraud in measure and weight, supporting harmful things.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 26 | Topic: Forbidden activities

---

Flashcard 11

Front: What does Islam regard as an honour and a duty for a human being?

Back: Living by halal work.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 26 | Topic: Foundations

---

Flashcard 12

Front: What should Muslims do about modern financial practices?

Back: Know which agree with Islam and which conflict with it, and act accordingly.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 26 | Topic: Introduction
