# Grade 11 – Chapter 26: Islamic Finance – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_26/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q16 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. Which verse says, "O you who believe! Do not consume one another's wealth unjustly"?

A. 2 Al-Baqarah: 275
B. 4 An-Nisa': 29
C. 62 Al-Jumu'ah: 10
D. 83 Al-Mutaffifin: 1

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: 4:29 – the foundation verse of Islamic finance.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. How will the one who earns in a halal way meet Allah on the Day of Judgement (at-Tabarani)?

A. With a face like the full moon
B. With light like the sun
C. With a record in his hand
D. In white clothes

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Whoever earns halal so as not to beg, and for his family and neighbours, will meet Allah with a face like the full moon.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q3. Who said, "Only those who know the rules of the religion should buy and sell in our markets"?

A. Abu Bakr (RA)
B. 'Ali (RA)
C. 'Umar (RA)
D. 'Uthman (RA)

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Caliph 'Umar (RA) – this confirms the need for knowledge of halal and haram in earning.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q4. Which of the following is NOT one of the transgressions in which others' property is enjoyed wrongfully?

A. Interest
B. Adulteration
C. Gambling
D. Qard hasan

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Qard hasan – a benevolent loan; permitted by Islam. Interest, adulteration, gambling and cheating – forbidden.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q5. In which trading method are goods sold at cost price plus profit?

A. Al-Mudarabah
B. Al-Musharakah
C. Al-Murabahah
D. Al-Ijarah

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: In murabahah the price may be bargained or fixed; payment may be all at once or in instalments.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q6. In murabahah, what right does the trader have when payment is in instalments?

A. The right to increase the profit
B. Only the right to take back the goods
C. The right to charge interest
D. No right at all

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: The textbook says the trader has the right to increase the profit when payment is in instalments.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q7. In which trading method does the owner of capital invest while an experienced entrepreneur works using his knowledge?

A. Al-Murabahah
B. Al-Mudarabah
C. Ar-Rahn
D. As-Salam

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Mudarabah – a method that benefits both the experienced person without capital and the person with capital but no experience.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q8. In mudarabah, if there is a loss (not caused by the entrepreneur's negligence), who bears it?

A. The entrepreneur alone
B. Both equally
C. The government
D. The investor alone

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Profit is shared between both parties; loss is borne by the investor alone.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q9. Which statement about musharakah (partnership) is correct?

A. Only one person puts in capital
B. The whole loss falls on one person
C. Partners take part in capital and manpower; loss is borne in proportion to each one's share
D. Profit is not shared

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Profit is shared by mutual agreement; loss is borne in proportion to shares.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q10. "I am the third of two partners as long as neither betrays the other" – what kind of hadith is this?

A. Hadith Qudsi (Abu Dawud)
B. A mawquf hadith
C. A verse of the Quran
D. A saying of 'Umar (RA)

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: A Hadith Qudsi, in which the Prophet ﷺ reports what Allah says; it encourages honest partnership.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q11. What does "ijarah" mean?

A. Pledge
B. Leasing / hire purchase
C. Advance-payment sale
D. Benevolent loan

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Ijarah – leasing; salam – advance-payment sale; rahn – pledge; qard hasan – benevolent loan.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q12. Which Arabic term is used for an advance-payment sale?

A. Rahn
B. Ijarah
C. Musharakah
D. Salam

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: As-salam – a sale in which the price is paid in advance and the goods are received later.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q13. What does "rahn" mean?

A. Pledge (pawning)
B. Loan
C. Rent
D. Partnership

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Ar-rahn – pledge; pledging and leasing are practices used in financial dealings.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q14. What is Islam's position on lending to someone in need?

A. It is forbidden
B. Lending, and helping a debtor repay, are qualities of a believer
C. One may lend only with interest
D. One may lend only to relatives

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: This is one of the foundations of Islam's teaching on earning; qard hasan is its form.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q15. Which of the following is completely forbidden by the principles of Islamic finance?

A. Murabahah
B. Ijarah
C. Producing, selling or supporting things that can cause harm
D. Musharakah

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Interest, gambling, cheating, adulteration, fraud in measure and supporting harmful things – forbidden.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q16. What is the benefit of earning through joint enterprise?

A. Only one person's wealth grows
B. Competition increases
C. Loss is unavoidable
D. Resources and abilities spread among many people come together, and many needs of society are met

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: The textbook says partnership trade is full of development.

Difficulty: Medium
