# Grade 11 – Chapter 7: Zakat – Flashcards

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_07/Flashcards.md` (Tamil), cards 1–18 in the same order.

---

Flashcard 1

Front: الزكاة (Az-zakat)

Back: Linguistically – purity, progress, growth; in usage – the specific share Allah has fixed in wealth for those entitled.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 7 | Topic: Definition

---

Flashcard 2

Front: النصاب (An-nisab)

Back: The minimum amount at which zakat becomes due.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 7 | Topic: Key terms

---

Flashcard 3

Front: الحول (Al-hawl)

Back: The completion of one (lunar) year for zakat.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 7 | Topic: Key terms

---

Flashcard 4

Front: What are the seven conditions for zakat to become due?

Back: Muslim, free, ownership, nisab, a full year, wealth that can grow, in excess of basic needs.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 7 | Topic: Conditions

---

Flashcard 5

Front: What is the starting nisab for sheep/goats, cattle and camels?

Back: Sheep/goats – 40; cattle – 30; camels – 5.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 7 | Topic: Nisab

---

Flashcard 6

Front: What is the nisab for grain?

Back: 5 wasqs ≈ 675 kg; paddy with its husk – 1350 kg.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 7 | Topic: Nisab

---

Flashcard 7

Front: What is the nisab for gold and silver?

Back: Gold – 20 mithqal (85 g / 10 sovereigns); silver – 200 dirhams (595 g); zakat is 1/40.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 7 | Topic: Nisab

---

Flashcard 8

Front: What is the starting nisab for money?

Back: The value of 85 grams of gold (there is also the view of using the silver nisab).

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 7 | Topic: Nisab

---

Flashcard 9

Front: Which fruits are liable to zakat?

Back: Dates and grapes – the rules of grain apply.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 7 | Topic: Nisab

---

Flashcard 10

Front: الفقير (Al-faqir)

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 7 | Topic: Recipients

---

Flashcard 11

Front: المسكين (Al-miskin)

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 7 | Topic: Recipients

Flashcard 13

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 7 | Topic: Recipients

Flashcard 14

Front: الرقاب (Ar-riqab)

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 7 | Topic: Recipients

Flashcard 15

Front: الغارمون (Al-gharimun)

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 7 | Topic: Recipients

Flashcard 16

Front: في سبيل الله (Fi sabil Allah) / ابن السبيل (Ibn as-sabil)

Back: Fi sabil Allah – those in the path of Allah; ibn as-sabil – a traveller with no means to complete the journey.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 7 | Topic: Recipients

---

Flashcard 17

Front: Who are the five not entitled to zakat?

Back: One with sufficient income, slaves, Banu Hashim / Banu al-Muttalib, those the giver must support, non-Muslims.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 7 | Topic: Not entitled

---

Flashcard 18

Front: Zakat as a collective duty – the Prophet's words (Bukhari)?

Back: "It is to be taken from their rich and given back to their poor" – when Mu'adh (RA) was sent to Yemen.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 7 | Topic: Collective duty
