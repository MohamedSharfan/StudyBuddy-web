# Grade 11 – Chapter 7: Zakat – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_07/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q22 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. What is the literal meaning of the word "zakat"?

A. Sacrifice, patience
B. Purity, progress, growth
C. Loan, interest
D. Worship, prayer

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Linguistically, zakat means purity, progress and growth. In Islamic usage it is the specific share Allah has fixed in wealth.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. Which verse says Allah's mercy will be decreed for those who give zakat?

A. 2:43
B. 9:34
C. 23:4
D. 7:156

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: 7:156 – "My mercy encompasses everything; I will decree it for those who fear Me and give zakat."

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q3. According to the report in Hakim, what will happen if a people withhold zakat?

A. Their wealth will double
B. Allah will withhold the rain from them
C. They will win in war
D. Nothing will happen

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Hakim – the rain will be withheld; Tabarani – they will be tested with famine.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q4. What change does the textbook say zakat brings about in the heart of the poor person?

A. He becomes lazy
B. He is freed from diseases of the heart such as envy and hatred, and prays for the wealthy
C. He hates the wealthy
D. He stops working

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Zakat purifies not only the giver's heart but also the receiver's.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q5. Which of the following is NOT evidence that zakat is a collective duty?

A. A command that everyone must give it alone and in secret
B. 9:103 commanding that it be collected from the wealthy
C. When Mu'adh (RA) was sent to Yemen, the words "taken from their rich and given to their poor"
D. 9:60 naming zakat collectors as one of the groups entitled to it

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: The Prophet ﷺ and the caliphs sent people to collect zakat – it is a duty to be carried out collectively.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q6. Which of the following is NOT a benefit of carrying out zakat collectively?

A. The wealthy do not need to give zakat
B. The self-respect of the poor is protected
C. Large programmes to end poverty can be planned
D. The wealthy cannot escape the duty

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: The collective system ensures that all the wealthy fulfil zakat.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q7. What is the correct ruling for someone who looks after the property of a child who has not reached puberty?

A. There is no zakat on that property
B. Only after the child reaches puberty
C. Although it is not his own, he is obliged to pay zakat on it
D. The government must pay it

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: This is an exception to the condition "owning the wealth"; zakat is not wajib only on public property.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q8. What does "nisab" refer to?

A. The day on which zakat must be paid
B. Interest on zakat
C. The receiver of zakat
D. The minimum amount at which zakat becomes due

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: The nisab differs according to the type of wealth; zakat becomes due only when that amount is reached.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q9. For which kinds of wealth is there no condition that a year must pass?

A. Grain, fruit, buried treasure
B. Gold, silver
C. Money, trade goods
D. Sheep, cattle

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Zakat on grain, fruit and buried treasure is due as soon as they are received; for the others a lunar year must pass.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q10. What are the two fards of zakat?

A. Money, gold
B. Making the intention, giving it to those entitled
C. Prayer, fasting
D. Writing, witnesses

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: The intention for zakat and giving it to those entitled are its fards.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q11. What is the starting nisab for sheep/goats?

A. 40
B. 30
C. 5
D. 100

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Sheep/goats – 40; cattle – 30; camels – 5. For 40 to 120 sheep, one female.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q12. A person has 150 sheep. How many female sheep must he give as zakat?

A. 1
B. 3
C. 2
D. 4

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: From 121 to 200 sheep, 2 females. 150 falls within that range.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q13. What is the zakat due on 40 cattle?

A. One two-year-old cow
B. One one-year-old bull calf
C. Two bulls
D. One sheep

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: For 30 cattle, a one-year-old male; for 40 cattle, a two-year-old female.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q14. Is there zakat on livestock used for work such as ploughing or pulling carts?

A. No
B. Yes, double
C. Only on cattle
D. Only on camels

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Zakat is not due on livestock used for work.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q15. What is the starting nisab for grain?

A. 85 grams
B. 20 mithqal
C. 200 dirhams
D. 5 wasqs – about 675 kg

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Grain – 5 wasqs ≈ 675 kg; for paddy stored with its husk, double (1350 kg).

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q16. When working out the nisab, which must NOT be added together?

A. Local rice and samba rice
B. Several gold items of the same kind
C. Two harvests in the same year
D. Wheat and rice

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Two different grains (wheat and rice) are not added together. Varieties of the same grain (local rice, samba) are counted together.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q17. What is the starting nisab for gold?

A. 200 dirhams – 595 grams
B. 5 wasqs
C. 20 mithqal – 85 grams (10 sovereigns)
D. 40 grams

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Gold – 20 mithqal = 10 sovereigns = 85 grams; silver – 200 dirhams = 595 grams; zakat is 1/40 on both.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q18. What is the textbook's ruling on jewellery in use?

A. All jewellery is liable to zakat
B. It is not liable to zakat; ornaments kept as extra savings are liable
C. Gold vessels are not liable to zakat
D. The mixed metal must also be counted

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Jewellery in use – none; ornaments kept as savings and gold and silver vessels – yes; any mixed metal is deducted.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q19. Among those entitled to zakat, who is the "miskin"?

A. An extremely poor person with no income at all
B. A person in debt
C. A zakat collector
D. One who does not have enough for himself and those he supports

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Faqir = an extremely poor person with no income; miskin = one who has some income but not enough.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q20. Who is "ibn as-sabil"?

A. One who fights in Allah's path
B. One drawn to Islam
C. One on a permitted journey who has no means to complete it
D. A slave

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Ibn as-sabil = a traveller; one who has fallen into need on a journey.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q21. Which of the following is NOT entitled to receive zakat?

A. A poor person who fell into debt for a lawful reason
B. A zakat collector
C. The zakat-payer's own mother
D. A traveller in need on the way

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Those whom the zakat-payer is obliged to support (wife, children, mother and father) are not entitled to zakat from him; supporting them is already his duty.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q22. Members of the Prophet's ﷺ family line are not entitled to zakat. Which clans are these?

A. Banu Umayyah, Banu Makhzum
B. Banu Hashim, Banu al-Muttalib
C. Aws, Khazraj
D. Banu an-Nadir, Banu Qaynuqa'

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: According to the textbook, Banu Hashim and Banu al-Muttalib are not entitled to zakat.

Difficulty: Medium
