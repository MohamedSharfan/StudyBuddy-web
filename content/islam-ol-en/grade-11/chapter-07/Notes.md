# Grade 11 – Chapter 7: Zakat

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_07/Notes.md` (Tamil). English rendering.

## 🎯 What you will learn
- Zakat – meaning and importance
- The individual and social benefits of zakat
- Zakat as a collective duty
- The conditions for zakat to become due, and its fards
- The kinds of wealth on which zakat is due, and their nisab
- Those entitled to zakat (8) and those not entitled (5)

> 📖 **Opening verse of the chapter (9:103 – at-Tawbah):** خُذْ مِنْ أَمْوَالِهِمْ صَدَقَةً تُطَهِّرُهُمْ وَتُزَكِّيْهِمْ بِهَا وَصَلِّ عَلَيْهِمْ
> **Meaning:** "(O Prophet!) Take from their wealth a charity by which you **purify them inwardly and outwardly**, and pray for them."

---

## 1. What is zakat? ⭐
- It has a most important place among **the five great duties** of Islam; it is **the unique method** Islam has shown to remove the suffering of the poor.

| | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Linguistically** | **Purity, progress, growth** |
| **In Islamic usage** | **A specific share that Allah has fixed in wealth** to belong **to those entitled to it**; and **giving** that share |

---

## 2. The importance of zakat – the Quran and hadith ⭐

| Point | Evidence (meaning) |
|---|---|
| It is **mentioned together with prayer** | **2:43** – establish prayer, give zakat, and bow with those who bow. **24:56** – prayer, zakat and obeying the Messenger, so that you may receive mercy |
| A way to receive **Allah's mercy** | **7:156** – My mercy encompasses everything; I will decree it for those who fear Me and **give zakat** and believe in Our signs |
| A condition for **Allah's help** | **22:40–41** – Allah will help those who help Him; if We establish them in the land, they establish prayer and **give zakat**… |
| A quality of **the people of Paradise** | **23:4** – and they give zakat |
| A basic quality of **the Muslim community** | **9:71** – the believing men and women are allies of one another… they establish prayer and give zakat |

### A warning to those who do not give zakat
- **(9:34) – meaning:** For those who **hoard** gold and silver and do not spend it in Allah's path – **a painful punishment.**
- **(Tabarani) – meaning:** If a people withhold zakat, Allah will test them **with famine**.
- **(Hakim) – meaning:** If a people withhold zakat, Allah will **withhold the rain** from them.

---

## 3. The benefits of zakat (8) ⭐
1. **The giver's heart is purified**; he is freed from greed and saved from the low life of "money is everything" → he serves society with compassion.
2. **The poor person's heart is also purified**; he is freed from diseases of the heart such as **envy and hatred** → he prays for the progress of the wealthy.
3. A spirit of **gratitude to Allah** grows; it is a way of protecting blessings – **(14:7) – meaning:** if you are grateful, I will surely **give you more**.
4. **Mutual love, shared responsibility and unity** grow in society.
5. Large **economic inequality** does not arise.
6. Crimes caused by poverty, such as **theft and robbery**, decrease → **social security.**
7. **Economic growth** – to keep their wealth from shrinking, the wealthy **invest it in useful ways**.
8. **A solution to unemployment** – **capital** for those who have none; **vocational training** for those who need it.

---

## 4. Zakat is a collective duty ⭐
- Verse **9:60** names **those who work to collect zakat** as one of the groups entitled to it → collecting it from individuals and giving it to those entitled is **the work of the community.**
- **9:103** – a command to **collect** zakat from the wealthy.
- When the Prophet ﷺ sent **Mu'adh (RA)** to **Yemen** (meaning): "It is **to be taken from their rich and given back to their poor**" **(Bukhari).**
- The Prophet ﷺ and the caliphs **sent people** to collect zakat.

### Benefits of carrying it out collectively
- No wealthy person can **escape** the duty.
- The poor's **self-respect is protected.**
- **Large programmes** to end poverty can be planned.
- **Everyone** who needs care can be cared for.
- Attention can also be given to **social welfare work**.

---

## 5. Conditions for zakat to become due (7) ⭐
1. Being **a Muslim**
2. Being **free**
3. **Owning the wealth** – *zakat is not wajib on public property; but a guardian looking after the property of a child who has not reached puberty, or of a person of unsound mind, must pay zakat on it even though it is not his own.*
4. **Reaching the nisab** – the **minimum amount** at which zakat becomes due = **nisab**; it differs according to the type of wealth.
5. **Completion of a year (hawl)** – **a lunar year**, counted from when the wealth came into his possession. *For grain, fruit, buried treasure and the like, a year need not pass – zakat is due as soon as it is received.*
6. The wealth must be **able to grow**
7. It must be **in excess of basic needs**

### The fards of zakat (2)
1. **Making the intention (niyyah)**
2. **Giving it to those entitled**

## 6. Wealth on which zakat is due, and the nisab ⭐

### (1) Livestock – sheep/goats, cattle, camels
⚠️ There is **no zakat** on livestock **used for work** such as pulling carts or ploughing.

| Livestock | Starting nisab | Zakat |
|---|---|---|
| **Sheep/goats** | **40** | 40–120 → **1** female; 121–200 → **2**; 201–399 → **3**; 400–499 → **4**; then one female two years old for every further 100 |
| **Cattle** | **30** | For 30 cattle, **one one-year-old male (bull calf)**; for 40 cattle, **one two-year-old female (cow)**; then for further multiples of 30 and 40 (separately or combined) |
| **Camels** | **05** | For 5 camels **1 sheep** … for 20 camels **4 sheep**; at **25**, **one one-year-old female camel** |

### (2) Grain
- It must be produced (grown), **able to be stored**, and **a staple food** → such as **rice (paddy) and wheat**.
- **Nisab = 5 wasqs ≈ 675 kg**; for paddy stored **with its husk**, **double (1350 kg)**.
- Note:
  - a) **Several harvests** within a year → zakat each time; if each does not reach the nisab separately, they are **added together**.
  - b) **Two different grains** are not added together (e.g. wheat + rice).
  - c) **Different varieties of the same grain** are counted together (e.g. local rice + samba rice).

### (3) Fruit
- **Dates and grapes** – the same rules as for grain (including the nisab).

### (4) Minerals – gold and silver

| | Starting nisab | Equal to | Zakat |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Gold** | **20 mithqal** | **10 sovereigns ("pawn")** / **85 grams** | **1/40** |
| **Silver** | **200 dirhams** | **595 grams** | **1/40** |

- a) **All items of one kind** are counted together for the nisab (necklace, bangles, gold biscuits, pendant, bowl).
- b) **There is no zakat on jewellery in use**; there **is** zakat on ornaments **kept as extra savings**.
- c) **Gold and silver vessels** are liable to zakat.
- d) **If another metal is mixed in**, it is deducted before working out the nisab.

### (5) Money
- Starting nisab = **the value of 85 grams of gold.**
- (There is also the view that **the nisab of silver** should be taken for money.)

### (6) Trade goods
- **The same nisab as money.**
- a) If the nisab **was not reached at the start of the year but was reached at its end**, zakat becomes due.
- b) The nisab is based on **that day's market price** (which may be higher or lower than the purchase price).

## 7. Those entitled to zakat – eight groups ⭐
- When a man asked for zakat, the Prophet ﷺ said (meaning): "Allah was not pleased for **a prophet or anyone else to decide** about zakat; He Himself decided and divided it into **eight parts**" **(Abu Dawud).**
- Verse **(9:60)** says the same.

| No. | Arabic | Name | Explanation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | الفقير | **Faqir** | **An extremely poor person with no income at all** |
| 2 | المسكين | **Miskin** | One who **does not have enough** for himself and those he supports |
| 3 | العامل | **'Amil** | A worker who **assesses, collects and distributes** zakat |
| 4 | مؤلفة القلوب | **Mu'allafat al-qulub** | Those **drawn to Islam, or who may be drawn** to it |
| 5 | الرقاب | **Ar-riqab** | A slave who has **a contract to buy his freedom in instalments** |
| 6 | الغارمون | **Gharimun** | One who **fell into debt for a lawful reason** and cannot pay it |
| 7 | في سبيل الله | **Fi sabil Allah** | Those **in the path of Allah** |
| 8 | ابن السبيل | **Ibn as-sabil** | A traveller on **a permitted journey** who has **no means** to complete it |

## 8. Those not entitled to zakat (5) ⭐
1. One who has **sufficient income**
2. **Slaves**
3. **The Prophet's ﷺ family line** (**Banu Hashim, Banu al-Muttalib**)
4. Those whom the zakat-payer is **obliged to support** (wife, children, mother and father)
5. **Non-Muslims**

## ✅ Quick revision
- Zakat = purity, progress, growth; a share fixed in wealth.
- Joined with prayer (2:43, 24:56); mercy (7:156); help (22:40–41); people of Paradise (23:4); the community's quality (9:71).
- Warnings: 9:34; famine (Tabarani); rain withheld (Hakim).
- A collective duty: 9:60 ('amil), 9:103, Mu'adh – Yemen (Bukhari).
- 7 conditions; 2 fards (niyyah, giving to those entitled).
- Nisab: sheep 40, cattle 30, camels 5; grain 5 wasqs ≈ 675 kg (paddy in husk 1350); gold 20 mithqal = 85 g (1/40); silver 200 dirhams = 595 g (1/40); money = the value of 85 g of gold.
- 8 groups: faqir, miskin, 'amil, mu'allafat al-qulub, riqab, gharimun, fi sabil Allah, ibn as-sabil (9:60).
