Flashcard 4

Front: What is the purpose of fasting (2:183)?

Flashcard 6

Flashcard 8

Flashcard 9

Front: ليلة القدر (Laylat al-Qadr)

Flashcard 10

Front: The five benefits of fasting?

Back: Spiritual growth, realising the value of blessings, understanding the suffering of the poor, physical health, training in complete submission.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 8 | Topic: Benefits

Flashcard 11

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 8 | Topic: Benefits
