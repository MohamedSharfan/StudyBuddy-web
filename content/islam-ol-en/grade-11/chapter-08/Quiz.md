# Grade 11 – Chapter 8: The Deeper Meanings of Fasting – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_08/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q15 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. What is the literal meaning of the Arabic word "sawm"?

A. Being hungry
B. Purity
C. Abstaining, refraining
D. Prayer

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Sawm = abstaining, refraining. Its plural is siyam.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. In Islamic usage, from when until when is the fast kept?

A. From dawn until midday
B. From dawn (when the east grows light) until sunset
C. From sunrise until 'Isha'
D. 24 hours

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Abstaining, with the intention of doing it for Allah, from eating, drinking and sexual relations from dawn until sunset.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q3. According to verse 2:183, what is the purpose of fasting?

A. To become God-conscious (taqwa)
B. To lose weight
C. To save food
D. To gather money

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: "La'allakum tattaqun" – so that you may become God-conscious.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q4. According to verse 2:185, what must the sick person and the traveller do?

A. Their fast is completely excused
B. They need only pay money
C. They must fast double
D. They must make up the missed fasts on other days

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Allah wants ease; the sick person and the traveller must make up the missed fasts later.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q5. What is the view of the imams on leaving a fast in Ramadan without a valid reason?

A. It is like leaving a sunnah
B. It is a grave sin
C. It is permitted
D. It is only makruh

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Fasting is a compulsory duty that cannot be avoided.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q6. According to the hadith in Ahmad, what will intercede for the servant in the Hereafter?

A. Prayer and zakat
B. Hajj and 'umrah
C. Fasting and the Quran
D. Dhikr and du'a

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Fasting will say, "I kept him from food and desires by day," and the Quran, "I kept him from sleep at night"; both will be accepted.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q7. In the Hadith Qudsi, what is meant by the exception "except fasting"?

A. There is no reward for fasting
B. Fasting is rewarded only tenfold
C. Other good deeds are rewarded 10–700 times; fasting is for Allah, and He Himself gives its reward (without measure)
D. Fasting is accepted only in Ramadan

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Because the fasting person gives up desire and food for Allah, he receives a reward without measure (Bukhari, Muslim).

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q8. What are the two joys of the fasting person?

A. When he eats and when he sleeps
B. When he breaks his fast and when he meets his Lord
C. On Eid and on Friday
D. When he eats suhur and when he breaks his fast

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: This hadith (Bukhari, Muslim) shows that fasting leads to happiness in both worlds.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q9. What is the name of the gate of Paradise that only those who fast will enter?

A. Bab as-Salam
B. Firdaws
C. Bab ar-Rahmah
D. Ar-Rayyan

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: The call "Where are those who fasted?" will go out and they will enter through ar-Rayyan; once they have entered, it is closed.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q10. Which of the following does the textbook NOT mention as happening in Ramadan?

A. All people automatically become sinless
B. The devils are chained
C. There is a night better than a thousand months
D. The gates of Paradise are opened

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: The atmosphere of Ramadan helps spiritual growth; but only those who make use of it and strive will benefit.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q11. According to the hadith in Ibn Majah, what "removes the fire in the heart"?

A. Fasting in the month of patience and fasting three days every month
B. Only night prayer
C. Only charity
D. Hajj

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Fasting in the month of patience (Ramadan) and three days each month removes the fire in the heart, such as anger and envy.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q12. Why does the textbook call fasting a long training in patience?

A. Every day, for about 14 hours, one goes without food and drink and keeps away from desires, and this training continues for a month
B. Fasting lasts only one day
C. One must not sleep while fasting
D. One must not speak while fasting

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: A continuous training of a whole month builds patience.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q13. Who, while minister of finance of Egypt, fasted a great deal, fearing "If I eat my fill, I will forget the hunger of the poor"?

A. Musa (AS)
B. Sulayman (AS)
C. Yusuf (AS)
D. Dawud (AS)

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: This is an example of the benefit that fasting helps us understand the suffering of the poor.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q14. How does fasting make us realise the value of blessings?

A. By making us eat more
B. By increasing our sleep
C. By giving us money
D. By holding back many of the blessings we enjoy – and so moving us to be grateful

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: When we enjoy blessings all the time we do not see their value; when they are held back, we realise it.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q15. Which saying is quoted as evidence for the physical benefit of fasting?

A. "Cleanliness is half of iman"
B. "Fast, and you will be healthy"
C. "Make use of your youth before your old age"
D. "Du'a is worship"

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: The medical world also says fasting helps greatly in curing some illnesses. (Note: hadith scholars generally regard this narration as weak – see the Notes.)

Difficulty: Easy
