### A1. 2017 – Islam I, Q11

Question ID: P2017-I-Q11  
Year: 2017  
Paper and question: Islam I, Q11  
Marks: not stated on the paper (Islam I multiple-choice question)  
Source: OL_2017_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

The month in which there is no occasion for a sunnah fast

- **A.** Ramadan
- **B.** Rajab
- **C.** Shawwal
- **D.** Sha'ban

**Correct answer:** A – Ramadan *(option (1) on the original paper)*

**Explanation:** Because the obligatory (fard) fast is kept throughout Ramadan, there is no occasion for a sunnah fast in that month.

### B3. 2020 – Islam II, Q3 (iii)

Question ID: P2020-II-Q3.3  
Year: 2020  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q3 (iii)  
Marks: 10 marks for the whole of Q3  
Source: OL_2020_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(iii) Briefly explain five ways in which Islam shows the importance of the obligatory fast.

**Suggested answer:**

1. A compulsory duty – prescribed for us as for earlier communities; its purpose is taqwa (2:183); leaving it without reason is a major sin.
2. It will plead in the Hereafter – fasting and the Quran will intercede for the servant (Ahmad).
3. Forgiveness – whoever fasts with iman and hoping for reward has his past sins forgiven (Bukhari, Muslim).
4. Countless reward – "Fasting is for Me, and I Myself will give its reward" (Hadith Qudsi).
5. The one who fasts has two joys; a special gate in Paradise called "ar-Rayyan" (Bukhari, Muslim).

**Answer explanation:** Give evidence for each point of importance.

**Marking guidance (suggested only – not an official marking scheme):** No official marking scheme.

**Answer status:** Suggested – not an official answer (no official marking scheme was supplied); based on the Grade 10/11 textbook content
