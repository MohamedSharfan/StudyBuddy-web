# Grade 11 – Chapter 8: The Deeper Meanings of Fasting

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_08/Notes.md` (Tamil). English rendering.

## 🎯 What you will learn
- Sawm – meaning and definition
- Quranic evidence that fasting is obligatory
- Five virtues of fasting
- Five benefits of fasting

> 📖 **Opening verse of the chapter (2:183):** يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِيْنَ آمَنُوْا كُتِبَ عَلَيْكُمُ الصِّيَامُ كَمَا كُتِبَ عَلَى الَّذِيْنَ مِنْ قَبْلِكُمْ لَعَلَّكُمْ تَتَّقُوْنَ
> **Meaning:** "O you who believe! Fasting has been prescribed for you as it was prescribed for those before you, so that you may become **God-conscious (have taqwa)**."

---

## 1. What is fasting? ⭐
- A unique duty among **the five great duties** of Islam; **a training camp** that forms people who submit completely to God.

| | Explanation |
|---|---|
| **Arabic word** | **Sawm (الصوم)**; plural – **siyam (الصيام)** |
| **Literal meaning** | **Abstaining, refraining** |
| **Islamic usage** | A Muslim, **with the sincere intention of doing it for Allah**, abstaining from permitted things such as **eating, drinking and sexual relations** **from dawn (when the east grows light) until sunset** |

- Fasting is **a compulsory duty that cannot be avoided.**
- The imams regard **leaving a fast in Ramadan without a valid reason as a grave sin**.

---

## 2. Fasting is obligatory – evidence from the Quran
- **(2:183) – meaning:** It has been prescribed for you as it was for those before you, so that you may become God-conscious.
- **(2:185) – meaning:** It was in the month of Ramadan that the Quran was sent down as **guidance for mankind, clear proofs, and the criterion between right and wrong**. Whoever witnesses the month must fast. **The sick person / the traveller** must make it up on other days. Allah **wants ease for you, not hardship.**

💡 The purpose of fasting (2:183) = **taqwa (God-consciousness).**

## 3. The virtues of fasting (5) ⭐

| No. | Virtue | Essence of the hadith | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **It will intercede in the Hereafter** | Fasting and the Quran will intercede for the servant: fasting – "I kept him from food and desires **by day**"; the Quran – "I kept him from sleep **at night**"; both intercessions will be accepted | Ahmad |
| 2 | **Forgiveness and expiation** | Whoever fasts Ramadan **with iman and hoping for the reward** will have **his past sins** forgiven | Bukhari, Muslim |
| 3 | **Reward without measure** | Each good deed is rewarded **ten to seven hundred** times, **except fasting – it is for Me, and I Myself give its reward**; he gives up his desire and his food for My sake (Hadith Qudsi) | Bukhari, Muslim |
| 4 | **Happiness in both worlds** | The fasting person has **two joys**: **when he breaks his fast**, and **when he meets his Lord** | Bukhari, Muslim |
| 5 | **A special gate in Paradise** | A gate called **"ar-Rayyan" (الريان)** – only those who fast enter through it; the call goes out, "Where are those who fasted?"; once they have entered, **it is closed** | Bukhari, Muslim |

## 4. The benefits of fasting (5) ⭐

### (1) Spiritual growth
Ramadan = a month with **an atmosphere** for growing spiritually. In that month:
- **The gates of Paradise are opened**; Paradise is adorned
- **The gates of Hell are closed**
- **The devils are chained**
- **The angels** ask forgiveness for those who fast
- The **du'a** of those who fast is **accepted**
- **The night better than a thousand months (Laylat al-Qadr)** is in this month
- **Freedom from Hell** is granted

→ Those who use this atmosphere **recite more Quran, make more dhikr, worship at night, give charity, look after their relatives, and focus on tawbah**.

#### Training in patience
- Every day, for **about fourteen (14) hours**, a person goes without food and drink and keeps away from desires; this continues for a month → **a long training in patience.**
- **(Ibn Majah) – meaning:** "Fasting **in the month of patience** and fasting **three days every month** removes **the fire (anger, envy) in the heart**."

### (2) Realising the value of blessings
- When we enjoy blessings all the time we **do not realise their value**; fasting, **by holding them back**, makes us realise their value and moves us to **be grateful**.

### (3) Understanding the suffering of the poor
- Fasting has been made obligatory on **everyone**, rich and poor alike → the wealthy understand **the pain of hunger** from experience.
- **Yusuf (AS)** is reported to have fasted a great deal while serving as **Egypt's minister of finance**; the reason (meaning): "**I fear that if I eat my fill, I will forget the hunger of the poor.**"

### (4) Physical health
- **The medical world** says fasting helps greatly in curing some illnesses.
- **(Saying – meaning):** "**Fast, and you will be healthy.**"

> ⚠️ The Tamil source quotes this saying as a hadith without naming a collection. Hadith scholars generally regard this narration as weak (da'if) – confirm with the teacher before quoting it as a hadith.

### (5) Training in complete submission to God
- **A long field training** to build the habit of accepting God's commands in every situation of life.

👉 Our predecessors regarded the time of fasting as **a rare opportunity to advance on the road to Paradise**; fasting gave them **God-consciousness, physical strength and noble qualities**.

## ✅ Quick revision
- Sawm = abstaining; plural siyam; from dawn until sunset.
- 2:183 – the purpose is taqwa; 2:185 – the Quran revealed in Ramadan; allowance for the sick and the traveller.
- Virtues: it will intercede (Ahmad); past sins forgiven; "it is for Me"; two joys; the gate of ar-Rayyan.
- Benefits: spiritual growth (14-hour training in patience), realising blessings, understanding the poor (Yusuf AS), health, complete submission.
