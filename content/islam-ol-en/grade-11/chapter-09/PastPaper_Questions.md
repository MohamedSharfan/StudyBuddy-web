### A1. 2016 – Islam I, Q11

Question ID: P2016-I-Q11  
Year: 2016  
Paper and question: Islam I, Q11  
Marks: not stated on the paper (Islam I multiple-choice question)  
Source: OL_2016_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

Column I below gives activities related to Hajj and Column II gives categories.

| Column I | Column II |
|---|---|
| (i) Men covering the head | A - wajib |
| (ii) Being of sound mind | B - fard |
| (iii) Performing 'tawaf al-wida'' | C - muharramat (prohibitions) |
| (iv) Standing at 'Arafah on 9 Dhul-Hijjah | D - shart (condition) |

The correct answer obtained when Column II is matched with Column I is

- **A.** A, C, B, D
- **B.** B, A, D, C
- **C.** C, D, A, B
- **D.** D, B, C, A

**Correct answer:** C – C, D, A, B *(option (3) on the original paper)*

**Explanation:** (i) men covering the head in ihram – muharramat (C); (ii) sound mind – shart (D); (iii) tawaf al-wida' – wajib (A); (iv) standing at 'Arafah – fard (B). So C, D, A, B.

**Answer status:** Suggested – not an official answer (no official marking scheme was supplied); based on the Grade 10/11 textbook content

### B2. 2018 – Islam II, Q3

Question ID: P2018-II-Q3.1  
Year: 2018  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q3  
Marks: 10 marks  
Source: OL_2018_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(i) Name two kinds of tawaf.
(ii) State three benefits gained through Hajj.
(iii) Briefly explain five wajib acts of Hajj.

**Suggested answer:**

(i) Any two of the following: tawaf al-ifadah (fard), tawaf al-qudum (sunnah), tawaf al-wida' (wajib).

(ii) 1. Whoever performs Hajj properly returns free of sin like a newborn baby (Bukhari).
2. Showing complete servitude by giving time, wealth, body and heart; taqwa grows.
3. Training in equality, brotherhood, unity, tolerance and remembrance of the Hereafter.

(iii) 1. Making the intention at the miqat – Sri Lankan pilgrims at "Yalamlam".
2. Staying at Muzdalifah on the night of the 10th of Dhul-Hijjah.
3. Staying at Mina on the days of tashriq (11, 12, 13 Dhul-Hijjah).
4. Throwing seven stones at Jamrat al-'Aqabah on the 10th of Dhul-Hijjah.
5. Throwing seven stones at each of the three jamarat after midday on the days of tashriq.
6. Performing tawaf al-wida'.
If a wajib is missed, a fidyah must be given (sacrificing a sheep / feeding six poor people / fasting three days).

**Answer explanation:** In (iii) briefly explain five wajib acts with their day/place.

**Marking guidance (suggested only – not an official marking scheme):** No official marking scheme.
