# Grade 11 – Chapter 9: Hajj and 'Umrah – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_09/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q17 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. Which of the following is NOT a condition for hajj to become obligatory?

A. Knowing Arabic
B. Financial means
C. A safe route
D. Reaching puberty

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Being a Muslim, mature, of sound mind, free, having the means, physical strength and a safe route, and enough time – these are the conditions.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. Which of the arkan of hajj is NOT required for 'umrah?

A. Entering ihram
B. Performing sa'y
C. Standing at 'Arafah on the 9th of Dhul-Hijjah
D. Cutting the hair

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Standing at 'Arafah is not required to perform 'umrah.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q3. What happens if a "fard" of hajj is missed?

A. The duty of hajj is not fulfilled
B. Giving fidyah is enough
C. Nothing happens
D. Charity must be given

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: If a fard is missed the duty is not fulfilled; only when a wajib is missed can it be made up through fidyah.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q4. Which of the following is NOT part of the fidyah for missing a wajib?

A. Sacrificing a sheep
B. Performing the whole hajj again
C. Fasting for three days
D. Giving food to six poor people in the Haram

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Fidyah – one of these: sacrificing a sheep, feeding six poor people, fasting three days.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q5. At which miqat do Sri Lankan pilgrims make their intention?

A. Dhul-Hulayfah
B. Juhfah
C. Yalamlam
D. Qarn al-Manazil

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Making the intention at the appointed miqat is wajib; Sri Lankan pilgrims make it at a place called Yalamlam.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q6. Which days are called "ayyam at-tashriq"?

A. 8, 9, 10 Dhul-Hijjah
B. 1, 2, 3 Dhul-Hijjah
C. 11, 12, 13 Dhul-Hijjah
D. 27, 28, 29 Ramadan

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: 11, 12 and 13 Dhul-Hijjah – on these days staying at Mina and stoning all three jamarat is wajib.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q7. On the 10th of Dhul-Hijjah, which jamrah is stoned, and with how many pebbles?

A. Jamrat al-'Aqabah – 7 pebbles
B. Jamrat al-Ula – 21 pebbles
C. Jamrat al-Wusta – 7 pebbles
D. All three jamarat – 7 each

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: On Eid day, the 10th of Dhul-Hijjah, seven pebbles are thrown at Jamrat al-'Aqabah only. On the days of tashriq, seven at each of the three.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q8. Which of the following is a wajib of hajj?

A. Standing at 'Arafah
B. Sa'y
C. Tawaf al-ifadah
D. Tawaf al-wada'

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Tawaf al-wada' is wajib; 'Arafah, tawaf al-ifadah and sa'y are arkan (fards).

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q9. Which sunnah tawaf is performed on entering Makkah?

A. Tawaf al-qudum
B. Tawaf al-wada'
C. Tawaf al-ifadah
D. Tawaf al-'umrah

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Qudum = arrival. It is sunnah. Ifadah – fard; wada' – on leaving, wajib.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q10. Which of the following is NOT among the things forbidden in ihram (muharramat)?

A. Men wearing stitched clothing
B. Reciting the talbiyah
C. Hunting
D. Women covering the face

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Reciting the talbiyah is sunnah, not forbidden.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q11. What must be done if one spoils the hajj by having relations with one's wife while in ihram?

A. Fast for one day
B. Give charity only
C. Nothing is needed
D. Sacrifice a camel / cow / sheep, continue that hajj to the end, and make it up without delay

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: This is more serious than the other prohibitions; a sacrifice and making it up (qada') are both obligatory.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q12. Which way involves one intention for hajj and 'umrah together?

A. Al-qiran
B. Al-ifrad
C. At-tamattu'
D. Al-ihram

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Ifrad – hajj only; qiran – both together; tamattu' – separate intentions for each.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q13. Where does tawaf begin, and on which side must the Ka'bah be?

A. From Maqam Ibrahim; on the right
B. From the Black Stone; on the left
C. From Safa; in front
D. From the well of Zamzam; behind

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Starting from the Black Stone (or standing in line with it if one cannot reach it), one goes round seven times with the Ka'bah on the left.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q14. Where are two rak'ahs of sunnah prayed after tawaf?

A. The Black Stone
B. Mina
C. 'Arafah
D. Maqam Ibrahim

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: After tawaf, pray two rak'ahs at Maqam Ibrahim, drink Zamzam, and then perform sa'y.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q15. Where do pilgrims go after sunset on the 9th of Dhul-Hijjah?

A. Mina
B. Makkah
C. Muzdalifah
D. Madinah

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: After sunset they go from 'Arafah to Muzdalifah, pray Maghrib and 'Isha' together, and stay the night there.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q16. According to the hadith in Bukhari, how does someone who performs hajj properly return?

A. As a rich man
B. Like a newborn baby
C. As a scholar
D. As a leader

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: His sins are forgiven and he returns pure, like a newborn baby.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q17. Who said that hajj is "the best act of worship, containing the features of all acts of worship"?

A. Imam ash-Shafi'i
B. Imam al-Ghazali
C. Imam Malik
D. Imam Abu Hanifah

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Imam Abu Hanifah (RH) said this after comparing hajj with the other acts of worship.

Difficulty: Medium
