# Grade 11 – Chapter 9: Hajj and 'Umrah

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_09/Notes.md` (Tamil). English rendering.

## 🎯 What you will learn
- The conditions for hajj to become obligatory
- The arkan (fards), wajibs, sunnahs and prohibitions (muharramat) of hajj
- Fidyah (compensation)
- The three ways of performing hajj and 'umrah
- How to perform 'umrah and hajj (step by step)
- The benefits of hajj

> 📖 **Opening verse of the chapter (2:196):** وَأَتِمُّوا الْحَجَّ وَالْعُمْرَةَ لِلّٰهِ – **Meaning:** "Complete the hajj and the 'umrah for Allah."

---

## 1. Hajj – introduction
- One of **the five duties** of Islam.
- An act of worship carried out by going **to Makkah in the month of Dhul-Hijjah**.

### Conditions for hajj to become obligatory (7) ⭐
The person performing hajj must be:
1. **A Muslim**
2. **Past puberty (mature)**
3. **Of sound mind**
4. **Free**
5. Having **the financial means to support himself and those in his care** until he returns from hajj
6. Having **physical strength and a safe route** for travel
7. Having **enough time** to go to Makkah and perform hajj.

---

## 2. The arkan (fards) of hajj – 6 ⭐
1. **Making the intention (entering ihram)**
2. **Standing (staying) at 'Arafah on the 9th of Dhul-Hijjah**
3. Performing **tawaf al-ifadah**
4. Performing **sa'y**
5. **Shaving or cutting the hair**
6. Doing them **in the proper order**

> 📌 Of these, **standing at 'Arafah is not required for 'umrah.**

### The difference between fard and wajib ⭐
- Other duties have **only arkan (fards)**; hajj also has **wajibs**.

| | If missed |
|---|---|
| **Fard** | The duty is **not fulfilled** |
| **Wajib** | **Compensation (fidyah)** must be given for it |

### Fidyah (الفدية) – compensation (doing one of these)
- **Sacrificing one sheep/goat**
- Giving food **to six poor people in the Haram**
- **Fasting for three days**

---

## 3. The wajibs of hajj – 6 ⭐
1. Making the intention **at the appointed miqat** (**Sri Lankan pilgrims make their intention at "Yalamlam"**)
2. Staying **at Muzdalifah on the night of the 10th of Dhul-Hijjah**
3. Staying **at Mina** on **the days of tashriq (11th, 12th and 13th of Dhul-Hijjah)**
4. Throwing **seven pebbles at Jamrat al-'Aqabah on the 10th of Dhul-Hijjah**
5. On the days of tashriq, **after midday**, throwing **seven pebbles each at all three jamarat** – Jamrat al-'Aqabah, Jamrat al-Wusta and Jamrat al-Ula
6. Performing **tawaf al-wada'**

> 📌 **Miqat (الميقات)** = the set boundary at which one must enter ihram (make the intention).

---

## 4. Sunnahs of hajj and 'umrah – 8
1. **Taking a bath (ghusl)** – before the intention, before entering Makkah, before staying at 'Arafah and Muzdalifah, before stoning the jamarat, before tawaf
2. **Applying perfume** to the body before the intention
3. **Saying the intention aloud**
4. Reciting the **talbiyah**
5. Performing tawaf on entering Makkah – **tawaf al-qudum**
6. Staying **at Mina on the night of the 9th of Dhul-Hijjah**
7. Reciting the set **du'as** at the set places
8. Drinking **plenty of Zamzam water**

---

## 5. Muharramat (what is forbidden in ihram) – 10 ⭐
1. **Men wearing stitched clothing**
2. **Applying perfume**
3. Putting **oil** on the hair or beard
4. **Removing hair or cutting nails** from any part of the body
5. **Marrying, or arranging a marriage**
6. **Sexual relations** or related acts
7. **Men covering the head**
8. **Women covering the face**
9. **Hunting**
10. Cutting trees or plants **within the boundaries of the Haram**

### If they are broken
- If one of these is done after entering ihram → **a fidyah like the fidyah for leaving a wajib.**
- ⚠️ If one **spoils** the hajj / 'umrah **by having relations with one's wife** → one must **sacrifice a camel, a cow or sheep**, **continue** that hajj / 'umrah to the end, and **make it up (qada') without delay**.

---

## 6. The three ways of performing hajj and 'umrah ⭐

| Way | Arabic | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| **Al-ifrad** | الإفراد | Intention for **hajj only** |
| **Al-qiran** | القران | Intention for **hajj and 'umrah together** |
| **At-tamattu'** | التمتع | **Separate** intentions for 'umrah and for hajj |

---

## 7. How to perform 'umrah
1. Before putting on ihram, **cut the nails, remove body hair, take a bath, apply perfume**, make the intention and pray **two rak'ahs of sunnah**.
   - Men wear **unstitched white** garments; women wear **clothing approved by Islam**.
2. On reaching the Ka'bah, starting **from the Black Stone (al-Hajar al-Aswad)** (or standing in line with it if one cannot reach it), go round **seven times** keeping **the Ka'bah on the left** = **tawaf.**
   - The du'a recited then: **رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ** – (meaning) Our Lord, give us good in this world and good in the Hereafter, and protect us from the punishment of the Fire (other du'as may also be recited)
3. After tawaf, two rak'ahs of sunnah at **Maqam Ibrahim**; then **Zamzam** water.
4. Sa'y **starting from the hill of Safa** – from Safa to Marwah and from Marwah to Safa, **seven times** in all; one may say tasbih, tahmid, takbir and tahlil.
5. After sa'y, the 'umrah is completed by **cutting or shaving the hair**.

---

## 8. How to perform hajj (day by day) ⭐

| Day | Actions |
|---|---|
| **8 Dhul-Hijjah** | Hajj **begins.** Take a bath, apply perfume, enter ihram with the intention and go to **Mina** calling the **talbiyah**. Pray Zuhr, 'Asr, Maghrib, 'Isha' and Subh there (shortening four-rak'ah prayers to two is sunnah) |
| **9 Dhul-Hijjah** | After sunrise, go to **'Arafah.** Pray Zuhr and 'Asr **combined and shortened**; spend much time in **du'a.** After sunset go to **Muzdalifah** – pray Maghrib and 'Isha' combined ('Isha' shortened); stay the night there |
| **10 Dhul-Hijjah** (Eid day) | Pray Subh at Muzdalifah and go to **Mina** (collecting pebbles for the jamarat). Before Zuhr throw **seven pebbles at Jamrat al-'Aqabah.** **Shave the head** (women cut a little hair). Take off the ihram garments and wear ordinary clothes. Go to Makkah and perform **tawaf al-ifadah** (it may also be delayed) |
| **11, 12, 13 Dhul-Hijjah** | Stay at **Mina** and, after Zuhr, stone **all three jamarat.** Anyone who has not yet done tawaf al-ifadah does it; after the tawaf, **sa'y** |
| **Finally** | Before leaving Makkah for home or for Madinah, **tawaf al-wada'** |

## 9. The benefits of hajj ⭐
- **(Tirmidhi) – meaning:** whoever **has the means but does not perform hajj** may as well die **a Jew or a Christian**.
- **(Bukhari) – meaning:** whoever performs hajj properly returns **like a newborn baby** (free of sin).
- Even if one **dies on the way** after setting out for hajj, one receives the reward of hajj.
- The servant shows his **complete servitude**; he devotes his **time, wealth, body and heart**.
- **Taqwa** grows; he is united with his fellow Muslims.
- Qualities trained: **equality, brotherhood, unity, giving way to others, tolerance, living together, remembering the Hereafter.**
- **Imam Abu Hanifah (RH)**, comparing hajj with other acts of worship, said hajj is **the best act of worship, containing the features of all acts of worship**.

## 10. Key Arabic terms
| Arabic | Meaning |
|---|---|
| الإحرام (ihram) | The sacred state entered with the intention for hajj / 'umrah; also its garments |
| الميقات (miqat) | The boundary at which ihram must be entered |
| عرفة ('Arafah) | The plain where pilgrims stay on 9 Dhul-Hijjah |
| طواف الإفاضة (tawaf al-ifadah) | The fard tawaf of hajj |
| طواف القدوم (tawaf al-qudum) | The (sunnah) tawaf on entering Makkah |
| طواف الوداع (tawaf al-wada') | The farewell tawaf before leaving Makkah |
| السعي (sa'y) | Going seven times between Safa and Marwah |
| جمرة العقبة / الوسطى / الأولى | The three jamarat where pebbles are thrown |
| الحجر الأسود (al-Hajar al-Aswad) | The Black Stone in the Ka'bah – the starting point of tawaf |
| مقام إبراهيم (Maqam Ibrahim) | The place where two rak'ahs are prayed after tawaf |
| زمزم (Zamzam) | The blessed water |
| التلبية (talbiyah) | The call "Labbayk Allahumma labbayk…" |
| التسبيح / التحميد / التكبير / التهليل | Subhanallah / Alhamdulillah / Allahu akbar / La ilaha illallah |
| أيام التشريق (ayyam at-tashriq) | 11, 12 and 13 Dhul-Hijjah |
| المحرمات (muharramat) | What is forbidden in ihram |
| الفدية (fidyah) | Compensation |
| النية (niyyah) | Intention |

## ✅ Quick revision
- 2:196 – complete the hajj and the 'umrah for Allah.
- 7 conditions; 6 arkan (ihram, 'Arafah, tawaf al-ifadah, sa'y, cutting the hair, order) – no 'Arafah for 'umrah.
- If a wajib is missed, fidyah: sacrifice a sheep / feed 6 poor people of the Haram / fast 3 days.
- 6 wajibs: intention at the miqat (Sri Lanka – Yalamlam), staying at Muzdalifah, staying at Mina (days of tashriq), 7 pebbles at al-'Aqabah, 7 pebbles at each of the 3 jamarat, tawaf al-wada'.
- Ways: ifrad, qiran, tamattu'.
- Benefits: like a newborn baby (Bukhari); one who has the means but does not go (Tirmidhi).
