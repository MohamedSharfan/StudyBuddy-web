# Grade 11 – Chapter 9: Hajj and 'Umrah – Flashcards

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_09/Flashcards.md` (Tamil), cards 1–16 in the same order.

---

Flashcard 1

Front: الإحرام (Al-ihram)

Back: The sacred state entered with the intention for hajj / 'umrah; for men, unstitched white garments.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 9 | Topic: Key terms

---

Flashcard 2

Front: الميقات (Al-miqat)

Back: The set boundary at which ihram must be entered; for Sri Lankans – Yalamlam.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 9 | Topic: Key terms

---

Flashcard 3

Front: What are the six arkan of hajj?

Back: Ihram, standing at 'Arafah (9 Dhul-Hijjah), tawaf al-ifadah, sa'y, cutting the hair, proper order.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 9 | Topic: Arkan

---

Flashcard 4

Front: What are the six wajibs of hajj?

Back: Intention at the miqat, staying at Muzdalifah, staying at Mina on the days of tashriq, 7 pebbles at al-'Aqabah on the 10th, 7 pebbles at each of the 3 jamarat on the days of tashriq, tawaf al-wada'.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 9 | Topic: Wajibs

---

Flashcard 5

Front: الفدية (Al-fidyah)

Back: Compensation – sacrifice a sheep / feed 6 poor people of the Haram / fast 3 days.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 9 | Topic: Fidyah

---

Flashcard 6

Front: أيام التشريق (Ayyam at-tashriq)

Back: The 11th, 12th and 13th of Dhul-Hijjah.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 9 | Topic: Key terms

---

Flashcard 7

Front: What are the three jamarat?

Back: Jamrat al-'Aqabah, Jamrat al-Wusta, Jamrat al-Ula.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 9 | Topic: Key terms

---

Flashcard 8

Front: طواف القدوم (qudum) / طواف الإفاضة (ifadah) / طواف الوداع (wada')

Back: Qudum – the arrival tawaf (sunnah); ifadah – the fard tawaf of hajj; wada' – the farewell tawaf (wajib).

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 9 | Topic: Kinds of tawaf

---

Flashcard 9

Front: The three ways of performing hajj / 'umrah?

Back: Ifrad (hajj only), qiran (both together), tamattu' (separate intentions for each).

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 9 | Topic: Three ways

---

Flashcard 10

Front: التلبية (At-talbiyah)

Back: "Labbayk Allahumma labbayk…" – called out by the person in ihram; sunnah.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 9 | Topic: Sunnahs

---

Flashcard 11

Front: الحجر الأسود (Al-Hajar al-Aswad)

Back: The Black Stone in the Ka'bah – the place where tawaf begins.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 9 | Topic: Places

---

Flashcard 12

Front: مقام إبراهيم (Maqam Ibrahim)

Back: The place where two rak'ahs of sunnah are prayed after tawaf.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 9 | Topic: Places

---

Flashcard 13

Front: السعي (As-sa'y)

Back: Going seven times in all from Safa to Marwah and back.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 9 | Topic: Key terms

---

Flashcard 14

Front: Four main things forbidden in ihram?

Back: Men wearing stitched clothes / covering the head, women covering the face, applying perfume, hunting (also: removing hair or nails, marriage, cutting trees in the Haram).

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 9 | Topic: Muharramat

---

Flashcard 15

Front: The du'a recited during tawaf?

Back: Rabbana atina fid-dunya hasanatan wa fil-akhirati hasanatan wa qina 'adhaban-nar.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 9 | Topic: How to perform 'umrah
