# Grade 11 – Chapter 4: Things That Harm Iman – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_04/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q15 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. What does the term "ilhad" refer to?

A. Associating partners with Allah
B. Leaving Islam
C. Atheism
D. Hypocrisy

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Ilhad = atheism. Its main aim is to abolish religions in the world; it rejects God and His messengers.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. Which verse is evidence that people with atheistic views existed even in the Prophet's ﷺ time?

A. 4:48
B. 39:66
C. 3:91
D. 45:24

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: 45:24 (al-Jathiyah) is about those who said, "There is nothing but our life in this world; nothing destroys us except time."

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q3. What historical fact does the textbook give to show that atheism goes against human nature?

A. All people are scientists
B. In human history there have never been societies without religion
C. Atheists are the majority
D. Religions are new

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: In every age people have followed a religion; this confirms that living with belief in God is human nature.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q4. What is the literal meaning of the word "kufr"?

A. Covering, concealing
B. Making partners
C. Turning back
D. Doubt

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Kufr = covering, concealing. Making partners is "shirk"; turning back is "riddah".

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q5. To which kind of kufr does "not believing that prayer is obligatory" belong?

A. Through actions
B. Through words
C. Through belief
D. It is not kufr

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Not believing in Allah, the prophets, Paradise and Hell, or the obligation of prayer is kufr through belief.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q6. Which kind of kufr is "mocking religious duties"?

A. Through belief
B. Minor shirk
C. Through actions
D. Through words

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Insulting Allah, insulting the Prophet and mocking religious duties are kufr through words.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q7. Tearing the Quran to insult it is which kind of kufr?

A. Through belief
B. Through words
C. Ilhad
D. Through actions

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Tearing the Quran / throwing it in the rubbish and worshipping created things are kufr through actions.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q8. Which verse says that for one who dies a disbeliever, even an earth full of gold offered as ransom will not be accepted?

A. 3:91 (Al 'Imran)
B. 24:39 (an-Nur)
C. 45:24 (al-Jathiyah)
D. 4:48 (an-Nisa')

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: 3:91 shows the danger of kufr; such people will have a painful punishment and no helpers.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q9. Which verse compares the deeds of the disbelievers to a mirage?

A. 24:39
B. 3:91
C. 39:66
D. 45:24

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: 24:39 (an-Nur) – a thirsty man thinks it is water but finds nothing when he reaches it; kufr blocks the reward of good deeds.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q10. What is the Islamic definition of "shirk"?

A. Leaving Islam
B. Believing that anyone or any power shares in Allah's divinity, or doing an act that reflects this
C. Denying that God exists
D. Committing a sin

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Shirk = making partners. Attributing a share in Allah's divinity to others, and acting on that, is shirk.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q11. What is the only great sin that is not forgiven without tawbah?

A. Major shirk (ash-shirk al-akbar)
B. Lying
C. Consulting fortune-tellers
D. Backbiting

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: 4:48 – "Allah does not forgive that partners be associated with Him, but He forgives what is less than that to whom He wills."

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q12. Which of the following is an example of "minor shirk"?

A. Worshipping idols
B. Denying that God exists
C. Swearing by other than Allah
D. Leaving Islam for another religion

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Swearing by other than Allah, charms and consulting fortune-tellers are minor shirk. It is the worst sin after major shirk.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q13. What is the literal meaning of the word "riddah"?

A. Covering
B. Returning to a former state, turning back, going backwards
C. Making partners
D. Atheism

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: In Islamic usage, riddah = a Muslim leaving Islam; the one who leaves is a murtadd.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q14. Which of the rulings about a murtadd is WRONG?

A. His wife is separated from him
B. He may inherit from his relatives
C. If he dies without tawbah, the funeral prayer is not performed for him
D. His property is not given to his heirs

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: A murtadd has no right to inherit from his relatives. His property is used to pay his debts and support his family.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q15. What subtle point does the textbook make in its conclusion about the things that harm iman?

A. Doubts do not harm iman
B. Only actions harm iman
C. Even doubts about Allah arising in the mind can harm iman
D. Nothing harms iman

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Pure iman is necessary for success in both worlds; iman must be protected even from doubts arising in the heart.

Difficulty: Medium
