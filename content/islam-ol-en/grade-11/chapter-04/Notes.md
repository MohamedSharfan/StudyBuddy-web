# Grade 11 – Chapter 4: Things That Harm Iman

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_04/Notes.md` (Tamil). English rendering.

## 🎯 What you will learn
Four things that stain iman:
1. **Ilhad** (atheism)
2. **Kufr** (disbelief)
3. **Shirk** (associating partners with Allah)
4. **Riddah** (apostasy – leaving Islam)

> 📖 **Opening verse of the chapter (4:48 – an-Nisa'):**
> إِنَّ اللهَ لَا يَغْفِرُ أَنْ يُشْرَكَ بِهِ وَيَغْفِرُ مَا دُوْنَ ذٰلِكَ لِمَنْ يَشَاءُ
> **Meaning:** "Indeed, Allah **does not forgive that partners be associated with Him**, but He forgives what is less than that to whom He wills."

---

## Introduction
- A Muslim regards iman as **the greatest blessing**; he builds his life on it and **does not hesitate to give up anything** to protect it.
- To protect iman, **knowledge of the things that stain it** is necessary.

---

## 1. Ilhad (الإلحاد) – atheism ⭐
- **Ilhad** = **atheism.**
- **Main aim:** to **abolish religions** in the world.
- By denying that God exists, it rejects **divine guidance and the messengers**.
- People with atheistic views existed **even in the Prophet's ﷺ time**:

> 📖 **(45:24 – al-Jathiyah) – meaning:** They (who do not believe in the Hereafter) say, "**There is nothing but our life in this world; we die and we live, and nothing destroys us except time.**" They have no knowledge of it; they only guess.

### Why is atheism wrong?
| Reason | Explanation |
|---|---|
| **It goes against human nature** | In every age people have followed a religion; **there have never been societies without religion** – living with belief in God is human nature |
| **It also goes against reason** | Atheists cannot answer **the proofs** of those who accept God |

👉 Protecting the world from atheism, which threatens human society itself, is **an urgent and necessary duty.**

---

## 2. Kufr (الكفر) – disbelief ⭐
- **Linguistically:** **covering, concealing.**
- **In Islamic usage:** **rejecting** the **beliefs** of Islam and its **practices** that are definitely known.

### Three ways kufr comes about ⭐
| Kind | Examples |
|---|---|
| **1) Through belief** | Not believing in Allah, not believing in the prophets, not believing in Paradise and Hell, **not believing that prayer is obligatory** |
| **2) Through words** | **Insulting Allah, insulting the Prophet ﷺ, mocking religious duties** |
| **3) Through actions** | **Tearing the Quran / throwing it in the rubbish to insult it, worshipping created things** |

### The danger of kufr
> 📖 **(3:91 – Al 'Imran) – meaning:** Those who disbelieve and die as disbelievers – even if they offered **an earth full of gold** as ransom, it would not be accepted; they will have a painful punishment and no helpers.

> 📖 **(24:39 – an-Nur) – meaning:** The deeds of the disbelievers are like **a mirage** in a desert; a thirsty man thinks it is water, but when he reaches it he finds nothing; instead he finds Allah, who settles his account.

👉 Kufr **blocks the reward of good deeds and leads to Hell forever**.

---

## 3. Shirk (الشرك) – associating partners with Allah ⭐
- **Linguistically:** **making partners, joining together.**
- **In Islamic usage:** **believing that anyone or any power shares** in Allah's **divinity**, or **doing any act** that reflects such a belief.

### The two kinds of shirk ⭐

| | **Ash-shirk al-akbar** (الشرك الأكبر) – major shirk | **Ash-shirk al-asghar** (الشرك الأصغر) – minor shirk |
|---|---|---|
| Definition | Doing **acts of worship that are for Allah alone for something other than Allah** | **Next to major shirk** among sins |
| Result | The doer's **good deeds bring no benefit**; it is **the only great sin not forgiven without tawbah** | It must not be denied that it is **as bad as** the major sins |
| Evidence / examples | **39:66** – if you associate partners your deeds will be wasted; worship Allah alone; **4:48** – He does not forgive shirk | **Swearing by other than Allah, using charms (sorcery), consulting fortune-tellers** |

👉 Keeping away from shirk, which dishonours Allah, is the quality of **those travelling the road to Paradise**.

---

## 4. Riddah (الردة) – apostasy ⭐
- **Linguistically:** **returning to a former state, turning back, going backwards.**
- **In Islamic usage:** a Muslim leaving Islam by **saying words** or **doing an act** that makes him a kafir.
- **In short:** a Muslim **changing his religion** = riddah.
- One who has left Islam = **murtadd (مرتد).**

### Rulings about a murtadd ⭐
1. The Muslim community **cuts off its ties** with him.
2. His **wife is separated from him.**
3. His property is used **to pay his debts and to support his family**.
4. His property **is not given to his heirs.**
5. He **has no right to inherit** from his relatives.
6. If he dies **without tawbah**, **the (funeral) prayer is not performed** for him.
7. He is **not buried in a Muslim cemetery**.
8. The Muslim community regards him as **a traitor to the religion**.

---

## 5. Conclusion
- **Pure, unmixed iman** is necessary for success in both worlds.
- Even **doubts about Allah arising in the mind** can harm iman.
- The goal of life is to live with **sincere (ikhlas) iman** and win Allah's pleasure.
