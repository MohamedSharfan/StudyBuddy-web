# Grade 11 – Chapter 4: Things That Harm Iman – Flashcards

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_04/Flashcards.md` (Tamil), cards 1–13 in the same order.

---

Flashcard 1

Front: What are the four things that harm iman?

Back: Ilhad, kufr, shirk, riddah.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 4 | Topic: Overview

---

Flashcard 2

Front: الإلحاد (Al-ilhad)

Back: Atheism – its main aim is to abolish religions.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 4 | Topic: Ilhad

---

Flashcard 3

Front: What does atheism go against?

Back: Human nature and reason.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 4 | Topic: Ilhad

---

Flashcard 4

Front: الكفر (Al-kufr)

Back: Linguistically – covering, concealing; in usage – rejecting the beliefs and practices of Islam.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 4 | Topic: Kufr

---

Flashcard 5

Front: What are the three ways kufr comes about?

Back: Through belief, through words, through actions.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 4 | Topic: Kufr

---

Flashcard 6

Front: الشرك (Ash-shirk)

Back: Linguistically – making partners, joining together; in usage – attributing a share in Allah's divinity to others.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 4 | Topic: Shirk

---

Flashcard 7

Front: الشرك الأكبر (Ash-shirk al-akbar)

Back: Major shirk – doing acts of worship meant for Allah for something other than Allah; the only sin not forgiven without tawbah.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 4 | Topic: Shirk

---

Flashcard 8

Front: الشرك الأصغر (Ash-shirk al-asghar)

Back: Minor shirk – e.g. swearing by other than Allah, charms (sorcery), consulting fortune-tellers.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 4 | Topic: Shirk

---

Flashcard 9

Front: الردة (Ar-riddah)

Back: Linguistically – returning to a former state; in usage – a Muslim leaving Islam (apostasy).

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 4 | Topic: Riddah

---

Flashcard 10

Front: مرتد (Murtadd)

Back: One who has left Islam (an apostate).

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 4 | Topic: Riddah

---

Flashcard 11

Front: Four key rulings about a murtadd?

Back: His wife is separated; his property does not go to heirs; he cannot inherit; if he dies without tawbah there is no funeral prayer and no burial in a Muslim cemetery.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 4 | Topic: Riddah

---

Flashcard 12

Front: What is the meaning of verse 4:48?

Back: Allah does not forgive that partners be associated with Him; He forgives what is less than that to whom He wills.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 4 | Topic: Shirk

---

Flashcard 13

Front: الإخلاص (Al-ikhlas)

Back: Sincerity – the goal is to live with sincere iman and win Allah's pleasure.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 4 | Topic: Conclusion
