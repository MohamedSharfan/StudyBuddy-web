# Grade 11 – Chapter 12: Developing Islamic Morals

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_12/Notes.md` (Tamil). English rendering.

## 🎯 What you will learn
- The difference between khalq and khuluq
- The importance of morals – hadiths
- Five categories of good morals
- How Islamic duties train good morals

> 📖 **Opening verse of the chapter (31:18 – Luqman):** وَلَا تُصَعِّرْ خَدَّكَ لِلنَّاسِ وَلَا تَمْشِ فِي الْأَرْضِ مَرَحًا إِنَّ اللهَ لَا يُحِبُّ كُلَّ مُخْتَالٍ فَخُوْرٍ
> **Meaning:** "Do not turn your face away from people (in pride), and do not walk proudly on the earth. Allah does not love **anyone arrogant and boastful**."

---

## 1. Khalq vs khuluq ⭐

| Arabic word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Khalq (الخَلْق)** | A person's **outward appearance** |
| **Khuluq (الخُلُق)** | A person's **character (morals)** |

- People who are drawn to someone with a beautiful khalq **turn away in dislike if his khuluq is bad.**
- People who avoid someone with an unattractive khalq **are drawn to him if his khuluq is excellent.**
- 👉 **Character matters more than appearance.**
- The Prophet's ﷺ supplication (meaning): "**O Allah! As You have made my appearance beautiful, make my character beautiful too.**"

### Who decides the limits of morality?
- Although everyone, of every race and religion, praises good morals, **people's beliefs and customs** set the limits, so what one group sees as good conduct another may not.
- Because Islam gives morals **great importance**, its scope is **very wide**, and it also gives **guidance** for moral training.

## 3. Five categories of good morals ⭐

### (1) Keeping to high values
- **Love, justice, truthfulness, honesty, keeping one's word, loyalty, a cheerful face.**
- Islam not only welcomes these but expects them to be **what identifies a Muslim**.

### (2) Respecting what deserves respect
- Honouring **parents, teachers, scholars and elders**; being **humble** before them.
- Respecting other people's **self-respect**; **a sense of modesty (haya').**

### (3) Avoiding uncivilised behaviour
- **Spitting, or relieving oneself, in public places**
- Speaking **loudly without need**
- Breaking **the manners of a gathering**
- When eating, **putting one's hand all over the plate**

### (4) Avoiding indecency
- Dress, adornment, words, revelry, shows and **mixed gatherings of men and women** that stir up sexual desire in a vulgar way.
- Islam has explained **the rules to follow** in these matters and **the punishments for breaking them**.

### (5) Keeping environmental values
- **Protecting useful plants**
- Keeping **public places and water sources clean**
- **Removing** from the environment anything that causes **harm or obstruction** to people or other creatures.

💡 **Memory tip – the 5 categories:** **High** values – **Respect** – **Civilised** manners – **Avoiding** indecency – **Environment.**

## 4. Good morals and iman
- The reason people do evil: **sound beliefs have not been formed in them, and they have not been well guided.**
- So Islam **links every good deed with iman and trains it through acts of worship**.
> 📖 The Prophet ﷺ (meaning): "**Good character and faith are bound up with each other. Whoever gives up one may as well give up the other.**"

---

## 5. How Islamic duties train good morals ⭐

| Duty | What it trains | Evidence (meaning) |
|---|---|---|
| **Prayer** | One who prays with life in his prayer gains **God-consciousness and closeness to Allah**; **light** in the heart; he **can tell good conduct from evil** | **29:45** – prayer **restrains from shameful and evil deeds and sins** |
| **Zakat** | **Love and generosity** towards the poor; **miserliness** goes; the heart is purified **from the impurity of love of the world** | **9:103** – take charity from their wealth to purify them… |
| **Fasting** | Controls desires and evil feelings → **God-consciousness, patience, generosity** | **(Ibn Khuzaymah):** fasting is not merely going without food and drink; it is **keeping away from evil and shameful things.** If anyone argues with you, say, "**I am fasting**" |
| **Hajj** | Training in **sacrifice and dedication**; among people from every direction – **brotherhood, equality, tolerance, understanding** | **2:197** – one in ihram must not have **sexual relations, use bad language or quarrel** |

---

## 6. The role model
- A **role model** is very important for following good morals.
- **(33:21)*** – In the Messenger of Allah you have an excellent example.
- **(68:4)** – O Prophet, you are of a great, noble character.
- 👉 Good moral qualities are **an expression of a person's inner feelings and beliefs** → to adorn life with good morals, **purifying the heart** is essential.

> ⚠️ *At this point the textbook prints "(33 al-Ahzab: 31)"; the verse is 33:21 (it is given correctly as 33:21 in Chapter 11) – confirm with the teacher.
