### A2. 2025 (2026) – Islam I, Q35

Question ID: P2025_2026-I-Q35

Marks: not stated on the paper (Islam I multiple-choice question)  
Source: OL_2025_2026_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

Which of the following statements about a Muslim's manners is WRONG?

- **A.** avoiding lying even in jest
- **B.** giving way, even when one is in the right, to avoid a pointless argument
- **C.** avoiding speaking loudly in public places without need
- **D.** when many eat together from one plate, reaching into all sides of it to take food

**Correct answer:** D – when many eat together from one plate, reaching into all sides of it to take food *(option (4) on the original paper)*

**Explanation:** When many eat together, good manners are to eat from what is nearest to oneself; reaching into all sides is bad manners.

### B1. 2023 (2024) – Islam II, Q6 (iii)

Question ID: P2023_2024-II-Q6.2  
Year: 2023 (2024)  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q6 (iii)  
Marks: 10 marks for the whole of Q6  
Source: OL_2023_2024_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(iii) Briefly explain 'how the basic duties of Islam train people to be drawn to good character'.

**Suggested answer:**

1. Prayer – fear of Allah, nearness to Allah, light in the heart; the ability to tell good from evil; "Prayer restrains from shameful and evil deeds" (29:45).
2. Zakat – love for the poor, a generous outlook; miserliness removed; the heart purified from love of the world (9:103).
3. Fasting – controlling desires and evil feelings, bringing taqwa, patience and generosity; "Fasting is not only refraining from eating and drinking; it is keeping away from what is evil… if anyone argues with you, say 'I am fasting'" (Ibn Khuzaymah).
4. Hajj – sacrifice and dedication; brotherhood, equality, tolerance; the one in ihram must not use evil words or quarrel (2:197).

**Answer explanation:** Give the moral quality each duty trains and the evidence for it.

### B2. 2024 (2025) – Islam II, Q7 (iv)

Question ID: P2024_2025-II-Q7.4  
Year: 2024 (2025)  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q7 (iv)  
Marks: 10 marks (short notes on two topics)  
Source: OL_2024_2025_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(iv) Keeping environmental values

**Suggested answer:**

- "Keeping environmental values" is one of the five classifications of good conduct.
- Protecting useful plants – planting trees is charity (Ahmad).
- Keeping public places and water sources clean.
- Removing from the environment things that harm people or other living things – removing harm from the road is a branch of iman.
- Love for living creatures – even najis animals such as dogs and pigs must not be treated cruelly.
- Man is the earth's steward (khilafah); developing the earth ('imarah) is his responsibility.

**Answer explanation:** Write, linking protection of the environment with iman and 'ibadah.
