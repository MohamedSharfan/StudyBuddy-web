# Grade 11 – Chapter 12: Developing Islamic Morals – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_12/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q17 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. In Arabic, what do the words "khalq" and "khuluq" refer to?

A. Khalq – character; khuluq – appearance
B. Khalq – appearance; khuluq – character
C. Both mean character
D. Both mean appearance

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Khalq (الخَلْق) = appearance; khuluq (الخُلُق) = character. Experience shows that character matters more than appearance.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q2. "Do not walk proudly on the earth; Allah does not love anyone arrogant and boastful" – which verse is this?

A. 68:4
B. 29:45
C. 2:197
D. 31:18

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: 31:18 (Luqman) – the opening verse of this chapter.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q3. What did the Prophet ﷺ pray for regarding character?

A. O Allah! Give me wealth
B. O Allah! Give me a position
C. O Allah! As You have made my appearance beautiful, make my character beautiful too
D. O Allah! Make me famous

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: This supplication shows the importance of character.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q4. Why do the limits of good conduct differ between societies?

A. Because morals are unimportant
B. Because everyone has the same beliefs
C. Because people's beliefs and customs set the limits
D. Because the government decides

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: What one group sees as good conduct is not seen that way by another.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q5. According to the hadith in Abu Dawud, whose reward does a believer reach through good character?

A. One who performed hajj
B. One who fasts and stands in prayer
C. A wealthy person
D. A scholar

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Good character brings a reward equal to much fasting and night prayer.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q6. What did the Prophet ﷺ say most often brings people into Paradise?

A. Wealth and position
B. Food and clothing
C. Education and fame
D. God-consciousness and good character

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: God-consciousness and good character are what most often bring people into Paradise.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q7. According to the hadith in Abu Dawud, where in Paradise is the house for one who "avoids lying even in jest"?

A. In the middle
B. On the edge
C. In the highest part
D. At the gate

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: One who avoids pointless argument – the edge; one who avoids lying – the middle; one with good character – the highest part.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q8. Where is the house in Paradise for one who avoids arguing even when he is in the right?

A. In the middle of Paradise
B. On the edge of Paradise
C. In the highest part of Paradise
D. There is no house

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: The Prophet ﷺ guarantees a house on the edge of Paradise for one who gives way even when he is in the right.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q9. Among the categories of good morals, which is NOT one of the "high values"?

A. Keeping public places clean
B. Truthfulness
C. A cheerful face
D. Justice

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Keeping public places clean belongs to "environmental values". High values: love, justice, truthfulness, honesty, keeping one's word, loyalty, a cheerful face.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q10. Putting one's hand all over the plate when eating is to be avoided under which category of good morals?

A. Avoiding indecency
B. Environmental values
C. Avoiding uncivilised behaviour
D. Respecting what deserves respect

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Spitting in public, speaking loudly without need, breaking the manners of a gathering, and putting one's hand all over the plate are uncivilised behaviour.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q11. Honouring parents, teachers, scholars and elders belongs to which category?

A. High values
B. Avoiding uncivilised behaviour
C. Respecting what deserves respect
D. Environmental values

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Being humble before them, respecting others' self-respect, and a sense of modesty also belong here.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q12. What does the textbook give as the reason people do evil instead of keeping to good morals?

A. Poverty alone
B. Too much wealth
C. Too much education
D. Sound beliefs have not been formed in them, and they have not been well guided

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: That is why Islam links every good deed with iman and trains it through acts of worship.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q13. "Good character and faith are bound up with each other; whoever gives up one…" – complete it.

A. …may as well give up the other
B. …will rise in the other
C. …will be forgiven
D. …will enter Paradise

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Iman is not complete without good character, and good character does not last without iman.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q14. Which verse says that prayer restrains from shameful and evil deeds and sins?

A. 9:103
B. 29:45
C. 2:197
D. 31:18

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: 29:45 (al-'Ankabut) – one who prays with life in his prayer can tell good conduct from evil.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q15. According to the hadith in Ibn Khuzaymah, what is the real aim of fasting?

A. Keeping away from evil and shameful things
B. Only going without food and drink
C. Losing weight
D. Sleeping

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: If anyone argues, one should say, "I am fasting" – fasting is moral training.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q16. Which verse says that one in ihram must not have sexual relations, use bad language or quarrel?

A. 2:197
B. 2:196
C. 9:103
D. 29:45

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: 2:197 – hajj is also a training in morals.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q17. Which of the following does the textbook NOT mention as a change zakat brings about in the giver's heart?

A. Love for the poor
B. Miserliness goes away
C. Purity from the impurity of love of the world
D. Pride increases

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Zakat builds not pride but love, generosity and purity (9:103).

Difficulty: Easy
