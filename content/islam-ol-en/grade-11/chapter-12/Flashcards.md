Flashcard 4

Front: The Prophet's supplication for good character?

Back: (meaning) O Allah! As You have made my appearance beautiful, make my character beautiful too.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 12 | Topic: Khalq and khuluq

---

Flashcard 5

Front: The three houses in Paradise (Abu Dawud)?

Back: On the edge for one who avoids pointless argument; in the middle for one who does not lie even in jest; in the highest part for one with good character.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 12 | Topic: Importance

---

Flashcard 6

Front: The five categories of good morals?

Back: High values, respecting what deserves respect, avoiding uncivilised behaviour, avoiding indecency, environmental values.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 12 | Topic: Five categories

---

Flashcard 7

Front: Examples of high values?

Back: Love, justice, truthfulness, honesty, keeping one's word, loyalty, a cheerful face.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 12 | Topic: Five categories

---

Flashcard 8

Front: Examples of uncivilised behaviour?

Back: Spitting or relieving oneself in public, speaking loudly without need, breaking the manners of a gathering, putting one's hand all over the plate.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 12 | Topic: Five categories

---

Flashcard 9

Front: What are environmental values?

Back: Protecting useful plants, keeping public places and water sources clean, removing things that cause harm.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 12 | Topic: Five categories

---

Flashcard 10

Front: Good character and faith – the saying of the Prophet?

Back: The two are bound up with each other; whoever gives up one may as well give up the other.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 12 | Topic: Morals and iman

---

Flashcard 11

Front: The four duties and their evidence for moral training?

Back: Prayer – 29:45; zakat – 9:103; fasting – the hadith in Ibn Khuzaymah; hajj – 2:197.

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 12 | Topic: Duties and morals

---

Flashcard 12

Front: What should one say while fasting to someone who comes to argue?

Back: "I am fasting" (Ibn Khuzaymah).

Grade: 11 | Chapter: 12 | Topic: Duties and morals
