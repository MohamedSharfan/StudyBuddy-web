# Grade 11 – Chapter 24: A Sri Lankan Muslim Pioneer – 2 (Dr T. B. Jayah)

## 🎯 What you will learn

- The birth and education of Dr Tuan Burhanuddeen Jayah
- His teaching career, and his mark as an educationist
- His work for the growth of Zahira College
- His ideas on learning in a religious environment
- His political contribution and diplomatic service
- His death, and the honours given to him

---

## 1️⃣ Introduction

- ⭐ The newspaper "**The Crescent – 1946**" praised him as "**the Sir Sayyid Ahmad Khan of Sri Lanka**".
- He holds a lasting place in the educational and political history of Sri Lanka – especially of its Muslims.

---

## 2️⃣ Birth and education

| Point | Details |
|---|---|
| Full name | Tuan Burhanuddeen Jayah |
| Community | ⭐ **Malay** |
| Born | ⭐ **1890**, **Galagedara**, Kandy |
| Childhood | Galagedara, Kurunegala |
| Religious education | From 'ulama, as a child |
| Primary education | **St Paul's** College, Kandy |
| Later | With a scholarship, **St Thomas'** College, Kotahena |

- At school he had the standing of "**an outstanding student**".
- Prizes he won: the J. A. C. Mendis mathematics prize, the Dr E. Bell Latin prize, the Christopher Obeyesekere first prize for Western literature, and the best-student-in-class prize.

---

## 3️⃣ Teaching career

| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 1910 | Assistant teacher at **Dharmaraja** College, Kandy |
| Later | Teacher of Western literature at **Prince of Wales'** College, Moratuwa |
| 1916 | **Bachelor of Arts**, through the University of London external examination |
| 1917 | Teacher at **Ananda College** – Western literature and history |

- Although he registered as a law student, he loved **teaching** most.
- ⭐ So he left a lasting mark as "**an educationist**".

---

## 4️⃣ Concern for Muslim education

- At that time the Sri Lankan Muslim community was behind in education, and was isolated and pushed aside in cultural and political life as well.
- His experience at Prince of Wales', Dharmaraja and Ananda helped him.
- The point of one of his speeches (summary): other communities have produced many lawyers, doctors, members of the State Council and experts; how many do we have?
- ⭐ **He set out to make Siddi Lebbe's dream come true** – he believed that it was through English education that Muslims could rise in education, society and politics.

---

## 5️⃣ His work at Zahira College ⭐

- ⭐ **1921** – **Principal** of Zahira College.
- The situation then: 6 teachers, 51 students; no proper furniture; the single building was used at night as a place for people to stay.
- Within two decades it grew rapidly to the standard of Ananda, Royal and St Thomas'.

| Work | Details |
|---|---|
| 1924 | A **playground** for the college |
| Main hall | Donated by **N. D. H. Abdul Caffoor**; opened by Governor **Sir Herbert** (Stanley) |
| Sports | Football, rugby, boxing and cricket introduced |

### Crises and expansion

- The economic depression of 1930 and the Second World War → setbacks.
- During the war the Muslims of Colombo were evacuated; Zahira became **military quarters**.
- ⭐ He turned this into an opportunity to spread education to the outer districts → branches at **Gampola, Aluthgama, Matale and Puttalam**.

---

## 6️⃣ Learning in a religious environment – Jayah's thinking ⭐

- Religion plays an important part in developing the human personality.
- Students should learn **in a religious environment**; at the same time there must be **coexistence that respects the beliefs of other religions** too.
- Education in a religious environment → should create **patriotism, concern for social welfare, and care for the underprivileged**.
- Education on the right path → the country's problems will be solved quickly.
- He also paid attention to **the education of Muslim girls**, which had been neglected.

### Teachers' welfare

- He wanted the economic condition of teachers to improve.
- ⭐ He was president of both the **All Ceylon Teachers' Union** and the **Ceylon Headmasters' Association**.

---

## 7️⃣ Political contribution

| Year / matter | Details |
|---|---|
| ⭐ 1924 | Entered politics – chosen for the **Legislative Council** |
| Relations | Sir Ponnambalam Ramanathan, James Peiris |
| Ceylon National Congress | Worked for independence together with Sinhala and Tamil leaders |
| Law | Helped ensure the proper implementation of the Muslim marriage and divorce law |
| Language | Contributed to making Sinhala and Tamil official languages |
| Jumu'ah | Worked to obtain time off for the Friday (Jumu'ah) prayer |
| Representation | Asked for the communal electorate system to be removed and for representation for Muslims too |

- ⭐ His statement (summary): "For me, there is no greater right than political freedom."
- Close to the **All Ceylon Muslim League**; he won for the League the standing of representative of the Muslim community.
- **1936 – 1947** – a political life that helped lead to national independence.
- ⭐ **Minister of Labour** in the cabinet of the first Prime Minister, **D. S. Senanayake**.

---

## 8️⃣ Diplomatic service

- ⭐ **1950** – **Sri Lanka's High Commissioner to Pakistan**.
- Chosen as head of the diplomatic corps there.
- ⭐ **The University of Karachi** awarded him an **honorary doctorate**.

---

## 9️⃣ Religious service and death

- In **1960** he went to Saudi Arabia for discussions on setting up a hostel in Makkah for Sri Lankan hajis (pilgrims).
- ⭐ **31 May 1960** – he died there of illness.
- He was buried in **Jannat al-Baqi'** with state honours from the Saudi government.
- ⭐ **31.05.1981** – the Sri Lankan government issued a **stamp**.

## ✅ Quick revision

- "The Sir Sayyid Ahmad Khan of Sri Lanka" – The Crescent, 1946.
- Malay community; born 1890 in Galagedara.
- 1916 University of London B.A.; 1917 teacher at Ananda.
- 1921 principal of Zahira (6 teachers, 51 students).
- Branches: Gampola, Aluthgama, Matale, Puttalam.
- 1924 Legislative Council; Minister of Labour (D. S. Senanayake).
- 1950 High Commissioner to Pakistan; honorary doctorate from the University of Karachi.
- Died 31 May 1960 in Saudi Arabia; Jannat al-Baqi'.
