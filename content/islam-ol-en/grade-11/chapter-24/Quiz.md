# Grade 11 – Chapter 24: A Sri Lankan Muslim Pioneer – 2 (Dr T. B. Jayah) – Quiz

> Source: `StudyBuddy_Islam/Grade_11/Chapter_24/Quiz.md` (Tamil), Q1–Q19 in the same order. Answer letters unchanged.

---

Q1. Which newspaper praised T. B. Jayah as "the Sir Sayyid Ahmad Khan of Sri Lanka"?

A. Muslim Nesan
B. Ceylon Almanac
C. Gnanadeepam
D. The Crescent – 1946

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: The newspaper "The Crescent – 1946" praised him in this way. Muslim Nesan and Gnanadeepam were published by Siddi Lebbe.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q2. To which community did T. B. Jayah belong?

A. Malay
B. Moor
C. Memon
D. Bohra

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Tuan Burhanuddeen Jayah belonged to the Malay community.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q3. In which year and where was Jayah born?

A. 1838, Kandy town
B. 1890, Galagedara, Kandy
C. 1890, Colombo
D. 1910, Kurunegala

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: He was born in 1890 at Galagedara in Kandy; he spent his childhood in Galagedara and Kurunegala.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q4. Which college did Jayah join with a scholarship?

A. St Paul's College, Kandy
B. Ananda College
C. St Thomas' College, Kotahena
D. Royal College

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Primary education – St Paul's College, Kandy; later, with a scholarship, St Thomas' College, Kotahena.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q5. At which college did Jayah begin work as an assistant teacher in 1910?

A. Zahira College
B. Prince of Wales' College
C. Ananda College
D. Dharmaraja College, Kandy

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: 1910 – Dharmaraja; then Prince of Wales', Moratuwa; 1917 – Ananda; 1921 – principal of Zahira.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q6. In which year did Jayah become a Bachelor of Arts through the University of London external examination?

A. 1916
B. 1910
C. 1921
D. 1924

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: He graduated in 1916 and became a teacher at Ananda College in 1917.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q7. Although he registered as a law student, what did Jayah love most?

A. Politics
B. Teaching
C. Business
D. Journalism

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Because he loved teaching, he left a lasting mark as "an educationist".

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q8. In which year did Jayah become principal of Zahira College?

A. 1917
B. 1924
C. 1921
D. 1930

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: When he took over in 1921 there were only 6 teachers and 51 students.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q9. What was the condition of Zahira College when Jayah became principal?

A. 6 teachers, 51 students; no proper furniture
B. 50 teachers, 1,000 students
C. The leading college in the country
D. The college had been closed

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: The single building had become a place for people to stay at night; within two decades he raised it to the standard of Ananda and Royal.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q10. Whose donation built the main hall of Zahira College?

A. Wapchi Marikar
B. M. C. Abdul Rahman
C. Governor Sir Herbert
D. N. D. H. Abdul Caffoor

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: It was built with Abdul Caffoor's donation and opened by Governor Sir Herbert.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q11. What happened to Zahira College during the Second World War?

A. It was closed and sold
B. It became military quarters
C. It became a hospital
D. It was not affected at all

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: The Muslims of Colombo were evacuated and the college became military quarters; Jayah turned this into an opportunity to spread education to the outer districts.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q12. Which of the following was NOT one of the places where branches of Zahira College arose because of the wartime crisis?

A. Gampola
B. Aluthgama
C. Badulla
D. Puttalam

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Branches: Gampola, Aluthgama, Matale, Puttalam. Badulla – one of the places where Siddi Lebbe set up a school.

Difficulty: Hard

---

Q13. Which of the following is part of Jayah's thinking on education in a religious environment?

A. Other religions should be ignored
B. Religious education is not needed
C. Students should learn in their own religious environment while respecting the beliefs of other religions and living in coexistence
D. English education alone is enough

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: He said education in a religious environment should create patriotism, concern for social welfare and care for the underprivileged.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q14. What opportunity did Jayah have to promote teachers' welfare?

A. The post of Minister of Education
B. The presidency of the All Ceylon Teachers' Union and of the Ceylon Headmasters' Association
C. The post of university Vice-Chancellor
D. The post of Governor

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Being elected president of the two associations helped him fulfil his wish to improve teachers' economic condition.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q15. In which year did Jayah enter politics?

A. 1924
B. 1921
C. 1936
D. 1948

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: In 1924 he was chosen for the Legislative Council; he had close relations with Sir Ponnambalam Ramanathan and James Peiris.

Difficulty: Easy

---

Q16. What post did Jayah hold in the cabinet of D. S. Senanayake, Sri Lanka's first Prime Minister?

A. Minister of Education
B. Minister of Finance
C. Minister of Health
D. Minister of Labour

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: He became Minister of Labour; later, in 1950, he became High Commissioner to Pakistan.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q17. Which university honoured Jayah with a doctorate?

A. University of London
B. Al-Azhar University
C. University of Karachi
D. University of Peradeniya

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: The University of Karachi awarded him a doctorate for his service as High Commissioner to Pakistan.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q18. What was the purpose of Jayah's journey to Saudi Arabia in 1960?

A. Only to perform Hajj
B. Discussions on setting up a hostel in Makkah for Sri Lankan hajis
C. To serve as ambassador
D. For medical treatment

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Because of an illness during that visit he died on 31 May 1960 and was buried in Jannat al-Baqi'.

Difficulty: Medium

---

Q19. On what date did the Sri Lankan government issue a stamp in Jayah's memory?

A. 31.05.1960
B. 11.01.1977
C. 04.02.1948
D. 31.05.1981

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: 31.05.1981 – the Jayah stamp. 11.01.1977 – the Siddi Lebbe stamp.

Difficulty: Hard
