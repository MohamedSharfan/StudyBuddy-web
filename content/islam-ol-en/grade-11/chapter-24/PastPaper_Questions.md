### B1. 2021 (2022) – Islam II, Q7 (ii)

Question ID: P2021_2022-II-Q7.2  
Year: 2021 (2022)  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q7 (ii)  
Marks: 10 marks (short notes on two topics)  
Source: OL_2021_2022_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(ii) T. B. Jayah

**Suggested answer:**

- Tuan Burhanuddeen Jayah – of the Malay community; born in 1890 at Galagedara, Kandy; educated at St Paul's College, Kandy, and St Thomas' College, Kotahena.
- Teacher at Dharmaraja, Prince of Wales' and Ananda colleges; B.A., University of London, 1916 – "an educationist".
- Principal of Zahira College in 1921 (6 teachers, 51 students) → raised it to the standard of Ananda and Royal; branches in Gampola, Aluthgama, Matale and Puttalam during the war.
- His thinking on education in a religious environment, coexistence that respects other religions, and the education of Muslim girls; president of the All Ceylon Teachers' Union.
- Member of the Legislative Council in 1924; the independence struggle through the Ceylon National Congress; Minister of Labour in D. S. Senanayake's cabinet.
- High Commissioner to Pakistan in 1950; honorary doctorate from the University of Karachi.
- Died in Saudi Arabia on 31 May 1960; buried in Jannat al-Baqi'; a stamp was issued on 31.05.1981.

**Answer explanation:** Mention his educational, political and diplomatic work in balance.

### B2. 2022 (2023) – Islam II, Q7 (iv)

Question ID: P2022_2023-II-Q7.4  
Year: 2022 (2023)  
Paper and question: Islam II, Q7 (iv)  
Marks: 10 marks (short notes on two topics)  
Source: OL_2022_2023_Islam_Tamil_Medium.md (page number not identifiable in the Markdown source)

**Question:**

(iv) The educational contributions of T. B. Jayah

**Suggested answer:**

- Teacher at Dharmaraja, Prince of Wales' and Ananda colleges – the mark of "an educationist".
- Aware of the educational backwardness of the Muslim community, he set out to make Siddi Lebbe's dream come true; social progress through English education.
- Principal of Zahira College in 1921 (6 teachers, 51 students) → within two decades raised to the standard of Ananda, Royal and St Thomas'; a playground in 1924, the main hall; football, rugby and cricket introduced.
- During the Second World War he spread education through branches in Gampola, Aluthgama, Matale and Puttalam.
- Education in a religious environment; coexistence that respects other religions; patriotism and concern for social welfare; attention to the education of Muslim girls.
- Teachers' welfare – president of both the All Ceylon Teachers' Union and the Ceylon Headmasters' Association.

**Answer explanation:** Leave out his political work and focus on his educational contributions.
